Accenture in Europe

We forge coalitions, with deep roots across Europe and are uniquely positioned to drive reinvention. Put simply, we enable Europe’s companies on their path to becoming reinvention leaders.

Creating positive change

We’re committed to creating positive change in Europe and around the world, ensuring we act as responsible business leaders for our people, our clients, and the communities we call home.

European Foundations, Accountable Governance

Close

We are deeply rooted in Europe, with strong local leadership and governance that ensure accountability and alignment with regulatory standards. As the largest provider of digital transformation services in the region, we offer the broadest set of capabilities and support leading enterprises as well as the European operations of global organizations.

We partner with governments across Europe to enhance citizen services, defense, border control, healthcare, justice, and policing.

We contribute to the resilience of European critical infrastructures through our cybersecurity practice, comprising more than 4,300 professionals.

We collaborate with Europe’s leading and emerging technology champions, accelerating the growth of major European-headquartered tech players (SAP, Siemens, Schneider Electric, Dassault Systems), LLM providers (iGenius, HCP, Aleph Alpha, Multiverse Compute), and sovereign technology providers (OVHcloud, S3NS, Schwartz Digits, StackIT, PSN, Aruba).

Aligned with European Values and Societal Goals

Close

Our values are deeply aligned with European principles, guiding our commitment to responsible technology use, the highest ethical standards, and strict compliance with global and local laws.

We use data and technology responsibly, applying responsible tech and AI practices by design.

Compliance and Security Measures: We implement specific protocols and advanced security measures to meet and exceed European regulatory requirements.

We embed environmental sustainability into how we operate our business, holding ourselves accountable to measurable objectives.

We help clients achieve their ESG goals by embedding sustainability into their strategy, operations, and value chains.

Community Skilling: We are equipping around 790,000 people in Europe in FY 2024 with the skills to succeed. We also pledge to support the hiring and skilling of 16,000 refugee job seekers and work with NGOs and local partners to leverage technology to drive social impact.
