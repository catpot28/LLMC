Information Security at Accenture

Protecting Accenture, our clients, and employees requires a laser-focused approach.

What we do

View Transcript

Accenture’s Information Security team provides the technical architecture, governance, operational infrastructure, client data protection and behavioral change programs to keep all data and information—for which Accenture is responsible—secure. The team, our processes and programs are 100% dedicated to fostering an overall enterprise culture where security is a top priority.

Read the report

Who we are

Governance, risk and compliance

Establishes and maintains cyber risk management controls, policies, processes and metrics to fortify Accenture's security posture.​

Deployment and acquisitions​

Leads client data protection, equipping client teams to identify and reduce risks. Embeds security controls in operations and acquisitions.

Culture and behavior change

Leads strategic initiatives to boost awareness and adoption of security practices that build a cyber mindset and help reduce risk.

Technology and operations​

Selects, deploys, and operates security technology for Accenture that monitors, mitigates and defends against vulnerabilities and threats.​

Strategy and emerging​ programs ​

Leads cyber strategy, oversees cyber regulatory compliance, third-party risk management, and programs to address emerging security risks.

Cyber incident response​

Detects and defends the Accenture network infrastructure against malicious cyber attacks and responds to cyber incidents 24/7/365.​
