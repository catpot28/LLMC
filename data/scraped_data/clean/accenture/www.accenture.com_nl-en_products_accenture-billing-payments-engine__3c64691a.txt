How it works

Scales as you grow

Handle high-volume, multi-entity billing with accuracy and control. Our platform allows you to launch new products, services and pricing models faster—while maintaining total control over revenue and cash flow.

Configurable by design

Our platform eliminates the need for expensive, custom development. You can now use tailored configurations to update pricing rules, calculations and billing cycles as your business requirements change. This flexibility reduces long-term technical debt and lowers the total cost of ownership.

Integrates seamlessly

Our 150+ pre-built APIs integrate seamlessly with existing platforms and systems, enabling real-time data synchronization and flexible deployment with existing ERP, CRM and legacy billing systems.

Automation at scale

We automate billing, payments and exception handling at scale, increasing on-time billing and collections and reducing the amount of manual effort needed as your business grows. The platform also automates payment reconciliation, drastically reducing errors and ensuring accurate financial reporting.

5 ways we support your billing and payments cycle

Advanced Payment Processing

We provide comprehensive payment processing capabilities, handling multiple payment types, including Automated Clearing House, credit cards, e-wallets and traditional bank transfers. The platform ensures timely and secure payment execution, improving cash flow and reducing operational costs associated with manual payment handling.

Flexible billing management

We support diverse billing models, including subscription-based, consumption-based and one-time billing, enabling businesses to tailor their invoicing to match unique customer needs. The platform also offers multi-tiered pricing structures and discount management to enhance flexibility in customer offerings.

Franchise and multi‑entity billing

A standout feature of the platform is its support for franchise billing, making it an ideal solution for organizations with decentralized operations or franchise models. It automates the aggregation of franchise fees, royalties and service charges, streamlining the flow of payments between franchisors and franchisees.

Real-time analytics and reporting

We offer powerful reporting and analytics tools, providing businesses with real-time visibility into billing, payments and overall financial performance. These insights enable informed decision-making, improved forecasting and better cash flow management.

Exceptions, credit and collections automation

We automate exception handling, credit scoring and collections workflows to identify billing issues early and reduce manual intervention. Configurable rules and actions help enterprises improve on time payments while maintaining control over customer risk and financial exposure.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Accenture Billing and Payments Engine designed for?

Accenture Billing and Payments Engine is designed for large enterprises and public-sector organizations with complex billing requirements—such as high transaction volumes, multi-entity operations, diverse pricing models and regulatory considerations. It is commonly used in industries including utilities, insurance and public sector programs, retail and subscription businesses, telecommunications and multi-entity services organizations.

How is Accenture Billing and Payments Engine different from traditional billing systems?

Many traditional billing systems rely on rigid configurations or extensive customization that can be difficult to adapt as business models evolve. Accenture Billing and Payments Engine is built around configurable billing models designed to support change—helping organizations manage complexity as products, pricing structures and operating models evolve.

Can Accenture Billing and Payments Engine work with our existing systems?

Yes. The platform is designed to operate within existing enterprise ecosystems. It can function as a centralized billing and payments platform or complement existing systems where flexibility, scale, or automation are limited. An API-first architecture, including 150+ REST APIs, supports integration with ERP, CRM, customer information systems and legacy billing platforms.

Does implementing the platform require replacing our current billing systems?

Not necessarily. Accenture Billing and Payments Engine supports phased adoption and coexistence with existing systems, enabling organizations to modernize billing capabilities incrementally rather than through large scale system replacement programs.

What types of billing and pricing models does the platform support?

The platform supports a wide range of billing and monetization models, including recurring, usage-based, subscription and one-time billing. It is designed to handle complex pricing structures such as tiered pricing, bundles, discounts, multi component charges and multi party settlement.

Will this help me manage compliance and reduce regulatory risk?

Yes. Our built-in features for global compliance and security protocols ensure your billing and payments are meeting regulatory, audit and governance requirements. This includes support for tax compliance, GDPR, PCI-DSS and other regulatory standards.
