Human Capital Management Software

HR document management made easy

Accenture Document Composer uses Employee Folders to simplify creating, storing, distributing, retrieving and retaining employee and candidate-related communications.

Contact us

What you’ll achieve

Save time with simplified HR document processes

Automate document generation and streamline candidate document creation. Employee self-service enables efficiency with quick PDF access.

Reduce effort in document creation and distribution

Create and refine documents with intuitive data field insertion from SAP SuccessFactors or SAP ERP HCM, saving drafts for flexibility and collaborative review. Enhance design with headers, images and tables and distribute using SAP SuccessFactors workflows.

Gain compliance confidence with reliable document storage

Store and share documents with employees, support GDPR compliance and retention and enable employees to submit documents, enhancing collaboration and security.

Enhance the user experience without sacrificing security

The software respects role-based permissions from SAP SuccessFactors for easier data access. Also improves the user experience by an easy to use intuitive interface

Boost skills with comprehensive learning and reporting

Experience a 24/7 digital learning platform with single sign-on, curated learning boards and assessments. A user-friendly interface offers detailed reports on document creation, template usage and storage statistics to optimize workflow.

Create, distribute and store HR documents more easily

Accenture Document Composer facilitates the creation, distribution and storage of employee and candidate-related communications.
