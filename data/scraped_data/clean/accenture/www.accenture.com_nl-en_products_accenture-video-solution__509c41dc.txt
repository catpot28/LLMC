Accenture Video Solution (AVS) enables content monetization, SVOD and digital services at scale, with modular architecture that configures to meet business needs without stringing multiple vendors together. Agile video microservices speed implementation of new features, reduce development effort, and provide a nimble platform for innovation – all on one online video platform.

Why Accenture Video Solution?

Migrate from legacy to a modern platform

AVS enables smooth migration from legacy IPTV or complex broadcast stacks to a modern digital video platform accelerating B2C and B2B growth, allowing Clients to choose a managed SaaS or tailored solution catering for green or brownfield scenarios.

Speed to market

AVS industrialized multi-cloud architecture, full-service feature set supporting SVOD, TVOD, AVOD, turnkey apps and low-code/no-code tools for UX creation and ecosystem integration allows to rapidly launch compelling digital video services at scale.

Scalability and resilience built-in

AVS cloud first design, high density micro-service architecture combined with intelligent operations allows to scale rapidly with audience demand for live events and entertainment while retaining stability for non-interrupted viewing experience.

Data driven insights & agentic AI optimization

Realtime multi-service insights into business operations and performance coupled with cross-functional AI agents embedded into end-to-end workflows enables businesses to accelerate path to enhanced user engagement, business growth and ops efficiency.
