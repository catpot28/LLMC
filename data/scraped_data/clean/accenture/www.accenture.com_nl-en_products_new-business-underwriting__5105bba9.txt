ALIP New Business and Underwriting

Tomorrow’s new business opportunities start with today’s digital strategies. Deliver more—choices, channels, faster decisions—profitably.

Your engine for continuous innovation

ALIP New Business and Underwriting enhances customer engagement, helping carriers move past manual processing and long underwriting times with new data sources to improve risk assessment and profitability. ALIP accelerates product launches and policy issuance with full automation and real-time data validation for both direct-to-consumer and producer-initiated models.
