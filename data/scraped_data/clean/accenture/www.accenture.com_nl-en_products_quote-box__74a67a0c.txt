Quote-in-a-Box (QIB2)

Appeal to brokers who can help grow your business in the structured settlement market.

What it does

Accenture has decades of experience helping carriers quote structured settlements, a product in growing demand. Quote-in-a-Box (QIB2) is a scalable SaaS solution with predictable costs, featuring deep, out-of-the-box functionality to speed up sales and improve broker engagement through simplified quote generation, submission, tracking, and communication.

Read more

Features

Optimized quoting

Gain new levels of business intelligence, such as buying behavior, through centralized quote management.

Easy-to-use interface

Streamline real-time communication with centralized dashboards between brokers and the home office.

Multi-broker integration

Quickly integrate with over half a dozen broker systems out-of-the-box and enable new distribution channels with open APIs.

Security and reliability

Maintain confidence with constant security monitoring and updates across our award-winning platform.

Cloud agnostic SaaS solution

Access from anywhere with a cloud agnostic SaaS solution.

Highly flexible and configurable

Single source of truth

Leverage existing technology and tools used in ALIP.

Platform stability and continuity

Built for reliability and smooth operations.

Mobile-first responsive UI

Optimized for tablets, enabling on-the-go use.
