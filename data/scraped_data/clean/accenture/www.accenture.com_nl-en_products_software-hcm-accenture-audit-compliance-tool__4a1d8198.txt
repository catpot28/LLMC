Support HR administration

Accenture HR Audit and Compliance Tool helps approvals or personnel cost planning processes be efficient, flexible and secure and supports the business with meaningful analysis.

Enable HR teams to manage superior audits effortlessly

Plan audits by scheduling configurable data collection runs. Catch missing or incorrect data before it causes issues. Automate audit processes with a job scheduler to free HR teams for other tasks.

Reduce IT dependency with configurable rules

Create exception rules with standard, configurable options tailored to your business needs. Access a library of more than 750 rules to meet evolving HR requirements with minimal effort.

Boost productivity and reduce costs

All-in-one software includes a rule library, workbench, exception tracking, powerful analytics and reporting, eliminating the need for multiple solutions. Quality assurance, increased data security and reliability speed up error resolution.

Adjust audit demands to fit business plans

Maintain flexibility with an audit plan that defines a test period, displayed in a comprehensive dashboard, independent of different payroll periods. Easily manage exceptions for specific data collections and analyze per pay period or type.

Enhance HR and Payroll efficiencies

Centralize your HR/Payroll exception reporting and resolution with a single, proactive workbench. Gain transparency by checking receipts, verifying travel information and implementing rules to validate cost categories.

Leveraging the Accenture HR Audit and Compliance Tool helps clients achieve high performance by constantly ensuring a high level of quality in HR data while reducing the risk and the cost of managing process compliance.

Accenture Audit and Compliance Tool

Complexity and legislative compliance demand ever greater transparency of organizations’ activities and finances. Radical new approaches to personnel data are required. Accenture HR Audit and Compliance Tool helps Human Resources (HR) and IT departments enhance their HR systems by enabling them to maintain personnel data at a consistently high quality. It simplifies exception checking, reduces the cost of managing quality assurance and, ultimately, helps to document your processes for internal and external auditors.
