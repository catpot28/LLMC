Accenture Clone and Test for Cloud

In a dynamic business environment, protecting sensitive data is vital, alongside maintaining the quality and accuracy of information. Accenture Clone and Test Cloud anonymizes data safely and securely in SAP’s SuccessFactors test instances, ready for ongoing post go-live testing and application error resolution. With a subscription-based model, HR teams only pay for the contracted period.
