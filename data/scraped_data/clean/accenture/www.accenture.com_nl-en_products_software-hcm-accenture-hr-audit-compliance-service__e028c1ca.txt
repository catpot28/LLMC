Gain HR data insight and control

Superior auditing to save valuable time and effort

Better manage internal and external HR audits by preparing the necessary data quickly. Apply the latest technologies in predictive analytics to free up HR teams for strategic tasks. Integrates with SAP SuccessFactors to resolve issues fast.

Greater efficiency and productivity

A job scheduler enables HR teams to plan and execute configured rules for audit purposes in the background while automated AI-driven audit reporting identifies inconsistencies before they become an issue for the business.

Gain versatility and reduce skills shortages

Enhance the user experience with features such as a user-friendly workbench, exception tracking and powerful analytics already built-in to the product. Eliminate the need for multiple software solutions to serve the needs of your organization.

Support business needs with customized configuration

Access business-driven or AI-assisted rules and criteria from a library of more than 500 rules. Customize rules to ever-changing HR requirements swiftly with limited effort by taking advantage of our Extended Productivity Service offering.

Lower cost and enhance performance

Gain flexibility with scalable performance with a cloud-based subscription service. Save time on searching and double-checking errors with fast and accurate mass correction options to lower the cost of managing quality assurance.
