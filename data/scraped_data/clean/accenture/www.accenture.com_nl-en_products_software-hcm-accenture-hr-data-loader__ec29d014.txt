Manage ongoing and recurring HR processes

Free up time for other strategic tasks

Enhance the reliability of HR data with validated records before updating SAP SuccessFactors, maintaining data accuracy and integrity. Support recurring human capital management processes like bonus payments and promotions.

Reduce the need for specialized skills

Users can access pre-configured templates for workforce changes, including new hires and terminations. Take advantage of an easy-to-use, two-step process: prepare data and upload to SAP SuccessFactors, with optional approvals.

Improve compliance and control of HR data

Automatically manage technical dependencies and data sequencing in SAP SuccessFactors. Maintain and update mass data efficiently, ensuring compliance with policies. Enjoy a controlled process with centralized, approved data changes.

Simplify HR team tasks and training

HR users are guided through specific scenarios using pre-defined templates, without needing to understand technical dependencies. Reliable and accurate data entry every time eases daily HR activities and enhances business operations.

Mass data changes in SAP SuccessFactors made easy

Dynamic workforces are constantly changing, and mass data maintenance with manual entry is open to errors. Accenture HR Data Loader is designed to manage ongoing and recurring HR processes in SAP SuccessFactors such as promotions, new hires or terminations. There is no need to gather, transform, populate or synchronize data in multiple templates for different objects.

Dynamic workforce changes, organizational restructuring and continuous HR processes demand frequent updates in SAP SuccessFactors. Often, mass data maintenance is necessary, which can be time consuming for organizations especially when operating in multiple locations.
