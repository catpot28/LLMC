Accenture Care Coach

Immersive training for high performance

Enhance the performance of your contact center agents with lifelike, immersive learning from Accenture Care Coach.

The training platform for service professionals

Accenture Care Coach is a vital tool for onboarding, retraining, and upskilling contact center agents. This GenAI-powered solution brings learning to life with immersive, lifelike simulations that offer personalized feedback and skills certification. Experience real-world training, precise performance assessments, and tailored coaching insights!

Request a demo

Why Accenture Care Coach

Generate revenue

Achieve a 35% Increase in cross-sell/upsell revenue

Improve customer satisfaction

Boost your CSAT to drive greater customer satisfaction and loyalty

Enhance performance

Increase knowledge retention for your agents and trainers

Lower ramp up time

Reduce Ramp Time to average performance

Dynamic Multimodal Simulations

A highly flexible Scenario Builder enables managers to create and assign custom, real-life simulations that mimic authentic customer personas. This tool effectively trains agents in managing Voice, concurrent Chat, and Email interactions.

Performance Insights

The Agent Dashboard features Scores, Feedback and a Leaderboard for comparison with peers, while the Manager's Dashboard provides an overview of the team's training completion status and overall performance. Tailored coaching and training plans can be developed based on individual agent performance.

Expedited Learning

Agents' learning curve is shortened, enhancing their effectiveness.

Soft Skills Scoring

Scoring and feedback are provided for good comprehension and grammar empathy.

Language Support

Training simulations and scoring/feedback can be created in your chosen language.

Multi-level Support

Different types of training are supported as agents progress through various levels of maturity.
