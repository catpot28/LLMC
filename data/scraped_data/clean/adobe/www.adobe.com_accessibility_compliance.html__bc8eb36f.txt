Adobe Accessibility Compliance Overview

Adobe Accessibility Compliance Overview

https://main--cc--adobecom.aem.live/cc-shared/assets/img/product-icons/svg/adobe-corp-logo-2024.svg

Accessibility conformance reports for Adobe solutions.

Choose software that will help you produce accessible content and meet accessibility standards. Adobe offers conformance information for its products that details applicable standards and outlines the accessibility features of each product.

Most reports listed on this page are Accessibility Conformance Reports that are based on the
ITI VPAT 2.x® template
. These reports include information on the revised Section 508 standards from January 2017 and corrections to the ICT Final Rule from January 2018, the
Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 level AA
, and the
EN 301 549 standard
.

If you do not see a document for a particular product,
please request the conformance document
on the accessibility feedback form.
