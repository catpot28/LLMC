Student Spaces FAQs

What are Student Spaces?

Student Spaces is an
AI-powered study hub
in Adobe Acrobat that helps you understand, organize, and learn from your materials. Upload your files, ask questions, and create study tools without jumping between apps or tabs. You can access Student Spaces on Acrobat web from any mobile or desktop browser.

How does Student Spaces help me study smarter?

Student Spaces turns your class materials into interactive study tools like flashcards, quizzes, study guides, mind maps, videos, and podcasts. You can chat with an AI tutor, take notes, and keep everything for a topic together so studying feels simpler and more focused.

What can I create in Student Spaces?

You can upload up to 100 files of 600 pages or fewer and generate tools such as flashcards, practice quizzes, study guides, study packets, presentations, videos, podcasts, and mind maps, so you can learn in the way that works best for you.

Is Student Spaces free for students?

Yes. Student Spaces is free for students for a limited time with fair-use limits on standard study tools. Premium tools, like podcasts, presentations, and video summaries, are available when you create a free personal Adobe account.

Can I trust the AI responses?

When you upload documents or links, AI-generated answers and study tools include clear citations. You can click any citation to see exactly where the information came from, so you stay in control of what you learn. While we always strive for accuracy across our tools, please be sure to double-check your responses for accuracy. For more information, please learn more about our
generative AI user guidelines
.

Can I collaborate with classmates?

Absolutely. You can share a Student Space with classmates or friends and choose their access level, so you can review notes, study tools, and materials together in one shared space.
