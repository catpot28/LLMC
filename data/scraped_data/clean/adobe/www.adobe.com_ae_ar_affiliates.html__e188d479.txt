Frequently asked questions

What is the Adobe Affiliate Program?

The Adobe Affiliate Program is a referral program that allows you to monetize your website and social media channels by promoting Adobe products and earning a commission for each qualified sale.

How does it work?

Approved partners in the program can post banners and text links or create custom links to content that directs their visitors to the Adobe website. When that visitor makes a qualified purchase within a 30-day period, the publisher is eligible for a commission to the sale.

Is there a cost to join?

There is no fee required to sign up in the program.

Is there a minimum audience size to be approved as an Affiliate?

No. Adobe recognizes the value of partners with smaller audiences that are a great fit for Adobe.

How do I join the program?

Sign up
here.

Accept the terms and conditions to join the Partnerize network. Once you‘re on the network, request to add the Adobe campaign to your account.

Who is Partnerize and why do I need to join this Network?

Partnerize is a global platform and services provider Adobe has retained to manage the Affiliate program. Partnerize teams support and grow businesses through enablement, a content portal, tracking, dashboards, and payment of commissions.

Why can’t I add the Adobe campaign directly to my Partnerize account?

Currently, Adobe is reviewing applications for the program prior to approval.

Where do I find information on setting up my Affiliate account?

You will receive a welcome email from Partnerize with links to several support tools, including a video demo, Getting Started guide, and instructions for setting up links, payments, and more.

In addition to the tools in your Getting Started guide, the Partnerize portal has a
Resource Center

What products are eligible and what is the commission structure?

Adobe Creative Cloud, including Single Apps, Document Cloud, Adobe Express subscriptions and Adobe Stock are eligible.

Commission for Creative Cloud, Adobe Express, and Document Cloud Monthly plans: 85.00% of one month subscription fee Annual plan (paid monthly): 85.00% of one month subscription fee Annual plan (prepaid): 8.33% of one year subscription fee Commission for Adobe Stock

Please note that Trial/Cancelled, Invalid, and Fraud orders will receive $0 commission.
Document Cloud and Adobe Stock are available only in specified countries.
