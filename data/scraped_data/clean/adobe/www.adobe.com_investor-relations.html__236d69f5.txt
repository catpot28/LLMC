About Adobe

As one of the largest and most diversified software companies in the world, Adobe empowers everyone to create by building innovative platforms and tools that unleash creativity, productivity and personalized customer experiences. Adobe has redefined how people and businesses imagine, create and deliver content that stands out across every medium and surface. Headquartered in San Jose, California, and with a diverse global population of more than 30,000 employees, we achieved revenue of $23.77 billion in fiscal year 2025. Our stock is traded on the Nasdaq under the symbol ADBE.
