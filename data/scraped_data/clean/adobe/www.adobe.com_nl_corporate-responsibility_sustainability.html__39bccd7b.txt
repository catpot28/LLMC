Sustainability

We practice sustainability in everything we do.

Product innovations that protect the environment.

Our commitment to sustainability starts with our products, which help customers operate more efficiently while reducing their environmental impacts. Innovations like cloud-based software delivery, paperless workflows, and virtual collaboration all help to reduce physical waste and cut emissions from transportation and shipping.

Adobe Blog sustainability collection

Managing our operations with sustainability in mind.

Operational sustainability is a hallmark of Adobe, inspiring us to build LEED-certified offices, set a
100% renewable energy goal
, establish our
Science-based Targets
, and more. We want to consistently reduce our impact on the planet while responsibly enhancing our bottom line.

Building partnerships for a brighter future.
