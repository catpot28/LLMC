Trade Restrictions on Importing, Exporting, and Using Adobe Products

Adobe products, services, and technologies are subject to import, export, and other trade regulations under the laws of the United States and other national governments. You are responsible for complying with any local laws in your jurisdiction that may impact your right to import, export, or use Adobe products, services, software, or technologies. Each Adobe product has an Export Control Classification Number (ECCN) and license information, and corresponds to a Harmonized Tariff Schedule number. The table below lists Adobe products with version numbers, ECCNs, license information, and the HTSUS number.
