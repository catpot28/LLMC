Frequently asked questions

What organizations are eligible?

To be eligible, an organization must be recognized as a charitable or nonprofit organization in the country where it is registered. For example, in the United States, a nonprofit must be an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization or a public library. Public libraries must either have valid 501(c)(3) nonprofit status or be listed in the Institute of Museum and Library Services (IMLS) database.

What product offers are available?

Nonprofits can get one-year subscriptions to Adobe Acrobat Pro at a substantial discount and Adobe Express for no charge. Nonprofits can get Adobe Creative Cloud individual one-year memberships at a discount. See product offers for the cost for each product.

Access to Creative Cloud is administered through our program partner
TechSoup
.

Are these individual or team licenses?

All Adobe for Nonprofits offers available here are for individual licenses. If your organization needs more licenses or team-based access, Adobe also supports nonprofits through discounted Education Teams plans available under our VIP buying program. To learn more, visit
Adobe Buying Programs
.

Which persons affiliated with the nonprofit are eligible?

Nonprofit staff are eligible to receive Adobe for Nonprofits product offers. Nonprofit beneficiaries, members, and donors are not eligible for Adobe for Nonprofits memberships.

How can we qualify?

Individuals need to validate their nonprofit status to request each product. In the application process, you will be asked to provide the organization’s name, a valid organization email address, and a few details about your organization. Validation takes one to three business days.

Do I need to qualify for each product offer?

Yes, you must complete the validation for each product offer. Adobe is working to streamline this process in the future.

Is there a limit on the number of subscriptions my organization can request?

If your organization qualifies as an eligible entity, you can access and take advantage of the following offers:

Up to 50 Adobe Express Premium plan licenses

Up to 10 Acrobat Pro licenses

Unlimited licenses for the Adobe Creative Cloud Pro plan at a discounted rate

For Creative Cloud, check the detailed information on
TechSoup
.
