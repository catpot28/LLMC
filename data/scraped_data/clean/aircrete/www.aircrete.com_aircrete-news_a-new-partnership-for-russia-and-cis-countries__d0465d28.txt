A new partnership for Russia and CIS countries

March 31, 2020

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe and Anton Ohlert GmbH announced a cooperation agreement covering Russia and CIS countries to identify new opportunities for Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) machinery and plant solutions to produce AAC prefabricated elements and AAC blocks.

Anton Ohlert GmbH has more than 140 years of experience in trade with Russia and represents leading European machinery builders in the building industry supporting the respective customers with offices in Moscow (Russia), Kiev (Ukraine), Almaty (Kazakhstan) and Tashkent (Uzbekistan).

Russia and other CIS countries can be characterized by a significant rise in the construction industry during recent years. The quality of construction projects also considerably improved. A great role in this process is played by modern building materials often produced now with the use of the first-class European equipment. This requirement in the region has increased a great demand for the unique Aircrete Europe Flat-Cake AAC production technology in the region.

We look forward to a productive and mutually beneficial collaboration. Please reach us via
info@aircrete.com
for more information.

Anton Ohlert GmbH

Founded in 1871 in Berlin, Anton Ohlert is a representative company for equipment of the food, cosmetic, chemical, pharmaceutical and construction industries. Equipment for the food, cosmetic, chemical, pharmaceutical and construction industries.

Equipment and automatic lines of food production- confectionery, pastry, bread, cakes, chocolate, wafers, biscuits, coffee etc.

Packing equipment of all types of packaging for food, cosmetics, chemical and pharmaceutical products, machines to produce and weld plastic products, metal containers, tin cans, plastic containers, devices for varnishing and text-putting on the tin and aluminium tare.

Automatic lines to produce
AAC products
, concrete, construction materials, bricks, sand-lime brick, floor tile and machines of stone processing.
