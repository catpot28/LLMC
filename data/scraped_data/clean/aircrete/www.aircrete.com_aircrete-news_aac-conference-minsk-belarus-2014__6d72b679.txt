AAC Conference – Minsk, Belarus 2014

January 24, 2013

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe will once again be present at the International Academic Conference on Autoclaved Aerated Concrete in the Republic of Belarus. The 8th edition is titled “Experience in manufacturing and application of AAC” and is going to take place from
11th to 13th of June 2014
in
Minsk
.

The focus of the conference will be given to “Energy-saving AAC green construction” and “Application of structural reinforced AAC panels in fast and efficient construction”.

Aircrete’s innovative AAC Housing and Industrial Solutions are perfect examples of speedy and economic building. Custom-made aircrete panels are easily installed on-site either vertically or horizontally, depending on the design. Finishing, such as paint or wallpaper, can be directly applied thanks to the SUPER SMOOTH surface of all the products. Aircrete Building Solutions include load-bearing reinforced elements and allow construction of up to 5 stories high to be built solely from AAC. In general,
construction with AAC panels is 3-5 times faster than building with AAC blocks
. See real life application of panels in our
Galleries
.

Minsk AAC conference is one of the most reputed conventions in Eastern Europe as it gathers technology specialists, plant professionals and machine builders from countries like Russia, China, Netherlands and Germany.

Join us this summer in Minsk to find out about latest developments in an AAC world.
