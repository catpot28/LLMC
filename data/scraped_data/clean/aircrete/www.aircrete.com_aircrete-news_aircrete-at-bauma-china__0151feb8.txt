Aircrete at Bauma China

January 24, 2013

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe was present at the largest trade fair in the world in the Shanghai New International Expo Centre – Bauma China 2014. In just a few days, november 25 – 28, we made a lot of good new contacts which can bring us into the great Chinese market, where new opportunities await.

Just like Bauma 2013, last year in Munich, was Bauma China once again a great opportunity to present the
unique characteristics of our technologies and innovations
as well as advantages of AAC as a building material of the future. Our team, six people including two Chinese translators, represented the booth of Aircrete Europe.

At the booth we showcased some unique products made in AAC plants which use Aircrete technology such as: ultra-thin cladding/ façade panels, partition panels, load bearing Casco panels (up to 8 meters long) and blocks. Visitors had a chance to experience themselves the unmatched in the market SUPER SMOOTH surface of the Aircrete products by giving them a sample block made of Aircrete. On the booth itself we showed our technology, how AAC is made and AAC products..

We would like to
thank all the visitors for showing interest
in the Aircrete Europe technologies and services. We are looking forward to new successful cooperation and exciting project opportunities in the future with your company. See you at the next Bauma!
