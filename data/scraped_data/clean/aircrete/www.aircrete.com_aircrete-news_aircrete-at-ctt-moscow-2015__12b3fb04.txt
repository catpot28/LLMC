Aircrete at CTT Moscow 2015

May 3, 2015

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe will be participating in the CTT 2015 in Moscow, Russia. The 2015 edition will take place from
2 – 6 of June 2015
in one of the largest facilities in the world
Crocus Expo International Exhibition Centre
.

CTT – is the leading construction event in Russia, the CIS and Eastern Europe offering an extensive range of innovative and technologically advanced construction equipment, machinery and technologies.

What do Aircrete Technologies offer Russian construction market?

Aircrete specializes in building turn-key AAC factories and sees an amazing opportunity in introducing high-quality AAC panels and blocks to complement fast growing building industry in Russia and surrounding CIS markets. Major construction projects in Russia increase the need for innovative engineering and equipment. Policies introduced by Russian officials are directly pointing towards increase in housing development and aircrete material is an answer to efficient and fast building. Government’s direction of import substitution is fully supported by Aircrete’s vision of efficient sourcing as we are fully capable to source majority of the plant equipment from Russia. Our Moscow-base partner Metaltek has extensive experience in AAC business in Russia.

Aircrete manufacturing plants have the most state-of-the-art machinery as per our latest SUPER SMOOTH technology
with fewer equipment and more automation
as well as a unique flat cutting techniques (no tilting). We also specialize in upgrading existing AAC plants (such as Ytong, HEBEL, Universal or any other tilt-cake technologies) to incorporate the required machinery to switch from block production to panels and blocks as per latest European norms.

Come and see us in Moscow at
Pavilion 3 – Hall 13 – Booth 479
to check our latest product innovations. For more information see CTT Moscow official
website
.
