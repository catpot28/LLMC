Aircrete at Project Qatar 2015

March 3, 2015

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe will be participating in the Project Qatar Trade Fair, or simply
PQ 2015
, in Doha, Qatar. The 2015 edition will take place from
4 – 7 of May 2015
. Project Qatar is the International Construction Technology & Building Materials Exhibition and is regarded as being the largest event of the Middle East.

The fair will be taking place at the most modern exhibition venue Qatar National Convention Centre (QNCC).

What do Aircrete Technologies offer Qatar AAC market?

Aircrete specializes in building turn-key AAC factories and sees an enormous opportunity in introducing high-quality AAC panels and blocks to complement fast growing building industry in Qatar and surrounding markets. Aircrete manufacturing plants have the most state-of-the-art machinery as per our latest SUPER SMOOTH technology
with fewer equipment and more automation
.

We also specialize in upgrading existing AAC plants (such as Ytong, HEBEL, Hotten or any other tilt-cake technologies) to incorporate the required machinery to switch from block production to panels and blocks as per latest European norms.

Visit us in Doha at
Hall 5 – Booth G87
to see our latest product innovations. For more information see Project Qatar official
website
.
