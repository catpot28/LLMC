Aircrete Brasil Operations

October 9, 2013

PDF Download

The management of Aircrete Group is proud to announce that as of Q4 2013, an additional local subsidiary has been founded, namely “Aircrete Brasil”.

The newly established company is an
exclusive distributor
in Brazil for Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) products made using Aircrete Europe technology. The product portfolio will mainly consist of (1) SUPER SMOOTH Partition Panels, (2) Reinforced Wall Panels and (3) Modular Housing Packages for commercial and residential construction.

The effort to bring Autoclaved Aerated Concrete Panels to the Brazilian and Latin American markets is in line with the Aircrete Group mission: “
Providing greener, faster and better building technologies for the world of tomorrow
.” Aircrete Brasil provides the Latin American markets with a superior building system for high-quality Partition- and Industrial Walls.

In order to successfully introduce the products and make it possible for local contractors to enjoy benefits of AAC, the Aircrete Group offers extensive supervision of installation, training and specialized tools for the local construction teams. As an experienced European partner, Aircrete Europe will directly assist the Brazilian projects through our local subsidiary.
