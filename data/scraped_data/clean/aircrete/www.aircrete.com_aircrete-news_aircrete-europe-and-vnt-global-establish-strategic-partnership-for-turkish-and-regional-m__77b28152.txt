Aircrete Europe and VNT Global Establish Strategic Partnership for Turkish and Regional Markets

March 10, 2026

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe
, the global leader in engineering and technology solutions for the Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (
AAC
) industry, is pleased to announce a strategic cooperation with
VNT Global
. Under this partnership, VNT Global will represent Aircrete Europe in Türkiye, Azerbaijan, and selected regional markets, supporting market development and project opportunities across the region.

Through this collaboration, VNT Global will act as Aircrete Europe’s official and exclusive regional representative, supporting customers with market development, project identification, and technical coordination for AAC production facilities. The cooperation aims to strengthen Aircrete Europe’s presence in a region with significant potential for modern, sustainable construction solutions.

Türkiye represents the largest AAC market and one of the most dynamic construction markets in the region, with growing demand for efficient and environmentally responsible building materials. Autoclaved Aerated Concrete technology plays an important role in meeting these needs by enabling the production of lightweight, energy-efficient building materials suitable for modern construction standards.

“Türkiye and the surrounding region represent an important strategic market for Aircrete Europe,”

said
Ralf Beier
, CEO of Aircrete Europe
. “
With VNT Global’s strong regional expertise, network, and understanding of local market dynamics, we look forward to jointly developing opportunities and supporting the growth of the AAC industry in the region.”

VNT Global will support the development of long-term market opportunities, working closely with local clients and industry partners to introduce Aircrete Europe’s advanced AAC plant technology.

“We are honored to collaborate with Aircrete Europe and represent their technology in Türkiye and selected regional markets,
”

said
Tümay Başkale
, Managing Partner at VNT Global.

“This partnership reflects our shared vision of developing sustainable building materials and supporting the long-term growth of AAC production in the region.”

Both companies see significant potential in strengthening the adoption of AAC technology across Türkiye and neighboring markets, particularly in regions where infrastructure development and reconstruction initiatives are expected to drive demand for modern construction materials.

About
Aircrete Europe

Aircrete Europe is the leading global provider of integrated plant solutions for the Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) industry. The company supplies engineering, equipment, and technology to AAC producers worldwide, supporting efficient plant operations, high product quality, and sustainable building solutions.

About
VNT Global

VNT Global is an international business development and consulting company specializing in industrial technology representation and market development. With strong regional networks and market expertise, VNT Global supports technology providers and investors in developing industrial projects across
Türkiye, Azerbaijan, and selected regional markets
.
