Aircrete Europe at Bauma 2013

January 24, 2013

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe will once again be present at the Bauma Trade Fair in Munich, Germany. The 2013 edition takes place from 15th to 21st of April 2013. It’s the world’s largest Trade Fair for Construction Machinery, Building Material Machines, Mining Machines, Construction Vehicles and Construction Equipment.

Visit us at the C1 hall, booth number 109.
