Aircrete Europe at Bauma 2019 – Experience the world’s largest construction machinery trade fair with us

March 26, 2019

PDF Download

Being the global leader in AAC panel technology and through involvement in operations of its own plants, Aircrete Europe is uniquely positioned to act as your true technology partner and not just a machine supplier. With proprietary innovations on
(Flat-Cake)
AAC systems, technology and process optimization, we offer innovative lightweight concrete products manufacturing technology that provides a proven, fast, durable and cost-effective
building system
.

Bauma
is the world’s largest construction trade fair. The show, with more than 3,400 international companies exhibiting, provides a unique and overwhelming experience for the 600,000 visitors from more than 200 countries attending. Bauma will take place in Munich, Germany, from 8-14 April 2019. It is an international and successful business platform, not to be missed by anyone with an interest in the construction and building materials industry.

We would like to invite you to our stand to demonstrate the unique characteristics of AAC products made on Flat-Cake Technology and discuss how we can assist you with building an innovative,
turn-key AAC plant
or
optimizing your current AAC production facility
.

Aircrete Europe is located in Hall B1, Booth #423. We look very much forward to welcoming you at our booth! You can pre-book a meeting by filling in the form below, however, you are of course also welcome to just drop by.

[caldera_form id=”CF5ca4a51694d16″]

We look forward to seeing you in April!
