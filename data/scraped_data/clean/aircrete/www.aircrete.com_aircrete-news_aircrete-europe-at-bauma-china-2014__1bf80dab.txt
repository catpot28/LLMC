Aircrete Europe at Bauma China 2014

January 24, 2013

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe will be present at the Bauma Trade Fair in Shanghai, China. The 2014 edition takes place from
25th to 28th of November 2014
in the Shanghai New International Expo Centre. It’s one of the world’s largest Trade Fairs for Construction Machinery, Building Material Machines, Construction Vehicles and Equipment. Aircrete Europe has been attending BAUMA Fairs in Munich since 2004.

How does Aircrete see China’s building material market?

Aircrete Europe is a leading technology expert and equipment supplier for the international autoclaved aerated concrete industry and we will present our products and services at the fair. Chinese construction market is in need of more efficient social and industrial construction and aircrete elements answer this need. Broad portfolio of modern AAC products offers major advantages of
increased construction speed, less finishing cost
and
improved fire rating and energy efficiency
. This trend will be the strongest for AAC partition panels and AAC reinforced wall, floor and roof panels in the coming years. There is a broad market prospect and economic benefits for introducing high-quality AAC panel building solutions.

What do Aircrete Technologies offer Chinese AAC market?

Aircrete’s own and unique technology is based on a
flat-cake cutting technology
and is specifically designed to produce high-quality AAC panels and lintels along with AAC blocks. A perfect example of our innovations is Aircrete state-of-the-art cutting machine which introduces SUPER SMOOTH product surface (i.e. closed pores) which substantially reduces plastering on-site.

Aircrete also specializes in building turn-key AAC factories and upgrading existing AAC plants (such as Ytong, HEBEL or any other tilt-cake technologies) to incorporate the required machinery to switch from block production to panels and blocks as per latest European norms. Aircrete technology will introduce a greater variety of products to the Chinese market (wall panels, floor panels, lintels, SUPER SMOOTH partition panels, façade/cladding panels) which will be a next step in development of China’s AAC market.

Visit us in Shanghai at Bauma 2014 at
Hall E7 – Booth E7.460
to see our latest product innovations. For more information see
Bauma China official website
.
