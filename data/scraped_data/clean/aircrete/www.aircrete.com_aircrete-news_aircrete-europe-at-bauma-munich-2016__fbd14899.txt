Aircrete Europe at Bauma Munich 2016

September 3, 2015

PDF Download

In April 2016, Aircrete Europe will be present at the Bauma Trade Fair in Munich, Germany. This 31st edition takes place from 11th to 17th of April 2016 in the Messe München. It’s the world’s leading Trade Fair for Construction Machinery, Building Material Machines, Construction Vehicles and Equipment.

Aircrete Europe has been attending BAUMA Fairs in Munich since 2004.

Aircrete Europe will be present with a strong team that speaks seven languages to assist you with all your questions about our
AAC technology
,
AAC services
and
AAC products
.

Visit us in Munich at Bauma 2016 at Hall C1 – Booth 526 to see our latest innovations and AAC Building Solutions
. For more information about Bauma 2016 check the official
website
.
