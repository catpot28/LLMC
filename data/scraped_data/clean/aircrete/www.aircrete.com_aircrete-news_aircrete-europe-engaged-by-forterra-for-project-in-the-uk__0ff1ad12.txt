Aircrete Europe engaged by Forterra for a project in the UK

October 19, 2018

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe has been selected by Forterra Building Products, one of UK’s leading building materials groups, as its technology partner to execute a project at its existing AAC facility at Hams Hall (Birmingham, UK). The project was executed within envisaged budgets and time frames and was successfully commissioned in Q3 of 2018.

Stuart McQuillan, Head of Engineering at Forterra, commented: “For this complex assignment we were looking for a true technology partner, and not just an engineering firm or machine supplier. Through their in-house process know-how resulting from their involvement in their own plants in Latin America, combined with over 4 decades of engineering experience, Aircrete Europe was perfectly positioned to fulfil this role.”

About Forterra Building Products

Forterra Building Products is one of the leading manufacturers of masonry products in the UK, which includes autoclaved aerated concrete (aircrete or AAC) blocks, clay bricks and concrete blocks. The company Forterra owns and operates 2 AAC production facilities with a total capacity of just under 4 million m
3
which makes it the second largest manufacturer of AAC blocks in the UK. AAC blocks are manufactured under the Thermalite brand.

About Aircrete Europe

With decades of experience and sales in over 20 countries on 6 different continents, Aircrete Europe is the recognized leading solution provider for Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) panels and blocks in the global AAC market. We design and deliver the most innovative turn-key AAC plants and cutting-edge plant technology around the world. Being the long-term, exclusive project partner for our customers, we guide you through all the steps of planning, building and operating your AAC factory. We are a full technology partner, not just a machine supplier, resulting in maximum value creation for our customers. Together we build your success!
