Aircrete Europe exhibits in North America in 2018

December 4, 2017

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe will kick 2018 off by exhibiting at two leading building equipment industry events in the United States. Between 23-26 January, Aircrete Europe will participate in World of Concrete (WoC) in Las Vegas. Between 9-10 February, Aircrete Europe will also take part in ICON EXPO in Indianapolis.

We will attend both events together with our strategic alliance partner,
Besser Company
. Besser Company is a leader for quality concrete products equipment and parts for the masonry, hardscape, pipe and precast markets of the industry.

Meeting with our team at both events will give industry players the opportunity to exchange ideas on the most recent and unique building solutions in the Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) world. Being the leading turn-key AAC plant delivery organization when it comes to AAC panel applications, Aircrete Europe has proprietary innovations on AAC systems, technology and process optimization. We offer innovative lightweight concrete products manufacturing technology that provides a proven, fast, durable and cost-effective building system. Our team on board will be ready to assist you with all your questions about
Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC)
, building a
turn-key AAC plant
and of course
Aircrete Building System
.

World of Concrete 2018

World of Concrete
(WoC) is the largest annual international event dedicated to concrete and masonry professionals, featuring construction machinery, building material machines, construction vehicles and equipment. Today, World of Concrete hosts more than 1,500 exhibiting companies and 60,000 registered industry professionals.

You can visit us in the Las Vegas Convention Center at the Besser booth located in the
North Hall – N1325
.

ICON EXPO 2018

ICON EXPO
Equipment & Machinery Show is the only trade show completely dedicated to the manufactured concrete masonry and hardscape products industry. Exhibitors display state-of-the-art machinery, products and services, and the latest equipment for the industry.

You can visit us in the Indiana Convention Center at the Besser
booth 43.

We hope you will be able to join us at one of the above events in the USA this year. Please complete and send the form
here
to request a meeting with Aircrete Europe team at World of Concrete or ICON EXPO.
