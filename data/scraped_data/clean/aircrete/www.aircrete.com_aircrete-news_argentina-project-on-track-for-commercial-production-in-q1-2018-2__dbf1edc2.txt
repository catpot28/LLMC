Argentina project on track for commercial production in Q1-2018

August 29, 2017

PDF Download

Brimax, our latest turn-key project in Latin America (Rosario, Argentina) is well on track.

Over the past months, the construction of the industrial building has been finalized. Currently, the equipment is being installed and commercial preparations have been initiated.

We envisage commercial production to commence in Q1-2018.

For more information about Brimax, please visit the company`s website:
www.brimaxargentina.com.ar

The challenging process of autoclave transportation has been successfully completed earlier this year.

The construction of the 10,000m
2
industrial building has been finalized
.
