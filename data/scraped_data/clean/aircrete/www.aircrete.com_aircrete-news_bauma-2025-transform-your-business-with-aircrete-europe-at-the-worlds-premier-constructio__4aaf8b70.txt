Bauma 2025: Transform Your Business with Aircrete Europe at the World’s Premier Construction Trade Fair!

January 16, 2025

PDF Download

Experience Innovation with Aircrete Europe

Aircrete Europe
is proud to be the global leader in
Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC)
technology, specializing in the production of premium AAC panels and blocks. We design and deliver cutting-edge,
turn-key AAC plants
and advanced plant technology tailored to your business needs.

As your exclusive project partner, we provide end-to-end support—from planning and building to operating your AAC factory. With hands-on experience running our own AAC plants and a team of international experts, we truly understand the nuances of creating a successful business in this industry. Our commitment to
continuous innovation
ensures optimized production processes and superior product quality, keeping you ahead of the curve.

As part of Aircrete Europe’s commitment to innovation and excellence, we are proud to introduce
Calsitec
, our new brand specializing in cutting-edge
calcium silicate technology
. Designed to complement our expertise in AAC, Calsitec offers state-of-the-art solutions for the production of
calcium silicate units (sand-lime bricks)
. With this expansion, we provide a versatile range of technologies to meet the diverse needs of the modern construction industry, ensuring sustainability, precision, and efficiency in every project.

Discover Bauma Munich 2025

Bauma
is the
world’s largest construction trade fair
, bringing together over 3,400 international exhibitors showcasing the latest advancements in construction, building materials, and mining machinery. This year, the event is expected to attract 620,000 visitors from more than 200 countries.

Taking place in Munich, Germany, from
7 to 13 April 2025
, Bauma is the ultimate platform to explore groundbreaking solutions and connect with industry leaders. For Aircrete Europe, this marks our
7th participation
at this prestigious event—a testament to our continued commitment to innovation and excellence in construction technology.

Visit Aircrete Europe – Hall B1, Booth #247

We invite you to visit our booth and experience firsthand the unique advantages of AAC products made using our
Flat-Cake Technology
. Discover the benefits of
Super Smooth
surfaces and learn how we can help you establish a state-of-the-art, turnkey AAC plant or enhance the efficiency of your current AAC production facility.

Our team is eager to share insights, discuss your goals, and explore how we can contribute to your success.

Interested in joining us at Bauma 2025? Mark Your Calendar!

Join us at Bauma 2025 and take the next step toward transforming your AAC business. Contact us at our dedicated email for more information:
bauma2025@aircrete.com
. We look forward to seeing you in April!
