New project partner in Russia

January 3, 2015

PDF Download

Aircrete is very pleased to announce the beginning of a new strategic partnership in Russia with the company METALTEK. The joint collaboration will enable a wider range of services, local manufacturing of Aircrete equipment in Russia, as well as a direct contact on any issues with a Moscow office.

NPK “METALTEK” – a modern, dynamic engineering company, whose divisions include design, technology, manufacturing, construction and assembly works. The company has been active in the CIS market for over 15 years and engineers and manufactures process equipment and metal structures. Extensive experience of METALTEK in the Russian market in the areas of metal structures and raw materials, as well as direct project involvement with existing AAC plants in Russia ideally complements the existing potential of Aircrete and will contribute to the successful implementation of turn-key projects from now on.

The basic philosophy of the two companies are an
individual approach to every client
and
integrated implementation of projects
, whether it’s building a new plant for the production of aerated concrete or upgrade of old AAC factories (HEBEL, Ytong, Universal, etc). Together, we can offer complete turn-key AAC plants, including not only the supply and installation of equipment from Europe, but also industrial engineering, warehousing and delivery of civil infrastructure. The joint collaboration will enable a
wider range of services, local manufacturing of Aircrete equipment in Russia
, as well as a
direct contact on any issues with a Moscow office
.

Read more about METALTEK on the official website
www.metaltek.ru
.
