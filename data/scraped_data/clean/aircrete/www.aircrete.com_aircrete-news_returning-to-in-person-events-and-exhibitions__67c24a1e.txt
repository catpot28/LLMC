Aircrete exhibits at trade fairs again

September 2, 2021

PDF Download

With more than a year of COVID-19 restrictions behind us, events and exhibitions are just around the corner. Now that lockdown restrictions have eased in many regions, we will take part in exhibitions in Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan and Russia until the year-end. Experts from both our team and representatives will share their extensive know-how with existing and potential customers and explain Aircrete Europe’s latest innovations in AAC production systems and machines.

The first upcoming event is
Kazbuild 2021
, the International Building and Interiors Exhibition which
will be held between
7-9th September
at the Atakent exhibition complex at Almaty. This exhibition is the largest construction and interior exhibition in Kazakhstan since 1994. More than 60 events within the business program are held annually: seminars, workshops, round tables, B2B and B2G meetings, delegate programs, etc. Aircrete Europe will be represented by
Anton Ohlert
in
Pavillion 10, Stand #03/2.
Admission to the exhibition is possible for professional visitors only with an
e-invitation via this link
.

Kazbuild 2020 was postponed due to the risks caused by the COVID pandemic, so the statistics reflect the numbers from 2019

Two weeks later,
from
22-24th September
,
Uzbuild 2021

will be held in Tashkent,
the capital city of Uzbekistan. This exhibition is an important gathering for the Uzbeki construction industry, as it presents the best achievements and experiences of domestic and foreign manufacturers of building materials, technologies and special equipment. Aircrete Europe will be present on
Stand #S12
at the exhibition venue at Anhor Lokomotiv Park. Entrance to Uzbuild 2021 is free of charge, but
online registration
is compulsory.

Aircrete Europe participates in Uzbuild 2021 with its own stand at S12

Looking at 2021 and beyond, we expect highly automated, versatile and volume production of AAC panels will keep on driving the industry. Therefore, the demand for our recent innovative solutions for reinforcement area automation, automatic mould door manipulators, thin panel production and plant control systems will keep on being on the rise. Visitors of these exhibitions will be able to learn in direct talks how this can be achieved through modern AAC production systems from
Aircrete Europe
.
