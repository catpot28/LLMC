Showcasing AAC machinery and production technology leadership

December 8, 2020

PDF Download

Over the past few weeks, we participated in three major events around the world that prove our commitment to a global presence. Despite the challenging circumstances, we are pleased to have been present at
Bauma China
,
International Building Industrialization of Construction Exhibition Asia
and
International Concrete Conference & Exhibition (ICCX) Russia
that gave us the opportunity to drive conversations, collaborate and connections directly with frontline AAC industry leaders and decision makers.

Being a leading player in the global AAC production technology, our proprietary AAC machinery and technology solutions allow for the manufacturing of high–quality AAC panels and blocks with exceptionally smooth finishing. Success does not happen overnight. It is a result of our hard work and constant research for the latest trends on the AAC and the building materials market. These efforts get into their best-practices shape in the form of Aircrete innovations and we take the pride of being at the front line of the most innovative company in the AAC production technology industry.

Inevitably, these innovations are always the focal point in our talks during events, conferences and exhibitions. These above-mentioned recent events were not any different. That is why we wanted to share some of these innovations here again in a nutshell, so that you can virtually keep up to date from the comfort of your home or office, even if you have not been able to show up on an Aircrete booth recently.

To mention just a few of our recent innovations:

Movable Mould Door

Panel Pack Profiling System

With all these innovative solutions, we are committed to bringing the best AAC machinery and the most recent practices in your production environment and help you maximize your profit and AAC product quality.

Contact us now
to find out more about the Aircrete innovations that are just right for your business.

During
Bauma China 2020
, we showcased our best technologies and products, like the HSCF, Super Smooth blocks and panels and a 3D model of an Aircrete factory

Organized by Consulate General of the Netherlands in Shanghai, we had representatives on our booth who also attended the forums at the
International Building Industrialization of Construction Exhibition Asia

Our representative in Russia, Anton Ohlert company executives represented us successfully at our stand in
ICCX Russia
