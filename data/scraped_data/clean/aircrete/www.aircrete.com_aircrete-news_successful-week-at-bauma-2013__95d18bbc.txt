Successful week at BAUMA 2013

April 24, 2013

PDF Download

Aircrete Europe was once again present at the largest trade fair in the world – Bauma 2013. The fair took place in Munich, Germany from 15.04 to 21.04. It was a great opportunity to present the unique characteristics of our technologies and innovations as well as advantages of AAC as a building material of the future. During the week more than 1200 people from over 70 countries visited our stand resulting in many insightful conversations and new contacts in the building materials and construction industries.

To assist our international visitors our company was represented by a team of six people speaking eight languages. The immense interest in Aircrete as a building material supported our belief in the international market growth in the coming years, mainly in AAC panel applications. The outlook is especially interesting for the Asian and Middle Eastern markets.

At the stand we showcased unique products made in AAC plants which use Aircrete technology. They included: ultra-thin cladding/ façade panels (3,5 cm thickness) , partition panels, load bearing Casco panels (up to 8 meters long) as well as blocks in various shapes and sizes (also made with fly-ash as raw material). Visitors had a chance to experience themselves the unmatched in the market SUPER SMOOTH surface of the Aircrete products.

We would like to thank all the visitors for showing interest in the Aircrete Europe technologies and services. We are looking forward to new fruitful cooperation and exciting project opportunities in the future. See you at the Bauma 2016!
