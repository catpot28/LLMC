Aircrete Europe at World of Concrete 2017

novembre 3, 2016

Téléchargement PDF

In January 2017, Aircrete Europe will be present at the World of Concrete Trade Fair in Las Vegas, United States. The 2017 edition takes place from
17
th
to 20
th
January 2017
in the Las Vegas Convention Center.

Aircrete Europe will attend the fair together with
Besser Company
, a leading global supplier of quality concrete products production systems, parts and services for the masonry, hardscape, pipe and precast markets.

Founded about 40 years ago, World of Concrete is the world’s ONLY annual international event dedicated to the commercial concrete and masonry construction industries, featuring construction machinery, building material machines, construction vehicles and equipment. World of Concrete now boasts more than 1,500 exhibiting companies filling more than 700,000 square feet (65,000 sq m) of exhibit space attracting more than 60,000 registered industry professionals.

Aircrete Europe will be present with a strong team that speaks multiple languages to assist you with all your questions about our
AAC technology
,
AAC services
and
AAC products
. If you plan to attend World of Concrete we can provide you with a promotional code that will reduce your cost to attend the trade show; if you’re interested contact us at
info@aircrete.nl

Visit us in Las Vegas at World of Concrete 2017 in the Besser Booth located in the
North Hall – Booth N1325
to see our latest innovations and AAC Building Solutions. For more information about World of Concrete 2017 check the official
website
.
