The following list includes "lightning talk" speakers at our first
Economic Futures Symposium
at the McCourt School of Public Policy at Georgetown University, as well as other Symposium awardees who did not present on stage.
Many of these proposals informed the nine categories of ideas we feature in
Preparing for AI’s economic impact: exploring policy responses
.
