Outlook
About
Join Anthropic for an in-person event on the morning of 17 March in London, bringing together thought leaders, industry figures and policymakers from across the UK AI ecosystem to explore the latest developments in agentic AI and preview how agentic AI can deliver benefits across the UK's public and private sectors.
The programme will feature discussions, networking and practical demonstrations of Anthropic's AI agents powered by Claude, including real-world use cases.
We are delighted to invite you to join our global teams of technical experts and an exclusive group of delegates to showcase how responsible agentic AI can streamline operations, support decision-making, and deliver better outcomes.
