Claude on AWS
Choose how your team works with Claude on AWS
Anthropic and AWS offer three ways to access Claude billed through AWS. Whether you're building on the API, putting Claude in front of every employee, or working within strict data residency requirements, there's a path built for your workload.
Claude Platform on AWS
The full Claude Platform, with AWS authentication, billing, and commitment retirement.
Get the native Claude experience and the full suite of Claude models and capabilities on AWS, with every new feature and model available on day 0, managed by Anthropic. Authentication runs through your existing IAM policies, and usage is billed by AWS and counts against your AWS commitment. The right starting point for most teams building agents and products on Claude.
Claude on Amazon Bedrock
Use Claude models, fully managed by AWS. Build with unified governance and international data locality.
Data and inference stay inside your AWS account, with AWS as the data processor and the same governance controls you use across foundation models. Drop Claude into the AWS toolchain you already use, making it the right fit for regulated industries and organizations with strict data residency requirements.
Claude Enterprise in AWS Marketplace
Secure products for employees, everywhere your teams work. Billed through AWS Marketplace.
Chat, Claude Cowork, and Claude Code are available across your whole organization, with enterprise controls included and everything on your existing AWS invoice. The right fit for knowledge worker rollouts, engineering teams adopting Claude Code, and cross-functional teams using Cowork.
Learn more about Claude on AWS.
From pilot to production on AWS
Thousands of organizations, from early-stage startups to large enterprises, run Claude on AWS across a broad range of industries. OpenRouter, Artemis, and Armanino are a few examples of what that looks like in production:
OpenRouter runs production developer traffic through Claude Platform on AWS, getting native Claude API features the day they ship.
Artemis cut mean time to resolution by 96% and increased detection coverage by 90% using Claude on Amazon Bedrock, replacing multi-hour security investigations with evidence-cited reports in under five minutes.
Armanino cut manual writing time by 65% and reduced follow-up client clarifications by 60% using Claude on Amazon Bedrock to support audit, tax, and consulting work across one of the largest accounting firms in the United States.
