Anthropic and AWS
Secure, reliable AI on the infrastructure you trust
Claude on Amazon Bedrock brings enterprise-grade security and seamless AWS integration out of the box. Deploy AI agents confidently across your organization—from pilot to production in minutes, with built-in compliance and reliability. Scale from dozens to millions of requests with the same consistent performance and cost efficiency.
Frontier intelligence that solves real-world problems
When NBIM, Thomson Reuters, and Pfizer needed AI agents for their most critical workflows, they chose Claude on Amazon Bedrock. Superior performance on complex tasks like autonomous coding, research, and financial analysis, combined with the ability to work directly in your existing tools and environments. Build AI systems that are powerful, reliable, and safe.
