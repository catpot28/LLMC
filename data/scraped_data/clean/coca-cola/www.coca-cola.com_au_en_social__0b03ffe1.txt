People Matter

We believe that a diverse, equitable and inclusive workplace makes us stronger as a company, enables us to create a better shared future for employees and communities, empowers access to equal opportunity and builds belonging in our workplaces and in society.

We have long known that our business is only as strong as the communities we proudly serve, and in addition to our own employees, we also work to impact the lives of consumers, customers and societies around the world.

We are taking action to create a better shared future through investments in economic empowerment; diversity, equity and inclusion; and giving through The Coca‑Cola Foundation.

Coca‑Cola Foundation

We are committed to improving the quality of life of Australians through programs on water stewardship, waste management, and well-being. We provide emergency assistance during disasters and have supported various projects on environmental conservation.

Learn More

Coca‑Cola in the Community

Across the Coca‑Cola System, we believe in giving back to the communities in which we live and work. Through our Coca‑Cola in the Community program, we aim to create meaningful connections that help make a positive impact and build resilient communities. Since 2002, Coca‑Cola Europacific Partners and Coca‑Cola in Australia have contributed over $19 million to local not-for-profit organisations.
