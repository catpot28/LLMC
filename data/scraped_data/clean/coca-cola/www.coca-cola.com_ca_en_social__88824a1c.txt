People Matter

We believe that a diverse, equitable and inclusive workplace makes us stronger as a company, enables us to create a better shared future for employees and communities, empowers access to equal opportunity and builds belonging in our workplaces and in society.

In addition to our own employees, we work with hundreds of partners, thousands of suppliers and millions of farmers around the world. Our aim is to help develop thriving and resilient communities as part of a secure, long-term supply chain.

We are taking action to create a better shared future through investments in economic empowerment; diversity, equity and inclusion; and giving through The Coca‑Cola Foundation.
