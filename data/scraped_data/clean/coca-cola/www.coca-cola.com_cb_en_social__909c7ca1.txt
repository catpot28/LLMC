People are our priority

We believe that a diversity, equity and inclusion strengthen us as a Company. Our commitment enables us to pursue a better shared future for our employees and communities, fosters equal opportunities and creates a sense of belonging within our workplaces and society.

Our business is as strong as the communities we are proud to serve, and in addition to our own employees, we also work to positively influence the lives of consumers, customers and societies worldwide.

Economic Empowerment

We promote regional prosperity through education initiatives such as Growing Together, our training program for traditional channel stores and small businesses on topics related to the management of their businesses such as digitalization, customer service, finances, innovation, among others.

Implemented successfully in the Dominican Republic, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados, Belize, Guyana, Saint Lucia and Jamaica; this program has already thousands of businesses throughout the Caribbean.
