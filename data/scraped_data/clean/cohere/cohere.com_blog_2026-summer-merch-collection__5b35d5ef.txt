At Cohere, we like to celebrate key milestones with special branded merchandise that showcases our culture, spirit, and love of design. Whether it’s for a product launch, new feature, company event, or just to kick off a new season, our unique clothing and accessories give us a chance to mark the moment in a fun and lasting way.
Now, we’re extending the opportunity to own a piece of Cohere history to our customers, fans, and fashion enthusiasts everywhere. The
Cohere Merch Store
is now live!
An e-store and archive, all rolled into one
The Cohere Merch Store is actually a living archive; it maintains an ongoing record of all the merch and swag that we’ve produced over the years. Over time, browsing the archive will give you a sense of the evolution of our aesthetic (and a hint of new things to come).
If you see something you like,
grab it today
before it sells out and gets archived!
