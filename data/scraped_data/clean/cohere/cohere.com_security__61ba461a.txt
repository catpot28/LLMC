Industry-leading AI security and data protection
Keep company data protected with enterprise-grade security protocols, robust access controls, and private deployment options.
Request a demo
Visit the Trust Center
Robust protection. Seamless control.
Multi-layered protection
Securely deploy AI with the Cohere platform. We use defense in depth and layer security controls to protect your data at every stage.
Built-in data autonomy
Maintain full control over your data with customizable handling and retention settings. Opt out of model training at any time — your data stays yours.
Private deployment options
Bring our models to your infrastructure for complete data privacy and control. Deploy through a virtual private cloud (VPC) or on-premises setup.
A secure AI platform, ready to deploy
North combines built-in security, compliance, and governance with an out-of-the-box experience, so you can move fast without compromise.
Go North
Designed for safety and scalability
Private deployments
We can bring our products and models to your infrastructure through virtual private cloud (VPC) or on-premises deployments
You can customize your security configurations and controls
We won’t have access to your computing infrastructure or data
Third-party cloud deployments
Our models run on any cloud AI/ML platform, including OCI, Azure, AWS, and Google CP
You’ll benefit from third-party cloud security standards and certifications
Secure model development
We simulate adversarial attacks on our systems to test for vulnerabilities and improve defenses
We carefully track the lineage of our training data to guard against data poisoning and malicious content
We evaluate our models against internal and external benchmarks
The Cohere API
Our API platform is hosted on industry-leading cloud infrastructure and is SOC 2 Type II compliant
Our advanced threat detection systems continuously monitor for suspicious activity
We conduct annual third-party audits and penetration tests to identify and address vulnerabilities
Reporting security issues
Have you detected a potential vulnerability in our systems? Let us know and you could get a reward.
The Cohere Bug Bounty program is designed to recognize the community’s support in protecting our users’ privacy and security.
