Why Join Dataiku?

Let’s make it short: We truly care about people. We are a tech company that builds software. Our culture is even pretty geeky. But our driving force is and will always remain people, starting with ours. We care about our employees more than anything else, and we are committed to ensuring that each of them gets the most rewarding, enjoyable, and memorable work experience with us.
