Dataiku Academic Program

Dataiku Academic Program

Empowering the analytics leaders of tomorrow.

At Dataiku, we want to help people everywhere grow their data analytics and predictive modeling skills. A vital part of that is providing free access to our software as well as support resources for educators, learners, and academic researchers.

For Learners

The Dataiku Academic Program empowers academic users to succeed in the data-driven world by providing
free access to its single, coherent platform that welcomes users with a wide range of skills and expertise and covers the full lifecycle of an AI product.

Why students use Dataiku:

Learn in-demand skills with guided learning paths and template projects.

Collaborate in a secure online environment.

Showcase your new skills with certifications from the
Dataiku Academy
.

It’s fun! Both data and domain experts feel at home in Dataiku.

Dataiku offers a free one-year single-user license to individual learners, hosted on Dataiku Cloud!

Get Your Dataiku Individual Learner Instance

For Educators

Dataiku provides free licenses and learning resources for educators and faculty to facilitate modern analytics, data science, and AI education.

Why educators use Dataiku:

Regardless of profile or technical ability, Dataiku enables everyone to participate in building (and consuming) AI-driven applications.

Facilitate group work and capstone projects in a centralized, controlled, and collaborative environment.

Leverage the most cutting-edge and in-demand technologies and tools, from coding languages (Python, R) to algorithms and frameworks (scikit-learn and XGBoost), to computation infrastructure (Spark and Kubernetes), and everything in between.

Give users a leg up in the workforce by using the platform for Everyday AI
across banking, insurance, pharmaceuticals, manufacturing, retail, and more
.

Dataiku offers academic partners a year-long Dataiku license to install on your data infrastructure or Dataiku instances hosted on the Cloud for classroom exercises (up to 20 users per instance).

Apply to the Academic Program

For Academic Researchers

Dataiku is proud to partner with academic institutions and support researchers in exploring their fields of study, publishing their findings, and advancing AI/ML knowledge.

Why researchers use Dataiku:

Collaborate seamlessly on group work and research, regardless of your profile or technical ability.

Dataiku AutoML enables quick data cleaning, exploration, and experimentation, supporting rapid iteration and the creation of best-in-class models.

Showcase achievements across the industry with a dedicated Awards program and communications opportunities.

Dataiku offers academic partners a year-long Dataiku license to install on your data infrastructure to run your research.

Apply to the Academic Program
