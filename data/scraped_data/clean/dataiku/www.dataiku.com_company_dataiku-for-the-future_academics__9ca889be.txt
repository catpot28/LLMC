The Dataiku academic program
At Dataiku, we want to help people everywhere grow their data analytics and predictive modeling skills. A vital part of that is providing free access to our software as well as support resources for educators, learners, and academic researchers.

The program is closed at the moment but you can still register interest.

Learn data and AI with Dataiku
The Dataiku Academic Program empowers academic users to succeed in the data-driven world by providing free access to the platform for AI success that welcomes users with a wide range of skills and expertise and covers the full lifecycle of an AI product.
You can download Dataiku free forever or start a free trial.
Learn in-demand skills with guided learning paths and template projects.
Collaborate in a secure online environment.
Showcase your new skills with certifications from the Dataiku Academy.
Both data and domain experts feel at home in Dataiku.

Teach data and AI: Dataiku for educators
Dataiku provides free licenses and learning resources for educators and faculty to facilitate modern analytics, data science, and AI education.
Dataiku offers academic partners a year-long Dataiku license to install on your data infrastructure or Dataiku instances hosted on the cloud for classroom exercises (up to 100 users per instance).
This program is at capacity at the moment but you can still register interest for next year.
Regardless of background or technical ability, Dataiku enables everyone to participate in building AI-driven applications.
Facilitate group work and capstone projects in a centralized, controlled, and collaborative environment.
Leverage the most cutting-edge and in-demand technologies and tools, from coding languages (Python, R) to algorithms and frameworks (scikit-learn and XGBoost), to computation infrastructure (Spark and Kubernetes), and everything in between.
Give users a leg up in the workforce by using the platform for Everyday AI across banking, insurance, pharmaceuticals, manufacturing, retail, and more.
