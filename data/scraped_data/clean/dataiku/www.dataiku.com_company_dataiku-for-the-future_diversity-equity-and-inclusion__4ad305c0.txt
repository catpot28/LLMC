Social impact, diversity, equity and inclusion at Dataiku
Building AI that benefits everyone starts with diversity and inclusion in the office.
We believe that a blend of experiences and perspectives creates the best-performing teams and enables us to build software shaping the future of data & AI in a way that is fair and inclusive.
Read the Dataiku Impact Report

Employee resource groups representing the diversity of Dataiku employees
AsiprASIAN
Our employee resource group celebrating the diversity of Asian cultures at Dataiku—fostering community, building awareness, and bridging connections across the global Asian diaspora and its allies.
Blackbox
Our employee resource group championing representation and empowerment for Black Dataikers—especially in leadership—while driving anti-racism efforts, mentorship, and community outreach.
Convergence
Embracing Dataiku's cultural diversity by sharing experiences, broadening perspectives, tackling discrimination, and creating a workplace where everyone feels they belong.
DatAble
Our employee resource group for those impacted by disabilities or health conditions—physical, mental, or cognitive—coming together to share experiences, advocate, and support one another.
Empower
Elevating women's voices, building solidarity, and driving representation—supporting the professional growth and belonging of women across the organization.
Queer Dataikers
Celebrating and supporting LGBTQIA+ employees while building a strong ally base—ensuring every Dataiker feels free to be fully themselves and raising awareness about the queer community.

Actively supporting women in AI
Dataiku X Women in Data science
Through our partnership with Women in Data Science (WiDS), we combine world-class tools with a global community of support—helping ensure that women in data science have the access, influence, and sense of belonging they truly deserve.
