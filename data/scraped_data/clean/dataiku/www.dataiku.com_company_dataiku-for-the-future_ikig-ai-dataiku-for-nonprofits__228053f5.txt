Dataiku for Nonprofits: Our AI-for-Good Program

We believe AI should be accessible to everyone, including nonprofit organizations working to solve critical social, environmental, and humanitarian challenges.

Offering Dataiku Licenses for Nonprofits

Dataiku, the Platform for AI Success, provides eligible nonprofits with missions that align with the AI-for- Good Program
free access to the platform
and
pro bono support from Dataikers
to help them unlock the value of their data and scale their impact.

Are you an NGO or nonprofit and would like to join our AI-for-Good Program?

To benefit from our AI-for-Good Program, you need to meet all the following eligibility criteria:

Be registered as a charitable organization and provide a charity registration number or nonprofit tax identification number in good standing.*

Submit an application with your intended use case and potential of impact on your mission and the communities you serve.**

*Please note that, Dataiku AI-for-Good Program is not available to schools or colleges (go to our
Academic Program
page instead), mutual organizations, healthcare, political, or government agencies.

**Applications are evaluated on the strength and maturity of the proposed use case —how you leverage analytics and AI— and potential impact on your mission and the communities you serve.
