Dataiku for nonprofits: Our AI-for-good program
At Dataiku, we believe nonprofit organizations should be able to harness the full potential of AI. Through our AI-for-good program, eligible nonprofits can apply for a free Dataiku license.
Register your interest in the nonprofit program

Dataiku for nonprofit organizations: AI success for everyone
With Dataiku, nonprofit teams can turn insights into action and help grow on the ground operations within a collaborative platform designed for scale.
Improve program effectiveness with data-driven insights. Optimize operational efficiency. Better understand and engage the communities you serve.
Attract the best AI-native talent thanks to a platform that saves time and increases impact.
Showcase your innovative data and AI use cases through Dataiku channels, blog, social, and awards.
Access Dataiku through our AI-for-good program
Dataiku partners with organizations whose missions align with social, environmental, and humanitarian impact, helping them move from data to measurable outcomes faster.
Selected nonprofits can receive:
Access Dataiku, the platform for AI success, for free, hosted on cloud or on premise.
Get free support from Dataiku experts on the development of your use case.
Self-service resources to leverage Dataiku: the Dataiku academy, community, and Dataiku user network events & content.
Who can apply: Requirements to join the nonprofit program
To benefit from our AI-for-good Program, you need to meet all the eligibility criteria. Applications are evaluated on the technical and impact potential.
Be registered as a charitable organization and provide a charity registration number or nonprofit tax identification number in good standing.
Submit an application with your intended use case and potential of impact on your mission and the communities you serve.
The Dataiku AI-for-good Program is not available to schools or colleges, mutual organizations, healthcare, political, or government agencies.
