Diversity, Equity and, Inclusion (DEI)

Diversity, Equity and Inclusion

Building AI that benefits everyone starts with diversity and inclusion in the office.

We believe that a blend of experiences and perspectives creates the best performing teams and enables us to build software shaping the future of data & AI in a way which is fair and diverse, for people and the planet.

Queer Dataikers

Our employee resource group celebrating and supporting LGBTQIA+ employees while building a strong ally base—ensuring every Dataiker feels free to be fully themselves and raising awareness about the queer community.

Convergence

Our employee resource group embracing Dataiku's cultural diversity by sharing experiences, broadening perspectives, tackling discrimination, and creating a workplace where everyone feels they belong.

DatAble

Our employee resource group for those touched by disability or health conditions—physical, mental, or cognitive—coming together to share experiences, advocate, and support one another.

Empower

Our employee resource group elevating women's voices, building solidarity, and driving representation—supporting the professional growth and belonging of women across the organization.

Blackbox

Our employee resource group championing representation and empowerment for Black Dataikers—especially in leadership—while driving anti-racism efforts, mentorship, and community outreach.

AsiprASIAN

Our employee resource group celebrating the diversity of Asian cultures at Dataiku—fostering community, building awareness, and bridging connections across the global Asian diaspora and its allies.
