Convergence

What We Stand For

Convergence embraces and celebrates the diverse backgrounds of Dataikers through providing a space to welcome employees from all cultures. We share experiences, resources, and bring new perspectives to better understand our colleagues and customers from around the globe, so that we can work with another seamlessly. We raise awareness on cultural similarities and differences, tackle discrimination, and ensure that Dataiku is a company where everyone feels they belong.

Meet Some Members of Our Team

Cécile

Cécile is Senior Counsel in charge of Southern Europe. Based in Paris, she also serves as the EMEA lead for Convergence, driving initiatives that foster inclusion and collaboration across the region. Cécile has always been deeply committed to fighting inequalities and ensuring that everyone, regardless of their background, has a fair chance to succeed. She values environments where diversity is embraced and individual identities are celebrated.

Focus Areas

Embrace diversity: We celebrate cultural diversity and bring new perspectives to enhance open-mindedness and tolerance

Make our company a better place: We raise awareness on biases, stereotypes, and cultural humility to ensure that Dataiku is a fair workplace.

Help Dataikers think global: We organize workshops to help Dataikers learn about different cultures, tackle racism strategically and make positive impacts
