Empower

What We Stand For

Empower serves as a place for Dataikers to discuss the challenges that women face in business and beyond. We have set up this community to speak freely and benefit from a form of collective empathy and strength so that we can elevate women’s voices, build relationships, and enhance solidarity with advocates. This allows us to grow a sense of belonging for all women so that people can have the freedom to choose how they want to show up at Dataiku.

Meet Some Members of Our Team

Arthy

Arthy is the Senior Director of Business Transformation at Dataiku and the AMER Co-Lead for the Empower ERG. She’s been a leader at Dataiku since August 2021 and collaborates with women and allies across Dataiku to provide not just a safe space, but also opportunities for professional development, and community.

Emilie

Emilie is a Customer Success Manager and serves as the EMEA Co-Lead for the Empower ERG. With a passion for creating spaces where women can thrive, Emilie collaborates with leadership and peers to ensure the ERG’s programs align with Dataiku’s commitment to fairness and inclusion.
