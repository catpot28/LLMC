About Dataiku

The Universal AI Platform™

Our Story

Our mission is to unite your people, data, governance, and technology with The Universal AI Platform™.

At Dataiku, we believe that the power of analytics and AI should belong to everyone. When people across an organization, from business leaders to data scientists, can create, share, and build together, intelligence doesn’t just add up, it multiplies.

Since 2013, our mission has been to bring people, data, governance, and technology into one shared space with
The Universal AI Platform™
. By meeting teams where they are, whether through no-, low-, or full-code capabilities, we help them turn everyday expertise into lasting impact.

Our platform is agnostic by design, connecting seamlessly to every cloud, data platform, AI services, and legacy systems, so organizations can focus on creating today while staying ready for whatever comes tomorrow.

Thousands of companies already trust Dataiku to spark innovation, guide better decisions, and unlock new possibilities. Our story is simple: Empower people with the tools to shape the future, and extraordinary things can happen.
