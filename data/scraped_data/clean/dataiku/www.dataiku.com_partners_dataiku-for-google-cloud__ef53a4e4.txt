Dataiku + GCP:
Create & Deploy Production-Ready AI

With Dataiku and Google Cloud, everyone across the organization — not just expert data scientists and engineers — can create, deploy, and manage AI-driven data projects at scale.

GET THE DATAIKU + GCP FLYER

WATCH THE DATAIKU + GCP DEMO

Empower Your Entire Organization

With Dataiku on GCP, all users can access and process data from any storage resource, enabling a true 360-view of data.

Dataiku features like External Models, Deploy Anywhere, and the LLM Mesh allow business teams to consume, validate, deploy, monitor, and govern models in GCP VertexAI using the no-code interface.

LEARN MORE ABOUT GCP & DATAIKU

Build Transparent & Traceable AI

Want to track progress of all data initiatives in a single place, ensuring the right workflows and processes are in place to deliver Responsible AI?

With Dataiku, consolidate oversight for greater risk management and compliance with the full support for Compute Engine monitoring native to Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

LEARN MORE ABOUT GOVERNANCE WITH DATAIKU

Increase Efficiency & Time-Savings

Run compute-intensive data science workloads, predictions, and models on BigQuery. Plus, benefit from rapid deployment of pre-built images with Dataiku on Google Cloud.

Dataiku also boosts efficiency with prebuilt components, processes, and the ability to reuse existing projects. Combined with Scenarios to automate repetitive tasks, teams will have more time to focus on what matters.

SEE HOW MANDM USES GCP & DATAIKU TO SAVE TIME

Centralize Your AI Infrastructure

With Dataiku and Google Cloud, IT teams can consolidate all users onto a single platform, deprecating outdated tools and reclaiming budget for other projects. This means reducing licensing costs by trading disparate analytics tools and solutions for a single platform.

Discover the Highmark Health Success Story

Scale the Use of Generative AI

The Dataiku LLM Mesh
addresses the concerns related to cost management, compliance, and technological dependencies while providing the flexibility to use and validate the growing number of models like Google Gemini Pro.

Dataiku’s integration with Google Vertex AI allows customers, both business and data experts, to take advantage of multiple LLMs offered by GCP with built-in security guardrails, such as PII or toxicity detection, to build and deploy GenAI applications.

LEARN MORE ABOUT GENERATIVE AI WITH DATAIKU

Leverage End-to-End Integrations

From native connections to Google BigQuery, Google Cloud Storage, and Google Cloud SQL to seamlessly leveraging GCP compute and much more, Dataiku and GCP integrate across all parts of the data and AI project lifecycle.

The result? Faster time to insights and more people across the organization leveraging your existing investments in Google Cloud for even more value.
