From data chaos to AI-driven success with Dataiku, Deloitte, and Snowflake
When organizations are faced with an outdated tech stack and untapped data potential for AI, the answer lies in the partnership between Dataiku, Deloitte, and Snowflake.The alliance helps enterprises not just modernize their core technology stack, but also deploy AI at scale. Providing the unified data foundation, enable the creation and governance of advanced GenAI models and intelligent agents, ensure strategic implementation, boosting efficiency, managing risk, and enhancing data quality for unparalleled business value.

Accelerate time to value with generative AI
Deloitte and Dataiku deliver a complete generative AI strategy, from change management and workforce training processes to the tools required to execute.
The Dataiku LLM Mesh enables organizations to efficiently build enterprise-grade applications while addressing concerns related to cost management, compliance, and technological dependencies.

Accelerate data science analyses with the Deloitte Asset Store
The Asset Store combines Deloitte’s depth and breadth of industry experience with the integrated technical platforms of Dataiku and Snowflake to help organizations easily build AI and Data-as-a-Service (DaaS) into their processes and operations.
One global life sciences and biopharmaceutical company improved time-to-value with data and reduced future technical debt within the organization by leveraging the Asset Store. With Deloitte and Dataiku, they delivered a user onboarding strategy for more than 200 users across 50 global teams, providing on-demand data science engineering services to support the effort on a single platform.
