Dataiku + NVIDIA: Accelerating Enterprise-Ready AI & Analytics

Stay competitive with Generative AI by embracing accelerated computing and making AI accessible to more people across the organization — all with NVIDIA and Dataiku.

GET THE DATAIKU + NVIDIA FLYER

SEE DATAIKU + NVIDIA IN ACTION

Empower Your Entire Organization With AI

With Dataiku, all data and domain experts work together in a single environment to create and deploy analytics and AI solutions. This allows teams to do more — try more experiments, use more sophisticated algorithms, and spend more time on responsible AI best practices — resulting in trusted, higher-quality projects and outcomes.

LEARN MORE ABOUT NVIDIA & DATAIKU

Scale the Use of Generative AI in Your Organization

With Dataiku, benefit from faster access to Generative AI models optimized specifically for NVIDIA GPUs like rerank-qa-mistral-4b, deepvariant, neva22b, and many more.

Beyond NVIDIA-accelerated computing, Dataiku provides built-in capability for you to self-host open source LLM models on NVIDIA GPUs. Using the Dataiku LLM Mesh, you can access NVIDIA NIM microservices to deploy LLM models (on-prem or cloud).

DISCOVER NVIDIA'S VIEWS ON GENERATIVE AI

Go Further With Advanced Analytics

Access turnkey Dataiku solutions for common use cases across industries to accelerate the delivery of your AI use cases with pre-built projects and ready-to-use templates.

Use no-code (with powerful AutoML) and code-based data science (with full support for code notebooks, IDEs, Git, and CI/CD tools) with Dataiku.

Streamline development and deployment of production AI with NVIDIA AI Enterprise extensive frameworks, pre-trained models, and development tools.

DISCOVER OUR DATAIKU SOLUTIONS

Ensure Responsible AI an Deliver Trusted Results

Safely scale with oversight by taking advantage of Dataiku’s built-in governance plans that ensure proper review and final sign-off for significant projects before deployment to production.

NVIDIA AI Enterprise is optimized and certified to ensure reliable performance and reduce the risk associated with moving from pilot to production caused by infrastructure and architectural differences between environments.

Embrace Accelerated Computing

With the NVIDIA RAPIDS™ Accelerator for Apache Spark, Dataiku’s support of NVIDIA GPUs, seamless integration with NVIDIA RAPIDS-Spark, RAPIDS ML, MONAI, and more, Dataiku and NVIDIA accelerate all parts of the AI lifecycle:

Speed up data processing and reduce operational costs by up to 5x.

Train and deploy models faster and more efficiently.

Scale data and ML processing.

Accelerate time-to-value.
