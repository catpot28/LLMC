Delivering Enterprise-Ready AI-Powered Energy Solutions

Dataiku and SLB drive enterprise-wide transformation leveraging Dataiku’s collaborative, end-to-end AI platform and the unmatched energy expertise SLB has built as innovators in the market for more than 100 years. Through comprehensive training, certification, incentive programs, and proprietary solutions, Dataiku and SLB are building an AI culture.

See SLB and Dataiku in Action

Leverage SLB Data Science — Powered by Dataiku

The Data Science Solution is a package of AI and analytics solutions for energy workflows that enables geoscientists and engineers to build, manage, and deploy AI to solve their day-to-day challenges. Deployable with the Delfi™ digital platform or the Data and AI platform from SLB.

Search, discover, visualize — add a new dimension to your analytics with visual data discovery.

The place to fuse data together — connect to most sources of data — generic or specific to the energy industry.

Wrangle, cleanse, reconstruct, splice — makes raw data ready for use in analytics, by integrating structured and unstructured data.

Visual data analysis — use an extended library of statistical tools to visually display your data — explore and understand the trends.

AI made simple for you —
With our vast array of AI tools, machine learning libraries, coding environments, and resources, it’s simple to piece together the perfect digital solution.

Read more about SLB Data Science — Powered By Dataiku

Unlock SLB Accelerator Pack for Dataiku

Connectors & Plugins — ready-to-use connectors and utilities to enable data science workflows.

Workflow templates — ready-to-use project templates to experiment and see platform capabilities in action.

Tailored solutions — proven digital solutions that can be rapidly customized for quick value realization.

Facies Classification From Well Logs With SLB

Accelerate the accurate classification of lithofacies, using machine learning powered by your well logs and speed up optimal well sites identification.

Quickly integrate and prepare data from well logs including gamma ray, resistivity, photoelectric factor, and more.

Easily train a machine learning model with full explainability and accuracy testing.

Expose outputs on specific wells using a Dataiku App.

Leverage the model for your wireline logs, integrate in more advanced analytics projects, and more.

Leverage Dataiku’s full connectivity potential to easily embed within your current and target IT set-ups, from on-premises to cloud.
