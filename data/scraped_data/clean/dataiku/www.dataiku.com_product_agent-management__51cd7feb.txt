Dataiku Agent Management
Know if your AI agents are delivering value. Dataiku Agent Management gives you a single control tower to track, evaluate, and govern agents across any platform so you can track business performance, detect drift, and ensure every agent is doing its job.

Full visibility and control over every AI agent
Track real business performance, not just uptime
Go beyond technical monitoring with KPIs that reflect business outcomes. Define evaluations for each agent and track performance against targets in one place.
Detect failures before they impact the business
Automatically identify when agents miss performance targets or drift from expected behavior, so issues are caught early, before they create downstream risk.
Manage agents across platforms from one control tower
Connect to agents running anywhere from n8n to Bedrock to Salesforce to Dataiku and more and manage them centrally without migration or rework.

One unified view of your entire agent ecosystem
Enterprises have agents spread across multiple platforms with no clear view of how they’re performing. Agent Management brings everything together into a single, platform-agnostic control tower, giving teams full visibility into performance, behavior, and governance.

From monitoring to accountability
Traditional monitoring tools tell you whether agents are running. Agent Management tells you whether they’re working.
By combining KPI tracking, behavioral analysis, and governance workflows, teams can move from reactive monitoring to proactive performance management with clear ownership and accountability for every agent.

Frequently asked questions
What is Agent Management?
A single control tower where central IT can manage and govern all agents across the enterprise, regardless of where they were built or where they run.
How does Agent Management connect to third-party frameworks?
Agent Management connects to third-party frameworks through native APIs and log streams. Users simply select the framework, authenticate, and the system automatically scans and inventories the agents. The depth of integration depends on what each platform exposes, with deeper integrations actively being developed.
