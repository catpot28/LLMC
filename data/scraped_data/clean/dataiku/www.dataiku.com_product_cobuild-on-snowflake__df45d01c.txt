Frequently asked questions
What is Cobuild on Snowflake Cortex?
Cobuild on Snowflake Cortex is Dataiku’s AI building agent. Powered by Snowflake Cortex and orchestrated visually in Dataiku, Cobuild transforms business intent into governed AI workflows, agents, applications, and operational AI systems running on Snowflake.
How does Cobuild work with Snowflake Cortex?
Dataiku Cobuild uses Snowflake Cortex to securely access leading foundation models inside the Snowflake AI Data Cloud. Dataiku Cobuild then translates business intent into structured, visual workflows for analytics, machine learning, AI agents, and applications.
How is Cobuild different from AI coding assistants?
Unlike coding copilots that generate opaque scripts, Cobuild creates fully inspectable visual workflows with governance, reviewability, and operational controls built in before deployment. Teams can collaboratively validate, refine, and operationalize what gets generated.
Can teams modify or refine what Cobuild generates?
Yes. Every workflow generated by Cobuild remains editable, inspectable, and reviewable. Teams can iteratively refine workflows conversationally while maintaining governance and operational oversight.
Does data leave Snowflake?
Cobuild on Snowflake is designed to operate close to enterprise data inside Snowflake environments, helping organizations maintain governance, security, and operational control.
How can I access Cobuild on Snowflake?
Cobuild on Snowflake is initially available for joint Dataiku and Snowflake customers using Snowflake Cortex. To learn more about availability and access, please reach out to your Dataiku or Snowflake account team.
