Where business needs become production AI
Turn ideas into fully built AI projects instantly
Describe what you want in plain language, and Cobuild generates an end-to-end AI project: data pipelines, machine learning models, agents, and webapps, automatically applying company and platform best practices.
Trust every step with visual, governed workflows
Cobuild doesn’t just generate code, it creates a visual Dataiku Flow. Every transformation is visible, every step is reviewable and editable with full governance built in. Nothing goes to production without validation and approval.
Scale AI creation beyond technical teams
Empower business experts, from supply chain analysts to operations managers, to build AI solutions. Cobuild expands who can create with data while freeing data scientists to focus on high-impact, complex work.

Trusted enterprise AI without the complexity
Cobuild, a conversational interface built into Dataiku, translates business intent into structured project logic, then leverages Dataiku’s execution engine to handle the technical complexity, deterministically and reliably.
The result: faster time-to-value, consistent architecture, portable projects, and AI building accessible to all enterprise employees.

Get governed AI projects, not just generated code
Unlike coding assistants that generate scripts, Cobuild produces a fully inspectable visual project. Users can iterate conversationally, refine steps, and validate outputs before anything runs.
It’s not about replacing expertise. It’s about embedding it into every build, with governance, transparency, and repeatability at the core.

Frequently asked questions
What is Dataiku Cobuild?
"AI to build trusted AI in the enterprise" - a conversational interface built into Dataiku that lets data and domain experts generate complete AI projects (pipelines, models, agents, webapps) from natural language descriptions, within a governed, business-contextualized environment.
Can you iterate with Dataiku Cobuild, or is it just doing a one shot prompt?
Dataiku Cobuild provides the ability to edit and amend the AI projects being built. More broadly, Dataiku Cobuild is fully conversational, with the capacity to interact and amend.
Will Dataiku Cobuild replace data scientists?
No, Dataiku Cobuild will not replace data scientists. Data scientists retain full code flexibility and control over their workflows, while Cobuild helps offload the backlog of requests from business users who do not require custom data science work but are currently taking up valuable data science time.
When will Dataiku Cobuild be available?
Dataiku Cobuild will launch in June 2026.
