Enterprise data governance platform by Dataiku
Extend and operationalize your existing data governance strategies by empowering people to correct, centralize, and share data across the AI project lifecycle.
Request a demo
Start trial

Centralize & share curated datasets
Leverage a centralized data catalog where trusted data is ready and available for use across the organization.
Control access with fine-grained permissions
Set permissions at the user, connection, project, compute, and global levels to ensure proper data access authorization.
Address data quality on the spot
Track, verify, and fix data quality to deliver powerful and trusted insights.

Conduct root cause analysis with data lineage
Go beyond simply identifying data quality issues to troubleshooting and fixing root causes with Dataiku. Data lineage is accessible from many locations within Dataiku — from the prepare recipe to the data catalog and more — allowing users to easily trace lineage between columns across all projects.

Connect to enterprise data governance tools
Dataiku integrates with your larger data governance software and tech stack so that you can benefit from Dataiku’s unified analytics and AI platform capabilities — from data preparation to model deployment — without disrupting your current strategy.
