Dataiku Expert-to-Agent (E2A)
Dataiku E2A (Expert-to-Agent) is the engine for translating SME expertise into governed AI agents grounded in enterprise data, structured reasoning, and human oversight — so teams can run reliable, decision-ready AI across real business workflows.

Ground agents in enterprise context
Ensure agents work from the same trusted sources your business relies on by connecting them to governed data, documents, and business systems.
Operationalize SME reasoning at scale
Achieve repeatable, explainable, and aligned outcomes across workflows by guiding agents with defined reasoning pathways — not just prompts.
Keep humans in control
Maintain oversight, accountability, and control without slowing execution by embedding review, validation, and human intervention directly into agent workflows.

Build trusted agents shaped by SMEs
Build AI agents that combine expert reasoning with predictable behavior using visual, rule-based logic and human-in-the-loop controls.
Structured Visual Agents let SMEs and technical teams compose deterministic agent systems using visual logic, branching, reflection, memory, and agent handovers.
Maintain strict control over complex workflows with human approvals and enforceable tool usage.

Optimize decision-making across the agent lifecycle
Define, test, and monitor agent behavior with built-in review and evaluation capabilities.
See how agents operate in practice — what tools they call, how decisions are made, and the outcomes they produce — with full auditability and accountability by default.
Use Agent Review to validate behavior against repeatable scenarios and SME-defined standards, and Agent Evaluation to track performance over time to ensure consistent, reliable outcomes as agents evolve.
From testing to production, performance visibility and control are embedded across the entire agent lifecycle.

Frequently asked questions
What is Dataiku E2A?
Dataiku E2A (Expert-to-Agent) is the engine that turns domain expertise into governed, production-ready agents that operate on real enterprise data and systems.
When will Dataiku E2A be available?
Dataiku E2A is available now in Dataiku 14.4+.
Is Dataiku E2A available in the cloud?
Yes. Dataiku E2A is available for cloud deployments. Ask your Dataiku team about the Cloud Fast-Pass pilot program, a managed cloud environment that provides access to the latest agentic AI capabilities and functionality.
