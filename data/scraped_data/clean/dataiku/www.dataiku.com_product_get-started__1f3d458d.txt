Frequently asked questions
What does the Dataiku free trial include?
The Dataiku free trial provides a fully functional, managed instance of Dataiku Cloud. It includes access to all of Dataiku's features, from data preparation and analysis to machine learning model building and deployment. You can work with your own data, invite teammates to collaborate, and experience the full power of the platform.
How long does the free trial last?
The free trial lasts for 14 days. After that you can request an extension by reaching the support team.
Do I need a credit card to start the trial?
No, you do not need a credit card to sign up for the Dataiku free trial.
How quickly can I get up and running?
You can get started with Dataiku in just a few minutes. After signing up, your cloud instance is provisioned automatically, and you can immediately begin exploring the platform and working with your data.
Is the trial cloud-based or can it be installed locally?
The free trial is a cloud-based offering, providing a fully managed Dataiku instance. You can download the Free edition for an on-premise experience. If you are interested in an on-premises or hybrid installation, [you can contact Dataiku's sales team](https://www.dataiku.com/home/contact-us/) to discuss your requirements.
How is data secured during the free trial?
Dataiku takes data security very seriously. During the trial, your data is hosted on a secure, managed cloud infrastructure. Dataiku complies with industry-standard security practices to ensure data integrity and confidentiality. [Read the cloud security addendum here.](https://downloads.dataiku.com/publicdocs/Dataiku_Cloud_Security_Addendum.pdf)
Which Dataiku features are available in the free trial?
All of Dataiku's features are available in the free trial except for Govern and advanced LLM Mesh. This includes the full suite of capabilities for data preparation, data visualization, AutoML, advanced analytics, MLOps, collaboration, and governance.
What are the limits of the trial (users, data, compute, features)?
While the trial provides access to all of Dataiku's features, there are some limitations on usage. The trial is designed to give you a comprehensive experience of the platform's capabilities for evaluation purposes. The free trial gives access to a space with 2 users, the API node with 1 API service, 4CPUs ,and 32GiB of elastic compute.
Can I collaborate with teammates in the trial environment?
Yes, you can invite your teammates to collaborate with you in the trial environment. Dataiku is designed for team-based data science, and the trial allows you to experience these collaboration features firsthand.
What onboarding resources are included in the free trial?
The free trial includes a comprehensive set of onboarding resources to help you get started. This includes access to the Dataiku Academy for self-paced training, the Dataiku Community for peer support and knowledge sharing, and a series of guided tutorials and projects within the platform itself.
What happens when the free trial ends?
When your free trial ends, your cloud instance will be deactivated. You will no longer have access to the platform or your projects.
