Dataiku centralizes everything for streamlined deployment and monitoring, ensuring optimal performance and governance across your entire AI ecosystem.

Maximize Computing Power Through GPUs & In-Database Execution

Leverage your existing infrastructure with Dataiku’s flexible computation architecture. Scale seamlessly across SQL databases, Spark, Kubernetes, and more, all while optimizing performance through intelligent workload distribution.

Dataiku adapts to your computing needs while maximizing resource efficiency — from training models with NVIDIA GPUs to running traditional analytics.

Deploy to Many, Monitor From One

Streamline model deployment and monitoring across your entire AI infrastructure through Dataiku’s flexible deployment architecture. Push models to your preferred infrastructure, including AzureML, Databricks, Snowflake, SageMaker, and VertexAl.

Dataiku’s unified monitoring dashboard gives you comprehensive visibility across all deployments, ensuring consistent oversight and performance tracking.
