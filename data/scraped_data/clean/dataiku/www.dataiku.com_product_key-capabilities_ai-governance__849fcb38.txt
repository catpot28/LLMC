Centralize Oversight With Dataiku Govern

Enforce consistent workflows and processes for AI governance all in one place, from “traditional” machine learning to generative AI projects.

Dataiku Govern brings:

Structured sign-off and approval requirements, including the ability to block deployment without proper compliance.

Registries for a centralized way to see all AI models and project bundles in one place — including large language models (LLMs) for generative AI oversight.

Project value and risk qualification matrixes for a standard way to compare initiatives and determine oversight requirements.

Specific frameworks for accelerating regulatory readiness (e.g., with the EU AI Act).

Dive Deeper on Dataiku Govern Features & Functionality

Bake Transparency Into Data Pipelines & Projects

Dataiku, The Universal AI Platform™, was designed from the ground up with an eye for AI governance.

Each Dataiku project has a visual flow that clearly represents the pipeline of data transformations and movement from start to finish — down to column lineage. Plus, every design decision and step is captured and displayed so current or future team members can clearly follow and explain the sequence of project logic, reducing onboarding times and supporting Responsible AI principles.

Watch the 12-Minute Dataiku End-to-End Demo

Build Trust With Robust Explainability Tools

Both technical and non-technical users can better understand the outcomes of machine learning models (including LLMs) in Dataiku with tools for white-box, explainable AI.

For example, Dataiku provides interactive reports for feature importance, partial dependence plots, subpopulation analysis, what-if analysis, and individual row-level prediction explanations.
When it comes to generative AI, Dataiku offers standard LLM evaluation metrics including LLM-as-a-judge techniques as well as statistical techniques for more trust and confidence in non-deterministic systems.

Dataiku gives organizations the tools they need to manage AI models throughout their lifecycle, including regulatory compliance, security, and ethical considerations.

Maintain Consistent Project & Model Records

Dataiku automatically generates comprehensive, customizable, and shareable documentation for models as well as data and AI project flows:

AI Explain supports on-the-fly descriptions of Dataiku flows or individual flow zones.

Flow Document Generator produces more robust documentation of all objects within data or AI projects.

Model Document Generator produces one document detailing what the model does, how the model was built (algorithms, features, processing, etc.), how the model was tuned, and model performance.

Reduce the Risk of Negative AI Outcomes

From models treating certain populations unfairly to data or model drift and now risks around sensitive information or malicious acts with generative AI, Dataiku has the tools you need to scale AI with ease.

Get robust tools to facilitate bias detection and drift analysis, plus built-in usage controls for data security when it comes to generative AI applications — all with Dataiku, The Universal AI Platform™.

Organize Data Governance for AI

At its core, Dataiku helps data owners and data governance teams improve, organize, and share their data at scale across the organization.
With its data catalog, fine-grained access permissions, data quality views, and more, Dataiku supports the operationalization of your data governance efforts across the entire AI lifecycle.

For broader data governance needs, Dataiku supports metadata management and popular enterprise cataloging tools for an even more comprehensive data governance strategy.
