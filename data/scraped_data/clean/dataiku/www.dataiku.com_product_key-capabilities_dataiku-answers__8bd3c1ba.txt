Dataiku Answers

Dataiku Answers is a packaged, scalable web application that empowers teams to deliver high-quality, conversational AI use cases across the enterprise in a fraction of the time.

WATCH A DEMO OF DATAIKU ANSWERS

Contact Us

Get Answers You Can Trust

Avoid hallucinations and maximize confidence in accurate answers by applying retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) techniques to ensure end users receive the most relevant, precise, and trustworthy information possible.

With Dataiku Answers, the LLM can source its answers from approved information in your knowledge bank and provide citations and extracted quotes for complete transparency and explainability.

RAG IN DATAIKU IN 3 MINUTES

Ask Your Datasets Anything

To get instant insights from structured data, just ask! Dataiku Answers will retrieve the right information from approved datasets, analyze it according to your request, and return the answer in natural language. For maximum transparency and auditability, review source citations and the generated SQL code used behind the scenes.

With seamless access to up-to-date, curated datasets, Dataiku Answers opens up a new path for business users to interact with trusted data sources — all within a secure, controlled environment.

Upload Your Docs & Images for On-the-Fly Analysis

Unlock the full potential of multimodal AI by uploading documents or images for analysis. Dataiku Answers enables you to perform custom tasks with your data, from summarizing text to extracting insights from images.

Whether you’re working with PDFs or visual data, the chatbot returns precise, task-specific responses, providing you with the ultimate multi-purpose productivity tool within your own secure environment.

Ensure High-Quality Responses

Take advantage of Dataiku LLM evaluation metrics, model comparisons, and human feedback to guarantee accuracy and relevance in every response:

Monitor conversations and collect user feedback via the built-in feedback mechanism, to continuously improve the underlying AI system.

Builders have complete control over data preparation and the full AI system.

All chats with Dataiku Answers are fully auditable, providing transparency and accountability for both quality assurance and AI governance purposes.

Set Up in Days,
Not Months

In a world where generative AI-powered chat use cases become the new normal, Dataiku Answers’ scalable framework helps teams address the increased demand for conversational AI apps.

Even large data science teams often lack application development resources — Dataiku Answers helps bridge that gap with a prebuilt, configurable user interface that speeds up the end-to-end delivery process.

LEARN MORE ABOUT BUILDING CHATBOTS AT SCALE

Make It Your Own

Dataiku Answers is fully customizable so you can adapt it to suit the needs of your organization’s specific use cases, and even provide a personalized experience for individual user profiles.

Designers can provide a variety of sample prompts to inspire end users and configure nearly every setting: language, retrieval method, look and feel, filters and source metadata, and more.

DIVE INTO THE DOCUMENTATION

Safeguard & Future Proof Your Chat Apps

Because Dataiku Answers is powered by the Dataiku LLM Mesh, connect to your choice of approved LLM, vector store/database, and knowledge bank in just a few clicks, and take advantage of caching capabilities for cost-effective and consistent answers to repeated questions.

Safety guardrails and content moderation enforced by the LLM Mesh mean you can also screen queries and responses for personally identifiable information (PII), toxic content, forbidden terms, or prompt injection attempts and take the appropriate action.

Learn More About The Dataiku LLM Mesh

With Dataiku, teams can move beyond the lab and safely deliver generative AI applications at enterprise scale. Dataiku offers a secure LLM gateway, no-code to full-code development tools, and AI-powered assistants to help everyone do more with generative AI.
