Dataiku Govern

Dataiku Govern

Make your AI governance aspirations a reality with Dataiku Govern, a single place for tracking and managing data and AI initiatives across the organization.

Contact US

SEE DATAIKU GOVERN IN ACTION

Oversee Projects & Reduce Shadow AI

Dataiku Govern centralizes work from across your organization so that you can see all data and AI projects in one single place. This allows for tracking progress and gives the ability to consistently document relevant information about data and AI project progress, scope, stakeholders, and more.

Leverage the Kanban view for a global look at project status, or link and view multiple projects together based on shared business goals.

Learn More About the Risk of Shadow AI

Accelerate Regulatory Compliance for Your Full AI Portfolio

Get a head start on EU AI Act compliance, or any number of emerging AI regulations, with Dataiku Govern.

The Dataiku Solution for EU AI Act Readiness, for example, accelerates readiness across all AI projects, enabling regulatory safety and more trusted deployments across teams.

Discover the Dataiku Solution for EU AI Act Readiness

Ensure Audit Readiness

Dataiku Govern provides a robust audit timeline for a view of who has done what, when, and on which projects. This makes it easy to answer questions like “Six months ago, what was the status of my system? Who approved the deployment?” — especially useful in case of an audit by regulatory authorities.

Explore Bundles,
ML Models, & LLM-Powered Agents

Want to know more than just high-level project information? Need to dig deeper into a specific type of project? Dataiku Govern acts as a one-stop shop with specific registries towards different assets. Ease monitoring and improve reusability for enhanced performance of your AI portfolio.

Learn more about Dataiku registries

Apply Consistent Qualification of Data and AI Projects

Assess AI project value and risk with consistent qualification aligned to your AI principles and regulatory requirements. Dataiku Govern allows you to choose when to apply control and when it’s appropriate to allow autonomy.

See how to map project risk and value

Dataiku gives organizations the tools they need to manage AI models throughout their lifecycle, including regulatory compliance, security, and ethical considerations.

IDC ProductScape — Worldwide Generative AI Governance Platforms 2025

Turn Processes Into Governance Workflows

Leverage customizable yet consistent templates with clear governance steps and gates for each step of a data or AI project.

Assign stakeholders, capture notes, and attach relevant documentation to each stage of a workflow to ensure the process is documented and tracked, from design to delivery.

Reduce Risk With Signoff Rules

Prevent the deployment of models and bundles into production without proper sign offs and approvals. Dataiku Govern blocks deployment to a Dataiku automation or API node without model review by designated stakeholders.

Dataiku Govern can also notify requesters and other users of mission-critical governance topics, including when signoff is completed, to ensure efficiency.

See how signoff rules work
