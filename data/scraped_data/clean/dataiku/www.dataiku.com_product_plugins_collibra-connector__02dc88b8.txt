Collibra Connector

Collibra Connector

The Collibra Plugin provides the ability to load Dataiku asset metadata into your Collibra data catalog.

Share

Connector Information

Version

2.3.0

Author

Dataiku & FTI Digital Science

Released

2021-01

Last updated

2021-01

Collibra
is a data governance and cataloging platform which empowers enterprises to find meaning in their data and improve the quality of business decisions.

Collibra Plugin provides the ability to load Dataiku asset metadata into your Collibra data catalog.

If you are an existing Dataiku customer, please reach out to your customer success manager for access to the plugin. Not a current Dataiku customer? Get started with a
free trial
, or
contact us
to connect with an account executive.

The plugin supports the following Dataiku assets:

Projects

Datasets

Recipes

Models

Managed Folders

Connections

Requirements

The installation setup for this connector requires:

Running instance of Dataiku DSS 8.0 or later, with Public REST API access (
Discover, Enterprise or Business license
)

Running instance of
Collibra

Technical requirements to install the connector:

Network connections to Dataiku and Collibra instances
