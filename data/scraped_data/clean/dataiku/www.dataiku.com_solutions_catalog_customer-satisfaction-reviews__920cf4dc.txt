Analyze Customer Reviews & Draw Actionable Insights

The Dataiku Solution for Customer Satisfaction Reviews provides a powerful start to customer voice listening with flexibility to adjust and refine strategies for all types of products and services.

WATCH A DATAIKU DEMO

DOWNLOAD THE SOLUTION FLYER

Easily Ingest Historical Review Data

Input data from different sources and aggregate customer reviews. Automatically detect text language and perform automatic spell checks with ready-to-use Text Preparation recipes.

Combine Traditional NLP Models and GenAI to Generate Insights

Be it to summarize reviews, extract topics, or qualify sentiment, leverage the provided NLP and GenAI approaches and quickly adjust to needs. Take a decisive step towards structuring your understanding of customer feedback, and build replicable approaches across lines of products and services.

CHECK OUT THE CUSTOMER SATISFACTION REVIEW DASHBOARD

Analyze Various Product and Service Reviews Over Time

Explore reviews through a series of interactive and predefined dashboards. Filter by product or service ID, or product category to analyze product ratings over time and by country.

SEE TOPIC AND SENTIMENT OVERVIEWS IN DATAIKU

Learn How Customers Describe Your Products and Services

Explore reviews on specific product categories or SKUs. Select a product and get a list of the most popular keywords customers use to describe them.

How do I analyze past reviews to understand what customers think about my products and services?

How can I study past reviews to evaluate their purchasing experience?
