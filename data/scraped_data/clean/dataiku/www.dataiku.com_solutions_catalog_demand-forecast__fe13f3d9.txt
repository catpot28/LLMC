Forecast Demand Reliably for More Informed Decisions

The Dataiku Solution for Demand Forecasting is the gateway to scaling a machine learning- and AI-based approach to trend prediction with a central environment for smarter and more holistic decisions across teams.

DOWNLOAD THE SOLUTION FLYER

Forecast Demand of Product Categories
& SKUs

Predict patterns at the individual product level using historical data sets and personalized parameters (discounts, holidays, marketing events, etc.) Use Time Series models to fully leverage your data history to identify seasonality and long-term sales trends.

As a single, centralized platform, Dataiku allows for scale across locations, markets, and products. Whether short, medium, or long-term demand forecasts, everyone has access to the right insights for better business decisions.

SEE THE DEMAND FORECAST EXPLORER IN ACTION

Compare Your Demand Forecast Model to Other Baseline Models

The Dataiku Solution for Demand Forecasting includes an interactive dashboard for evaluating, interpreting, and monitoring forecast models. Leverage Dataiku’s time series forecasting recipe with a wide array of models (e.g. Prophet, DeepAR, AutoARIMA) and select the best-performing one with integrated evaluation and hyperparameter optimization.”

EXPLORE THE DASHBOARD

Perform Seasonal Clustering of Products
& SKUs

Analyzing short-term fluctuations in customer demand at different times of the year is critical to accurate demand forecasts. The Dataiku Solution for Demand Forecasting provides a ready-to-use, interactive product seasonality dashboard.

SEE THIS SOLUTION IN ACTION

Monitor Past Sales & Project Future Sales

Use the pre-built sales monitoring dashboard included in the Dataiku Solution for Demand Forecasting to keep an eye on sales globally. Quickly identify the most popular items or identify trends by store at a glance.

From there, build the demand forecast scenario of your choice. Leverage the interactive web application to understand future sales of selected products.

Move from analytics to decision making through an interactive dashboard to view your aggregated forecast intervals (daily, weekly, monthly). Define your forecast settings through a user-friendly Project Setup page: determine your forecast specifications (e.g. granularity, time horizon, historical window) and get started.

Answer Key Strategic Questions

The Dataiku Solution for Demand Forecasting helps answer a broad range of questions like:

What products will be the best sellers in three months (sales forecasting)?

How can I optimize inventory levels across seasonal fluctuations?

To which areas should we ship products, and in what quantities?

How should I adjust my product purchasing strategy and inventory levels for more effective supply chain management?

Which marketing channels can help boost product purchases?

Which discounts and special offers resonate with online visitors?
