Manufacturing Process Control: Validate With Precision

The Dataiku Solution for Manufacturing Process Control embeds statistical process control (SPC) directly into your data workflow, eliminating the need to move trial run data between disparate tools, and accelerating your path to validated production settings.

WATCH A DATAIKU DEMO

Upload Trial Run Data & Configure Quality Standards

Upload data collected during trial runs to validate new parameter settings. The solution integrates with Dataiku’s Parameter Analyzer application to automatically import saved studies and pre-fill configuration settings. Define your specification limits and targets to establish the foundation for Statistical Process Control (SPC) validation.

See the Solution in Action

Generate Market-Standard SPC Control Charts

Automatically create industry-standard SPC control charts to monitor process stability and identify special cause variations. The solution produces X-bar, R, S, and individual measurement charts. Customize your analysis by defining dataset parameters, study variables, and subgroup sizes to enable immediate identification of out-of-control conditions.

See SPC Control Charts Dashboard

Calculate Process Capability Analysis Metrics

Assess process capability with automatic calculations of key indices including Cp, Cpk, Cpm, and Cpkm. The solution generates histograms that visualize data distribution against specification limits. These metrics provide quantifiable proof that optimized parameters will deliver consistent quality at scale, supporting confident transitions from trial to full production.

Preview Process Capability Dashboard

Customize Analysis With Low-Code Manufacturing Analytics

The solution works out of the box and is fully customizable through low-code capabilities. Easily adapt control limit calculation formulas to match your team’s specific SPC methodologies. Customize dashboards to align with your process needs and reporting requirements as your manufacturing environment evolves.

Explore the Visual Flow

Transform Validation Into Continuous Improvement

This solution transforms the SPC validation step into a seamless part of your process. Dataiku embeds process capability analysis and SPC control charts directly within your data workflow alongside parameter optimization and quality prediction. Execute the full spectrum of Industry 4.0 use cases with a single, governed platform.
