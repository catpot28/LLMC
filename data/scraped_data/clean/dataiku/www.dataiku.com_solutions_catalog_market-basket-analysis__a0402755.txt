Personalize & Boost Sales With Market Basket Analysis

The Dataiku Solution for Market Basket Analysis builds robust foundations for improved recommendation by creating a scalable system to calculate association rules between products.

WATCH A DATAIKU DEMO

DOWNLOAD THE SOLUTION FLYER

Monitor Past Transactions at a Glance

The Dataiku Solution for Market Basket Analysis makes it easy to explore sales history at a glance. Keep tabs on the number of transactions, items purchased, and items per transaction on daily, weekly, and monthly cadences.

Simply upload your data and leverage the transaction monitoring dashboard that comes ready to use with this Dataiku Solution.

EXPLORE THE DASHBOARD

Analyze Purchasing Patterns

Use a pre-built, interactive web application to explore and analyze frequent items. This includes identifying both the most frequent products sold as well as popular combinations of products. Plus, for each customer, find the best business rules to trigger repeat purchase and cross-sell scenarios.

Identify Product Associations

The Dataiku Solution for Market Basket Analysis comes with a pre-built “rules browser” web application. This enables you to identify product associations, which you can then use across campaigns to promote relevant products.

GET YOUR HANDS ON THE DATAIKU SOLUTION

Personalize the Shopping Experience & Better Engage With Customers

Armed with this Dataiku Solution, people across business lines can personalize the customer experience both online and in store.

For example, the brand loyalty team can use the data to run email campaigns. Dataiku makes it easy to make data-driven decisions around which products to feature.

Another example is the supply chain director, who might use Dataiku to discover product A is strongly associated with product B. From there, (s)he can make recommendations to merchandising teams for product placement in store displays featuring products A and B.
