Effectively Enforce Model Risk Management

The Dataiku Solution for Model Risk Management (MRM) improves time to market for critical banking and financial models. It removes friction across teams and systems by reducing iterations — all without compromising on process integrity and rigor.

WATCH A DATAIKU DEMO

Accelerate MRM
With Centralization

Refactoring models, performing multiple manual tasks, and switching between systems often leads to unwanted cost, friction, and market opportunity misses.

Centralize your model risk management framework in Dataiku Govern to reduce context switching between systems, blockers, and delays through better collaboration.

DISCOVER DATAIKU GOVERNANCE FEATURES

Increase Collaboration With a Unified Workflow

Feedback loops and iteration processes in MRM are time-consuming. Align all stakeholders in a single unified workflow with Dataiku, which allows for clean tracking and handling of common observations to save time.

From data scientists, financial engineers, and credit modelers to model risk teams and auditors — everyone has access to the same data, in real time, and can easily review model implementation and model outputs.

See Dataiku AI Collaboration in Action

Guarantee Validation
& Sign-Off

Managing stakeholder approvals for critical models as well as ensuring full traceability of tests and decision making is a critical requirement for effective MRM in banks and financial instutitutions.

Dataiku is a one-stop shop for building and deploying all financial models and AI projects. It makes it easy to customize approval workflows to collect sign offs from key stakeholders at each step of the building and validation processes before deploying.

WATCH THE GOVERNANCE DEMO

Easily Document Changes

Thanks to a unified system, all changes and process histories are captured and searchable in one place. This single auditable log reflects all decision making throughout the MRM process, simplifying ex-post audits, with all historical model versions stored and easily retrievable.
