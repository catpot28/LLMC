Predict Real Estate Pricing With
Machine Learning

The Dataiku Solution for Real Estate Pricing offers real estate professionals and connected teams to take a strong start towards simplified modeling thanks to a flexible template using machine learning (ML) fueled by historical sales and other feature data.

DOWNLOAD THE SOLUTION FLYER

Understand Your Properties in Their Market

Get insights quickly with easy and clear information integrated into well-defined flow zones. Create custom dashboards and share specific insights on different real estate portfolios.

GO TO THE DATAIKU FLOW

Generate Property Price Predictions

Generate ML-powered real estate purchase prices for a chosen scope of properties. Enter property features and geographic proximity to relevant areas, then get estimates and insights quickly and easily.

CHECK OUT THE REAL ESTATE PRICING MODEL

Process Disparate Data

Adjust the pricing engine to different regions, input types, and asset types. Enrich the processing further by integrating large amounts of data, a critical challenge in most geographic information systems (GIS) projects.

Analyze & Understand Price Modeling

Gain full transparency and ease of understanding of model features and input data sources. Easily comprehend model design and performance, and get comprehensive breakdowns of price estimates with detailed visual and technical insights.
