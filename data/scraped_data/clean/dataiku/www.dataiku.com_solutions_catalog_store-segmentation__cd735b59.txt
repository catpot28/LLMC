Optimize Retail Strategies With
Store Segmentation

Develop sales and marketing strategies that deliver on shoppers’ needs at the local level. The Dataiku Solution for Store Segmentation uses AI to clusters stores based on similar characteristics for maximum sales impact.

WATCH A DATAIKU DEMO

Optimize Distribution Strategies With Segmentation

Group stores with similar characteristics using demographic and sales data to better localize and personalize store planning, product allocation, promotion strategies, and assortment execution.

Compute multiple segmentation strategies to see which one delivers most value, and adjust to each market needs.

Explore a Sales Per Category Segmentation Example

Maximize Sales With Differentiation

The evolving landscape requires an increased focus on the consumer. With this solution, retailers can differentiate their product assortment and offer the right items in the right areas by:

1. Detecting unique cluster results with natural language descriptions.

2. Diving into key metrics and characteristics that impact their trade area and product categories.

3. Identifying differentiating factors based on consumer preferences.

Enable Data-Driven Decisions With Advanced Visualizations

Easily visualize and consume segmentation results to learn what makes up your store clusters:

Leverage interactive dashboards exploring clusters and their unique characteristics.

Deep dive into data using filters to adjust by store cluster, store, and product category.

Boost Customer Satisfaction With Localization

Understand localized demand, improving customer loyalty and satisfaction, and increasing sales performance.

With demographic segmentation, get insights about the surrounding population to tailor marketing efforts and more to the local shopper.

Answer Key Strategic Questions

The Dataiku Solution for Store Segmentation helps answer a broad range of questions like:

In which store should we distribute which products to meet customer preferences?

How can I define distinct yet scalable marketing strategies for groups of stores?

How should I take into account demographic specificities and other elements in my store management activities?
