Optimizing Insurance With AI and Dataiku

Top insurance organizations use Dataiku — the world’s leading data science, machine learning, and AI platform — to augment processes across departments and business lines, including for customer retention, acquisition, improved customer service, optimized claims forecasting and processing, regulatory reporting automation, underwriting, fraud detection, and much more. The result? Less risk, more revenue, decreased costs, and (most importantly) satisfied customers.
