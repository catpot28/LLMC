Improve & Accelerate Complex Processes With AI

Put the power of data in the hands of the many with these Dataiku Solutions, all while ensuring AI efforts are collaborative, transparent, and explainable — a proven formula for speeding time to market of new therapeutics and optimizing patient outcomes.

Clinical Site Intelligence

Easily uncover similar and/or competitive studies as well as clinical trial sites leveraged, run site reviews, identify selections in novel studies, and get access to sponsor overviews to guide data-driven decision making.

Ensure Quality Manufacturing and Resilient Supply Chain Operations

Pharma 4.0 principles promise a digitally connected ecosystem to optimize the production, timely availability and accessibility of medicines, therapeutics, and devices on the market. Dataiku Solutions for Manufacturing and Supply provide an accelerated framework to improve these essential operations.

Predictive Maintenance

Quickly turn vast volumes of maintenance history into optimized maintenance plans. This solution allows reliability engineers and maintenance teams to move from time-based preventive approaches to data-adjusted ones.
