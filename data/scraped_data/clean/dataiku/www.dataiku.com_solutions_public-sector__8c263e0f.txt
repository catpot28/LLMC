Advancing the Public Sector With AI and Dataiku Solutions

Public sector organizations and governments across the globe use Dataiku — the platform for Everyday AI — to tackle a wide range of use cases, from agriculture to defense. No other platform provides the unique ability for data experts (like data scientists) to work hand-in-hand with subject matter experts for impactful AI solutions.
