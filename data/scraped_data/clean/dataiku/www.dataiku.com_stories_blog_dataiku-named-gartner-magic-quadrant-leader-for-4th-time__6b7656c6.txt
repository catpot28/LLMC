Dataiku Named a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for 4th Consecutive Year

Jun 2, 2025

2 min read

/

Catie Grasso

Table of Contents

Today, we’re proud to announce that we’ve been named a Leader for the fourth year in the 2025 Gartner™ Magic Quadrant™ for Data Science and Machine Learning Platforms. Dataiku was recognized for “Completeness of Vision” and “Ability to Execute.”

Dataiku Is a Leader for the 4th Time & The Universal AI Platform™

We believe Dataiku continues to lead with both foresight and execution. Our architecture was designed to meet enterprise needs
before
the market demanded it — offering a scalable, secure, and interoperable platform that lets businesses create, connect, and control their AI with confidence.

"During a time when the only constant in the AI market is change, we’ve focused not just on keeping up with the market, but in shaping it — building an enduring foundation that gives our enterprise customers governed, trusted control of AI’s raw power. We believe our position reflects the depth and breadth of our platform, the strength and vision of our global team, and the trust our customers place in our ability to help them turn AI into a differentiated business advantage."

Florian Douetteau, co-founder and CEO of Dataiku

Analytics, Models, & Agents for Maximum Flexibility

Dataiku gives enterprises the freedom to innovate without friction. We integrate with your tech stack to de-risk the adoption of new technologies, such as LLMs, while optimizing costs and managing risks associated with your legacy systems.

Unlike platforms that tie innovation to proprietary stacks, Dataiku is deeply integrated yet entirely vendor-agnostic. Organizations can connect to any infrastructure, model provider, or AI service — giving you full control of your strategy as technology continues to evolve.

A core part of that innovation engine is the
Dataiku LLM Mesh
— our secure, scalable, and agnostic architecture for generative and agentic AI. Rather than simply embedding LLMs, Dataiku built a comprehensive framework that allows teams to manage, route, and govern LLMs at scale.

The LLM Mesh serves as a unified gateway to thousands of models — from top-tier providers to open-source and self-hosted options. And with
Dataiku LLM Guard Services
, teams can monitor usage, enforce budget limits, evaluate performance, and ensure responsible model and agent behavior over time.

The Only Platform for the Future of Enterprise AI

We believe that what sets Dataiku apart isn’t just the tech — it’s our people-first approach to AI. Built for everyone from data scientists to business users, the platform makes AI accessible, collaborative, and easy to govern.

As AI evolves, enterprises need more than point solutions. They need flexibility, control, and the confidence to scale responsibly. That’s what Dataiku delivers: a single platform to power what’s now and build what’s next.
