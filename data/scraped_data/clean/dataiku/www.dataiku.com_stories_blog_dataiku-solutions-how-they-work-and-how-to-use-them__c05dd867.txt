Dataiku Solutions: How They Work and How to Use Them

Dec 18, 2024

3 min read

/

Lauren Anderson

Most of the time, business teams have a good idea of what they want to achieve when implementing AI use cases. However, when it comes to getting started they often face roadblocks. These projects often require specialized skills and a strong understanding of industry best practices.

Without this expertise, data initiatives can stall or require extensive resources and time. This is where
Dataiku Solutions
can help! Designed to accelerate the deployment of AI and empower a wider range of users, these pre-packaged use cases help diverse teams leverage AI at scale, whether they are data scientists looking to speed up projects, data leaders looking to get more ROI out of data initiatives, or business teams that are struggling to achieve what they want when it comes to AI.

By packaging AI use cases in a flexible, easy-to-deploy format, Dataiku Solutions make AI accessible to more organizations. This article explores what Dataiku Solutions are, how they work, and why they’re essential to fostering a truly inclusive AI journey in any organization.

What Are Dataiku Solutions?

Dataiku Solutions are ready-to-use projects that address specific business needs or industry use cases, such as customer segmentation, financial forecasting, or maintenance planning. Each solution is designed to speed up the deployment of these use cases by providing end-to-end workflows, data pipelines, predictive models or large language model (LLM) calls, and visualizations tailored for actual business decision-making. These solutions not only allow teams to save time but also provide best practices for implementation, ensuring that even complex data initiatives can be executed with ease.

[Dataiku Solutions] allowed us to accelerate topics that simply could not fit into our busy roadmap. The implementation only took a few hours, and we are now ready to launch a marketing campaign powered by the Market Basket Analysis solution.

-
Ben Powis, Head of Data Science at MandM

Dataiku Solutions are fully customizable, meaning users can adapt them to fit their own data, add or remove features, and tailor them to meet unique business requirements. By packaging advanced analytics capabilities into a ready-to-use format, Dataiku Solutions empower teams to tackle data challenges without needing to build everything from scratch, helping both beginners and seasoned analysts focus on insights instead of infrastructure.

How Do Dataiku Solutions Work?

Dataiku Solutions provide a structured framework that guides users through the entire process of data preparation, modeling, and business decision. Here’s an overview of the components and workflow:

Data Pipeline
: Each solution includes a data pipeline that is pre-configured to prepare, clean, and transform raw data into a format suitable for analysis. The pipeline leverages Dataiku’s robust recipes to handle diverse data requirements. Projects contain sample data that can then be fully customized with your teams' data.

Predictive and Generative Models
: Dataiku Solutions come with configurable machine learning (ML) models or integration of LLMs that align with specific business objectives and can be adjusted based on your business data.

Dashboards and Reporting
: Each solution typically includes pre-built dashboards, reporting templates and, in some cases, interactive applications, making it easy to share results with stakeholders. Visualizations, summaries, and customizable reports allow teams to communicate insights effectively, fostering data-driven decision-making across the organization.

How to Use Dataiku Solutions

Using Dataiku Solutions is straightforward, and they are designed to accommodate users with varying levels of data expertise. Here’s a step-by-step guide to get started:

Select a Solution
: Browse Dataiku’s Solution catalog, which categorizes solutions by industry and business function. Choose a solution that aligns with your objectives and review its requirements to ensure it matches your data sources and analytics goals.

Easily select the Dataiku Solution for your use case.

2. Install and Configure
: Regardless of the solution you choose, it can be installed directly into your Dataiku environment with just a few clicks. You can easily get in touch with your admin for any package or plugin addition to your instance. Once installed, you’ll have access to a project, complete with pre-built data pipelines, models, and dashboards. Start by exploring to see what adjustments would be needed.

3. Data Preparation and Exploration
: Import your data into the solution and follow the guidance provided in the documentation. Dataiku’s interactive tools allow you to explore and clean data, and you can make adjustments to the pre-configured pipeline if necessary to better suit your data.

4. Model Training and Tuning
: Use the solution’s pre-built ML models or modify them to better fit your use case. In the case of GenAI embedded solutions, adjust the approach to your choice of LLM and refine prompts. Dataiku offers various options for model tuning and validation, allowing users to refine models based on their data.

5. Deploy and Monitor
: Once satisfied with the project performance, deploy it to a production environment. Dataiku’s monitoring capabilities allow you to track the project's quality over time, ensuring it continues to deliver reliable results. Additionally, you can configure alerts or retraining schedules to keep the model up-to-date as data changes.

6. Share Insights
: Use the solution’s dashboards and reporting features to share findings with stakeholders. Dataiku makes it easy to create visually engaging reports, which can be customized and shared directly within the platform or exported as needed.

Why Choose Dataiku Solutions?

Dataiku Solutions are more than just templates. They’re accelerators that help organizations overcome common data challenges with minimal setup. By providing pre-configured workflows and models, Dataiku Solutions reduce the time and resources needed to achieve data-driven results. For data experts, solutions offer a foundation that can be expanded and customized, while for business users, they deliver accessible, actionable insights that drive decision-making.

Dataiku Solutions empower teams to leverage data efficiently, whether they’re predicting trends, optimizing processes, or personalizing customer experiences. With Dataiku, your organization can tackle data challenges with confidence, transforming insights into tangible business value.
