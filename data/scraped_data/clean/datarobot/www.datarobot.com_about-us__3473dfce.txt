About DataRobot

Headquartered in Boston with a global team, we’re on a mission to make business better with AI.

Since our founding in 2012, DataRobot has pioneered some of the most important applied technological advancements in AI and machine learning, from inventing AutoML and Automated Time Series to trailblazing MLOps and generative AI.

More than 1,000 organizations worldwide — including BCG, Boston Children’s Hospital, FordDirect, Old Mutual, Razorpay, and the U.S. Army — rely on DataRobot for AI that makes sense for their business, today and for the future.

Leadership Team

Debanjan Saha

Chief Executive Officer

Debanjan Saha

Chief Executive Officer

As the Chief Executive Officer at DataRobot, Debanjan Saha works to make businesses better with AI. Debanjan is a visionary technologist and the co-author of over 50 patent applications with twenty years of product, engineering, and operational leadership experience (Google, Amazon Web Services, and IBM). He is also a Fellow of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) and a Distinguished Member of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM).

Rae Naidoo

Senior Vice President, Strategy and Operations

Rae Naidoo

Senior Vice President, Strategy and Operations

Rae Naidoo serves as the Senior Vice President of Strategy and Operations at DataRobot. Rae leads the corporate strategy and development, revenue and business operations, and enterprise applications. Prior to DataRobot, Rae worked as a consultant at McKinsey where he led AI and digital transformation strategies and at AI startups.

Jeffrey Wilmot

Chief Revenue Officer

Jeffrey Wilmot

Chief Revenue Officer

Jeffrey Wilmot, Chief Revenue Officer at DataRobot, leads the global charge to accelerate enterprise AI adoption. A 25-year veteran across Public, VC, and PE-backed sectors, Jeff specializes in architecting high-performance teams built for hyper-scale. Prior to DataRobot, he served as CRO at Tufin, delivering consistent double-digit growth, and held leadership roles at BeyondTrust, PTC, and two successfully exited startups. His career is defined by a proven ability to operationalize and accelerate global growth across North America, Europe, and Asia.

Brian Brown

Chief Legal Officer and Chief Financial Officer

Brian Brown

Chief Legal Officer and Chief Financial Officer

As the Chief Legal Officer and acting Chief Financial Officer at DataRobot, Brian Brown is responsible for all financial and legal affairs, including financial planning, accounting, investor relations, corporate governance and compliance, and global legal matters. Before DataRobot, Brian held roles as Associate General Counsel and Head of Corporate Legal (DoorDash) and Executive Vice President and General Counsel (Ellie Mae). Brian also has extensive experience as a corporate attorney at several international law firms.

Venky Veeraraghavan

Chief Product Officer

Venky Veeraraghavan

Chief Product Officer

As Chief Product Officer at DataRobot, Venky Veeraraghavan drives the definition and delivery of the DataRobot Enterprise AI Suite. Venky has twenty-five years of experience focusing on big data and AI as a product leader and technical consultant at top technology companies (Microsoft) and early-stage startups (Trilogy).

Amit Purohit

Senior Vice President, Engineering

Amit Purohit

Senior Vice President, Engineering

Amit Purohit is the Senior Vice President of Engineering at DataRobot. Amit is a seasoned engineering leader in the enterprise, cloud, data, and AI industries who has spent his career driving growth and impact in some of the most successful technology companies in the world (AWS, Meta).

Lisa Aguilar

VP of Marketing, DataRobot

Lisa Aguilar

VP of Marketing, DataRobot

Lisa Aguilar leads marketing at DataRobot, where her focus is simple: helping enterprises get real, measurable ROI from AI. With a background spanning Alteryx and ThoughtSpot, she has grown pipeline, launched major campaigns, and repositioned brands at the forefront of the data and analytics market. Her expertise lies in making enterprise AI less abstract and more actionable, for both the market and the business.
