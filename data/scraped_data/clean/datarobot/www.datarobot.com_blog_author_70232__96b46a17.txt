Author Prachi Hetamsara

Principal Product Manager for Access and Security

headshot

Prachi Hetamsaria is the Principal Product Manager for Access and Security at DataRobot, bringing deep technical expertise from her Master’s in Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE). She is passionate about empowering users to build AI models that are as secure as they are innovative, ensuring that every breakthrough is backed by a standard of uncompromising safety and trust.
