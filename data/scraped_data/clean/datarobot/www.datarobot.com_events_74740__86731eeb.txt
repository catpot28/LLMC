Big Data & AI Toronto

Big Data & AI Toronto

This event has ended

Big Data and AI Toronto is going virtual for its 5th edition! Canada’s #1 data and analytics event will be delivered to you exclusively online. Join the Canadian data community on September 29-30, 2020 online from the comfort of your home. Throughout 2 days, the event will be hosting thousands of data heroes, 200+ speakers, and showcasing 100+ leading solutions providers through a virtual exhibition.

Speaking slot(s)

Tuesday, Sep 29, 2pm-3pm

How to develop and monitor best in class Time Series models, adaptive to today's changing times with DataRobot

Bobby Van Zetta

Learn how we at DataRobot develop best in class Time Series models, resilient to today's changing times. The models we build at DataRobot are used by a large number of the top companies from almost every industry. It is not enough to just build these adaptive Time Series models but also monitor how the data and accuracy is drifting in real-time, which we will also cover during the session. Bring increased value to your business and reduce risk by building trusted machine learning models.
