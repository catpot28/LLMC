Big Data London Virtual 2020

Big Data London Virtual 2020

This event has ended

Big Data LDN (London) is the UK’s largest free to attend data & analytics conference and exhibition, hosting leading data and analytics experts, ready to arm you with the tools to deliver your most effective data-driven strategy.
In 2020 Big Data LDN will be delivered entirely online. The event will still take place on 23rd/24th September however visitors, speakers and exhibitors will all be joining us remotely until it is safe enough to gather in large numbers in-person again.

Our online platform will still give delegates the opportunity to network with their peers and discuss their business requirements with leading technology vendors & consultants. Delegates can watch live demos, video chat and hear from expert speakers in our comprehensive conference programme.

Register free for Big Data LDN’s 2020 Virtual Event and learn how to build a bright data-driven future for your business.
