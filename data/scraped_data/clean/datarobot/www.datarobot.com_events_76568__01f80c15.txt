Analytics & Data Science Finland Virtual 2021

Analytics & Data Science Finland Virtual 2021

This event has ended

The demand for analytics and data science is booming. Despite the crisis, it is not slowing down and will remain strong for the upcoming decades due to industry's need to leverage on this growth for better results. While focusing on business outcomes and future growth, the support of C-levels is crucial for creating a data-driven culture in an organization and implementing it as a core strategy. Join us in 2021, as we continue to bring Analytics and Data Science growth to you in virtual realm. This time we shift the focus towards effective and efficient execution of your AI and data strategies, digging deeper into building data and analytics capabilities. Discover Finish industry benchmarks on best execution strategies, connect with peers to explore approaches to data governing and evaluate your own progress in developing a data-driven culture. Showcase your solutions and build fruitful long-lasting connections with key industry executives.
