Middle East Banking AI & Analytics Virtual 2021

Middle East Banking AI & Analytics Virtual 2021

This event has ended

SUMMIT AT A GLANCE: Banking industry currently is going through rapid turbulence caused by the global pandemic and we have no option but to look at all possible levers to push their revenue and cut down costs as much as possible. The 3rd Middle East Banking AI & Analytics Summit in GCC which brings to together 250+ senior level executives & decision-makers from the Banking & Financial Services sector to move one step forward towards adapting futuristic technologies like Artificial Intelligence, RPA, Machine Learning, Big Data & Analytics. In the age to intelligence, the worlds of AI and Analytics are converging and as organizations in the region adopting an “AI-first” approach, the shared responsibilities for CIO’s, CISO’s, CRO’s and CXO’s dealing with customer experience platforms has never been more mission-critical in providing the perfect banking solutions to its consumers. To meet these challenges, Middle East Banking AI & Analytics Summit creates the conversations and networking opportunities to address today’s concerns in adopting futuristic technologies, learn from emerging best practices and you’ll dissect through real case studies, presentations, panel discussions, and face to face meetings with technology experts. This is an invaluable 2-day opportunity to expand understandings of AI & Analytics-driven culture which would facilitate attendees to gear up for the adoption of contextual banking and delve into the use of AI and advanced analytics to not just stay competitive but to improve operational efficiency and strategize. Come and find out what other top institutions from the sector are doing to follow and develop real, implementable systems.
