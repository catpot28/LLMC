Snowflake VHOL APAC 2021

Snowflake VHOL APAC 2021

Virtual - online

This event has ended

Data science is helping organisations around the world solve the most challenging analytical problems.

Snowflake’s Data Cloud accelerates data science with DataRobot Automated Machine Learning, bringing AI and data science within reach for every company. Join this technical-expert-led lab to get firsthand experience preparing data in Snowflake and building and training a customer churn model with DataRobot. No prior experience is necessary.

YOU’LL LEARN HOW TO:

Prepare data and conduct feature engineering
Build and train models
Build a broadly applicable customer churn model
Deploy, monitor, and manage models
Write back data to Snowflake
Visualise and analyse the resulting data in Snowflake
Speakers

Vijay Rajan – Data Scientist, DataRobot

Mike Adkins – Partner Sales Engineer, Snowflake
