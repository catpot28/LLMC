AIX Summit Livestream: Architecting Enterprise-Ready Agentic AI

Featuring NVIDIA

A live-streamed event built for real-world execution featuring speakers from NVIDIA and more.

Beyond the hype. Beyond the pilot. This is how you infuse agents into your enterprise.

This summit is your direct path to enterprise-grade agentic AI. For CIOs, we’re walking you through the framework you need to confidently cross the gap of experiments to a full agent workforce. For developers, it’s a non-hyped look at the steps you’ll need to overcome to deploy agents that work for your organization.

Get the real-world lessons behind agents that actually scale. No fluff. No filler. Just what it takes.

What you’ll experience:

CIO playbook
: Get a real-world framework from CIOs putting agents into production

Expert panel
: Hear trends and execution strategies from top tech and AI firms

Hard-won developer insights
: The tools, patterns, and pitfalls to avoid for real-world agent success

Platform unveiling & demos
: A front-row seat to NVIDIA and DataRobot’s new agent workforce platform
