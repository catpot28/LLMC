Events

Fewer surprises. Better forecasts. Less firefighting.

Tuesday, 9 December

|

SAP, Level 20, 140 Creek St, Brisbane City

Practical plays from real customers across demand, labour, maintenance, and cash flow.

Forecasting is hard—and it matters. In this customer story session, DataRobot shares what actually works in the enterprise so you can save time, boost confidence, and drive tangible business results.

In 60 minutes, you’ll learn how you can leverage AI to:

Right-size inventory to free trapped working capital

Proactively schedule labour and cut last-minute hires

Replace parts just-in-time, not just in case

Forecast cash with confidence to reduce borrowing costs

Who should attend
:

Finance, Supply Chain, Operations, Asset/Engineering, and Data leaders looking to improve forecast accuracy, adoption, and impact.

Event details

Title: Why forecasting is hard—and how to improve it by a little bit with some help from AI

Date & time: Tuesday 9 December, 8:30am to 10:30am

Location: SAP Office @ Level 20, 140 Creek St, Brisbane

Format: 30 mins Breakfast & Registration + 60 mins Presentation + 30 mins Networking

Speakers

Fergus Dobbins

Regional Director, APAC, DataRobot

Eu Jin Lok

Customer Facing Data Scientist at DataRobot

Kevin Jones

Account Executive, DataRobot

Agenda

Why forecasting is hard (common challenges) – 10 min

Customer stories from the field – 35 min

Demand: Free up working capital

Labour: Own your schedule, reduce agency spend

Maintenance: Replace parts just-in-time

Finance: Lower borrowing costs with confidence

Q&A – 10 min

A reusable framework for your next use case – 10 min
