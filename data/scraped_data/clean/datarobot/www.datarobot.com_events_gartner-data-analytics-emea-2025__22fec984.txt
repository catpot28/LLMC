Gartner Data & Analytics EMEA 2025

May 12-14
ExCel London | London, UK

Meet with DataRobot

Infuse Agentic AI into your Business

DataRobot helps organizations develop, deliver, and govern AI solutions that drive business impact while minimizing risk. The DataRobot Enterprise AI Suite streamlines deployment with AI apps and agents, enabling faster, smarter, and more effective AI use cases. By selecting the right tools, optimizing infrastructure, and learning from real-world examples, businesses can accelerate their AI initiatives and scale efficiently.

Let DataRobot and NVIDIA guide your organization to success with agentic AI. Join us at Gartner Data & Analytics EMEA 2025 to explore the AI strategies of companies like BMW, DHL, and VidaCaixa, and learn how your team can confidently infuse agentic, predictive, and generative AI into your business to maximize results and minimize complexity.

NVIDIA Agentic AI in Action with Real-World Use Cases at BMW, DHL and VidaCaixa

As organizations race to harness agentic AI, the challenge is not just prototyping but picking the right use cases and confidently deploying them. Discover how DataRobot and NVIDIA are helping organizations like BMW, DHL and VidaCaixa on their journey and learn the key considerations for developing, deploying, and governing agentic AI:

– Picking the right tools and techniques: Knowing what frameworks and models to use and when.

– Streamlining AI Infrastructure: How to efficiently manage and scale your AI infrastructure.

– Real-world use cases: Examples demonstrating agentic AI in action.
