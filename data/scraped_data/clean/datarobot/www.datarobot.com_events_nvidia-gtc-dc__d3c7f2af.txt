NVIDIA GTC DC

October 27-29

|

Walter E. Washington Convention Center | Washington, D.C.

We’ve entered a new era of AI, one where AI agents can be assembled into a powerful workforce that automates, predicts, and optimizes across your entire business.

At NVIDIA GTC, we’re excited to showcase our industry-leading
Agent Workforce Platform
, co-engineered with NVIDIA. This powerful collaboration offers an enterprise-ready solution, integrating the DataRobot platform with NVIDIA’s Enterprise AI Factory to simplify how you build, operate, and govern AI agents in a secure environment.

Visit our booth to experience the future of AI:

See a live demo
of our agent workforce platform to discover how to build, operate, and govern sophisticated AI agents with NVIDIA.

Talk to our AI experts
to solve your unique business challenges and learn how to drive real results with an agent workforce.

Explore mission-critical use cases
for industries like the Federal government and see how AI agents can deliver on your objectives.

Book your personalized meeting

Ready for a deeper dive? Schedule a one-on-one meeting with our AI experts at GTC for a more personalized experience. We’ll set aside dedicated time to discuss your specific challenges and tailor a demo directly to your organization’s unique goals. Spaces are limited, so reserve your spot today!

Meet with DataRobot

Join us at the public sector networking reception

Unwind after a busy day at GTC and connect with others bringing AI to the public sector. On Tuesday from 7:30 – 10:00 pm, join DataRobot and Carahsoft for a relaxed evening of conversation, good food, and great company. Swap ideas, share experiences, and see how your peers are putting AI to work across the public sector.
