AUGUST 16-18, 2022

Discover DataRobot at Ai4 2022

Over three days, Ai4 2022 brings together business leaders and data practitioners to facilitate the responsible adoption of artificial intelligence and machine learning technology. DataRobot is excited to be a part of an incredible lineup of speakers at Ai4 as we talk about Preserving AI Investments during Budgets Cuts and Managing Governance and Risk at Global Scale with Modern MLOps. Want to learn more? Schedule a 1:1 meeting with our onsite team to chat live and in person at Ai4.

Schedule Now

Agenda

Wednesday, August 17, 2022
11:40 AM PT – 12:00 PM PT
Financial Track

Managing Governance and Risk at Global Scale with Modern MLOps

Mitigating risk is essential to the success of every financial services organization. In an increasing regulatory environment, every AI model for risk management must be built on best practices for model documentation and provide ongoing monitoring. Learn how organizations are building modern MLOps centers of excellence, automating model compliance documentation, seeing deployment reports, and setting performance guardrails while simultaneously automating real-time performance monitoring for service health, data drift, and accuracy over time.

Ted Kwalter DataRobot

Ted Kwartler

VP of Trusted AI, DataRobot

Thursday, August 18, 2022
9:30 AM PT – 9:50 AM PT
General Session

How to Preserve AI Investments During Budget Cuts

We’re seeing the warning signs. We’ve gone from business execs clamoring for AI to a creeping doubt about data science impact. With the impending recession, you can be sure that business leaders will be turning a magnifying glass on data science teams. But, this isn’t true for all. A small subset of organizations are exceeding their business executive expectations. What are they doing that’s different? Building on 10 years of AI success with global customers, we’ll share the vital steps data science teams have taken to be seen as profit driving centers.

Lisa Aguilar

Global Head of Field CTO Team
