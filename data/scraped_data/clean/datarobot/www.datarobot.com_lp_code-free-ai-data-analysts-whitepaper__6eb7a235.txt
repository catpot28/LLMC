Code-Free AI for Your Data Analysts

In the past, uncovering deep insights from machine learning and artificial intelligence (AI) required extensive programming skills and an intimate knowledge of math – experience in short supply in most enterprises. A new class of augmented analytics tools is making it possible for data analysts and and other business professionals to take machine learning and AI initiatives into their own hands.

Download this white paper to learn from analytics industry expert Jen Underwood how your organization can develop world-class predictive modeling capabilities with automated machine learning, without having to hire and train a data science team.
