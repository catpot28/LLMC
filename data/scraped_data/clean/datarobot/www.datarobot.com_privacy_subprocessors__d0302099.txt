DataRobot maintains a list of third-party Subprocessors that may be involved in the processing of our customers’ personal data in the course of the provision of DataRobot products and services (pursuant to the respective agreement in place). This webform allows the subscription to our automatic mechanism which will notify you regarding any updates to DataRobot’s list of Subprocessors.
