Frequently asked questions

How does agentic AI enhance enterprise workflows?

Agentic AI creates a workforce of specialized AI agents that collaborate to automate complex, end-to-end workflows, connecting diverse tools and data to drive smarter business outcomes.

How does DataRobot ensure agentic workflows are production-ready?

DataRobot provides a sandbox for testing, end-to-end tracing for observability, and guardrails to mitigate risks like prompt injections and hallucinations.

Can DataRobot's Agentic AI integrate with existing data sources and tools?

Yes, DataRobot’s open architecture and pre-built connectors allow seamless integration with your existing data sources, LLMs, and a wide range of enterprise AI tools.
