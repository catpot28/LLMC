Supply chain & operations

Support an elastic supply chain with AI that anticipates disruptions, optimizes logistics, and automates maintenance across your entire operation.

Procurement Orchestration

Identify supply shortages, find alternative vendors, and draft purchase orders for approval.

Predictive Maintenance

Analyze sensor data to predict failure, check parts inventory, and schedule maintenance in the ERP.

Logistics Route Optimization

Re-route shipments based on weather or congestion while notifying customers of ETA changes.

Quality Control Intelligence

Identify production anomalies from vision feeds and adjust machine parameters to reduce scrap.

Warehouse Labor Forecasting

Predict volume spikes and request shift coverage through staffing partner portals.

Inventory Rebalancing

Monitor stock levels across regions and initiate transfers to prevent stockouts.

Customer service

Deliver proactive, high-resolution support with AI that manages complex customer journeys and automates multi-step resolutions.

Smart Resolution Orchestration

Analyze customer history and sentiment to autonomously offer personalized substitutions for out-of-stock items.

Intelligent Refund Processing

Validate return requests against policy, initiate shipping labels, and trigger bank credits without manual intervention.

Proactive Engagement

Monitor delivery delays and autonomously send personalized apology credits or alternative solutions to impacted customers.

Agent Coaching & Assistance

Provide real-time, context-aware talk tracks and automatically summarize calls into CRM records.

Self-Service Expansion

Transition basic chatbots into action-oriented agents that can update account details or change service tiers via API.

Staffing Optimization

Predict call volume and automatically adjust agent shift schedules based on real-time demand signals.

Financial planning & analysis

Accelerate financial decision-making with AI that synthesizes disparate data, automates reporting, and executes complex scenario modeling.

Autonomous Financial Modeling

Synthesize data across ERP and CRM systems to generate and update dynamic financial forecasts.

Dynamic Scenario Analysis

Execute “what-if” models for market shifts and automatically distribute impact reports to stakeholders.

Vendor Contract Review

Scan and compare thousands of vendor contracts to identify savings opportunities or compliance risks.

Budget Variance Monitoring

Automatically flag budget deviations and request justifications from department heads via integrated workflows.

Revenue Leakage Detection

Scrutinize billing records against contract terms to identify and reclaim unbilled services.

Audit Readiness Assistance

Continuously gather and organize documentation for compliance, reducing manual year-end audit preparation.

Sales & marketing

Drive growth with AI that personalizes outreach and optimizes spend to move from static segments to proactive execution.

Hyper-Personalized Content

Analyze intent signals to autonomously draft and schedule tailored email and social outreach.

Market Intelligence

Scrape competitor pricing and news to adjust discount ceilings and alert sales teams to threats.

Lead Qualification Orchestration

Score leads based on behavior, research SEC filings, and prepare prospect “cheat sheets.”

Churn Mitigation

Identify at-risk accounts, analyze support history, and initiate win-back discount workflows.

Campaign Optimization

Monitor ad performance in real-time and reallocate budget based on pipeline efficiency.

Customer Reference Matching

Match new sales opportunities with existing customers based on industry and product fit.

Credit risk

Streamline lending and recovery with AI that automates documentation review, monitors risk, and optimizes collection strategies.

Lending Operations

Review loan applications against policy, fetch missing applicant data via API, and draft the final credit memo.

Credit Decision Automation

Regulatory Compliance Monitoring

Portfolio Risk Monitor

Recovery & Collections Optimization

Analyze payment history and price elasticity to determine and execute the best outreach timing and channel.

Dispute Resolution

Handle credit card chargeback disputes by gathering merchant evidence and communicating with the cardholder.
