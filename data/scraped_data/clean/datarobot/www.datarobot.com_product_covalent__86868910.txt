Covalent

Run agentic AI anywhere with efficient,
infrastructure-aware compute orchestration

Automate and optimize compute for cost-efficient AI

Dynamically match workloads to the most cost-effective infrastructure.
Manually configuring runtimes to optimize cost doesn’t scale. Covalent OSS reduces compute costs by auto-routing workloads—spot, reserved, even legacy nodes—based on pricing, latency, and task requirements. No config files, no scripting, no hassle.

Route tasks across clouds and regions—no infra to manage.
Run workloads across clouds, regions, or on-prem—no rewrites, no reconfig. Each task is dispatched to the best-fit environment based on dynamic runtime constraints—directly from a Python-defined DAG.

Develop agentic workflows without the DevOps toil

Write Python, not infrastructure code.
Define dynamic, multi-step workflows entirely in Python—no YAML, cloud configs, or infrastructure code. Covalent handles containerization, packaging, and execution—no DevOps expertise required.

LLM fine-tuning that pauses, resumes, and scales with you.
Launch multi-node GPU training without writing orchestration code. Pause, resume, or extract checkpoints mid-run to avoid wasted compute. Covalent gives you full control over long-running jobs, so you can iterate without starting over.

Debug faster with real-time visibility.
Track every task in flight and see what’s running, what failed, and why. Spot issues early, trace errors instantly, and inspect partial results as they execute. Make informed adjustments mid-run, without waiting for full workflows to fail.

Reuse, rerun, and share—without rewriting code.
Reuse workflows anywhere—no refactoring, no rework. Build once and rerun across environments with consistent behavior using Redispatch. Teammates get the same results as long as they’re running on compatible infrastructure.

Run agentic workloads anywhere, without vendor lock-in

Execute anywhere with environment-agnostic workflow.
Deploy to any environment—on-prem, Kubernetes, or public cloud—without rewriting workflows. Covalent’s pluggable executors support AWS, GCP, Azure, and custom backends, giving you flexibility across hybrid and multi-cloud setups.

Orchestrate on your terms with open-source infrastructure.
Integrate with your existing tools and workflows using open-source orchestration. Covalent works with your infra, CI/CD pipelines, and observability stack—no proprietary formats or platform constraints.

Lightweight.
