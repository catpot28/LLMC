451 Research: DataRobot Adds Generative AI Development, Trust and Governance to Its Data Science Platform

As organizations continue to explore generative AI, we are learning more about the potential benefits and roadblocks to realizing value.

DataRobot has identified a confidence problem around the deployment of generative AI in a business setting and is seeking to remedy this issue by unveiling new capabilities that will enable organizations to build and use generative AI in a trusted and governed way.

Download
DataRobot Adds Generative AI Development, Trust and Governance to Its Data Science Platform
to find out:

Which new DataRobot features address the confidence gap with generative AI

How newly introduced features improve LLM management and operations

How DataRobot addresses the issue of “responsible AI” with custom tests and fairness metrics

DataRobot adds generative AI development, trust and governance to its data science platform, Krishna Roy, Sr. Research Analyst - 451 Research, part of S&P Global Market Intelligence
