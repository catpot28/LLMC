Operate with confidence: Agent observability and monitoring for enterprise AI

Most production dashboards can't see what your agents are actually doing

Your AI agents can be “running” and still failing.

They can loop through tool calls, burn budget, miss expected actions, or create safety and compliance exposure before a traditional dashboard shows anything is wrong.

Inside this ebook:

The five stability signals every production agent system needs to monitor

A tracing foundation for reconstructing decisions, tool calls, and downstream actions

Cost and safety monitoring practices for catching failures before they spread

A 30-day checklist for making agent monitoring operational

Get the ebook

Agents are already acting across your enterprise. This is how you see what they’re doing — and make sure they’re operating within the boundaries you set.
