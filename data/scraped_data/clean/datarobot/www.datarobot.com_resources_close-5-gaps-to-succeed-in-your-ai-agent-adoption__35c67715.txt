Industry Analyst Report

Close 5 gaps to succeed in your AI agent adoption

AI agents promise a new era of automation, but a two-sided gap exists between their potential and enterprise-wide adoption. We believe this Gartner® report identifies the five critical gaps holding back both the technology and the enterprises trying to use it. Get a direct look at the challenges, a clear roadmap to close them, and the steps needed to move from hype to tangible outcomes.

Download the report to learn how to:

Address the immaturity of AI agent technology by using task-specific point solutions.

Overcome integration challenges with applications by modernizing legacy systems.

Resolve data accessibility and quality issues by making data agent-ready.

The path to AI agent success is clear. Get the research.

Source: Zhang, Tong, Ramos, Leinar, Yan, Ben, Fang, Mike, and Olliffe, Gary. “Close 5 Gaps to Succeed in Your AI Agent Adoption.” Gartner, 17 July 2025.
