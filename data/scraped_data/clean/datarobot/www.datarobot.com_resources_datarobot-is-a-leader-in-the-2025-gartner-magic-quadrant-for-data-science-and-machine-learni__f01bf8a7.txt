DataRobot is a Leader again in the 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Data Science and Machine Learning Platforms

DataRobot is recognized for both Completeness of Vision and Ability to Execute.

DataRobot is proud to announce that Gartner® has once again named us a Leader in the 2025 Magic Quadrant™ for Data Science and Machine Learning Platforms, recognized for both Completeness of Vision and Ability to Execute.

We believe DataRobot’s recognition in the 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for DSML Platforms reflects our ability to help teams build and deliver production-ready AI agents, the flexibility to build and orchestrate your own agent-driven applications, and our highly-regarded governance and safety capabilities.

Download the report to discover what to look for in your AI and agentic AI platform and insights about recognized vendors for their Completeness of Vision and Ability to Execute.
