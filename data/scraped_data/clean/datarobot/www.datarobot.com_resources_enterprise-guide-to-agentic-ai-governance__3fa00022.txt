Ebook

The enterprise guide to agentic AI governance

How to govern AI agents across the full lifecycle

AI governance frameworks were built for models that predict. Agents don’t predict — they act. And the controls your organization built for traditional AI weren’t designed to follow an agent across systems, tools, and decision points.This ebook gives you the framework to govern them.

What changes after you read this:

Permissions scoped to function, revocable on demand

Controls that travel with the agent, wherever it runs

Audit trails that show exactly what any agent did, when, and why

Fleet oversight that scales. 100 agents, not 100 times the effort

Get the ebook

Agents are already acting on your organization’s behalf. This is how you govern them.
