GigaOm Sonar for AI Governance

DataRobot named a Leader and Fast Mover in AI governance in the GigaOm Sonar Report.

According to the GigaOm Sonar, the DataRobot approach to governance is exceptional in machine learning and generative AI, risk controls and guardrails, monitoring and observability, and use case management.

One of the primary features of DataRobot is its auditability module that examines ML predictions, as well as genAI prompts and responses. On the GenAI side, it includes a full set of “moderation” functionality, including guards around prompt injection, hallucination, and relevance (or how “on topic” something is) in the context of RAG input and output.

Source: “GigaOm Sonar for AI Governance”, By: Andrew Brust, October 2024

Download an excerpt of the GigaOm Sonar for AI Governance Platforms to learn more about:

Why DataRobot was named a Leader.

How automating resource and access regulations leads to strong AI governance.

What features are considered critical for AI governance with generative and predictive AI.
