Meet your agentic AI workforce

You’ve heard the hype. But when it comes to applying AI in your business, the question is simple:
What’s actually working?

This ebook introduces AI use cases that are the building blocks of an agentic AI workforce: ready-to-run, customizable, and aligned to real business needs.

Whether you’re just getting started or looking to expand your AI investment, this is your practical starting point.

What you’ll find inside:

Real use cases across generative, agentic, and predictive AI

Examples of how teams are turning AI into action

Inspiration for your own roadmap, grounded in what’s already working

A growing library of use cases, updated year over year
