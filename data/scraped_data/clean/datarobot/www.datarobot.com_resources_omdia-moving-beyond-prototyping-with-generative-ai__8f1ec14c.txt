Moving Beyond Prototyping to Production with Generative AI to Unlock Real-World Value

While generative AI has quickly and deeply permeated society, the reality is that most organizations are still not yet realizing the full value of the technology.

How can companies move from a prototype to a production-grade generative AI project?

Today’s early generative AI adopters can unleash the transformational power of both generative and predictive AI outcomes both individually and together into differentiated solutions by investing in a highly operationalized AI solution. A concept referred to as LLMOps.

Moving Beyond Prototyping to Production with Generative AI by Omdia

The basic building blocks of the AI lifecycle that minimize risks and remove obstacles to achieving value and scale with generative AI initiatives

How organizations are tackling their generative AI lifecycle, its scalability, and security
