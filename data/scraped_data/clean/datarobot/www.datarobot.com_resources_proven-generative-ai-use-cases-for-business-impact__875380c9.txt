Proven Generative AI Use Cases for Business Impact

The DataRobot Gen AI Playbook is your comprehensive guide to low-effort, high-impact use cases that create real value for you and your organization. Build your expertise and AI capabilities with a holistic look at the technical, infrastructure, and skill requirements to reach your goals and discover how to manage common risks throughout the AI lifecycle for true long-term value.

Download the DataRobot GenAI Playbook to explore use cases like:

Faster suspicious activity reporting (SAR)

Automatic resolution for common customer service inquiries

Easier data analysis and reporting

Improved forecasting with genAI

Democratized information access with a customized chatbot
