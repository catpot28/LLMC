The Unmet AI Needs Survey

With qualitative and quantitative responses from over 700 AI practitioners worldwide, the Unmet AI Needs survey is an in-depth and unprecedented view into the day-to-day frustrations preventing AI teams from delivering the AI their businesses need – and how real-world organizations are overcoming those challenges.

Insights include:

66% of AI teams don’t have the tools to deliver the models necessary to meet business goals

71% of AI practitioners lack confidence in their AI solutions

1-in-3 teams are struggling to deliver generative AI

57% of practitioners prefer vendors who provide best practices in developing and deploying AI

Download the report to learn how organizations can make smarter investments in their AI infrastructure.
