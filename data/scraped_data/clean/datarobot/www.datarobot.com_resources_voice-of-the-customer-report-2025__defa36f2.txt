DataRobot is again a Customers’ Choice for Gartner®️ Peer Insights™️

Voice of the Customer Report for Data Science and Machine Learning Platforms

DataRobot
has once again been named a Customers’ Choice in the October 2025 Gartner® Peer Insights™ “Voice of the Customer” for Data Science and Machine Learning Platforms. We believe this recognition reflects the real-world experiences of customers who trust DataRobot to deliver production-ready AI solutions, drive business value, and accelerate their AI initiatives.

Download the report to see what customers are saying about DataRobot’s ability to help teams build and deploy AI agents and applications with speed, flexibility, and enterprise-grade governance.

Hear from our customers in Gartner Peer Insights™

DataRobot has been an incredible time saver for my team of analysts. I estimate it has reduced model development time by 90% on some of our larger projects.

Chief Credit Officer, Banking

DataRobot has been a game-changer for our team. It has significantly accelerated our data science experiments, allowing us to iterate and refine models much faster than before

Senior Data Scientist, Education

Our experience with DataRobot has been exceptional, from the sales process all the way through to implementation and ongoing support. The team is very responsive, knowledgeable, and willing to go the extra mile to ensure we maximize the platform's potential.

Senior Director of Finance, Services
