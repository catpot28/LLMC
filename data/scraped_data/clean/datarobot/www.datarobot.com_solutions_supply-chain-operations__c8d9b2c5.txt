Frequently asked questions

What is the DataRobot Supply Chain and Ops AI App Suite?

It is a suite of applications designed to integrate with SAP supply chain management software to help with demand forecasting, inventory management, and disruption mitigation.

How can DataRobot improve SAP enterprise planning for supply chain?

DataRobot enhances SAP supply chain planning by providing data-driven insights and agentic flows to improve inventory management, forecast demand, and boost overall efficiency.

How does DataRobot help companies manage supply chain disruptions?

DataRobot helps businesses proactively anticipate and adapt to supply chain changes, manage risks with predictive models, and mitigate the impact of disruptions on their operations.
