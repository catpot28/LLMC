Accelerate deployment of your on-prem AI Stack with Nutanix and DataRobot

Many organizations are under strict data security and regulatory requirements, and can’t afford to rely solely on cloud vendors for their AI stack. However, this doesn’t mean you have to fall behind in the AI revolution. With the combined power of DataRobot and Nutanix, you can quickly build a turnkey AI stack tailored for high-security, on-premises environments that unleashes the latest in AI technology.

Join our upcoming webinar to discover how this powerful AI stack can:

Maintain full control over you data while accelerating the deployment of your AI infrastructure

Adopt the latest, GPU-powered AI models and tools without depending on cloud service providers or stitching together open source solutions

ScaleAI initiatives confidently and securely from a self-managed environment with the DataRobot AI lifecycle platform

Speakers

Chris deMontmollin

Product Marketing Manager, Partner and Tech Alliances, DataRobot

Mike Barmonde

Senior Product Marketing Manager AI, Networking and Security, Nutanix
