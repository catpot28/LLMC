AI-Powered Supply Chains: Real-World Stories with Norfolk Iron & Metal and SAP

See how supply chain and AI leaders are driving measurable impact with intelligent, adaptive planning. Join Norfolk Iron & Metal Director of Data Analytics Ben DuBois, SAP, and DataRobot AI experts to explore how AI-driven forecasting is reshaping supply chain decision-making for increased agility and reduced costs in the face of constant disruption.

Get a real-world look at what’s possible with a live showcase of a next-gen AI app for demand planning—designed for SAP IBP—that’s enabling faster, more adaptive forecasting and execution at scale.

This session is built for leaders focused on performance, transformation, and competitive edge. You’ll learn how to:

Align AI with supply chain strategy to unlock real business value

Improve forecast accuracy and demand responsiveness

Streamline operations and reduce planning cycle times

Empower teams with real-time, AI-driven insights and recommendations
