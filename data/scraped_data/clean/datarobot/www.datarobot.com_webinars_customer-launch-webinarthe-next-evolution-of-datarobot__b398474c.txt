Customer Launch Webinar:
The Next Evolution of DataRobot

Discover how DataRobot’s latest features help turn your predictive and generative models into impactful, high-quality apps that drive business results in our Fall ‘24 Launch webinar!

In this exclusive customers-only event, you will
connect directly with our product managers
to provide feedback and gain insights on how
our new direction gives your teams the tools they need to succeed
.

We will deep dive into all of the newest, most-highly requested features in our Fall ‘24 release, including:

AI app-building frameworks

Large data handling techniques

Expanded SAP integrations

Geospatial monitoring

Space is limited – register now to save your spot!

Speakers

Brian Bell Jr.

Senior Director of Product, AI Production, DataRobot

Vika Smilansky

Senior Product Marketing Manager - Platform & Solutions, DataRobot

Senior Product Marketing Manager, DataRobot
