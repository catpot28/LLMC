DataRobot & AWS: Planning for Success with Generative AI

Do you have a robust and long-term plan to help you build generative AI projects that can withstand the test of time? In an increasingly dynamic AI landscape, it’s imperative to ensure flexibility that allows your tools, practices, and infrastructure to maintain their efficacy.

Join experts from AWS, IDC, and DataRobot to explore some of the key observations and foundational tactics necessary for keeping your generative AI journey on the right track.

Speakers

Anthony Smith

Head of Customer Engineering, DataRobot

Dylan Cotter

Sr. Partner Development Manager, AWS

Ritu Jyoti

Group Vice President, Worldwide Artificial Intelligence and Automation Research Practice, IDC

Watch the Planning for Success with Generative AI webinar and find out about:

Key components of a robust generative AI plan

Important AI infrastructure considerations that can future-proof your AI strategy

The DataRobot Generative AI Catalyst Program, designed to get your organization to real-world value as quickly as possible
