Federal Executive Forum: Artificial Intelligence Strategies in Government Progress and Best Practices 2026

How do the world's most secure agencies operationalize AI at scale?

In this exclusive executive webinar,
DataRobot Chief Customer Officer Chad Cisco
joins a distinguished panel of federal leaders to discuss the roadmap for AI in 2026 and beyond. This isn’t just a discussion about technology—it’s a masterclass in
mission-driven innovation
.

A Peer-to-Peer Executive Exchange

Gain rare insights from a panel of experts at the forefront of national security and government modernization:

Donald Coulter
, Senior Science Advisor for Cybersecurity, Science and Technology Directorate, Department of Homeland Security

Israel Soong
, Deputy Director, Office of Artificial Intelligence, Central Intelligence Agency

Chad Cisco
, Chief Customer Officer, DataRobot

Nate Riley
, Field Sales Manager, Public Sector, BMC Helix

Janak Kalaria
, Senior Vice President, ICF

Luke McCormack
, Host of the Federal Executive Forum

Expert Insight: Chad Cisco on the Agentic Shift

While the panel explores the broad government landscape, Chad provides the tactical blueprint for scaling impact. As a veteran in automated machine learning and operations, Chad breaks down why most AI initiatives fail at the “Day 90” mark and how to build an infrastructure that lasts. You’ll learn:

The Three Pillars of Success
: Why rapid experimentation must be balanced with unified governance and fit-for-purpose model sizing.

Real-World Triumphs
: How US Transcom shortened decision loops from weeks to hours and improved delivery forecasting by 40%.

The Rise of the Agentic Workforce
: A look at the future where AI isn’t just a tool, but a collaborator handling routine tasks across 13+ applications.

Intelligence at the Edge
: Discover the Radio Intelligence Agent — a breakthrough in edge computing that processes massive data locally to deliver only the most critical insights to the cloud.

Why Watch?

The fast progression of AI has created a gap between those who can build a demo and those who can deliver a result. This webinar provides the framework to bridge that gap, focusing on the trade-offs between
cost, accuracy, and latency
that every leader must understand.

Unlock the full discussion to see how the CIA triages content, how DHS secures the border with AI, and Chad Cisco’s deep dive into the sovereign AI architectures of tomorrow.
