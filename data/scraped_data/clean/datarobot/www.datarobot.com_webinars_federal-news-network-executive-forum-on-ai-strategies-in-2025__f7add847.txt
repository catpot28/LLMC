Federal News Network: Executive Forum on AI Strategies in 2025

Hear how federal agencies are building, scaling, and securing AI for real-world missions.

Learn how AI is being used today across the Department of Defense, Navy, Intelligence Community, and more. This panel of private and public sector leaders discusses emerging AI capabilities, how AI is supporting mission-critical programs, and how government organizations are securely implementing AI.

Panelists also share lessons learned, challenges and solutions, and a vision for the future.

Jonathan (Jon) Elliott, Deputy Director, Artificial Intelligence Rapid Capabilities Cell, Chief Digital & Artificial Intelligence Office (CDAO), Department of Defense

Stuart Blair Wagner, Chief Data & Artificial Intelligence Officer, Navy

Ramesh Menon, Chief Technology Officer, Defense Intelligence Agency

John Dvorak, Chief Technology Officer, Red Hat Public Sector

Greg Carl, Principal Technologist, Pure Storage Public Sector

Chad Cisco, Chief Customer Officer, DataRobot

Moderator: Luke McCormack, Host of the Federal Executive Forum
