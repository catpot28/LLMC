Navigate your agentic AI strategy with DataRobot

Learn why Gartner® named DataRobot a Leader again

DataRobot has been named multiyear Leader – again – in the Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Data Science and Machine Learning (DSML) Platforms, recognized for its Completeness of Vision and Ability to Execute.

Discover how we believe DataRobot empowers AI teams to go beyond experimentation and deliver real-world impact with production-ready agents. Learn how you can integrate these agents with core systems like SAP and NVIDIA, enforce robust governance, and scale AI across the enterprise—safely and efficiently.

In this session, you’ll get an inside look at DataRobot’s latest agentic AI innovations, hear firsthand from a featured customer about their transformation journey, and watch a live demo of a powerful enterprise agent in action.

Join us to learn:

How to manage cost, latency, accuracy, and risk—from your first deployment to enterprise-wide scale

How we think DataRobot simplifies AI operations with built-in guardrails, policy enforcement, and reusable agent templates

How enterprises are integrating agents into mission-critical workflows using trusted systems and infrastructure

What’s next on the horizon for agentic AI, including real-time decisioning, autonomous workflows, and cross-system orchestration

Speakers

Savita Raina

Sr. Director of Product Marketing, DataRobot

Santiago Hernandez

Chief Data Officer, Mercury Financial

Zach Ingbretsen

Data Scientist, DataRobot

Whether you’re just getting started or looking to scale your AI investments, this webinar will give you a practical path forward with agentic AI.
