Effective Prompting: Getting the Most out of LLMs

LLMs are immensely powerful productivity tools – if you can communicate with them effectively. Effective communication requires understanding your audience – what they know, how they think, and what they might get wrong.

In this webinar, you will learn how language models are built and trained, how to think effectively about language models and get practical advice for using and building chatbots of your own.

Speakers

Alex Conway

Principal Software Engineer, DataRobot

Join to hear:

How LLM training influences their behavior

How to limit hallucinations and get consistent, high-quality responses

Learn about state-of-the-art research into the science of LLM prompting
