Governance in the Era of AI

DataRobot was named a Leader by Gartner in the 2024 Magic Quadrant™ for Data Science and Machine Learning (DSML) Platforms and ranked highest in the Governance Use Case in the Critical Capabilities report.

To learn what differentiated our enterprise AI platform and governance capabilities, join DataRobot AI experts to discuss:

Emerging trends in the AI and DSML landscape,

The criticality of governance and observability with evolving business needs, regulatory requirements, and technology sprawl, and,

How DataRobot guardrails, real-time intervention and moderation, and compliance capabilities can secure your business.

Speakers

Lisa Aguilar

VP, Product Marketing, DataRobot

Justin Swansburg

VP Applied AI & Technical Field Leads, DataRobot

Gartner, Magic Quadrant for Data Science and Machine Learning Platforms, Afraz Jaffri, Aura Popa, Peter Krensky, Jim Hare, Raghvender Bhati, Maryam Hassanlou, Tong Zhang, June 17, 2024.
