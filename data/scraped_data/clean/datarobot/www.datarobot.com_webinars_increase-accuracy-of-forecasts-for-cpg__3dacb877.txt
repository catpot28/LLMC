On-Demand Webinar

Enriched Data = More Accurate Models: Ready Signal’s External Data Platform for DataRobot

The importance of leveraging external data to enrich demand plans and sales forecasts is incredibly important in almost every industry.. Companies are presented with very different external factors at a Brand, Category, SKU or Geography level that impact the accuracy of forecasted models. This makes Efficiency, Accuracy and Scalability incredibly important in the modeling process.

This webinar will showcase an AI Accelerator that integrates Ready Signal’s external data platform with DataRobot’s advanced machine learning engine. Learn how you can enhance your models with instant access to 1000s of external data sources, spanning Consumer Behavior, Micro/Macro Economics, Weather, Demographic trends, and so much more.

Speakers

Jeremy Pernicek

Senior Data Scientist, Team Lead, DataRobot

Colby Miller

Partner Demand Gen Lead, DataRobot

Baylen Springer

CEO, Ready Signal

Matt Schaefer

Chief Growth Officer, Ready Signal

What you’ll learn in this session:

A demo of Ready Signal’s AI Accelerator in the DatARobot Platform

How enhancing models with specific external data sources increases Efficiency, Accuracy, and Scalability

Various external factors that impact model accuracy
