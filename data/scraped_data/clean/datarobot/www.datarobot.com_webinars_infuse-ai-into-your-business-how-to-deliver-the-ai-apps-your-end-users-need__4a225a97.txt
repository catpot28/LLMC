Infuse AI Into Your Business: How to Deliver the AI Apps Your End Users Need

Despite increased AI investments, only one-third of AI teams have the tools they need to meet business goals. Despite the billions invested in AI, there remains a critical gap between how AI is built and how it ultimately impacts the business.

When AI teams have the right tools and the right infrastructure to rapidly develop and deliver AI into their business applications, they can deliver the outcomes their businesses need. Join experts from DataRobot as they discuss the core elements organizations need to confidently infuse AI into their business, exactly where users need it most.

Discover how real-world AI teams are delivering value with demos and an interactive conversation featuring DataRobot Product Marketers Chris deMontmollin and Vika Smilansky, with DataRobot Technical Field Lead Luke Shulman.

Speakers

Vika Smilansky

Senior Product Marketing Manager - Platform & Solutions, DataRobot

Chris deMontmollin

Product Marketing Manager, Partner and Tech Alliances, DataRobot

Luke Shulman

Director, Vanguard Apps, DataRobot

Pragnya Paramita

Director, AI Product Marketing, SAP BTP, Amplitude
