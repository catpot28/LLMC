Live Demo: Demand Planning App for SAP

See how the Demand Planning App enhances forecasting in SAP IBP

Join DataRobot experts for a live walkthrough of how the Demand Planning App integrates with SAP IBP to deliver accurate, scalable forecasts—without disrupting existing workflows.

In this live demo we’ll cover:

How to extract, model, and forecast data from SAP IBP using the Demand Planning App.

How AI-driven time series forecasting improves SKU-level precision across high-volume planning scenarios.

How supply chain and operations teams can stay ahead of demand shifts and reduce inventory surprises.
