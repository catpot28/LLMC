On-demand: Talk to My Data Agentic App

Build a self‑service AI agent in weeks, not months.

Join a live, 30‑minute deep dive led by a DataRobot Principal Data Scientist and see how our Talk to My Data template lets you:

Query your enterprise data and predictive models in plain English

Generate interactive charts, summaries, and next‑step recommendations on demand

Securely connect multiple sources—warehouse, lake, or spreadsheets—without moving data

Deliver impactful predictions that support everyday decisions in sales, marketing, finance, supply chain, and beyond.Walk away with a step‑by‑step deployment guide, architecture diagrams, and best‑practice tips to spin up your first agentic workflow right after the session.

In this technical demo, you will learn how to:

Run ML algorithms

Perform deep dive data analysis

Leverage it as a reporting tool
