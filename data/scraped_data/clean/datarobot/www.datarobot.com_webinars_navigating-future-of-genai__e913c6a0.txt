Navigating the Future of Generative AI

Generative AI has seen tremendous growth in recent years, with applications ranging from image and video generation to natural language processing and music composition. As the technology continues to advance, it is important for businesses and organizations to understand how to navigate the future of generative AI.

In this webinar, DataRobot CTO Michael Schmidt will highlight the latest developments in the field of generative AI and dive into the top trends to consider in 2024. Whether you are a business leader looking to stay ahead of the curve or a researcher interested in the latest developments in generative AI, this webinar is a must-attend event.

Speakers

Michael Schmidt

Chief Technology Officer
