Predictive & Agentic AI: Transforming Government & Society

In this latest episode of Chat with Chet, Chet Hayes, CTO of Vertosoft, and Chandler McCann, CTO of DataRobot, delve into the evolving landscape of AI technologies. They explore the enduring importance of Predictive AI, including classical supervised machine learning, amidst the rapid advancements in generative AI and large language models. Despite the hype, predictive AI remains crucial for driving outcomes, especially in federal and civilian organizations. They also discuss the rising prominence of ‘agentic AI,’ which offers improved autonomy and accessibility for non-data scientists, significantly impacting the public sector. Chandler McCann shares insights on breaking down big problems into smaller tasks to reduce workloads.
