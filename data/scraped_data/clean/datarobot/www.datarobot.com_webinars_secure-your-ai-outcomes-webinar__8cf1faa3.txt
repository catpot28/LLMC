Secure Your AI Outcomes: Eliminate AI Risks and Scale with Confidence

Is your AI secure and compliant with evolving regulations and policies?

45% of AI professionals cite difficulties ensuring the reliability and consistency of their models, as their top challenge. Monitoring outputs in real-time and ensuring dependable performance remain a significant concern for AI teams.

Join May Masoud, Product Marketing Manager, AI Cloud and MLOps at DataRobot to learn how you can mitigate AI risks and scale your AI projects with confidence.

The webinar will cover:

The latest tools and technologies for AI observability and governance; explore cutting-edge solutions that can help you automate security, enforce policies, and continuously monitor your AI systems.

How leading organizations have successfully implemented robust AI observability and governance frameworks to scale AI initiatives while reducing business risk.

Clear next steps to confidently monitor and scale your AI projects.

Speakers

May Masoud

Data Scientist

Aslihan Buner

Senior Product Marketing Manager, AI Observability, DataRobot
