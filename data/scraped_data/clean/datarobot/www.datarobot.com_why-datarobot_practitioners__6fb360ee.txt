Why DataRobot

Why AI practitioners choose DataRobot

The DataRobot value.
Help your team develop and deliver better models in record time.

Collaborate in a centralized and interoperable workspace

Consolidate datasets, features, experiments, models, and deployments into a single experience. Eliminate roadblocks by connecting your data, infrastructure, tools, and business applications through pre-built integrations or our open API.

Ensure model quality from end-to-end

Maintain model integrity from experimentation to deployment. Customize preprocessing, feature engineering, model evaluation metrics, hyperparameter tuning, AI guardrails, and custom metrics at every stage of the process.

Explore at scale with speed

Evaluate and implement cutting-edge predictive models and LLMs. Rapidly experiment with hundreds of suggested approaches for data preprocessing, vector database chunking, multimodal feature engineering, and prompt engineering to maximize accuracy.

Streamlined processes and workflows

Shorten time to deployment by automating complex, repetitive, or manual steps, including data cleaning, compliance documentation, configuring AI pipelines, and managing deployment resources.

Frequently asked questions

How does DataRobot help practitioners collaborate efficiently?

AI practitioners work together in a centralized, interoperable workspace, consolidating datasets, experiments, models, and tools into one streamlined, collaborative environment.

How does DataRobot ensure model quality throughout the workflow?

The Agent Workforce Platform maintains model integrity with preprocessing, feature engineering, tuning, evaluation, AI guardrails, and personalized metrics.

How do practitioners scale experimentation in DataRobot?

Teams can explore at scale—rapidly trying hundreds of predictive or LLM approaches, leveraging multimodal feature engineering, vector chunking, and prompt tuning for better accuracy.
