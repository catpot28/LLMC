ElevenLabs Affiliate Partner Guide
Welcome partner,
We're excited to have you on board and we can't wait to see what you'll create as an ElevenLabs Affiliate. To make your setup seamless, we've divided this guide into two parts: a step-by-step guide to get you started and earning and an overview of ElevenLabs technology.
Getting Started with ParterStack
After signing up on our ElevenLabs Affiliates landing page, you’ll be prompted to enter your email address. If you already have an ElevenLabs account, we recommend using the same email; otherwise, feel free to use any active email address.
You’ll be directed to our web platform to sign up for PartnerStack, our third-party affiliate management platform.
Once you’ve completed the onboarding with PartnerStack, you’ll be taken directly to your personal dashboard’s Home page. On this page, you’ll find an overview of the programs you’re partnered with, a summary of your performance, and recent referral activity.
Click into your ElevenLabs program to track your referral link metrics, view your commission earnings, and explore our ElevenLabs resources.
Here, you’ll also be able to access your unique partner link and create custom links tailored to your name, social media handle, business, or product.
To withdraw your commissions, you’ll need to set up your bank account. Simply click Commissions on the top navigation bar, then select Setup Withdrawals. PartnerStack’s payment period runs from the 1st to the 15th of each month.
All things payment
For every new paid subscriber plan, you’ll earn 22% on all payments from our Starter, Creator, Pro and Scale Payments for the first 12 months. You’ll also earn 11% on all payments from our Business Tier.
Once your referral has made their first payment, your commission fee is valid 90 days after. This is due to the dispute deadline of customers’ credit cards. For example, if someone you refer purchases a paid license on Feb 10th, that referral becomes 'active' on May 10th and you will be paid on the next payment period (in June).
The accumulated active commission fees are paid out once per month via PartnerStack. If there are payment issues, please reach out to them directly.

FAQ'S
How do I withdraw my commissions?
Available payment options include Stripe, PayPal, and Direct Deposit, depending on your country. You can find more details on withdrawal options in PartnerStack.
What do my PartnerStack Dashboard statistics mean?
Clicks: The total number of clicks on your referral links.
Signups: The total number of individuals (both paid and unpaid) who signed up on a program’s website within 90 days of clicking your link.
Revenue: The total revenue generated from your referred customers.
Pending Commissions: The total earned commissions with statuses such as scheduled, pending approval, or approved and awaiting payment over the next 12 months.
Paid Commissions: The total commission you’ve earned that has been approved and paid by your partner programs.
How do I add a custom link?
In your Dashboard, go to Links, then select Custom Links. For more detailed instructions, check our help guide: https://support.partnerstack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009476833-Creating-custom-referral-links
I received an elevenlabs.io affiliate link but can’t see it on my dashboard. Will this still work?
If you generated an instant signup elevenlabs.io link on our webpage, it is automatically connected to your PartnerStack account via the email address you provided. You can find this link on your ElevenLabs Dashboard under the Affiliates section, not in your PartnerStack Dashboard. While your elevenlabs.io commissions are linked to PartnerStack, please note that certain metrics (such as the number of clicks) will not be visible, as it functions as an instant link without tracking metrics.
I have both an elevenlabs.io affiliate link and a PartnerStack try.elevenlabs.io link. Will they both work?
Yes, your referral links are connected through the email address you provide. As long as you use the same email address, both links will connect seamlessly and contribute commissions to your PartnerStack Dashboard.
Did you remove the ability to generate an instant signup link on your webpage?
Yes, we have removed direct link generation on our webpage for new customers to streamline metrics. This change only affects new affiliates, who will use the PartnerStack links moving forward.
If I have multiple links, will they all work?
Yes, you can set up to 100 custom links for different products, personal brand names, social media handles, or webpage links. Each link will have its own metrics and will contribute to your overall commissions.
Can I start using my referral link straight away?
Yes, you can start using your referral link immediately; it will be active right away.
Can I run Google Ads with this affiliate link?
No, we don’t allow partners to use affiliate links in Google Ads. Violation of this policy will result in removal from the partner program.
Are there any help articles for ParterStack?
Yes, there are many, check out this intro to PartnerStack guide, you’ll also find many others.
