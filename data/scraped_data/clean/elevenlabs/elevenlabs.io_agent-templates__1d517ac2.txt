Frequently asked questions
What are AI agent templates?
AI agent templates are pre-built, configurable frameworks that allow you to deploy functional AI agents instantly without starting from scratch. They generally support voice and/or chat channels such as telephony, sms, whatsapp and more.
How are these AI agents being built?
These agents are built using ElevenAgents, a visual tool for building AI Agents. You can provide your SOPs and connect your company's systems quickly to create a customized agent for your use case in minutes not days.
Can I customize the agent logic?
Yes. Once you clone the template we will guide you through setting it up with all the right configurations and you will be able to modify logic blocks, prompts, or integrations to fit your business needs.
Are AI agent templates free?
Yes. ElevenAgents offers free AI agent templates across all categories, customer support, sales, coaching, HR, education, and more. You can deploy and customize any template at no cost on the free plan, with optional upgrades for higher usage limits and premium features. There's no credit card required to get started, and free-tier agents are fully functional, not limited demos.
How long does it take to deploy an AI agent from a template?
If you have all the right information and integrations for your business ready you can deploy an AI Agent from a template in under 10 minutes. Select a template, customize the persona and knowledge base to match your business, and click publish. Your agent immediately gets a shareable link, an embeddable chat widget you can drop onto any website, and full API access for custom integrations. No infrastructure setup, no DevOps, no waiting for deployments.
What AI models power the templates?
ElevenAgents supports most publicly available AI Models and you can even bring your own self hosted models. For a
full list of supported models refer to our docs
Can I embed the agent on my website?
Yes. Every published agent comes with a lightweight embed widget you can use to add it to your website. The widget renders as a floating bubble that opens into a full conversation interface. It's fully responsive, works on mobile, and loads asynchronously so it won't slow down your page.
