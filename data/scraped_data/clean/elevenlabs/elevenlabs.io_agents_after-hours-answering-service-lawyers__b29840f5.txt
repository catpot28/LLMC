Frequently asked questions
What is an after-hours answering service for lawyers?
It’s a 24/7 solution that ensures your firm never misses a client inquiry. ElevenLabs’ AI agents answer calls, perform legal intake, and log data securely, even outside office hours.
How is this different from a traditional virtual receptionist?
Unlike human call centers, our AI agents respond instantly, never miss a call, and integrate directly with your legal tools—reducing cost per lead while improving response times.
Can the system handle conflict checks and intake forms?
Yes. Our agents follow your intake workflows, perform preliminary conflict checks, and gather the data you need for attorney review.
Is it secure and compliant with legal regulations?
Absolutely. ElevenLabs supports HIPAA, GDPR, and U.S. data residency. All call data can be stored privately or not retained at all, depending on your compliance needs.
How quickly can we get started?
Most firms are live within days. Our team helps you configure scripts, connect your systems, and launch your AI receptionist with no downtime.
