Frequently asked questions
How is this different from a traditional after-hours medical answering service?
Unlike human-staffed call centers, ElevenLabs AI voice agents operate 24/7 without downtime. They respond instantly, capture every detail accurately, and follow your clinic’s specific workflows while maintaining a natural, human-like tone.
Is the service HIPAA-compliant?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and our platform fully complies with HIPAA, GDPR, and SOC 2 requirements. You can also enable zero-retention or EU data residency modes for stricter control.
Can the agent escalate emergencies to an on-call provider?
Absolutely. Agents identify urgent cases through triage protocols and can immediately alert on-call staff via phone, text, or email with full context from the conversation.
How quickly can we deploy the service?
Most practices go live within days. Integration with your telephony and EHR systems is straightforward, and our team provides full setup and support to ensure compliance and reliability.
What results can we expect?
Clinics typically see a 90% reduction in missed calls, 35% faster patient response times, and over 60% lower after-hours staffing costs compared to traditional services.
