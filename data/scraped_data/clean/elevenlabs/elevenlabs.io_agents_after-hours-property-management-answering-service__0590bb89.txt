Frequently asked questions
How quickly can we set up the after-hours answering service?
You can deploy your first AI agent within days. Our onboarding team helps you configure scripts, integrations, and escalation rules for your properties.
Does it integrate with our existing property management software?
Yes. ElevenLabs Agents integrate seamlessly with major systems like Yardi, AppFolio, Buildium, and HubSpot for real-time updates and recordkeeping.
What happens in a true emergency?
The AI instantly detects emergency keywords, verifies details, and dispatches the correct on-call vendor or technician per your escalation workflow.
Can the service handle bilingual tenants?
Absolutely. ElevenLabs Agents communicate fluently in English and Spanish and can switch languages dynamically mid-conversation.
Is tenant data secure?
Yes. Our infrastructure meets enterprise-grade security standards, including SOC 2 and GDPR compliance, and offers optional zero-data-retention modes for added protection.
