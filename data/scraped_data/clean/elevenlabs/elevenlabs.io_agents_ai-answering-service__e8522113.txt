Frequently asked questions
How can a virtual receptionist help my small business?
AI receptionists answer every call, greet customers professionally, and handle scheduling or basic questions. This ensures you never miss a call, even outside business hours, and frees your team to focus on core work instead of constant interruptions.
Is this service affordable for small businesses?
Yes. AI answering services cost far less than hiring a full-time receptionist. You only pay for usage, making it flexible and scalable as your business grows.
Can I customize how the AI greets callers?
Absolutely. You can choose the voice, tone, and greeting style to match your brand. Many small businesses create a consistent, professional first impression by tailoring the AI receptionist to sound like part of their team.
Will the AI be able to handle scheduling and messages?
Yes. The virtual receptionist can book appointments, manage calendars, and take detailed messages. It can also send notifications or log details into your CRM so you never lose track of a customer inquiry.
Can it support multiple languages for my customers?
Yes. The AI can converse in 70+ languages and even switch mid-conversation, making it ideal for small businesses that serve diverse communities.
Does it replace human staff?
No. The AI receptionist takes care of routine, high-volume calls and ensures nothing slips through the cracks. When needed, it can transfer customers to a human team member with full context.
How secure is the system for customer data?
Enterprise-grade security features are included, such as encryption, GDPR compliance, and optional zero-retention mode. Even small businesses benefit from the same protections trusted by large enterprises.
How quickly can I set up a virtual receptionist?
Most small businesses can launch within a few days. Setup is simple, with out-of-the-box integrations for calendars, CRMs, and phone systems.
What kind of results can I expect?
Small businesses typically see fewer missed calls, more booked appointments, and higher customer satisfaction. Some report resolving calls 50% faster and doubling first-contact resolution rates.
