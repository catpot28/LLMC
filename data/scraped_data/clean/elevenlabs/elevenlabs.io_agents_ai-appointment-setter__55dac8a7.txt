Frequently asked questions
What is an AI appointment setter?
An AI appointment setter is a conversational AI agent that contacts leads, qualifies their intent, and books meetings directly into your calendar. Across voice, SMS, WhatsApp, or chat. Without requiring a human rep to be available.
How does AI appointment scheduling work?
When a lead submits a form or calls in, the AI agent engages them in real time, asks qualifying questions, checks calendar availability, and confirms a booking. Then logs the outcome in your CRM automatically.
Which calendars and CRMs does it integrate with?
ElevenAgents connects with Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly, Salesforce, HubSpot, and most other CRM and scheduling systems via native integrations or API.
Can the AI appointment setter make outbound calls?
Yes. You can configure outbound voice campaigns to follow up on cold lists, re-engage stale leads, or recover no-shows. In the prospect's preferred language across 70+ supported languages.
Is AI appointment setting compliant with HIPAA and GDPR?
ElevenAgents is SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS Level 1, and GDPR certified. You can enable zero retention mode, choose regional data residency, and encrypt conversations end to end.
How quickly can I deploy an AI-powered appointment setter?
Most teams go from prototype to live agent in days using the ElevenAgents no-code builder. For custom integrations, Forward Deployed Engineers work with your team from day one to accelerate deployment.
