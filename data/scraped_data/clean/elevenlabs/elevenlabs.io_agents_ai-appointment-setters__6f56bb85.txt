Frequently asked questions
How do AI appointment setters integrate with my systems?
ElevenLabs agents connect directly to your CRM, calendar, and communication tools via API or native integrations. They can automatically log leads, update contact records, and manage schedules in real time.
Can these AI agents make outbound calls?
Yes. They can run compliant, large-scale outbound campaigns — calling, qualifying, and booking appointments at scale while transferring complex cases to human reps instantly.
How natural do the AI voices sound?
Our agents use ElevenLabs’ industry-leading voice synthesis, trained for human-like tone, pacing, and emotion. Many clients report higher engagement and satisfaction versus traditional bots.
Are these AI agents multilingual?
Yes. Agents detect and switch languages in real time, ideal for global teams managing leads across regions.
Is the system secure and compliant?
Absolutely. ElevenLabs provides enterprise-grade security with encryption, zero-retention options, and full compliance with SOC 2, GDPR, and HIPAA standards.
