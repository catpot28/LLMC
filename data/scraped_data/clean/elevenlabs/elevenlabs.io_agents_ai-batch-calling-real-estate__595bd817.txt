How realistic do the AI batch calls sound?
ElevenLabs voice agents use industry-leading voice synthesis and emotional intelligence to sound completely natural – matching tone, pace, and empathy for local markets.

Yes. The platform includes TCPA, regional DNC, and consent controls, ensuring all batch calling campaigns remain compliant across U.S. and international jurisdictions.
