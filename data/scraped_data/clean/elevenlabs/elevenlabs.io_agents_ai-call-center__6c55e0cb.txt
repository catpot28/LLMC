AI call center automation is transforming how enterprises handle millions of customer conversations. ElevenLabs voice agents sound natural, act with precision, and integrate seamlessly into your workflows. They cut costs, reduce wait times, and improve customer satisfaction while giving human teams more time to focus on complex, high-value interactions.
"With ElevenLabs as our technology partner, we're creating a new standard where every interaction builds confidence. Our voice AI solution is cutting resolution times in half and boosting customer satisfaction."

Head of AI and Innovation, CARS24
Automate inbound calls at scale
Handle high-volume contact center interactions with AI agents that are always on
Resolve routine queries instantly
AI agents deflect common calls like order tracking, billing, and password resets in seconds. This lowers cost per resolution while maintaining service levels during peak hours.
Deliver consistent, multilingual support
With sub-second latency and real-time language switching, agents provide accurate, empathetic answers 24/7, across 72+ languages.
Free up staff for complex cases
By automating the majority of inbound traffic, agents give your team bandwidth to handle nuanced conversations that require human judgment.
Drive revenue with outbound AI
Automate collections, sales, and verifications with compliant, human-like voice agents
Scale outbound engagement cost-effectively
Run thousands of outbound calls in parallel for lead qualification, renewals, or vendor outreach. Batch calling and voicemail detection reduce cost per lead.
Automate compliant collections
AI agents verify identity, negotiate payments, and log outcomes in your CRM while following compliant scripts and guardrails. This accelerates recovery and lowers cost-to-collect.
Enable seamless handoff
When high-intent or complex calls arise, agents transfer to humans with full context, ensuring nothing is lost.
Monitor, optimize, and stay compliant
Enterprise-ready analytics, training, and governance for AI-powered contact centers
Track performance in real time
Dashboards show agent accuracy, task completion, and customer experience so you can optimize quickly.
Train agents and staff with role-play simulations
AI role-play agents help onboard new hires faster and reinforce compliance standards before going live.
Deploy with enterprise-grade security
Data is encrypted end-to-end, with support for SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, EU data residency, and zero-retention modes.

Frequently asked questions
How natural does the AI voice sound? Can it match our brand voice?
ElevenLabs voices are built to sound indistinguishable from humans, with emotional nuance, pacing, and multilingual support. You can also clone your brand’s voice for consistent customer experience.
Which languages are supported?
Our AI call center agents operate in 32+ languages with real-time language detection and switching, ideal for global customer bases.
How secure is customer data?
Security is enterprise-grade. We offer SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance, plus options for EU-only data residency and zero-retention configurations.
What integrations are supported?
Agents connect out of the box with CRMs, CCaaS, telephony, and workflow tools like Salesforce, Zendesk, Twilio, and more.
How quickly can we get started?
Most deployments begin delivering value in days, not months. Our workflow tools and SDKs allow fast integration into your existing systems.
