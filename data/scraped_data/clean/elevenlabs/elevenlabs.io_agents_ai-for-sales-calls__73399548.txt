Frequently asked questions
How does conversational AI handle cold-calling objections?
Our agent-assist surfaces real-time objection handling scripts and next-best-action suggestions, ensuring reps respond with confidence.
What languages and voices are supported?
ElevenLabs agents operate in 70+ languages with natural, human-like voices. Agents can switch languages mid-call to adapt to global prospects.
How do reps receive AI support during live calls?
Reps see an assist dashboard with transcripts, prompts, and suggested actions in real time—without disrupting the natural flow of the conversation.
Does the platform record and summarize every sales call?
Yes. Every call is transcribed, summarized, and synced to your CRM automatically, including follow-up actions and sentiment insights.
How do you ensure compliance with sales call regulations?
ElevenLabs enforces TCPA-safe calling workflows and provides SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance options. Enterprise clients can configure data residency and retention policies.
