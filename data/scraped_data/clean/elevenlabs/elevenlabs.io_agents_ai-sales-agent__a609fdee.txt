Frequently asked questions
How long does it take to launch an AI call agent?
Most agents go live in under three weeks. Prebuilt templates for sales, support, and scheduling speed up deployment.
Can I use my existing phone numbers and telephony system?
Yes. ElevenLabs integrates with SIP trunking, Twilio, Genesys, and 200+ providers to work with your current setup.
Will AI call agents replace my human team?
No. They handle repetitive, high-volume interactions, freeing staff to focus on high-value conversations.
What languages are supported?
Agents support 32+ languages with automatic in-conversation switching, ensuring global reach.
How much does it cost?
Pricing is usage-based, starting from per-minute rates for outbound and inbound calls. Contact sales for an ROI estimate tailored to your call volumes.
How do AI agents ensure compliance and security?
AI call agents can be configured to follow calling regulations like TCPA and GDPR, and enterprise deployments include SOC 2, HIPAA, and EU data residency support. Features like zero-retention mode and encrypted storage ensure sensitive data is secure.
Do AI agents replace human call center staff?
Not entirely. They’re designed to handle repetitive, high-volume interactions and free humans for complex or sensitive conversations. Businesses often see the best results with a hybrid model — AI for scale and humans for nuance.
How quickly can I deploy an AI call agent?
Most teams launch in days. With prebuilt integrations and low-code workflow tools, you can set up both inbound and outbound call flows quickly, then scale up to thousands of concurrent calls.
What ROI can I expect from AI call agents?
Companies typically see faster response times, reduced cost per call, and higher customer satisfaction. Some report 66% lower call costs, 60% more efficient collections, and major boosts in conversion rates.
