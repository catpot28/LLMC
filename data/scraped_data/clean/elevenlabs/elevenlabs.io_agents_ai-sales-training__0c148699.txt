Frequently asked questions
What is AI sales training?
AI sales training uses artificial intelligence to help sales reps practice conversations, receive personalized feedback, and identify skill gaps at scale. Instead of relying on manager roleplays or classroom sessions, reps train on-demand with AI buyer personas and get instant coaching on their pitch, discovery, and objection handling.
How is AI sales training different from traditional sales training?
Traditional training is typically one-size-fits-all, manager-dependent, and hard to scale. And reps forget most of it within 90 days. AI sales training delivers personalized practice and feedback continuously, so skills compound rather than decay. It also gives managers data on exactly where each rep is weak.
How realistic are the AI roleplays?
The AI buyer personas are trained on real sales call data and your company's ICP, objections, and messaging. They push back, ask follow-up questions, go off-script, and adapt based on how the rep performs, mimicking how a real prospect would behave on a live call.
Can I customize the AI to match our product, personas, and sales methodology?
Yes. You upload your product docs, call recordings, ICP definitions, and qualification framework (MEDDIC, SPIN, BANT, Challenger, or custom). The AI generates personas and scenarios specific to your business and grades reps against your playbook.
What skills can reps practice?
Cold calling, discovery, qualification, demo delivery, objection handling, negotiation, closing, upsell/cross-sell conversations, and handoff calls. You can also build drills for product knowledge checks and competitive positioning.
Does this replace human sales managers?
No. AI sales training handles the repetitive, scalable parts of coaching so managers can focus on high-impact 1:1s, strategy, and deal reviews. Teams typically see managers spending more quality coaching time, not less.
Can reps practice on mobile?
Yes. Browser and mobile are both supported, including voice roleplays so reps can practice cold calls hands-free between meetings.
