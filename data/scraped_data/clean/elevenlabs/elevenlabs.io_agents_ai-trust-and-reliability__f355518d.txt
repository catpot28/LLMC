Frequently asked questions
How do ElevenLabs Agents deliver reliable, predictable behavior in production?
Our platform allows you to define, test, and validate agent behavior before launch. You can run simulations of potential real-world conversations, assessing whether agents abide by guardrails, and confirm compliance before deploying agents in production. Explore how testing works
here
How can I evaluate whether my agents are performing as intended after launch?
Use our built-in evaluation and analysis tools to measure success against defined criteria like task completion, accuracy, or conversation outcomes. You can iterate quickly based on real user data to continuously optimize performance. Learn more about agent evaluation
here
What visibility do I have into how my agents perform?
Our dashboard and analytics suite provides full visibility into every interaction. Track metrics like latency, success rates, cost per call in real time. Use these insights to refine flows, improve CX, and maintain full operational transparency. Learn more about the analytics dashboard
here
Which enterprise security and compliance standards do ElevenLabs Agents meet?
ElevenLabs is SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and PCI DSS Level 1 certified, with HIPAA and GDPR attestations. You can configure regional data residency, apply Zero Retention Mode, and control data access at a granular level. Review our latest compliance overview
here
What support does ElevenLabs provide for enterprise deployment and scaling?
We offer a Forward Deployed Engineers service to large enterprises that need to design, test, and scale conversational agents safely. Our experts work alongside your teams to configure guardrails, integrate systems, and ensure compliant, reliable performance at scale. Please inquire about these services with our sales team to evaluate mutual fit.
