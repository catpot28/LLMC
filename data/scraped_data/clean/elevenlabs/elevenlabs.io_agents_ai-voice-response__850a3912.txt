Frequently asked questions
What is AI voice response?
AI voice response uses artificial intelligence to automatically answer and handle phone calls using lifelike speech. ElevenLabs’ solution goes further — understanding context, resolving issues, and taking actions in real time.
Can caller AI handle complex conversations?
Yes. Our agents combine natural language understanding with tool integrations to perform real tasks, from checking order status to scheduling appointments.
How secure is ElevenLabs caller AI?
Our platform supports SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance with optional EU data residency and zero-retention modes for maximum data protection.
Can the AI switch between languages during a call?
Absolutely. ElevenLabs caller AI automatically detects and switches languages mid-conversation — ideal for global customer support lines.
How quickly can we deploy an AI voice agent?
Most businesses launch in days, not months. Our SDKs, APIs, and guided onboarding make it simple to test, integrate, and scale instantly.
