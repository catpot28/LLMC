Frequently asked questions
What is an AI-powered attorney answering service?
An AI-powered attorney answering service uses conversational voice agents to handle inbound calls on behalf of a law firm. Qualifying leads, collecting intake details, scheduling consultations, and escalating urgent matters. Unlike traditional answering services, AI agents are available 24/7 with no per-call staffing cost and can operate across voice, chat, and SMS from a single configuration.
Can the AI agent handle client intake and conflict screening?
Yes. Agents can be configured to walk callers through structured intake questions. Collecting case details, contact information, and conflict-check data before the first attorney interaction. Details are logged directly to your CRM or practice management system in real time.
Is the platform HIPAA-compliant?
ElevenAgents supports HIPAA-ready deployments with a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) available, zero retention mode for sensitive data, end-to-end encryption, and regional data residency options. For deployments involving protected health information, we recommend enabling zero retention mode and reviewing your configuration with our compliance team.
Which legal software integrations are supported?
ElevenAgents connects to practice management and scheduling tools including Clio, MyCase, Calendly, and other platforms via API. The platform also integrates with CRM systems and supports custom integrations through MCP and our REST API.
What languages does the AI attorney answering service support?
ElevenAgents supports 70+ languages with consistent voice quality and natural tone across all of them. This allows law firms to serve diverse client populations without separate staffing or vendor arrangements for multilingual coverage.
How quickly can a law firm get started?
Most firms can configure and deploy a basic AI-powered answering agent within a day using the ElevenAgents platform. More complex deployments. Such as multi-attorney routing, CRM integration, or custom intake flows. Are supported by our Forward Deployed Engineers from day one.
