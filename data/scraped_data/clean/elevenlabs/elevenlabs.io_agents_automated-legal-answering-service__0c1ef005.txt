Frequently asked questions
How does an automated legal answering service work?
Our AI receptionists answer and manage calls in real time using advanced natural language understanding. They capture details, route calls, schedule appointments, and log every interaction automatically into your system.
Can AI handle sensitive or complex legal questions?
Agents are designed to gather facts, not provide legal advice. They follow your firm’s approved scripts, escalate when needed, and ensure compliance by routing sensitive inquiries to the right attorney.
Does the system integrate with existing tools?
Yes. ElevenLabs Agents integrate seamlessly with popular practice management platforms such as Clio, MyCase, and PracticePanther, as well as your CRM and scheduling tools.
Is client data secure?
Absolutely. ElevenLabs meets enterprise-grade compliance standards including SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR, with end-to-end encryption and optional zero-retention configurations.
How quickly can we deploy it?
Most firms are live within days. Our setup process connects your telephony, CRM, and intake scripts, and we help you train the agent to mirror your firm’s tone and workflow.
