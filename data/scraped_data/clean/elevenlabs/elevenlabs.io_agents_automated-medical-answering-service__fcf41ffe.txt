Frequently asked questions
Is the automated medical answering service HIPAA compliant?
Yes. ElevenLabs’ platform is fully HIPAA and HITECH compliant, with encryption, audit trails, and optional zero-data-retention for strict privacy control.
Can it integrate with my EHR or practice management system?
Yes. Our agents connect to leading systems like Epic, Cerner, and Athenahealth via secure APIs to read and update appointments, patient notes, and call logs.
How does the AI differentiate between urgent and routine calls?
The voice agent uses intent recognition and sentiment analysis to determine urgency. Emergency or high-priority calls are automatically escalated to on-call clinicians or routed per your triage policy.
What languages does it support?
Agents can detect and switch between 72+ languages in real time, making it ideal for multilingual practices or global telehealth providers.
How quickly can we deploy?
Most healthcare organizations go live within two weeks. ElevenLabs provides guided setup, compliance validation, and full integration support.
