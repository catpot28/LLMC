Frequently asked questions
What languages does the bilingual answering service support?
We currently support 70+ languages, including English, Spanish, French, Mandarin, and Portuguese. Our automatic language detection allows agents to switch mid-conversation without human intervention.
Can the service integrate with my existing systems?
Yes. ElevenLabs agents integrate with CRMs, scheduling, and telephony platforms like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Twilio to ensure seamless data flow and message delivery.
How fast can we get started?
Most clients launch in under a week. Our onboarding team helps design call flows, upload scripts, and connect systems for instant deployment.
Is the service suitable for regulated industries?
Absolutely. ElevenLabs meets enterprise-grade compliance standards, including SOC 2, GDPR, and HIPAA, with optional data residency and zero-retention configurations.
What’s the business impact of implementing bilingual answering?
Companies using our bilingual agents typically see a 25–35 % increase in lead capture, 40 % faster resolution times, and a measurable boost in customer satisfaction.
