Frequently asked questions
How does the bilingual legal answering service work?
Your firm’s calls are routed to an AI voice agent that greets the caller in their language, qualifies their case, and captures all intake information. Integrations with your CRM and calendar ensure nothing is lost or delayed.
What languages are supported beyond Spanish and English?
Our agents can be deployed in 72+ languages, including Portuguese, Mandarin, and French, with automatic language detection mid-conversation.
How do you ensure compliance with legal and data regulations?
We meet enterprise standards including SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with optional zero-retention and EU residency configurations.
Can I use my existing phone number?
Yes. You can forward your existing number to your AI answering line or integrate the agent directly into your phone system via SIP trunking.
How quickly can I launch?
Most firms go live within days. We’ll help configure your agent, connect your tools, and test call flows so your bilingual answering service is ready immediately.
