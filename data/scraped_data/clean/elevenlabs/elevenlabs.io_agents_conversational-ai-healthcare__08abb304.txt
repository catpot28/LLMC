Frequently asked questions
What is conversational AI in healthcare?
Conversational AI in healthcare refers to AI-powered systems that understand and respond to natural speech or text in real time. ElevenLabs Voice AI Agents apply this technology to automate patient communication, triage, scheduling, and documentation—reducing administrative burden and improving access to care.
How is voice AI different from chatbots?
Chatbots rely on text and pre-scripted logic, while voice AI agents understand speech, tone, and intent. ElevenLabs Voice AI Agents enable natural, two-way voice conversations that handle complex medical workflows securely and with empathy.
Are ElevenLabs Voice AI Agents HIPAA compliant?
Yes. ElevenLabs supports HIPAA, GDPR, and EU data residency requirements. Deployments can run on private clouds or on-premise with zero-retention data options to ensure complete control over patient information.
Can the AI agent integrate with EHR and call systems?
Yes. Voice AI Agents integrate seamlessly with leading EHRs, IVR systems, and telephony platforms to streamline workflows. This allows for real-time updates, scheduling, and call transfers with full context.
What results can healthcare providers expect?
Organizations using conversational AI in healthcare typically report a 40–60% reduction in call handling costs, up to 35% faster scheduling, and improved patient satisfaction scores of 20–30%. These gains free clinical teams to focus on care quality, not administration.
