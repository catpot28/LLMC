Frequently asked questions
How do I create an agent from our SOPs and documentation?
Upload your SOPs, policies, and help desk content, and ElevenAgents generates a custom support agent you can deploy and refine in minutes.
Is the platform suitable for non-technical team members?
Yes it is - non-technical support managers can configure, deploy and improve agents without code. They can give direct instructions like “handle this scenario like this next time” or “add this to the knowledge base.” The system uses these updates to improve responses and workflow decisions. ElevenAgents for Support removes the engineering lift to deploy and continuously improve conversational agents.
What channels does ElevenAgents for Support cover?
Deploy on phone (SIP or native telephony) and digital channels - website, apps, email, and WhatsApp - for a consistent experience everywhere.
How do human handoffs work?
You define escalation rules for complex cases, then hand off to humans with complete history - synced to your CCaaS, CRM, and ticketing stack.
