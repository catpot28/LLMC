Frequently asked questions
What makes ElevenLabs an enterprise conversational AI platform?
Our platform combines generative AI, low-latency voice synthesis, and RAG retrieval to power real-time voice and chat agents that integrate directly into enterprise systems with full governance and security controls.
Which enterprise use cases benefit most from conversational AI?
Common deployments include customer support, outbound collections, lead qualification, employee training, and compliance monitoring. Each delivers measurable ROI through cost savings, improved CSAT, and faster resolution times.
How is data privacy and compliance managed?
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. ElevenLabs supports EU data residency, zero-data-retention configurations, and enterprise SLAs to ensure compliance with regulatory frameworks like SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR.
Can I use my own models or data?
Yes. Bring your preferred LLM (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, or custom) and connect your internal data sources through our RAG framework for grounded, accurate responses.
What kind of business outcomes can enterprises expect?
Clients have reported up to 66% reductions in cost per call, 35% higher first-visit conversions, and 25% improvements in customer satisfaction from deploying AI voice agents at scale.
