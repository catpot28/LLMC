Frequently asked questions
How do agents remain compliant with finance regulations?
Agents enforce configured guardrails and deterministic checks, log every decision for audit, and can be tested via large-scale simulations to validate adherence to policies.
Can ElevenAgents access our core systems?
Yes. We integrate with CRMs, core banking, claims platforms, and telephony via secure connectors so agents act on authoritative data and write back actions where appropriate.
How do you measure ROI?
We track task completion, deflection rate, resolution time, and revenue impact (for example product issuances or recoveries) so you can measure cost reduction and revenue lift.
What about data residency and security?
We support regional data residency, encryption in transit and at rest, role-based permissions, and options for zero retention to meet stricter regulatory needs.
Do you support outbound campaigns like collections or sales?
Yes. Agents can run compliant outbound campaigns at scale with staged dialing, voicemail detection, and dynamic scripts that adapt to caller responses.
