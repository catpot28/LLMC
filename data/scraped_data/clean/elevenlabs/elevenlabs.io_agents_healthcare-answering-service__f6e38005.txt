Frequently asked questions
How does the AI healthcare answering service comply with HIPAA?
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. ElevenLabs agents operate in secure environments with support for HIPAA, SOC 2, and HITRUST compliance. You can also enable zero-retention modes for additional privacy.
Can it integrate with Epic, Cerner, or my EHR system?
Yes. ElevenLabs agents connect to major EHR and HIS platforms to read and update patient data, schedule appointments, and log call details securely.
Does the service replace human operators?
No. Our agents complement your team by automating routine calls and forwarding complex or sensitive conversations to staff with full context.
What languages does the service support?
Agents detect and switch between over 30 languages in real time, supporting multilingual hospitals and cross-border telehealth providers.
How quickly can I deploy the service?
Most healthcare organizations go live within days. Our onboarding includes integration setup, testing, and compliance review to ensure a seamless launch.
