Frequently asked questions
Is ElevenAgents HIPAA compliant?
We support HIPAA-ready deployments, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logs, role-based access, and regional data residency options. We work with customers to meet specific compliance and contractual requirements.
Can agents integrate with our EHR or billing system?
Yes. We provide connectors, APIs, and webhook support to read and write contextual patient data, verify eligibility, update appointments, and push billing events to your RCM stack.
How do you ensure clinical safety?
Agents use deterministic workflows, pre-launch simulations, approval gates, and full conversation logs so clinical teams can validate behavior and tune escalation rules before going live.
Can we use voice agents for dental and therapy workflows?
Yes. We deploy voice-first virtual receptionists and simulated patient interactions for dental triage, therapy screening, medication management, and after-hours outreach while preserving clinical handoffs.
