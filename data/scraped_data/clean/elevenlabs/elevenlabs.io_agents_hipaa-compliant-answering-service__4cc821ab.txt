Frequently asked questions
Is ElevenLabs’ AI answering service fully HIPAA compliant?
Yes. ElevenLabs meets HIPAA, SOC 2, and GDPR compliance standards. We sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) and enforce encryption at every stage of data handling.
Can this service integrate with our EHR or scheduling software?
Absolutely. ElevenLabs AI connects with leading healthcare systems via secure APIs, syncing call logs, appointments, and patient data directly to your existing platforms.
How does the AI differentiate between urgent and non-urgent patient calls?
Our AI uses natural-language understanding and real-time sentiment analysis to assess urgency, escalate critical cases, and route routine calls automatically.
Can we customize scripts and escalation rules?
Yes. You can define call flows, custom scripts, escalation triggers, and message templates—all within a visual workflow builder designed for healthcare teams.
How quickly can we deploy the service?
Most healthcare providers go live in days, not months. ElevenLabs offers guided setup, data compliance verification, and full integration support from day one.
