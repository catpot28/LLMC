Frequently asked questions
How do agents connect to our property management system?
We provide prebuilt connectors and APIs for common PMS and booking engines. Integrations keep inventory, rates, and guest profiles synchronized - preventing conflicts and enabling accurate transaction handling.
Can agents handle restaurant bookings and POS interactions?
Yes. Agents integrate with restaurant reservation systems and POS to confirm availability, place orders, and reconcile bills while preserving transaction integrity.
How quickly can we launch an agent across hotels or properties?
You can launch a pilot in weeks. We prioritize core flows - check-in, reservations, and guest requests - then scale across properties with templates, region-specific voices, and policy guardrails.
What security and compliance controls are available?
ElevenAgents supports encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, audit logs, and options for regional data residency. We also provide validation tooling to enforce payment, privacy, and local regulatory requirements.
Do non-technical teams need engineering support?
No. Operations and guest experience teams can configure and improve agents via our UI. Engineering support is available for deeper integrations and custom workflows.
