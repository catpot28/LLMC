Frequently asked questions
How fast can I set up a law firm answering service?
Most firms are fully live within days. We help configure your scripts, integrations, and security settings so your AI agent performs accurately from day one.
Can the AI handle conflict checks and legal intake?
Yes. Agents can ask screening questions, capture required data, and flag potential conflicts for human review before scheduling consultations.
What integrations are supported?
We support legal tools like Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, and Zapier, as well as calendar, CRM, and payment systems.
How is client data protected?
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You can enable zero-retention mode, EU data residency, or on-premise options for maximum compliance.
What if I still need a human receptionist sometimes?
You can set hybrid routing so calls can escalate to your human team when needed. The AI passes full context and call history so no information is lost.
