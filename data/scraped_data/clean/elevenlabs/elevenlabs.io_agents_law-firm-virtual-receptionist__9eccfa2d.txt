Frequently asked questions
What is a virtual receptionist for law firms?
A virtual receptionist is an AI or human-assisted system that handles incoming calls, schedules appointments, and manages intake for your firm. ElevenLabs’ AI receptionists use advanced voice intelligence to deliver natural, human-like conversations – available 24/7.
Can I train the AI on my firm’s specific intake process?
Yes. You can upload your scripts, FAQs, and intake questions directly. The system adapts to your firm’s tone and terminology to ensure every interaction feels on-brand.
Is it compliant with legal and privacy regulations?
Absolutely. ElevenLabs Agents support SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance and can be deployed in secure environments to meet your jurisdiction’s requirements.
Does it integrate with our current software?
Yes. ElevenLabs integrates with major legal CRMs, scheduling, and telephony platforms like Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, Calendly, and Twilio.
How quickly can we get started?
Most firms go live in under a week. Our team handles onboarding, script optimization, and integration – so your AI receptionist is ready to take calls almost immediately.
