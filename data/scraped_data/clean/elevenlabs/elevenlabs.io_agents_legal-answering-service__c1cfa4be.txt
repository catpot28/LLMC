Frequently asked questions
What is an ai-powered legal answering service?
An ai-powered legal answering service uses voice AI agents to answer calls, capture client information, schedule consultations, and route matters to the right attorney. 24/7, without human receptionists. ElevenAgents handles intake, triage, and follow-up across phone, chat, and other channels, with responses that sound natural and adapt to the caller's tone.
Can ElevenAgents handle sensitive legal inquiries securely?
Yes. ElevenAgents is built with enterprise-grade data protection including SOC 2 Type II certification, end-to-end encryption, zero retention mode, and configurable data residency options. Sensitive client information shared during intake calls can be handled without long-term storage, depending on your configuration.
Which legal practice management tools does ElevenAgents integrate with?
ElevenAgents connects to case management platforms like Clio, MyCase, and PracticePanther, as well as CRM and calendar tools. Call summaries, intake records, and scheduling updates sync automatically after each conversation.
Can I use my own language model or bring my own AI provider?
Yes. ElevenAgents is LLM agnostic. You can use ElevenLabs-hosted models for ultra-low latency, or connect your preferred provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google. You can also fine-tune models on legal terminology and your firm's specific intake workflows.
How quickly can a law firm deploy an ai-powered answering service?
Most firms can go from configuration to live calls in a matter of hours using the no-code agent builder. You can upload your intake scripts, connect your practice management tools, and set routing rules without engineering resources. For more complex deployments, our Forward Deployed Engineers are available from day one.
Does ElevenAgents support multilingual legal intake?
Yes. ElevenAgents supports 70+ languages, enabling law firms to serve clients in their preferred language with consistent tone and clarity. This is particularly valuable for immigration, family law, and personal injury practices serving diverse communities.
How does the ai-powered answering service handle after-hours calls?
ElevenAgents operates continuously. 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. Callers receive the same professional experience regardless of when they call, with intake data routed to the appropriate attorney or paralegal for follow-up the next business day or sooner if marked urgent.
