Frequently asked questions
Is ElevenAgents HIPAA compliant?
Yes. ElevenLabs offers a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and is HDS certified. All agent interactions can be configured with zero retention mode, end-to-end encryption, and VPC deployment to keep patient health information secure.
Can the AI answer calls after hours and on weekends?
Yes. AI-powered voice reception operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Patients always reach a live, human-sounding agent. Whether it is 2am or a public holiday.
How does the agent handle urgent or emergency calls?
You define the escalation rules. When a patient describes an urgent situation, the agent can immediately transfer the call to an on-call provider or emergency line, passing along full conversation context.
Which EHR and scheduling systems does ElevenAgents integrate with?
ElevenAgents connects to your existing systems via our integration library and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), supporting EHR platforms, practice management systems, and CCaaS providers. Custom API integrations are also supported.
Can I customize the voice to match my practice's brand?
Yes. You can choose from 10,000+ expressive voices or clone your own. Pronunciation dictionaries let you fine-tune how the agent says medical terms, provider names, and practice-specific language.
What languages does the medical answering service support?
ElevenAgents supports 70+ languages. Agents can detect a patient's language and respond accordingly. Serving diverse patient populations without requiring bilingual staff.
