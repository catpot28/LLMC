Frequently asked questions
What is a medical office answering service?
A medical office answering service handles patient calls, messages, and scheduling for clinics and healthcare practices. ElevenLabs’ AI-powered service replaces manual reception tasks with intelligent, natural-sounding agents available 24/7.
Is the ElevenLabs medical answering service HIPAA-compliant?
Yes. Our platform supports HIPAA, SOC 2, and GDPR compliance with full encryption, data residency options, and zero-retention configurations.
Can the AI handle scheduling and patient intake?
Absolutely. Agents integrate with your scheduling system to book, modify, and confirm appointments, and can capture patient intake information securely before visits.
How does integration with my EMR work?
We connect your agent directly to your EMR or practice management system via API. This enables secure data exchange, ensuring calls, notes, and updates sync automatically.
Will AI replace my front-desk staff?
No. ElevenLabs agents complement your staff by handling routine calls and data entry. Your human team remains focused on patient care, complex inquiries, and in-office experiences.
