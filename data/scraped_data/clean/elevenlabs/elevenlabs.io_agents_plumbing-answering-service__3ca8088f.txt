Frequently asked questions
How does an AI-powered plumbing answering service handle emergency calls?
When a customer calls with an urgent issue. A burst pipe, flooding, or a pressure failure. The agent asks the right qualifying questions, flags the call as an emergency, and routes it to your on-call technician immediately. No hold music, no voicemail.
Can I use my own voice or brand persona?
Yes. You can clone your own voice or design a custom brand persona using ElevenAgents. Customers will hear a consistent, professional voice that sounds like your business. Not a generic answering service.
What scheduling tools does ElevenAgents integrate with?
ElevenAgents connects with Google Calendar, ServiceTitan, Jobber, and other field service and CRM tools via API. Calls convert to confirmed appointments automatically, with job details synced directly to your system.
What happens after each call?
After every call, your team receives a full transcript and a summary of key details. Job type, customer contact information, urgency level, and any notes. Delivered via SMS or email.
Does the answering service work after hours and on weekends?
Yes. AI agents are available 24/7, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays. Coverage never drops. Even during peak season or when your front desk is unavailable.
Can the agent handle calls in languages other than English?
Yes. ElevenAgents supports 70+ languages with consistent tone and clarity. If your service area includes Spanish-speaking, Portuguese-speaking, or other communities, the agent can handle those calls without a separate setup.
How do I know it's secure?
ElevenLabs is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. You can enable Zero Retention Mode so call data is never stored, and you can configure regional data residency to meet your compliance requirements.
