Frequently asked questions
What is an ai-powered property management answering service?
An ai-powered property management answering service uses conversational AI voice agents to handle inbound tenant calls 24/7. Covering maintenance requests, leasing inquiries, emergency triage, and more. Unlike traditional call centers, AI agents scale instantly, follow your exact scripts and routing rules, and integrate directly with your property management software.
Can the AI handle emergency calls?
Yes. Agents detect urgency from the content and tone of a call, escalate genuine emergencies like flooding or lockouts to your on-call team immediately, and route non-urgent requests to the appropriate queue. Reducing unnecessary escalations and costly delays.
Which property management systems does it integrate with?
ElevenAgents connects to your existing tools via API and MCP integrations. Common integrations include Yardi, AppFolio, Buildium, and leading CRM and ticketing platforms. If you use a different system, our team can work with you to build the right connection.
Does the service support multiple languages?
Yes. Agents support 70+ languages with automatic language detection and real-time switching. Allowing you to serve diverse tenant communities without separate workflows or additional staffing.
How is tenant data protected?
ElevenLabs is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. All deployments support end-to-end encryption, zero retention mode, and regional data residency (US and EU). Role-based access controls and full audit trails are included for enterprise deployments.
How quickly can I deploy?
You can build and launch your first agent directly from the ElevenAgents platform. No engineering required. For more complex integrations or enterprise rollouts, our Forward Deployed Engineers work with your team from day one to configure and go live as fast as possible.
