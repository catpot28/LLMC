Frequently asked questions
How does the ElevenLabs real estate answering service work?
Our AI agents handle incoming calls and chats from potential clients, qualify leads, and schedule appointments directly through your CRM or calendar. They speak naturally, follow your scripts, and can escalate to you or your team when human input is needed.
Can the answering agent integrate with my CRM or scheduling tool?
Yes. ElevenLabs integrates with major CRMs like HubSpot, Salesforce, and Follow Up Boss, as well as calendar tools such as Google Calendar or Outlook – ensuring full workflow continuity.
Is this AI answering service suitable for small brokerages or solo agents?
Absolutely. Whether you manage a large brokerage or operate independently, ElevenLabs scales with your call volume. You can start small and expand as your business grows.
Does it support multiple property listings or offices?
Yes. Agents can manage calls for multiple listings, offices, or team members, routing inquiries to the right person automatically or tagging them by property ID.
How quickly can I get started?
You can launch your AI real estate answering agent in days. Once configured with your FAQs, listings, and call flows, it’s ready to handle real-world conversations immediately.
