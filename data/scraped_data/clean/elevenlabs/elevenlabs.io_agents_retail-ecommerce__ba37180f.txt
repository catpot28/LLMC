Frequently asked questions
How does an agent confirm an order?
Agents connect to your order management and payment systems to validate transactions, reserve inventory, and send a branded confirmation via the customer's preferred channel.
Can ElevenAgents complete purchases or process refunds?
Yes - with integrations to payment gateways and your OMS, agents can execute purchases, authorize refunds, and log actions under deterministic workflows configured by your team.
How do you ensure agents follow pricing and promotion rules?
We provide pre-launch simulations, policy guardrails, and runtime checks so agents apply the correct prices, promotions, and legal disclosures consistently.
What security and compliance controls are available?
Our platform supports encryption in transit and at rest, SOC 2-level controls, GDPR-ready features, regional data residency, and role-based access for enterprise deployments.
