AI Transparency
At ElevenLabs, we are committed to transparency about how artificial intelligence is used in our products.
Our services may integrate or interact with third-party AI systems to provide and improve functionality. These providers currently include, and may be updated from time to time:
Google Gemini (Google LLC)
OpenAI
Anthropic via Google Vertex AI
These AI services may be used to support features such as content generation, processing, and analysis within our products.
We take steps to ensure that integrations with third-party AI providers are implemented responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy standards. For more information on how we handle personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.
If you have questions about our use of AI technologies, please contact us at
legal@elevenlabs.io
