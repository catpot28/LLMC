Frequently asked questions
What is the ElevenLabs Ambassador Program?
A program for people who want to help the world discover what is possible with ElevenLabs - by hosting events, creating content, or both. Ambassadors get early access, perks, and direct support from the ElevenLabs team.
Who can apply?
Anyone 18 or older with an active ElevenLabs account, a genuine community or audience, and a real plan to contribute.

What are the two ways to participate?
Community Builder: you organize local ElevenLabs events (meetups, hackathons, workshops, and more)
Content Creator: you produce and publish content about ElevenLabs to your audience (tutorials, demos, reviews, workflows). You can apply for both.
Do I need to already be using ElevenLabs?
Yes. We ask for your account email and to share something you have made with ElevenLabs.

What do Ambassadors get?
Perks depend on how you contribute and how active you are, but include: platform credits, early feature access, event funding, merch, support, and invitations to ElevenLabs events. This is a community program, not a paid role - though Content Creators can unlock paid opportunities over time.
Can I apply for both tracks?
Yes. If you want to host events and create content, select 'Both' in the application form. You will answer questions for each track.
How does the application process work?
Fill out the form. The form adapts based on your track. If your application moves forward, we will schedule a short 15-minute intro call.
Can I be a Content Creator Ambassador and still join the Affiliates program?
Yes. The Ambassador Program and the ElevenLabs Affiliates program are separate. You can participate in both independently.
What happens after I am accepted?
You will receive a welcome email with everything you need: your onboarding playbook, Discord invite, platform credits, and merch claim link. From there, you contribute according to your track.
How do I organize my first event as an ambassador?
Once accepted, you will receive an event playbook with all the information to start organizing your first event.
How do I get started as a Content Creator ambassador?
Once accepted, you will receive a content creation playbook with all the information to start creating your first piece of content.
