Frequently asked questions
How does the voice quality compare to traditional audiobook narration?
Our voice technology creates narration that listeners consistently describe as natural, expressive, and emotionally engaging. Major publishers use the same voices for their audiobook catalogs because the quality meets professional standards. Most listeners can't distinguish our narration from traditional recordings.
Can I hear samples before I commit?
Yes. You can preview different voices and styles before creating your audiobook to ensure you find the perfect narrator for your story.
How long does it take to create an audiobook?
Most authors go from manuscript to finished audiobook in days, depending on length and how much customization you want. Compare that to traditional production, which typically takes 3-6 months.
Can I edit the narration and fix pronunciations?
Absolutely. You have complete creative control to adjust pacing, pronunciation of character names or specialized terms, emotional tone, and emphasis until the narration sounds exactly how you envision your story.
What languages are available?
You can create audiobooks in over 90 languages, making it easy to reach global audiences with authentic-sounding narration that captures the nuances of each language.
Where can I distribute my finished audiobook?
You can download your finished files to distribute anywhere you choose, or publish directly to platforms like Spotify, inaudio, and ElevenReader through our integrated distribution.
Can I publish the same audiobook on multiple platforms?
Yes. You retain full distribution rights and can publish your audiobook on as many platforms as you'd like, with no exclusivity requirements.
What does it cost?
We offer flexible pricing based on your needs—whether you're publishing one book or building a catalog. Contact our team to discuss options that work for your publishing goals.
Do I need technical experience to use the platform?
Not at all. The platform is designed to be intuitive for authors and publishers of any technical skill level. Simply upload your manuscript, select a voice, and the platform handles the rest. If you prefer a completely hands-free experience, our producers can manage everything for you.
Who owns the rights to my audiobook?
You retain full rights to your work and the finished audiobook. We simply provide the technology and platform to help you create and distribute it.
