Bringing ElevenLabs’ omnichannel conversational agents platform to 3Shape customer support - starting with web and phone.
3Shape customers now get fast help via:
AI Web Widget:
An FAQ agent embedded across 3Shape’s core websites.
Voice AI:
A conversational agent integrated into support hotlines, featuring seamless "warm handovers" to 3Shape support specialists when needed.
Always-on conversational agents are designed to help reduce wait times, speed up resolution, and improve overall customer experience, while keeping 3Shape support experts involved in complex cases.
This initiative reflects a shared goal of making customer support faster and more consistent, without sacrificing quality.
