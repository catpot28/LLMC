At ElevenLabs, we believe voice should be an enabler in the creative process. For eLearning professionals, voice isn’t just a finishing touch. It’s a core element that brings courses to life, improves comprehension, and boosts learner engagement.
AI voices
from our
Voice Library
are now available directly in the Adobe Captivate app. Course creators can convert closed captions into lifelike narration with a library of over 150 AI voices in 10 languages, providing control over tone, style, and delivery. Whether crafting corporate training, compliance modules, or interactive lessons, course creators can produce narration that feels natural and immersive without leaving their workflow.
Early adopters report faster production cycles, more dynamic course content, and the ability to offer highly personalized audio experiences to learners.
Adobe Captivate x ElevenLabs
Captivate Users Say:
I’ve been using third-party AI voice solutions for years, but they always required extra steps. Now, with AI voices built directly into Captivate, I can generate lifelike narration without disrupting my workflow. It’s a game-changer.
We’re proud to see ElevenLabs voice technology helping drive innovation in learning platforms like Adobe Captivate. By embedding high-quality speech into the heart of the content creation process, educators and course designers can focus on what really matters – delivering powerful, accessible learning experiences to audiences around the world.
