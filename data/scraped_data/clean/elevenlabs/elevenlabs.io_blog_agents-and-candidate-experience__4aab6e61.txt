Scaling Talent at the Pace of AI
In July 2025, ElevenLabs received over 31,000 job applications from candidates from all around the world with diverse backgrounds and experiences. In the first half of 2025, we conducted 3,200 interviews and hired 143 new teammates across 39 countries. And we’re not slowing down - we aim to grow our headcount by 50% each quarter.
But scaling at this pace presents challenges. How do we identify the right person, for the right role, at the right time without compromising on quality, candidate experience, or recruiter focus?
Putting our own AI to work
Candidates often have similar questions about our interview process, benefits, remote policy, and company culture. We realised these were questions that could be answered even before the first interview, which would help candidates feel informed and engaged from the start, as well free up time for our recruiters to focus on more meaningful conversations. Here we saw an opportunity to apply our own product to the recruiting funnel itself. And so,
AI Becky
and
AI Oscar
were born - digital counterparts modelled after Becky, one of our GTM recruiters, and Oscar, one of our technical recruiters. Built using ElevenLabs, these agents are now an integral part of the interview experience, answering common questions before a candidate’s first live conversation with a recruiter.
To understand how this could
actually
help candidates, the real Becky, collaborating with the real Oscar, put this concept
out to the public on LinkedIn
. They invited candidates, recruiters, and hiring managers to chime in, asking:
"If you were interviewing at a company like ElevenLabs, what would you want to know before your first call with a recruiter?"

With this input, we set out to clone the voices and knowledge of our recruiters. Within hours, we had used our own Conversational AI platform to build, voice-clone, and deploy both agents, giving every candidate a scalable, informative, and deeply ElevenLabs-style introduction to our recruiting process.
Every day, these agents go out to dozens of candidates we invite to interview, and the initiative does more than just introduce candidates to our product and platform - it embodies a fundamental value of our company: use AI to creatively solve real problems.
Interview smarter with AI
We want every person interviewing with us to feel energized, confident, and prepared. So just as we use AI to streamline our internal operations, we encourage candidates to use it to empower your preparation.
In that spirit, here are some specific ideas you can try:
Turn AI into your research assistant:
Preparing for an interview means understanding the company inside and out. There are a number of AI tools that can become your ultimate research assistant. If you’re interested in joining a company, feed blog posts, news articles, and documents into an AI tool. Elevate your understanding of the company’s recent breakthroughs, think deeply about its strategic direction, and prepare thoughtful questions.
Listen on-the-go:
Using the
ElevenLabs Reader
app, you can convert materials into a personalized podcast. The GenFM co-hosts will summarize the content, so you can even prep on-the-go, during a walk, workout, or commute.
Run a mock interview with an AI agent:
Using our
Conversational AI
product, you can spin up an AI agent to simulate an interview with a recruiter, and give it your resume, the job description, and any other context. Practice answering tailored questions, and get feedback on your structure and response.
From how we recruit to how we prepare candidates, we believe in using AI to scale what we can achieve. The better prepared our candidates are, the better our conversations become, and the better we are as a collective team.
We're building something fast, global, and ambitious. If you’re exploring ElevenLabs, we hope AI Becky and AI Oscar can help you get started - and that you’ll bring your own creativity to the table. Let’s build the future together.
