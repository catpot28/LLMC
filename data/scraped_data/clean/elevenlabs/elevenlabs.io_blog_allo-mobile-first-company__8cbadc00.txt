Introduction
AI receptionists that are fast, multilingual, and responsive
Low-latency voice that keeps callers engaged
The new standard for
Allô
is a mobile-first business phone system for SMBs. It’s the latest app from the Mobile First Company, a Lightspeed Venture Partner backed startup building for entrepreneurs and small businesses that run their operations from a smartphone. With features like AI-driven call summaries, spam blocking, and smart routing, Allô turns any mobile device into a complete business communications hub.
AI receptionists that are fast, multilingual, and responsive
To support both French and English users, Allô relies on ElevenLabs voices—
Mylene

for France and
Tanya
for the US. These voices handle every spoken touchpoint across the system, from voicemail greetings to live AI agents.
Allô integrates ElevenLabs’
Text to Speech
and
Conversational AI
to deliver low-latency, natural-sounding voice across their telephony stack.
Text to Speech
is used for all pre-recorded messages:
Greeting and voicemail flows
Smart routing and transfer prompts
Contextual information updates during call transfers
Conversational AI
powers real-time AI agents that can answer calls on behalf of users. Each agent is configurable—with custom instructions, voice settings, and tone—and integrates directly into Allô’s SIP telephony infrastructure via web sockets.
“We started with a different vendor who over-promised and under-delivered. We were already using ElevenLabs for pre-recorded speech, so when they launched Conversational AI, we switched—and it was a dramatic improvement,” said Jérémy Goillot, CEO of Allô. “Voice latency is critical for a good experience. With ElevenLabs, we finally found voices that sound natural, respond quickly, and perform well in both English and French”.
