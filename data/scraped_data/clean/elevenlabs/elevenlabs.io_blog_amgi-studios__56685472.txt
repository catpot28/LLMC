Building at the future of gaming and animation
AMGI Studios is an independent gaming and animation technology company creating groundbreaking content and interactive animated characters. When they set out to build
The Others,
a set of several hundred unique, interactive, highly dynamic characters, they chose to partner with ElevenLabs to power the voices.
Through a combination of existing character voices in the ElevenLabs Voice Library, training new voices with recording of the AMGI Studios team, and dynamic pitch shifting, they created a completely unique set of character voices that are captivating early users. They’ve paired this with a set of custom sound effects they generated on our platform to create an immersive fan experience.
“With any amazing new technology there is a process of discovery and play. Eleven Labs gives us the tools and supports our sense of exploration. While training our own custom voices and training our own LLM models, we found a way to turbo boost the expressiveness and personalities of Eleven Labs voices to create the most animated AI driven characters anywhere.” - Colin Brady, CCO & CTO of AMGI Studios

Upcoming Launch
AMGI Studios is set to release their product this month. Early testers are impressed by the organic, character-driven voices. Combining our voice generators with AMGI’s Chatbuddy technology is setting a new standard and a fresh experience for chatbot users. AMGI plans to continue pushing the boundaries, using our tools to create even more animated and expressive AI-driven characters.
About AMGI Studios
AMGI Studios
is an independent gaming and animation technology company that lives at the intersection of gaming, technology, and storytelling. They develop and produce traditional entertainment IP, gaming, and Web3 native properties through the use of their proprietary technology, AI integration and innovations in real-time animation.
