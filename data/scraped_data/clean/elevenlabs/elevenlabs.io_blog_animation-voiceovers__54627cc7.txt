FAQs for AI Animation Voiceovers
What is
dubbing
and voiceover?
Dubbing replaces the original language in a video with another, matching the characters' lip movements. It's used in movies and TV shows for language accessibility. Voiceover adds a narrative track where the speaker isn't seen, used in documentaries and commercials for information or commentary. Both techniques adapt content for different audiences and enhance storytelling.
