After reviewing 4500 submissions (190 hours of film!) to the world's largest AI Film Festival —
Project Odyssey
— we can now finally reveal the grand prize winners of the ElevenLabs award.
We were truly staggered by the creativity and ingenuity of artists all over the world harnessing ElevenLabs, Civitai, Kling AI from Kuaishou Technology, Lambda, Viggle, Hailuo by MiniMax, Hedra, Nebius, RunPod, Higgsfield AI, ThinkDiffusion and a host of other tools to create groundbreaking new media.
Congratulations to our grand prize winner:
Pip & The Giant Clockwork

by Nicolas Bellavance.
We were captivated by the style of this intriguing film.
Pip & The Giant Clockwork
Congratulations, also, to
2nd place:
Memory Maker
by Kleverow
3rd place:
Velocium
by Calvin Herbst
and Runner Up:
Dear Grandma
by dannyt_film
There was an astonishing level of talent, craft and dedication throughout all the categories.
Thank you to everyone who created and submitted their creations.
