About Arcads
Arcads
is redefining how brands produce ads. By combining proprietary video generation with ElevenLabs voices, they’ve built the leading platform for AI UGC ad creation.
User-generated content (UGC) has long driven high-performing ads because they match the content of people’s feeds. With AI, the process is 10x faster—and with ElevenLabs, it sounds as real as ever.
Voice is critical to AI UGC
In every high-converting AI UGC ad, two elements matter most: the visuals and the voice.
Arcads tested every leading text-to-speech provider before choosing ElevenLabs. The decision was clear—our API delivers the most natural and emotionally resonant voices on the market.
“To create a highly converting AI UGC ad, you need to nail two things: the visuals and the voice. ElevenLabs is by far the best solution to create emotionally engaging
AI voices
.”

— Romain Torres, founder & CEO,
arcads.ai
Today, every Arcads AI actor is powered by ElevenLabs thanks to the wide range of voices available in ElevenLabs, more than 5000 and counting. Brands can select an AI influencer, customize its voice, and use it to generate their advertising campaigns.
More than a 1B ad impressions
Arcads isn’t just building better ads. They’re doing it at scale. Over 1 billion ad impressions have been delivered using AI UGC videos created with ElevenLabs voices. That number is growing fast as marketers shift to AI-first creative workflows. They let performance advertising teams scale their campaigns and ad production seamlessly. From product explainers to direct response, the ads can go global with more than 30 languages supported by our AI Voice models.
