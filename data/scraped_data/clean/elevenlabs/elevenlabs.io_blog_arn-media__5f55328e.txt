Thy is a new kind of radio host-created with AI, built on consent, and designed to fit into CADA’s daily programming. Developed by ARN Media, one of Australia’s largest networks, it’s part of a broader test of how synthetic voices might be used in radio without taking away what people like about it.
AI radio host
Thy
is a new radio host on Australia’s
CADA network
. She plays hip hop, runs a four-hour daily show called Workdays, and sounds just like someone you might know. Because, in a way, she is.
Thy
was created with ElevenLabs’ AI voice technology. She’s based on an ARN Media employee who works in finance and gave consent for her voice to be used. Within an hour of uploading the voice samples, the synthetic version was live.
It’s part of a broader move by ARN Media - one of Australia’s largest networks - to test how AI can open up new forms of creative expression. They’re not replacing people. They’re experimenting with new ways to connect. Thy offers a fully-automated listening experience, powered by our
Text to Speech
and
voice cloning
tools.
CADA reaches around 160,000 digital listeners.
Thy
is part of how they’re making radio more personal, without losing what makes it compelling. You can listen to her
on the CADA site
or through
iHeart Radio
.
Others are moving fast too.
Audacy
uses AI to generate ads and podcasts.
Futuri
,
SuperHiFi
and Radio.Cloud are building full-stack automation tools for stations and the number of use cases keeps growing.
If you’re thinking about how voice AI might fit into your creative work, we can help. Our
Enterprise team
supports everything from voice onboarding to live deployment.
