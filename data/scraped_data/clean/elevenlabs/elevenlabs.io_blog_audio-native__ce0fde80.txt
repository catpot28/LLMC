There’s a play button positioned just above this line you’re reading now. Press play and you can listen to a narration of this post that was automatically generated with an ElevenLabs voice. We call this embedded speech player, Audio Native.
Audio Native creates an automated, embeddable voiceover for any article, blog or newsletter. It’s customizable, easy to set up, and helps drive reader engagement while making your content accessible for readers (and listeners) around the world.
