Revolutionizing audio content creation
Audio Pitara is a podcast production company with original content in a variety of genres including History, Horror and Romance. Last year, they started exploring AI Audio solutions in the hopes of finding something that could help them achieve low production costs and quick turnaround times, while maintaining the depth of narration needed to engage fans. They chose ElevenLabs because of the quality of our Hindi
Text to Speech
and are large library of Indian voices.
Bringing "Dil se Dil tak" to life
Audio Pitara's flagship project, "
Dil se Dil tak
," is a daily podcast featuring romantic stories voiced by ElevenLabs. Initially, they kept the AI nature of the voice under wraps, concerned about potential audience reactions.
The results were astounding. Tapan Gupta, Co-Founder of Audio Pitara, shares:
"We asked our friends and people from the voicing industry for their thoughts on the podcast and the narrator's voice. To our delight, everyone loved both the story and the voice, remarking that it sounded like one of us was narrating the story. No one suspected it was an AI voice."
You can listen to Dil se Dil tak on
Audio Pitara here.

The success of "Dil se Dil tak" speaks volumes about the quality and acceptance of AI-generated voices.

The podcast was also awarded the
HT Smartcast Podmaster Award 2025
for
Best Use of AI
in a Podcast. Tapasya Gupta, Co-Founder of Audio Pitara and creator of
Dil se Dil tak
, shared:
Winning the HT Smartcast Podmaster Award 2025 for Best Use of AI in a Podcast is not just a personal milestone – it’s a validation of how creativity and technology can come together to tell powerful stories.
Dil se Dil tak with Khushi
was a bold experiment in emotionally resonant, AI-voiced storytelling. ElevenLabs’ voice technology gave our character ‘Khushi’ not only a voice, but a soul. This award belongs as much to the innovation as it does to the intent behind it.
Looking Ahead
Audio Pitara isn't stopping at "Dil se Dil tak." They're using ElevenLabs to voice audiobooks and other audio shows, with plans to expand aggressively into content creation for every age group and ethnicity.
Tapan Gupta elaborates on their vision:
"AI-enabled voice overs and dubbing are set to revolutionize the future of podcasts and audiobooks. With advancements in AI technology, we can achieve low-cost production, faster delivery, and seamless editing, making high-quality audio content more accessible than ever. This transformation not only enhances the listener's experience but also empowers creators to bring their stories to life with unprecedented efficiency and creativity."
As Audio Pitara continues to push the boundaries of what's possible with AI-generated voices, they're setting new standards in the podcasting industry. We’re proud to see AI Audio make more content accessible for global audiences, now in Hindi and soon every language and voice.
