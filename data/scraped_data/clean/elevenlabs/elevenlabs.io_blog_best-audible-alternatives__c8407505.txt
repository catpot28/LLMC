The top 5 alternatives to Audible
The audiobook market has evolved beyond Audible's credit-based model. Today's listeners want flexibility in format, pricing, and content. Here are the top platforms challenging Audible's dominance, each offering unique advantages for audiobook enthusiasts.
1.
ElevenReader
ElevenReader, a free Audible alternative, stands out by using ultra-realistic AI voices to turn any text into audio. Unlike traditional audiobook platforms, it lets you listen to PDFs, articles, newsletters, and other written content. The app supports 32 languages and includes features like focused listening with word highlighting. Its unique "GenFM" feature generates smart AI-hosted
podcasts
from your content. For independent authors, the platform offers publishing opportunities through ElevenReader Independent Publishing. The subscription includes unlimited access to the platform's tools and AI narration capabilities.
2.
Everand
A comprehensive digital reading service, Everand (formerly Scribd) offers access to over 13 million titles including audiobooks, ebooks, magazines, podcasts, and sheet music for $10.99/month. Its audiobook collection features works from major publishers and independent authors, plus exclusive originals from authors like Stephen King and Margaret Atwood. The platform excels at content discovery through editorial expertise and machine learning recommendations. All content is available across devices with offline access through their mobile apps.
3.
Spotify
Beyond its music roots, Spotify has emerged as a significant audiobook platform with over 250,000 titles available through Premium subscriptions. Members get 15 hours of audiobook listening time monthly, with the option to purchase additional time. The platform seamlessly integrates audiobooks with music and podcasts in one app. Non-Premium users can still purchase audiobooks individually from a catalog of over 375,000 titles. The service particularly shines in its cross-device syncing and offline listening capabilities.
4.
Libro.fm
Libro.fm
differentiates itself by partnering with independent bookstores. When you purchase audiobooks through their platform, a portion of the sale supports local bookstores. The service offers a similar credit-based model to Audible ($14.95/month for one credit) but provides DRM-free downloads, meaning you truly own your audiobooks. Their app features clean design, quick loading times, and seamless offline listening. Members get 30% off additional purchases.
5.
Audiobooks.com
This platform offers a straightforward subscription model with one credit monthly for $14.95, plus access to a VIP member catalog of audiobooks. The service includes free audiobooks, member-exclusive deals, and a family plan option that allows sharing with two additional users. Their mobile apps support offline listening and feature sleep timers, variable playback speeds, and bookmarking. The platform regularly runs sales and offers promotional pricing on popular titles.

languages

FAQs
How does ElevenReader compare to digital library apps and audiobook subscription services?
Unlike traditional audiobook apps or subscription services where you borrow audiobooks from a limited catalog, ElevenReader converts any text to audio. While services like Libby app or Hoopla app require a library card and have waiting lists, ElevenReader provides instant unlimited access to your own content.
Can I save money compared to a traditional audible subscription or monthly credit system?
Yes. Instead of paying per audiobook or using monthly credits like Audible members do, ElevenReader offers unlimited audio content conversion for one monthly fee. This means significant savings for audiobook lovers who consume multiple titles monthly.
Does ElevenReader work with public domain content and free alternatives?
Absolutely. You can convert texts from Project Gutenberg, local libraries, and other free sources. The platform handles everything from short stories to extensive collections, making it perfect for accessing public domain classics like A Christmas Carol or creating audio from e-books.
How does the content library compare to traditional audiobook options?
While traditional services offer fixed audiobook collections with new titles added periodically, ElevenReader gives you an unlimited number of audio creation options. Convert any document, from local business newsletters to academic papers, creating an extensive library of audio content.
What makes ElevenReader better than physical CDs or traditional audiobook formats?
ElevenReader eliminates the need for physical CDs or device-restricted digital audiobooks. With all content accessible through one free app, you can download audiobooks for offline listening across devices, without worrying about subscription plans or library waiting lists.
