Tap into the growing audiobook market
In our digital age, audiobooks have taken the literary industry by storm. Providing a practical alternative to traditional reading, their popularity has grown massively as modern life continues to get busier.
Literary connoisseurs and reading enthusiasts worldwide rely on audiobooks to entertain themselves during traveling and daily commutes, relax, or preserve healthy vision and avoid eye strain.
Likewise, readers with vision-related problems can enjoy their favorite novels and non-fiction works, making audiobooks an accessible alternative to traditional paperbacks and hardcovers - a feat that is becoming more popular thanks to the ease by which audiobooks can be generated with AI tools.
Whether you're planning on self-publishing your
AI audiobook
or partnering with publishing companies to assist you with the process, choosing the right platform for audiobook publishing and distribution can make or break audiobook sales and success.
In this article, we dive into several well-known and niche audiobook publishing and distribution platforms. Keep reading to explore your options and choose from the best audiobook publishing companies and platforms available.
The best audiobook publishing companies and platforms
With so many audiobook publishing platforms to choose from, deciding on a single platform may seem overwhelming. To help you make an informed decision, we have compiled a list of audiobook publishing platforms, from the most popular options like Amazon's Audiobook Creation Exchange (ACX) to niche options like Scribe Media. Note that their policies around use of AI narration are evolving so you should consult any platforms terms of service before uploading, particularly in regard to the use of AI narration and text to speech.
Without further ado, let's dive into the best audiobook production companies and publishing companies on the web!
Amazon's Audiobook Creation Exchange (ACX)
Amazon's Audiobook Creation Exchange (ACX) audiobook distribution platform is undeniably an industry leader. ACX connects authors, publishers, and professional narrators, providing audiobook production, publishing, and distribution services under one roof.
Pros

ACX possesses a vast market share, opening you up to a wider audience. It distributes audiobooks to Audible, Amazon, and iTunes, allowing you to access audiobook enthusiasts on all three platforms. It's the largest audiobook store on the market, with over 200,000 titles available to audiobook listeners.
Likewise, ACX allows you to upload ready audio files or produce your own audiobook directly through the platform (for instance, by assisting you with professional narration by helping you hire an ACX professional narrator).
Cons
Under their current policy
, ACX/Audible does not allow the publishing of audiobooks narrated by
AI voices
. This means you will not be able to publish your ElevenLabs audiobook there.
Although ACX has many benefits, publishing your audiobook on this platform has drawbacks. Firstly, publishing your audiobook on major audiobook platforms like ACX opens you up to significant competition.
Moreover, higher royalty rates (40%) on ACX require exclusivity, meaning your audiobooks can only be distributed to Audible, Amazon, and iTunes. If you're not exclusive with the platform, authors only receive 25%.
Findaway Voices
Another industry leader in the audiobook industry, Findaway Voices, provides all the essential services to self-published authors to reach a wide audience and boost audiobook sales. Independent audiobook publishers can find everything they need at Findaway Voices.
Pros
Findaway Voices is an excellent self-publishing platform. With no exclusivity required, the audiobook publishing platform allows you to distribute your audiobook to a broader range of retailers and reach more listeners.
Likewise, audiobook authors are given control over critical aspects such as audiobook prices. They also receive 80% of the 25% royalties from Audible, Amazon, and iTunes at a non-exclusive rate and 80% of the 40-50% royalties from other retailers.
Cons
Although Findaway Voices provides authors with a vast distribution network, it doesn't have the same visibility as platforms like Audible. Additionally, some consider the platform's pricing structure complex.
Kobo Writing Life
Kobo is a well-known Canadian ebook, audiobook, e-reader, and tablet retailer. Regarding audiobook publishing, the company also has an ebook and audiobook platform called "Kobo Writing Life" - a helpful and comprehensive platform for self-publishers and independent authors.
Pros
Kobo Writing Life may not have reached industry giant status just yet, but it's growing rapidly, generating over 150% more book sales in 2023 compared to 2022.
Regarding distribution strategy, Kobo Writing Life lists your book on the main Kobo platform, as well as Walmart, Indigo (Canada's top bookstore), and Bol.
Regarding royalties, Kobo's structure is relatively simple: 35% royalties for audiobooks priced $2.99 or lower, 45% royalties for audiobooks priced over $2.99, and 32% for specific subscription customers.
Cons
While Kobo is growing rapidly, it has a considerably smaller audiobook market share compared to industry giants like Amazon and Audible. It also has a limited audiobook catalog, and audiobook marketing and promotional tools are limited.
Google Play Books
Alongside thousands of other services, Google also offers self-publishing options for ebooks and audiobooks via Google Play Books, allowing audiobook authors to tap into a giant market.
Pros
As a Google sub-service, Google Play Books has a massive reach, especially considering that the Google Play Store comes pre-installed on every Android device. Reach customers in over 40 countries, receive 50% royalties with distributors like Publish Drive, and benefit from non-exclusive publishing.
Cons
Google may be an industry leader, but its audiobook section is smaller than that of publisher-only platforms. Regarding revenue share, some authors may prefer flat royalty rates offered by other platforms compared to Google Play Books' revenue share model. Lastly, marketing and promotional tools are also limited.
Author's Republic
Known as a user-friendly platform among audiobook enthusiasts and publishers, Author's Republic is a staple choice for audiobook publishing and distribution.
Providing access to major retailers and libraries, transparent royalty rates, and advanced analytics tools, Author's Republic is an excellent choice for publishers looking to upload and distribute audiobooks and track performance.
Pros
Author's Republic offers transparent royalty rates, with authors typically receiving 35-70% of the list price. Royalties depend on distribution channels and pricing models, allowing you to choose from several options.
Other pros include a vast distribution network and non-exclusive distribution for more flexibility and selling options.
Cons
Like the other publishing platforms listed above, Author's Republic provides limited promotional tools and lower visibility than industry giants.
Additionally, while some authors consider the platform the most user-friendly on the market, others find it less intuitive, making it a matter of personal preference.
Apple Books
Apple Books is a popular platform for those looking to tap into Apple's broad audience. It is also an excellent choice for authors who want to distribute their ebooks or audiobooks across the Apple Ecosystem.
Pros
One of the platform's advantages is full distribution and integration across iOS devices. Other benefits include non-exclusive distribution and attractive royalties (45-70% of the list price, depending on your chosen sales channel and pricing model).
Cons
Apple's Ecosystem is undeniably large yet still smaller than Amazon and Audible, leading some authors to opt for platforms with more comprehensive audiobook selections.
Final thoughts

FAQs
What factors should I consider when choosing a platform for publishing my audiobook?
When choosing a platform for audiobook publishing, consider the following factors: audience reach, royalty rates, exclusivity requirements, distribution options, marketing support, and ease of use.
What is the advantage of choosing a platform with non-exclusive distribution options?
A key advantage of choosing a publishing platform with non-exclusive distribution options is the ability to distribute your audiobook across several platforms, allowing you to reach a wider audience.
How do royalty rates vary among different publishing platforms?
Depending on your distribution model and sales channel, royalty rates can vary across different platforms. While some audiobook publishing platforms offer a list price percentage, others offer flat royalty rates.
Is choosing a platform with a larger market share better, even if it means accepting exclusivity requirements?
The answer to this question depends on your priorities and goals. Although platforms with larger market shares like ACX provide you with better reach and visibility, they often require exclusivity, which limits distribution options (and vice versa for platforms with smaller market shares).
How important is marketing and promotional support when choosing an audiobook publishing platform?
Depending on your goals, marketing and promotional support availability can be crucial or less critical. If you plan on promoting your audiobook through other platforms, lacking such tools shouldn't be too much of a headache.
