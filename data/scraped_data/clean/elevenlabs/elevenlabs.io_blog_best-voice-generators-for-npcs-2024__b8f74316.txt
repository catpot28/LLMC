2. Replica Digital Voice Studio
Replica Studios were the first to open the floodgates on AI usage for game character voices. They’ve been building up the capabilities of their AI voice actors since 2021, and last year announced
Replica Smart NPCs,
promising NPC-specific software for gaming that can fully voice 100s of characters in no time.
Pros
Lifelike process:
Taking inspiration from the traditional voice acting process, creators using Replica’s tools can audition and direct the ‘performance’ of their AI voice actors.
Trusted software:
Replica’s voice API is trusted by some pretty serious partners—
Google Cloud, GlobalLogic
, and
Unreal Engine

A range of export formats ensure compatibility with new games.
Cons
Expense:
reflecting their use by big clients, Replica’s premium features come at a high price point.

Replica offers a complex model with a range of uses. While this software is well-suited for established studios, its tools are not designed with the new or emerging developer in mind.

3. Speechify
The Speechify Voice Over Generator creates natural voiceovers from text, allowing the user to select from 100+
AI voices
in 60 languages.
Pros
Accessibility:
Speechify has an intuitive, simply designed user interface—it’s used by anyone from students, editors, and readers to workplaces.
No limits on audio upload:
Unlike some software, Speechify has no limit on the amount of text the user uploads to be converted, well-suited for large chunks of NPC dialogue.
Commercial usage rights:
Speechify’s AI voices grant the user rights for full commercial usage, including in video games.
Cons
Limited voice generation:
Users can only generate 50 hours per user per year, which is unlikely to be enough for game developers.
Lack of customization:
While Speechify offers a choice between 60+ synthetic voices, it doesn’t generate new voices from scratch, limiting the app’s scope as a creative tool.
Not game-specific:
While other software on this list was developed with gaming in mind, Speechify is mainly used as a transcription service, and is therefore not best suited to the purpose of generating NPC voices.
Pricing
Speechify is costly. Users can test out the tool online for free, but to download your generated voices,
plans start at $24.00/month.

4. PlayHT
PlayHT’s voice cloning and text-to-speech tools are designed specifically for use in games, movies, and animation. Voices generated are industry quality and offer
Pros
Vast range of languages:
PlayHT boasts one of the best multilingual capabilities on the market, producing content in 142 languages from across the globe.
Unique features:
The Multi-Voice Feature allows creators to create conversations between different voices in the same audio file, while Custom Pronunciations can be saved and reused, perfect for fantasy games.
Real-time generation:
PlayHT’s Voice Cloning and Voice Generation API can generate output in real-time, ideal for meeting tight development deadlines.
Customization options:
Few providers offer as high a level of control over their AI voices—users can fine-tune each character's voice based on emotion, expression, dialect, and language. In addition, voices are trained to be as human-like as possible, taking into account intonation, pauses, and speech style.
Cons
Cost:
Starting at $31/month per user, PlayHT is one of the most expensive AI voice generation tools on the market, limiting its affordability for individuals or indie developers.
Teething problems:
The PlayHT 2.0 model is still in Beta, and users have experienced some errors and issues with accuracy when using the software.
Intonation issues:
Several users report issues with intonation and non-verbal utterances, which can add a time burden to developers using the tool to generate large amounts of character speech.
Pricing
PlayHT does offer a free plan, but it’s limited to online usage and only allows for 5,000 free words per month.
Professional plans currently start at $31.20/month.

5. Synthesia
Synthesia is primarily an AI video generator. However, it has recently added a built-in text-to-speech function to its wheelhouse, allowing creators to generate natural-sounding voiceovers.
Pros
Diversity:
Synthesia offers 400 different male and female voices in 120+ languages. You can also use SSML tags (Speech Synthesis Markup Language) to fine-tune realistic accented voices.
Previews:
The software allows for a preview of the AI voice narration, before taking the time to download the generated audio. This is helpful for a streamlined game development workflow.
Helpful instructions:
The Synthesia website offers a host of helpful support tools and explainers, ensuring picking up the new software is as easy as possible for new users.
Cons
Slow generation:
Users report that it can take several minutes to search the library for a suitable AI voice. This could pose a problem for developers hoping to quickly turn around new games.
Pronunciation issues:
Synthesia’s model can’t pronounce all words, and sometimes needs the user to input phonetic spelling, which could become a real time-suck for developers generating large volumes of NPC speech.
Not tailored to gaming:
This voiceover software is mainly used for corporate scripts, meaning it’s unlikely to be well-suited for more creative uses, like video game production.
Pricing
After a free trial of some simple demo features, subscriptions to Synthesia range from £23-£69/month [or approx. $29-87/month], making it one of the more expensive options.

Listnr is a Generative AI Engine that uses a library of 1000+ voices to create voiceovers, along with offering voice cloning capabilities.
Pros
Rapid generation:
Listnr’s Text-to-Speech engine works in seconds, bringing huge time benefits to game developers

How to choose the perfect NPC voice generator
When selecting your NPC voice generator, bear the following considerations in mind.
Avoiding robotic speech
Synthetic speech varies in its ability to recreate the work of real voice actors. While tools like Listnr are good for corporate presentations and voiceovers, engaging NPC voices need to be expressive, emotive, and characterful.
ElevenLabs'
text-to-speech tool
,
for example, personalizes each character's voice, taking into account their narrative role, personality, and background. These characters will respond with life-like emotion as the storyline unfolds, each maintaining consistency in a unique tonal quality and personality.
Global reach
If you’re hoping to capture the imagination of gamers worldwide, it’s critical to make sure your NPCs will sound at home in a variety of languages. Some platforms offer a range of languages, but differences in dialect that would be obvious to a native speaker are left lacking. On the other hand, tools like ElevenLabs can generate authentic, life-like speech in
28 languages
.
Creative control
NPCs can provide a high level of creative control for game developers—these characters can be personalized by the game creators in every aspect, especially voice. ElevenLabs' synthetic voices can be customized to adapt accent, language, emotion, and speaker profile. The more you can fine-tune character voices and adapt them to different situational contexts, the more lifelike, engaging, and exciting a collection of NPCs can be.
Time-efficiency
NPC speech generation can be an incredibly time-consuming process—the more NPCs you want to create, and therefore how fleshed-out and engaging your game will be, the greater this time-suck. AI tools present the opportunity for game developers to turn months into seconds, so it’s important to consider how rapid, easy to learn, and easy to adjust your character voice model would be.
Final thoughts
AI voice generators like ElevenLabs, Replica Studios, and Speechify enable game developers to animate NPCs with striking, lifelike voices, enhancing narrative depth and player immersion. These technologiesoffer vast voice libraries, multilingual support, and customizable features, optimizing NPC dialogue for emotional resonance and global reach.
With the perfect voice generator for NPCs, developers can ensure narratives are rich, engaging, and accessible, all while cutting costs and labor-intensiveness in their development workflow.
As the demand for more interactive and immersive games grows, selecting the ideal voice generation platform becomes crucial, shaping the future of storytelling and game development.
