Our partnership with
Pictory
AI continues to make video creation simpler and faster. You can now rely on a single platform for your video and voiceover needs. Pictory offers a media library with over 12 million royalty-free videos, and we provide ultra-realistic
AI voices
.
What’s new?
We started with support for English and German only. Thanks to our Turbo v2.5 model, we’ve now expanded to 32 languages, giving users even more flexibility to reach audiences worldwide with Pictory’s video tools. This saves time and costs, letting you work more efficiently without needing multiple subscriptions or tools.
With
Pictory
, you can easily turn text, articles, websites, presentations into professional videos without the need for video professionals. Our AI voices take this a step further, allowing you to take your content global with voices that sound perfectly human and natural across all these languages.
In the last six months alone Pictory users have converted 2.5 billion characters into voice-overs using our audio AI models, translating to 50,000 hours - or about 5.7 years - of voiced video content.
Focus on creativity
This means that with Pictory and ElevenLabs, you can focus on your message, rather than time-consuming production details. For professionals who need to create training videos or marketing content, this offers an one-stop-shop solution. It’s built for creators who want to reduce the high costs of video production while still delivering high-quality results.
Proven results
All Weather Industries manufactures and markets flood barriers to protect homes and businesses from flooding. They work across 70 different markets and use Pictory for both their product and training videos. Pat McKeown, Head of Sales Training at All Weather Industries, says: “
Pictory allows us to localize our content easily, using the right voice or language for our target market, making our videos more effective worldwide
.” With our voice model integration, they can now also reach customers and employees in their native languages, wherever they are, and in a voice suited to the use-case.
