A certification hub with a challenge
School leaders are under pressure to understand how artificial intelligence can support teaching and learning. The information exists, but it’s often scattered across frameworks, case studies, prompt guides, and policy briefings.
AiED Certified is now the home for the UK’s AI in Education Certification,

backed by a clear framework, guidance, and a secure evidence portal. Alongside this, the site still provides access to its legacy library of more than 250 curated resources, including case studies, prompt guides, and policy briefings.
While these materials are no longer directly browsable, the ElevenLabs-powered voice agent has knowledge of the full archive and can be asked about it. The aim was to refocus the site on the certification framework while keeping the resource collection available through a more intuitive, conversational interface.
Voice as an interface
To achieve this, AiED Certified embedded a conversational voice agent—powered by ElevenLabs—across the new site. Instead of clicking through menus or guessing keywords, users can now ask directly:
"What are the five elements of the framework?"

"Do you have a case study about using AI to support SEND in schools?"
The agent responds instantly in a natural, human-sounding voice, surfacing the right part of the certification framework or retrieving relevant items from the resource library.
It turns complexity into clarity, helping busy school leaders get the information they need in seconds.
See the AiEd conversational agent in action
Unmute
Why this matters for access, inclusion, and reducing workload
For educators with visual impairments or reading difficulties, voice navigation lowers barriers. For colleagues newer to AI or from diverse linguistic backgrounds, it reduces the friction of jargon and unfamiliar structures.
By bridging the new certification content with the retained resource archive, the ElevenLabs voice agent helps every user find the right guidance at the right time.
For school leaders, speed to clarity matters. Instead of paging through long documents, they can ask the agent and jump straight to the relevant framework criterion, evidence exemplar, or case study. Senior leaders can now reach the right information in seconds, supporting quicker decision-making and smoother implementation.
Expanding access through the Impact Program
AiED Certified is using ElevenLabs tools
at no cost
through the ElevenLabs Impact Program.
This means schools with tight budgets, particularly in under-resourced areas, can benefit from advanced voice interfaces without prohibitive expense. Removing this barrier helps ensure innovation reaches the communities that need it most.
More than technology
This is about more than a new tool. It’s about:
Democratizing access
: making advanced voice technology available to nonprofits and underfunded schools through the ElevenLabs Impact Program.
Supporting inclusion
: reducing barriers caused by sight or learning disabilities, language differences, and varied levels of literacy.
Restoring time and dignity
: cutting the burden of search and administration.
Advancing equity
: ensuring under-resourced schools aren’t left behind.
Projects like this are reshaping how people interact with complex information online. As voice technology becomes more natural and accessible, it’s changing how we learn, search, and make decisions. This shift goes beyond convenience. It represents a new standard for access: faster, clearer, and more human.
With ElevenLabs, AiED Certified has turned a static resource hub into a voice-enabled guide—creating a more accessible path to safe, effective, and ethical AI adoption in every school.
For organizations that manage a large library of resources or multi-page frameworks, embedding a conversational
voice agent
could make a real difference—helping visitors get the right information instantly, by asking rather than searching.
