We’re proud to welcome Cid Moreira to Iconic Voices on the
ElevenReader App
.
For decades, his voice has brought gravity and warmth to journalism, scripture, and literature for millions of Brazilians. Best known as the voice of
Jornal Nacional-
Brazil’s most-watched news program for over 25 years - and for his celebrated Bible narrations, Cid has left a lasting mark on the country’s cultural life.
Now, his voice lives on in ElevenReader - narrating Psalms, prayers, and classic works in a voice known across generations. We’re honored to help preserve and share his legacy, so more people can discover and enjoy it in a new digital format.
Cid joins a growing collection of voices that shaped culture, made available for streaming exclusively in the app.
Download the ElevenReader App on iOS or Android to start listening:
https://elevenreader.io/
