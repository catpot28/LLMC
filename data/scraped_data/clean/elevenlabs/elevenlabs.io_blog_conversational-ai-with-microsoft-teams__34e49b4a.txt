What is Microsoft Teams?
Microsoft Teams has emerged as the central nervous system of modern workplace collaboration, serving as a unified platform for chat, video meetings, file sharing, and application integration. Since its launch in 2017, Teams has evolved from a simple communication tool into a vital digital workspace that connects employees across departments, time zones, and continents.
At its core, Teams functions as a hub where various Microsoft 365 applications converge — from SharePoint and OneDrive for file management to Outlook for email and calendar integration. This consolidation of tools has made Teams the go-to platform for organizations seeking to streamline their digital operations. Whether it's quick chat messages, scheduled video conferences, collaborative document editing, or project management, Teams provides a single interface for all workplace interactions.
That's not all. Through the Microsoft Teams App Store, organizations can integrate third-party applications, custom workflows, and specialized tools that transform Teams into a tailored workspace for their specific needs. This flexibility has made Teams particularly valuable for remote and hybrid work environments.
However, as Teams adoption has grown, so has the complexity of managing and navigating this digital workspace. Users often find themselves switching between multiple channels, searching through conversation histories, and managing an ever-growing number of notifications.
The solution? To implement Conversational AI with Microsoft Teams.

How to integrate ElevenLabs Conversational AI with Microsoft Teams
Integrating ElevenLabs' Conversational AI with your Microsoft Teams chats is simple using Make's visual workflow builder. Here's a step-by-step guide to get you started.
Create your Make account:
Sign up for a free Make account to access their no-code automation platform. Make offers a user-friendly interface for connecting apps like Microsoft Teams and ElevenLabs without any coding required.
Set up your connections:
In Make's interface, select your Microsoft Teams application and ElevenLabs from the app library. Authenticate both services by following the prompts. Make handles all the security and permissions automatically, ensuring your data remains protected.
Choose your triggers:
Select what actions in Teams should initiate your voice AI workflows. Make offers several trigger options, including:
New messages in specific channels
Replies to selected messages
Team creation events
Channel updates
Configure your voice AI actions:
Set up the ElevenLabs actions that should occur when your triggers fire. Common configurations include converting text messages to speech, generating voice responses to queries, creating audio summaries of channel discussion, and delivering voice notifications for important updates.
Test and deploy:
Before going live, use Make's testing features to verify your workflow. The platform provides detailed logs and real-time monitoring to ensure everything works as intended. Once satisfied, activate your workflow to start automating voice interactions in Teams.
