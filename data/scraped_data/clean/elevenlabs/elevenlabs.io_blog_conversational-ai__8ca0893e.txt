Today we're proud to introduce
Conversational AI
- our all in one platform for building customizable, interactive
voice agents
. With Conversational AI, you can create outbound sales dialers, scheduling agents, interactive game characters, tutors,
customer support agent
s, and more.
Our platform is designed for low latency, thanks to fewer server calls. Conversational AI handles turn-taking and interruptions gracefully using a real-time model to predict when a speaker is finished. We’ve also simplified the technical setup so you can focus on customizing your agent.
Conversational AI connects our life-like Text to Speech,
Speech to Text
, an LLM of your choice, with our custom turn-taking and interruption logic that makes conversation feel natural. This leaves you to focus on:
Building your knowledge base
Crafting your system prompt
Connecting your apps with function calling
Selecting your favorite voice from our library or cloning your own
Finding ways to use Conversational AI to delight your customers with unbelievable experiences
Conversational AI includes advanced features to help you build more interactive agents, including:
Native Twilio integration for handling calls
Server-side and client-side tool calling for added flexibility
Dynamic prompting to create personalized conversations
Here’s our overview on how to
configure your first agent
. If you prefer complete control, you can connect your own LLM or server with
this guide
. We have a
Python, Javascript, React and Swift SDK
, or you can integrate with the
WebSocket API
directly.
