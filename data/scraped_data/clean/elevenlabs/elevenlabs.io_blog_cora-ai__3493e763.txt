Doctors spend 10-15 minutes per visit on manual note-taking. This reduces the number of patients they can see and adds to administrative burnout.

Cora AI
set out to change this by creating a voice assistant that transcribes consultations in real time and generates complete medical documentation.
Choosing the right Speech to Text provider
Cora AI’s first solution relied on another provider’s model. While functional, it struggled with clinical accuracy in Polish, particularly with noise and patient diction. After testing, ElevenLabs’

Speech to Text
model now delivers:
40 percentage points higher accuracy
in Polish clinical settings.
2-3 second transcription time
for multi-minute visits.
Accurate speaker diarization
with no loss of quality on short segments.
“This wasn’t just an upgrade - it was a breakthrough. ElevenLabs helped us cross the threshold where doctors stop testing AI, and start trusting it.” - Hubert Zieliński, CEO, Cora AI
To ensure patient trust, the system also processes all audio securely in RAM, complies with GDPR and HIPAA, and integrates a proprietary anonymization module to automatically remove patient identifiers.
Immediate impact in clinical settings
With ElevenLabs integrated, Cora AI has achieved measurable results, reducing medical documentation time by up to 70 percent.
Doctors can now preview notes in real time during consultations, which not only shortens the waiting period for finalized documents but also increases adoption of the tool.
