CreatorKit
’s platform lets businesses create video ads with hyper-realistic AI actors that look and sound like real people. Traditional AI video tools often fell short by delivering the same generic expression regardless of the script’s tone. CreatorKit changes that with
Emotive AI Actors
, powered by ElevenLabs’
voice cloning
technology, which adds emotional nuance to each video.
What’s new?
With Emotive AI Actors, businesses can now produce videos by simply typing a script. CreatorKit’s technology identifies the intended emotion for each line, creating video content that feels as real as a human performance. Each Emotive AI Voice is crafted using our Professional Voice Cloning technology, with all voices cloned with full permissions, ensuring the highest fidelity and robust IP security. Every emotion is backed by a unique voice clone, capturing the tone needed for authentic delivery—whether the scene calls for excitement, curiosity, or calm.
Since implementing Emotive AI Actors, CreatorKit has seen a
16% increase in conversion rates
for videos created with emotionally tuned voices. See and hear for yourself how real their AI actors look and sound.
“ElevenLabs is the only company capable of capturing and maintaining emotions when doing voice cloning,”
notes Kevin Natanzon, founder & CEO at CreatorKit.
“This capability was critical for Emotive AI Actors, and it would have been impossible to build this feature without ElevenLabs.”
Together, we provide an easy way for businesses to create video ads that connect with audiences on a human level - all within a smooth, intuitive process.
