Dashverse
is an AI-native entertainment ecosystem built to unify the creation and distribution of video storytelling. Their platform addresses a core bottleneck in modern video production - the cost and complexity of producing high-quality, serialized content at scale.
Through two tightly integrated products, Dashverse enables a new generation of creators to operate as a fully autonomous one person studio.
Frameo.AI is a creation platform where users describe their story, characters, and mood in simple text. Frameo then orchestrates the full video generation pipeline behind the scenes. DashReels is the distribution layer, delivering these AI-generated stories to a global audience.
At the center of this system is audio. Voice and music are treated as foundational storytelling primitives, not post-production add-ons.
Audio as a core layer of the creation pipeline
Dashverse integrates ElevenLabs directly into the Frameo creation workflow to power all voice and music generation.
Creators use ElevenLabs voices to:
Select accents and tones that match each character and story arc
Generate character dialogue for AI-driven cinematic scenes
Add narration and voice-over where pacing and delivery shape emotional impact
ElevenLabs Music
is used commercially across final Frameo outputs to set mood, pacing, and narrative tension. Background scores are generated to match the beat and emotional arc of each scene, helping creators achieve a cohesive cinematic result without relying on stock libraries or manual licensing.
Why Dashverse chose ElevenLabs
Dashverse evaluated multiple audio providers, including Murf, Lyria, Suno, and Hume. ElevenLabs stood out across three dimensions that were critical for narrative video production:
Fine-grained controllability of voice output
Consistent, high-quality results that support iterative edits
A broad library of ready-to-use voices suitable for serialized storytelling
The team adopted ElevenLabs most-expressive
Text to Speech
Model V3 for voice generation and ElevenLabs Music for background scoring, integrating both directly into Frameo’s conversational editing system.
Replacing fragmented audio workflows with a native solution
Before integrating ElevenLabs Music, creators often relied on generic stock tracks or manual licensing. This approach was difficult to scale and frequently misaligned with the narrative tone of the video.
By embedding music generation directly into the creation pipeline, Dashverse replaced fragmented workflows with a single, native solution. Audio generation now happens in context, alongside story and scene generation, enabling faster iteration and more cohesive results.
Impact on creators and the business
ElevenLabs has had a direct impact on both creator behavior and platform performance:
Faster creation cycles by removing manual audio steps
Higher perceived production quality across final videos
Increased creator confidence when publishing finished work
Improved completion rates and willingness to pay
Audio quality became a lever for both creative output and commercial outcomes.
Partnering on the future of AI storytelling
Audio is a critical layer in how stories land emotionally on Frameo. ElevenLabs has been a strong partner in helping us deliver high-quality voice and music that integrates smoothly into our broader storytelling stack,
said Soumyadeep Mukherjee, CTO, Dashverse.
By treating voice and music as first-class components of video generation, Dashverse is building an AI-native entertainment system where individual creators can produce cinematic content at scale. And ElevenLabs is proud to power the audio foundation that makes this possible.
