Introduction
Deepak Chopra has spent over forty years exploring consciousness, health, and human potential. Now, with ElevenLabs and
Supertab
, his insights are available in a new, accessible way - through an AI-powered version of his own voice at
deepakchopra.ai
.
At the heart of this project is ElevenLabs' Reader App and
Voice AI
. Combined with Supertab’s platform, which supports flexible payments for digital content, people can easily engage with Chopra’s teachings. They can listen to his books, follow guided meditations, or even have conversations directly with his AI voice.
Making wisdom easy to access
Supertab ensures Chopra’s spoken insights are available without large subscriptions or upfront payments. Users decide how they engage - whether buying a single session, brief access, or browsing freely before committing. This approach removes financial barriers, making Chopra’s content accessible to everyone.
An authentic voice, recreated
Using ElevenLabs’
Voice Cloning
technology, Chopra’s distinctive voice, including his natural tone and rhythm, has been precisely replicated. This AI version isn't merely an imitation - it's created in close collaboration with Chopra himself. Users can hear him explain mindfulness, guide breathing exercises, or discuss complex ideas naturally and clearly.
In the ElevenLabs Reader App, Chopra’s voice is part of our Iconic Voices collection. Users can load any text - his books, essays, or articles - and hear it narrated authentically in his voice.
From listening to engaging directly
At
deepakchopra.ai
, Chopra’s voice powers an interactive chatbot trained on his extensive body of work. Users can ask questions about meditation, health, or consciousness and receive immediate, spoken answers from Chopra’s AI voice. Thanks to Supertab, this
conversational ai
experience is available without subscription barriers, enabling anyone to explore Chopra’s insights openly and conveniently.
This interaction transforms passive listening into active dialogue, aligning closely with Chopra’s belief that awareness is something we actively develop rather than passively consume.
Building voice technology for wellness
The Chopra project shows how advanced voice technology combined with thoughtful payment models can help share valuable ideas widely. It demonstrates ElevenLabs' ability to preserve important voices and Supertab's strength in enabling easy, affordable content access.
We see this as the future of mindful technology - voice as a clear way to share understanding, available to everyone.
Download the ElevenLabs Reader App to listen to Chopra’s voice or visit
deepakchopra.ai
to experience interactive conversations directly.
