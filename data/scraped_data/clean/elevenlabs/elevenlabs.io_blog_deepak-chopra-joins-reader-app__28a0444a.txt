Deepak Chopra’s voice joins the ElevenLabs Reader App for more mindful listening

World-renowned author Deepak Chopra’s voice is now available to all users on the ElevenReader app
On this page
Introduction
ElevenLabs is thrilled to announce
the latest addition of Deepak Chopra to our Iconic Voices collection
on the

Reader App
.
Deepak Chopra, MD, FACP, FRCP has been at the forefront of meditation and spiritual wellness for over thirty years. He is the author of over 90 books translated into over forty-three languages, including numerous New York Times bestsellers. He was named as one of TIME Magazine’s top 100 most influential people.
In June, Deepak launched
Digital Deepak
, a chatbot trained on Deepak’s writing and videos where his students can ask personalized questions and get answers in his voice through ElevenLabs. Fans were excited at the opportunity to connect with Deepak in new ways - leading us to explore more ways to partner. Now that Deepak is on the ElevenLabs reader app, global listeners can stream their favorite articles, text, and e-books in his voice.
“I am proud to announce my partnership with ElevenLabs. Listening can help cultivate emotional nurturing and engagement. This is no different in the age of AI, it is only more important,” said Chopra. “I have always written to connect with people, and now I can connect on a deeper level with a global audience as I make my teachings available for everyone in my own voice."
“Deepak has always been ahead of the curve when it comes to the mind-body connection and the significance of understanding ourselves, so it’s no surprise to see him finding creative ways to leverage the latest and best in AI technology with ElevenLabs in a safe and responsible way. Being able to access his content in his calm and soothing voice is a big win for his fans around the world.”
Mayim Bialik, PhD, Actor, Author, Neuroscientist.
Launched earlier this year
, the Reader App transforms any digital text into an emotionally rich, context-aware voiceover. The voice of Deepak Chopra joins our existing
collection of iconic voices
on the app, which includes
Judy Garland, James Dean, Burt Reynolds,
and
Sir Laurence Olivier.
Our partnerships ensure that listeners can access memorable voices in a trusted way – all are available exclusively on the Reader app for streaming and are not part of our wider Voice Library for creating content to share.
“At ElevenLabs, we're committed to preserving and celebrating cultural legacies while pushing the boundaries of technology," said Dustin Blank, Head of Partnerships at ElevenLabs. "By bringing voices like Deepak Chopra to our platform, we're not just enhancing our app – we're creating new ways for people to connect with the most influential figures and their work.”
The addition of Deepak Chopra to the Reader App’s Iconic Voices collection underscores our commitment to making content accessible in any language and voice around the globe.
