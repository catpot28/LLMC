AI Studios
- DeepBrain AI’s flagship video platform - helps organizations scale communication. By combining photorealistic avatars with high-quality voice synthesis, DeepBrain empowers users to generate hyper-realistic videos for education, enterprise communication, and media.
To power this transformation, DeepBrain AI selected ElevenLabs as its primary
Text to Speech
provider.
From file uploads to full integration
Our collaboration began with a simple workflow: users manually uploaded MP3 files generated by ElevenLabs into AI Studios. As demand for voice-powered content surged, the DeepBrain team identified a clear opportunity—tight integration with ElevenLabs could unlock faster localization, richer expressiveness, and seamless avatar deployment.
The result is a native integration that makes ElevenLabs’ voices a core part of the AI Studios experience.
“With over 2,000 new custom avatars launched and powerful new API features rolling out, AI Studios is scaling faster than ever. Having ElevenLabs as our TTS partner gives us the confidence to localize globally without sacrificing quality or emotion. It’s an essential part of our stack.” — Seyoung “Eric” Jang, CEO, DeepBrain AI
Key use cases: custom avatars and AI
dubbing
AI Studios relies on ElevenLabs for two of its most impactful features:
Custom Avatars
Users can create lifelike avatars with just a short video and pair them with expressive, natural
voiceovers
. This enables scalable training videos, internal communications, and public-facing content with minimal production time.
AI Dubbing
With ElevenLabs, DeepBrain delivers multilingual dubbing and
voice cloning
at scale—without sacrificing clarity or emotional nuance. This allows content creators to repurpose video across languages and regions, expanding reach and impact.

Measurable results
Since integrating ElevenLabs, DeepBrain AI has seen:
7× growth in AI-generated videos using avatars and dubbed voice
40% faster localization across more than 10 languages
2,000+ avatars deployed using ElevenLabs voices
3× increase in monthly active users of voice-powered avatar tools
Communicating across borders
DeepBrain AI shows how expressive speech and visual realism can converge to enable new modes of global communication.
“AI Studios is transforming how people communicate across borders. With ElevenLabs, our avatars speak to the world clearly, naturally, and instantly.” — Seyoung “Eric” Jang, CEO, DeepBrain AI
