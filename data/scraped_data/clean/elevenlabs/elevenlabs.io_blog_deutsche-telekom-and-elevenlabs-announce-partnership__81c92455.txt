We’re proud to announce our partnership with Deutsche Telekom to bring ElevenLabs'
AI voice agents
to the customer service of Europe’s largest Telco (via app and phone).
Deutsche Telekom customers will soon experience realistic AI voice
agents
- available 24/7 and without any waiting time - to augment customer support with a more personal, human-like interaction.
This initiative is part of a shared vision to democratize access to AI, making advanced voice technology simple, accessible, and valuable to everyone.
