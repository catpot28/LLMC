Today, we launched
Eleven Music
– the next step on our mission to build the most comprehensive AI audio platform in the world. With Eleven Music, businesses, creators, artists, and every single one of our users can generate studio-grade music from natural language prompts, with:
- Complete control over genre, style, and structure
- Vocals or just instrumental
- Multi-lingual, including English, Spanish, German, Japanese and more
- Edit the sound and lyrics of individual sections or the whole song
