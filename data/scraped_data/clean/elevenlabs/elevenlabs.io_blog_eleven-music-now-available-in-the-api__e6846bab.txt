Today, we are launching Eleven
Music
in our API. This is the first Music API for developers trained on licensed data and cleared for broad commercial use. Developers can now integrate AI music generation directly into their products and workflows.
With the Eleven Music API, you can:
Generate high-quality tracks from simple text prompts
Create vocal or instrumental versions in any genre
Customize track length, structure, and language
This makes it possible to produce music that is tailored to specific creative, commercial, or enterprise needs in seconds. Since launch, creators have already generated over 1 million songs with Eleven Music.
Commercial use
Created in collaboration with labels, publishers, and artists, songs created with the Eleven Music API are available for broad commercial use.
It’s designed for building apps across media and entertainment. Whether you’re delivering personalized mediations, producing music for video games, or AI generated ads.
Certain use cases like film & TV require an Enterprise plan, for more information on supported usage across plans:
https://elevenlabs.io/music-rights
.
Already powering leading enterprises
Eleven Music is already powering leading enterprises. We have been working closely with partners to integrate Eleven Music into real-world workflows.
With
VEED
's AI background music, simply tap on AI Background Music, and watch as it creates a custom track—all without a single instrument. The AI analyzes your video's transcript to generate a track that perfectly matches the subject and tone of your content.
AMP, a global creative agency, is adding Eleven Music to its
Sonic Hub
to expand services for brands, agencies, and creators. Michele Arnese, Founder & Global CEO said:
“Sonic Hub just got a game-changing upgrade: With Eleven Music, we're supercharging our audio operating system for brands, agencies and creators, fusing our deep expertise in understanding and coding the sound of a brand with Eleven Music unparalleled quality of execution in AI music creation. Get ready for a new industry benchmark!”
Hedra Labs
is using Eleven Music to create
original music videos
and bring avatars to life with singing voices.
