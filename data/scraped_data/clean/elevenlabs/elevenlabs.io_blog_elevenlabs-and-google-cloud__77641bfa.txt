ElevenLabs' advanced voice AI models are now available on Google Cloud Marketplace, enabling businesses to enhance customer engagement, automate workflows, and scale AI-driven content creation with Google Cloud’s powerful AI and infrastructure services.
By combining Google Cloud’s AI capabilities and models—including Google’s highly efficient model, Gemini 2.0 Flash—with ElevenLabs’
Text to Speech
, businesses can create low latency, human-like voices that drive efficiency, engagement, and global reach.
“Bringing ElevenLabs' solution to Google Cloud Marketplace will help customers quickly deploy, manage, and grow the text to speech, dubbing, and conversational AI on Google Cloud's trusted, global infrastructure," said Dai Vu, Managing Director, Marketplace & ISV GTM Programs at Google Cloud. “ElevenLabs can now securely scale and support customers on their digital transformation journeys.”
Key Use Cases:
Conversational AI: Deploy interactive
voice agents
for customer support, education, sales, and gaming.
Content Localization & Dubbing: Translate and voice content across multiple languages while preserving emotion and tone.
Media & Ad Production: Generate lifelike voiceovers for video, ads, internal resources, and interactive brand experiences.
Why Google Cloud + ElevenLabs?
Gemini 2.0 Flash Compatibility: The perfect AI brain for voice-driven applications, delivering ultra-fast response times, high level reasoning, and reliable function calling.
Seamless Deployment via Marketplace: Enterprises can quickly purchase and integrate ElevenLabs with a few clicks, avoiding lengthy procurement cycles.
Scalability & Performance: Leverage Google Cloud’s infrastructure to scale ElevenLabs’ voice AI technology for high-performance enterprise applications.
AI-Powered Innovation: Enhance customer interactions with AI models optimized for real-time, natural conversations.
With Google Cloud Marketplace, businesses can deploy ElevenLabs’ state-of-the-art voice AI models faster and more efficiently, leveraging Google’s scalable infrastructure to drive innovation and engagement at scale. For customized solutions and private offers, contact our sales team at
elevenlabs.io/contact-sales
.
Kabir Gill, GTM Partnerships at ElevenLabs, noted, “By leveraging Google Cloud’s scalable infrastructure and AI services, we deliver high-performance voice AI solutions to enterprises worldwide. Our partnership with Google Cloud is instrumental in helping us bring the highest quality voice AI to more businesses, and we look forward to continuing to scale both our product and our collaboration with Google Cloud.”
