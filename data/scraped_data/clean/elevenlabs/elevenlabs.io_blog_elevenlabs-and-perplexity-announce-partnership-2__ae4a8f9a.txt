We’re excited to partner with
Perplexity
– the fastest and most accurate way to search the web. Today, we are releasing our first collaboration:
Discover Daily
, a short-form daily podcast powered by ElevenLabs' voices that covers the latest headlines in innovation, science, and culture.
Discover Daily is available on all podcast platforms and listeners can now enjoy a daily selection of curated knowledge in audio form, making it easier to engage with the material on-the-go. By combining ElevenLabs' voice technology with Perplexity's powerful search and content engine, we have created a new way to stay up to speed on the latest breakthroughs.
Subscribe to Discovery Daily and listen now
.
About Perplexity
Perplexity is the fastest and most accurate way to search the web. With access to real-time data across the internet, Perplexity curates relevant sources (from academic research to Reddit threads) and creates answers far better than a list of sponsored and SEO-optimized links on a traditional search engine. Unlike other AI offerings, Perplexity must include in-line citations for every source it uses in the response. This means trusted answers and the ability to fact-check.
