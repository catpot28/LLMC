Brand guidelines and press kit
This page provides essential guidance and assets for the correct use of ElevenLabs trademarks and copyrighted materials. It is designed for all external entities engaging with our brand. Adherence to these guidelines is crucial when incorporating ElevenLabs branding elements into third-party assets and materials, ensuring proper representation of our intellectual property.

Clearance
To ensure visual impact our logo needs to be surrounded by ample and unobstructed space. All communications must adhere to a minimum area of clearance, which is determined by the logo's size and is equal to its height.
Symbol
The visual shorthand for our brand is our 11 icon, constructed from two distinctively simple vertical lines. It abstracts the number eleven from our name while also subtly referencing the pause icon seen when listening to audio.
Construction
Our icon is positioned centrally and surrounded by generous space to ensure it stands out amidst others. The amount of surrounding space is calculated based on the logo's size, with height of the holding device equivalent to three times the height of the icon.
Avatars and apps
The 11 icon is most effective for representing us in situations where space is limited, such as social media platforms and app ecosystems. It is designed to work with various holding shapes, from circles to squares.

What to avoid
Stacked
Avoid stacking the wordmark as two separate words. Likewise, avoid altering the height of the 11 icon to match the wordmark's height.
Shorthand
We may occasionally use a shorthand in plain text, ensure that this is not reflected in any logo variants.
Don’t apply effects like drop shadows
Don’t apply strokes or outlines
Don’t rotate or angle our logo
Don’t apply color
Don’t stretch or deform it
Don’t recreate the logo in other fonts

The ElevenLabs ecosystem includes three platforms: ElevenAgents, ElevenCreative, and ElevenAPI, each with its own distinct color palette and graphic approach.
ElevenAgents
The platform is called ElevenAgents and has its own logotype, which must be used in all related materials.
Use the correct written form: ElevenAgents (no space).

Color palette
Blue is used as the primary color, with additional shades to create a more nuanced palette. We layer image-based gradients with analog, tactile textures to balance the digital precision of the platform with a more human, material presence.
Graphics
Graphic elements include different representations of agents, ranging from image-based voice orbs to code-generated visuals. The primary motif used to distinguish Agents platform is the circle or sphere.
