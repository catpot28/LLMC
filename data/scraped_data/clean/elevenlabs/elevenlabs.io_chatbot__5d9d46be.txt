What is a chatbot?
Human-like chat interactions at business scale
Until recently, automating customer interactions required scripted flows that could not adapt to user queries. Agentic chatbots replace these rigid systems with assistants that understand context, take action, and resolve issues in real time.
Watch intro
Last decade's chatbots
Rigid decision trees that force customers down pre-built paths. One off-script question and the conversation hits a wall.
Pre-written answers only. The bot cannot check an order, process a return, or do anything that resolves the issue.
No awareness of tone or urgency. Every customer gets the same scripted response regardless of who the customer is or how urgent the request.
Agentic AI chatbots
Customers describe their problem in their own words. The agent interprets intent, handles edge cases, and adapts to the conversation.
Connected to your systems. The agent checks balances, updates orders, triggers workflows, and resolves issues without handing off to a human.
Detects sentiment and adjusts in real time. Responses adapt based on who the customer is, what they need, and how urgently they need it.

Frequently asked questions
What is an AI chatbot?
A chatbot is a computer program that simulates human conversation with an end user. They can operate through text or voice on websites, messaging apps, SMS, and social media. Modern chatbots increasingly use
conversational AI
techniques such as natural language processing (NLP) to understand user questions and automate responses. In practice, this means customers can type a question in their own words and get a relevant, accurate answer instantly without menus or hold times.
What types of chatbots are there?
There are three main categories:
1. Rule-based chatbots
map out conversations through predetermined rules and respond to specific pre-defined options or keywords. They're simple and predictable but limited.
2. Generative AI chatbots
are capable of understanding context and creating new, dynamic responses that simulate natural human language.
3. AI agents,
the next generation of AI-powered bots, purpose-built for CX, that seamlessly integrate into backend systems to resolve even complex issues without human intervention
ElevenLabs's chatbots are the 3rd type, allowing businesses to build complex workflows and solve real customer problems
How long does it take to set up a chatbot?
It depends on complexity. But the ElevenAgents platform allows non technical users to setup chatbots at record speed. In a few minutes you can have your own bot customized to your business and use case. The biggest variables are usually how well-organized your existing knowledge base is and how complex are the agentic flows you want to build. The cleaner your data & clearer the SOPs, the faster the setup.
What can a chatbot do for my business?
Quite a lot. Chatbots can answer customer questions, automate repetitive tasks, cut operational costs, schedule appointments, send surveys, collect leads, and transition conversations to a live human agent. They're also used in sales, when a prospect asks about pricing, features, or compatibility, the chatbot can deliver accurate answers and capture contact information for follow-up. The key benefit is freeing your human team to focus on high-value conversations rather than repetitive ones.
Does the chatbot work 24/7?
Yes, and that's one of the primary reasons businesses adopt them. Teams are investing in chatbots to deliver customer service at all hours, no matter what day of the week, while improving team productivity. Unlike human agents, a chatbot doesn't need breaks, doesn't call in sick, and can handle thousands of conversations simultaneously. For businesses with international customers across time zones, this is especially valuable.
an the chatbot hand off to a human agent when needed?
Absolutely. You can configure your chatbot to handle the bulk of customer questions, then hand the conversation over to a human agent once the query is qualified or too complex. This hybrid approach is best practice. Focus the time of your team on the high value & nuance situations and let the bot handle routine questions.
What channels do chatbots support?
