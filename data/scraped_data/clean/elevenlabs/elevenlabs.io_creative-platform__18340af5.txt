Frequently asked questions
What is the ElevenLabs Creative Platform?
The ElevenLabs Creative Platform is an AI-native creative workspace for generating, editing, and localizing audio and video content at scale. It brings together industry-leading voice, music, sound effects, image, and video models in a single platform, designed for professional and enterprise use.
What types of content can I create?
You can create and edit:
Voiceovers and narration
Music tracks and songs with lyrics
Sound effects
Dubbing and localized audio
Images and videos
Training, marketing, and branded content
All assets can be produced, edited, and exported from the same workspace.

Is the content generated safe for commercial use?
Yes. ElevenLabs provides broad commercial licensing for outputs generated using its native models. Eleven Music requires an additional license for marketing campaigns, advertising, film, TV, games, and enterprise distribution.
How does localization work?
Localization is built directly into the platform. You can translate voices, captions, and audio into 70+ languages, while preserving tone, timing, and speaker identity. Teams use this to create dozens of market variants without duplicating production effort.

Can I create a custom voice for my brand?
Yes. You can:
- Design a voice using text prompts (tone, accent, age, emotion)
- Clone a real voice with permission
- Use voices from the ElevenLabs voice library
This allows teams to maintain a consistent, recognizable brand voice across all content.
What is ElevenLabs Studio?
Studio is ElevenLabs’ production environment for composing and editing content. It lets you combine voice, music, sound effects, captions, and video into finished assets, with fine-grained control over timing, delivery, and structure.
Do you offer managed services?
Yes. ElevenLabs Productions provides managed services for dubbing, subtitling, transcription, and large-scale localization. This is designed for teams that need expert support or high-volume execution alongside the platform.
How is the Creative Platform different from the Agents Platform?
The Creative Platform is focused on content creation and production — voiceovers, dubbing, music, sound effects, video and localization workflows.
The Agents Platform is designed for interactive, real-time conversational experiences, such as voice agents, characters, and marketing activations.
Together, they allow teams to create content and deploy it across both static and interactive experiences.
Is the Creative Platform built for teams?
Yes. The platform includes:
- Multi-seat workspaces
- Shared credit pools
- Content libraries
- Role-based access and approvals
This makes it easy for marketing, creative, and production teams to collaborate across regions.
Is the platform enterprise-ready?
Yes. ElevenLabs supports enterprise requirements including:
- SOC 2 compliance
- Single sign-on (SSO)
- Audit logs
- Master Service Agreements (MSAs)
- Data privacy and security controls

Can credits be shared across the team?
Yes. Credits are pooled at the workspace level and can be used across all supported workflows, helping teams scale usage efficiently without managing per-seat limits.
Are APIs available?
Yes. All core Creative Platform capabilities — including voice, music, sound effects, dubbing, and transcription — are available via APIs, allowing teams to integrate ElevenLabs directly into their production pipelines.
