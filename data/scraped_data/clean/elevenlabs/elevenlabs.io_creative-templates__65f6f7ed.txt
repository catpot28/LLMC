Frequently asked questions
How do templates work?
Each template connects multiple AI models for image, video, and audio generation into a single guided flow. You follow simple steps, like uploading assets or adding prompts, and the best models are automatically selected for each stage.
Do I need to know which models to use?
No, templates handle model selection for you. Our team and partners curate and configure the models that deliver the best results for each task.
Can I edit the results after generating them?
Yes, you can continue refining your output in Studio or the Image & Video tool, upscale results, or download them directly
How is pricing calculated?
Pricing is the same as if you performed each step manually. Some templates show price estimates before generation, while others (like Lip Sync) depend on variables that can’t be estimated exactly.
Can I pause or resume a template later?
Yes, long templates can be resumed anytime from the History tab. You can also revisit previous generations to pick up where you left off.
