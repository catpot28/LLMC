Frequently asked questions
What is a Culture Partnership?
A Culture Partnership is an ongoing collaboration between a cultural institution and ElevenLabs. It goes beyond a free license- Culture Partners co-create content with us, distribute free ElevenLabs licenses to their community (on mutually agreed terms), and serve as public ambassadors for what voice AI can do in the cultural space. Think of it as a shared commitment to pushing creative boundaries with voice technology.
What do Culture Partners receive?
Culture Partners receive an ElevenLabs Scale account, the ability to distribute free ElevenLabs licenses to their audience or network (on mutually agreed terms), co-branded content assets such as a case study, press release, or joint announcement, early access to new ElevenLabs features and tools, and featured placement on the ElevenLabs Culture Partners page.
What does ElevenLabs receive in return?
In exchange, ElevenLabs receives a publicly referenceable partnership, brand visibility with your audience, a co-created asset- whether that's a content piece, event integration, or product showcase- and measurable audience reach. The partnership should be mutually valuable: we invest in your work, and together we show what's possible when voice AI meets culture.
Who is eligible to apply?
We're looking for registered nonprofits or public cultural institutions with an annual audience reach of 30,000 or more. Applicants should be willing to be publicly named as an ElevenLabs Culture Partner and come with a specific collaboration in mind, not just a request for free licenses. Existing media presence, an international audience, or multi-language programming are all strong signals.
What happens after I apply?
After you submit your application, our partnerships team will review it and follow up directly. If your project is a strong fit, we'll schedule an intro call to learn more about what you're building and explore how a Culture Partnership could take shape. From there, we'll work together on scoping the collaboration, aligning on deliverables, and getting you set up with the tools you need.
