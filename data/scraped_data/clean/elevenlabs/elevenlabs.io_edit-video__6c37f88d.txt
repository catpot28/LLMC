Frequently asked questions
What is an AI video editor?
An AI video editor uses artificial intelligence to automate video editing, enabling users to create professional-quality videos quickly.
How does voice integration work?
Voice integration lets you add AI-generated voiceovers and synchronize audio to video, enhancing content engagement.
Can I edit videos for free?
Video generation requires a paid plan. Credits are shown before generation.
What video formats are supported?
Supported formats include MP4 with H.264 and H.265 codecs, allowing up to 4K resolution.
How do I create a talking video?
Use Omnihuman 1.5 to animate lips on images or Veed LipSync for videos, adding voice to create talking content.
