1. Commercial Use.
(a) In General.

Commercial use of Outputs is permitted, subject to the restrictions set forth in these Service Terms.
(b) Restrictions. Commercial use of Outputs is restricted as follows:
(i) Free Plan. If Customer is on a Free Plan, Customer is prohibited from using Outputs for any commercial purpose. This includes a prohibition on the distribution of Outputs on streaming music platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and Pandora.
(ii) Starter, Creator, Pro, Scale, or Business Plans. If Customer is on a Starter, Creator, Pro, Scale, or Business Plan, it is prohibited from using Outputs in the following commercial contexts:
(1) offline
(i.e., not on the internet or a mobile app) television or radio programs, films, other visual media, live broadcasts, or advertisements.
(2) high-traffic public events, including conferences, expos, or concerts;
(3) music libraries or repositories that include more than 100 Outputs, from which Customer intends to license or otherwise distribute music to third-parties; or
(4) in any context, Making Available (as defined in the
OEM Terms
) the Service or Output to any third-party for use in a manner that would violate this section or any other provision of these Service Terms.
(iii) Enterprise Plans. If Customer is on an Enterprise Plan, it is prohibited from using Outputs in the following commercial contexts:
(1) music libraries or repositories that include more than 100 Outputs, from which Customer intends to license or otherwise distribute music to third-parties; or
(2) distribution on streaming music platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music and Pandora.

(i) May generate up to 11 minutes of Output per month.
(ii) No downloads permitted.
(b) Starter Plan.
(i) May generate up to 30 minutes of Output per month.
(ii) May download up to 30 minutes of Output per month.

(i) Generation and download limits scale by plan type (see table below).
(d) Enterprise Plan.
(i) Custom generation and download limits agreed in writing.
