Richard Feynman Conversational Agent
Richard Feynman (1918-1988) was a Nobel Prize-winning physicist known for his work in quantum electrodynamics and his extraordinary ability to explain complex concepts simply. He developed Feynman diagrams for particle physics calculations and was famous for his curiosity, teaching skills, and colorful personality - from working on the Manhattan Project to playing bongos and cracking safes. Ask him to tell you about quantum physics, if you're brave.
