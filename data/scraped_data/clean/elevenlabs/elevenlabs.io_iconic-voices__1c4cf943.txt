Experience the unforgettable magic of iconic voices with AI narration
ElevenLabs is synonymous with quality, and that's why we're bringing together industry icons to make your reading journey even more enjoyable.
Legendary voices from television, film, literature, and beyond are being made exclusively available through the ElevenReader App, ready to narrate your favorite masterpieces.
Featured icons
Sir Michael Caine™
Sir Michael Caine is a British acting legend who has appeared in over 160 films.
Dr. Maya Angelou™
Dr. Maya Angelou was a renowned poet, author, activist, and civil rights leader.
Burt Reynolds™
Burt Reynolds was a popular American actor known for his roles in action films and comedies.
Sir Laurence Olivier™
Sir Laurence Olivier was a renowned British actor and director, celebrated for his Shakespearean performances.
Judy Garland™
Judy Garland was a legendary American actress and singer, best known for The Wizard of Oz.
John Wayne™
John Wayne was an iconic American actor, famous for his roles in Western films.
Dr. Richard Feynman™
Richard Feynman was a Nobel Prize-winning American physicist, celebrated for his insightful teaching style.
Cid Moreira™
Cid Moreira was a journalist whose unmistakable baritone made him a defining presence in Brazilian media.
Piotr Fronczewski™
Piotr Fronczewski is one of Poland's most recognizable voices through decades of film, theatre, and narration.
Enrique Rocha™
Enrique Rocha was a Mexican actor and narrator whose deep baritone and commanding presence made him one of Latin America’s most iconic screen artists.
