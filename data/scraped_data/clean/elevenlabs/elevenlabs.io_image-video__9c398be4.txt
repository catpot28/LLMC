Frequently asked questions
How does image to video AI work?
Image to video AI uses text prompts and models to animate images into videos. Select a model, describe your video, and generate the output.
What formats are supported?
Images are supported in PNG format. Videos can be exported in MP4 format, up to 4K resolution with H.264/H.265 codecs.
Is AI voice integration available?
Yes, the platform offers AI voice synthesis, voice cloning, and multilingual voice-over. Lip-sync features animate images to create talking videos.
