Frequently asked questions
What image models are available?
Image generation includes Google Nano Banana, Seedream 4, Flux 1 Kontext Pro, Wan 2.5, and OpenAI GPT Image 1. Each model offers unique capabilities for different creative needs, from photorealism to stylized artwork.
What are the key capabilities?
Create high-quality images from text descriptions or reference images. Generate static visuals in any style, upscale for higher resolution, turn images into talking videos with lip-sync, and add voiceovers, music, or sound effects.
What file types can I upload?
Image generation supports PNG format for upload and download. You can also export images to Studio where they can be combined with video and audio elements.
How do I add voices to my images?
After generating your image, click Export to Studio. In Studio, you can add AI voices, create talking photos with lip-sync animation, add music and sound effects, and export complete multimedia projects.
Can I create talking photos?
Yes. Export your image to Studio and use OmniHuman lip-sync to turn static photos into talking videos. Add voiceover narration using AI voice synthesis or your cloned voice for natural, expressive results.
Is image generation free?
Image generation is available on free plans with credit-based usage. Paid plans unlock higher resolution output, priority generation, and access to premium models.
