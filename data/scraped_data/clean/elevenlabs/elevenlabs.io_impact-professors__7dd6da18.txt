Professors shape the future. We’re building alongside them.
The ElevenLabs Impact Program × Professors is a global initiative supporting university educators and their students in thoughtfully exploring voice AI in teaching and research. Participating professors receive one year of ElevenLabs Pro access, along with optional time-bound access for their students.
