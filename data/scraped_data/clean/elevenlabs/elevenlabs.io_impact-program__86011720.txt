Tutorials: How to apply for a free license
Watch tutorial
1. Getting started: individuals with permanent voice loss or blindness/low-vision
Apply for a 5-year, renewable ElevenLabs Pro license if you have permanent voice loss and a 1-year, renewable ElevenReader Ultra license if you are blind/low-vision.
2. Getting started: Clinicians
Apply for a 1-year Pro license if you are a clinician (e.g., SLP, OT, AAC specialist) supporting individuals with permanent voice loss .

NOTE: The purpose of these licenses is to demonstrate voice cloning and support patients. This is NOT to create voice clones for them, or use ElevenLabs for personal, company, or nonprofit projects.
3. Create a voice clone
Learn how to create your Instant or Professional Voice Clone with ElevenLabs, and complete manual voice verification, so that you can continue to communicate in your own voice. See our FAQs for the manual voice verification form.
4. Edit your audio
Sign up to connect with trained volunteers who will carefully edit and enhance your recordings at no cost, creating the highest-quality samples for your Personalized Voice Clone (PVC).
Frequently asked questions
What is the Impact Program?
The ElevenLabs Impact Program provides free access to our AI voice tools for both nonprofit organizations and individuals with accessibility needs. Through tailored partnerships with nonprofits, we help expand access across healthcare, education, and culture. Individuals, clinicians, and blind or low-vision users can also apply directly for free licenses to communicate, learn, and create without limits.
What types of organizations are we looking to partner with?
We partner with nonprofits across healthcare, education, and culture to expand access to voice and audio technology for people and communities who need it most.

Healthcare: We collaborate with healthcare nonprofits and clinics using ElevenLabs to create multilingual patient education, train clinicians, and develop AAC-related programs that enhance accessibility and communication in care. We also work with organizations that support individuals with permanent voice loss, providing free access to personalized AI voices. We’re especially interested in nonprofits serving diagnoses or regions not yet represented by our current partners.

Education: We work with nonprofits that use ElevenLabs to make learning content more inclusive and engaging, from converting curricula, textbooks, and courseware into expressive audio to creating multilingual resources and read-aloud tools that support learners of all abilities. We also partner with organizations supporting individuals who are blind or have low vision to expand access to information through ElevenReader.

Culture: We partner with cultural nonprofits to preserve and amplify human stories through multilingual audio guides, oral histories, and descriptive audio, making art and heritage accessible to global audiences.

Through these partnerships, we’re building a network of organizations advancing accessibility, education, and creative expression — and empowering one million voices to communicate, learn, and create without limits.
How can I express interest in partnering together?
Any nonprofit can express interest and apply by clicking the “I am a nonprofit” button above or by going directly to our Impact Program application page.
If I have permanent voice loss (e.g., MND, mouth cancer, MS) how do I apply for a voice?
First, you will make a free ElevenLabs account. Then, once you are logged in and on the homepage, click on your profile in the bottom lefthand corner. Click, “Apply for Impact Program” and follow the instructions. See the video tutorials above. Look through our partner directory above to learn more about which conditions and geographical areas are supported by our partner organizations.
Can I get assistance editing my audio?
Yes. Through our Impact Voice Lab, trained volunteers will carefully edit and enhance your recordings at NO COST, creating the highest-quality samples for your Personalized Voice Clone (PVC). Learn more here: https://elevenlabs.io/impact-voice-lab
Is it possible to clone a child's voice?
No. ElevenLabs does not permit the cloning of voices under the age of 18. If you have any questions, please contact support.
How will I hear back from ElevenLabs?
Unfortunately, we can't organize calls with every applicant but we guarantee that your application will be looked at carefully. Someone on the partnerships team may reach out to you if we have additional questions about your application.
What are some examples of ways we partnered with organizations?
In partnership with Bridging Voice, The Scott-Morgan Foundation, MND Association and many other organizations globally, ElevenLabs provides free access to their platform to people who have lost or are at risk of losing their ability to speak. These organizations help us distribute free licenses to these individuals and help people get onboarded to our platform.

Additionally, we provide free licenses (Impact Program Grants) to nonprofit organizations completing projects in healthcare, education, and culture.

Check out our blog and the partner directory above for more case studies about how nonprofit organizations are using ElevenLabs technology.

I already have a paid ElevenLabs account and I now qualify for an Impact Program code as a person with voice loss. Can I transfer my account and keep any personal voice clones I have created?
Yes. Select “Apply for the Impact Program,” and follow the instructions (remember to submit any required documentation). If approved, your ElevenLabs account will be moved automatically to a free Pro account. Any existing Instant and Pro Voice Clones will remain available to you.

Can SLPs, AAC specialists, and other clinicians get free access to ElevenLabs?
Yes. Clinicians, AAC specialists, and other professionals working with qualifying Impact Program participants can apply for free 12-month access to ElevenLabs Pro through the Pro Clinician Account. This program provides clinicians with a year of complimentary access to explore and understand voice cloning technology—allowing them to practice, demonstrate, and learn how to best support their patients through the voice creation process.

To apply, first create a free ElevenLabs account. Once logged in and on the homepage, click on your profile in the bottom-left corner, select “Apply for Impact Program,” choose “Clinicians and Staff,” and follow the instructions to submit the required documentation. See tutorials above.
Can clinicians create Professional Voice Clones (PVCs) for their patients within their own ElevenLabs account?
No. Clinicians should only use their ElevenLabs Pro Clinician Account to practice and demonstrate the voice cloning process. While clinicians are encouraged to assist and guide patients through every step, all Professional Voice Clones (PVCs) must be created and stored within the patient’s own ElevenLabs account, not within the clinician’s account. This ensures that patients retain full control, privacy, and ownership of their voice data.
How long are Impact Program accounts free for?
Individuals with permanent voice loss qualify for 5 years free and renewable ElevenLabs licenses. Clinicians/professionals and individual nonprofit organizations qualify for 12-month free and renewable ElevenLabs licenses. Individuals who are blind or have low-vision qualify for 12-month free and renewable ElevenReader Ultra licenses.
If my voice has changed since the time my recordings were made, how do I complete Manual Voice Verification?
Watch the tutorial above "3. Create a voice clone" for step-by-step instructions. The manual voice verification form can be found at this link: https://drive.google.com/file/d/18rcBAg5Lc_gTHjNCeRiCpQHAWg6O9zTp/view?usp=sharing
