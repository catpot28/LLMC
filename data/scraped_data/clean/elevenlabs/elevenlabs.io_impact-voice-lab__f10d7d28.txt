Impact Voice Lab: Volunteer to edit audio and change lives
Volunteer to clean audio files for Voice Creation or request assistance to preserve your voice
What is Impact Voice Lab?
People who have lost their ability to speak due to disease are now able to re-create their natural speaking voice through ElevenLabs. Clean, high-quality audio is essential for this process, but not everyone has the tools or skills to make this happen. This page connects volunteers with patients to create clear audio recordings and ensure every voice is heard.
Volunteer as an Audio Editor
Make a meaningful impact by helping people preserve their voices. As a volunteer audio editor, you’ll assist in cleaning up audio recordings, removing background noise, and improving clarity. Often what people have left of their voices are voice mails, wedding videos, or archival recordings. They’re not studio-quality files, so we need to be flexible and creative for the sake of the patients who, with the help of the Audio Editor, will regain their comfort in life.

Request Audio Editing Support
If you struggle with dysarthria, know that your voice is still yours. With just a past audio recording—no need for scripts or set phrases—we can help bring it back to life. Sign up to connect with trained volunteers who will carefully edit and enhance your recordings, creating the highest-quality samples for your Personalized Voice Clone (PVC).
