Frequently asked questions
How do ElevenAgents determine whether a lead is high intent?
Agents apply your scoring model - combining answers to required questions, behavioral signals, and firmographic data - to produce a numeric score and a deterministic handoff decision.
Can agents write back to our CRM and calendar?
Yes. We provide native integrations and webhooks to update records, create opportunities, log transcripts, and propose or book calendar slots automatically.
How do you ensure compliance for telemarketing?
We simulate millions of conversations, enforce deterministic guardrails for required disclosures and opt-outs, and maintain secure logs to support audits and regulatory reviews.
Which LLMs can we run with ElevenAgents?
You can use ElevenLabs-hosted LLMs for co-located low-latency performance or connect your own LLM keys - enabling flexibility for performance, cost, and compliance requirements.
What ROI can we expect from telemarketing lead qualification?
Typical benefits include dramatically reduced speed-to-lead, higher qualified-lead throughput during peak demand, lower cost per qualified lead, and improved rep productivity. We recommend running a short pilot to measure baseline KPIs and project full-scale impact.
