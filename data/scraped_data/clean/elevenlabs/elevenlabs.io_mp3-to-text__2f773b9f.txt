Frequently asked questions
What file formats are supported for transcription?
We support all major audio formats including MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, and FLAC. Upload directly from your device or cloud storage—no conversion required.
How fast can I convert MP3 to text?
Our AI processes MP3 files in seconds—even long recordings. With Scribe, you get accurate, speaker-labeled transcripts almost instantly.
Can I edit the MP3 transcript after it’s generated?
Yes. You can edit directly in the transcript editor. Click on any word to adjust, cut, or format. Word-level timestamps and speaker labels make fine-tuning simple.
What makes this MP3 to text converter better than others?
Our transcripts capture more than words. Scribe detects speaker turns, timing, and audio events like applause or laughter—producing structured transcripts in 99 languages.

Download your transcript in multiple formats—TXT, DOCX, PDF, JSON, SRT, VTT, or HTML. Perfect for editing, subtitles, publishing, or integrating into your workflow.
