Frequently asked questions
What MP4 formats do you support for transcription?
We support MP4 along with MOV, AVI, MKV, and other major formats. Just upload your file—our MP4 to text converter handles the rest, no conversion required.
How accurate are the transcripts?
Our Speech to Text model, Scribe, achieves industry-leading accuracy in 99 languages. It provides speaker labels, timestamps, and audio-event tagging to ensure context-rich transcripts.
Can I edit the transcript after it's generated?
Yes. Edit directly in the interface—click on words to make changes, split or merge segments, or add notes. Edits are fast and precise with word-level timing.
What export formats are available?
You can export transcripts in TXT, DOCX, PDF, JSON, SRT, VTT, and HTML. Each option is optimized for use cases like captioning, publishing, or indexing.
Can I use this for multilingual content?
Absolutely. Our model supports 99 languages and works seamlessly with multilingual MP4 files—whether for podcasts, global meetings, or international video content.
