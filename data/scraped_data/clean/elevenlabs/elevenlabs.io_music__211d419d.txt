Music API & SDK
Generate studio-quality music with prompts, audio reference, and section-by-section editing across any genre, language, or vocal style.

import
{ ElevenLabsClient }
from
"@elevenlabs/elevenlabs-js"
;

const
elevenlabs
= new ElevenLabsClient();

const
track
=
await
elevenlabs.music.compose({

modelId:
"music_v2"
,

prompt:
"Create an intense, fast-paced electronic track for a high-adrenaline video game scene. Use driving synth arpeggios, punchy drums, distorted bass, glitch effects, and aggressive rhythmic textures. The tempo should be fast, 130–150 bpm, with rising tension, quick transitions, and dynamic energy bursts."
,

musicLengthMs: 10000
,
});

// Save the track to a file

await
pipeline(Readable.from(track), createWriteStream(
"path/to/music.mp3"
));

Frequently asked questions
What languages does Music v2 support?
Music v2 supports a variety of languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and many more.
Is there an API?
Yes, Music v1 is currently available via ElevenAPI. Please see the Music API documentation for more details. Music v2 is coming soon to ElevenAPI. Please contact our sales team for early access.
Can I use the music commercially?
Yes. On Self-Serve plans, online and offline commercial use is permitted, except for film, TV, and Studio Games. On Enterprise plans, all online and offline commercial use is permitted. For further information see the Music Terms (
elevenlabs.io/eleven-music-model-specific-terms
).
What are the best practices to prompt Music v2?
Make sure to read our prompting guide to get the best out of Music v2.
What genres does Music v2 support?
With Music v2 you have complete control over genre, style, and structure. Test it and share your best generations with us.
