Frequently asked questions
What local deployment options are available?
We offer two deployment paths: on-premise for GPU-enabled servers, and on-device for edge and embedded environments with constrained compute and memory.
Does any data leave my environment?
No. All inference and data processing run entirely within your infrastructure. License validation and telemetry are optional and configurable, including fully air-gapped setups.
Which model versions are available?
Our on-premise and on-device models are built specifically for these environments. They do not mirror the full cloud portfolio, but they reflect our highest quality standards. Updates are delivered on a controlled cadence aligned with enterprise needs for stability and long-term support.
Can models be fine-tuned or customized?
Yes. We support fine-tuning for specific languages or dialects. Deeper customization is also available - contact us to discuss your requirements.
What are the hardware requirements?
On-premise deployments run on standard GPU-enabled servers. On-device deployments are optimized for entry-level GPUs, NPUs, and modern CPU and ARM-based chips. Contact us for detailed specifications.
How is pricing structured?
Pricing is defined case by case to reflect differences in deployment environments and use cases. It typically includes a license fee and a usage-based component. Contact sales for a custom quote.
When will local deployments be available?
We expect initial on-premise and on-device releases in the first half of 2026.
