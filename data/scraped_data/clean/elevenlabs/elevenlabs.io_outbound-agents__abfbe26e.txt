Frequently asked questions
How quickly can we launch an outbound campaign?
Most teams can run pilot campaigns in days. A typical rollout includes data import, script validation, simulation testing, and staged production traffic.
Is outbound calling compliant with regulations?
Yes. ElevenAgents enforces region-specific disclosures, suppression lists, and consent capture. We provide searchable logs and exportable records to support audits.
How do agents handle live handoffs?
Agents escalate to humans with full context. conversation history, intent, and account details sync to your CRM or CCaaS so handoffs are seamless.
Can agents update our CRM automatically?
Yes - agents log outcomes, update lead records, and trigger downstream workflows via native connectors or APIs.
What ROI can we expect from outbound AI agents?
Teams typically see faster follow-up, higher connect rates, and lower cost per dial compared with manual calling. ROI depends on volume, workflows, and integration depth; we work with customers to model outcomes.
