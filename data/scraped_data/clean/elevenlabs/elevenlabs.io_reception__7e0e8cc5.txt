Frequently asked questions
Do I need a new phone number?
If you're based in the US, Reception by ElevenAgents assigns you a dedicated phone number during setup. You can forward your existing business line to it or share the new number directly.
If you're based outside the US, there's some brief additional setup required to connect your phone number.
Does the AI receptionist sync with my calendar?
Reception by ElevenAgents integrates directly with Google calendar and has a calendar built in so you can manages all appointments in one place.
Can it handle multiple calls at the same time?
Yes. Reception by ElevenAgents can handle many simultaneous calls so your callers don't get put on hold.
What languages can the AI Receptionist speak?
Reception by ElevenAgents supports English, Spanish, and 70+ languages across a wide range of accents.
Does the AI receptionist sound natural?
Reception by ElevenAgents sounds like a real person, not a phone tree. It's built on ElevenLabs' voice AI - the same technology used by major media companies and millions of creators.
What countries does it support?
Reception by ElevenAgents is now available for businesses globally.
How much does it cost?
Reception by ElevenAgents offers plans starting at $22/month.
Can it book appointments?
Yes. Callers describe what they need, and Reception by ElevenAgents checks your staff availability, matches the right service, and books the appointment.
Is Reception by ElevenAgents secure?
Reception is built on ElevenAgents, which supports millions of AI agents for businesses of all sizes. ElevenAgents is ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 certified and complies with applicable data privacy regulations, including CCPA.
Does it support HIPAA-compliance?
Reception by ElevenAgents does not currently support HIPAA compliance so of your team handles protected health information, the best option is the broader ElevenAgents platform which can support HIPPA compliance for eligible customers though a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
