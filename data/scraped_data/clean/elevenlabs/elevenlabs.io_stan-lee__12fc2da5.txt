Frequently asked questions
What is the Stan Lee Collection?
The Stan Lee Collection is a set of AI creative templates inspired by the legendary comic book creator. Each one places you inside a different comic book scene, from cover star to action figure, using a single photo.
How do I use the templates?
Open any template in the ElevenLabs app, upload a photo, and let the model generate your result. You can refine the look and download the final image in seconds.
Is it free to try?
You can explore the collection and start creating with a free ElevenLabs account. Higher volume and premium options are available on paid plans.
What can I make with it?
Silver age comic covers, pop art hero shots, a stroll with Stan Lee, and your own collectible action figure. Mix and match to build a full comic book persona.
Do I need any design experience?
None at all. The templates handle the art direction, so all you bring is a photo and an idea.
