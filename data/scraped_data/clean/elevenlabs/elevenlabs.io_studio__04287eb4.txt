Frequently asked questions
What is Studio designed for?
Studio is our end-to-end workflow for producing audiobooks, podcasts, and narrated videos. Structure long-form projects, assign speakers, align audio and video on a timeline, add music and SFX, and publish with captions
Does Studio support multilingual audio and captions?
Yes. Studio supports 32 languages and recognizes mixed-language text. You can generate video transcripts and multilingual subtitles and export captions
Can I assign particular speakers to specific text fragments?
Yes. Assign different voices to selected text fragments or sections to give each character or presenter a unique voice
Which file types can I upload?
Studio supports EPUB, PDF, TXT, HTML, MP4, MOV, MP3, WAV, and FLAC. You can also initialize your project from a URL
How does Studio integrate with other tools from your ecosystem?
Studio integrates with Voice Library, Voice Design, Professional Voice Cloning, Multilingual models, and Eleven Music for a complete production workflow
How do I edit video online with Studio?
Import MP4 or MOV and use the timeline editor to trim, merge, and edit video. Align audio and video, place music and SFX, and export ready-to-publish files
Can I add voiceover to video?
Yes. Record or generate a voiceover, then add voiceover to video and sync it on the timeline. You can also edit the narration by editing text
How do I add music to video or audio?
Use Eleven Music to generate a soundtrack or import tracks. Add background music to video, mix levels, and loop ambient SFX for seamless beds
Can Studio transcribe audio to text or video to text?
Yes. Upload audio or video to generate accurate transcripts. Edit speech by editing text and export transcripts or captions
How do I remove background noise from audio or video?
Use voice isolator to reduce background noise and remove reverb. It cleans dialogue for podcasts, voiceovers, and on-set recordings
Can I merge videos or fix out-of-sync media?
Yes. Use the video merger to join clips and the timeline to sync audio and video precisely
Does Studio include an online audio editor?
Yes. Edit MP3, WAV, and more. Trim, adjust, regenerate lines, and apply voice changer or isolator for clean, consistent output
