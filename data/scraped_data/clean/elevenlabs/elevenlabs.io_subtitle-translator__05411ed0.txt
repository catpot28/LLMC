Frequently asked questions
What subtitle formats do you support for translation?
We support all major formats including SRT, VTT, SUB, ASS, SBV, and STL. You can also generate subtitles directly from audio or video files.
How accurate are the translations?
Our AI delivers industry-leading accuracy across 150+ languages. It leverages context, speaker detection, and timestamp alignment to ensure subtitles remain precise.
Can I edit translations after they’re generated?
Yes. You can edit directly in the interface—adjust text, fix timing, or merge/split segments. Word-level timestamps make editing simple.
What export formats are available?
You can export translated subtitles in SRT, VTT, TXT, DOCX, PDF, JSON, and HTML. Each format is optimized for publishing or archiving.
Does it support multilingual outputs and dubbing?
Absolutely. You can translate subtitles into 150+ languages and pair them with natural AI voice dubbing for fully localized content.
