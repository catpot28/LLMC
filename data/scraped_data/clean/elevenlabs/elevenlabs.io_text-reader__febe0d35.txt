Frequently asked questions
What content can I import into the app?
You can upload files like ePubs and PDFs, import articles via links, scan content from images, or even copy and paste text directly into the app.
Can I export audio from the app?
No, today the app is only for personal listening and reading. You can only create an export audio on our web platform.
Is the app free?
Yes. The app is completely free to download and use today. Listening to content on the app will not consume credits from your monthly web plan. We do plan to eventually launch some premium version of the app, but even then we will maintain a generous free plan.
Which languages does the app support?
The app lets you listen to text content voiced in
32 languages
today. Currently, the app is localized for 8 languages and includes popular listening content in English-only. We plan to localize the app into additional languages to continue supporting our users around the world soon.
What voices are available in the app?
The app features hundreds of curated, high-quality voices produced with ElevenLabs, including a selection from our Iconic Voices collection. We provide recommended voices based on your language settings and any voice clones you create from your paid ElevenLabs account on the web may be enabled for your personal use on the ElevenReader app. The app does not support voices created with Voice Design on the web. By deleting one of your voices from the app, you will also delete this voice from those available to you on the web.
