Frequently asked questions
What is drawing AI?
Drawing AI uses artificial intelligence to create and edit images and videos based on user input. It can generate visuals from text prompts or enhance existing content.
How does the platform integrate voice?
The platform integrates voice by offering AI voice synthesis, cloning, and multilingual voice-over. Users can add audio to visuals or create lip-sync animations.
Can I generate videos with the free plan?
The free plan supports image generation only. Paid plans are required to access video generation features and additional tools.
What models are available for image creation?
Available models include Google Nano Banana, Seedream 4, Flux 1 Kontext Pro, Wan 2.5, and OpenAI GPT Image 1 for diverse creation options.
What output formats are supported?
Image outputs are in PNG format, while videos are exported as MP4 files using H.264 or H.265 codecs, offering high-quality results.
