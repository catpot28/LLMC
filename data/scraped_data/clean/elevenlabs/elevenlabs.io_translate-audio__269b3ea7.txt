Frequently asked questions
What file types are supported?
You can upload MP3, WAV, or M4A files directly. Each file is processed automatically and translated into your selected target language.
How many languages can I translate to?
You can translate audio between 90+ supported languages, with natural-sounding pronunciation and tone for every translation.
How accurate is the translation?
AI models provide 99% transcription accuracy and preserve natural timing and emotion for realistic-sounding results.
Do I need an account to get started?
No — you can translate directly in your browser. Just upload your audio and click “Translate Now.”

Yes. You can export your translations as text (TXT), subtitles (SRT or VTT), or audio (MP3).

Yes. All uploads are encrypted, processed safely, and deleted automatically to protect your privacy.
