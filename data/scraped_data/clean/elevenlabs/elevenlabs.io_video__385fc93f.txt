Frequently asked questions
What video models are available?
Video generation includes OpenAI Sora 2 Pro, Sora 2, Google Veo 3.1, Veo 3, Kling 2.5, Seedance 1 Pro, and Wan 2.5. Each model offers unique capabilities for different creative needs, from photorealism to stylized animation.
What are the key capabilities?
Create high-quality videos from text descriptions or reference images. Generate dynamic video in any style, upscale to 4K resolution, add lip-sync animation, and combine with voiceovers, music, or sound effects in Studio.
What file types can I upload and download?
Video generation supports MP4 format with H.264 or H.265 encoding. You can upload reference images in PNG format and download videos up to 4K resolution.
How do I add voices and music to videos?
After generating your video, click Export to Studio. In Studio, add AI voices, lip-sync animation, music, sound effects, captions, and timeline editing for professional video production.
Can I upscale videos to 4K?
Yes. Use Topaz Upscale to boost video resolution up to 4x, delivering sharper, high-fidelity output at 4K quality. Upscaling preserves detail while enhancing clarity and visual quality.
Is video generation free?
Video generation requires a paid plan with credit-based usage. Free plans support image generation. Paid plans unlock video models, higher resolution output, and priority generation.
