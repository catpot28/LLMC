ElevenLabs Voice Library Addendum
Last Updated 6 March 2026
This Voice Library addendum applies if you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland, or UK. If you live outside of the EEA, Switzerland, or UK, this other
Voice Library Addendum
applies to you.
This Voice Library Addendum (“
Addendum
”) supplements the
ElevenLabs Terms of Service
(“
Terms of Service
”) and, together with the Terms of Service, applies to your use of our Voice Library Service, including the sharing of User Voice Models on or through the Voice Library Service. Your use of the Voice Library Service is also subject to our general
Prohibited Use Policy
and our
Privacy Policy
. Defined terms used in this Addendum have the meanings set forth in the
Terms of Service
.
Please read this Addendum carefully. If you do not agree with or accept this Addendum, you may not use the Voice Library Service, including making your User Voice Models available on the Voice Library Service.
1. Content on the Voice Library
The Voice Library Service makes available ElevenLabs Voice Models, provided by ElevenLabs, as well as User Voice Models, provided by ElevenLabs users, including you, through the Services. By making a User Voice Model available in our Voice Library, you may be eligible to receive compensation in the form of Financial Rewards (each a “
Reward
”), pursuant to the terms of this Addendum. We may charge fees for the access and use of these User Voice Models as set forth in this Addendum and through the Services.
The rights in and to ElevenLabs Voice Models and User Voice Models, including those made available as part of the Voice Library Service, are set out in our Terms of Service.
2. Sharing User Voice Models in the Voice Library
You may share User Voice Models you’ve generated on our platform through the Voice Library Service. If you chose to do so, you hereby authorize us to make available the User Voice Models for others to access and use through the Voice Library Service in accordance with the Terms of Service and this Voice Library Addendum (“
Voice Library License
”). For clarity, others may be able to generate Output with the User Voice Models you have shared in our Library.
The Voice Library Sharing Options available are described on our Website (the “
Options
”). We may update, discontinue or terminate Options from time to time in accordance with the variation provisions set out in the Terms of Service.
3. Eligibility & Conditions
As a condition of making available any User Voice Model in the Voice Library, you agree that you satisfy (and will continue to satisfy) the following requirements:
You must create and share a User Voice Model based on your voice using our technology and platform. You must not create and share a User Voice Model based on another person’s voice or an altered version of your voice.
You have the appropriate rights and permissions to create and share the User Voice Model.
You will use the Services in full compliance with our policies and other guidelines available on our Website.
You must consent to the processing of your voice data as provided for in the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and any additional consent forms presented during the user signup process.
4. Sharing Options
The following settings and Options may be available with respect to your sharing of User Voice Models in the Voice Library:
Financial Rewards
: Financial compensation (“
Financial Rewards
”) are only available for certain types of voice models. Financial Rewards will be payable in US dollars or for users in the European Economic Area (“
EEA
”), the UK, or Switzerland, your local currency. To be able to receive any such Financial Reward for which you are eligible, you will need to create a Stripe Connect account. By creating a Stripe Connect account, you acknowledge and understand that there are third-party terms associated with such accounts provided by Stripe to which you are bound. We may impose additional terms associated with Financial Rewards or request additional information from you to participate in Financial Rewards, and you acknowledge and understand that you must comply with any additional terms or requests for information to continue your participation in or be able to receive Financial Rewards. Your participation in or receipt of Financial Rewards is at our sole discretion, and we may suspend or terminate your participation in or access to Financial Rewards.
Notice Period
: When you share your User Voice Model in the Voice Library, you will be asked to specify a “Notice Period.” You may choose to remove a User Voice Model you shared through the Voice Library Service at any time. However, (a) any Outputs generated using your User Voice Model prior to the end of the Notice Period will continue to exist and remain available for use thereafter, and (b) the User Voice Model will remain accessible for the duration of the Notice Period to users that added that User Voice Model to their account prior to removal of the User Voice Model from the Voice Library Service. For instance, if you select a Notice Period of 30 days, it means that the User Voice Model will be available for 30 days in other users’ accounts starting from the date on which you decide to remove the User Voice Model. This is to ensure that users who have added your User Voice Model to their account have enough time to migrate to a different User Voice Model. If you select a longer Notice Period, the rate to which you may be entitled per this Addendum may be higher than the rate which would apply under a shorter Notice Period, but your voice may continue to be used for a longer period of time after you remove it. Once you share your User Voice Model in the Voice Library, you will not be able to reduce your Notice Period, but you will be able to increase it.
Rate System
: When sharing your User Voice Model in the Voice Library, the following rate system for rewards will apply:
Default Rate
: Your rewards are calculated based on factors that we determine, such as the pricing tier of users that use the User Voice Model, the number of characters they generate using the User Voice Model, and the Notice Period you select. At the time you submit the User Voice Model, we may present to you our good-faith estimate of what we expect your Reward amount to be based on currently available information, however this is not a guarantee of earnings. The variability between expected and actual Reward amounts is determined, in part, by the type of user that uses the User Voice Model (e.g., a Free User vs. a Paid User subscribed to an Enterprise plan) and how much that user type pays us per character. Our estimates assume that usage of your model will follow a typical distribution of user types across pricing tiers. Both Free Users and Paid Users will be able to use the User Voice Model, however in no case will Rewards be provided for use of your User Voice Model by a Free User.
Live Moderation
: If you enable the “Live Moderation enabled?” setting, we will use tools to check whether requests to use your User Voice Model contain text that belongs to a number of prohibited categories. These tools are intended to, but are not guaranteed to, stop users from applying your User Voice Model to text belonging to a prohibited category. Moderation may result in extra latency for users using your User Voice. You can change the "Live Moderation enabled?" setting at any time. However, the change will only apply to new users that add your User Voice Model to their account after the setting has been changed. Existing users of your User Voice Model will remain under the previous moderation setting.
Reader App
: If you enable the “Available in Reader App” toggle, we may make your User Voice Model available to users of the ElevenLabs Reader App and you will earn rewards in USD per 1,000 characters when users select your User Voice Model for audio generation through the ElevenLabs Reader App.
ElevenAgents:
We may make your User Voice Model available to users of ElevenAgents and you will earn rewards when our customers select your User Voice Model when using ElevenAgents. Specifically, your rewards will depend on the number of conversation minutes that have utilized your User Voice Model as part of our customers' usage of ElevenAgents. Further, if a particular conversation powered by ElevenAgents utilizes multiple User Voice Models, the rewards will be distributed proportionally, based on the duration of each User Voice Model being utilized and other relevant factors. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, if your User Voice Model is being used via a Free-tier plan, such usage will not generate rewards. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, your earning rate for the ElevenAgents product is capped at $0.01 per minute.
5. Personal Data
Nothing in this Addendum shall be construed as waiving or limiting any rights or protections conferred to the user under personal data protection and privacy laws. For more information about our use of personal data, please read our
Privacy Policy
.
6. Termination
You can terminate your participation in the Voice Library service by deleting the User Voice Models you have shared from your account, deleting your account or by otherwise terminating your relationship with us, subject to the Notice Period described above (and further subject to applicable law in your jurisdiction). In the event you terminate your participation in the Voice Library service, your User Voice Models will be available until the end of the Notice Period as set forth above.
The termination of your participation in the Voice Library service will not impact the transfer or licensing of intellectual property and other rights up to the point of termination.
7. Payment details
We will use reasonable efforts to issue Reward payments on a weekly basis.
