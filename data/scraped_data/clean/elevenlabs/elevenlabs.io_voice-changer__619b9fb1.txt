Frequently asked questions
What are common uses for AI voice changers?
AI voice changers are used across many industries: in gaming for creating character voices, in content creation for unique narrative voices on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, in podcasts for adding variety, in marketing for professional voiceovers, in e-learning for engaging educational content, and in entertainment for dubbing and animation.
What makes ElevenLabs the best AI voice changer?
ElevenLabs offers unmatched quality, naturalness, and customization options. Our technology preserves emotional delivery including whispers, laughs, and subtle cues, something most voice changers cannot achieve. With thousands of voices, 29 language support, fast processing times (~400ms), and powerful API integration, ElevenLabs serves both casual creators and enterprise users.
How many languages does the voice changer support?
The voice changer supports 29 languages including English (USA, UK, Australia, Canada), Japanese, Chinese, German, Hindi, French, Korean, Portuguese, Italian, Spanish, Indonesian, Dutch, Turkish, Filipino, Polish, Swedish, Bulgarian, Romanian, Arabic, Czech, Greek, Finnish, Croatian, Malay, Slovak, Danish, Tamil, Ukrainian, and Russian.
Can I use my own custom or cloned voice?
Absolutely. You can use any voice from our library or create custom voices through our voice cloning feature. Simply provide your custom voice's ID when using the voice changer, and your recordings will be transformed into that specific voice.
How many languages and accents does the voice changer support?
The voice changer supports 29 languages, including many regional accents and dialects. We regularly update our collection with new languages and accents. For a particular accent, record audio in that accent and use it with the voice changer.
Can I change my voice to a different gender?
Yes. Our AI voice changer can transform voices across genders while maintaining natural delivery. Change a male voice to female, female to male, or to any voice in our library regardless of the original speaker's gender.
What audio file formats are supported?
Our voice changer accepts common audio formats including MP3, WAV, and AAC. You can also record audio directly through our web interface using your microphone.
How much does the voice changer cost?
Our voice changer is accessible with a generous free plan that lets you experience the technology at no cost. Paid plans start at competitive price points and offer additional features, more processing time, and API access. Enterprise pricing is available for organizations with high-volume needs.
Is there API access for developers?
Yes. Our Speech to Speech API provides programmatic access to voice transformation capabilities. Developers can integrate voice changing into applications, games, and services with comprehensive documentation and SDKs. The API supports batch processing, custom voices, and advanced parameter controls.
