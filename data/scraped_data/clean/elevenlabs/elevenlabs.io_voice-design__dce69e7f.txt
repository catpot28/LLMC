Frequently asked questions
What is ElevenLabs Voice Design?
Voice Design allows you to create a unique voice from a text prompt by describing the unique characteristics of the voice you need.

Where can I find Voice Design?
Head to Voice -> My Voices -> Add a new voice -> Voice Design.
When should I use Voice Design versus looking for a voice in the Voice Library?
We created Voice Design to help you fill the gaps when the exact voice they are looking for isn’t available in the Voice Library. Professional Voice Clones are still the highest quality voices on our platform and if there is one available that fits your needs, we recommend using it. But if you can’t find a suitable voice in the Voice Library to fit your vision, use Voice Design.
What types of voices can I create with Voice Design?
Voice Design is great for creating character voices like “An angry old pirate, shouting” or “A massive evil ogre, troll” and realistic voices like “A young Indian female with a soft, high voice. Conversational, slow and calm”. Refer to our
prompting guide
for tips and tricks.
