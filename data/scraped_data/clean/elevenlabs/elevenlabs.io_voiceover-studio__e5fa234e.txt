Frequently asked questions
How much does Voiceover Studio cost?
Voiceover Studio is a single workflow that combines text to speech, speech to speech, and sound effects. Text to speech and speech to speech usage within Voiceover studio costs the same as within our speech synthesis tool - roughly 1000 characters per minute of audio.
Can I have multiple speakers in one Voiceover?
Yes, you can add additional speakers by selecting "Add Voiceover Track". You'll have the option to choose a voice from our library and generate dialogue from that speaker.
How can I use Voiceover Studio?
Voiceover Studio is great for creating automated, AI voiceovers for social media content on Youtube, Instagram, and Tiktok as well as commercials, podcasts and courses.
