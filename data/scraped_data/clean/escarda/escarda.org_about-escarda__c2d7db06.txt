We are Farmers, Engineers and AI Specialists.

ESCARDA develops laser‑weeding systems for farms that need reliable, herbicide‑free weed control. We work closely with farmers – improvements come directly from field practice.

Daniel

He

Software Engineer

I studied Computer Science at TU Darmstadt and completed my Master’s degree in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at TU Munich, including my thesis at the German Aerospace Center (DLR). At Escarda, I can apply my AI expertise where it really matters: outdoors, in the field, in real‑world applications.

That unique combination of high‑tech and hands‑on impact is what convinced me. At Escarda, I take AI out of the lab and into the real world—making agriculture smarter. And honestly, as a computer scientist, it’s refreshing to get some sunshine and fresh air along the way.

Lucy

Harrison

Sales

“Because you are the foundation of everything” – agriculture truly matters to me.

I’ve been working in the agricultural sector since 2009, whether on farms in New Zealand, on an organic farm, or in plant research. Today, as a trained agribusiness manager at Escarda, I represent your voice and serve as your point of contact in sales. My heart belongs to the most important job in the world: agriculture.

Marco

Climaco

CEO

For me, Escarda represents the rare combination of true high‑end technology and real industrial scalability—that’s exactly what I stand for. I am an entrepreneur and managing director with more than 20 years of experience in building and scaling technology‑driven companies.

My focus is on turning complex hardware and high‑tech products into industrially viable solutions and bringing them successfully to market. Escarda brings all of this together for me: technological excellence, industrial substance, and genuine market potential.

YOU?!

Ready to join us?

As you can see, we’ve got big plans – and we’re working on topics that truly make a difference.

If you’d like to be part of it, we’d love to hear from you!

Bring in your ideas, help shape the future with us, and grow alongside a team that’s driven to make an impact.

We support one of the most important professions in the world – with calm, practical, field proven engineering.

Industry at our Back.

Lasers in our DNA. Results in the Field.

We work with those who build – not those who merely imagine.

Together with leading European dealer networks, research partners, and OEM integrators, we operate in an ecosystem that moves real technology: market reach, field‑proven insights, engineering depth, and reliable scalability.

This network ensures our technology never gets stuck between idea and prototype –
it works, it grows, and it delivers across Europe.

The Future of Industry – Made in Berlin.

Escarda stands on a technological foundation with real weight: the
B.I.G. – Berlin.Industrial.Group.

A collective of companies that have spent decades developing, industrializing, and deploying laser, engineering, and AI technologies.

The B.I.G. gives us access to infrastructure you can’t copy: the Laser Application Center, high‑end engineering, and experts who build industrial solutions every single day. It’s more than capability – it’s technological substance, and it’s our strongest advantage.

Meet us!

We value real conversations. Come by, share your challenges, and let’s discover what laser‑weeding can do for your farm.
