Homburg is located in Stiens, the Netherlands, and produces its own draincleaners, sensor beams, and innovative SmartFarming solutions like GPS implement steering.

Pamberger Landmaschinentechnik GmbH is based in Obritzberg, Austria, and is a trusted partner for agricultural machinery, service, and precision farming solutions. The company offers sales, maintenance, and repair of tractors and implements, complemented by modern GPS and RTK guidance systems for efficient and precise field operations.

In addition to a broad portfolio of leading agricultural brands, Pamberger supports farmers with technical expertise and reliable service, providing integrated solutions for modern, future‑oriented agriculture.
