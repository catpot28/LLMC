Smart Farming Explained: How AI Enables Precision Farming at Plant Level

Definition:

Smart farming refers to the use of digital technologies such as sensors, AI, and data processing to support decision-making in agriculture.

Smart farming creates value only when technology leads to precise action. Data alone does not improve field performance unless it enables accurate, timely decisions.

How Smart Farming Supports Precision Farming:

Smart farming provides the technological foundation that allows precision farming to operate at plant level. AI-based camera systems enable machines to distinguish crops from weeds, detect growth stages, and act in real time.

Key components of smart farming in weed control:

Computer vision for crop and weed identification

AI-based real-time decision-making

Immediate execution of targeted actions

Smart Farming vs. Precision Farming:

Smart farming focuses on how decisions are generated using technology. Precision farming focuses on where and how actions are applied in the field. Smart farming enables precision farming but does not replace it.

From Data to Action:

Effective smart farming systems analyze visual data in real time and translate insights directly into physical action. This is essential for scalable, reliable precision farming.

At Escarda, smart farming technologies such as AI and computer vision are used to enable precision farming through plant-level laser weed control – integrated into existing machinery and dealer-supported workflows.

When smart farming serves precision farming, technology becomes a controlled, reliable tool rather than an experimental risk.
