The One That

Lasts

We're Fairphone, from Amsterdam. For everyone that wants tech to fuel life, not interrupt it.
We’re all about giving you quality tech that’s built to last and made better.

Our Journey so Far

For over a decade, we’ve done what others said couldn’t be done. Modular phones. Built to last. Made fair from the inside out.
Longer lifecycles, smarter upgrades, and real progress with every new generation. What sets Fairphone apart? It’s not just our values. It’s how those values show up — in the product itself.

Designed with Care

At Fairphone, innovation doesn't mean adding more — it means adding intention. It means asking: what do people really need? And how do we design for that — while still thinking about the makers of our devices and the planet itself?

Innovation doesn’t start on the surface. It starts at the source — in how a phone is made, who it empowers, and how long it lasts. We call it impact innovation. Great performance. Real values.

Make an

Impact

Pushing boundaries isn’t a feature — it’s our foundation.

It’s more important than ever for companies to take responsibility. To lead by example. To show what’s possible when you build not just for the next product cycle — but for the next generation. And that’s exactly what Fairphone is all about.

Net Zero by 2045

The biggest commitment we can all make is to hold onto our phones for longer. If we did that, we’d reduce carbon emissions created through new phone production, transport, waste, and usage. That’s why our devices are built to last. Beyond that, as a company, we realize we can do more. And for us, that means going to net zero.

SEE OUR IMPACT REPORT

Meet the Makers

Better pay. Safer factories. Less harm to people and planet. We work directly with our suppliers to improve wages and build safer, fairer workplaces. Together, we co-create real plans — and invest in making them happen.

Since 2019, we’ve invested over $1 million in living wage bonuses for the people who make our products. It’s one step toward a more equal supply chain — and we’re just getting started.
