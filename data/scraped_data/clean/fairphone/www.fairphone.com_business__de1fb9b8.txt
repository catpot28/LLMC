Better for your bottomline

Save big in the long run

When you switch to Fairphone, you are switching to bigger savings in the long run. Fairphone devices stay in active use for twice as long as the industry average. And the longer you keep your Fairphone going, the longer you can delay spending on a new device. Here’s why holding onto a Fairphone is easier than the competition.

Industry-leading software support

Fairphone smartphones come with a promise of eight years of software support from the year they are launched. That means you can always expect regular software updates and security patches to make sure your device is as safe and secure as possible, always. You can also expect timely roll-outs of major Android OS upgrades within this period.

Extended five-year warranty

Fairphone leads the industry when it comes to warranty periods. All our devices come with standard two-year warranty. With our smartphones, we push that further with an extended three-year warranty... for free! That’s a total of five years of warranty coverage. Because accidents can happen. And if they do, you can rest assured we got you covered.

Easy to repair modular design

According to insurance statistics, screen damage accounts for 67.4% of phone repairs, with battery problems coming in at 33.9%. Fairphone’s modular design minimizes repair costs and downtime for both of these problems and more. Your employees can replace their screen or battery in minutes. That too, at a fraction of the cost of non-modular phones.
