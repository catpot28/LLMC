Reporting

How to report a security vulnerability

If you have identified a security vulnerability on one or more
of our devices,
please report it to

[email protected]
and provide us with the
following information:

Vulnerability details

Product name affected.

Software version(s) affected.

Steps to reproduce the issue.

Extra details

A PoC (Proof of Concept) or exploit, if available.

Workaround found, if applicable.

Disclosure plans, if any.

Keep in mind

During the investigation and resolution phase, it is recommended not to disclose any information about the vulnerability and/or
exploits connected to it, to avoid third parties using the security issue for malicious purposes.

We will acknowledge receipt of your product security or vulnerability report within 7 days. We will provide status updates on the investigation and its resolution at least once per week.
