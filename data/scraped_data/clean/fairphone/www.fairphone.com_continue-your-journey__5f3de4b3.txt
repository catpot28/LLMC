Frequently asked questions

What is the special upgrade offer for Fairphone 3(+) customers?

We are offering a €50 discount on a new Fairphone (Gen. 6) alongside a free protective bundle (screen protector and case) for a limited time. This offer is exclusive to active owners of a Fairphone 3 or Fairphone 3+ who received the campaign email or the push notification to their Fairphone. The unique voucher code is exclusively linked to your Fairphone account and is non-transferable.

Why is there a special upgrade offer for Fairphone 3(+) customers?

We are officially retiring the Fairphone 3 and 3+ this summer. Software support will end in August, 2026. Additionally, global 2G and 3G network shutdowns mean Fairphone 3(+) devices will soon lack fundamental features like voice calling in many regions. We will also be ceasing sales of spare parts and accessories for the device, and after August 5, 2026, customer support will be limited to spare parts that are still under warranty. We are incredibly proud to have met and exceeded our initial five-year promise of support from the 2019 launch. We are launching this offer to help our Fairphone 3(+) users transition to the Fairphone (Gen. 6).

How long is this special upgrade offer available?

This offer for active owners of Fairphone 3 and 3+ devices is available from April 7, 2026, until June 30, 2026.

How do I claim my special upgrade offer?

Add the Fairphone (Gen. 6), a protective case in a color of your choice, and a screen protector of your choice to your cart in our webshop. During the checkout process, apply your unique voucher code in the required field. This will automatically deduct €50 from your total bill while making the protective case and screen protector free.

Note: Please ensure you select the correct case color and protector type before finalizing your order.

Can I combine this offer with other promotions or Keep Club points?

While you can still use your accumulated Keep Club points for a further reduction to your order, your unique voucher code is not compatible with other promotional offers available on our web shop (terms and conditions apply).

What should I do with my old Fairphone 3(+)?

We highly encourage recycling it! You can hand in your device for recycling at e-waste recycling points at larger electronic retailers and supermarkets for free. These are official collection points for material recovery as well. Alternatively, you can also recycle it through our Reuse and Recycling Program. For every Fairphone 3 and 3+ device you send in, you will be eligible for a €10 gift card from our end, redeemable on our web shop.

Will spare parts and accessories for the Fairphone 3(+) still be available after August 2026?

No, they will not be. We had promised extended support and spare parts availability for the Fairphone 3 for a period of five years when we launched the device in 2019. We were able to prolong this support period for two additional years after this deadline, with the final software update set to roll out in June 2026. After this, we will officially be retiring the Fairphone 3 and 3+ in August. However, any spare parts or accessories bought prior to this will still be eligible for a standard two-year warranty.

Can I still use my Fairphone 3/3(+) after software support ends?

Yes, you can. However, performance and security will be compromised. Certain apps, like government and banking services, might not work as expected without the latest security updates. Calling might also get impacted, with more and more countries phasing out 2G and 3G networks. In line with our commitment to longevity, we strongly encourage our community to explore alternative operating systems. Much like we saw with the Fairphone 2, the open-source community provides incredible ways to keep your phone secure and functional for years to come. Switching to an alternative OS allows you to receive securicty patches and feature updates long after the official life cycle. We suggest looking into community-driven projects such as
LineageOS
(currently running Android 15),
/e/OS
(who will continue to support the Fairphone 3 for at least two years more), and
postmarketOS
. By transitioning to an alternative OS, you’re helping us prove that hardware can, and should, last. We will also be publishing all the development work we have done on Android 14, hoping this work will serve open source communities.
