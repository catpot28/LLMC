#ChangeIsComing

10 years ago they said fairer phones were impossible. Well, look at us now! In this decade, our community grew from 25.000 to over 400.000 – and we’ve already done so much together. But there are still so many people we want to reach out to.

Let’s kick off our next decade and show the world that change is in your hands – with a movie starring YOU! This is our community’s letter to the world.

Watch the video on Youtube >>

Lights, camera, action!

Together, we made a short film that only this community can make – a film that stars you. Because we wanted the world to see this vibrant community that’s powering the change. We collected your beautiful patchwork of submissions and, with some editing magic, made a powerful community short film from it.

Stay tuned as we reveal what’s next for Fairphone soon…
