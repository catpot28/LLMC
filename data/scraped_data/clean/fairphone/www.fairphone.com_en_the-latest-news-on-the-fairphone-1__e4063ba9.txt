Thanks for being one of the first

In 2013, we embarked on a journey to shake up the industry with the Fairphone 1. Thanks to you, the pioneers who supported our mission, we started building a movement for fair electronics. We’re proud of everything we’ve achieved together, but unfortunately, we’ve reached the point where we can no longer support our first phone. Read more about why we made this difficult decision in our
blog post
.

Below you’ll find resources to help keep your phone going for as long as possible, as well as responsibly say goodbye if the time has come.

What we’ve achieved together

The people who bought a Fairphone 1 helped us pave the way towards fairer electronics. Here are just a few of our accomplishments:

Conflict free Tin and Tantulum
The Fairphone 1 started it all. We got our first two minerals conflict free in the first Fairphone ever.
Learn more

Created a Worker Welfare Fund
The first Fairphone worker welfare fund: from dream to reality. The Fairphone 1 made it happen.
Learn more

Sparked a growing movement for fair electronics
The Fairphone community now has tens of thousands people promoting fairer electronics. We keep on proving that people want a fairer production for their products.
