Climate conscious

Climate conscious and the Fairphone 5

Climate conscious is an approach to avoiding and reducing carbon emissions by designing products
that last, working with our suppliers to reduce our products’ footprint and reinvesting in the planet and projects that put people first. How
do we do this?

By working with our suppliers

We’ve partnered with our final assembly and battery manufacturers to implement renewable energy for the
production of Fairphone 5. But this is not where we stop. By working with our suppliers to set climate targets we stimulate carbon reduction
in our supply chain - beyond Fairphone products. We also stepped up our materials sourcing with your Fairphone 5. Inside your phone is
recycled aluminum, magnesium, rare earth elements, tin, nickel, zinc, copper, plastics, and for the first time, indium.

By investing in the planet

Through investing in Gold Standard certified projects in Uganda and the Democratic Republic of the Congo,
we’re helping to reduce CO2 emissions while contributing to community-based activities. Our contribution matches the remaining emissions of
Fairphone 5’s lifecycle.

Frequently asked questions

Where can i buy fairphone 5 with /e/OS?

We sell Fairphone with /e/OS only in the European Economic Area (EEA). We do not ship to European
countries that are not in the EEA, such as Switzerland, UK, Monaco and San Marino.

Does the fairphone 5 with /e/OS come with standard google apps, like google maps?

There are no standard Google Apps on Fairphone 5 with /e/OS. However, /e/OS comes with equivalent,
open-source apps to cover your common needs, both personal and professional. There’s a navigation app, a web browser, a calendar app and
many more. You can check your email, plan your week ahead, chat with your friends and coworkers, browse the web, check the weather, check
your itinerary for your next meeting, and so on, without depending on Google. Having said that, /e/OS is still compatible with all your
favourite Google apps, should you want them. You can download them from Murena’s AppLounge. With /e/OS, you get to decide what you want,
and what you don’t want. After all, keeping your data private shouldn’t mean one or the other. You should always get to choose.

Who do i contact if i have a hardware or software problem with my fairphone 5 with /e/OS?

You can contact Fairphone’s Customer Support team, who will help you aided by a dedicated team from
Murena, the creators of /e/OS.

Will i be able to receive the 5-year warranty for my fairphone 5 with /e/OS?

Yes, absolutely. To learn more about warranty with e/OS,

go to our support article.

What can i do if i want to change the software on my fairphone 5 with /e/os? can i still receive support?

If you change the operating system back to Android, you can reach out to Fairphone for all your
customer support needs.

Can i install /e/OS myself on a standard fairphone (with android as the operating system)?

Of course. You can do this using Murena’s Easy Installer, a software application compatible with
