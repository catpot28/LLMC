Impact Report 2025

Better starts here.

READ the full report

Explore the highlights

We're raising the bar on fairness every year, from tracing materials to their source, designing our products for longevity, and working with our suppliers on fairer production.

“How do you make tech sustainable?”

Here are a few highlights

More fair and recycled materials than ever

The Fairphone (Gen. 6) contains more than 50% fair and recycled materials by total weight.

Better conditions for more people

Over 11,000 people experienced fairer working conditions in terms of health, safety, and worker representation.

2,083 tons of CO₂e avoided

in 2025 thanks to Fairphone’s device longevity

30 metric tons of e-waste collected

for responsible recycling in 2025

US$1.25M paid in living wage bonuses

since 2019 through our Living Wage Bonus program

30% lower product carbon footprint

The Fairphone (Gen. 6)’s total is just 29 kg CO₂e

Our product range is 97% e-waste neutral

Almost every Fairphone product we sell is 100% e-waste neutral, from phones to audio devices to accessories.

Longer device lifespans are better for the planet

Longer-lasting devices helped us avoid 981,474 m3 of freshwater use and 14.7 tons of raw material extraction in 2025.
