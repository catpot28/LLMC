Frequently asked questions

What is ChatLicense?

ChatLicense offers an app for families that can be seen as a ‘driving license’ for your child’s first smartphone. Using a science-backed program, the app empowers families to overcome the challenges of connected technology, while also unlocking the great potential it comes with. As a Fairphone user, we offer you a free ChatLicense subscription for one year (which ends automatically) for up to two kids and two adults (worth €59.99).

How do I claim my free ChatLicense subscription?

Copy the code on this webpage and enter it at check-out when buying a Fairphone. When you click ‘Apply’, ChatLicense will be added to your cart for free. The Fairphone models eligible for this offer include Fairphone 5, Fairphone 5 with /e/ OS, The Fairphone (Gen. 6), and The Fairphone (Gen. 6) with /e/ OS. After your product(s) are paid for, you will receive an email from Fairphone with your unique ChatLicense voucher. With this voucher, you can activate your free Family subscription in the ChatLicense app.

What ChatLicense subscription will I get for free?

If you buy a Fairphone, you will receive a voucher that gives you free access to the Family subscription (for up to two kids and two parents).

Do I need to cancel the ChatLicense subscription myself?

No, the subscription will end automatically after one year, with no automatic renewal.

I would like to keep my subscription after one year; can I extend it for free?

The code is only valid for a one-year subscription that will end automatically without extra costs. If you want to extend your subscription, you can do so within the ChatLicense app at your own expense.

Is the ChatLicense app pre-installed on Fairphone? How do I gain access to the app?

No, it is not pre-installed on Fairphone devices. The ChatLicense app is available on Google Play Store, Apple’s App Store, and the /e/OS App Lounge. You can download the app to any supported device, and activate your free Family subscription using the voucher you receive from Fairphone via email.

Is the app available across all European countries?

Yes, it is. However, the app is only available in English, Dutch, Spanish and French at the moment, with German support coming soon.
