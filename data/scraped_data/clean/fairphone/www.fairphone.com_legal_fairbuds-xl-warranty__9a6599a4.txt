Fairbuds XL Warranty

FairPhone B.V., a Dutch limited liability company located at Van Diemenstraat 200, 1013 CP, Amsterdam, The Netherlands, registered with the Trade Registry number 55901964 (“Fairphone”) warrants to the original purchaser (“You”) that your Fairphone Fairbuds XL (“Product”) is free of any manufacturing defects caused by improper or inferior workmanship, materials, and/or design during the designated warranty period as defined below, and subject to the following terms and conditions, when used normally and in accordance with all operating instructions (“Normal Customer Use”). This Warranty is guaranteed for Fairphone original Products only, and may be extended or supplemented by additional or extended warranty products or services offered by Fairphone.
This Warranty, except where otherwise specified, applies to both generations of Fairbuds XL: Fairbuds XL (2023) [formerly known as Fairbuds XL] and Fairbuds XL [launched on December 9, 2025].
This version of Our Fairbuds XL Warranty terms applies from 09.12.25.

1. The European Union consumer protection and national consumer protection laws

1.1 This warranty statement gives You specific rights, and You may have other rights under the consumer law of your country. The Fairphone Warranty (“Warranty”) does not replace, restrict, limit or otherwise affect your rights under local consumer law or the free of charge, 2-year legal guarantee for all goods provided by Directive 1999/44/EC on certain aspects of the sale of consumer goods and associated guarantees, as amended and supplemented from time to time.

1.2 You can always contact the European Consumer Centre in the country of purchase, or where applicable, the country of residence to obtain detailed information on your rights. For a summary of your rights under EU consumer law visit https://www.fairphone.com/en/legal/

2. Warranty period

2.1 Product Identification & Applicable Warranty
2.1.1 Effective 9 December 2025, the Fairbuds XL launched in 2023 is referred to as Fairbuds XL (2023).

2.1.2 This document applies to both:
the Fairbuds XL launched in 2023 now marketed as Fairbuds XL (2023), and
the Fairbuds XL launched on December 9, 2025 (also marketed as "Fairbuds XL”).

These are separate products with different characteristics.
2.1.3 Both Products are covered by Fairphone’s standard 2-year warranty, which applies equally and independently of the product name.
2.1.4 In addition, the Fairbuds XL launched in 2025 come with an extended commercial warranty (“Extended Warranty”), described in Sections 2.3 and 3 of this document.
The Fairbuds XL (2023) do not come with this Extended Warranty.
2.1.5 The applicable coverage (standard warranty only, or standard + Extended Warranty) is determined by the product’s colour variant and (internally) by its SKU and/or serial number.
2.1.6 These identifiers ensure that consumers receive the correct warranty conditions for the specific model purchased and avoid any confusion between generations.
2.1.7 For avoidance of doubt, the distinction is made as follows:
2.1.7.1 Fairbuds XL (2023) Green and Fairbuds XL (2023) Black: standard warranty
2.1.7.2 Fairbuds XL Horizon Black and Fairbuds XL Forest Green: standard + Extended Warranty
2.1.8 Any Fairbuds XL purchased before the renaming is now considered to be designated as Fairbuds XL (2023). Its warranty coverage corresponds to the standard warranty for this product and does not include the extended commercial warranty available for the Fairbuds XL launched in 2025.
2.1.9 This naming clarification does not affect consumers’ EU statutory rights, which remain unchanged.
2.2 Standard Warranty
2.2.1 The Fairphone Fairbuds XL (2023) and Fairbuds XL and replaceable parts (“Spare Parts”) are covered by this Warranty for 24 months from the date of delivery to the original purchaser.
2.2.2 The Warranty in 2.1 shall not apply to the batteries of the Fairphone Fairbuds XL (2023) and Fairbuds XL batteries. The batteries of the Fairphone Fairbuds XL (2023) and Fairbuds XL battery is covered by this Warranty for 12 months from the date of delivery by the original Purchaser.
2.2.3 In case You sell or donate your Product to someone else, this person (“Second Owner”) can benefit from In-Warranty repairs and replacements as long as he/she can show the proof of purchase we or one of our authorized partners issued to You (“Transferred Warranty”). Fairphone does not refund second-hand or donated Fairphone products unless You can demonstrate the price paid for the Product by You, and the original proof of purchase. The transferred Warranty cannot extend beyond the period of Your original Warranty from the date of Your delivery.
2.3 Extended Warranty
2.3.1 Fairphone is proud to be able to offer an extended warranty for the new generation of Fairbuds XL (launched on December 9, 2025). The Extended Warranty applies to only to the aforementioned Product and is not applicable to the Fairbuds XL (2023) independently of their date of purchase.
2.3.2 In the following sections, We will walk You through the nitty gritty of Fairphone’s extended warranty (“Extended Warranty”) so that You can make the best use of it!
