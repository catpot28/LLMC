Fantastic company, quality phone,ethics and support

I got my first Fairphone in December..the Fairphone 6 . I wanted to make sure I got a phone that wasn't using unethical mined materials and workers treatment and that could be repaired for longevity. So far it's been a brilliant phone. So many impressive features. The camera is really high quality too and the phone is a good size. It is powerful and fast and easy to use and put in different SIM cards and USB cards. There's excellent customer service support and products to go with it like the privacy filter screen. I wanted the Google free phone but was concerned it would be hard to use certain apps. I would have gone for that if there was some more guidance and troubleshooting support on using that without Google. Postage prices are a bit expensive for small items, e.g replacement screen protector, so be sure to order 2 or 3 when you place an order in case one breaks. The Fairphone case is smart too and strong and id recommend that. One issue with the phone I've experienced is it occasionally stalling on me. I've done the updates but it still sometimes happens and I don't know why. I like the backwards swipe function to move back to the previous page. However I'd like a forwards swipe too after you have gone back. All the setup instructions were clear and the setup was smooth from my old phone, although transferring everything over took a while. I like the information they send out to customers too and the Q&A with the CEO. I wouldn't consider going with any other phone provider and hope this phone will last me for 10-20 years. There are some small features I think could make things better and smoother. I wish there was a shop you go into in person but I recognise how hard this can be to setup. I hope many more move to Fairphone and would recommend to anyone and for what you get and the length of time it should last (and impact on the planet) I think it's very good value.
