Fairphone 4 Regulatory Update (SAR)

In compliance with the reporting methodologies of Radio Equipment Directive (RED) (2014/53/EU), the current Specific Absorption Rate (SAR) values for the Fairphone 4 are as follows:

Head SAR 10g :
0.467 W/Kg

Body SAR 10g :
0.867 W/Kg

Limb SAR 10g :
0.867 W/Kg

Your Fairphone has been tested and meets applicable limits for radio frequency (RF) exposure.
