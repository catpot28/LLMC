1. The European Union consumer protection and national consumer protection laws

1.1 This warranty statement gives You specific rights, and You may have other rights under the consumer law of your country. The Fairphone Warranty (“
Warranty

https://www.fairphone.com/en/legal/

2. Warranty period

2.1 The Fairphone smartphone device, the separately bought Fairphone accessories and replaceable smartphone parts (“
Spare Parts
”) are covered by this Warranty for 24 months from the date of delivery to the original purchaser.

2.2 The Fairphone smartphone battery and the in-box earphones are also covered by this Warranty for 24 months from the date of purchase by the original Purchaser.

2.3 In case You sell or donate Your Product to someone else, this person (“
Second Owner
”) can benefit from In-Warranty repairs and replacements as long as he/she can demonstrate the device was registered for this Warranty by the Original owner and he/she can show the proof of purchase we or one of Our authorized partners issued to You (“
Transferred Warranty”
). For clarity, Fairphone does not refund second-hand or donated Fairphone devices unless the Second Owner can demonstrate the price paid for the device by them through their proof of purchase. The transferred Warranty cannot extend beyond the period of Your original Warranty from the date of Your delivery.

3.1 Fairphone’s Warranty service is provided to customers located in continental European territories of:
Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France Métropolitaine (excluding oversea territories), Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Monaco, The Netherlands (excluding Bonaire, Saba and St. Eustatius), Norway, Poland, Portugal (excluding Azores and Madeira), Romania, San Marino, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain (excluding Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla), Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom (excluding the Channel islands).

3.2 Please be advised that the geographical coverage of Fairphone and the subsequent Warranty service may be subject to expansion. Any changes will be communicated
here

4.1 The Warranty only applies to Fairphone original products. The easiest way to make sure that You are purchasing a Fairphone original product is to either buy it directly from Us or from a professional reseller. Customer Support
who will be able to provide You with information regarding Our authorised Partners.

4.2.3 Cosmetic damages, including but not limited to dents or scratches in decorative, structural, or non-operative parts resulting from Normal Customer Use;

4.2.5 Damage that is the result of use with non-Fairphone products, which includes the use of a third-party module or battery in a Fairphone smartphone device, except non-Fairphone chargers which meet the provided technical requirements;

4.2.6 Damage caused when transporting the Product from You back to Us;

4.2.7 Damage that is the result of abuse, vandalism, accident, misuse, improper storage, lack of adequate ventilation, blown fuse, improper use of any electrical source, exposure to liquid, moisture or dampness, or prolonged exposure to heat or cold. Accidental damage is understood as any deterioration or destruction of the Product, which prevents it from working properly and which is the result of an external, sudden and unforeseeable cause;

4.2.8 Damage that is the result of external causes such as from collision, fire, flooding, sand, dirt, windstorm, lightening, earthquake, tsunamis, volcanic eruptions, epidemics, pandemics, quarantine, atypical cyclonic storm, falls of sidereal bodies and aeroliths, as well as any other natural catastrophe;

4.2.9 Damage due to the effects produced by nuclear reaction, nuclear weapons, or any source of radioactivity, biological or chemical. Damage as a result of pressure waves caused by airplanes traveling at supersonic or sonic speeds. Damage as a result of electromagnetic waves, whether caused by man or by natural phenomena;

4.2.11 Damage that is the result of computer or internet viruses, bugs, worm, Trojan Horses, cancelbots introduced into the Product;

4.2.14 Damage that is the result of Service performed by anyone who is not a Fairphone representative or an authorized Fairphone service provider;

6.1 You can reach out to Fairphone for both in-warranty and out-of-warranty repairs service, however only the in-warranty service shall be free of charge. Warranty service will be limited to the options available in the country where warranty service is requested. Service options, parts availability and response times may vary according to country.

6.2 Continuing to use a malfunctioning or defective Product can create additional damage, which makes it difficult or impossible to verify the cause of the initial defect. Therefore, please read and follow the instructions in this section, as soon as You experience any defect or malfunction with Your Product:

6.2.1 When attempting to diagnose or repair Your Product, You should make a separate backup of any material stored on the Product such as software, applications, and other data, including contacts, photos, and music, which may be erased during the repair process and cannot be reinstalled by Fairphone. Fairphone disclaims any representation and/or warranty that we will be able to repair or replace Your Product under this warranty without risk of and/or loss to information and/or data stored on Your Product.

6.2.2 Make sure You are running the latest software on Your Product. If Your Product is covered by this Warranty, You may be required to download and accept software updates. You are responsible for any third-party data costs charged to You when obtaining the downloads.

6.2.3 If Your Product is operating on a third party operating system, please contact the third party software provider for any queries. If You are operating the Product with a third party operating system and want Your Product to be covered by this Warranty, We will require You to revert back to the software embedded in the Product at purchase. This Warranty covers only the Product as provided by Us and, unless the Third Party Operating System is removed, it will not be possible for Us to assess whether the defect is caused by the Product hardware or by the Third Party Operating System.

Customer Support

6.2.4.1 Sometimes a Spare Part may not function properly due to other causes like software issues. Thus, changing the Spare Part might not solve Your problem. In case of doubt, please contact Fairphone
Customer Support

6.2.4.2 Although the Fairphone smartphone device is designed for self-repair, it requires careful handling to perform a repair as intended. To ensure safe handling of Your Product, we advise You to consider the following:

6.2.4.2.1 Ensure that Fairphone
Customer Support

Customer Support

6.2.4.2.2 Carefully review and follow the specific instructions on the Customer Support page. These instructions serve as formative documents meant to guide You through the process of opening up Your Fairphone smartphone device for repair;

6.2.4.2.3 Use the correct tools when taking out or installing Spare Parts. If You take out and install the Spare Part of Your Product without the correct tool, this will likely cause damage not covered by this Warranty. For the recommended tools please visit Our
website

This warranty shall not apply if the IMEI 1 and/or the serial number on the product have been altered, defaced or removed.

Fairphone will destroy any remaining material from the Product upon receipt.

6.2.8 Fairphone does not provide a replacement phone to End-Users for the duration of the repair.

6.2.9 If You send Your Product for repair and the Product is not covered by this Warranty or any other consumer rights, Fairphone will inform You of the availability, price, and other conditions applicable to such out-of-warranty repair of the Product. If You do not wish to have the Product repaired, Fairphone will return the Product to You and charge You shipping, handling and diagnostics costs. You may also request Fairphone to recycle Your device free of charge if You do not wish to have the product returned.

If the Product cannot be repaired, it may be replaced by a Product with the same, similar or with equivalent features, as the case may be.

7.3 A repair or replacement will not renew or extend the Warranty Period unless such renewal or extension is mandated by local law, in which case the Warranty period is extended by the minimum time required by law. In countries where a renewal or extension of warranty is not mandated by local law, the product is covered by the remaining warranty period or a period of (90) days from the date of replacement or repair, whichever is longer. The malfunctioning or defective Product that Fairphone has replaced shall become Fairphone’s property.

7.4 Fairphone will return the Product or a replacement product to you with the same configuration as your original purchase plus any relevant updates. In this case, Fairphone may update the system software to a newer version, which will not allow the recovery of older software versions. A software update of this kind may prevent the correct operation of third-party programs. You are responsible for re-installing all third-party software programs, data and information. Recovery and re-installing third party software programs, data and information do not fall under the Fairphone Warranty service

7.5 If the Product is returned to Fairphone after the expiration of the limited warranty period or if one of the warranty exclusions applies to the malfunction or defect, Fairphone’s normal repair policies as stored on Our
Legal Hub
shall apply and You will be charged accordingly. All warranty information, pricing, product features and specifications are subject to change.

7.6 In support of Our mission we may use refurbished spare parts in case we repair Your defective Product. We ensure that the appearance and the performance of the refurbished spare part are in good working condition. In the case of repair, the defective part of Your device will be at Our disposal and will become Fairphone property. If You decide to self-repair the defective Product, we encourage You to recycle the defective parts locally. Please check with Your local municipality to participate in the recycling of materials and components, when the option to do so is available in Your area.
