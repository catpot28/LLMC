Terms & Conditions – Fairphone x Daan Tech Giveaway

1. Promoter

This promotion is organized by FairPhone B.V., a Dutch limited liability company located at Van Diemenstraat 200, 1013 CP Amsterdam, The Netherlands, and Daan Tech SAS, headquartered in Vendée, France.

2. Promotion description

The Fairphone x Daan Tech Giveaway begins on September 22, 2025 at 12:00 a.m. CEST and ends on October 5, 2025 at 23:59 p.m. CEST (“Promotion Period”). After this date, no further entries will be permitted.

3. Who can participate

The giveaway is open to residents of the European Union aged 18 years or over, except employees of Fairphone or Daan Tech, their close relatives, and anyone otherwise connected with the organization or judging of the competition. Only one entry per participant is allowed.

4. How to enter

To enter the giveaway, participants must:

Follow
@fairphone
and
@daan_tech
on Instagram.

Comment under the giveaway post which prize they would like to win (either the Fairphone (Gen. 6) or the Bob eco-compact dishwasher).

(Optional) Solve the posted crossword puzzle, find the hidden keyword, and include it in their comment to increase their chances of winning.

There is no entry fee and no purchase necessary to enter this competition. By entering, participants agree to be bound by these terms and conditions.

5. Winner selection and notification

Two winners will be randomly selected: one for the Fairphone (Gen. 6) and one for the Bob eco-compact dishwasher. Winners will be contacted via Instagram direct message on October 6, 2025. If a winner does not respond within seven (7) days, the prize will be forfeited and another winner may be selected.

6. Prize

One (1) Fairphone (Gen. 6) smartphone

One (1) Bob eco-compact dishwasher

Each participant can only win one of the prizes.

6. General prize conditions

No cash alternatives or prize substitutions will be offered. The prizes are non-transferable.

8. Other conditions

The promoters reserve the right to cancel or amend the competition and these terms and conditions without notice if circumstances arise outside their control. Any changes will be communicated to participants as soon as possible.

This giveaway is governed by Dutch law, and any disputes will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of The Netherlands.

Winners agree to the use of their name and image in promotional material, including but not limited to Instagram announcements.

This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by, or associated with Instagram.

9. Data Protection

By entering this contest, participants provide their information to Fairphone and Daan Tech, not to Instagram. Any personal data relating to participants will be used solely for administering the contest in accordance with applicable data protection laws and Fairphone’s Privacy Policy.
