Regulatory Update (SAR)

Regulatory Update (SAR)

In compliance with the new reporting methodologies of Radio Equipment Directive (RED) (2014/53/EU), the current Specific Absorption Rate (SAR) levels for the Fairphone 2 are:

Head SAR 10g : 0.65 W/kg

Body SAR 10g : 1.1 W/kg

As a result, the SAR levels shown in the user guide under the Specific Absorption Rate (SAR) section are no longer applicable.
