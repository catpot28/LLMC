14 reasons why you’ll love Fairphone’s audio range

For those of you who don’t know this already, Fairphone doesn’t just make smartphones. It was in 2021 that we took our first steps into the world of sustainable audio, releasing the Fairphone TWS earbuds. And while they boasted a longer battery life than the competition and used Fairtrade gold in the supply chain, they still featured an ‘off-the-shelf’ design that wasn’t repairable. So we went back to the drawing board and started tinkering the idea of making truly sustainable audio products that were more in line with our design philosophy. The result? The
Fairbuds
and the
Fairbuds XL
. They’re both big on sound, and even bigger on impact. Here are 14 reasons why you’ll fall in love with them.
They are fully repairable
Our Fairbuds range features our signature modular design, meaning you can replace the stuff inside instead of tossing them if something stops working. From individual earbuds to batteries to headbands, everything’s replaceable.
They sound excellent
Caring about the planet shouldn’t mean compromising on quality. Both the Fairbuds and Fairbuds XL deliver a rich and balanced sound profile, thanks to titanium-coated dynamic drivers that reproduce high fidelity with zero distortion.
They let you own your sound
Use the Fairbuds app (compatible with iOS and Android) to customize your EQ settings and truly own your sound. The app is also where you can download the latest firmware updates, or look up DIY manuals on switching parts in and out.
They let you own your space
Switch to ambient mode and let the world in, or choose active noise cancellation (ANC) to tune out distractions and focus on what matters—whether it’s your playlist or your thoughts.
They are crystal clear
Miscommunication is a thing of the past with the Fairbuds range. With features like advanced wind reduction, environmental noise cancellation, and arrays of multiple mics onboard, you’ll hear everything loud and clear every time.
They are comfortable all day
They sound great and feel great. From the over-ear softness of the Fairbuds XL to the lightweight fit of the Fairbuds, you can tell comfort was key to their design. Great sound shouldn’t hurt your ears.
They also run comfortably all day
No one likes running out of juice mid-song. The Fairbuds XL lasts up to 30 hours, while Fairbuds easily lasts more than a day. If you’re in a bind, charge the Fairbuds for just ten minutes, and you’ll an hour and a half of playback time. Talk about epic battery life.
Speaking of runs…
Hitting the gym? Going for a run? No problem. Both our Fairbuds and Fairbuds XL are IP54-rated for dust and sweat, built to handle whatever everyday life throws at them with ease.
They are super easy to use
Tap, swipe, or press—our intuitive controls make it easy to manage your music, calls, and ANC settings on the go.
They work with any Bluetooth-enabled device
You don’t need to own a Fairphone to use a pair of Fairbuds or Fairbuds XL. They’re compatible with any Bluetooth device, be it Apple, Android, Windows or anything else. They also have dual-point connectivity, which means you can seamlessly switch between your phone, laptop, and tablet without the hassle of disconnecting and reconnecting.
They come with a three-year warranty
Because accidents happen. And in case one does, you can always rest assured that we have your back. Although, thanks to their modular design, the Fairbuds and Fairbuds XL should last WAAAAY longer than three years.
They are (partly) made with fair and recycled materials
Like everything we do at Fairphone, our audio products are made with fairer materials, with a focus on ethical sourcing and sustainability. This includes recycled plastics and aluminium, and Fairtrade Gold integrated in the supply chain. So you can listen with a clear conscience.
The people who make them are taken care of
We partner with ethical factories and fair labor initiatives to improve working conditions and wages for the people who make your audio gear. Our commitment to living wage bonuses ensures workers receive fair compensation, improving lives and communities.
They are 100% electronic waste neutral.
We’re proud to be 100% e-waste neutral, meaning for every product we make, we recycle an equivalent amount of electronic waste. Even the packaging is 100% recyclable and designed to have a minimal impact on the planet.
When you choose the Fairbuds or the Fairbuds XL, you’re not just getting great audio—you’re supporting a movement for fairer electronics. For us, that’s the best reason there is. The more people who support Fairphone, the bigger impact we can make, and the more change we can push throughout the industry. Now that’s music to our ears.
Convinced?
