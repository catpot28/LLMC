Description

The Fairphone 4 Loudspeaker does exactly what you think it does. It plays crystal clear audio for every occasion: hands-free calls, video playback, music streaming, games, you name it. So when your music or gaming sounds like it's from the 1920s, it's time to replace your loudspeaker.
This little part also features a piece of the antennas that enable 5G network support, and it hosts the vibration motor for that familiar notification buzz. Did we mention that it pairs with the Earpiece speaker to produce glorious stereo sound? And this sound gets even better, when you know it contains post-consumer recycled Rare Earths and conflict-free, fair tungsten. It's a lot of parts coming together in this one Loudspeaker unit. But you deserve high-quality sound and functionality made with high-quality materials.
