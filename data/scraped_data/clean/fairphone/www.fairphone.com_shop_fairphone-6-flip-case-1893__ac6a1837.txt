Extra protection for your cameras

Features raised bumpers around the iconic Fairphone camera triangle.

Ergonomic design that’s built strong

Made with 100% PCR plastic, the case offers better comfort with maximum impact protection.

Designed for your add-ons

Works perfectly with the Fairphone (Gen. 6) cardholder, finger loop, and/or lanyard.

Classic design built to last
Protect your Fairphone from all sides with this sleek flip case. The fold-over design shields your screen while keeping your device slim and pocket-friendly. Raised bumpers around the camera keep your lenses safe, too. Made with recycled materials, it’s sturdy, durable and fair. It’s also designed to fit the new line of modular accessories for the Gen. 6, ensuring you get the best Fairphone experience with zero compromise.
