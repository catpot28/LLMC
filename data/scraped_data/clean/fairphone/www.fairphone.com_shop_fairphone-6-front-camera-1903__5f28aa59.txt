Up your selfie game

The 32MP front camera on the Fairphone (Gen. 6) packs a Samsung ISOCELL KD1 sensor that offers excellent dynamic range and true-to-life colors. It also comes with electronic image stabilization (EIS) and autofocus to make sure your selfies are on point every time. Swapping a new front camera is fuss-free, thanks to the Fairphone’s modular design that allows you to get in and out in minutes.
