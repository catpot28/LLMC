What’s in the top unit?

The top unit houses the flash for your camera as well as the time-of-flight (ToF) sensor. The camera flash is also what doubles up as your torch, while the ToF sensor plays an important role in image post-processing. It is also used for object detection, which is useful for certain AI and AR functionalities. Please note the individual camera units and SIM/SD Card tray is sold separately.
