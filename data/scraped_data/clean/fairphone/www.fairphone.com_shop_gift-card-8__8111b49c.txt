FAQ

Can I use my gift card more than once?

Yes, you can. If the total of your order is less than the value of your gift card, you can use your gift card later to redeem the remaining balance.

Can I use multiple gift cards on a single order?

Yes, you can use multiple gift cards on a single order.

Can I use a gift card and a discount code together? What about on products on sale?

Gift cards can be used on any and all orders, along with discount codes and products on seasonal promotions.

Do gift cards expire?

No, they do not.

What happens if I return an item bought with a gift card?

You will have to reach out to one of our customer support agents to help process your refund. If your gift card still has balance on it, it will be topped up. If not, then a new one will be created with the required amount.

Can I return a gift card?

Unused gift cards can be returned within the first 14 days after purchase. Please keep in mind that the money will be refunded to the original account that purchased the gift card.
