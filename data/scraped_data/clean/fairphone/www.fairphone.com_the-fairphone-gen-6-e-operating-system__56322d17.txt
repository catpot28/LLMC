Frequently asked questions

What is /e/OS and how is it different from the Fairphone (Gen. 6) (Android)?

The Fairphone (Gen. 6) is available with two operating system confirmations on our web shop. While the hardware is identical, the software experience is different.

The Fairphone (Gen. 6)

The standard version provides the conventional Android experience with Google Mobile Services pre-installed.

Considering this version?

These Fairphone (Gen. 6) FAQs

may be useful to you.

The Fairphone (Gen. 6) with /e/OS

This version provides a privacy-focused experience with /e/OS. This is an open-source operating system that does not include Google apps or services, a design intended to reduce data tracking. The /e/OS version comes with its own set of default applications for core functions and uses the App Lounge to provide access to a wide range of Android applications.

Can I change the operating system from /e/OS to Android after purchase if I decide it’s not for me?

Yes, the initial choice of operating system is not permanent. It is possible to switch between the standard Android OS and /e/OS after purchasing the device.

This process will require unlocking your bootloader along with other steps.

Here is our guide

on reinstalling Android to you Fairphone

Can I still use my banking app and other essential Android apps on the /e/OS version?

Yes, the /e/OS version provides access to a wide range of Android applications through the App Lounge. This includes many common banking, social media, and messaging services. The App Lounge also displays a privacy rating for each application, detailing its data trackers and required permissions to help you make an informed choice.

While most applications are available and function as expected, those that depend heavily on Google Mobile Services (Drive, Gmail etc) for their core features may have limited functionality or be unavailable.

Check /e/OS’s community forum

to search for specific app compatibility.

Banking app compatibility with /e/OS

Interested in whether your banking app is supported on /e/OS?

Check the community-maintained list of banking apps

on the /e/OS website.

Does using /e/OS (or another OS) affect support and warranty?

Yes, the support process is different depending on the operating system you use. While you can switch between the standard Android OS and /e/OS at any time

using Fairphone's ‘Easy Installer’ tool

, it's important to understand the support structure.

/e/OS support

/e/OS is a separate operating system developed and maintained by our partner, Murena. Because Fairphone's diagnostic tools and support services are built for the standard Android OS, the device must be ideally running this software for our team to provide effective hardware and software support.

For any warranty claim to be processed by Fairphone, the device must first be restored to the standard Android OS. Exceptions to this rule are only for devices that were bought with /e/OS pre-installed by Fairphone or Murena, in which case you do not have to install Android.

Custom ROMs

This policy also applies to all other alternative operating systems (custom ROMs). While using a different OS does not void your warranty for unrelated hardware failures, damage caused by the software itself is not covered. Warranty does not cover issues caused during installation of an alternative OS.

For more information, read our support article

about warranty with an alternative OS.
