For the professional who values environmental responsibility without compromising on modern functionality, the Fairphone 6 is the definitive choice.

Highlights:
- Modular Design: Easy DIY repairs with standard tools.

What do we mean by refurbished?

A refurbished smartphone is a pre-owned device that has been professionally restored to like-new condition. Refurbished Fairphones undergo rigorous testing and quality checks to ensure optimal performance. Batteries are swapped out if the battery capacity is found to be under 85%. Displays are replaced if there are more than two scratches. Mid frames and back covers also get replaced if they have deep, visible scratches.

All refurbished models are completely wiped software-wise, and updated to the latest Android version available, providing you a with clean, secure start, just like a new device.

The refurbished device comes with the same five-year warranty and 14-day return period as a brand new Fairphone, and sports the exact same tech specs and modularity.

Why choose refurbished?

Choosing a refurbished Fairphone helps reduce electronic waste while giving you access to the same sustainable, repairable technology at a lower price. Refurbishing Fairphones prevent unnecessary resource extraction and manufacturing, and, by virtue of already being in use for a few years, come with smaller carbon footprints compared to a new device. Frequently asked questions

What are the Fairphone (Gen. 6)'s SIM and SD card capabilities? Can I use two SIMs?

The Fairphone (Gen. 6) is designed with

one physical slot for a nano-SIM card
. For a second SIM connection, you can activate the phone's

built-in digital eSIM
.

Using the physical SIM and the eSIM together provides dual SIM functionality, and both lines are 5G-enabled.

The device also features a

dedicated microSD card slot in the SIM card tray
. This allows you to expand your storage by up to 2TB without sacrificing the use of your physical SIM. This configuration allows the simultaneous use of a nano-SIM, an eSIM, and a microSD card.

What comes in the box with the Fairphone (Gen. 6)? Is a charger or cable included?

A charging cable and power adapter are not included with the device. This approach is taken to reduce electronic waste from redundant accessories.

What is /e/OS and how is it different from the Fairphone (Gen. 6) (Android)?

The Fairphone (Gen. 6) is available with two operating systems on our webshop. While the hardware is identical, the software experience is different.

The Fairphone (Gen. 6)

The standard version provides the conventional Android experience with Google Mobile Services pre-installed.

The Fairphone (Gen. 6) with /e/OS

This version provides a privacy-focused experience with /e/OS. This is an open-source operating system that does not include Google apps or services, a design intended to reduce data tracking. The /e/OS version comes with its own set of default applications for core functions and uses the App Lounge to provide access to a wide range of Android applications. Please bear in mind not all Android apps are compatible with /e/OS. For more information on specific app compatibility,
