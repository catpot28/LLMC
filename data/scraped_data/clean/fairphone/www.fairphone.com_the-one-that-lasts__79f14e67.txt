Impact Innovation

Inside The Fairphone (Gen. 6)

For over a decade, we’ve been redefining what great tech looks like—devices that are built to last and made with care for people and the planet. With the new Fairphone, we're pushing innovation that matters, where it matters most.

Built to Last

Designed for longevity with modular, repairable hardware and long-term software support—because the longer your phone lasts, the smaller its footprint.

100% Electronic Waste Neutral

For every phone we sell, we take one back. That means less e-waste and more circular use through reuse, repair, and recycling.

More than 50% Fair or Recycled Materials

Now made with more fair or recycled materials than ever.

Recycled materials include:

Aluminum, Copper, Steel, Tin, Indium, Magnesium, Nickel, Plastics, Rare earths, Zinc.

Fair materials include:

Fairtrade gold, Fairmined silver (via credit), cobalt (via credits) & tungsten

Made in Fair Factories

Final assembly happens in a factory that supports living wages, prioritises worker wellbeing, and works to reduce environmental impact.

Powered by Green Energy

Manufacturing uses renewable energy, supporting our climate goals and lowering emissions at the source.

Climate Conscious by Design

Being climate conscious means building a phone that lasts, reducing carbon emissions, and giving back to the planet.

Working with Suppliers

We team up with our suppliers to cut carbon at the source—setting climate targets and using recycled materials like aluminum, magnesium, rare earths, tin, nickel, zinc, copper, plastics, and indium.

Investing in the Planet

We offset the remaining emissions of the Fairphone 6 by supporting Gold Standard climate projects reducing CO₂ and helping local communities thrive.

Fairtrade Gold

We’re the first and only smartphone company to integrate Fairtrade gold into our supply chain.

Frequently asked questions

How long will The Fairphone (Gen. 6) be supported for?

We will be supporting the new Fairphone till 2033 at least, with regular security patches, software updates, and spare parts availability. You will also receive at least seven Android OS upgrades during that time.

How is the Fairphone (Gen. 6)’s performance compared to the Fairphone 5?

The new Fairphone comes with a powerful Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 processor that promises faster performance that is energy efficient. Software support is guaranteed until 2033, and at least seven Android upgrades. The removable 4415mAh battery lasts up to two days on a single charge as well.

Is The Fairphone (Gen 6) truly the most sustainable and ethical smartphone from Fairphone?

Yes. The new Fairphone contains more than 50% fair and recycled materials (by total weight), while supporting living wage bonuses and worker voice programs. It is climate-conscious and 100% e-waste neutral, and, as per the data available to us, will have the lowest carbon footprint of all our smartphones yet. To learn more about our impact work,

read our latest Impact Report here
.

What are the plans for the support of alternative OSes?

The Fairphone (Gen. 6) is

available for sale with Murena’s deGoogled /e/OS pre-installed
. We also encourage open source development, making our source code public and working closely with communities such as LineageOS, IodéOS, CalyxOS, Mobian, PostmarketOS, and Ubuntu Touch. Please note that, in order to receive warranty support from Fairphone, it is necessary to switch your phone back to the stock Android operating system.

What makes Fairphone's five-year warranty unique?

Our five-year warranty is the longest warranty offered by any smartphone manufacturer currently. It consists of a standard two-year warranty, plus a free three-year extended warranty.

Learn more about it on our Warranty page
.

Why should I choose Fairphone?

When you switch to Fairphone, you are switching to sustainable tech that is built to last. With a robust, modular design that makes repairs and upgrades easy, you can keep your phone going strong for years. Not only is this better for the planet, it’s also better for your wallet.
