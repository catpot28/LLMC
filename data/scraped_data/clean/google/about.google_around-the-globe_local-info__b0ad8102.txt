Portraits of AI Across America
: Meet the people using AI to grow and serve their communities

To capture how folks across the country are using AI to help fuel a new era of American innovation, we’re sharing stories from all 50 states.

ASCO is turning to Google AI to equip oncologists with the latest research

The American Society of Clinical Oncology is building the tools for faster, easier access to trusted cancer care and treatment information with Google AI.

Helping bridge the distance for our nation’s military service members

We’re working with the United Service Organizations (USO) to launch a Google Beam pilot program to bring AI-powered 3D video communication to deployed military and their families.

The National Cancer Institute spurs scientific discoveries with Google AI, so breakthroughs happen sooner

The National Cancer Institute developed NanCI, an app that uses Google AI to help cancer researchers collaborate, share data, identify patterns, and recommend research papers, ultimately accelerating the pace of scientific discovery.

Detecting smaller wildfires with better frequency using a constellation of satellites

Google partnered with leaders in the fire community to create FireSat - a purpose-built constellation of satellites designed to detect and track wildfires as small as a classroom.

Color Health supports early cancer detection and treatment with Google AI to help improve patient outcomes

Color Health’s Virtual Cancer Clinic, built with Google AI, helps accelerate and target the care process from screening through treatment and survivorship.

Using AI to help communities better predict natural disasters with Groundsource

Groundsource, a new Gemini-powered methodology, transforms millions of public reports into a high-quality data archive to aid crisis prediction — starting with urban flash floods.

AlphaFold 3 predicts the structure and interactions of all of life’s molecules

Google DeepMind’s AlphaFold is accelerating the pace of scientific innovation by helping researchers predict the intricate 3D shapes of proteins.

Unlocking new ways to communicate across language barriers for more than half a billion people

Google Translate breaks down language barriers to help people connect and better understand the world around them. Now, we’re using AI to expand the variety of languages we support.

Supporting government innovation

Governments are beginning to see how AI can help them serve their communities better, faster and with limited resources. From fixing potholes to addressing threats to public health, AI can support a government’s ability to deliver on its promises. To help local governments seize AI’s potential, Google has partnered with the U.S. Conference of Mayors to develop an
AI Playbook
that equips leaders with foundational steps and replicable examples for responsible AI adoption.

NOAA's National Hurricane Center, with support from Google AI, is giving people more decision-making confidence and more time to prepare. Google is working with the NHC to provide earlier, more accurate predictions to agencies, emergency services, and the public.

The Air Force Research Laboratory partnered with Google Cloud to leverage AI to tackle complex challenges, from materials science and bioinformatics to human performance optimization.

The City of Los Angeles is equipping city workers with Google AI, helping to accelerate communications, automate time-sensitive tasks, and streamline complex projects, ultimately delivering faster and more accessible public services for the city's four million residents.

The State of Connecticut is using Google Al to identify and respond to cybersecurity threats faster and more effectively, reducing forensic investigation times from months to hours.

The City of Chattanooga, TN is using Google Al to centralize crash data, with the aim of making more informed, life-saving decisions: from prioritizing new traffic signals or redesigned crosswalks, to reallocating police patrols to high-risk corridors.

The Central Texas Regional Mobility Authority is partnering with Google Cloud to develop smarter tolling systems. By applying Al, CTRMA is providing efficiency across the tolling experience, from pricing to processing, resulting in reduced congestion and better traffic flow for Texans.

The Oklahoma Department of Transportation partnered with Google Cloud to use AI to monitor bridge conditions and prioritize maintenance needs, helping ensure safe and efficient travel for Oklahomans.

Google partnered with the Department of Veterans Affairs to enable developers to create new tools and applications that make it easier for veterans to access healthcare, submit claims, and manage their benefits.

Strengthening the American economy

We’re already seeing the ways AI is strengthening the American economy by helping American businesses and workers solve problems, save time, and accomplish more with their resources. At Google, we’re investing heavily in AI development and technical infrastructure, empowering businesses of all sizes with leading technology, and providing access to training and resources to ensure opportunities of AI are widely shared.

Google AI helps small businesses get back to what they love

Walmart is equipping associates with practical AI training from Google to help grow their careers

Google is partnering with Walmart to develop customized learning programs to help Walmart employees gain new skills. Developed by Google experts, the Google AI Professional Certificate equips associates with the AI skills to improve how they work, so they can provide a better customer experience and further their careers.

etA and Google are equipping 100K electricians with AI skills

With funding from Google.org, the electrical training ALLIANCE (etA) will integrate AI tools into its curriculum and boost the number of apprentices nationally, aiming to increase the electrical workforce pipeline by 70% within the next five years.

Bringing the best of Google Gemini and AI training to college students

We launched the Google AI for Education Accelerator to offer free AI training and Google Career Certificates to college students across America. Over 400 public universities, including the University of Michigan, Ohio State University, the University of Virginia and the University Systems in Texas, North Carolina, and Pennsylvania have signed up.

Google is partnering with local chambers of commerce and SBDCs to train Americans in AI skills

Google is partnering with local chambers of commerce and small business development centers (SBDCs) for an in-person “Make AI Work for You” tour. These hands-on workshops will help small businesses use AI to solve key challenges, tackle delayed tasks, brainstorm solutions, and automate repetitive work with a personalized action plan.

Helping small businesses reach their customers with Google AI

Etsy shops are full of unique stories and special finds, but getting them in front of the right buyers can be a challenge. Using Google AI, Etsy is helping shoppers discover these creations and making it easier for sellers to connect with the people who’ll love them most.

Google is offering free AI training and tools to U.S. small businesses, so they can grow their communities

Built by Google experts, the Google AI Professional Certificate equips small businesses with practical AI skills. Through hands-on projects, they can start using AI to get more done and unlock new opportunities for their business.

Google and Cook County are equipping communities with AI skills, so they can build career opportunities

Through a collaboration between Cook County’s Project Rainbow Initiative and Grow with Google, no-cost scholarships will be made available for Google Career Certificates, helping hundreds of residents gain the skills they need to grow their careers or start new ones in fields such as cybersecurity and data analytics.

Supporting small business growth with AI tools

Veteran-owned GO-KOT uses Google AI-powered advertising tools to transform their online presence, connecting with new customers nationwide. They also use Google AI tools to help with marketing and customer-facing communications, saving them time to focus on their goal of growing GO-KOT and hiring more people.

Safeguarding deliveries with AI

UPS Capital launched DeliveryDefense Address Confidence, which uses machine learning and UPS data to provide a confidence score for shippers to help them determine the likelihood of a successful delivery.

Helping farmers through weather forecasting

Benchmark Labs uses Vertex AI to develop weather forecasting and insights tailored to specific locations, helping farmers gain more actionable alerts at a microclimate level across their farms.

Accelerating innovation in the auto industry

Ford and Google are collaborating to optimize database management, with the aim to fully harness the potential of managed services, generative AI, and cloud database technology to drive efficiency, resilience, and innovation.

Boosting family-owned business revenue

Hill Top Contractors, a storage tank removal, environmental remediation and construction company based in Roanoke, VA, uses Google’s AI-powered search to inform their business practices - helping them stay up-to-date on industry regulations and equipment maintenance information.
