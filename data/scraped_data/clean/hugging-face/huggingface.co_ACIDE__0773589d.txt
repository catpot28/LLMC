ACIDE Team - ISIR, Sorbonne University

The ACIDE team explores
embedded action, cognition, interaction, and decision-making
, considering the behavior of agents—both living and artificial—within dynamic environments. Through
experimentation and modeling
, we study human and animal cognition while designing
transparent, adaptive, and personalized
human-machine interaction systems.

Research Areas

Robotic Learning
for mobile manipulation and social interaction

Infant Laughter Analysis
to develop socially interactive robots

User Behavior Modeling
for human-computer interaction (HCI)

Haptic Platforms
for immersive virtual reality (VR)

Counterfactual Reasoning
for AI awareness architectures

Adaptive Digital Interfaces
for older adults

Virtual Patient Simulation
for medical training

Language & Semantics
research

Social Affordance Learning
for human-robot collaboration

Advanced Robotics
for handling soft materials
