AI & ML interests

I am deeply fascinated by the intersection of artificial intelligence and narrative construction, particularly within the domain of role-playing games (RPGs). My interests lie in exploring and expanding the capabilities of large language models (LLMs) to generate dynamic, context-rich dialogues and scenarios that push the boundaries of interactive storytelling. Through my research, I am committed to understanding and advancing the nuances of natural language processing, aiming to achieve nuanced character development and plot progression that feels both authentic and engaging. The ultimate goal of my explorations is to create AI that can not only participate in but also facilitate and enrich the RPG experience, bringing to life complex worlds and characters with depth and personality.

My research is dedicated to developing sophisticated text-generation algorithms that can handle the complexities of RPG contexts. I focus on creating AI models capable of understanding and maintaining long conversations, tracking a multitude of parameters and state conditions, and responding with high-quality, relevant content. This involves rigorous testing and fine-tuning of LLMs to produce text that is not just coherent and context-aware, but also rich in detail and adaptable to the fluid nature of RPG narratives. The research I conduct is designed to push the envelope of what is possible in AI-driven text generation, aiming for a seamless blend of AI responsiveness within the imaginative realms of role-playing games.
