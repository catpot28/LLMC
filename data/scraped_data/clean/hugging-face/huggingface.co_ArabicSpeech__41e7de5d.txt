About org cards

ArabicSpeech

We welcome
everyone working on Arabic speech
- researchers, engineers, students, and practitioners, to use this space to share or link your models, datasets, benchmarks, tools, and demos.

Our goal
is a
central, open repository
spanning Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) and Arabic dialects (Egyptian, Levantine, Gulf, Maghrebi, etc.) across tasks such as ASR, TTS, and more.
Publish your work here so the community can discover, reuse, compare, and build on it.

Mission

ArabicSpeech
began as a volunteer and community effort to catalog Arabic speech resources and grew into a community hub for MSA and dialects. Over time, it expanded from curated lists to open datasets, benchmarks, and models, --- now hosted on Hugging Face, welcoming everyone to share, reuse, and build together.
