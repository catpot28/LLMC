Affordable furniture and home inspiration for a better everyday life at home

Welcome to IKEA Australia, where you'll always find affordable furniture, stylish
home décor
and innovative modern home solutions – as well as design inspiration and
unique home ideas
. Whether you're shopping online for your next furniture purchase or visiting a local IKEA store near you, you can expect low prices on a wide variety of home essentials that are perfect for any space – big or small. So, what are you waiting for? Refresh your home with affordable options for the living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom and more today.

If you want to save even more when you shop at IKEA,
join IKEA Family
– it’s free! Enjoy rewards and instant benefits, as well as a range of convenience features. Love to shop sales? It’s the reward program for you. Enjoy offers across our range, everything from small updates like curtains and sheets, to full-scale home renos like a DIY kitchen or built-in wardrobe.

Sounds great, but how do I find an IKEA store near me? Find all our IKEA locations with our helpful
store locator
.

home office
