Member offers and discounts
All our favourites enjoy offers and discounts, just for being them. Look out for the blue IKEA Family labels in-store and online.
Rewards from IKEA Family
As an IKEA Family member you can collect points to choose from our selection of handy, helpful and tasty rewards.
Just-in-case protection
Our favourites never get in trouble when they break something during transport or assembly. If you have an accident, we'll exchange it for free.
Just-for-members food offers
Feeling hungry? Whether it’s a quick coffee, breakfast or dinner, you’ll always have access to our just-for-members prices on a range of meals in the Swedish Restaurant and Swedish Food Market.
Thank-you surprises
Like a $10 voucher to help celebrate your birthday. We’re grateful for our IKEA Family members and value every one of them like our favourite. So, occasionally we’ll surprise you with something nice, just to say thanks.
Workshops and events for our favourites
Are you a dreamer or a doer? Well, now you can be both. Our events and workshops show you how to bring your home ideas to life.
