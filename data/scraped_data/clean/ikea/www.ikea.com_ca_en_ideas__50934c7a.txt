Get ready for cottage season
It is that time of year when we open up our cabins to let in the fresh air and start preparing for the upcoming season. Begin by cleaning and organizing every space so things stay tidy for you and all your guests. Transport fresh linens, food, and supplies in large clear bins so you can see exactly what you have and where it should go.
