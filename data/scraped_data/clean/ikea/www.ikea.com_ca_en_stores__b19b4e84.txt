IKEA Canada Plan and order points

Our Plan and order points are specialized IKEA service locations where you can work one‑on‑one with a planning specialist to design and order products for more complex home projects—such as kitchens, wardrobe systems, or any room in your home. We can also help you with everything for your business from furnishing advice and orders, to product delivery and assembly. Plan and order points are focused on personalized planning rather than a full-store shopping experience.
