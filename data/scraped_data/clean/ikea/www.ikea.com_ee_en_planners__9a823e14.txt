Kitchen planning
We know that planning and buying a kitchen is a complex project, with lots of choices and decisions to consider. Need some help? Use our 5-step guide to make a journey towards your new IKEA kitchen as convenient and stress free as possible.
Book a consultation here
Use this program to create your kitchen plan in 2D or 3D format.

Note:
If you are planning the kitchen yourself, after saving your plan in the Kitchen planner, click the “Proceed” button and your project will be automatically sent to our kitchen specialists. Within 5 working days, we will revise your project, adjust the plan if needed, and send you an email with instructions on how to purchase the required products. You can also see the revised and adjusted project by logging in to your Kitchen planner account.
