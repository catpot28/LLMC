Smarter shopping with the IKEA app
Whether you've got a project in mind or just need fresh ideas, the IKEA app is your first stop for inspiration. Explore, find personalised deals and offers, visualise your dream space and find everything you need to shop online or prepare for your next store visit.​

Scan the QR code or click on the button to download the app

Download the IKEA app

Personalised inspiration, offers & deals

Be one of the first to know about all the latest collections, offers and deals, and get personalised recommendations.

Your IKEA Family membership at your fingertips

See your order updates, your IKEA Family card, member discounts and lists. It's everything you need, all in one place.

Scan as you shop and enjoy a faster checkout in-store

Scan your items as you find them in-store using the IKEA app. When you have finished shopping, go to the payment counter queue and save time.
Scan as you shop and save time when paying!

What is it and what are its benefits?

Use the IKEA app when you visit your nearest store and you can enjoy a
faster
checkout. Scan your items as you pick them up in-store using your phone. That way you can control what you spend as you go round and pay much more quickly. Simple and convenient.

Choose your new sofa without leaving your old one

This may sound like a paradox, but it's a reality with the IKEA app. You can shop from anywhere, track and manage your orders, see your shopping list, check availability in-store or online, or, if you prefer, get ready to visit us in store.

Save lists of your favourites and find them when you need

Lists work seamlessly across your IKEA account. Save what you love online, then find it in the IKEA app ready for your next shopping trip. Future you will be grateful.

Visualise your dream space and view products in 3D

If you're wondering what that bed would look like in your room, or if a particular desk would fit in the space you have set out for it, this function is for you. With IKEA Kreativ, scan your space with the IKEA app, visualise items together and wonder no more.

Relax, we'll show you the quickest route

If you don't want every store visit to feel like you've walked the Camino de Santiago, the IKEA app has a function that shows you a map of your IKEA store, where you are, and where your product is. Everything you need for a smooth sailing. For the moment, this feature is available in all of our stores except: A Coruña, Asturias, Badalona, Sevilla and Zaragoza.

All your IKEA store's information in one place

Every IKEA store has its own features, opening hours and offers. In the IKEA app, select your favourite store and we'll show you all of its relevant information on a single page.

Place your bistro order online and pick it up

How many times have you seen your IKEA store's bistro queue and not fancied the wait? The IKEA app lets you order online and we'll tell you when your order is ready to pick up. You can check whether it's available in your favourite IKEA store.
