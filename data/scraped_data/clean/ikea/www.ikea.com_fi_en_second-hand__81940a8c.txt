FAQ
Can I sell back my IKEA products?
Yes, you can sell back your IKEA products.
Find out more about the options and conditions on our buyback estimator tool
.
What is the Second chance corner online?
The
Second chance corner
(link opens in a new window to our Finnish website) is the place where you can find second-life, second-hand and discontinued IKEA products.
Can I return a product from the Second chance corner?
Products from the Second hand corner can be returned. See our
terms and conditions
.
Can I use the delivery service from IKEA for Second chance corner products?
For the delivery of Second chance corner products you can use our trailer- and van rental. Check our
website
for more details.
Can I sell back any other furniture that is not from IKEA?
Unfortunately, we only accept IKEA products for resale. For other products, there are alternative options available.
