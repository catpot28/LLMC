The products available at Re-shop and Re-use come to us in several ways:

Discontinued products that are in perfect condition but are no longer being sold in-store.

Preloved products from our Buyback service.

Display products from our Showroom that have been used to inspire you and deserve a home.

Some furniture might have a few cosmetic imperfections, but they are all functional and safe to use.

The condition of the products at Re-shop and Re-use will vary, so you will receive the product as it's described. Whether it's in its original packaging or fully assembled, you will buy the product as-is.

Can I sell back my IKEA products?
Yes, you can sell back your IKEA products. Find out more about the options and conditions on our
website
.
What is the Re-shop and Re-use online?
The
Re-shop and Re-use
is the place where you can find second-life, second-hand and discontinued IKEA products.
Is there a maximum of items I can sell back to IKEA?
Per price offer that you make, you can sell back 10 items. See more information
here.
Can I return a product from the Re-shop and Re-use?
Products from the Re-shop and Re-use can be returned. See our
terms and conditions.
Can I use the delivery service from IKEA for Re-shop and Re-use products?
For the delivery of Re-shop and Re-use products you can use our van hire service. Check our
website
for more details.
Can I sell back any other furniture that is not from IKEA?
Unfortunately, we only accept IKEA products for resale. For other products, there are alternative options available.
