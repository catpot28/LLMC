Visit the Ingka Group Newsroom

Ingka Group, which IKEA UK is a part of, is the largest IKEA franchisee, generating over 89% of total IKEA Retail sales in FY22.

Driven by the IKEA vision to create a better everyday life for the many people, Ingka Group brings the IKEA brand to millions of homes.

Visit the Inter IKEA Group Newsroom

IKEA is a franchise business, with many companies operating under one IKEA brand. Inter IKEA Systems B.V. is the franchisor, responsible for continuously developing the IKEA Concept and ensuring its implementation in new and existing markets.

Inter IKEA Group is the group of companies that connects IKEA franchisees with range development and suppliers, and aligns the overall IKEA strategic direction.
