IKEA Store Facilities

The IKEA store offers a variety of facilities so that customers can enjoy shopping comfortably. We have IKEA restaurants and cafés where you can taste traditional Swedish food, as well as a play area where you can play for small children.​

Return・Exchange・Cancellation​

You can return assembled furniture and used items within 365 days of purchase, provided certain conditions are met. We also offer guidance on exchanges for damaged items received and order cancellations.
See Return・Exchange・Cancellation
Spare parts

Find and order screws, cam locks, dowels and more for BILLY, HEMNES, POÄNG, MALM and other IKEA products. Get for free and delivered to your address in about ten days.

Assemble for you​

With IKEA assembly service, you decide how to spend your time. Because some days, assembling furniture is fun and fulfilling. Other days, not so much. Our professional, convenient service lets you get life sorted instead of sorting screws.
See Assembly Service
Buyback & Resell

With Buyback & Resell, you sell us used IKEA furniture you don’t need in a safe, streamlined way year-round. You’ll get IKEA credit. Someone else buys your old stuff second hand from us and gives it a new home. Everyone wins, especially the planet.

Sofa and Mattress Take Back Service​
Need to make room for your new sofa or mattress? On delivery of your new IKEA mattress or sofa, we are happy to take the old one away and recycle/reuse/dispose of it for you. Free of charge.
