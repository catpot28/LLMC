Stores in Tokyo metropolitan area extend opening hours to 20:00
From Monday, 2 March, we’re making it even easier to visit IKEA stores across the Tokyo metropolitan area. All stores in the area will extend their opening hours to 20:00.

IKEA Tokyo-Bay offers even greater accessibility, with opening hours extended to 21:00 on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays — so your visit can fit naturally into your day.

IKEA Shibuya continues to welcome you daily from 10:00 to 21:00.

With these new hours, we hope to make it even easier for you to enjoy your time at IKEA. Stop by whenever it works best for you—we look forward to welcoming you.

Facilities​
IKEA Store​
The IKEA store offers a variety of facilities. You can enjoy shopping comfortably. Please check the details page of the IKEA store you plan to visit for information on how to use it and the facilities you can use.
Shuttle bus

From Shin Yokohama Station to IKEA Yokohama, and from Umeda, Namba, and Taisho to IKEA Tsuruhama, shuttle buses are available. For more details, please visit the store pages for IKEA Kohoku and IKEA Tsuruhama.
Free parking

​IKEA stores, excluding city shops, offer parking lots that can accommodate approximately 650 to 2,000 vehicles. A more spacious parking area is also available near the elevators. Passengers with strollers or wheelchairs can get on and off easily. Please use the designated loading zone for loading large items. ​
Parking fee
: IKEA Tachikawa you pay 1000YEN which will be reimbursed after shopping at the checkouts. IKEA Sendai offers free parking up to 3h. For more details please visit the local store page​
EV Charging​

Available at stores with charging stations for electric vehicles (EV).​
Bicycle parking lot

If you come by bicycle or motorcycle, please use the bicycle parking lot at the store.​
Car sharing service

​We have set up a Times car sharing station. Please see the Times Car website for details on how to use it.​
Wheelchairs​

Our stores are barrier-free so you can enjoy shopping with peace of mind. Wheelchairs are available at all IKEA stores. please ask the staff at the store entrance. Only IKEA Tachikawa and IKEA Sendai are available on the 2nd floor.​
Baby Care Room

​Nursing rooms are available near the entrance on 1F and next to the IKEA restaurant and café on 2F. For hot water and other facilities please ask our co-workers. ​
Playroom SMÅLAND

​Children's favorite Småland is a supervised IKEA playground where you can leave your children in safe hands while you shop – free of charge. In the Småland playroom you can swim in the ball pool, watch movies, draw, color or otherwise just play. Playtime is 1 h (during peak hours 45 min). We welcome all children aged 4-10 years old to play​​​
Free luggage locker

There are lockers near the 1F entrance where you can store your luggage for free. If you have a lot of baggage, leave it in a locker and enjoy convenient shopping ​​
ATM​

A Seven Bank ATM(セブン銀行) is located near the 1F entrance.
Free Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi service is available in all areas of the IKEA store. It is possible to connect to the Internet from devices without 3G/4G (LTE) lines. You can use it on the spot by selecting a designated network name (SSID) and agreeing to the terms of use. ​
SSID: IKEA WiFi​
Water Station

IKEA supports sustainable living and to reduce the use of plastic. You can use the app “mymizu” that allows you to search for water supply locations for free. Please feel free to bring your own bottle and use the water stations.​
Smoking

​Smoking is not allowed inside the store. We have a designated smoking area outside of our buildings.​
