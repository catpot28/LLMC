Why IKEA Gift Registry?
On IKEA.com, you can browse thousands of products by category, by using the search function, or through our inspirational image galleries. Any product can be added to your gift registry, just by the click of a button. Changed your mind? You can always remove or add new products by coming back to your registry anytime.
What is a gift registry?
The IKEA Gift Registry makes it easy to celebrate special events in life with your family and friends. For example, these events can be baby showers, weddings, housewarmings and more.
When you create a gift registry at IKEA.com, you can choose gifts from our vast range of products. Your family and friends can then choose what to give from your list and complete the purchase either online or in an IKEA store near them.
