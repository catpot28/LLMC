Buying and selling second hand IKEA furniture is easy
Do you want to buy?
Just browse items, put them in the shopping cart, and pay without extra fees, for a limited time. Then arrange a place and time to pick up and complete the purchase.

Do you want to sell?
Decide which IKEA furniture you want to sell and post it with just a few clicks. When it’s sold, you can choose whether to have the money transferred to your account or receive an IKEA gift card valid for 3 years. The gift card will have a value 15% higher than the money you receive in your account.
Buy & sell
What can I sell on IKEA Marketplace?
IKEA Marketplace allows you to sell nearly all historic and current IKEA products. However, you cannot sell food items, mattresses, kitchen appliances, or products that are not from the IKEA brand. Your listing will always be reviewed by the IKEA Customer Support team before it’s published.
Who am I buying from?
You are purchasing an IKEA product from another customer who has decided to sell it. You can see their name in the listing. The IKEA Marketplace team, powered by Archive, is here to answer any questions you have throughout this experience.
Does IKEA guarantee the quality of products sold on this site?
IKEA does NOT guarantee:

The existence, quality or authenticity of the products advertised

The truth or accuracy of sellers’ content or listings on the service

The ability of sellers to sell products through the service and arrange for the products to be picked up by the buyer.

However, your purchases are protected when you buy with IKEA Marketplace. Upon pick-up, you can cancel any products that were misrepresented and get a refund.
Why is IKEA doing this?
This is seen as a complement to the existing IKEA business and as part of fulfilling the IKEA vision to create a better everyday life for the many people. As a Home Furnishing Retailer, it makes sense for IKEA business to enter the second-hand home furnishing market.
