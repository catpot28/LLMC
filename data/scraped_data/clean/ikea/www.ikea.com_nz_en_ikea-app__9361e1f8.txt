Smarter shopping with the IKEA app
Whether you’ve got a project in mind or just need fresh ideas, the IKEA app is your first stop for inspiration. Get inspired, explore personalised deals and offers, visualise your dream space with IKEA Kreativ and find everything you need to prepare for your next store visit.​

Scan the QR code or click on the button to download the app

Download the IKEA app

Personalised inspiration, offers and deals

Be one of the first to know about all the latest collections, offers and deals, and get personalised recommendations.

Your IKEA Family membership at your fingertips

See your order updates, your digital IKEA Family card, member discounts and lists. It's everything you need, all in one place.

Choose your new sofa without leaving your old one

Shop from anywhere, track and manage your orders, see your purchase overview, check availability in-store or online, or prepare for a store visit.

Save your favourites in lists and find them on-the-go

Lists work seamlessly across your IKEA account. Save what you love online, then find it in the IKEA app ready for your next shopping trip. Future you will thank you.

Scan as you shop and enjoy a faster check-out

Find and scan items as you shop in-store with the IKEA app and enjoy a quicker exit at the express check-out.

Save time. Order food on your phone

Enjoy shorter waiting times when ordering at the Swedish Restaurant and Swedish Bistro with Food Mobile in the IKEA app.
