Together with our social business partners, we’ve designed the MÄVINN collection to match the skills of artisans in Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, Jordan and Thailand. With every MÄVINN product, you help support a more inclusive society while purchasing unique handmade home furnishings made with a lot of heart.
