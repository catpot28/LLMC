FAQ
Can I sell back my IKEA products?
Yes, you can sell back your IKEA products. Find out more about the options and conditions on our
website
.
What is the Second Chance Corner online?
The
Second Chance Corner
is the place where you can find second-life, second-hand and discontinued IKEA products.
Is there a maximum of items I can sell back to IKEA?
Per price offer that you make you can sell back 10 items. See more information
here.
Can I return a product from the Second Chance Corner?
Products from the Second Chance Corner can be returned. See our
terms and conditions.
Can I use the delivery service from IKEA for Second chance corner products?
For the delivery of second chance corner products you can use our trailer- and van rental (not available at IKEA Delft). Check our
website
for more details.
Can I sell back any other furniture that is not from IKEA?
Unfortunately, we only accept IKEA products for resale. For other products, there are alternative options available.
What can I sell on the IKEA second-hand marketplace?
IKEA second-hand allows you to sell nearly all historic and current IKEA products. Your listing will always be reviewed by the IKEA Customer Support team before it’s published.

Items you can't sell:

Food items

Mattresses

Kitchen appliances

Products that are not from the IKEA brand

Who am I buying from on the Second-hand marketplace?
IKEA second-hand products are sold by other customers. The names of the sellers are always displayed in the listings. Contact the
IKEA second-hand team
if you have any questions via our support function.
Why IKEA Second-hand marketplace?
Every year millions of good, functional secondhand furniture is thrown away. By keeping IKEA furniture out of landfills and finding them new homes instead, we hope to encourage people to reduce waste and prolong the life of our products.
