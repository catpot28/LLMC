About us

What started as a one-man mail order company has become a global home furnishing brand that brings affordable, well-designed home furnishing to people all over the world. Here you can learn more about our heritage.
Learn more about us

IKEA Qatar Newsroom

Welcome to IKEA Qatar Newsroom, here you’ll find our latest news and related press material.
Social impact
We want to help build a society that’s fairer and more equal. To do this we will put people first and grow our business in an inclusive way.
Making a positive social impact
A sustainable everyday
We want to empower people to live more sustainably, so we’re focusing on affordable, resource and energy efficient products.
A more sustainable life at home

How to live more sustainably at home
We understand that more and more people are interested in reducing the impact they have on the planet, but that many don’t really know how to do it.
Delicious, healthy and sustainable food for the many
At IKEA, we believe that making healthier and more sustainable food the easy, affordable, and delicious choice is a vital part of creating a better everyday life for the many people.
Choosing more sustainable materials
We aim to use only renewable or recycled materials and to provide new solutions for our customers to prolong the life of products and materials.

Climate and environment
As a global brand meeting many millions of customers around the world every day, we have a big climate footprint, and therefore a big responsibility to reduce it.
