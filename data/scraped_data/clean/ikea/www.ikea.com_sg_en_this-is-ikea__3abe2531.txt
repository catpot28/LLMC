How to live more sustainably at home
We understand that more and more people are interested in reducing the impact they have on the planet, but that many don’t really know how to do it.
Delicious, healthy and sustainable food for the many
At IKEA, we believe that making healthier and more sustainable food an easy, affordable, and delicious choice is a vital part of creating a better everyday life for the many people.

We aim to use only renewable or recycled materials and to provide new solutions for our customers to prolong the life of products and materials.
