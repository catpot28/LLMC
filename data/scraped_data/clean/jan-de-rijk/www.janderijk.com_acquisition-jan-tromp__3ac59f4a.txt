Jan de Rijk Logistics continues to grow with the acquisition of Jan Tromp Transportgroep
Roosendaal / Eindhoven, December 5, 2025

Jan de Rijk Logistics has acquired Jan Tromp Transportgroep. This step expands our capacity, specialist expertise and reach across Europe. Customers gain more options, a broader service offering and a stronger network.

The activities of Jan Tromp Transportgroep align directly with our portfolio in road transport, ADR transport, conditioned and time-critical transport, air cargo logistics and industrial relocations. Our services will continue as usual.

A clear step forward.
That’s how we move forward.
