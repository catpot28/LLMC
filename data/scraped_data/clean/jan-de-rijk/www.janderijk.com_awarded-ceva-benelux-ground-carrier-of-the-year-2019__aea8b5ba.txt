Awarded CEVA Benelux Ground

Carrier of the Year 2019

Roosendaal, November 15 – CEVA Logistics Benelux announced at its 2nd Carrier Connection Event that Jan de Rijk Logistics is the Ground Carrier of the Year 2019!

We are extremely proud of the award by CEVA to our organization, as we work to be the most trusted Logistics Service provider for every customer we serve.

Supply chain visibility is at the core of any Jan de Rijk Logistics service, which gives our clients maximum control over their shipments. This was also explicitly praised by the jury.

Special thanks to the Jan de Rijk operational and customer service team members! Their hard work made this award possible!
