Why working at Jan de Rijk Logistics?

Has logistics always been your passion and do you now want to expand that passion into a career? Are you curious, driven and a real go-getter? If so, use those talents! Then you are at the right place at Jan de Rijk Logistics. You will have the opportunity to learn and grow together with a diverse group of colleagues. Together we make it happen!

Discover what you can achieve with us

At Jan de Rijk Logistics there are no predetermined career paths. From day one you have the freedom and responsibility to create your own future direction. Because we find it important to deploy the right person for the right role, we continue to invest in your development.

Whether you want to work for us as a driver, warehouse employee, customer service employee, mechanic, sales employee or in another role: we will encourage you to develop your talent.

A working environment with room for differences

In our various business units, we have a wide variety of activities for various levels of education and specialisms.

Would you like to work at Jan de Rijk? Or are you looking for an instructive and challenging internship or graduation position?
