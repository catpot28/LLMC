Working as a driver at Jan de Rijk Logistics
Working as a driver in the transport and logistics sector is working in a sector that matters: it is a vital profession. We never stand still. The work is always there and we go for it. We warmly welcome you to work with us at Jan de Rijk Logistics. Together we make it happen!
THAT’S HOW.

Your career never stands still

Working at Jan de Rijk Logistics means working for one of the top players in the market. We are growing and are therefore looking for people who want to grow with us and continue to learn. We support you with education and training.

What are you looking for as a driver?

The variety, social contact, working hours, freedom and independence are all attractive aspects of driver work. Jan de Rijk Logistics can give you that freedom as a driver. Our areas of work are diverse, we have permanent or part-time opportunities, whether you have a BE, C or CE driving license. There is certainly a suitable job for you.

What we take care of

We ensure that you can perform your work in a safe, comfortable and reliable working environment. Not only by allowing you to work in a modern vehicle, but also by providing the right facilities along the way.
