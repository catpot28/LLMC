Working in logistics at Jan de Rijk Logistics
Delivering the right products at the right time and in the right place is not easy. No two days are the same in logistics. If you are interested in processes from the initial product to the end product at the customer, then you will certainly also like working in logistics. On this page you will find our
vacancies in logistics
. We therefore welcome you to get started with us. Together we arrange every step; from start to finished product!
THAT’S HOW
.

Logistics is the future!

Working at Jan de Rijk Logistics means working for one of the top players in the market. We are growing and are therefore looking for people who want to grow and continue to learn. The logistics sector is constantly evolving and you have to keep up. Behind a large-scale automation is a complex logistics process. Our logistics employees come up with innovative solutions to renew, simplify and make the process more sustainable. You make that possible and we support you with education and training. So when our customers grow, you grow with them.

A dynamic logistics world

The logistics sector is challenging. The logistics industry is one that has to keep up with the times. It is constantly necessary to look at how business processes can be used more effectively so that the final supply chain runs smoothly. With the growing e-commerce market and globalization that has brought with it new issues for years, logistics will only continue to grow.

Logistics is for everyone

You can spend your entire career in logistics. Depending on your background and starting position, there are always opportunities for personal development and growth. Variety enough, no day is ever the same.

Are you an organizational talent, do you like to roll up your sleeves; then we are looking for you.
