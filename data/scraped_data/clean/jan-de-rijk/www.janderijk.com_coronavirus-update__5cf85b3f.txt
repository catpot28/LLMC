Update 20 March 2020

Roosendaal, 20 March 2020 – TLN (Transport and Logistics Netherlands) published an update that answered the question: “Which borders are open and which borders are not open?”. In this update, we will provide you a short summary of this document of TLN. This document includes information about the Coronavirus in Europe and the borders in Europe.

Belgium

There will be no measures for freight transport in Belgium. Besides, there is a relaxation of driving and rest times for transport from 14 to 31 March 2020 for drivers involved in transport of medicines and essential goods.

Denmark

Denmark closed its borders, but not for freight transport. Freight transport from and to Denmark can cross the border in both directions. There are measures taken at the border crossing points to ensure that trucks can more easily cross the border. This arrangement lasts until 13 April 2020.

Germany

There are extra controls, but no other restrictions on border crossing points for transport of goods. The German Government has asked the federal states to suspend the weekend bans on truck movements to alleviate the situation during the crisis.

Finland

Transport of freight and goods continues as normal in Finland.

France

Freight transport is still possible and therefore the borders are currently not closed for the transport of goods.

Hungary

The border from Austria to Hungary is closed for freight transport. Moreover, the borders from Hungary to Slovenia are closed. Freight transport arriving and departing from Hungarian destinations is allowed to cross the border and can continue the journey without restrictions.

Italy

Goods can still be transported to and from Italy. The transport of goods is considered as essential, therefore truck drivers can enter and leave Italy.

Netherlands

Transport is still possible in Europe.

Austria

Austria suspends enforcement of driving and rest times for the period until 13 April 2020.

Poland

In order to prevent the spread of the Coronavirus, location forms at the border are mandatory, also for truck drivers.

Portugal

They will carry out border controls. Freight transport is still possible, but only the large border crossings are opened.

Slovenia

Foreign trucks over 3.5 tons are not allowed to pass the border. Only trucks that have goods for Slovenia as final destination or trucks that are loaded with special stamps for medical equipment or pharmaceuticals are allowed.

Slovakia

Freight transport is still possible and allowed.

Spain

International freight transport from and to Spain remains possible.

Czech Republic

The Czech Republic closes its borders, but freight transport is still possible. The Czech Republic suspends enforcement of driving and rest times until 16 April 2020.

United Kingdom

The United Kingdom has taken measures, but these measures do not affect international and domestic freight transport.

Sweden

Freight transport is still possible.

Switzerland

International transport of goods remains possible.

Despite these developments, it is important to keep in mind that there will be border delays as a result of the additional controls at the borders. Look here for the
Truck Border Crossing Times
.
