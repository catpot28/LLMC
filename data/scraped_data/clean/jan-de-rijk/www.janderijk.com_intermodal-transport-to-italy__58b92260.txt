Intermodal transport: reliable, economical and sustainable!

Roosendaal, 14 May 2020 –
Jan de Rijk Logistics offers reliable, economical and sustainable logistics solutions to Italy. Slowly but surely Europe is coming out of the lockdown again due to the Coronavirus (COVID-19). We are therefore going to further increase the frequency of our trains to Italy. Discover more about the possibilities for
Intermodal Transport
in this blog and discover what we can do for you!

Own company train for intermodal transport to Italy

Operating with our own company train, we connect the BeNeLux and the German Ruhr Area with Northern Italy. 10 trains depart weekly, Monday till Saturday. We make sure that the containers are loaded on the train in route order, so that we can save our customers in Italy a day of lead time. The goods can then be delivered to the unloading address as soon as possible.

Options for LTL and FTL transport

Jan de Rijk Logistics offers various options for transporting your goods to Italy. In addition to FTL, it is also possible to transport groupage and partial loads (LTL transport).

Reliable door-to-door service

We provide a reliable door-to-door service. We pick up the goods for you during the day at the location you want, after which we leave them by train the same evening. With our own transport equipment, we ensure that LTL and FTL loads are delivered reliably and quickly throughout (Northern) Italy after arriving by train.

Different types of containers

Did you know that we can transport different types of containers with intermodal transport? It concerns the following container:

Tilt containers;

Box containers;

Cooler containers;

Mega tautliners.

Intermodal transport to Austria

In addition to options for intermodal transport to Italy, Jan de Rijk Logistics also offers options for intermodal transport to Austria. For this we have a connection between Neuss and Wels.
