Jan de Rijk

invests in Autostore

Advanced fulfiment solutions

Jan de Rijk Logistics invests in their warehouse with the innovative AutoStore system! Lalesso Logistic Solutions will supply this fully automated solution integrated with all its extensions, like conveyors, box erectors and closures, label applicators, and related software.

Don Brewel – Director Contract Logistics:
“ I am extremely proud of this investment, as it will give a significant boost to our contract logistics activities. With the AutoStore, we can offer a tailormade fully automated solution to our customers. The solution is ideal for high volume cost-efficient piece picking. With this investment, we can expand our b2c e-fulfillment service offering for our customers .
”

This high-tech warehouse solution gives Jan de Rijk Logistics the ability to offer super-efficient, scalable and future-proof services to its clients. With 15,000 bin locations, 11 robots, and 3 stations, Jan de Rijk is capable of reaching more than 150 picks per station per hour. Should the business grow? No problem, just add robots for higher performance, or expand the grid and add more bins for increased warehouse capacity.

The solution is engineered in close corporation with the UC Group. With this development, Jan de Rijk Contact Logistics is kicking off its ambitious growth strategy for the coming years and is aiming at growing in specific warehouse segments such as Technology, Retail and eCommerce, Automotive and Engineering, and Manufacturing.
