JDR Freight Solutions, your logistics challenge is our concern

Roosendaal, 10 April 2020 –
Freight Solutions is a relatively new department within Jan de Rijk Logistics. Freight Solutions is Jan de Rijk Logistics’ answer to the many Control Tower solutions that can now be found in the logistics market. A one-stop shop for all services. We ensure seamless coordination between the various departments within Jan de Rijk Logistics, but we also manage your or our suppliers in all European countries.

We offer you a transport competitive solution for all your logistics challenges. Whether the challenge is 1 pallet or 100 tons, we have the solution!

Jan de Rijk Freight Solutions offers you the best solutions at the right price and with the best service. We do this not only with our own trucks, trains and locations/buildings, but also by using an extensive partner network. In this way, JDR Freight Solutions offers competitive prices with the quality and service you can expect from Jan de Rijk Logistics.

Innovation

By applying extensive digitization, Jan de Rijk Freight Solutions strives for continuous process optimizations so that you always have insight into the operation and get the desired optimal solution. We offer you access to our online portal, “
J-Connect
”. In this online portal you can not only place your order, but also find direct and real-time tracking & tracing information about your shipment. After delivery you will also find the proof of delivery signed by the recipient. If you prefer personal contact, our Customer Service employees are of course always available.

Whether you are looking for logistics solutions for
road transport
,
intermodal transport
,
contract logistics
or other logistics services: our Freight Solutions Control Tower will take care of it!
