Air Cargo
With over forty-five years experience in air cargo road feeder services, Jan de Rijk Logistics has the capability, knowledge and network to offer end-to-end air cargo logistics solutions. We are our aviation customers’ extension on the ground. More information? Feel free to contact us or request a quote on our website!

An unrivaled network between European airports

For many years, Jan de Rijk Logistics has been the leading provider of Road Feeder Services to the Air Cargo industry, with LTL and FTL departures to every airport in Europe. Our LTL schedules can be found by clicking on one of the following links (

With our modern and hi-tech fleet and qualified and experienced staff, Jan de Rijk Logistics has the scale to cope with the capacity challenges faced by the Air Cargo industry.

Control Tower Air Cargo

Airlines can safely outsource their complete ground operation to Jan de Rijk Logistics. Via our Control Tower we can take care of all your road transport and offline airport handling requirements. We have a proven track record of cost effectively improving service standards with our specialist systems, equipment and people.

Our central planning department with dedicated staff is available 24/7/365. Advanced dynamic order management and planning systems, with real-time tracking and tracing of vehicles and shipments, give Jan de Rijk Logistics’ customers maximum transparency and visibility. We offer an extensive network between 100 airports in Europe, which is unrivaled by any of our competitors. Find here more information about JDR Freight Solutions.
