Automotive Logistics
With a varied transport fleet, dedicated and multi-user warehouses and fully customized customer assembly lines, Jan de Rijk Logistics can solve even the most complex logistical challenge.

Jan de Rijk Logistics is
ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and IATF 16949:2016 certified
. These certifications rely heavily on the quality of people, equipment and processes. This quality is what allows us to fully support and optimize your supply chain. This applies to the OEM, TIER1 and TIER2 markets, as well as the After-Market and Parts markets.

As part of our
Assembly
activities, Jan de Rijk Contract Logistics supports OEMs (Original Equipment Manufacturers) in assembling, testing and delivering modules, systems and complete machines. The delivery of the executed activities is done by our own specialized fleet Just-in-Time (JIT) and Just-in-Sequence (JIS).

New products are implemented via the
APQP
(Advanced Product Quality Planning) process. This guarantees the delivery of reproducible series of high-quality products. To make this happen, we apply all core tools: SPC, FMEA, Control Plan, MSA and PPAP.

Assembly

Assemble products as you wish

Jan de Rijk Logistics supports OEMs (Original Equipment Manufacturers) in assembling, testing and delivering modules, systems and complete machines.

The coveted IATF 16949:2016 certificate proves that Jan de Rijk Automotive Logistics offers all the in-house expertise necessary to assemble products to meet every requirement. In addition to assembly, we deliver JIT & JIS; LEAN and Six Sigma ensure fully aligned processes. Client specifics are inventoried at an early stage.

New products are implemented via the APQP (Advanced Product Quality Planning) process. This guarantees the delivery of reproducible series of high-quality products. To make this happen, we apply all core tools: SPC, FMEA, Control Plan, MSA and PPAP.

Jan de Rijk Logistics is a high-quality assembly partner for OEMs.

Value Added Services for Automotive Logistics

Fully traceable and delivered with zero defects

With 5 strategic locations in the Netherlands, Jan de Rijk Logistics is the gateway to Europe. All locations have an advanced Warehouse Management System (WMS). This enables us to offer a variety of high-quality Value-Added Services. Item-picking, bonded storage, kitting and piece picking, sequencing, repacking, labeling and wrapping, fiscal representation and quality controls.

Even controlled delivery from non-EU countries through owned container transports and customs is an option. Moreover, thanks to our advanced IT and in-house expertise, it is possible to successfully interface all other systems.

Automotive Logistics transport

State-of-the-art Transport Management System

With a modern and varied fleet, controlled by a state-of-the-art Transport Management System, Jan de Rijk Logistics is able to cater all transport disciplines. From dedicated transport to inbound logistics, from on-site transport to day and night distribution, and from (inter)national FTL and LTL transport to intermodal and container transport.

All vehicles are also suitable for carrying out JIT & JIS transport services. Even if clients decide not to use the Jan de Rijk Logistics fleet, they can still have us carry out their Control Tower activities.

Industry Verticals

Jan de Rijk Contract Logistics has identified five verticals including automotive as their strategic framework for developing new customized logistics solutions, investing in warehouse automation, and real estate development. These developments will contribute to achieving our strategic growth path and to further transform Jan de Rijk Logistics into an all-round logistics service provider.
