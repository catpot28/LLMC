General Cargo

Jan de Rijk Logistics’ dedicated employees, innovative IT systems and vision combine to continually exceed our customers’ high service expectations. Whether you’re looking for a one-off ad-hoc truck movement or a full logistics partnership, our excellent transport and handling network makes Jan de Rijk Logistics a reliable partner for the transport of General Cargo throughout Europe. Feel free to
contact
us for more information or request
a quote
on our website!

Our Europe-wide road freight network enables us to offer fast connections between production plants and key markets. Our customers benefit from seamless communication, tracking & tracing, and the professional assistance of our local employees. Across Europe
Jan de Rijk Logistics
also cooperates with local partners.

Dangerous Goods (ADR / DGR) and Customs

All our drivers are ADR-certified and trucks are fully equipped. Jan de Rijk Logistics is also AEO-certified and our trailers are TIR-certified, so that we are able to transport your

(bonded) shipments inside but also outside the European Union.

Digital and transparent

Transparency is key in any supply chain. Jan de Rijk Logistics supports your business by using state-of-the-art systems to offer seamless communication. We make business easy. You can directly connect your ERP-system to our Enterprise Service Bus (ESB) using various industry communication standards, or via our customer portal, making bookings and documentation easy. Jan de Rijk Logistics will provide insight during every step of the process from the point of origin until the scheduled delivery at the final destination. In order to support your administrative processes, you will receive a digital copy of every shipment document.

We offer wide ranging solutions for all shipments using our versatile fleet of:

Mega trailers (100 m3)

Box trailers

Frigo trailers / reefers

Tilt trailers

Double floor trailers

Multimodal Transport for General Cargo

An economical transport solution

Alternatively, we also offer the combined transport between road and rail. Next to cost advantages such as the reduction of tolls and fuel surcharges, this mode of combined transport lowers the CO2 emissions and helps to protect the environment.
