Retail & E-commerce

Meeting the demand and expectations of today’s consumer is becoming more and more challenging. Jan de Rijk Contract Logistics is able to handle peak sales periods, but at the same time maintain the overall performance throughout the supply chain. The combination of highly automated and conventional warehouse solutions enables us to easily adapt to the required demands.

Whether you’re an
retail
chain, small business owner or a logistics fulfilment provider, Jan de Rijk Contract Logistics has a solution that fit your needs. From high volume pallet storage with distribution throughout Europe to a dense
e-commerce
operation with parcel deliveries directly to the consumer. We’ve got it covered.

As an extension of our warehouse-related activities, Jan de Rijk Logistics has an extensive
BeNeLux retail fleet
, with which hundreds of shops are supplied every week. Based upon the 3PL concept we’re able to execute the full chain to its extend.
