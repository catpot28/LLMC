Minimize risk in the electronics supply chain

Goods are typically stolen while in transit, cargo theft predominantly threatens the supply of electronics and consumer goods, and, thus, hotspots for cargo theft are typically places where goods transit between supply chain sites. Jan de Rijk Logistics can offer a very secure supply chain having highly secured sites for warehousing and in the extend of the warehouse services, Jan de Rijk Logistics offers safe
TAPA-Certified
logistics solutions across the whole of Europe to clients for whom a fully controlled process and time-to-market are essential. Transport is executed with TAPA-TSR1 certified vehicles, movements are monitored 24/7, and route-fencing and geo-fencing are used.

The Transported Asset Protection Association (
TAPA
)
is the world’s leading supply chain resilience and security association. TAPA aims to ensure the integrity and resilience of global supply chains, enabling sustainable supply chain risk management and loss prevention through the adoption of proven industry security standards, the gathering and sharing of threat and loss intelligence, training, networking and collaboration with all stakeholders to ensure the secure movement and supply of goods for businesses and consumers.

Network equipment & Datacenters

All that technology requires state of the art networks and data centers that constantly have to be reinvented and redeveloped. The modern data center is an exciting place, and it looks nothing like the data center of only 10 years past; in general they are changing at an exponential pace. The build up of
data centers, communication networks
and installations of
automated warehouses
require vast amounts of
hi-tech and (fully populated) server racks
against a very strict and tight schedule, often during small service windows. Failure is not an option. This is where we can offer all-in-one transport, in which capacity, planning and coordination are the key factors. We are able to carry out a complete (international) relocation. Speed, expertise, and coordinating power is what customers can expect from us.

Healthcare equipment & highly sensitive machinery

These transports require expertise, state of the art equipment and a vast experience. Carefulness and craftsmanship offer our customers a guarantee that their equipment will be delivered correctly and without fail. It is precisely our expertise and experience that allows us to develop a well-thought-out plan for each specific occasion. With every special project we put together an experienced team of specialists who come to a project proposal in close collaboration with the client. Jan de Rijk has therefore for years been successful in the transportation of highly sensitive medical scanners to hospitals and lithography systems for the semiconductor industry.
