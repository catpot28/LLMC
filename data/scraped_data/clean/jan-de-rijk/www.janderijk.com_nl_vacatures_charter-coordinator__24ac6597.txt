Charter Coördinator

Roosendaal – 40 uur per week

Jan de Rijk Logistics (founded in 1971) is a leading European logistics service provider, operating with a modern fleet of approximately 1,000 vehicles and various specialised warehouses. The company provides a wide range of services, including intermodal solutions, international transport, contract logistics, Benelux and retail distribution, container transport, temperature-controlled transport, event logistics and forwarding.

With more than 1,300 employees spread across various offices and countries in Europe, this family business not only offers challenging and varied tasks, but also modern workplaces in a familiar environment with motivated teams.

Do you get energy from continuous dynamics in logistics? Are you curious, driven and a real go-getter? Then Jan de Rijk Logistics is the right place for you. You will have the opportunity to develop personally and grow further together with a diverse group of colleagues. Together we can do it!
THAT’S HOW
.

What are you going to do?

As Charter Coordinator, you are the linchpin that ensures charter capacity planning runs smoothly, efficiently and flawlessly. You take ownership, ensure clear communication and optimize resources so that collaboration, quality and results continuously improve. In this way, you deliver measurable impact and demonstrate where real operational strength comes from. You achieve this by focusing on the following tasks:

Acting as the link between departments, with clear communication towards teams, charters and stakeholders, with an eye for accuracy, quality and a well-balanced network.

Identifying issues and ensuring fast and efficient handling of claims.

Act as a connecting link between departments, with clear communication towards teams, charters and stakeholders.

Lead or support improvement and digitalization initiatives to make processes smarter, more efficient and more transparent.

Ensure accurate registration and reporting to support continuous improvement and transparency.

You will work from our head office in Roosendaal, where you will be part of an experienced and enthusiastic team.

Are you the
Charter Coordinator we are looking for?

We are looking for a new colleague who takes responsibility, exudes quality and adapts effortlessly to a dynamic environment. Someone who shows ownership, communicates clearly and quickly establishes connections within the organization. You have an eye for detail and the big picture, know how to set priorities and remain effective, even when everything is happening at once. You understand how processes interact, think ahead, and act with sensitivity to the organization around you. Flexible, committed, and results-oriented, you bring structure, energy, and quality together.
THAT’S HOW.

Who are you?

We are looking for a customer-oriented and accurate professional with a passion for logistics and administration. You are independent, stress-resistant and have the following skills and qualifications:

Knowledge of logistics processes, including transport, distribution and purchasing processes.

Experience with Microsoft Office and WMS systems.

Speaks fluent English and preferably also Romanian or Bulgarian.

Excellent command of the English language, both spoken and written.

Proactive and solution-oriented attitude; you can respond quickly to questions and complaints.

Able to work both independently and as part of a team.

What we offer?

A fixed-term employment contract (12 months) with the prospect of permanent employment;

You will be paid every four weeks;

A modern working environment with a team that is driven to innovate;

Attractive fringe benefits such as 8% holiday allowance, good pension scheme, company laptop, 27.5 days’ holiday, 8% holiday allowance, good pension scheme.

Interested?

If reading this vacancy has sparked your interest, please send your CV and cover letter to
werken@janderijk.com
.
