FAQ – Benelux Transport

What is Benelux transport?
Benelux transport refers to road transport between locations in the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg, often extended to Northern France and the Ruhr area.

Do you offer 24-hour delivery across the Benelux?
Yes. We operate a structured network designed for 24-hour transport across the Benelux and nearby regions.

Which regions do you cover?
We cover the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Northern France and the Ruhr area through a fixed network of operational hotspots.

Where are your hubs located?
Our network uses operational hotspots in Roosendaal, Eindhoven and Beilen. These serve as control and routing points. Transloading only takes place when there is a change in mode of transport.

How do you ensure reliable delivery times?
We plan flows through fixed corridors and operational hotspots, supported by central planning and real-time tracking.

Do you provide real-time visibility?
Yes. Shipments are monitored in real time, allowing full visibility and proactive management of delays.

What types of shipments do you handle?
We focus on full truckload (FTL) transport between A and B, designed for time-critical and production-linked flows.

Can you support production-linked logistics?
Yes. Our network is designed to support production schedules, ensuring consistent and predictable transport performance.

Do you offer electric truck transport in the Benelux?
Yes. Electric trucks are deployed on selected routes where range, charging infrastructure and planning allow. These are typically fixed, high-density corridors.

When is electric transport a good fit?
Electric trucks work best on predictable A–B flows with stable volumes and defined time windows, allowing efficient planning and charging.

Does electric transport impact lead time?
No. Lead times remain aligned with agreed service levels. Planning accounts for charging and range constraints.

Can electric transport support CO₂ reduction targets?
Yes. Electric trucks significantly reduce emissions on applicable routes and can support your sustainability reporting.
