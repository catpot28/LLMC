Container Transport
Our container transport services are an integral part of our customers’ end-to-end supply chain solutions. As well as arranging transportation, we manage administration and the preparation of Customs documents.

Our skilled drivers are known at the various container terminals in the Netherlands (ports of Rotterdam and Amsterdam) and Belgium (port of Antwerp), where we pick up or return the containers. All our trucks are equipped with a Track & Trace system.

Inbound container transport

Jan de Rijk Logistics operates a large dedicated fleet of transport equipment. Our container transport services between (inland) container ports and our customers’ warehouses cover the entire Benelux.

We load daily from the ports of Antwerp and Rotterdam, and our European road feeder network enables us to deliver to and collect from any European harbor or destination. Jan de Rijk Logistics operates as an extension of your supply chain.

Our inbound services can easily be combined with contract logistics, distribution, and transport solutions.

Container Trucking Service Network

As well as booking and coordinating the movement of sea containers worldwide, we can also arrange administrative activities, including the preparation of customs documentation.

Supply chain solutions

We offer complete supply chain solutions, including warehousing, fulfilment, intermodal transport, and distribution. We also offer value added services (VAS/VAL), cross-docking and storage services at our own warehouses. Naturally, we also offer stand-alone container transportation, operated by our large dedicated fleet of container trucks.

Customs Services

We also offer customs services, like import, export and transit declarations.

Container trucking forms an integral part of our complete transportation service. We work alongside our customers to find the best specialist solutions and can arrange additional capacity and equipment on demand
