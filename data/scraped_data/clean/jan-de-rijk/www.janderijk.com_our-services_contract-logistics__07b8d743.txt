Customized logistics solutions and fulfilment services: What sets us apart
At Jan de Rijk Logistics, we are not just a logistics provider; we are your partner in achieving supply chain excellence! We design custom supply chain solutions and offer comprehensive fulfilment services matching the unique needs of your business. With expertise in industries such as tech/consumer electronics, ecommerce, regulated FMCG, fashion, and luxury goods, our dedication to customization and detail sets us apart. From high-tech electronics to fashion logistics, we understand the complexities of each sector and provide specialized handling to meet your exact requirements.

Our Eindhoven Location, Built for Brainport Supply Chains

For companies operating in the Brainport region, our Eindhoven site offers direct access to a strong production and technology ecosystem.

From this location, we support production-critical and value-intensive supply chains with integrated warehousing, technical handling and dedicated transport solutions.

Whether supporting automotive suppliers, high-tech manufacturers or sensitive cargo flows, our Eindhoven operation is designed around control, continuity and operational ownership.

How we deliver

Customized solutions
: We start by understanding your challenges and requirements, creating solutions that are precisely tailored to meet your needs.

Security
: Our high-security premises and bonded warehouses ensure your goods are safe and secure at every stage.

Personalized approach
: We believe in building strong, lasting partnerships with our clients, offering a personal touch beyond traditional logistics services.

Tech-Savvy solutions
: Utilizing the latest logistics technology, including Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), Transportation Management Systems (TMS), Business Intelligence (BI), and Enterprise Service Bus (ESB), we provide advanced solutions that enhance efficiency and visibility throughout your supply chain.

Fulfilment services
: Our core end-to-end fulfilment services include inventory management, order processing, picking and packing, shipping, and returns management, ensuring a seamless and efficient flow from warehouse to customer.
