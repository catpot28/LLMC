GDP certified

All our employees are GDP (Good Distribution Practices) trained; they know exactly how to handle medicines and related products. Jan de Rijk Logistics offers its customers quality, flexibility, high delivery reliability and solutions for transport and storage of, among other things, time-critical and high-quality products.

Our employees all have an official governmental Statement of Good Conduct. They are also in possession of the correct diplomas and certificates. In addition, they are regularly retrained. The team consists of experienced employees who are focused on unburdening our customers. Our personnel is essential for delivering good quality to our customers and are therefore carefully selected for this job.

In short, we unburden your logistics process and provide a suitable logistics solution!

The right temperature throughout the entire chain

In our warehouse and during delivery, we measure for each medicine whether the temperature remains within the norms of 2-8 or 15-25 degrees Celsius.

In addition, we have our own storage space and a varied fleet of vehicles from small delivery vans to tractor-trailer combinations with a track & trace system and continuous real-time monitoring of temperature. This enables us to offer our customers a total logistics package. We stand for short communication lines and fast service. The customer is always central to us! Within two hours we can serve the whole of the Netherlands from our location in Houten.

Are you curious about which logistics solution we can offer you? Please contact one of our experts without any obligation. We are there to help!

Environmentally friendly fleet

A fleet that meets the latest requirements

Sustainable distribution service is of great importance to Jan de Rijk Logistics and its customers. The fleet of vehicles for deliveries within the Benelux, and in particular the inner-city areas, meets the latest requirements with regard to environmental and noise standards. Examples are hybrid trucks, whisper trailers, and eco-combinations.

Temperature conditioned transport

Temperature secured

Our entire fleet, from small delivery vans to tractor-trailer combinations, has a track & trace system and continuous real-time monitoring of temperature. This allows us to guarantee the temperature at all times. This is real-time trackable and transparent. The temperature profile is continuously recorded and stored electronically. You also know the location and the current temperature of the transport space at any time.

Home delivery

We deliver refrigerated medicines (2-8 degrees and 15-25) and medical devices with extra care directly to the patient. Fully GDP validated and traceable.

Beyond the doorstep

The service of Jan de Rijk Logistics goes beyond the doorstep. Jan de Rijk Logistics provides distribution to pharmacies throughout the Benelux; also outside opening hours. The goods are placed in the goods locker at agreed window times, which can be reached by means of an access code and/or key.
