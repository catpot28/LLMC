International transport by Jan de Rijk Logistics

When you have international transport arranged by Jan de Rijk Logistics, we ensure that you are always aware of the status with our advanced and dynamic order management and planning system. With our real-time tracing and tracking system you can monitor 24/7 where, for example, the truck is currently driving with your shipment. All our vehicles are equipped with on-board computers. This means that not only we, but also the customer, always has a real-time overview of the status of shipments. With a document scanner in every vehicle, we have a signed freight document immediately after delivery.
Transparent and insightful.

Jan de Rijk Logistics arranges transport throughout Europe. All shipments are tracked via our own
logistics portal
or via the EDI integrations system. An optional
Control Tower solution
based on
smart algorithms also provides full operational visibility. Something that saves time and money.

Specialist in international transport for the following markets

Air Cargo

Jan de Rijk Logistics is the leading provider of Road Feeder Services to the
Air Carg
o industry for many years, with a LTL and FTL service to every airport in Europe. We offer Road Feeder Services, direct delivery & pickup services and supports customers with handling and documentation. With its transport units and qualified staff, Jan de Rijk Logistics has the scale to cope with the capacity challenges of the industry.

Healthcare

With 200+ temperature-controlled vehicles and full GDP certification, Jan de Rijk Logistics can safely arrange transport for the pharmaceutical industry. A high-quality logistics solution that can guarantee product safety. A necessary requirement in the
healthcare
.

Perishable transport

Controlling temperatures is essential for the transport of perishable products. Jan de Rijk Logistics offers daily, conditioned transport of perishable goods throughout Europe.

We offer full national coverage in Western Europe and in particular groupage shipments to and from
Italy
. Jan de Rijk Logistics has been transporting flowers and plants to and from this region for many years.

General cargo

We are a reliable partner throughout Europe for customers who require fast and flexible delivery of shipments. Through vision, dedicated staff and excellent IT systems, we succeed time and again in exceeding the high expectations of customers in various industries. Whether it’s ad hoc transport or a logistics partnership, our European network makes Jan de Rijk Logistics a reliable partner for
general cargo
across the continent.

Fast-moving consumer goods food/non-food

Food products can spoil and therefore need to be transported in the right way. Jan de Rijk’s international transport is arranged with online temperature registration and the route is as short as possible thanks to the train connection in Europe. We are
ISO 22000:2005 certified
for food. With more than 200 refrigerated trailers (reefers), we ensure that all food products are transported safely and arrive at their destination in good condition.
