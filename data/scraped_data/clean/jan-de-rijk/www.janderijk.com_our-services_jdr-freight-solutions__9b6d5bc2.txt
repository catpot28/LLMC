Customs solutions

Brexit means that there is no longer any free movement of goods to and from the United Kingdom. Goods transactions have since been subject to customs formalities.
Jan de Rijk Freight Solutions
was ready on time! For example, we have further expanded our own customs team and linked our customs system to the system of the customs. We also have a wide network of customs agents within the United Kingdom. This allowed us to offer the total solution for our customers from the start.

Customs Clearance

Goods imported or exported into Europe must be declared to customs for import or export. Freight Solutions will arrange the entire handling of the customs formalities for you.

Fiscal representation

When clearing goods, every company must pay VAT. This is only possible if you as an importer are a Dutch company and have a VAT number. Freight Solutions , together with the customs team, provides fiscal representation for companies outside the EU.

Control tower solution

Our control tower solution provides insight into your supply chain across divisions, countries and modalities.

Relevant information from various sources comes together in the control tower. Via the Enterprise Service Bus, the system is able to collect and combine enormous amounts of data and yet present it clearly to the operators. We use this to improve logistics processes through fast, fact-based decisions.
