Renewing our IATA CEIV pharma certificate
Roosendaal 09-09-2024:
We are excited to announce that Jan de Rijk Logistics has successfully renewed its
IATA CEIV Pharma certification
for our transport services for another 3 years. This certification is a clear sign of our commitment to the safe and efficient transportation of pharmaceutical products.

Picture our fleet of temperature-controlled vehicles, carefully maintained to ensure smooth transport. When you open the door, you feel the cool, stable air—your cargo is kept at the perfect temperature, safe every kilometer of the way. The IATA CEIV Pharma certification is more than just a label; it’s proof of our dedication to meeting the highest standards in pharmaceutical logistics.

Our team has put in countless hours to ensure that every part of our transport service meets the strict IATA CEIV Pharma requirements. We take great pride in delivering your sensitive products with the utmost care and precision, every single time.

At Jan de Rijk Logistics, we don’t just transport—we excel. This renewed certification strengthens our position as a trusted partner in the pharmaceutical industry, committed to providing the safest and most reliable logistics solutions.
