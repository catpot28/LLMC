Sustainable Collaboration for a Greener Future!
Roosendaal, March 11,
2025:
At Jan de Rijk Logistics and
Bonduelle
, sustainability is at the heart of everything we do. We believe that by working together in a responsible way, we can create a brighter and greener future. Our focus on sustainability guides our strategies, shapes our decisions, and influences how we manage our supply chains every day.

Our partnership with Bonduelle is built on a shared commitment to environmental care. Every step we take, from planning and organizing logistics to delivering products, is planned with sustainability in mind. This means that we always look for ways to reduce waste, lower our carbon footprint, and support eco-friendly practices. Working together, we make sure that our operations not only meet today’s needs but also protect the environment for the future.

We are very proud to share that our successful collaboration is one of the highlights in the special edition of Business Enquirer. The article in Business Enquirer offers a closer look at how our two companies join forces to build a stronger and more sustainable supply chain. It explains how our combined efforts create a more resilient and environmentally responsible business model. To learn more about this inspiring journey and the many benefits of our partnership, please follow this link to read the full article:
Business Enquirer Issue 135
.

The article is a great example of how sustainability and smart collaboration can work hand in hand. It describes our shared vision of a future where business success and environmental care go together. Our work not only benefits our companies but also supports a better community and a healthier planet.

At Jan de Rijk Logistics and Bonduelle, we are excited about what lies ahead. We continue to explore new ways to improve our practices, reduce our impact on the environment, and set an example in the industry. We are convinced that by combining our strengths and working together, we can overcome challenges and create new opportunities for growth.

We can’t wait to keep building our collaboration and achieving even more together.
