The future of

supply chain resilience

Jan de Rijk Logistics attends the TAPA EMEA conference in Dublin on 23 & 24 October 2019.

Supply chains need to adapt and respond to unprecedented levels of disruption. The need for end-to-end supply chain security and resilience has never been greater.

The Transported Asset Protection Association (TAPA) is a unique forum that unites global manufacturers, logistics providers, freight carriers, law enforcement agencies, and other stakeholders with the common aim of reducing losses from international supply chains.

Jan de Rijk Logistics has been an active TAPA member for over 15 years. Jan de Rijk Logistics offers
secured TAPA transport
that ensures a seamless supply chain integration that gets your product to the customer in a safe and quick way and at the same time keeps your inventory cost low.

Meet our professionals:

and
Rick Vermunt
.
