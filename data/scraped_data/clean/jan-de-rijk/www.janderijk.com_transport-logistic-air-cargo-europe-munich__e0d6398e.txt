Jan de Rijk Logistics at Transport & Logistics Messe 2025 — June 2-5, 2025

Your high value and sustainable solution provider in Air Cargo and Road Feeder Services.

We are excited to announce that
Jan de Rijk Logistics
will be part of this year’s
Transport and Logistics Messe in Germany
— Europe’s leading event for shaping the future of global transport. As a
specialist in Air Cargo and Road Feeder Services
, this is a key moment for us to connect with partners, clients, and innovators across the industry.

As a trusted
Air Cargo and Road Feeder specialist
, Jan de Rijk Logistics is known for
secure and time-critical deliveries
across an
extensive European network
. At the Messe, we’ll share with you our solutions on
reliability, sustainability, and innovation
— from smart route planning to low-emission transport solutions.

Join us to discover how we’re moving logistics forward — faster, greener, and smarter.

We look forward to connecting with you at our booth, which is located at
A1.231
!
