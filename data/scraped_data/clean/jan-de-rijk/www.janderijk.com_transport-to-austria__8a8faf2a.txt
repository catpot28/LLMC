Transport to Austria by road

With Jan de Rijk Logistics’ accumulated experiences, it had thrived throughout the years to be the specialized logistical solution provider in Austria. Our trucks will gladly transport your desired goods to and from Austria, daily, as per your needs, to the locations you require, and the timings you decide! We can also provide you with an economic transport alternative with our intermodal train connection to Austria. Feel free to contact for more information!

By road and rail: intermodal transport to and from Austria

Jan de Rijk Logistics is your specialist for intermodal transport for Austria.

Why use intermodal transportation? Transporting large volumes whether these are FTL or LTL is our core business. We transport in a sustainable and ecological way as we believe this is the best solution for our customers and the environment.

Intermodal transport
is an environmental friendly and economic alternative to transport by road. We arrange door-to-door transport in line with your desired turnaround time for FTL and LTL transport!

Security.
Intermodal transport is very secure. Using dedicated intermodal cargo units protects the freight during the transport cycle, reducing the risk of theft and damage to a minimum. Our service is from door-to-door, from pickup to delivery. Strict procedures ensure a safe transition between first and final mile movements and the train. On the train, the shipment is in a sealed closed container on a direct rail connection to the destination. We make sure your container is continuously on the move.

Environmental benefits.
Sustainability is important for us – and for our customers. We are focused on sustainability and we are developing solutions to limit our impact on the environment. Compared to road transport intermodal transport results in a reduction of your carbon footprint. Intermodal connections can result in up to 80% lower CO2 emissions compared to road

Why Jan de Rijk Logistics is the right partner for the transport of your goods to Austria?

You’ll always be with safe hands if you choose Jan de Rijk Logistics! Our experts in Austria have been in the Austrian market for years. Hence, they’re fully familiar with the Austrian culture, language, legal system, and geography. Also, our office being in Hörsching puts us in the middle of the market. Hörsching is located near the capital of the federal state of Upper-Austria: Linz. We’ll keep you at complete ease by arranging you with the transport service best suitable for your needs.

Daily destinations

Daily destinations in Austria include Linz and Vienna. Are you curious about other destinations in Austria or all possibilities? Please do not hesitate to contact us. Based on your wishes, we are happy to provide the logistics solution by offering an appropriate offer.

Groupage or LTL services

Does the transport of your goods not need to be delivered in a hurry? One of our specialties is groupage of partial shipments. Do you need to ship some goods urgently? Then choose our shipping service! In addition to that, we offer FTL transport, multimodal or LTL transport to Austria with a longer transit time and lower costs.

Intermodal transport to Austria

In addition to Jan de Rijk Logistics’ own train connection with Italy, we also offer intermodal transport to and from Austria. The train runs between Neuss in the Benelux and Wels in Austria. From there, we ensure that your goods are delivered to the right place through road transport. Still unsure about which logistical solutions will best fit your Austria transport requirements? We’re more than happy to walk you through the options we have!

High value and high secured transport to Austria

Jan de Rijk Logistics is an experienced partner for high secured transport to different destinations in Europe. The trailer fleet of Jan de Rijk Logistics is TAPA TSR 1 and 2 certified. With GDP/GMP-certified personnel and vehicles, Jan de Rijk Logistics offers customers from the pharmaceutical industry a high-quality logistical solution for transport to Austria. With more than 200 temperature-controlled vehicles (including standard, mega and combined trailers) and the online J-Connect® monitoring system, Jan de Rijk Logistics is your partner for this industry.
