Transport to Belgium

With Jan de Rijk Logistics’ accumulated experiences, it had thrived throughout the years to be the specialized logistical solution provider in Belgium. Our trucks will gladly transport your desired goods to and from BeNeLux, within 24 hours. We will deliver to the locations you require and the timings you decide!

Jan de Rijk Logistics is the right partner for the transport to or from Belgium.

Jan de Rijk Logistics operates with a modern fleet of more than 1,000 own trucks. In combination with our experienced drivers and innovative concepts, we take care of transport to Belgium. With our office in Brussels, we are right in the middle of the Belgian market and we are the ideal partner for solving your logistics problem. In addition, Jan de Rijk Logistics offers warehousing options: we provide your logistics solution with fast and flexible services.

Benelux Distribution

We arrange distribution for you in the Benelux, northern France and the German Ruhr region in accordance with consistent high quality. We ensure that deliveries are made within 24 hours in the Benelux. Jan de Rijk Logistics’ fleet meets the latest requirements with regard to noise and environmental standards. Consider, for example, transport in eco-combi, hybrid trucks and whisper trailers.

Air Carco Road Feeder Services

Jan de Rijk Logistics has a lot of experience in Air Cargo Road Feeder Services. We have the capability, knowledge and network to offer end-to-end air cargo logistics solutions. We are our aviation customers’ extension on the ground. Jan de Rijk Logistics offers direct delivery, Road Feeder Services, and pick-up solutions

Retail Distribution

Jan de Rijk Logistics offers Retail Distribution in Belgium. We are happy to meet the challenges of urban distribution, including changing window times, difficult-to-reach locations, congestion, and delivery outside opening hours with our smart distribution concepts. We are a reliable partner for every retailer. Jan de Rijk Logistics has a modern fleet, well-trained drivers, innovation projects, and smart IT systems for your Retail Distribution.

Groupage or LTL services

In addition to the transport of FTL, we also offer options for groupage of partial shipments or LTL transport. Ideal to use if, for example, your goods are less in a hurry. Do you want your goods to be transported to Belgium as soon as possible? Then choose our regular service and ask for the options that suit you best.

Route optimization for your distribution

Logistics optimizes your distribution process by means of advanced software. This optimization saves you the necessary kilometers and shortens delivery times. In addition, an increase in reliability. With our route optimization, we ensure that known traffic bottlenecks are avoided wherever possible.

For you as a customer, you’ll have maximum transparency and visibility. You can track the vehicles and loads with our real-time tracking & tracing system. Jan de Rijk Logistics works with an advanced and dynamic order management and planning system to ensure you a great quality service and goods safely
