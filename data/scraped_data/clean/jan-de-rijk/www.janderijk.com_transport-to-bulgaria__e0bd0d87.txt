Knowledge of the Bulgarian market

Jan de Rijk Logistics has its own location near Sofia Airport. With our own location in Sofia, we are right in the middle of the Bulgarian market. We are fully familiar with the Bulgarian language, culture, geography, and regulations. Our Bulgarian experts have been working in this market for years and can therefore advise and help you perfectly. We ensure reliable and safe transport to Bulgaria and other European destinations. Customers have trusted us with their most vulnerable and urgent products for many years now, and for good reason. We provide the best suitable route for the transport of your goods.

LTL or groupage to Bulgaria

Is the transport of your goods less urgent? One of our specialties is groupage of partial shipments. In addition, we also offer LTL and multimodal transport with a longer transit time and lower costs. Are you in a hurry with transport to Bulgaria? Then choose our regular service to Bulgaria.

IT-Systems of Jan de Rijk Logistics

IT systems are indispensable for our logistics solutions. Our logistics solutions stand or fall with our IT solutions and systems. Thanks to the use of advanced systems, we ensure optimal control of our service and distribution. Think, for example, of transport management, planning and tracking & tracing software.
