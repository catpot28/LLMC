Why Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport to Croatia?

Jan de Rijk Logistics
is a leading logistics service provider in Europe. We operate with a young and modern fleet of 1,000 vehicles and have various specialized warehouses. Thanks to our own capacity, we are flexible and we offer various options for transport throughout Europe. In addition, our central planning is 7 days a week and 24 hours a day available to help you.

Safe and reliable transport

We are completely familiar with the different routes that can be taken to your destination in Croatia. We adjust our route depending on the circumstances or nature of the goods. It is not without reason that our customers have entrusted us with their most vulnerable and urgent products for years. we also drive GMP/GDP certified vehicles and our staff is also GMP/GDP certified. The trailer fleet of Jan de Rijk Logistics is TAPA TSR 1 and 2 certified.

Groupage or LTL to Croatia

Is the transport of your goods less urgent? Groupage of partial shipments is one of our specialties. In addition to this service, we also offer multimodal or LTL transport with a longer transit time and lower costs. Are you in a hurry to transport to Croatia? Then choose our regular service for Croatia transport. We also offer
Contract Logistics

Route optimization for the distribution

Jan de Rijk Logistics uses route optimization. We optimize your distribution process with advanced software. The route optimization saves the necessary kilometers and shortens delivery times. In addition, this increases reliability. With the route optimization, Jan de Rijk Logistics ensures that the known bottlenecks in traffic are worked out and where possible avoided.
