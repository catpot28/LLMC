Why Jan de Rijk Logistics is the right partner for the transport of your goods to Denmark?

At Jan de Rijk Logistics, visibility of the supply chain is key. We ensure maximum visibility and transparency with the dynamic and advanced order management and planning system with real-time tracking & tracing of vehicles and loads. Shipments can be traced via the logistics portal or via system EDI integrations. In addition, an optimal Control Tower Solution provides the basis of smart algorithms, full operational visibility and saves both time and costs. Thanks to our scale, we offer a very reliable and flexible service for transport to Denmark and other European destinations.

Conditioned to high secured transport

We provide a logistics solution that suits your goods. How about, for example, the transport of temperature-sensitive products? Jan de Rijk Logistics has more than 200 temperature-controlled vehicles. For example, with GMP/GDP-certified employees and vehicles, we offer customers from the pharmaceutical sector a high-quality logistics solution. In addition, Jan de Rijk Logistics offers a secure TAPA-certified logistics solution throughout Europe for secured transport. This transport takes place in TAPA-TSR1 certified vehicles. Route fencing and geo-fencing are used and the vehicle movements are monitored 24/7. There is also in-house screening, safety awareness training and crime prevention. In short, every effort is made to ensure that your goods are delivered safely and reliably to the destination.

The Coronavirus and transport to Denmark

Currently, the Coronavirus (Covid-19) keeps us all busy. That is why we think it is important to keep you informed of the latest developments and news. You will find more information about our transport services and the Coronavirus on this page.

Different types of shipments

Jan de Rijk Logistics takes care of the transport for various shipments. From FTL (Full Truck Load) to LTL (Less than Truck Load) shipments. In addition, groupage of partial shipments is our specialty. Do you want to have your goods delivered to Denmark as soon as possible? Then choose our regular service. If your goods are not in a hurry, you can use our services with a longer transit time and lower costs.

Route optimization for your distribution

Logistics optimizes your distribution process by means of advanced software. This optimization saves you the necessary kilometers and shortens delivery times. In addition, an increase in reliability. With our route optimization, we ensure that known traffic bottlenecks are avoided wherever possible.

For you as a customer, you’ll have maximum transparency and visibility. You can track the vehicles and loads with our real-time tracking & tracing system. Jan de Rijk Logistics works with an advanced and dynamic order management and planning system to ensure you a great quality service and goods safely
