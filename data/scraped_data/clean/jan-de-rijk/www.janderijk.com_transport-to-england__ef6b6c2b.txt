What crossing do you want for your transport to England?

We arrange suitable transport to England by ferry or via the Eurotunnel. Our experts are fully familiar with the schedules of the many ferries departing from the European mainland to England and Scotland. Depending on the destination and the nature of the goods, we can advise on which mode of transport to the UK is best suited for your shipment.

It is not without reason that we transport the most urgent and vulnerable products to and from England for our customers. Examples include express shipments, dangerous goods, air freight and perishable goods.

In addition, our colleagues from the London office are always available to assist you.

Groupage or LTL transport

Are your goods in a hurry? Groupage of partial shipments is our specialty. In addition to this service, we also offer LTL or multimodal transport to England with a longer transit time and lower costs. Is your transport in a hurry? Then choose our regular service.

We use some strategically positioned permanent partners for transport to the United Kingdom. As a result, we have fast lead times, which offers you the opportunity to supply your customers reliably, sustainably and efficiently throughout England.

Route optimization for your distribution

Logistics optimizes your distribution process by means of advanced software. This optimization saves you the necessary kilometers and shortens delivery times. In addition, an increase in reliability. With our route optimization, we ensure that known traffic bottlenecks are avoided wherever possible.

For you as a customer, you’ll have maximum transparency and visibility. You can track the vehicles and loads with our real-time tracking & tracing system. Jan de Rijk Logistics works with an advanced and dynamic order management and planning system to ensure you a great quality service and goods safely
