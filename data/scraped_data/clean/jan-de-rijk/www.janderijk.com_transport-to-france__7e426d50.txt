Safe Transport to France

Jan de Rijk Logistics is an experienced partner for high secured transport to France. The trailer fleet of Jan de Rijk Logistics is TAPA TSR 1 and 2 certified.

With GDP/GMP certified personnel and vehicles, Jan de Rijk Logistics offers customers from the pharmaceutical industry a high-quality logistical solution for transport to France. With more than 200 temperature-controlled vehicles (including standard, mega and combined trailers) and the online J-Connect® monitoring system, Jan de Rijk Logistics is your partner for this industry.

For you as a customer, you’ll have maximum visibility and transparency. You can track the vehicles and loads with real-time tracking & tracing system. Jan de Rijk Logistics works with an advanced and dynamic order management and planning system to ensure you a great quality service and goods safely delivered.

Reliable Transport to France

Due to our familiarity with the different routes and destinations in France. We will provide you with the most efficient route to the final destination of your products depending on the nature of the goods.

It is not without reason that our customers have entrusted us with their most urgent and vulnerable products to and from France for many years. Think for example of express shipments, plants, flowers, and air freight. Jan de Rijk Logistics is a specialist in the transport of high value and high secured goods throughout Europe.
