Transport Greece

We manage and take care of all shipments to and from Greece.
Whether your request concerns full truck loads or LTL services to or from Greece, we are your reliable partner, and always offer our customers an individual and professional transport solution.

Why Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport?

Jan de Rijk Logistics
offers a wide range of services: from container transport and forwarding to temperature-controlled transport. We operate with a modern fleet of more than 1,000 vehicles, and we also have various specialized warehouses. Thanks to our large European network, we provide a suitable logistics solution for you goods? We do everything we can to improve, guarantee and strengthen our quality management system. Thanks to our own capacity, we are flexible and provide a fast service with well-trained staff. Did you know that our central planning is also 24 hours a day, 7 days a week available? Jan de Rijk Logistics takes care of the transport to Greece and other destinations in Europe.

Groupage or LTL transport to Greece

Is the transport of your goods to Greece less urgent? One of Jan de Rijk Logistics’ specialties is groupage of partial shipments. In addition, we offer multimodal and LTL transport options with a longer transit time, but lower costs.
