Why Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport to Hungary?

Jan de Rijk Logistics
offers a wide range of services. With a large European network, we offer the logistics solution for your goods. From container transport to temperature-controlled transport and forwarding: we offer many options for transport to Hungary. Jan de Rijk Logistics makes every effort to guarantee, improve, and strengthen the quality management system. Thanks to our own capacity, we are flexible and can provide fast service with well-trained employees.

Office in Budapest

We have an office in Budapest, near Budapest Airport. Thanks to our employees, we can provide you with perfect advice and help. They are fully familiar with Hungarian culture, language, rules, and geography. Our Hungary experts have been working in this market for years. Jan de Rijk Logistics ensures safe and reliable Hungary transport and is also known in the rest of Europe. We provide the most suitable route for the transport of your goods: fast and flexible. Consider, for example, possibilities for double-manned transport for continuous and fast transport. Customers have entrusted us with their most urgent and vulnerable products for transport for many years. This is not without reason!

Not only FTL transport to Hungary

Jan de Rijk Logistics provides FTL transport, but there are even more options. We are happy to offer a solution that suits your needs. That is why groupage of partial shipments, LTL or multimodal transport to Hungary is also possible. Choose the logistics solution that suits you best!
