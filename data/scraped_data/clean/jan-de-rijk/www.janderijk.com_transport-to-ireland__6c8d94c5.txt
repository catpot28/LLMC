Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport to Ireland

Jan de Rijk Logistics knows like no other that your goods arrive safely and on time in Ireland. In addition, we deliver your goods to various destinations throughout the United Kingdom. We are familiar with the various options that the Eurotunnel and ferry services offer. In addition, our safety advisor can advise you on the transport of dangerous goods (ADR transport) to Ireland. We also have an exceptional transport department that can advise you for width transport or project loads. Jan de Rijk Logistics has the right permits and the right material to provide exceptional transport for you to Ireland. In addition, we offer Contract Logistics for your transport throughout the United Kingdom.

The crossing for your transport to the United Kingdom

For the crossing, you can go to the United Kingdom by ferry or via the Eurotunnel. Our experts are fully familiar with the schedules of the ferries departing from the European mainland to England and Scotland. We can advise you about which mode of transport to the UK is most suitable for your shipment. This depends on the destination and nature of the goods you wish to transport to the United Kingdom.

It is not without reason that our customers have entrusted us with their most vulnerable and urgent products for transport to Ireland for many years. Think, for example of express shipments, air freight, perishable goods, and dangerous goods (ADR transport).

Safe and reliable transport to Ireland

Jan de Rijk Logistics is an experienced partner for high secured transport. The trailer fleet is TAPA TSR 1 and 2 certified. With GDP-certified personnel and vehicles, we offer customers from the pharmaceutical sector a high-quality logistics solution for transport. With more than 200 temperature-controlled vehicles (including standard, mega and combined trailers) and the online J-Connect® Monitor system, Jan de Rijk Logistics is ideally suited as a partner for this industry.

LTL or groupage transport to Ireland

One of our specialties is groupage of partial shipments. Ideal if your goods are not in a hurry. In addition, we also offer LTL or multimodal transport with a longer transit time and lower costs. If the transport of your goods is in a hurry, choose our shipping service. Jan de Rijk Logistics uses several strategically positioned permanent partners for transport to the United Kingdom. As a result, we have fast lead times that offer you the opportunity to supply your customers efficiently, sustainably, and reliably.

Daily destinations in Ireland

We provide daily guided transport for FTL, groupage, and LTL to Ireland. The daily destinations are Dublin, Shannon, and Cork. However, there are many more options for Ireland transport. Would you like more information about your desired final destination in Ireland? Please do not hesitate to contact us for more information.
