Transport to Latvia

Jan de Rijk Logistics takes care of the transport of your goods throughout Europe, including to the Baltic States. We arrange transport to Latvia and transport from Latvia for you. Operating with our modern and young fleet, we provide your logistics solution. Are you curious about which logistics solution best suits the transport of your goods? Request

Why Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport?

As a leading logistics service provider in Europe,
Jan de Rijk Logistics
takes care of the transport of your goods throughout Europe. We operate with a modern and young fleet of approximately 1,000 vehicles. In addition, Jan de Rijk Logistics has various specialized warehouses. We provide the logistics solution that best suits your needs. Jan de Rijk Logistics offers a wide range of services: from international transport to Contract Logistics and forwarding.

International transport?

We use a dynamic and advanced system for national and
international transport
. Every place in Europe is within reach for Jan de Rijk Logistics. This is possible thanks to our advanced and reliable fleet, our local offices in many countries and our always available planning department. Supply chain visibility is at the heart of every service. Did you know that you can track the vehicles and load with real-time tracking & tracing? In this way, we ensure maximum visibility and transparency.

The Coronavirus and transport to Latvia

Given the current circumstances surrounding the Coronavirus (COVID-19), we think it is important to keep you informed. That is why you will find more information about the latest developments and our transport services on this page.

LTL or Groupage transport to Latvia

We offer, next to FTL transport, more possibilities for Latvia transport. Jan de Rijk Logistics also offers possibilities for groupage of partial shipments and LTL transport. Moreover, we offer Contract logistics.
