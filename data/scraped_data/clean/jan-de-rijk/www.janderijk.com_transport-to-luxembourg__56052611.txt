Transport to and from Luxembourg

Jan de Rijk Logistics takes care of the transport to and from Luxembourg for you. Our trucks depart daily to Luxembourg and other destinations in Europe to deliver your goods. Jan de Rijk Logistics delivers within 24 hours to the entire BeNeLux thanks to our short lines. We provide your logistics solution from A to Z. Why Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport to Luxembourg?

Jan de Rijk Logistics is a leading logistics service provider in Europe. We operate with a young and modern fleet of approximately 1,000 vehicles. We also have various specialized warehouses. Jan de Rijk Logistics offers a wide range of services and takes care of your logistics solution. From Benelux and Retail Distribution to temperature-controlled transport, Contract Logistics, Event Logistics and forwarding: Jan de Rijk Logistics offers a solution that suits the transport of your goods!

Benelux Distribution

We ensure that your
distribution in the Benelux
, northern France and the German Ruhr region is arranged to a high consistent quality. We ensure this with a modern fleet, experienced drivers and innovative concepts. Jan de Rijk Logistics delivers your products to a destination in the Benelux within 24 hours. From container transport to pallet distribution and deliveries with tail lift. With real-time tracking & tracing of the vehicles, we offer maximum visibility and transparency during the distribution process.

Retail Distribution

All your shipments to customer and shop, that is what Jan de Rijk Logistics takes care of throughout the Benelux. Every week we provide
distribution for approximately 2,000 stores
throughout the Benelux and in parts of Germany and France. We can also supply stores outside opening hours. The goods are then placed at the agreed window times in the goods lock, which can be reached by means of a key and/or access code.

LTL or Groupage transport to Luxembourg

In addition to the transport of FTL, we also offer options for groupage of partial shipments or LTL transport. Ideal to use when the transport of your goods is less urgent. Do you want to transport your goods to Luxembourg as soon as possible? Then choose our regular service and ask for the options that suit you best.

Route optimization for your distribution

Logistics optimizes your distribution process by means of advanced software. This optimization saves you the necessary kilometers and shortens delivery times. In addition, an increase in reliability. With our route optimization, we ensure that known traffic bottlenecks are avoided wherever possible.

For you as a customer, you’ll have maximum transparency and visibility. You can track the vehicles and loads with our real-time tracking & tracing system. Jan de Rijk Logistics works with an advanced and dynamic order management and planning system to ensure you a great quality service and goods safely
