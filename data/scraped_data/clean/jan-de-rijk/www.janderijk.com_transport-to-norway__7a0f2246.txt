Norway transport: safe and reliable

Jan de Rijk Logistics ensures safe and reliable transport. We are fully familiar with the different routes that can be taken to Norway. We adjust our route based on the circumstances or nature and destination of the goods. Our customers have trusted us with their most vulnerable and urgent products to and from Norway for many years. Jan de Rijk Logistics’ trailer fleet is also TAPA TSR 1 and 2 certified. This makes us an experienced partner in the field of
high secured transport
. Thanks to our IT solutions, you can track your goods via a track & trace system.

Groupage or LTL transport

Groupage of partial shipments is one of our specialties, ideal if you goods are less in a hurry. In addition, we also offer multimodal or LTL transport with a longer transit time and lower costs. Is the transport of your goods to Norway in a hurry? Then choose our regular service to Norway. We also offer
contract logistics

Customs matters for Norway

Norway is not a member of the European Union. Norway transport is therefore different from transport to European destinations. We help you completely with transport to Norway. Jan de Rijk Logistics ensures correct, efficient, and fast handling. Thanks to our many years of experience, we have the necessary knowledge in the field of customs matters to help you. Feel free to ask us about the possibilities, we will take care of your logistics solution.
