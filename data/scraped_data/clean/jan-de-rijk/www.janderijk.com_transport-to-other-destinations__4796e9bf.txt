Why Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport to other destinations?

Jan de Rijk Logistics
is a leading logistics service provider in Europe. We operate with a modern fleet of 1,000 vehicles and have various specialized warehouses. Jan de Rijk Logistics offers a wide range of services: from Contract Logistics to intermodal solutions, container transport and forwarding. As a leading transport and logistics service provider, we operate throughout Europe and we provide transport from A to Z for you.
