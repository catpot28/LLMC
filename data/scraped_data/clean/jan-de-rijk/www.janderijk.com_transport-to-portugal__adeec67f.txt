Transport to Portugal by Jan de Rijk Logistics

Jan de Rijk Logistics
knows like no other how to ensure that your goods arrive in Portugal at the right place, time and safely. We are a leading logistics service provider in Europe and operate with a young and modern fleet of more than 1,000 vehicles. We can completely unburden you for transport to Portugal and other destinations in Europe. Jan de Rijk Logistics offers a wide range of services: from

Transport your goods to and from Portugal reliably

We are fully familiar with the different routes to the Portuguese destinations. We therefore adjust the route based on the circumstances or nature and destination of the goods. Jan de Rijk Logistics provides the most suitable route to the final destination of your products in Portugal. We are ‘at home’ all over Europe and ensure safe and reliable transport. It is not without reason that our customers have entrusted us with their most urgent and vulnerable products for transport for many years.

LTL or groupage transport to Portugal

In addition to FTL transport, Jan de Rijk Logistics offers even more possibilities. One of our specialties is groupage of partial shipments. Moreover, we offer multimodal and LTL transport to Portugal with a longer transit time and lower costs. Thanks to the wide range of possibilities, we offer the logistics solution that suits your needs.

Continuous service

In addition to being reliable and safe, Jan de Rijk Logistics is known for its fast and flexible service. Thanks to our large own fleet, our trucks can be found all over Europe and we also find a logistics solution for you. We also offer options for double-manned transport. In this way we provide a continuous service and your products will arrive faster. Thanks to our IT solutions you can, among other things, track and trace your shipment. In addition, our central planning is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You can reach them at any time of the day for any help or questions.

High value and high secured transport by Jan de Rijk Logistics

Are you looking for an experienced partner for
high secured transport
in Europe? Then you are at the right place at Jan de Rijk Logistics! We are an experienced partner and hold the TAPA TSR 1 and 2 certifications for our trailer fleet. We also have GMP/GDP certified personnel and vehicles. This allows us to provide customers from the pharmaceutical industry with a high-quality logistics solution. With more than 200 temperature-controlled vehicles and the online J-Connect® Monitor system, Jan de Rijk Logistics is your ideal partner for these industries.
