has a large European network and offers a wide range of services. Thanks to the many possibilities, we offer the logistics solution for your goods! From temperature-controlled transport to container transport and forwarding: Jan de Rijk Logistics offers many options for transport to Romania. Groupage or LTL transport

If the transport of your goods is in a hurry, we advise you to choose our regular service. It may of course also be the case that your goods are less in a hurry. One of our specialties is groupage of partial shipments. Feel free to ask about the possibilities for groupage or LTL transport. This way you will benefit from lower costs.
