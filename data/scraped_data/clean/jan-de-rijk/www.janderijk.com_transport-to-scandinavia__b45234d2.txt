Reliable transport to Scandinavia

Jan de Rijk Logistics is fully aware of the different routes that can be taken in and to Scandinavia. We therefore adapt our route to the current circumstances and the nature of the goods. Thanks to the use of advanced software, we ensure the route optimization of your distribution process. With our online portal,
J-Connect
, you are up to date with the latest news for the transport of your goods. Here you can, among other things, follow your shipment live via the track & trace. Here you will also find the delivery notes signed by the recipient after delivery. Our Customer Service employees are of course also available to you and our central planning is available 24 hours a day and 7 days a week!

LTL or groupage transport to Scandinavia

One of Jan de Rijk Logistics’ specialties is groupage of partial shipments. This is ideal if the transport for your goods is less urgent. We also offer options for LTL or multimodal transport. These forms of transport have a longer transit time, but lower costs. Is the transport of your goods to Scandinavia in a hurry? Then choose our options for scheduled services to Scandinavia.
