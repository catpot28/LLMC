Why Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport to Slovakia?

Jan de Rijk Logistics
is a leading logistics service provider in Europe. The core of every service at Jan de Rijk Logistics is the supply chain with the dynamic and advanced order management and planning system – with real-time tracking & tracing of loads and vehicles – Jan de Rijk Logistics provides maximum transparency and visibility. Shipments can be traced via the Jan de Rijk Logistics portal or via system EDI integrations. In addition, an optimal Control Tower solution based on smart algorithms offers full operational visibility. It saves you both time and costs.

Groupage or LTL transport to Slovakia

In addition to FTL transport, we offer even more options. Is the transport of your goods less urgent? One of our specialties is groupage of partial shipments. We also offer options for LTL transport. These transport options have a longer transit time, but lower costs. Is the transport to Slovakia in a hurry? Then we advise you to choose our regular service to Slovakia.
