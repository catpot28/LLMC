Transport to Slovenia

Jan de Rijk Logistics provides transport services throughout Europe. We also arrange your transport to Slovenia and transport from Slovenia from A to Z. Why Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport to Slovenia?

Jan de Rijk Logistics
is a leading logistics service provider in Europe. We have several specialized warehouses and operate with a modern fleet of around 1,000 vehicles. International Transport

LTL or Groupage transport to Slovenia

In addition to the FTL transport, we also offer options for groupage of partial shipments or LTL transport. You can use this if the transport of your goods is less urgent. Do you want your goods to be transported to Slovenia as soon as possible? Then choose our regular service to Slovenia. We also offer
Contract Logistics
. Ask for the options that suit you best.

Reliable and safe transport

We are fully familiar with the different routes that can be taken to Slovenia. Depending on the circumstances or nature of the goods, we adjust our route with route optimization. It is not without reason that our customers have entrusted us with their most urgent and vulnerable products for transport across Europe for many years. in addition, Jan de Rijk Logistics has the necessary
certificates
, for example for the pharmaceutical industry and high secured transport.
