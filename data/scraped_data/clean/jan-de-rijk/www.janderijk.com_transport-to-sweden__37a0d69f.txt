Transport to Sweden

Why Jan de Rijk Logistics for transport to Sweden?

Jan de Rijk Logistics
is is a leading logistics service provider in Europe. We operate with a young and modern fleet of around 1,000 vehicles. We also have various specialized warehouses at strategic locations. Jan de Rijk Logistics provides a wide range of services: from Contract Logistics to Container Transport and temperature-controlled Transport. We take care of your logistics solution throughout Europe, so we also take care of the transport for you.

Reliable transport

We are fully familiar with the different routes that can be taken to Sweden. Depending on the circumstances or nature and destination of the goods, we adjust the route accordingly. We take care of the route optimization of your distribution process with the use of advanced software. For years, our customers have entrusted us with their most vulnerable products and that is not without reason! Jan de Rijk Logistics ensures reliable transport to Sweden and other European destinations.

Groupage or LTL transport for Sweden

Is the transport of your goods less urgent? Groupage of partial shipments is one of our specialties. In addition to this service, we also offer options for multimodal or LTL transport. These transport options have a longer transit time and lower costs. Is the transport of your goods in a hurry? Then choose our regular service. Did you know that we also offer
Contract Logistics
? Discover the many possibilities!

Safety during Sweden transport

Jan de Rijk Logistics is an experienced partner in the field of high secured transport. The trailer fleet is TAPA TSR 1 and 2 certified. In addition, with GMP/GDP-certified personnel and vehicles, we offer customers from the pharmaceutical industry a high-quality logistics solution. Jan de Rijk Logistics is ideally suited as partner for this industry with more than 200 temperature-controlled vehicles (including standard, mega and combined trailers) and the online J-Connect® Monitor system.
