Switzerland transport: reliable and safe

We ensure reliable and safe transport to Switzerland. Jan de Rijk Logistics is fully aware of the different routes that can be taken to Switzerland. For example, we adjust our route based on the circumstances or nature and destination of the goods. Our customers have entrusted us with their most vulnerable and urgent products to and from Switzerland for many years. our trailer fleer is also TAPA TSR 1 and 2 certified, making us an experienced partner in the field of

LTL or groupage transport

Groupage of partial shipments is our specialty. Ideal if your goods are not in a hurry. We also offer LTL or multimodal transport with lower costs and a longer transit time. Is the transport in a hurry? Then choose our regular service to Switzerland. We also offer Contract Logistics.

Customs matters for Switzerland

Switzerland is not a member of the European Union. Switzerland transport is therefore different from transport to European destinations. However, you do not have to worry. Our Switzerland specialists can help you. Jan de Rijk Logistics ensures fast, correct and efficient handling. Thanks to our many years of experience, we have the necessary knowledge and expertise in the field of customs matters to help you. Ask Jan de Rijk Logistics about the possibilities.
