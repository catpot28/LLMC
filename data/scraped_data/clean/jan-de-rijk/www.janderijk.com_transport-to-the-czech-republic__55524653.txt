Safe and reliable transport to the Czech Republic

Jan de Rijk Logistics is fully aware of the different routes that can be taken to the final destination in the Czech Republic. Depending on the nature and destination of the goods or circumstances, we provide the most suitable route for the transport of your goods. We provide safe and reliable transport to the Czech Republic and are at home all over Europe. It is not without reason that our customers have entrusted us with their most vulnerable and urgent products for years.

LTL or groupage transport to the Czech Republic

In addition to FTL transport, Jan de Rijk Logistics also offers other transport options. Think for example of LTL transport, multimodal transport or groupage of partial shipments. Is the transport of your goods in a hurry or less? Choose our shipping service if you are in a hurry. In case that the transport of your goods is less in a hurry, groupage of partial shipments would be an option. With a longer transit time, you benefit from lower costs.
