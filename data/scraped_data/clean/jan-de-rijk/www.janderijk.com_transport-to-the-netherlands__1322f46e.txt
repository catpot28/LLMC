Transport to the Netherlands

The Netherlands is the home country of Jan de Rijk Logistics. Therefore, we also offer many options for transport to the Netherlands, from the Netherlands and within the Netherlands. Every day many trucks depart from the Netherlands abroad, but also come back to the Netherlands.
Contact us
for the logistics solution that suits you for transport to the Netherlands, from the Netherlands or in the Netherlands. You can also request a
quote

The expert for transport to the Netherlands

We drive all over Europe every day with more than 1,000 trucks. Since the Netherlands is our home base, we can help you to find a logistics solution for transport to the Netherlands. In addition to transporting goods, we also have various warehouse and cross-dock options. Thanks to our own capacity, we can switch quickly and we are very flexible. Jan de Rijk Logistics ensures that your goods are delivered to the desired location at the right time. We are also available 24 hours a day and 7 days a week.

Benelux Distribution

We ensure that deliveries are made within 4 hours in the BeNeLux. Our experienced drivers, modern fleet and innovative concepts make this possible. Jan de Rijk Logistics takes care of your
distributon
in Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Northern France and the German Ruhr Area. Groupage or LTL transport to the Netherlands

Is the transport of your goods less urgent? One of our specialties is groupage of partial shipments. In addition, we offer LTL transport with a longer transit time and lower costs. Do you want to have your goods transported faster? Then our shipping services are a good choice! Jan de Rijk Logistics ensures that your products are transported quickly and is very flexible.
