Transport to the United Kingdom (UK)

Jan de Rijk Logistics operates multiple daily transport services between mainland Europe and the United Kingdom. We book the appropriate ferry or Eurotunnel crossing and ensure that your shipment is delivered at the right time and place. We offer both accompanied and unaccompanied options. Customized transport to the United Kingdom

Jan de Rijk Logistics’ dedication, efficiency and experience ensure that consignments arrive in the UK safely and at the right time. Our British trucking experts have been working in this market for many years and are fully conversant with all applicable transport regulations.

Our in-house Dangerous Goods Safety Advisers (DGSAs) are on hand to offer advice on the transport of Dangerous Goods (ADR), and our
Aerospace and Exceptional Transport team
specialize in out of gauge and project cargo. We have trucking permits, and our trailer fleet is TAPA TSR 1 & 2 certified.

Safety and security

Jan de Rijk Logistics is highly experienced in the movement of
secure and vulnerable cargo
. Our trailer fleet is TAPA TSR 1 and 2 certified.

With GMP/ GDP certified personnel and vehicles, Jan de Rijk Logistics offers a high quality logistics solution to the UK and is the ideal partner for customers in the pharmaceutical industry. We have more than 200 temperature controlled vehicles, including mega, standard and combi trailers, and the online J-Connect® monitoring system.
