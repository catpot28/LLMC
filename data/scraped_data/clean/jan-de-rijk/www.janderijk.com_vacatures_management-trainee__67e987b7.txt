Management Traineeship

University of applied sciences / University (HBO / WO)

North Brabant / Limburg region

Are you ambitious, do you have a clear vision and would you like to further develop your leadership skills?

In addition, did you graduate at HBO/WO level almost or at most 2 years ago, in a business economics, commercial or logistics direction? Then come and work at Jan de Rijk Logistics and become our new Management Trainee! In two years we will prepare you for a position at management level in the field that suits you best.

What are you going to do?

In 2 years you will fulfill 4 full functions within different business units in our organization. This includes the following functions; Account Manager, Controller, Engineer, Forwarder, Planner, Project Manager or Supervisor.

In addition to fulfilling an operational role, you will also deal with current issues on a project basis, you will participate in development sessions and you will work together with our managers on strategic cases.

By participating in our traineeship, you receive the ideal combination of learning and gaining practical experience. The traineeship is aimed at developing you both personally and professionally. You become aware of your motivations, qualities and pitfalls. During the traineeship you learn to get the best out of yourself. You think along about innovative ways to optimize our services, so that we always stay one step ahead of developments in the market.

Your profile

You have (almost) graduated at HBO/WO level, in a business economics, commercial or logistics direction. You are driven and have a clear vision. You are analytically strong and know how to quickly create an overview. You do not wait but investigate, ask and do. Of course you have good communication skills in the Dutch and English language.

You are decisive, goal-oriented, stress resistant and have the drive to grow into a great position within our company.

Your Skills:

Achieving high performance;

Make customer successful;

Developing others;

Analyzing and solving problems;

Offer

We offer you an extensive traineeship with a lot of attention for your development with a good salary. Of course you will receive a laptop and telephone from the company. During the traineeship you will be intensively supervised by senior managers and board members. After completing the traineeship, we offer you a job at management level within one of our business units.

Interested?

Are you interested in our Traineeship and do you meet the profile? Send your motivation letter and CV to:
werken@janderijk.com

Application procedure

You will receive a substantive response within two weeks of receiving your application. We make a first selection based on all letters and CVs received. If you are selected from this, you will receive an invitation for a (digital) introduction to the IT director and HR Manager. After this, an in-depth discussion will take place with 2 Business Unit Directors.
