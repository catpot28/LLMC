Employee Container Loading / Unloading

Jan de Rijk Logistics is a logistics service provider active in the field of international transport, warehouse services, Benelux distribution and supply chain solutions. Jan de Rijk Logistics is active in the fields of air freight, automotive, healthcare, retail and high-tech.

With 27 offices in 13 countries and more than 1300 employees in Europe, this family business offers not only challenging and varied tasks, but also modern workplaces in a familiar environment with motivated teams.

Job Description

Getting started as a loader/unloader? Do you enjoy working with colleagues in a fun team? Your work involves loading/unloading trucks and containers by hand. And if there is no supply, you will be assigned to other departments according to your capabilities.

Keep in mind that it is physical hard work.

Who are you?

We invite you to apply for this job opening if the following applies to you:

Hard worker

Physically fit

Able to speak Dutch or English
