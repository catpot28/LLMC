Crossdocking, warehousing and distribution
The logistics facility offers a prime strategic location for efficient distribution throughout Northern Italy. Situated close to major roads and Milan airports, it allows for seamless connectivity to key transportation hubs, minimizing transit times and maximizing efficiency. The facility is also located near the Melzo terminal, which is part of the main GENOVA – LA SPEZIA railway line. This proximity to a major rail link provides excellent options for rail freight, contributing to flexible logistics solutions and smooth integration into larger supply chains.

The building has been constructed with a focus on environmental sustainability. Equipped with energy-efficient systems like heat pumps and solar panels, it aims to lower energy consumption and reduce operating costs. Additionally, the facility includes rainwater recycling systems, which further enhance its eco-friendly profile and support sustainable resource management. This commitment to sustainability not only minimizes the environmental footprint but also supports clients’ green initiatives, making it an ideal choice for companies with a focus on reducing their carbon impact.

Security and accessibility are key strengths of this logistics facility. It is situated in a fenced area with a single controlled entrance, which helps in maintaining a secure perimeter. The facility is monitored round the clock with 24/7 surveillance to ensure a safe working environment and protect both personnel and goods. The combination of controlled access and continuous monitoring means that clients can have peace of mind, knowing that their inventory is being kept in a secure, reliable environment.

Effortless logistics between Italy and the Netherlands

When you’re shipping goods between Italy and the Netherlands, you need reliability, speed, and a team you can trust. That’s exactly what we deliver at Jan de Rijk Logistics. With our daily transport services, we make sure your goods move smoothly, every step of the way.

Imagine your goods travelling seamlessly across borders, passing through our efficient cross-docking centres in Milan and Roosendaal. We know timing is everything, so our Milan warehouse is ready to store and handle your products, ensuring they’re always in the right place at the right time. Whether it’s pallets of fresh produce or general cargo, our specialised team treats each consignment with care and efficiency.

Our transport network is primed to handle the load – securely, quickly, and without a fuss. We focus on dependable solutions that keep your supply chain flowing smoothly, day in and day out.
