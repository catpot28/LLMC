Fueling our commitment to accelerating sustainable and inclusive growth

We bring this ambition to life through our pro bono projects and

Catalyzing our colleagues in their communities
We create opportunities for our colleagues in over 130 cities and 65 countries to transform their passion into meaningful action within their local communities. In 2024, our colleagues supported 4,500 nonprofits worldwide through:

Service:
In addition to pro bono consulting teams, we provide paid volunteer time off for our colleagues to serve their communities, with many regions hosting an annual Day of Service. We also support our colleagues serving on the boards of local nonprofits.

Cash and in-kind donations
: We fund nonprofits selected by colleagues with financial grants provided by our firm. We also match individual colleague donations to organizations that are making a positive impact.

Fellowships:
We offer a paid leave of absence where consultants are embedded in nonprofits instead of working for our firm for six to 12 months, helping deliver key projects that enhance organizations’ performance and impact.

Our impact in action: Generation
In 2024, we celebrated the ten-year anniversary of Generation, the independent global employment nonprofit we founded based on our education-to-employment research.

Since its founding, Generation has helped more than 125,000 learners reskill for careers that would have otherwise been inaccessible, with an 83% job placement rate and $1.5 billion in life-changing wages earned by graduates to date.

We have been proud to provide funding, fellows, and pro bono engagements to enable their impact.
