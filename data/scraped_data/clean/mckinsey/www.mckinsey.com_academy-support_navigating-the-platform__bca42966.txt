Navigating the Platform
Learn how to navigate the various features of the platform.
Understanding the home page and programs

Once you sign in, you will be taken to your home page. From your home page, you can:

View all your assigned programs

Select a program to start and enter program

Understanding your program page

Once you enter a program you will see a list of modules that need to be completed for that program

This page will highlight your progress and help you navigate your learning journey

The modules will contain components with various learning materials and experiences such as:

Digital

Workshops

Surveys

Navigate the modules and subsequent components to complete your learning journey

Navigating content within a digital component

Update to “Digital” component

Your program learning plans include self-guided program digital components, which contain lessons on various topics and interactive content.

Once you launch a digital course, select ‘Start now’ or ‘Continue’ to start moving through content pages.

In the top left, select the menu ☰ to view all pages within a module. Use this menu to navigate to a different page.

Keep the video and anything related to Elucidat her

Navigating a workshop component

If applicable to your program, you will see a workshop component in your journey

A workshop may be virtual or in-person

This component will share the summary, objectives, logistics, and materials related to the workshop

Navigating back to the learning plan

Once you have complete a component, you can navigate back to your module or program page in the following ways:

Using the breadcrumbs at the top of your screen

Using the back arrow on your browser to go back a page

Clicking the “McKinsey” logo at the top left of your screen to navigate back to the home page, where you can re-enter your program page

Accessing your resources from the home page

Find all your resources, including supplemental readings, key takeaways, and more, in one place by selecting “Resource library” from the program or module landing pages.
