Internship Opportunities
Master’s degree internship program
For master’s students with strong ties to Belgium or Luxembourg and an interest in a long-term career with us.

Open to first-year students in two-year master’s programs.

Flexible start dates; minimum duration of eight weeks.

Skills required: Strong academics, analytical and communication skills, extracurricular experience, and commitment to learning.

Languages: Fluency in French or Dutch and an excellent command of English.

Apply here

Business research & analytics internship
