CEO Excellence: what the world's greatest leaders have in common
To identify what the world's best CEOs have in common, McKinsey analyzed 20 years of data and interviewed high-performing leaders. The result is the book “CEO Excellence: the six mindsets of the best leaders”, best seller in The New York Times and The Wall Street Journal. Tracy Francis, Senior Partner at McKinsey in São Paulo, had Vik Malhotra, Senior Partner at McKinsey in New York and one of the authors of the book, to talk about what really makes a great CEO.

McKinsey in Brazil
Our work in Brazil
We are committed to helping our clients develop new strategies, improve organizational performance, increase efficiency in operations, and navigate uncertainties to reach new levels of growth. We serve a broad range of clients across different industries, in the financial sector, telecommunications, public services, basic materials, manufacturing, retail and consumer goods, and many others. McKinsey’s global expertise helps leading organizations in Brazil and the surrounding region solve their most challenging problems.
Digital Capability Center São Paulo
The Digital Capability Center in São Paulo offers immersive learning opportunities that allow participants to capture the value available from tech-enabled operations. Bringing together lean principles and Fourth Industrial Revolution technologies, it delivers a learning experience that demonstrates the impact of successful digital transformations.
Careers in Brazil
At McKinsey Brazil, expect the unexpected. You’ll work alongside leaders of the world’s top 100 companies and numerous emerging start-ups. No two projects will be alike. Each will be meaningful, creative, and original as you work with global teams on clean tech, big data, innovative M&A, digital reinvention, and new paradigms in education and healthcare. Working with McKinsey could be a challenging and rewarding next step.
