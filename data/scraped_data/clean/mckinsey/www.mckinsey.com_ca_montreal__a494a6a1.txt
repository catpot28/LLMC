Established in 1991, we help Québec’s leading companies and institutions develop and implement strategies to reach their full potential.
We are a vibrant, entrepreneurial office in a city that combines the best of North America and Europe. Our leaders are well networked through community board involvement and lead important knowledge efforts on topics such as improving school drop-out rates and retirement savings. Deeply rooted in the community, we have had the privilege of teaming up with dozens of non-profit organizations to help them succeed, grow, and have a lasting impact on our broader community.

Working in Montréal
Our bilingual office is young and dynamic with more than 115 consultants and practice experts serving clients in the basic materials, financial services, manufacturing, retail, and public sectors. The city of Montréal offers the best of all worlds: a cosmopolitan metropolis with the warmth of a small city and vibrant French and English cultures. The office is well-connected to the broader Montréal and Québec communities through its pro bono, public sector work, and involvement with community boards.
