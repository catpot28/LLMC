2026 Inspire

We are thrilled to introduce Inspire 2026, a program co-hosted by our Black, Hispanic and Latino, and Indigenous networks for incoming US MBA students. During the event, attendees will connect with members of our host affinity networks, which are open to all McKinsey colleagues interested in joining. Participants will explore career opportunities in consulting and enhance their problem-solving and leadership skills. Attendees will gain insights into our work, culture, and people, and learn how we aim to foster inclusivity within our firm, for our clients, and in society at large. Through interactive activities, case workshops, panels, and networking events, you will learn about our work, culture, and people, and hear examples of how we strive to make our firm, clients, and society at large more inclusive and equitable.

You’ll get to know peers and McKinsey colleagues during the program and through a series of interview preparation and connectivity sessions afterwards.

Learn more about our commitment to diversity
Event Details

Virtual event: June 26-27, 2026

In person event: July 10-11, 2026 in Chicago

Inspire is an immersive event designed for incoming US MBA students who want to connect with members of the Black, Hispanic and Latino, and/or Indigenous Networks.

Application and selection process

In order to be considered for Inspire you must indicate interest in the program through the
McKinsey Early Access
application by May 11, 2026
(Deadline extended through May 15)
. When completing the application, you are encouraged to also complete the affinity interest form so that we can tailor your experience and engagement with McKinsey based on those interests.

We are looking to get to know incoming MBA students who have been admitted to a full-time MBA program in the US or Canada with an anticipated graduation date of 2028.

Please note: participation is limited to students entering full-time programs only. Dual-degree program students (such as MD/MBA and JD/MBA) are also eligible to apply.

We will begin sending invitations to Inspire in June, though some invitations may be sent later.
