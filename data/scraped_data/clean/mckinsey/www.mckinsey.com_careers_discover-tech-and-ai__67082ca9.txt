Developing future tech leaders
Discover Tech&AI is a two-day in person event that will give you the chance to work on a digital business case study alongside McKinsey consultants. At Discover Tech&AI, you will:

Crack a real-life case—take a behind-the-scenes tour of a digital business case study and learn more about the meaningful work we do

Build on your strengths—work with our consultants and professional coaches to sharpen your problem-solving and interpersonal skills

Get to know us—meet our colleagues from the United Kingdom, and learn about the various career opportunities that McKinsey offers

Event details
Dates:
April 16-17, 2026

The application deadline is
Sunday, March 15, 2026.

We would like to get to know individuals based in the United Kingdom and Ireland who:

Are in their first or penultimate year of undergraduate studies in the fields of science, technology, engineering, or math (STEM)

Have an outstanding record of academic performance and leadership

Enjoy problem solving and creative thinking

Are interested in learning more about a career in consulting
