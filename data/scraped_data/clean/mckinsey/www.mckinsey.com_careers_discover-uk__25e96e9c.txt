Developing Future Leaders
We invite you to Discover, taking place April 16-17, 2026. Discover is a two-day event that will give you the chance to work alongside McKinsey consultants and professional coaches. At Discover, you will:

Crack a real-life case—take a behind-the-scenes tour of a business case study and learn more about the meaningful work we do

Build on your strengths—work with our consultants and professional coaches to sharpen your problem-solving and interpersonal skills

Get to know us—meet our colleagues from the United Kingdom, and learn about the various career opportunities that McKinsey offers

Event Details
Dates

April 16-17, 2026

The application deadline is Sunday, March 22, 2026.

Eligibility
We would like to get to know individuals based in the United Kingdom who:

Are in their first or penultimate year of undergraduate studies

Have an outstanding record of academic performance and leadership

Enjoy problem solving and creative thinking

Are interested in learning more about a career in consulting
