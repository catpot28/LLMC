E-magine your future
Milan, Italy

May 19-21, 2022
Do you have a passion for solving real, challenging, and meaningful problems? Do you want to deepen your operational skills? A career with
McKinsey’s Operations Practice
may be the right place for you.

E-magine your future will take place in Milan from May 19-21, 2022. You will participate in operations-related workshops where you will gain new insights into sustainability and hands-on experience in e-mobility. You will have time to connect with consultants from McKinsey’s Operations Practice in Europe and learn how they help improve clients’ operational processes within the areas of manufacturing, procurement, product development, service operations and supply-chain management.

One of the highlights of the event will be a trip to the
Vairano Ring
, where you will test your talent on a racetrack with the latest e-cars. The Vairano circuit is one of the most important specialist-automotive centers in Europe and features, among other things, new tracks, all of which respect the architectural style of the existing buildings and the landscape of the Lombardy countryside. In parallel, you’ll be given the opportunity to meet varied challenges with trendy, small to medium-sized high-tech electric vehicles, as well.

Participation in all activities is voluntary.
We are no longer accepting applications for this event.

Application details
Eligibility

To be eligible for E-magine your future, you should be based in Europe and meet one of the following requirements:

current students working toward a master’s degree, an MBA, or a PhD, with a focus on engineering or business

working professional with a background in engineering or business and (initial) work experience in operations. For instance, capital excellence, implementation, manufacturing, procurement, product development, quality management, service operations, or supply-chain management

Please note, a business background or previous consulting experience is not required to apply. E-magine your future is an opportunity for you to get to know us and learn more about working in
McKinsey’s Operations Practice
.

How to apply

Your application should include your CV, in English, as well as your transcripts and certificates from secondary school and university with all relevant grades. No cover letter is needed.

We will inform you of the outcome of your application by April 29, 2022.
