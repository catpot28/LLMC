Firm management and delivery essential functions
There are a core set of essential functions that all firm management and delivery colleagues must be able to perform, with or without reasonable accommodations. Where applicable, McKinsey will provide eligible colleagues with reasonable accommodations that enable them to perform an essential function but generally will not eliminate an essential function. The essential functions for all firm management and delivery colleagues are:

Work in-person, in-office (each team’s schedule will vary).

Create and execute complex project plans with clear goals and expected outcomes.

Develop work product-focused materials / outputs, which may include PowerPoint decks, Excel models, articles, or other written deliverables.

Use data, facts, and logical reasoning to make rational decisions against tight timelines.

Digest and comprehend content (e.g., email, articles, spreadsheets) and synthesize into a clear and communicable format (either written or verbally).

There are a core set of essential functions that all McKinsey colleagues are expected to perform. They are:

Organize and initiate tasks independently and see them through to completion.

Communicate effectively in a structured manner, both verbally and in writing, including with colleagues with different perspectives and seniority levels.

Manage time effectively to ensure responsibilities are completed efficiently and all deadlines are met in a complex and largely autonomous work environment.

Use relevant digital productivity tools (e.g., Microsoft Office) and digital communication tools (e.g., email, Slack, Zoom).

Be able to adapt to meet our dynamic business needs that may change or evolve over time.

Give and receive frequent mentoring and targeted feedback to ensure your own growth, achieve project/work objectives, and contribute to team effectiveness.
