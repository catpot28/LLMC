Explore McKinsey Greece
Arachova | March 17-19, 2023
We invite you to apply to Explore McKinsey Greece, taking place in Arachova from March 17-19, 2023.

During this event, you will have the opportunity to do the following:

Crack a real-life case.
Get a behind-the-scenes tour of a real problem we have tackled and learn more about the meaningful work we do.

Build on your strengths.
Work with our consultants to sharpen your problem-solving and project management skills.

Get to know us.
Meet our colleagues from Greece and learn about the various career opportunities that McKinsey offers.

Connect with other participants.
Share your experiences with your peers who come from various backgrounds.

Event details
Location
We look forward to welcoming you to Arachova—known for its modern ski resort and close proximity to Athens.
Application process

Your application should include your CV in English, as well as your transcripts and certificates from university and secondary school (if available), with all relevant grades.

No cover letter is needed.

Digital Assessment
As part of your application, you will be requested to take an
online assessment
that should take approximately one hour to complete. This does not require any preparation or prior business knowledge. More details will be shared shortly after you submit your application.
We would like to get to know students in their final year of studies, recent graduates and young professionals who meet the following criteria:

Have an outstanding record of academic performance and leadership

Enjoy problem solving and creative thinking

Actively seek ways to maximize their own potential and make a difference

Are fluent in Greek and English

Apply by February 20, 2023
