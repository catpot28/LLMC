Welcome to #explore
Discover Operations. Together.
The #explore program gives students and professionals across Europe unparalleled access to
McKinsey’s Operations Practice
and the opportunity to build a network of other passionate future leaders in operations. Through #explore you will discover why leading organizations turn to McKinsey to transform their operations.
Listen to insights from our consultants on their experience with #explore.
Why join #explore
Updates, insights, and news
You’ll receive regular updates, insights, and news about McKinsey’s fast-growing Operations Practice and gain access to exclusive information through our #explore channels, such as our online portal, newsletters, and email announcements.
Invitations to local networking events
At local events, you’ll hear how our consultants face challenges and grow together, supported through mentoring and collaborative relationships.
Invitations to training sessions with McKinsey consultants
You’ll receive invitations for in-person and online training sessions and workshops with McKinsey consultants and recruiters, designed to help you bring your skills to the next level.
Personal guidance on our interview process
Get access to coaching sessions with our recruiting team, who can help with your CV and consultants who will guide you through McKinsey’s interview process
Join #explore
The #explore program is open to undergraduate, graduate, MBA, and PhD students, as well as professionals based in Europe with an interest or experience in Industry 4.0, manufacturing, supply-chain management, product development, procurement, service operations, operations transformations, capital projects, or advanced analytics.
To apply, please complete our brief form. No cover letter is needed.

Attending events of the program is completely voluntary and non-evaluative. The program is a non-binding and informal way of getting to know the Operations Practice.

A refusal to the program will not affect the evaluation of an application to McKinsey in any other context or at a later date.
