Inspiration for Tomorrow – Leadership journey for professionals in Europe
Leadership training

September to October 2022
McKinsey believes in developing exceptional leaders, both at our firm and throughout society.

Our four-week “Inspiration for Tomorrow” program gives experienced professionals in Europe the chance to learn what leadership training and development at McKinsey means.

We invite individuals from all backgrounds who want to take the next step in building their leadership capabilities to apply.
#GetInspirationForTomorrow
What to expect
The program is designed to fit into the schedule of a working professional. While we focus on high-impact training, we also provide flexibility for when you take it. The training sessions can be completed over two-to-three hours each week, and are supplemented with both virtual and in person events as well as personal mentoring from McKinsey colleagues.
Program kick-off
The program will begin on September 7, 2022, with a virtual kick-off to connect you with peers from across Europe. You will hear keynote presentations around the topic of sustainability delivered by senior McKinsey leaders.
Mentoring by McKinsey colleagues
You will be assigned a dedicated mentor who began their McKinsey career as an experienced professional. Your mentor will check in with you regularly to discuss any topics that are top of mind for you.
Self-paced learning and optional check-ins
You will complete the two-to-three hours of leadership training per week at your own pace. You have the option to have a weekly check-in with your peers and McKinsey faculty to discuss the most recent learnings.
Program culmination
The program will conclude with regional in person events on October 1, 2022, where you will connect with other participants. You will also network with peers and McKinsey colleagues, and hear keynote presentations delivered by inspiring speakers.
Why you should apply to the program
Build your leadership capabilities
The program—designed for participants of any background and tailored to the needs of someone with prior work experience—focuses on problem solving, communication, and leadership. After completing the program, you will receive a certificate of attendance and be invited to further networking sessions with McKinsey colleagues and alumni of the program.
Learn about our various opportunities
McKinsey is a diverse firm, comprised of people with different areas of expertise and backgrounds. This program will help you understand how your talent, skills, and experience could be a good fit for our open roles.
Get to know McKinsey
Through our in-person and virtual events, you will hear personal stories from our colleagues and learn why we believe McKinsey is an exciting and rewarding place to work. You will gain a better understanding of the type of work we do at McKinsey and learn more about us as a firm.
The application deadline for Inspiration for Tomorrow 2022 has passed.
Application details
How to apply

To apply, please submit your CV in English. No cover letter is needed.

We will inform you of the outcome of your application by August 9, 2022.

Please note: The program is free of charge and all event-related travel expenses are covered.
Eligibility

We look for creative thinkers and problem solvers from many fields and professions, such as technology, finance, government, engineering, law, the military, and medicine. To be eligible, you should be based in Europe and have an advanced graduate degree such as an MBA, PhD, or the equivalent, which is typically three or more years’ work experience.

If you are an early-stage professional or have extensive work experience, this program will help you advance your leadership skills.
