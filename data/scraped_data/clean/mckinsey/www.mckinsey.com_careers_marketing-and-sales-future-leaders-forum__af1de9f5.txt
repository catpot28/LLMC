Marketing & Sales Future Leaders Forum
October 6, 2022

Virtual

6:00pm-8:30pm ET
McKinsey passionately believes in developing outstanding leaders and promoting diversity, equity, and inclusion, both at our firm and in the world at large.
Diversity, equity, and inclusion are embedded into the fabric of our firm and everything we do. We invite you to join our Marketing & Sales Practice for a virtual symposium where you will learn about the work we do and hear from members of our firm's affinity networks. Our colleagues will share unique interactive impact stories that bring our practice's work to life, and will also describe their personal experiences with diversity, equity, and inclusion at McKinsey. We hope you will engage with us and each other as we look to identify the future leaders at our firm!
