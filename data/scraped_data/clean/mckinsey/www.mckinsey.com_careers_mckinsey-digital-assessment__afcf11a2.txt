Solve, McKinsey’s assessment game
One of the exciting steps in our consultant recruitment process is Solve, a gamified assessment created to showcase your problem-solving abilities. There’s no need to prepare—approach it with curiosity and creativity. Your performance on Solve will be considered in conjunction with the rest of your application and any other assessments you are asked to take.
Learn more about Solve and see what it will feel like to play
Values and integrity
At McKinsey, we are committed to providing a fair and objective assessment experience for every candidate. To ensure we can uphold this commitment, except as an accommodation for a disability approved in writing by McKinsey, you are not permitted to:

Use applications, websites, AI tools, or pre-prepared notes

Record or capture screenshots of any part of the assessment

Additionally, we expect that you participate in this assessment alone, without assistance from others. We consider failure to adhere to these rules as a violation of our values, which may result in exclusion from consideration, rescinding of an offer, restrictions on future applications, or legal action. Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
