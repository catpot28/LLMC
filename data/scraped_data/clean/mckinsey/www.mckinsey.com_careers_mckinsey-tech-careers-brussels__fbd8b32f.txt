McKinsey Tech Careers: Brussels
July 2, 2020
Virtual Event

Do you want to meet the tech teams who
help some of the world’s most innovative and influential organizations perform at their best? Join us for the McKinsey Tech Careers: Brussels virtual event and engage with a data scientist, product engineer, delivery manager, data engineer and digital consultant who will share their story and explain how our multidisciplinary client service teams make lasting impact.
Event details
Date

Thursday, July 2, 2020 from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m. CET

Eligibility

We invite you to apply if you meet the following requirements:

hold an undergraduate degree and have an outstanding record of academic or professional achievement

have 2-6 years of technical work experience such as software or data engineering, product management, delivery or data science

authorization to work in the EU

How to apply

To express your interest, please submit your CV in English. No cover letter is needed.

Applications due by July 1, 2020 (by 11:59 PM CET)

McKinsey Tech Careers: Brussels is a non-evaluative event. All event applications will be reviewed regardless of the result of any previous applications to McKinsey roles or events.
