PROGRAM DETAILS
Date

3-4 April 2020

Location

Amsterdam

Application process

To express your interest, please submit your CV, in English,

by 8 March 2020.

Your CV should include details of your education, work experience, and any extracurricular activities and achievements.
At the event, you will:

build on your strengths—work with our consultants in a series of workshops to sharpen your problem-solving skills

present with impact—learn how to present your findings in a convincing way

get to know us—meet our LGBTQ+ colleagues and allies, and learn about the various career opportunities that McKinsey offers

Eligibility
Who is eligible?

We would like to get to know students and professionals with up to eight years of experience who:

identify as LGBTQ+ or a LGBTQ+ ally

Study or live in one of the BeNeLux/Nordic countries

or

Speak at least one of the following languages: Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French, Norwegian or Swedish

McKinsey will cover all event related expenses, including travel and accommodation, for individuals selected to attend. Please note that we are only able to cover the cost of travel from within Europe.

Proud Leaders is a nonevaluative event. It is an opportunity for you to meet us and learn more about working at McKinsey, build your problem-solving and communications skills, and network with peers. All event applications will be reviewed regardless of the result of any previous applications to McKinsey roles or events.
Applications are now closed.
