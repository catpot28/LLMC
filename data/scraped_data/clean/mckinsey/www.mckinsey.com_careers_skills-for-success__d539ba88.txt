Skills for Success - Europe
November 4–18, 2025

Before your next bold move, comes the smart start.

Skills for Success is a series of virtual events designed to equip you with skills you can use in any career. Learn how to craft a standout CV, solve problems with structure, and tell stories that stick, all while getting to know McKinsey. Join us to build the skills that set you up for long-term success.
At McKinsey, we know that a diverse meritocracy enriches creativity, innovation, and problem-solving abilities. We are passionate about progressing representation in social mobility and welcome individuals from a wide range of backgrounds to join us.

If you are currently pursuing any degree in Europe, or are a recently graduated professional, join us for Skills for Success! It is a series of four informal virtual skill-building sessions and has been designed for individuals with diverse socioeconomic backgrounds.* You will have the chance to learn more about McKinsey, hear from members of our
Prism network
(Progressing representation and inclusion in social mobility), as well as learning skills to help you on your career journey.

To sign up for any of these sessions you will be asked to submit your CV. This is for tracking purposes only. Please don’t worry if your CV is not job-ready as this program is separate to a job application! You will learn these skills as part of the series and will always be able to update this at a later stage. You only need to apply once and this will ensure you get information on all the sessions in the series. We encourage you to participate in all the sessions, however if you cannot attend them all please do not worry about missing any. You can apply at any point during the series to receive information on the upcoming sessions. To sign up for the full program please apply by November 2, 2025. To join the program later in the series, please apply by the final deadline of November 16, 2025.

*This may encompass a variety of experiences, including but not limited to being the first generation in your family to attend university, having received a scholarship based on financial needs or demographics, or growing up in a low-income household. Skills for Success is just one of our many worldwide initiatives aimed at helping individuals get to know McKinsey better.
