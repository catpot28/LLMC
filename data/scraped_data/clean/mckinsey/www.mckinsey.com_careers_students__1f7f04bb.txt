Why
McKinsey
Exceptional, curious and driven people tend to have a lot of choices. Here are some of the most common reasons why our colleagues and alumni say they chose a career at McKinsey.
The world’s top leadership factory
Ranked #1 in TIME’s “top company for future leaders” list in 2024 and 2025. Whether you stay a few years or decades, you’ll leave with a credential that opens doors and the skills and experience to lead at the highest level.
Learn more
The broadest and deepest exposure of any firm
86% of the Forbes Global 500 turn to us when the stakes are highest. From day one, our consultants work with senior leaders like boards and CxOs, shaping decisions that transform industries and economies.
Collaboration, not competition
Since our founding nearly 100 years ago, we’ve operated as one global profit pool. When one client or colleague succeeds, we all do—so each of us is incentivized to bring our best expertise across our closely-connected firm. As a global Partnership by design, collaboration—not competition—is the cornerstone of our culture.

Benefits that go

Performance at this level calls for more than standard benefits. We provide the support you need to be at your best—from healthcare, retirement plans, and parental leave to on-demand wellness resources.

But our definition of “benefits” is broader. It also includes mentorship and development to help you thrive at McKinsey here, plus a sense of belonging in a lifelong global network that stays with you long after you leave. It’s a package built to support you now and set you up for success that lasts a lifetime.
