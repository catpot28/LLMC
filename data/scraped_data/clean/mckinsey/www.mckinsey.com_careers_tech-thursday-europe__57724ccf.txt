Registration details
To be eligible, you must be a student (at undergraduate, Master's, or PhD level), a recent graduate, or an experienced professional in the field of science, technology, engineering, or math.

The series is open to individuals studying or working in Europe, regardless of nationality.
We recognize that each attendee’s circumstances may be different and we want to ensure your privacy is considered. We encourage you to do what feels most comfortable during these sessions, including joining without your camera turned on and/or changing your name as it appears on the Zoom screen upon joining.
