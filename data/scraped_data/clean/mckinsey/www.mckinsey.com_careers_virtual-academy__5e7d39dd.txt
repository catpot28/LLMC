Virtual Academy
June – July 2020
Develop your skills from home
Are you a student or early career professional* in Europe and want to further develop your problem-solving and analytical skills? Are you excited to learn about digital topics or design thinking? Do you want a chance to win an Apple Watch?

*To be eligible, you must be based in Europe and a current (non-MBA or business) Masters student or PhD student filling a postdoctoral post. We also invite early career professionals, with (non-MBA or business) Masters degrees or PhDs to apply.
What you can expect
Our Virtual Academy combines modules from one of our capability building programmes,
McKinsey Academy
which was designed for our C-suite clients, as well as virtual skills workshops and a team challenge.
Most of the Virtual Academy modules are self-paced so participants can complete them at a time suitable for them. During the programme, participants will be paired with a mentor for career advice and a glimpse into life at McKinsey. At the conclusion of the Virtual Academy, there will be an online team challenge, which will help participants connect with others in the programme.
Applications are closed.

Participants will collect points for completing each module
Want to unlock the McKinsey Academy online learning modules?

To progress to the next stage, you will need to collect a certain number of points. You will have a chance to unlock the online learning (stage 3) upon completing stages 1 and 2.

Want to win an Apple Watch?

The 5 participants who complete all designated modules and have the most points will win an Apple watch.
