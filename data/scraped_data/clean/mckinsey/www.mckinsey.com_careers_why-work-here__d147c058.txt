What
we offer
Working here gives you the skills, network, and experiences that change your career trajectory and shape future opportunities, earnings, and impact.
Unrivaled alumni network
With 60,000+ alumni globally, including thousands in senior leadership roles, the McKinsey community extends well beyond your time at our firm. It’s a lifelong network.
Leadership factory
McKinsey alumni have founded dozens of unicorns and lead more Fortune 500 companies than any other source. For C-suite or entrepreneurial ambitions, you’re in the right place.
Credential for life
A McKinsey tenure opens doors because it is recognized globally as proof you can solve complex problems, influence senior leaders, and deliver impact.
Mentorship
From day one, you work side-by-side with senior colleagues who provide continuous feedback and take personal responsibility for your development.
Learning and development
We invest $200M+ annually in learning, offer ≈4,400 programs, and ensure 100% of colleagues receive regular feedback and career reviews.
Hallmark of collaboration
Our single global profit pool brings the best expertise to every client and creates broader, more diverse opportunities for our colleagues.
