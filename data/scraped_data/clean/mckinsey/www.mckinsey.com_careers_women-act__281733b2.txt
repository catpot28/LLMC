Women Act
Creating sustainability in operations

Copenhagen, Denmark, March 9-11, 2023
We invite you apply to Women Act—creating sustainability in operations—taking place in Copenhagen from
March 9-11, 2023
.

What to expect

As a Women Act participant, you will gain insights into the various types of sustainability projects that our Operations Practice consultants in Europe work on.

During the event, you will have the opportunity to work on an interactive business case linked to a sustainability topic in operations. There will also be ample time to connect with McKinsey colleagues as well as like-minded women from across Europe in a relaxed setting.

On the second day of our program, we will visit
CopenHill
, a waste-to-energy power plant built in 2017 in Copenhagen's industrial area that was received the
World Building of the Year 2021
award in the category Production, Energy and Recycling at the World Architecture Festival. After a guided plant tour, we will explore the rooftop slope where you will have the option to test your athletic talent on skis. This activity is suitable for beginners and no experience is required. For those who wish to opt out, you can simply enjoy the impressive view of Copenhagen at a height of 85 meters.

Eligibility

We are looking for women based in Europe, who meet at least one of the following requirements:

Professionals who have a background in engineering or business and some work experience in operations—for instance, manufacturing, supply-chain management, product development, procurement, service operations and capital excellence

Students working toward a master’s degree, an MBA, or a PhD, with a focus on engineering or business

How to apply

Your application should include your CV in English, as well as your transcripts and certificates from secondary school and university, with all relevant grades.

No cover letter is needed.

We will inform you of the outcome of your application by February 17, 2023.
