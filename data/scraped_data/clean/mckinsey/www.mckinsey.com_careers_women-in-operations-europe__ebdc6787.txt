Let’s Meet – Women in Operations
Are you an experienced professional with a background in Operations and looking for new challenges and areas to grow in? Join us for one of our in-person events across Europe to learn more about what we do and what a career as an Operations consultant looks like.
Event details
Let’s Meet – Women in Operations is a series of on-site, networking events for experienced professional women who want to connect and explore careers with McKinsey Operations across different locations in Europe. Come and join us to:

Listen to our consultants
as they share their professional and personal experiences.

Gain
insights
into our current projects in manufacturing, procurement, product development, supply chain management, capital excellence, and many other fields.

Learn how we help our clients
transform organizations
to increase efficiency, productivity, agility, and competitiveness.

Get to know, network, and engage
with our McKinsey Operations consultants and other like-minded women.

How to apply
Submit a CV in English by April 21, 2024. Please include your transcripts and certificates from secondary school and university with all relevant grades. No cover letter is needed. We will inform you of the outcome of your application on May 2, 2024.
Eligibility
Eligible are all women, who are working towards a master’s degree, PhD or MBA and have a background or interest in
Operations
(product development, procurement, service operations, capital excellence, manufacturing or supply chain).

Our Women in Operations events are intended for women and are fully transgender and nonbinary inclusive.

aimed at helping candidates get to know McKinsey better. Visit our
