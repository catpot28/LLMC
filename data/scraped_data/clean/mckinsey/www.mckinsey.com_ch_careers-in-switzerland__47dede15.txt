Careers in Switzerland

McKinsey Switzerland offers opportunities for learning, support, and growth, as well as a dynamic, collaborative community with a mix of languages and nationalities.

Experience the energy of our Zurich office, home to over 400 colleagues, or the close-knit network of our Geneva office with 90+ members. Join a team that helps you reach your full potential and bonds through
our affinity groups
, sports initiatives, and other local events.
Explore careers in Operations
Join McKinsey’s Operations Excellence Program (OEP)—a 24 months journey for professionals with up to two years of experience to build expertise in areas like Manufacturing, Supply Chain, and Product Development. Based in Zurich, you’ll receive tailored training, mentorship, and global exposure through our network of 7,500 consultants and 1,300 experts.
