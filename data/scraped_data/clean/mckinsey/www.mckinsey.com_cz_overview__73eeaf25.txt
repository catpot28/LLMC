McKinsey Czech Republic
In our Prague office, we combine local insight with global expertise to empower clients to grow and lead.
Embedded in the Czech Republic and Slovakia for over 30 years, our Prague office bridges deep local insight with the global expertise of a world-leading firm. We partner with top leadership on their most pressing challenges, from large-scale transformations to bold business building, with deep expertise in sectors like finance, energy, telecommunications, consumer, and private equity. It’s all made possible by our people: a diverse team defined by a collaborative spirit, a commitment to excellence, and the integrity to deliver lasting impact for our clients and communities.
