Welcome to
McKinsey Dominican Republic
We help companies throughout the Caribbean and Central America accelerate sustainable and inclusive growth
Since 2020, our Dominican Republic office has supported clients in their journeys to increase their competitive value and generate new business opportunities. Our teams integrate interdisciplinary knowledge with top-grade technology to achieve holistic transformations and accelerate innovation. We focus on ensuring the greatest impact while contributing to the future progress and growth of the country.

The Dominican Republic has one of the largest and most dynamic economies in Latin America, thanks to its culturally diverse population. We are passionate about tapping into this pool of talent and developing leaders within the region.

Our region
Hispanoamérica en potencia
Spanish Latin America is one of the most versatile emerging regions on the planet. Dynamic and diverse, with young talent, abundant natural wealth, and entrepreneurial vitality. In this context, the global scenario presents today a unique opportunity to close historic gaps through sustainable and inclusive growth. Learn more about Hispanoamerica en Potencia in our website (in Spanish).
