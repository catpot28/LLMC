Agents, Robots, and Us: What executives need to know about AI and work
AI is no longer just a technology investment—it's a strategic force reshaping how work gets done, how organizations create value, and how leaders think about talent and productivity. According to new research from the McKinsey Global Institute, more than half of today's work hours could be automated using existing and emerging technologies, meaning that people, AI agents, and robots will increasingly collaborate side by side.

Join McKinsey senior partners
Alexis Krivkovich
and
