Welcome to
McKinsey Finland
We partner with Finland’s leading companies, multinationals, scale-ups, and the public sector, to help them
accelerate sustainable and inclusive growth
.
Founded in 1988, our Helsinki office was established to support Finland’s evolving business landscape and drive long-term growth. For over 35 years, we’ve combined global expertise with deep local insights to help clients solve complex challenges and build lasting resilience.

With 250+ alumni now leading in business, entrepreneurship, and the public sector, our impact extends well beyond our client work. As part of a global firm with 140+ locations, we bring the best of McKinsey’s global capabilities to every engagement.

How can we help you?
From strategy and transformation to digital and operational excellence, we bring deep expertise and capabilities across industries to help you tackle your most pressing challenges.

Whether you’re navigating growth, leading change, or seeking new opportunities, our experts are ready to help you. Explore our team to find the right expertise for direct contact or reach out generically to start the conversation:
helsinki_contactus@mckinsey.com
