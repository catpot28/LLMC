My profile/User Registration
I forgot my McKinsey.com password. How do I reset it?

If you don't remember your password, please follow the instructions on the
Forgot Your Password?
page.

If your Internet provider or email application filters incoming email, please add McKinsey.com to your list of approved senders to ensure that you receive our reset password email.

I tried to reset my password but didn't receive a reset email.

Unfortunately, spam blockers sometimes prevent our emails from getting through. If your Internet provider or email application filters incoming email, please add McKinsey.com to your list of approved senders to ensure that you receive our reset password email. After doing so, you may need to go through the reset process again.

Why should I register?

Most articles, reports, and other material on McKinsey.com are available without registration. However, in most cases we ask you to register or log in to our site before downloading or printing our content.

As a registered member of McKinsey.com, you can also sign up for our regular email newsletters and receive alerts when new content is available.

Your registration credentials will work on the McKinsey Insights app too, offering mobile access to our content and additional personalization options.

To take advantage of these services,
please register now
.

When I tried to create an account, I received an error message stating that my email address is already in use.

Our system requires a unique email address for each member profile. You are receiving this error message because the email address you entered is already associated with a profile, most likely one you set up previously.

If you don't remember your password, please follow the instructions on the
Forgot Your Password?
page.

If your Internet provider or email application filters incoming email, please add McKinsey.com to your list approved senders to ensure that you receive our reset password email.

How do I update my profile information?

To update your profile information, please
log in to Mckinsey.com
, click on your name in the upper-right corner of any page, then select
Edit profile
from the drop-down menu.

If you receive print copies of
McKinsey Quarterly
directly from a McKinsey partner, please send your address updates to
mckinsey_quarterly@mckinsey.com
.

If you are a McKinsey alumnus, please update your information at the McKinsey
Alumni Center
or email
mckinsey_alumni_relations@mckinsey.com
.

How do I sign up for email newsletters and alerts?

Our newsletters and alerts keep McKinsey.com members informed about the latest content from McKinsey. If you would like to take advantage of this service,
please register
and select the
email newsletters and alerts
you would like to receive.

How do I unsubscribe from email newsletters and alerts?

To change your email preferences, please
log in to McKinsey.com
and select
Edit subscriptions
from the drop-down menu that appears under your name in the upper-right corner of any page.

How is my personal information protected?

McKinsey is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal-identification information. Please see our
privacy policy
for more details.

I still can't resolve my registration issue. Can I contact McKinsey?

Please email questions and concerns regarding registration to
My member account
.
