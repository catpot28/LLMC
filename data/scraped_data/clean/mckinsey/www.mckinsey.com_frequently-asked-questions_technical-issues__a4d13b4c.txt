Technical Issues
How do I report a technical issue related to Mckinsey.com?

To report a technical issue with mckinsey.com, please
contact us
. We appreciate your feedback.

Why haven’t I received any emails regarding McKinsey publications for a while?

We are sorry you have not been receiving our emails. This may be the result of spam filter settings on your computer or company network. Please contact
My member account services
and we will attempt to identify the cause.

If your Internet provider or email application filters incoming email, please add McKinsey.com to your list of approved senders to ensure that you receive our email newsletters and alerts.

Why can't I download a PDF?

Please note that only registered users can download PDFs. In order to view PDF files, you must have Adobe Reader installed on your computer. To obtain a copy of this software, please visit the Adobe website.

I am having technical issues submitting my online application.

For questions or issues related to your online application submission, please visit
McKinsey’s online application FAQs
for guidance.
