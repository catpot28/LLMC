Welcome to
McKinsey Greece
Our Athens office has an outward perspective, impact beyond its size, and a focus on rigorous thinking.
Inspired by Greece’s history of achievement, we foster a culture of client service, innovation, and learning. We serve two-thirds of the top companies in Greece’s most important industries—including financial services, telecommunications, and energy—helping them improve their performance and address their most serious challenges.
Our consultants are talented and service-oriented professionals, innovative and pragmatic problem solvers, and adept at managing challenging situations. We foster a caring meritocracy and a close-knit environment that supports all of our people in reaching their full potential, and we take pride in supporting the advancement of women leaders in Greece.

Working in Greece
Our Athens office offers unparalleled opportunities for our people to grow. Our consultants work in teams that foster apprenticeship, and they interact with senior clients from their very first days at McKinsey. They are able to experience a broad range of sectors, functions and geographies, allowing them to bring valuable perspectives and insights to their clients.
