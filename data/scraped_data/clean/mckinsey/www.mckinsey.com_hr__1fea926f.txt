Welcome to
McKinsey Croatia
We help leading companies in Croatia and the surrounding region to unlock growth, build leaders, and transform the economy.
Since opening our first office in Southeast Europe—in Zagreb, early 2003—McKinsey Croatia has led our growth in the region, paving the way for new offices and projects. Today, our expanding office is a trusted partner of companies and governmental organizations throughout Southeast and Central Europe, and we partner with both local and international companies in telecommunications, financial services, oil and gas, consumer products, and utilities—as well as public sector organizations. We are passionate about developing leaders and attracting the brightest talent. Our alumni serve in leadership roles across the region’s major companies and institutions.
