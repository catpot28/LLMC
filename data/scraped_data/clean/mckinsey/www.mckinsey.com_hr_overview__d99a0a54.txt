Welcome to
McKinsey Croatia
We help leading companies in Croatia and the surrounding region to unlock growth, build leaders, and transform the economy.
Since opening our first office in Southeast Europe—in Zagreb, early 2003—McKinsey Croatia has led our growth in the region, paving the way for new offices and projects. Today, our expanding office is a trusted partner of companies and governmental organizations throughout Southeast and Central Europe, and we partner with both local and international companies in telecommunications, financial services, oil and gas, consumer products, and utilities—as well as public sector organizations. Working in Croatia
A career at McKinsey Croatia is an opportunity to work with remarkable people while constantly learning and growing. Our consultants help shape the growth of leading companies and solve major social challenges—both in Southeast Europe, and globally. We are always looking for talented people with diverse backgrounds who are passionate about solving some of the most pressing business and social issues. browse open positions with McKinsey Croatia
