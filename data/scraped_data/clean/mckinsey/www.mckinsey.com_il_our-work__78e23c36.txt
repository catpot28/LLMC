Our Work in Israel
Bringing the best of our global firm
We combine McKinsey’s global expertise with deep local insights to help our clients accelerate sustainable and inclusive growth.

McKinsey works across all major sectors of Israel’s economy—from financial services to digital, and with several of the country’s entrepreneurial firms. We help our clients shape bold growth strategies, drive operational improvement, and reconfigure organizations for high performance. Working with key companies in Israel’s world-leading technology hub, we lead the development of McKinsey’s global service line on rapid process digitization and automation.
