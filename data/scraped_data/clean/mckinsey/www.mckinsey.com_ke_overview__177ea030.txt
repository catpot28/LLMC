Welcome to
McKinsey Kenya
We combine global expertise with deep local insights to help
accelerate sustainable and inclusive growth
in Kenya.
Since opening our Kenya office in 2014, we have led with a local mindset, forging close ties with the community and developing a deep understanding of the business landscape within Kenya and across the East Africa region.

We serve clients in nearly every sector and industry, leveraging our global expertise to help large enterprises, government ministries and organizations to create long-term impactful change. We believe in supporting local talent and empowering business leaders to develop strategies that maximize the many opportunities that support
sustainable and inclusive growth
in East Africa.
