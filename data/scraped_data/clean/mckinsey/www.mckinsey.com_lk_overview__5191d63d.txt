Welcome to

Sri Lanka
As the first global management consulting firm to open an office in Sri Lanka, we are proud to partner with private- and public-sector organisations to
accelerate sustainable and inclusive growth
and promote prosperity in a culturally diverse country.
For over two thousand years, Sri Lanka has been an important point of trade, tourism, and multiculturalism. Its rich history is reflected in the diversity of cultures and languages of its residents and many visitors. We are inspired by Sri Lanka’s past and are proud to partner with its leaders to help transform organizations, build new businesses, and strengthen institutions. Our commitment is to help our clients in Sri Lanka compete and win, and build the capabilities of people, communities, and society at large in the country.

Impact on Society
Core to our values is a commitment to help achieve meaningful social impact.

One key priority is the development of future leaders. In Sri Lanka, we are passionate about supporting high-potential local students and helping them become the next generation of leaders of Sri Lanka. In 2023, we launched Young Leaders Sri Lanka (YLSL), a 9-month capability-building program. It includes training workshops focused on a range of topics, co-leading a social impact project, networking events with Sri Lankan and international industry leaders, and personal mentoring.

For our inaugural cohort, 15 exceptional students were handpicked from a competitive pool of more than 150 applicants, embodying the promise and potential of Sri Lanka’s brightest talent.
