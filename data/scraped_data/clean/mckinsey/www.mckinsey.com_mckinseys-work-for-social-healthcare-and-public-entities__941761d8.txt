Revitalizing Ohio’s economy
$54.8 billion in new capital investment attraction between 2013 and 2019

For over a decade, McKinsey has supported JobsOhio’s economic growth plan to attract investments in the state and reskill and upskill talent. Between 2013 and 2017, we helped Ohio add $30 billion in payroll and outpace 25 other states in growth. During the COVID-19 pandemic, we worked with the state to launch Ohio to Work, a reskilling and upskilling program that has attracted new investments and created new job opportunities for Ohioans, serving approximately 4,000 jobseekers to date.
Read more about our work with JobsOhio
Committing to carbon removal technologies
$925 million commitment to support the development of carbon removal technology

McKinsey has collaborated with Alphabet, Shopify, Meta, and Stripe to launch Frontier, an eight-year, $925 million commitment to accelerate the development of permanent carbon removal technologies by guaranteeing future demand. The firm provides pro bono advisory support to carbon removal start-ups and businesses taking part in the initiative. By joining as a founding member of Frontier, we are committing to help rid the atmosphere and ocean of gigatons of existing CO
2
, through further expansion of the global supply of carbon removal.
Read more about our commitment to carbon removal technology
Eradicating polio in Nigeria
No new polio cases since 2016

In collaboration with Nigeria’s Ministry of Health and the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, McKinsey designed and deployed a set of emergency operations centers (EOCs) to combat the country’s polio endemic. We developed the EOCs’ organizational structure, trained staff to collect and analyze data, and worked with national leaders to improve the overall vaccination campaign. Thanks to these efforts, Nigeria has successfully eradicated polio and significantly increased polio immunity among its population.
Read more about our work to eradicate polio in Nigeria
Building a future-ready workforce in Australia
20 million Australian dollars forecast over three years from increased productivity and talent retention

Working with the Department of Regional New South Wales (DRNSW), an Australian government agency supporting rural communities across the state, McKinsey has shown what’s possible in building new workforce capabilities—and what scale could look like. Over 12 months, DRNSW and McKinsey built an enterprise Digital Skills Program, preparing workers to thrive in the skills-based economy. Results exceeded expectations in record time, with over 90 percent employee satisfaction and 85 percent of participants reporting improved applied skills.
Read more about our work with the DRNSW
Made in Africa: Catalyzing stronger, sustainable, and inclusive economies
Attracted more than $850 million in foreign-direct investment creating more than 16,000 jobs

In 2019, the UK’s Foreign Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO) worked with McKinsey to implement a flagship economic development program, Manufacturing Africa. The aim of the program - which is funded with UK aid from the UK government and includes partners from BDO, Steward Redqueen, Reformatics, and TechnoServe - is to attract £1.2 billion in foreign direct investment and create 90,000 jobs across the continent by 2026. Twenty-eight manufacturing deals have already been closed through Manufacturing Africa, which has attracted more than $850 million in foreign-direct investment, creating more than 16,000 jobs.
Read more about our work with Manufacturing Africa
