Next Generation Women Leaders (NGWL) is a three-day, in-person experience for women who are ready to become the leaders shaping the future.

This program will equip you with the confidence, skills, and connections to grow alongside other ambitious women.

Next Generation Women Leaders is a three-day, in-person experience for driven women ready to grow as leaders and shape what’s next, alongside peers and trailblazing women. Hear it directly from former participants.

What you’ll do
Day 1: Leading Self
Arrive, connect, and set your intentions for your journey. Day 1 is all about discovering your leadership potential and building meaningful connections with your peers. Hear from inspiring keynote speakers, engage in dynamic panel discussions, and celebrate the start of this transformative experience.
Day 2: Leading Together
Step up your leadership skills through collaboration and hands-on learning. On Day 2, you’ll embark on a leadership quest, working in small groups to tackle challenges and build practical skills as a team. In the afternoon, you’ll join our brave spaces—small-group dialogue sessions where diverse voices and perspectives come together. These conversations encourage openness, curiosity, and courage, creating room to explore the topics that often go unspoken and spark deeper connection and growth.
Day 3: Leading Forward
Shape the future of leadership. On our final day together, you’ll co-create a leadership manifesto with your peers—a shared vision of what female leadership means and how you’ll bring it to life to create lasting impact. Reflect on the skills, values, and insights gained over the past few days, and leave feeling inspired by a closing keynote. The event concludes with a farewell lunch, sending you off ready to make your mark on the world.
How to apply
To be considered for NGWL Europe, you must:

Be based in or have strong ties to Europe.

Be a bachelor, master’s, PhD, or MBA student graduating no later than 2027 OR a professional with up to eight years of work experience.

To apply, please submit the following by
February 23, 2026
:

Resume

Your preferred
office location
(based on geographic or industry ties) OR

When you apply, you will choose up to three office locations or business functions that will review your application (see FAQ for the list of participating offices/practices). We advise you to make your choices based on geographic or industry ties (e.g., you have studied or lived in the country), or you have work experience in the industry. You should also be fluent in the local language.
