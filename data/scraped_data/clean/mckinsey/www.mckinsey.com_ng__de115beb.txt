Welcome to
McKinsey Nigeria
We are helping Nigeria accelerate
sustainable and inclusive growth
, develop leaders, and strengthen its institutions.
With over 200 million people, Nigeria is Africa’s most populous country. We believe that for all people to prosper, economies must grow. This is why McKinsey has played an active part in Nigeria’s growth story since 2002, combining bold strategies and transformative technologies to help organizations accelerate
sustainable and inclusive growth
.

Every day, our team of highly qualified consultants has the opportunity to apply their talents to complex, significant challenges and engage with leading thinkers on the most pressing issues facing our clients and society. Our work is founded on a rigorous understanding of Nigeria’s institutional context, sector dynamics, and macroeconomic environment. No matter the challenge, we focus on delivering practical and enduring results and equipping our public- and private-sector clients to grow and lead.
