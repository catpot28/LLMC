Netherlands Advanced
Since our beginnings in Amsterdam in 1964, McKinsey & Company has significantly impacted the economic and societal landscape of the Netherlands through the work we do with our clients. For our anniversary celebrations, we traditionally reveal insights on a topic that we feel is crucial for the country's development. Therefore, we are excited to present our Netherlands Advanced: Building a future labor market that works report.
