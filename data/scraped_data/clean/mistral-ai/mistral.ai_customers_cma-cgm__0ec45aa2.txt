CMA CGM and Mistral.

Transportation and Logistics

April 24, 2026

CMA CGM streamlines global maritime operations with Mistral.

Back to Customers

1 min read

CMA CGM is a global leader in shipping and logistics, with more than 155,000 employees across over 160 countries. Mistral is working with CMA CGM across its key domains—shipping, logistics, and media—to deploy AI-powered use cases that streamline operations and enhance productivity. These include assisting with cargo release, fact-checking content, addressing client requests for information, and processing shipping documents. Together, Mistral and CMA CGM are building AI tools that support faster, higher-quality responses and improve customer-facing processes, helping CMA CGM deliver better service to its customers.
