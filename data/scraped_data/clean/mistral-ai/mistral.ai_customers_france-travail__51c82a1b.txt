France Travail empowers job seekers with AI.

April 24, 2026

Empowering job seekers with AI.

Back to Customers

0 min read

France Travail is leading the way in assisting job seekers in finding suitable employment opportunities with the help of Mistral models. For instance, France Travail agents utilize Large Language Models (LLMs) to access job seekers’ data. This enables them to summarize job seekers’ histories, profiles, and areas of interest. Additionally, they leverage AI through an internal RAG-based chatbot that helps agents better access and understand labor laws and allocation policies, ensuring compliance and efficiency.
