GoJob enhances recruitment capabilities with Mistral.

Technology and Software

April 24, 2026

Enhancing productivity with Mistral.

Back to Customers

0 min read

Gojob, a leading French HR-tech platform specializing in temporary worker staffing, operates in Europe and North America. With over 2 million applications processed annually and approximately 60,000 recruitments completed, Gojob is a pioneer in AI-driven recruitment. In their quest to enhance productivity, Gojob leveraged Mistral to fully automate the candidate screening process. Gojob integrated Mistral Large into their recruitment platform, utilizing a GenAI recruiting assistant to streamline the hiring process. The assistant conducts real-time conversations with potential candidates, facilitating pre-qualification and automating the initial screening. By integrating Mistral Large on Azure, Gojob has significantly enhanced its recruitment capabilities, providing a more efficient and effective way to match candidates with job opportunities.
