Developing next-generation AI systems for European defense.

Back to Customers

0 min read

Helsing and Mistral have formed a strategic partnership to develop next-generation AI systems for European defense. This collaboration, announced at the Paris Global AI Summit, combines Helsing's expertise in AI-powered military technology with Mistral's advanced generative models. The partnership focuses on Vision-Language-Action models, enhancing defence platforms' ability to understand their environment, communicate with operators, and make rapid, reliable decisions in complex scenarios. This initiative aims to strengthen Europe's technological leadership in AI and defence, fostering human-AI collaboration on the battlefield and revolutionizing defence operations.
