Mirakl, the world leader in SaaS marketplace management, has transformed its catalog onboarding process by integrating Mistral’s models and solutions into their “Catalog Transformer”—an automatic system for sellers to onboard products on marketplaces. Handling around 10 million new products each month, Mirakl is leveraging both Mistral’s open-source models and their platform’s API for large language models (LLMs). This integration not only improved operational efficiency but also maximized catalog performance, ensuring an optimal user experience.
