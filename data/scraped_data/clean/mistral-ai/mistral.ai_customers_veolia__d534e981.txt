Veolia is enhancing operational efficiency and transparency with AI.

Energy

Enhancing operational efficiency and transparency with Mistral.

Back to Customers

0 min read

Veolia, a global leader in ecological transformation, aims at revolutionizing the management and monitoring of industrial sites for water, waste, and energy. With thousands of facilities worldwide, Veolia seeks to enhance operational efficiency and transparency through advanced technology. By integrating Mistral’s cutting-edge foundational models with their data and knowledge base, Veolia enables the group's 218,000 employees with a revolutionary new approach to co-pilot water, waste and energy plants through interactive discussions. This partnership significantly improves access to knowledge, operational transparency, and efficiency, supporting Veolia’s mission to accelerate ecological transformation and sustainability.
