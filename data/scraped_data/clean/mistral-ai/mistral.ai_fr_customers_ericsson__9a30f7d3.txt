Retour aux clients

Mistral is partnering with Ericsson to apply advanced, customized AI to real telecom challenges, combining Mistral’s model customization platform with Ericsson’s R&D and network expertise to make networks smarter, more efficient, and more trusted. Initial work targets high-impact use cases like automating legacy code translation and enabling AI-assisted 6G research.
