Government of Luxembourg integrates AI solutions across government operations and public research institutions.

Public sector

April 24, 2026

Bolstering technological innovation with Mistral.

Torna ai clienti

0 min read

The Government of Luxembourg, represented by the Prime Minister, has signed a strategic partnership with Mistral to bolster technological innovation and promote the development and adoption of artificial intelligence within the country. This collaboration aims to integrate AI solutions across government operations and public research institutions, with a focus on trusted and explainable AI to ensure compliance. Additionally, a specific contract with the Ministry of Defence will facilitate the integration of advanced AI tools into the activities of the Luxembourg Armed Forces, enhancing their operational capabilities.
