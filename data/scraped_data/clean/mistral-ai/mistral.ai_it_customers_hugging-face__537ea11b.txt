Hugging Face

April 24, 2026

Torna ai clienti

0 min read

HuggingFace has introduced HuggingChat, a platform to try out different Open Source models, and HuggingChat Assistants that enables users to create customized assistant tailored to specific requirements and exhibiting unique personalities. By default, both the HuggingChat and the HuggingChat Assistant are powered by the Mixtral 8x7B model, ensuring a powerful and high-quality experience, and providing users with contextually relevant responses. Currently, our Mixtral 8x7B model is the most popular model used on Hugging Chat.
