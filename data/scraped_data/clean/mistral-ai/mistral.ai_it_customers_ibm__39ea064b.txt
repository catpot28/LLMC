IBM is committed to pushing the boundaries of what AI can achieve. In a significant stride forward, IBM announced the availability of Mistral Large 2, the next-generation flagship LLM from Mistral, on IBM watsonx™. This integration marks a pivotal moment in enhancing AI capabilities for enterprises worldwide. By integrating Mistral Large 2 on IBM watsonx™, IBM provides businesses with access to advanced natural language processing and machine learning capabilities.
