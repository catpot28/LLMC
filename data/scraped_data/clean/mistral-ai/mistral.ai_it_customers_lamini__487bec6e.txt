Lamini

Technology and Software

April 24, 2026

Torna ai clienti

0 min read

Lamini is the all-in-one LLM platform for enterprises to build open LLMs on proprietary data in production. Lamini makes it easy to scale with confidence by being the only solution available to run on AMD GPUs, on parity with NVIDIA A100 and H100 GPUs. Lamini customers are in the Fortune 1000, in industries spanning manufacturing, insurance, biotech, governments, and more. ‘Mistral-7B is one of the most popular open base models that our customers reach for on Lamini to tune and ship in production,’ says Sharon Zhou, CEO of Lamini. ‘This is because Mistral-7B is extremely easy and fast to get started with: with high-quality results out of the box, tuning the model on Lamini on proprietary data is easy. This has made it possible for customers to break the POC-to-production gap and deploy their proprietary LLMs in production.’
