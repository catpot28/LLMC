Ministry of High-Tech Industry of Armenia revolutionizes public services.

Public sector

April 24, 2026

Revolutionizing citizen and public services with Mistral.

Torna ai clienti

0 min read

Through a strategic partnership with Mistral, the Ministry of High-Tech Industry of Armenia is set to revolutionize its citizen and public services by leveraging state-of-the-art AI technology. This collaboration aims to enhance the quality and accessibility of services across various government functions, with AI assistants streamlining processes for civil servants. The partnership marks a significant step in Armenia's journey to develop AI solutions that support both citizens and public institutions. Committed to increasing productivity, Armenia is focused on making advanced technologies practical and impactful, thereby improving service quality, accessibility, and efficiency. It was crucial for Armenia to collaborate with a partner like Mistral, whose open and adaptable models align with the country's unique language, infrastructure, and specific use cases. Mistral's dedication to openness, co-development, and technical collaboration makes them an ideal partner in this effort. The cooperation is expected to unlock new possibilities and remain committed to harnessing cutting-edge technologies to drive innovation that is built with and for Armenia.
