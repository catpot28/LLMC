Giving enterprises a competitive edge with MongoDB and Mistral.

Torna ai clienti

0 min read

Mistral’s frontier sparse model of experts (SMoE) architecture sets a new bar in performance and efficiency. MongoDB extends that performance and efficiency from the model layer to the data layer by securely unifying application data, metadata, and vector embeddings in a single platform. Developers are able to create rich, real-time AI applications that span an array of use cases. From conversational AI for assistance through to complex reasoning and autonomous agents for augmentation and analysis, enterprises gain a competitive edge by building with Mistral and MongoDB.
- Greg Maxson, Global Lead, AI Ecosystems, MongoDB
