MINDEF augments decision support with Mistral.

Torna ai clienti

0 min read

MINDEF, Defence Science and Technology Agency (DSTA), and DSO National Laboratories (DSO) partner with Mistral to co-develop generative AI models to augment the SAF's decision support capabilities in areas such as mission planning. The collaboration focuses on fine-tuned Mistral models and developing new mixture-of-experts (MoE) models, with support from AI Singapore for the local operating context.
