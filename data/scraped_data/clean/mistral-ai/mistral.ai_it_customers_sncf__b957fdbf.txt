The SNCF Group is partnering with Mistral to drive innovation and efficiency in the transportation sector.

Transportation and Logistics

April 24, 2026

Driving innovation and efficiency in the transportation sector.

Torna ai clienti

0 min read

The SNCF Group has partnered with Mistral to expand the use of generative artificial intelligence across its operations. As a pioneer in AI adoption since 2010, SNCF is integrating advanced AI technologies to address complex challenges and improve operational efficiency. This collaboration emphasizes accessible, ethical, and responsible AI, aligning with social and environmental values. The Groupe SNCF GPT has been deployed to over 100,000 employees offering a secure AI engine to support daily tasks. Additionally, SNCF developers are utilizing Mistral Vibe, Mistral's coding assistant, to enhance software development. This partnership aims to drive innovation and efficiency in the transportation sector, benefiting both customers and employees.
