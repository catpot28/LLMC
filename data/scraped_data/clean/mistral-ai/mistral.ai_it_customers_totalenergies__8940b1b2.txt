TotalEnergies and Mistral develop solutions to optimize energy production and reduce emissions.

April 24, 2026

TotalEnergies enhances its multi-energy strategy with Mistral.

Torna ai clienti

0 min read

TotalEnergies has partnered with Mistral to enhance its multi-energy strategy through advanced artificial intelligence. This collaboration focuses on leveraging AI to improve performance in low-carbon energies and industrial operations. By establishing a joint innovation lab, the partnership aims to develop AI-driven solutions that optimize energy production and reduce carbon emissions. TotalEnergies, with its extensive expertise in energy and a history of integrating AI in predictive maintenance and earth sciences, is now expanding its AI capabilities with Mistral's cutting-edge technologies. This initiative underscores TotalEnergies' commitment to digital transformation and sustainability, driving innovation in the energy sector.
