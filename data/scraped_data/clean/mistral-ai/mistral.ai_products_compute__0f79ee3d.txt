AI-optimized clusters.

Direct hardware access with no virtualization overhead. The speed, throughput, and reliability we demand for our own flagship training.

Managed Kubernetes.

Standardized provisioning and operations. Your hardware is managed as native Kubernetes resources. Multi-tenant by design, with policy enforcement across every cluster.

Managed Slurm.

Built for AI teams running training jobs at scale. Queue management, priority tiers, and fair-share across teams, with topology-aware scheduling.

Built-in across every tier.

Observability.

Dashboards, logs, and metrics built in. Zero extra cost. GPU health, power, thermals, ECC, throughput. Per-job telemetry, streamed to the tools you already run.

Governance.

SSO and SCIM out of the box. RBAC maps to SLURM accounts. Secrets, key management, audit trails, CI/CD webhooks.

Reliability.

Enterprise-grade SLAs across training and inference, with dedicated incident response.

Support.

Infrastructure and AI specialists on every tier, with direct escalation to the engineering team behind the platform. Auto-healing is instrumented by our own models.

Security.

Purpose-built security for large-scale AI workloads: EVPN-VXLAN network isolation, AES-256 encryption at rest with BYOK, and defined data wiping protocol.
