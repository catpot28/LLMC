Bringing the combination of top-tier AI science with deep experience and expertise in developing AI-powered solutions tailored for effectiveness and efficiency, Mistral AI helps institutions build AI-powered solutions that serve, protect and enable citizens.

Why AI for Citizens?
Mistral AI works with States and public institutions to deliver AI solutions based on the following principles.
Openness and transparency.
As a leader in open technologies for AI, Mistral AI can help you to build solutions that prevent lock-in and make it possible to expand access.
Deep collaboration.
By working closely with governments and local partners, we help you create solutions that are tailored for local language, culture and needs while helping to build local AI ecosystems.
Flexibility and choice across the AI stack.
We empower the public and private sector to choose the infrastructure, hosting, technologies and partners needed to reach their unique goals and foster their AI ecosystems without locking them into inflexible, closed solutions.
Autonomy and ownership.
Our solutions ensure that governments, organizations and businesses can deploy AI solutions that ensure the strictest data sovereignty and avoid risks from dependence on closed solutions.

Proudly partnering with agencies worldwide.
Prime Minister of Greece
"I was particularly struck by the sensitivity that Mistral has shown towards preserving linguistic and cultural heritage. Greek is a language that has been spoken continuously over thousands of years. The ability to understand the nuances of the language was particularly important to us."
Ministry of Digital Transition, Morocco
Morocco, driven by an ambitious royal vision and major structural reforms, is accelerating its digital transition to consolidate its position as a regional technology hub and a key player in global innovation, with artificial intelligence at the forefront.
Through this partnership with a leading technology player such as Mistral AI, Morocco affirms its ambition to make AI a driver of sustainable and inclusive growth, while consolidating its position as a regional and African leader in the digital field.
France Travail
Working with Mistral AI, France Travail is leading the way in assisting job seekers in finding suitable employment opportunities with the help of Mistral AI models, enabling them to summarize job seekers’ histories, profiles, and areas of interest. Additionally, an internal chatbot helps agents better access and understand labor laws and allocation policies, ensuring compliance and efficiency.
Helsing
Helsing and Mistral AI have formed a strategic partnership to develop next-generation AI systems for European defence. The partnership focuses on Vision-Language-Action models, fostering human-AI collaboration on the battlefield and revolutionizing defence operations.

"Europe’s defence must be built on technologies we understand, trust, and control. Helsing’s collaboration with Mistral reflects a shared commitment to advancing AI capabilities that serve that mission — rooted in sovereignty, openness, and the reality of the threats we face. Helsing and Mistral AI’s partnership shows how leading in tech can give democracies the competitive edge we need in a hostile world."

Antoine Bordes, Chief Scientist, Helsing
HTX Singapore
With the help of Mistral AI, HTX is fine tuning pretrained models and also building their own series of LLMs, codenamed Phoenix. Phoenix has been pre-trained on extensive corpuses of knowledge specific and relevant to the Home Team and Singapore, as well as 10 major languages spoken in Singapore, including Mandarin, Bahasa Melayu, and Tamil. This will allow Home Team officers to get more accurate responses compared to other kinds of LLMs.
Singapore’s Ministry of Defence (MINDEF)
MINDEF, Defence Science and Technology Agency (DSTA), and DSO National Laboratories (DSO) partner with Mistral AI to co-develop generative AI models to augment the SAF’s decision support capabilities in areas such as mission planning. The collaboration focuses on fine-tuned Mistral models and developing new mixture-of-experts (MoE) models, with support from AI Singapore for the local operating context.
French Ministry of Defense
Through its AI agency AMIAD, the French Minister of Defense and Mistral AI are collaborating on advanced research themes such as multimodal models, robotics, automation, and embedded systems. It also includes the industrialization of key products for French defense, ensuring France's technological independence.

"We are proud to announce our strategic partnership with Mistral AI. This collaboration will enable us to develop state of the art AI solutions to the unique needs of our defense sector. This agreement encompasses two primary areas: a collaboration on advanced research themes such as multimodal models, robotics, automation, and embedded systems, and the industrialization of key products essential for French defense."

Bertrand Rondepierre - CEO, French Agency for AI in Defense
Republic of Armenia
Through the partnership with Mistral AI, Armenia is positioned to revolutionize its public and private sectors by utilizing state-of-the-art AI technology to enhance the quality and accessibility of services across various government functions.

"For us, it was critical to collaborate with a partner whose open and adaptable models can be aligned with our unique language, infrastructure, and specific use cases. Mistral’s dedication to openness, co-development, and technical collaboration makes them great partner in this effort.
We look forward to the possibilities this cooperation unlocks and remain committed to harnessing cutting-edge technologies to drive innovation that is not imported, but built with and for Armenia."

Mkhitar Hayrapetyan, Minister of High-Tech Industry of the Republic of Armenia
French Ministry of Digital Transition
Minister Laurent Marcangeli announced a partnership with the French startup Mistral to "equip 10,000 civil servants" within the public sector. Ultimately, the goal is to make le Chat available to all 5.7 million public servants in France.
Government of Luxembourg
The Government of Luxembourg signed a strategic partnership with Mistral AI aimed at integrating AI within the government and partnering with public research institutions, with a focus on trusted and explainable AI for compliance. A contract with the Ministry of Defence will further integrate advanced AI tools into the activities of the Luxembourg Armed Forces.

“This partnership is a crucial step in our strategy to make Luxembourg a world leader in the sovereign data economy. European-style artificial intelligence with a Luxembourg touch. Beyond the development of new AI tools, it is above all the adoption of new technologies by citizens, businesses and public administration that will make all the difference.”

The Prime Minister of Luxembourg
University of Groningen
"The University of Groningen is integrating Mistral's state-of-the-art AI into their academic and business frameworks. These agents are particularly adept at handling repetitive tasks governed by clear rules, freeing our staff to focus more deeply on substantive work. This shift is anticipated to enhance both the quality and efficiency of our research, education, and operational processes. We're also excited about Mistral's ability to offer control and freedom of choice over our infrastructure deployment and data sovereignty."
