Your corporate security as a basis for successful working from home

Companies need to adapt their internal structures and prepare their employees for safe and efficient working from home.

Modern security solutions with firewalls and VPN access enable a protected and trouble-free working environment for employees working remotely.

Netpoint supports companies with advice and solutions for secure home offices to maintain productivity without data risks even in times of crisis.

Seeing a crisis as an opportunity.

The majority of all companies are currently affected by the extent of the Corona crisis. This means that companies must adapt their internal structures and prepare their employees to work successfully in a home office.

With professional security solutions consisting of modern firewall and VPN access from top manufacturers, we secure your company. This allows your employees a risk-free and smooth working environment, even in the home office.

True to the current motto
#stayathome
, companies should use home offices as extensively as possible. Sustainable work without major downtime can thus continue to be ensured. In 2020, digitization is more advanced than ever before. Nevertheless, home office is still a foreign word in many companies today.

Netpoint answers all your questions about working safely from home. We work with top manufacturers and are happy to advise you personally. Our goal is to help you continue to maintain employee productivity efficiently and without data risk, even in difficult times.
