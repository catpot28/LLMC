Innovation Technology.

In the business world of tomorrow, competitive advantages are challenged daily by rapid technological revolutions. The industry leadership of any company is therefore directly related to its IT competence.

With our vision: "Scoring competitive advantage through innovative IT" we set new requirements for our future-strong system house. This makes Netpoint not only an executor of service measures, but also a partner for constant innovation.

Our values

Be Precise.

As a service provider for global corporations, we have a major influence on the productivity of our customers. In doing so, we turn the most important adjusting screws every day in order to offer our customers the best IT! This requires a high degree of precision; we have to be very familiar with the smallest details, because they have a big impact. Our communication must therefore not be superficial or flow around the corner - we communicate precisely and in a direct way.

Be United.

Networking is undoubtedly our core business, but it's not just our computers that are networked - we are too. We know that great results are a result of seamless collaboration, and we achieve that not only internally, but also with and at our customers. We work in partnership with all stakeholders and think beyond connected. We think united!

Be Visionary.

In no other industry is thinking ahead as important as in IT. We have to deal with tomorrow's issues today in order to secure our customers' technological progress in the long term. That's why we are hungry for knowledge and always on the lookout for a better solution. In doing so, we like to look beyond current areas of IT to drive innovation not by necessity, but with our vision!

Your contact persons.

With pure enthusiasm for the technologies of tomorrow, our team is our most important asset. We rely on a constellation of experienced experts who have been working in the industry for decades and fresh, innovative minds who are eager to break new ground in IT. The result is a strong, international and diverse team, all working towards the same goal: delivering the best performance to our customers.
