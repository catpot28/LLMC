What Is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI
uses sophisticated reasoning and planning to solve complex, multi-step problems.
Agentic AI systems
ingest vast amounts of data from multiple data sources to analyze challenges, develop strategies, and complete tasks independently.

AI agents
transform enterprise data into actionable knowledge.

Over time, AI agents learn and improve by creating a
data flywheel
, where human and AI feedback is used to refine models and improve outcomes.
