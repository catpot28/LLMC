Explore and Build Claws

Ready to dive into OpenClaw? Here’s your chance. Join us to build your own AI agent.

Build-a-Claw Resource Hub

You've seen what autonomous agents can do. Now it's time to build your own. Use these resources to continue your journey. Whether you're installing locally, trying it free on the cloud, or connecting with our community.

Get started quickly with NemoClaw

NVIDIA NemoClaw is an open source reference stack that simplifies running OpenClaw always-on assistants more safely, with a single command. It bundles OpenClaw with the OpenShell secure runtime and NVIDIA Nemotron models — so you get security guardrails out of the box.

Install OpenClaw on your NVIDIA DGX Spark/OEM GB10

Ready to run OpenClaw locally on your hardware? Follow this guide to get OpenClaw up and running on your NVIDIA DGX Spark or OEM GB10 system.

Install NemoClaw on your DGX Spark/OEM GB10

Want the full stack with security guardrails? This guide walks you through installing NemoClaw on your DGX Spark or OEM GB10 system — one command to deploy OpenClaw, NVIDIA OpenShell, and Nemotron models together.

Install OpenClaw on your NVIDIA Jetson

Run a fully local AI personal assistant on Jetson with OpenClaw and WhatsApp, no cloud APIs needed.

Try OpenClaw for free on the cloud

Want to try OpenClaw without the security layer? This Launchable gives you standard OpenClaw on a cloud VM — the fastest way to get started and see what autonomous agents can do.

Get support from our agent

Have questions? Need help troubleshooting? Chat with our support agent to get answers and guidance on your Build-a-Claw journey.

Try NemoClaw for free on the cloud

No hardware? No problem. Spin up NemoClaw on a free cloud VM with NVIDIA Brev. Nothing to install on your machine. It's a clean sandbox to experiment and learn without risk. Free Brev account required (brev.nvidia.com)

Join our community on Discord

Connect with experts, share what you're building, and get help from the community. Our Discord is where developers, researchers, and AI enthusiasts come together to talk about OpenClaw, NemoClaw, and autonomous agents.

Notice and Disclaimer

OpenClaw and NemoClaw are open source projects provided as-is. Running autonomous agents involves inherent risks including system access, data exposure, and potential security vulnerabilities. Always run agents in secure environments and review security best practices before deploying in production.

Use of Brev Launchables is subject to Brev's terms of service. By using these tools, you acknowledge and accept these risks.
