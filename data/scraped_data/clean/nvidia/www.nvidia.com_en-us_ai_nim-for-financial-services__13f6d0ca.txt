With support for a wide range of AI foundation models, including open community and domain-specific models, and customization capabilities within the financial services industry,

By packaging advanced performance optimizations into user-friendly containers, NIM empowers organizations to seamlessly accelerate AI workloads at any scale.

From data center to edge to cloud, NVIDIA NIM empowers companies to scale AI with unmatched performance and simplicity—driving innovation and pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in the financial services industry.
