Advances in reasoning accuracy and model efficiency are accelerating the adoption of generative AI at the edge. From NVIDIA Jetson Orin™ to Jetson Thor™, the NVIDIA Jetson family is purpose-built physical AI hardware for robotics deployment, delivering optimized runtime inference for leading open models. These include NVIDIA Nemotron™, Cosmos™, Isaac™ GR00T, and a growing ecosystem of community models.

Introducing NVIDIA Jetson Thor

The NVIDIA® Jetson AGX Thor™ Developer Kit gives you unmatched performance and scalability. It’s powered by the NVIDIA Blackwell GPU and 128 GB of memory, delivering up to 2070 FP4 TFLOPS of AI compute to effortlessly run the
latest generative AI models
—all within a 130 W power envelope. Compared to NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin™, it delivers up to 7.5x higher AI compute and 3.5x better energy efficiency.

Learn More

The Ideal Software for Robotics and Generative AI at the Edge

NVIDIA Jetson software supports all Jetson modules, delivering real-time sensor processing, visual AI, and advanced robotics features in a unified ecosystem. The software is agentic ready, so you can build and deploy AI agents directly on Jetson hardware. With the JetPack 7 SDK,
Isaac simulation frameworks
, and seamless cloud integration, Jetson software empowers rapid, scalable innovation in
physical AI
. It’s a key part of the Jetson platform, optimized for generative AI performance and supporting a wide range of popular open-source models and openclaws through
Jetson AI Lab
.
