Medical Emergencies

Take Time for Preventive Care

Routine exams and screenings reduce your risk of serious health issues. In-network preventive care is covered 100% under every
NVIDIA medical plan
. Take the time you need to schedule routine medical, dental, and vision check-ups–plus well-being sessions–and stay on top of your health.

Explore Ways to Stay Healthy

Medical

Serious conditions are much easier to treat if caught early. Here are some ways you can do so:

Annual Checkup

Everyone at every age qualifies and should have their once-a-year visit.

Cancer Screenings

Colorectal if you’re age 45 or older

Prostate if you’re age 50 or older (or 40 or older if you’re at higher risk)

Breast if you’re age 40 or older

Here’s More

Telehealth
- 24/7 virtual access to a medical professional.

Crossover Health
- concierge-style healthcare services for Cigna members.

Critical Illness or Hospital Indemnity Insurance from MetLife
— earn a benefit just for taking care of your health. For more info, register with your
Alternate ID
on the
MyBenefits portal
.

Learn more about our medical plans

Dental

Taking care of your oral health with preventive dental care can help:

Reduce your risk of tooth decay, gum disease, and other more serious problems.

Reduce dental problems related to chronic medical conditions like diabetes, osteoporosis, some cancers, and eating disorders

Alert you to other health issues for your doctor to monitor

Your oral health and your heart are closely related. Certain gum and tooth conditions may be warning signs for bacterial infections, heart valve issues, or coronary artery disease.

Routine dental care makes sure you receive that important health message.

Learn more about our dental coverage

Vision

Just like your medical and dental health, your vision can tell you a lot. Having your once-a-year exam can ensure you’re able to see what’s happening both around you and inside you. Watch out for these eye issues that can signal bigger problems like undiagnosed thyroid problems, diabetes, high cholesterol, cancer, stress, alcohol misuse, and more:

Blurred vision

Bulging eyes

Eye twitches

Yellow whites of your eyes

Ring around your cornea

Your NVIDIA vision coverage includes hearing aid discounts.

.

Flexible Spending Account

We’re also announcing these updates, allowing you to set aside even more in your
FSA
:

The 2026 Day Care FSA limit has increased to $7,500 up from $5,000 in 2025.

The 2026 Health Care and Limited Purpose FSA limit has increased to $3,400 up from $3,300 in 2025.

A Flexible Spending Account (FSA) lets you set aside pre-tax money from your paycheck to pay for qualified healthcare or dependent care expenses. By contributing pre-tax dollars, you reduce your taxable income.

Remember, those enrolled in a 2025 Health Care or Limited-Purpose FSA have until December 31, 2025, to use their balance​, and up to $660 of your remaining balance in 2025 will carry over into 2026.​

Those enrolled in a 2025 Day Care FSA can incur expenses until March 15, 2026. You must submit 2025 expenses for reimbursement by June 15, 2026.

Coverage Through Another Medical Plan

If you’re covered through another medical plan, you may not need to enroll in medical coverage with NVIDIA. Enrolling in multiple health plans isn't recommended, as the plans will need to coordinate to process each claim, which can take time and often leads to manual re-processing. Keep in mind that you may need to pay contributions for both plans, as well as meet deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums under both plans.

If you choose to waive NVIDIA medical, you may be eligible for a $50 credit each paycheck, up to $1,200 a year. Log in to the
Benefits Enrollment
site to waive coverage.

Additionally, the IRS does not allow you or your dependents to enroll in our HSA medical plan if you’re also enrolled in a non-high-deductible health (HMO or PPO) plan. For example, if your partner already covers you and your family in their employer’s PPO plan, you wouldn't be eligible to contribute to an HSA.
