As a new NVIDIAN, you can expect not only unique opportunities and a satisfying work environment, but also a comprehensive benefits package that includes healthcare coverage, income protection, and compensation programs.

When Can I Sign Up?

Eligible employees
will be automatically enrolled in many benefits. For other benefits, some rules apply:

Newly hired employees have 31 days from the date of hire to make elections.

All other employees can make changes during open enrollment (effective January 1 of the following year) or within 31 days of a
qualified status change
(such as a marriage, birth, or divorce).

Coverage for most plans–including medical, dental and vision–will be retroactive to the date you joined. If you experience a medical emergency prior to enrollment, you can pay medical expenses out of pocket and submit your claims for reimbursement once plan enrollment has been confirmed.

If you don’t take any action to waive coverage, you’ll be automatically enrolled in the
NVIDIA HSA Plan
. You must actively waive medical coverage to receive the medical waiver credit.

You’ll need to create your HSA through Fidelity to receive NVIDIA’s HSA contribution.

Kaiser members will receive a medical ID card in the mail within 10 business days. There will be no ID cards for Vision and Dental plans. Cigna members need to download the myCigna app to access your digital ID card. You can request a physical ID card via myCigna.com or by calling 1-855-568-4342.

NVIDIA uses an
Alternate ID
for many benefits so you don’t need to sign up with your SSN.

How Much Will It Cost?

There are two costs to consider:

What you pay out of your paycheck for your elected benefits. See this
PDF
for medical, dental, and vision plan contributions. You’ll find all other contributions when you make your election on the
U.S. Benefits Enrollment Site
.

What you pay during the course of the year through deductibles, copays, and coinsurance. Make sure to research the plan designs and compare to your expected needs to budget appropriately.

How Do I Sign Up?

Visit the
U.S. Benefits Enrollment Site
and log in to enroll. You’ll receive 401(k) enrollment information through email on the Wednesday after your date of hire.

If you don’t make an election, you’ll be automatically enrolled in the
NVIDIA HSA medical plan
and the 401(k) Plan at 6% pre-tax contribution. These may not be right for you, so be sure to make an election. Also, you won't have dental or vision coverage, and you won’t be able to participate in the Flexible Spending Account programs.

Eligible employees are automatically enrolled in
employee life, accident
, and
disability insurance
. You have 31 days from your date of hire to update your benefit elections. You may also be able to make changes after a
qualified status change
(like a marriage, birth, or divorce).

What Medical Plan Is Right for Me?

Depending on where you live, you have up to several
NVIDIA medical plans
