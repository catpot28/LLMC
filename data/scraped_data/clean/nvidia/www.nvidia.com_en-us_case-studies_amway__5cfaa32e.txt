Scaling Success: How Amway Elevates AI Infrastructure With NVIDIA AI Enterprise

Explore the Solution

Objective

Amway is an international company that delivers exceptional products and services to consumers around the world. Using NVIDIA AI Enterprise, Amway is establishing a secure, efficient, and scalable AI infrastructure. This system enhances computational resource allocation and elevates the engineering standards of inference services, providing robust support for the large-scale deployment of AI across all business scenarios.

Customer

Amway

Use Case

Generative AI / LLMs

Products

NVIDIA AI Enterprise
NVIDIA NIM™
NVIDIA NeMo™ Guardrails
NVIDIA Base Command

Key Takeaways

With the optimization capabilities of NVIDIA NIM, Amway’s AI Office Assistant and AM Copilot models have seen significant improvement in inference performance.

Amway experienced a
40% increase in inference efficiency
under equivalent computing power, optimizing resource use and providing a blueprint for cost-effective, sustainable AI infrastructure.

To address data privacy and ethical risks in generative AI applications, Amway integrated NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails, building multi-layer security controls.

To keep data safe, the company integrated NeMo Guardrails across the AI workflow, blocking 99.9% of sensitive information and ensuring everything remains secure and compliant.

With the automated orchestration capabilities of NVIDIA Base Command Manager Essentials (BCME), Amway has achieved full-lifecycle management of AI models, covering training, deployment, and inference.

Recognizing the transformative impact of
generative AI
, Amway China is accelerating the strategic leap from traditional general-purpose computing to the era of intelligent computing.

Amway is building a secure, efficient, and scalable AI infrastructure with
NVIDIA AI Enterprise
, strengthening resource allocation and raising the engineering standards for
AI inference
services. This foundation enables large-scale deployment of AI across all business scenarios, prioritizing both technical capability development and deep integration with the enterprise's knowledge map and business chain — transforming AI from a tool-based technology to a strategic asset.

Leveraging this infrastructure, Amway is enhancing its employees and business partners, streamlining collaboration, improving overall operational efficiency, and accelerating the development of a new type of operation system powered by data and intelligence.

Amway has incorporated data security and compliance governance into its strategic oversight and established an end-to-end, multi-level AI security and privacy protection mechanism to ensure that AI innovation moves forward steadily on a controllable, trustworthy, and sustainable track.

With generative AI, Amway is reshaping business processes, promoting enterprise digital transformation, and driving business innovation and value growth. By building an AI application ecosystem, Amway is accelerating the implementation of its enterprise strategy as “Total Wellness” and building an intelligent enterprise for the future.

Amway Drives Generative AI Innovations in Performance and Security

Amway China has pioneered an enterprise-level AI evolution path, progressing from small-scale pilots to large-scale deployment, and has established a sustainable and replicable AI practice paradigm.

In the early stages of AI investment, many enterprises remain cautious due to uncertain returns - particularly when evaluating options like self-built computing power, leased resources, and SaaS services. Amway opted for a cost-effective 'lightweight startup' approach —building its own computing platform to ensure security and compliance, while laying a solid foundation for technological innovation and business exploration. This approach allows Amway to control initial investments while maintaining technological independence. Once the returns on AI applications became evident, Amway gradually integrated external computing resources, creating a more flexible and scalable AI infrastructure to support long-term growth.

By building an efficient agent development platform, Amway has significantly shortened the time from idea to real-world application of AI services, reducing the average delivery timeline for an agent from
35 days to 15 days
. Leveraging NVIDIA's powerful application ecosystem enables rapid deployment of intelligent solutions across various business scenarios, enhancing delivery efficiency and business agility. By unifying model development, inference deployment, and scenario customization, Amway delivers reusable, scalable AI solutions that help enterprises accelerate implementation and rapidly achieve business value.

By adopting NVIDIA's full-stack hardware and software solutions, along with advanced acceleration libraries and optimization tools, Amway has ensured system stability while significantly improving model inference efficiency. Through practical validation, Amway's solution has shown a
40% increase in inference efficiency
under equivalent computing power conditions. This initiative not only optimizes resource utilization but also provides valuable experience for building cost-effective and sustainable AI infrastructure for enterprises.

As generative AI moves into practical applications, Amway has prioritized system stability and security. Using NVIDIA AI Enterprise, the company has established a comprehensive operation and maintenance system covering the entire lifecycle of models — encompassing core aspects such as performance monitoring, version control, inference stability assessment, and security policy enforcement. With standardized update processes and real-time monitoring, Amway maintains full visibility into AI service performance, anticipates potential risks, ensures safe and controlled iterations, and establishes a robust foundation for the continuous and stable operation of AI services.

How NVIDIA Powers Amway's New AI Infrastructure

Using the NVIDIA AI Enterprise software stack, Amway has successfully established an efficient, flexible, and secure AI ecosystem:

1. NVIDIA DeepSeek R1 NIM: Boosting Model Inference and Efficient Use of Resources

With the optimization capabilities of
NVIDIA NIM
, Amway’s AI Office Assistant and AM Copilot models have seen significant improvement in inference performance. This enhancement supports high-concurrency access for large numbers of marketing personnel while ensuring low-latency, high-stability of service responses. By leveraging a hybrid cloud architecture, the DeepSeek R1 NIM dynamically allocates GPU resources, enabling intelligent computing power distribution across diverse scenarios, maximizing the utilization of computing resources and supporting the elastic scaling needs of Amway's 'three-in-one' strategy.

2. NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails: Building a Robust AI Security and Compliance Defense

To address data privacy and ethical risks in generative AI applications,
NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails
has built multi-layer security controls for Amway. These include filtering sensitive information, verifying dialogue compliance, and dynamically managing user permissions, ensuring that AI services comply with global regulations (such as GDPR). This framework is deeply integrated into the AM Copilot so that account managers can access health management recommendations or community operation cases with fully controlled data interaction, protecting both consumer privacy and Amway’s business confidentiality.

3. NVIDIA BCME: End-to-End Automation and Transparent Operations

With the automated orchestration capabilities of NVIDIA Base Command Manager Essentials (BCME), Amway has achieved full-lifecycle management of AI models, covering training, deployment, and inference. This innovation has improved operational efficiency by over 60%. By integrating a visual monitoring dashboard, the IT team can track GPU resource utilization, task execution status, and anomaly alerts in real time, establishing a ‘resource-aware and risk-predictive’ operations framework. This system ensures 24/7 seamless access to AI tools for marketing personnel.

“Amway is accelerating its digital transformation by leveraging NVIDIA's powerful GPU computing capabilities and NVIDIA AI Enterprise's enterprise-level solutions. With the help of NVIDIA's robust hardware performance and its end-to-end AI software ecosystem, Amway has efficiently deployed large language models on-premises. It has also established a full-cycle AI application development and operations framework, enabling deep customization and large-scale deployment of AI capabilities in core business scenarios.”

Paul Wu

Vice President of Information Technology, Amway (China) Co., Ltd.

From Resource Optimization to Intelligent Decision-Making

In today’s digital landscape, competition extends beyond traditional supply chains to encompass sophisticated operations in data and computing power. NVIDIA AI Enterprise empowered Amway to achieve a leap in business value through innovative technology paths: from resource optimization to intelligent decision-making.

Amway has built several tools and applications, including:

AI Office Assistant Boosts Internal Efficiency:
Leveraging the powerful acceleration capabilities of the NVIDIA DeepSeek R1 NIM, Amway built an intelligent office assistant in just two weeks. This assistant automates the processing and analysis of documents, increasing the efficiency of employees' daily work by up to 50%. The assistant also features AI-powered PowerPoint generation, which can quickly extract key information from uploaded documents and apply company or custom templates to produce high-quality presentations. The system is deployed on-premises, ensuring data security and compliance.

AM Copilot Productivity:
Designed for sales teams, Amway AM Copilot is becoming a key engine for improving frontline efficiency. It helps employees complete tasks more efficiently through multiple
AI agents
for processes like case recommendations and data retrieval. This evolving tool lays the groundwork for a truly intelligent assistant, moving beyond simple automation to provide customized, context-aware support.

ABO AI Assistant Delivers AI-Powered Precision at Scale:
Amway Business Owner (ABO) AI Assistant, powered by NVIDIA AI Enterprise, supports marketers by handling up to ten million case retrievals daily. It enables rapid, accurate, and personalized health solutions by accessing a dynamic knowledge base and integrating Amway’s business logic. For data security, NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails are embedded throughout the AI process, achieving a 99.9% interception rate for sensitive information and ensuring strict information control and compliance.

With these AI-driven applications, Amway is accelerating the integration of AI into daily business operations, significantly improving efficiency and user experience. In the future, Amway will continue working with NVIDIA to drive business upgrades through technology, explore the deep integration of AI with the industry, and lead the intelligent transformation to achieve greater success.
