Clinomic Improves Intensive Care Intelligence With AI Agents

Learn More

Objective

Clinomic is streamlining intensive care unit (ICU) workflows and improving patient care with Mona, an AI-powered smart assistant that consolidates, analyzes, and visualizes patient data in real time. Powered by
NVIDIA® Jetson
AGX™, Mona helps clinicians deliver better care, enabling hands-free documentation, sharper diagnostics, and smarter decisions at the bedside.

Easing the Strain on ICUs

ICUs are under pressure—facing staff shortages, information overload, and the constant demand for swift, accurate decisions.

With over 1,000 data points per patient per hour, Clinomic, a healthcare technology company, is helping hospitals turn information into insight. Founded by intensive care physicians at RWTH Aachen University Hospital, Clinomic recognized the need for a solution that could enhance patient care while streamlining clinical workflows.

Their answer was Mona—short for medical on-site assistant—a smart,
agentic AI
and telemedicine platform designed to transform critical care. Mona consolidates and analyzes patient data in real time, enabling healthcare providers to make faster, more informed decisions, ultimately improving patient outcomes.

Smarter Patient Care With AI

Mona is more than a device—it’s a modular, open, and scalable platform that integrates with third-party and AI applications. It aggregates vital patient data, responds to voice commands, supports remote consultations, and automatically flags critical changes in patient status, all while maintaining stringent privacy standards.

Powered by
NVIDIA Jetson AGX Xavier™
, Mona brings telemedicine directly to the bedside. Its real-time edge processing eliminates reliance on cloud connectivity, delivering faster, more reliable performance in hospitals.

With native support for frameworks like
TensorFlow
,
PyTorch
,
NVIDIA TensorRT™
, and
ONNX
, Clinomic uses NVIDIA Jetson™ to launch pretrained models and merge Mona with existing medical imaging and monitoring systems, accelerating time to deployment. Jetson’s compact design—featuring low noise output, efficient heat management, and minimal energy consumption—allows for a seamless fit within Clinomic’s environment.

Mona equips care teams with capabilities designed to enhance diagnostics, streamline workflows, and improve patient outcomes. Features include:

Voice Recognition:
Clinicians can dictate notes in real time with hands-free documentation and use secure voice authentication for speedy access to patient records. And real‑time, AI‑powered translation across 30+ languages breaks down communication barriers, enabling seamless care in diverse, multilingual settings.

Real-Time Image Processing:
Mona enhances ultrasound imaging through AI-driven noise reduction and edge detection, improving diagnostic clarity and accuracy.

Predictive Analytics:
By analyzing vital signs, lab results, demographics, and more, Mona anticipates serious conditions like acute kidney injury or sepsis before they escalate, enabling life-saving interventions.

Telemedicine:
Mona facilitates remote consultations, bringing expert knowledge directly to the patient’s bedside, proving invaluable in rural or underserved areas where access to specialized care is limited.

Clinomic

Real Results, Real Impact

Built for ICU doctors and nurses, Mona is intuitive and easy to adopt with minimal training. Since implementing the AI platform in over 40 hospitals across 8 EU countries, healthcare providers have seen measurable improvements:

68% reduction in documentation errors, enhancing the accuracy of patient records and improving overall care quality

33% reduction in perceived workload, leading to better job satisfaction and reduced burnout

550 hours of time savings per year per bed, allowing healthcare providers to allocate more time to patient care

143% increase in patient care capacity, helping healthcare facilities to handle more patients efficiently

26% reduction in transfers to other hospitals, reducing the burden on the healthcare system

Amid rising demands and a shortage of skilled staff, healthcare providers need intelligent, reliable AI assistance. Clinomic delivers just that—bringing expert insight to the bedside, with a goal to set the standard for assistance and patient data management systems (PDMS) in intensive care medicine.

“Mona transforms ICUs: Increased workforce flexibility, fewer errors, more time, capacity, and better patient care.”

Dr. med. Arne Peine, MHBA
