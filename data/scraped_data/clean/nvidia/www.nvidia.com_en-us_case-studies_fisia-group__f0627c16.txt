Fisia Group Brings AI Shopping to Nike Brazil With Doris.ai

Watch Session

Doris.ai

Objective

Fisia, the official Nike distributor in Brazil, partnered with Doris.ai and NVIDIA to deliver a seamless virtual try-on experience, resulting in higher conversion rates, larger average purchases, and fewer product returns.

Customer

Fisia Group, official distributor of Nike in Brazil

Partner

Doris.ai
Marvik

Use Case

Simulation / Modelling / Design

AR / VR

Products

NVIDIA TensorRT

NVIDIA Dynamo

Key Takeaways

In-Store Conversion Rate

150% increase in in-store conversion rates using Doris.ai’s 3D avatar virtual try-on and sizing solution

Increase Ticket Size

45% increase in average ticket size delivered through real-time, AI-powered fit recommendations and outfit visualization

ROI

128% return on investment (ROI) achieved by upsell strategies that strengthened multi-piece apparel purchases and expanded footwear sales

Bridging the Gap Between Digital and Physical Shopping

Retailers struggle with high return rates and lost sales because customers face uncertainty choosing the right fit and style across digital and physical retail channels. This challenge is significant as it directly impacts profitability, operational efficiency, and competitiveness in an industry where seamless, personalized shopping experiences are increasingly expected.

Doris.ai, in partnership with Marvik, is solving this challenge by delivering AI-powered virtual try-on allowing customers to virtually try on clothes with accuracy. Their solution empowers retailers to enhance the customer experience, streamline operations, and drive higher conversion rates through immersive, data-driven shopping journeys.

Powering Immersive Retail With Advanced AI

The boundary between digital and physical shopping is dissolving, ushering in a new era of experience-commerce, the bridge between in-store and online retail where technology and human experience merge. Doris.ai, part of NVIDIA’s independent software vendor (ISV) ecosystem, is redefining how customers engage with fashion, no matter where or how they choose to shop.

Doris.ai’s approach is remarkably user-friendly: With just two photos from a shopper’s phone—one from the front and one from the side—the platform instantly captures accurate body measurements. This unlocks a sophisticated virtual try-on experience.

Whether browsing online, interacting with a smart mirror in-store, or using a mobile device on the go, shoppers can see themselves in any outfit, mix and match styles, and experiment with different sizes on a highly realistic, personalized 3D representation of their own body in real time. The result is a seamless, deeply personal shopping journey that adapts to every context.

Behind the scenes, Doris.ai leverages
NVIDIA® TensorRT™
and
Triton Inference Server™
to deliver tailored recommendations and answer nuanced customer queries. The platform’s virtual try-on and avatar generation capabilities are powered by a combination of seven APIs, hosted on a Kubernetes cluster of AWS instances equipped with
NVIDIA A10 Tensor Core GPUs
. Each API can scale horizontally based on traffic, ensuring robust performance even at peak demand.

This high-performance infrastructure enables Doris.ai to deliver a seamless, real-time experience for shoppers and retailers alike. Drawing from product catalogs, purchase histories, and real-time inventory data, the platform suggests outfits, recommends sizes, and highlights trending styles. Its multi-query capability allows customers to interact naturally—asking for fit advice, alternate colors, or complementary pieces in a single, fluid conversation.

To develop the sophisticated technical capabilities that power its experience-commerce platform, Doris.ai partnered with Marvik, an AI consulting firm and service delivery partner (SDP) with deep expertise in
computer vision
,
generative AI
, and predictive analytics.

Marvik developed and optimized generative models, such as
Stable Diffusion
, to enhance garment visualization, and built computer vision algorithms to accurately extract body measurements from user images. It also engineered robust
recommendation systems
that personalize garment suggestions based on both predicted fit and product attributes. By optimizing these solutions, Marvik enabled Doris.ai to deliver high-resolution, real-time performance at scale across both online and in-store environments.

Boosting Conversion, Revenue, and Customer Satisfaction

The impact of this technology is profound. In a recent proof of concept with Fisia Group, the official Nike distributor in Brazil, in-store Doris.ai users saw conversion rates rise from 8% to 20%—a 150% increase—while average ticket size climbed by 45%.

The return on investment reached a remarkable 128%, demonstrating how AI-driven experiences can boost both customer satisfaction and business performance.

In another example, a major retailer leveraging Doris.ai achieved a 66% reduction in product returns and a 130% increase in online conversion rates, rising from 4.6% to 10.6%. Both retailers are now expanding their use of Doris.ai.

Doris.aI

“With Doris Mirror, consumers can see clothes on their own bodies directly in the shop window, in a touchless and personalized way. ... The technology connects the display window to the virtual fitting room, reduces returns, increases conversions, and elevates the experience to a new level. Innovation that transcends traditional retail. A new way to shop has arrived. Hello, experience-commerce.”

Marcos de Moraes
CEO, Doris.ai

Doris.ai Unifies Retail for Customers and Businesses

Doris.ai transforms the retail experience for both customers and retailers, offering a wide range of benefits that go beyond the traditional shopping journey. Whether it’s buying a stylish jacket for a friend or selecting baby clothes for a growing family, Doris.ai’s technology removes the guesswork, ensuring the perfect fit and a seamless gifting experience for the customer.

For retailers, Doris.ai unifies the retail value chain. The platform not only enhances the customer experience but also creates a data-rich experience that benefits every touchpoint. Insights from customer interactions provide real-time intelligence on demand, sizing, and emerging trends, empowering smarter inventory management and more accurate forecasting, reducing waste, and ensuring the right products reach the right customers.

By merging immersive virtual try-ons, intelligent recommendations, and unified data insights, Doris.ai, in collaboration with NVIDIA and Marvik, isn’t just transforming how people shop—they’re delivering innovative solutions for deeply connected, sustainable experiences that delight customers and drive lasting business growth.
