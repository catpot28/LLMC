NVIDIA Jetson

Effortless Lawn Care and Reliable Security

Imagine making your lawn care easier and boosting your home security at the same time. The
EEVE Willow
multi-functional AI-powered personal robot does both, making everyday life a whole lot more simple.

Willow’s self-driving technology and high-performance Toadi mowing tool can cut the wildest grass, and night vision equips the robot to act as a security camera after sunset. The mowing tool is mounted on Willow and follows the robot in its tracks, with embedded AI capabilities to ensure optimum efficiency.

Built on the EEVE Robot Operating System (eOS) and powered by the
NVIDIA Jetson Nano™
platform, Willow uses advanced hardware, customized software, and AI to help customers effortlessly maintain their lawns, protect their properties at night, and so much more.

EEVE

EEVE

Smarter Gardens, Smarter Security

With over 2,500 EEVE robots deployed, the integration of the
NVIDIA Jetson Nano
platform has been crucial to EEVE's success in the personal robotics market. Willow's user-friendly interface, efficient lawn maintenance, and reliable security features have attracted a growing customer base. And the Jetson Nano's AI capabilities let EEVE continuously innovate and expand Willow's functionalities, ensuring a versatile and future-proof solution for customers.

The NVIDIA Jetson Nano module delivers all the processing power needed to drive Willow's AI-driven features. Its edge computing capabilities also enable the robot to make decisions in real time and respond to changes in its environment. GPU acceleration and deep learning frameworks, such as
TensorFlow
, make it easier to develop complex algorithms for computer vision and autonomous navigation. This ensures Willow can adapt to changes, move around obstacles, maintain your garden, guard your property, sweep your patio, transport items, and even get involved in games.

This AI-powered personal robot for lawn care and home security is empowered by:

NVIDIA Jetson Nano

NVIDIA® CUDA® Toolkit

NVIDIA CUDA® Deep Neural Network library (cuDNN)

EEVE Robot Operating System (eOS)

TensorFlow

OpenCV

EEVE

Weeding With Willow

As a pioneering company in personal robotics, EEVE is committed to Willow’s continuous improvement.

The company has already deployed over 2,500 of its flagship product, and is working on expanding its functionalities to include an innovative weeding solution. EEVE’s proprietary eOS ensures seamless integration and operation of a wide variety of AI-driven features.

“NVIDIA's Jetson Nano platform empowers EEVE's Willow to deliver cutting-edge AI capabilities in personal robotics, reshaping lawn care and home security experiences for consumers.”
