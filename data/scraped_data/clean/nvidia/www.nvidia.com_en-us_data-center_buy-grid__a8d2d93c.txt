How to Buy NVIDIA Virtual GPU Solutions

4 Easy Steps

Get Started

Our portfolio of GPU virtualization software products for the enterprise data center includes: NVIDIA Virtual Applications (vApps), NVIDIA Virtual PC (vPC), and NVIDIA RTX Virtual Workstation (vWS).

To run these products, you will need an NVIDIA
®
GPU and virtual GPU software license that addresses your use case.

1

Choose a Virtual GPU Software Product

NVIDIA offers four software products suited for enterprise organizations.

vWS

For professional graphics applications; includes an NVIDIA RTX Enterprise driver.

vPC

For virtual desktops delivering standard PC applications, browser, and multimedia.

vApps

For use with Citrix Virtual Apps or other RDSH solutions like VMware Horizon Apps.

NVIDIA Virtual GPU Packaging, Pricing, and Licensing Guide.

2

Choose the License Model That Fits Your Business

NVIDIA virtual GPU software can be purchased by enterprise customers per concurrent user (CCU) as an annual subscription or perpetual license or per GPU as an annual subscription. With software sold separately from the physical GPU, you have maximum flexibility to deploy on the GPU that is best suited for your data center and can stay current with the latest features and enhancements released throughout the year.

A current SUMs contract (Support, Upgrade, and Maintenance) ensures you have access to technical support – including notification of new software releases, help implementing updates and patches, access to the latest drivers for your apps, and maintenance.

qualified partners
