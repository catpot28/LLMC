What’s Included in This Analyst Report

Generative AI has disrupted businesses of all sizes in all industries around the world, compelling them to shift priorities and redefine goals. Learn how you can accelerate your adoption of generative AI by identifying platforms for different generative AI applications and optimizing them for faster time to market and ROI.

Identify a Path to Generative AI Adoption

Identify the approaches that best fit your generative AI initiatives and the platforms that support building your large language models (LLMs).

Optimize your Generative AI Platform

Understand how generative AI use cases that require custom LLMs can benefit from a hybrid-based infrastructure designed for foundation model customization and multi-node training.

Speed Time
to Market

Compare the limitations of current cloud infrastructures as a service (IaaS) and the benefits of a hybrid-based platform for fast-tracking your generative AI applications.

Discover the Platform Purpose-Built For AI

Gain AI infrastructure at scale with x86-based NVIDIA DGX™, powered by NVIDIA Tensor Core GPUs and Intel® Xeon® CPUs.
