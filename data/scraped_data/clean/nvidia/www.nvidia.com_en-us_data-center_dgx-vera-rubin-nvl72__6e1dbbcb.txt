Overview

Scaling Intelligence in the Age of Agentic AI

With the convergence of pretraining, post-training, and inference-time scaling laws, modern data centers face performance and efficiency bottlenecks when trying to support models that require complex, multi-step chains of thought.

Purpose-built to meet these challenges, NVIDIA DGX™ Vera Rubin NVL72 is a turnkey AI infrastructure solution that delivers leading-edge performance while vastly improving energy efficiency. DGX Vera Rubin NVL72 delivers industry-leading performance per watt and tokens per watt for any AI workload, allowing enterprises to dramatically scale intelligence while improving token economics.

NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD Sets the Stage for Rubin-Based Systems

Announced at CES 2026, NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD
™
serves as a blueprint for deploying AI factories built on the NVIDIA Rubin architecture.

Features

Accelerated Training and Inference for Any AI Model

Maximum Efficiency With NVIDIA Vera Rubin Superchips

DGX Vera Rubin NVL72 pairs the power-efficient
NVIDIA Vera CPU
with
NVIDIA Rubin GPUs
to deliver leading performance per watt. This enables enterprises to scale training and long-context inference within their existing energy footprint, turning the data center into an energy-efficient engine for AI innovation.

The Universal Engine for AI at Scale

NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD
with DGX Vera Rubin NVL72 systems eliminates the guesswork from deploying large-scale AI infrastructure. With a simple, easy-to-deploy rack architecture, it’s designed to take advantage of the latest advancements in data center engineering.

Accelerating Operations With Intelligent Software

NVIDIA Mission Control™
accelerates every aspect of operations, from configuring DGX Vera Rubin NVL72 to integrating with facilities to managing clusters and workloads. Enabled by intelligent, integrated software, enterprises gain control over cooling and power events to redefine infrastructure resiliency.

The Proven Standard for Enterprise AI

Built from the ground up for enterprise AI, the NVIDIA DGX platform, featuring NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD, combines the best of NVIDIA software, infrastructure, and expertise in a modern, unified AI development solution.

NVIDIA DGX Vera Rubin NVL72 Specifications

GPU and CPU

72x NVIDIA Rubin GPUs, 36x NVIDIA Vera CPUs

Total GPU Memory | Bandwidth

20.7 TB | Up to 1,580 TB/s

Total Fast Memory

75 TB

Performance

NVFP4 Inference: 3,600 PFLOPS
NVFP4 Training: 2,520 PFLOPS*
FP8/FP6 Training: 1,260 PFLOPS*

Networking

> 144x OSFP single-port NVIDIA® ConnectX®-9 VPI with 800 Gb/s NVIDIA InfiniBand and Ethernet
> 18x dual-port NVIDIA BlueField®-4 VPI with 400 Gb/s NVIDIA InfiniBand and Ethernet

NVIDIA NVLink™ Switch System

9x L1 NVIDIA NVLink Switches

Management Network

Host baseboard management controller (BMC) with RJ45

Software

NVIDIA Mission Control, NVIDIA AI Enterprise, NVIDIA DGX OS

Enterprise Support

Three years of enterprise business-standard support for hardware and software
