FAQs

What is NVIDIA Innovation Lab?

The NVIDIA Innovation Lab is an exclusive 60-day accelerator program designed specifically for companies in the NVIDIA Inception and Connect ecosystems. Selected participants receive hands-on access to NVIDIA GPUs through a developer-friendly self-serve platform, and the opportunity to explore cutting-edge software frameworks and curated onboarding experiences.

During the 60-day program, participants can rapidly prototype, optimize, and validate their AI innovations, reducing the infrastructure barriers that can slow AI development, enabling teams to focus on breakthrough research and experimentation.

Do Inception and Connect members automatically qualify for this credit?

No, members need to submit an application to be considered for the Innovation Lab. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and approval is subject to availability and fit.

How do I apply for access to the NVIDIA Innovation Lab?

To apply for the Innovation Lab benefit:

In the Inception or Connect Portal, go to “Profile,” then “Add product,” and complete the form (at least one product required).

Navigate to “Benefits,” then “Explore Benefits.” Search for and select the “Innovation Lab” tile.

Click “Request Benefit.” Allow 5–10 seconds for the application page to open in a new tab.

Complete the application form for the Innovation Lab program.

Can I reapply for the NVIDIA Innovation Lab?

Yes, you can reapply if previous applications have not been accepted into the program and at least 3 months have passed since your last submission.

Can I bring my own compute capacity to NVIDIA Innovation Lab?

No, Innovation Lab provides the cloud instance for the 60-day program.
