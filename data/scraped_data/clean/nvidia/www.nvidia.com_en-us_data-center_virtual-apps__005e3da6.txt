Engineered to Take Apps to Bold Places

Creating a user experience where teams can realize the full potential of their tools is critically important to keep your company on top of its game. Elevate productivity with virtual GPUs and NVIDIA Virtual Applications (vApps) software for accelerated application streaming with Remote Desktop Sharing Host (RDSH) solutions, including Citrix Virtual Apps and VMware Horizon vApps. NVIDIA vApps allow users to access any Windows app at full performance on any device, anywhere.

View Document: NVIDIA vApps Solution Overview
(PDF 501 KB)

App Streaming, Elevated

NVIDIA Virtual Apps software delivers an amazing user experience for the digital workplace, so teams can use their apps virtually without losing performance or productivity. With GPU sharing, multiple VMs serving as VDAs can be powered by a single data center GPU to maximize utilization and affordability. With support for all major hypervisor virtualization platforms, vApps enables data center admins can use the same management tools for their GPU-accelerated servers as they do for the rest of their data center.
