Modernizing the Data Center with VMware and NVIDIA

VMware and NVIDIA are working together to transform the modern data center built on VMware Cloud Foundation and bring AI to every enterprise. Utilizing the NVIDIA AI Enterprise suite and NVIDIA’s most advanced GPUs and
data processing units (DPUs)
, VMware customers can securely run modern, accelerated workloads alongside existing enterprise applications on
NVIDIA-Certified Systems
. Our long-standing partnership extends from the on-prem data center to the hybrid cloud, from virtual machines to containers—and this is only the beginning.

Introducing NVIDIA AI Enterprise Software Suite

The NVIDIA AI Enterprise software suite gives IT teams the power to support and manage AI deployments within their virtualized data center infrastructure. Licensed and supported by NVIDIA, the end-to-end suite is certified to run exclusively on VMware vSphere with NVIDIA-Certified Systems.

NVIDIA End User Computing Platform

Powering the Remote Workforce

VMware Horizon, accelerated by
NVIDIA virtual GPU technology
, securely and cost-effectively delivers immersive, graphics-rich virtual desktops and apps that are easily accessible across devices and locations, ensuring that everyone from mobile and knowledge workers to power users and designers can enjoy a graphics experience that’s equivalent to a physical PC or workstation even when they are working remotely on their own devices.

Raising the Bar on Productivity

NVIDIA Virtual PC (vPC)
and Virtual Apps (vApps) are virtualization solutions that deliver a user experience nearly indistinguishable from a native PC. With server-side graphics and comprehensive management and monitoring capabilities integrated with VMware vSphere, NVIDIA vPC future-proofs the VDI environment.
