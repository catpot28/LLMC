SketchUp has emerged as the standard 3D idea sketching tool on both Windows and Mac platforms by offering advanced 3D creation and visualization capabilities via a deceptively simple interface. Sophisticated enough for complex projects yet accessible for beginners, SketchUp allows everyone from architects to hobbyists to design in 3D.
