How many graphics cards can I use with SLI technology in a workstation?
You can use up to two NVIDIA Quadro® FX graphics cards.

Will NVIDIA have OpenGL support for SLI technology?
SLI will support Open GL 1.4, 1.5, 2.0, and all future releases.
Back to top

How does using SLI technology affect application performance?
For applications that are significantly bottlenecked by graphics hardware, SLI technology can nearly double performance. For applications that are less bottlenecked by graphics hardware, SLI technology delivers significant performance gains.
Back to top

What NVIDIA Quadro graphics cards can work together in an SLI configuration?
NVIDIA Quadro FX 5600, 5500, 4600, 4500, 4400, 3500, 3450, 3400, 1500, 1400, 560, 550, 540 and 350 cards are SLI enabled and can work in an SLI configured multi-GPU system. Two identical cards are required for SLI Frame Rendering and SLI FSAA .
Back to top

Does NVIDIA Scene Graph (NVSG) technology take advantage of SLI technology?
Yes.
Back to top

Is SLI technology just for gaming?
No. Many professional applications can take advantage of SLI.
Back to top

Do the SPEC benchmarks validate this performance gain?
SPECviewperf 8 applications scale well with SLI technology. OEMs and system integrators post results that demonstrate these performance gains on www.spec.org.

Does SLI technology work on dual CPUs? What configuration do you recommend?
Yes, SLI technology is supported on a dual processor system. The final configuration will depend on the target applications. For multithreaded applications, a dual processor is preferable.

What is SLI technology for NVIDIA Quadro graphics solutions?

SLI Frame Rendering:
Combines two PCI Express graphics cards with an SLI connector to transparently scale application performance on a single display by presenting them as a single graphics card to the operating system.

Benefits

Visual simulation, broadcast, and video applications that are fill rate–limited will benefit from using the split frame rendering (SFR) mode.

SPECviewperf 8 and other applications that are geometry–limited will benefit from using the alternate frame rendering (AFR) mode.
Requirements:

Two identical NVIDIA Quadro FX 5600, 5500, 4600, 4500, 3500, 3450 or 3400 graphics cards

SLI connector

SLI FSAA
: Combines two PCI Express graphics cards with an SLI connector to transparently increase image quality on a single display by presenting them as a single graphics card to the operating system.

Benefits

Design review, DMU (Digital Mock Up), Visual simulation, and broadcast applications that require a high level of image/line quality will benefit from using the SLI FSAA mode.

Requirements:

Two identical NVIDIA Quadro FX 5600, 5500, 4600, 4500, 3500, 3450 or 3400 graphics cards

SLI connector

SLI Multi View:
Combines the power of two NVIDIA Quadro PCI Express graphics cards to span a single hardware-accelerated OpenGL application window across multiple displays, run a single application per GPU with multiple display outputs, or enable other flexible usage of two PCI Express graphics cards.

Benefits

View professional applications over multiple displays to increase visual real estate. (currently available on Microsoft® Windows® Linux )
Requirements

Two identical NVIDIA Quadro FX 5600, 5500, 4600, 4500, 4400, 3500, 3450, 3400, 1500, 1400, 560, 550, 540 and 350 graphics cards.

What NVIDIA Quadro graphics cards can work together in an SLI Frame Rendering configuration?
The supported models are NVIDIA Quadro FX 5600, 5500, 4600, 4500, 3500, 3450 or 3400 graphics cards.
Back to top

What NVIDIA Quadro graphics cards can work together in an SLI FSAA configuration?

The supported models are NVIDIA Quadro FX 5600, 5500, 4600, 4500, 3500, 3450 or 3400 graphics cards.
Back to top

What NVIDIA Quadro graphics cards can work together in an SLI Multi View configuration?
The supported models are NVIDIA Quadro FX 5600, 5500, 4600, 4500, 4400, 3500, 3450, 3400, 1500, 1400, 560, 550, 540 and 350.
Back to top

How will my application take advantage of the SLI technology?
SLI technology is application transparent and is enabled through the graphics drivers. All OpenGL applications can take advantage of SLI technology. When multiple NVIDIA Quadro FX graphics cards are configured with SLI technology, applications will automatically be able to take advantage of performance improvements with no modifications.
Back to top

What types of output does SLI support?
SLI technology supports both digital and analog output/display.
Back to top

Can I use two NVIDIA Quadro G-Sync or two NVIDIA Quadro FX SDI graphics cards in the same system when using SLI technology?
You can use an NVIDIA Quadro G-Sync card in an SLI configuration with NVIDIA’s latest drivers (rel90 windows, rel85 linux). G-Sync and Stereo is only supported with Multi View mode. The NVIDIA Quadro SDI graphic cards and NVIDIA Quadro FX 4500 X2 do not support SLI technology.
Back to top

Can you connect more than two graphics cards with SLI technology?
Currently, NVIDIA only supports dual GPU scaling.
