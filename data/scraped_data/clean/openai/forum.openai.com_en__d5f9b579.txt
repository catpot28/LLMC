About
The OpenAI Forum is a vibrant global community of experts committed to advancing our mission:
building artificial general intelligence (AGI) that benefits everyone.
Since launching in 2023, the OpenAI Forum has built an ecosystem of collaborators with the intention of forging impactful partnerships, exchanging resources and ideas, and amplifying AI use cases working to solve humanity's most challenging problems.

Membership Criteria
Members
Full members can fully participate in the Forum community—connecting directly with peers, joining discussions, and receiving invitations to exclusive in-person gatherings at OpenAI offices. They’re also visible to other members, can collaborate across disciplines, and help shape the conversations and initiatives that guide OpenAI’s mission.
How to join as a member
Receive an invitation from a current OpenAI Forum member or OpenAI staff
Complete your OpenAI Forum profile and meet the membership criteria outlined below:
Demonstrated expertise in your field or academic discipline
Interested in learning more about the intersection of your domain and AI
