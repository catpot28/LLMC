We're excited to finally announce BYOK for general access. It's a powerful new way to use OpenRouter while leveraging your existing API keys and credits from major providers.

🚀 Key Benefits

Higher Rate Limits: Combine your provider limits with OpenRouter's for increased throughput. Works with free models now as well!

Use 3rd Party Credits: Make the most of your existing credits from OpenAI, Google Cloud, AWS, and dozens of other platforms

Unified Analytics: Track all your LLM usage in one place through our Activity dashboard

BYOK is available for 5% of the upstream provider's cost, while giving you all the other benefits of OpenRouter.
