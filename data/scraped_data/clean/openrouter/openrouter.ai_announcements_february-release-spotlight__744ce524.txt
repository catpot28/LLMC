February Release Spotlight
Our monthly Product Spotlight highlights a few of our biggest releases from the past month.

Benchmarks on Model Pages

Every model page now shows industry-standard benchmark scores powered by Artificial Analysis: programming, math, science, long-context reasoning, and more. See how models actually perform before you pick one. See an
example with Claude Sonnet 4.6

Announcement

Effective Pricing

Model pages now have an Effective Pricing tab showing what you actually pay per provider, including tiered pricing. Full cost transparency before you route. See an
example with GLM-5

Announcement

Rankings & Leaderboard Updates

The Rankings page now includes benchmark scatter charts and expanded tables. Plus, long-context generations are surging. See what's trending for 100K–1M token requests.

Announcement

Free Router

New router: openrouter/free — the easiest way to route to all free LLMs, automatically selected for compatibility with your request. See the
top free models here
