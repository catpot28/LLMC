Simplifying Our Platform Fee
🔄 What’s Changing

We’re removing the fixed $0.35 Stripe fee

For most credit purchases, the total fee will now be lower

Easier to calculate—no more reverse-engineering formulas

💵 New Fee Structure

Non-crypto payments: 5.5% of the order amount, with a minimum fee of $0.80

Crypto payments: 5.0% flat, no minimum fee

BYOK fee: The current 5% usage-based fee will be removed in the future and replaced with a fixed monthly subscription (pricing TBD — suggestions welcome!)
