Trinity Large Thinking is a powerful open source reasoning model from the team at Arcee AI. It shows strong performance in PinchBench, agentic workloads, and reasoning tasks. Launch video:
https://youtu.be/Gc82AXLa0Rg?si=4RLn6WBz33qT--B7
(opens in new tab)

This model is optimized for agentic workflows and performs best when reasoning is preserved (aka interleaved thinking). Learn how to preserve reasoning in our docs:
