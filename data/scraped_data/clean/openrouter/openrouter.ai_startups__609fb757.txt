Why is OpenRouter offering this program for startups?
OpenRouter's goal is to help early-stage teams build AI products without infrastructure constraints. The program allows startups to experiment across models, optimize cost and performance, and scale without vendor lock-in while building on OpenRouter.
What is the offer worth?
Eligible startups can receive up to $3,000 in universal inference credits, usable across 400+ models available on OpenRouter, along with 0% processing fees for 12 months (including both platform fees and BYOK fees).
Who is eligible for the program?
The program is currently open to Pre-Series B startups building AI-native products that are referred by an approved OpenRouter partner. Applicants must meet all eligibility requirements in order to qualify, including having a public company website, a professional company email address, and a valid OpenRouter account with less than $500 in lifetime spend. Each applicant is allowed to receive startup credits once, even if they are affiliated with multiple partners.

Review the full program's terms & conditions here.
How do I apply to receive the offer?
You can apply by submitting the application form on this page. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. If approved, the credits and waived fees will be automatically applied to your OpenRouter account associated with an applicant email, and the applicant will be notified.
Is there any specific deadline to apply?
No. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis. However, program availability may change over time.
Can I bring my own API key to OpenRouter and how is the offer applied?
Yes. OpenRouter supports both OpenRouter credits and the option to bring your own provider keys (BYOK). The cost of using custom provider keys on OpenRouter is 5% of what the same model/provider would cost normally on OpenRouter and is deducted from your OpenRouter credits. This fee is waived for the first 1M BYOK requests per month. However, if your OpenRouter for Startups application is approved, BYOK fees are waived for 12 months.
Do OpenRouter for Startups credits expire?
Yes. If approved, most credits expire 6 months after issuance. You'll receive an email notification about your credit expiration date. Your platform fees will be waived for 12 months.
What happens after I use all my credits or they expire?
Once you've used all your granted credits or they expire—whichever comes first—your account will continue operating under standard OpenRouter pricing. While some models are free to access, you'll need to purchase OpenRouter credits to use certain models. Your processing fees will remain waived for 12 months.
What happens if I need more credits?
You can continue using OpenRouter with standard pay-as-you-go usage.
Can I resell or exchange my credits for cash?
No. Startup credits are non-transferable, cannot be resold, and cannot be exchanged for cash.
Do you sign NDAs before an application is submitted?
No. OpenRouter does not sign NDAs as part of the application process. Please avoid submitting confidential information in your application.
