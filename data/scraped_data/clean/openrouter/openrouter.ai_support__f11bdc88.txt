Getting started
Why should I use OpenRouter?
OpenRouter provides a unified API to access major LLM models, aggregate billing, and track usage with analytics.
We pass through provider pricing while pooling uptime, so you get the same provider rates with a unified API and automatic fallbacks for better reliability.
How do I get started with OpenRouter?
Create an account and add credits on the

Credits

page. Credits are used for LLM inference.
Use the chat room or create API keys and start using the API. See our

quickstart

guide.
How do I get support?
Join our community

Discord

and ask in the #help forum.
For billing and account questions, email

[email protected]
.
How do I get billed for my usage on OpenRouter?
Pricing is per model and may differ for prompt/completion, images, and reasoning tokens. See the model page for details.
We compute cost from provider token usage and deduct it from your credits. Review your history in

Activity
.

usage:

Pricing and Fees
What are the fees for using OpenRouter?
We charge a small fee when you purchase credits. Model inference pricing is passed through from providers without markup.
Crypto payments may include an additional processing fee.
Is there a fee for using my own provider keys (BYOK)?
Using BYOK includes a free monthly request allowance; subsequent usage incurs a small percentage fee based on the equivalent OpenRouter cost. This is deducted from your credits.
Models and Providers
What LLM models does OpenRouter support?
Access a wide variety of models, including frontier models. See the

models browser

or the

models API
.
How frequently are new models added?
We add models regularly, often in partnership with labs. If something’s missing, reach out on

Discord
.
What are model variants?
Variants are suffixes that modify behavior. Static variants are available on specific models, listed in the models API:
:free
– free model with low rate limits
:extended
– longer context length
:thinking
– reasoning by default
Dynamic variants can be used on all models:
:online
– attach web results to the prompt
:nitro
– optimize for faster throughput
:floor
– prioritize lowest price
I am an inference provider, how can I get listed on OpenRouter?
See

provider requirements
. For contact, email is best.
What is the expected latency/response time for different models?
Model pages show latency and throughput by provider. Use
:nitro
to route to faster providers.
How does model fallback work if a provider is unavailable?
OpenRouter automatically falls back to the next provider on errors. Configure provider routing in

documentation
.
API Technical Specifications
What authentication methods are supported?
Cookie-based auth for web/chatroom, API keys for completions and core endpoints, and management API keys for administrative actions.
How are rate limits calculated?
Free model rate limits depend on your purchased credits. See details in the

rate limits documentation
.
What API endpoints are available?
We implement OpenAI-compatible
/completions
and

/chat/completions
, plus
/api/v1/models
. See the

API docs
.
What are the supported formats?
Text and images are supported. Images can be URLs or base64. PDF and other file types are coming soon.
How does streaming work?
Streaming uses SSE for real-time token delivery. Set
stream: true
.
What SDK support is available?
OpenRouter is a drop-in replacement for OpenAI; most OpenAI SDKs work. See

SDK docs
.
Privacy and Data Logging
See our

Terms of Service

and

Privacy Policy
.
What data is logged during API use?
We log basic request metadata (timestamps, model, token counts). Prompts and completions are not logged by default.
You can opt in to log prompts/completions for a small discount via

privacy settings
.
What data is logged during Chatroom use?
Conversations are stored locally on your device and do not sync across devices. You can export/import conversations from chatroom settings.
What third-party sharing occurs?
Requests are proxied to model providers. We aim to avoid provider logging or training by default; routing respects your privacy settings. If no providers match, the request will error.
Credit and Billing Systems
What purchase options exist?
OpenRouter uses a credit system denominated in USD. You can top up manually or enable auto top up.
Do credits expire?
Unused credits may expire after one year per our

terms
.
My credits haven’t showed up in my account
For Stripe purchases, allow up to one hour. If no charge/receipt appears, your card may have been declined; try a different method.
If charged but credits are missing, email

[email protected]

with purchase details. For crypto payments, email support as well.
What’s the refund policy?
Refunds for unused credits may be requested within 24 hours of purchase via the refund button on the

Credits

page. Platform fees are non-refundable. Cryptocurrency payments are not refundable.
How to monitor credit usage?
Use the

Activity

page or the

credits API

for balance and remaining credits.
What free tier options exist?
New users receive a small free allowance to test OpenRouter. Many free models are available but have low rate limits and are not suitable for production. Rate limits increase with purchased credits.
How do volume discounts work?
We don’t currently offer volume discounts. If you have an exceptional use case, contact us.
What payment methods are accepted?
Major credit cards, AliPay, and USDC crypto. PayPal is in progress. For other methods, reach out on

Discord
.
How does OpenRouter make money?
We charge a small fee when purchasing credits. We never mark up provider pricing.
