Describe the Bug

When I update the auto recharge, it requires a minimum of $50 now. Used to accept $5.

Now when I switch auto to off, then click Save, it cannot save, always shows and error and the form setting is not updated.

https://console.perplexity.ai/group/xxxxxxx/billing

Seems like three issues:

Minimum recharge for me is now $50

Cannot switch auto recharge off (error on save, settings not updated)

Auto recharged another $50 when my balance dropped by a tiny amount ~S0.50

Expected Behavior

I can set minimum balance of $5 before recharge

I can switch off auto recharge

Actual Behavior

Error on save when set auto recharge to off

Error message when enter $2 or $5 as minimum recharge “
Amount too low. Please enter an amount of at least $50.”

Steps to Reproduce

Login to
https://console.perplexity.ai/

Click on Billing, then

Click “Change preferences”, then

Update preferences per above, then

Click Save,

Error message displayed top right of screen “Error. Please try again.”
