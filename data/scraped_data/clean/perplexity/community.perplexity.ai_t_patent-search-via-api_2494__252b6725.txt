– Retrieve actual patent documents (USPTO / EPO / WIPO IDs) rather than synthesized summaries.

Artifact name

Patent Reality Layer (PRL)

Category

AI-assisted patent validation and grounding layer

Problem it solves

General-purpose AI search systems frequently hallucinate patent numbers, misattribute assignees, or conflate jurisdictions. This creates downstream risk for IP teams, researchers, startups, and students who rely on early-stage patent discovery.

What the artifact is

Patent Reality Layer is a visual and conceptual artifact that represents a safety boundary between generative AI and authoritative patent sources. It formalizes a workflow where AI is used for interpretation and synthesis, while patent existence and metadata are verified against real registries.

Core functions (conceptual)

Separates generation from verification

Forces grounding against authoritative patent databases

Flags unverifiable or ambiguous patent claims

Prevents hallucinated citations from entering workflows

Makes AI-assisted patent research auditable

Intended use cases

IP team early-stage patent landscaping

Academic or student patent research tools

Startup novelty checks before attorney review

AI search products that need legal-grade trust boundaries

Teaching responsible AI use in IP contexts

What it is not

Not a patent database

Not a legal opinion

Not a replacement for patent attorneys

Not dependent on any single AI vendor

Why it matters now

As AI search expands faster than verification infrastructure, patent hallucinations become a silent failure mode. This artifact makes that failure mode visible, explicit, and correctable.

Status

Conceptual artifact + visual system

Deployable today as:

Product concept

UX metaphor

Investor or internal alignment asset

Community signal for unmet API demand
