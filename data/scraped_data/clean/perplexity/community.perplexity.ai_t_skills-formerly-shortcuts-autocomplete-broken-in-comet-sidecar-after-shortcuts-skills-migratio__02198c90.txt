Description:

After Perplexity migrated “Shortcuts” to “Skills”, the
/
autocomplete in the Comet browser sidecar no longer works as expected.

Steps to reproduce:

Open any YouTube video in Comet browser

Open the Comet sidecar (side panel)

Click on the query input field

Type
/
or start typing
/video...

Expected behavior:

A dropdown list of saved Skills appears with autocomplete suggestions, allowing the user to select a skill (e.g.
/video-techniques
) without typing the full name.

Actual behavior:

No dropdown appears. No autocomplete suggestions. The only way to trigger a Skill is to type the full exact name manually — which defeats the purpose of the feature, especially since users can’t remember exact names after the migration.

I have 11 Skills saved (visible at perplexity.ai/account/shortcuts):
/top-10
,

,

,

,

,

,

,
/teach-me-comet

This issue appeared after the Shortcuts → Skills migration

The banner at perplexity.ai/account/shortcuts confirms the migration happened but autocomplete was not preserved
