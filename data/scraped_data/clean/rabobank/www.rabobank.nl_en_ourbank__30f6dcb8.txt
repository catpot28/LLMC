Our bank
The cooperative Rabobank

Welcome to the cooperative Rabobank
We are a bank without shareholders, but with members. More than 2,3 million of our customers are already members. We like to think of them as our ears and eyes. They mean so much to us because they tell us what’s going on and what people’s concerns are . Their input helps us know where we should invest our knowledge, time, and money. That is what makes the cooperative Rabobank our bank. Read more about how our customers can become members and how members can reinforce our local Member Councils.

How we know what's happening
We believe that every voice counts. That’s why we are in touch with our almost 9 million customers every day. We have special teams who can help people with financial worries and mobile teams who can help the elderly with their banking business. We even have our own Dialogue Expertise Center which talks with thousands of people about financial and societal themes throughout the entire year.
Members enjoy even shorter lines of communication and are more closely involved in determining the bank’s course. Does that sound appealing? We warmly invite everyone to become a member. Membership is free of charge and easy to apply for in our banking app.

Our engaged members
Our members are represented across all parts and layers of society. They are like our ears and eyes in the world. Using the Rabo app they can help decide which clubs, teams, and associations we support in every community. In this way, our members make a real contribution to their communities.
Rabo &Co
Rabo &Co is our member magazine. Members receive Rabo &Co free of charge. It is published three times a year and is filled with inspiring stories. In every issue, we highlight special people and initiatives in your region through word and image. The magazine also covers social topics and financial matters. Each region has its own magazine.

The voice of our member councils
Every local Rabobank has its own Member Council. Combined, the Member Councils are the heart of our cooperative—an essential link between the members and our bank. The Member Councils are our sounding board, our ambassadors, and our sparring partners.
We also have youth councils. Our Member Councils determine what we will invest our cooperative dividend on—that is, the part of our profits that we invest back into Dutch society each year. In this way, our members really make a difference in their communities. In 2023, we were able to offer all communities an extra sustainability contribution. Local Member Councils played a crucial role in that process as well.
We make an impact together
Positive results in 2023 have enabled Rabobank to offer more to society. In 2023, Rabobank made EUR 400 million available for our customers, members, partners and employees to help achieve the transition to a more sustainable future. EUR 360 million has been reserved for five extra cooperative sustainability initiatives, each of which addresses different challenges and needs. The other EUR 40 million will go into our bank’s cooperative dividend and be ploughed back into Dutch society.
Cooperative dividend
Our bank has members, not shareholders. And our members help decide how we allocate what we call our “cooperative dividend”—the part of our profits that we invest back into society. In 2023, more than EUR 40 million of our cooperative dividend was spent on projects and initiatives in local communities, Rabo ClubSupport and the Rabo Foundation. This is one way we are improving livability in your neighborhood.
Our cooperative initiatives
Cooperative Insulation Budget
Having more sustainable homes helps make the world a better place. So in March 2024, Rabobank started the Cooperative Insulation Budget. Customers who have a mortgage with us and a home that has a “G” or “F” energy label are eligible for a free energy consultation and EUR 1,000 to insulate their homes. Mortgage customers can still apply for this initiative.
SME Sustainability Contribution
When it comes to making the Netherlands more sustainable, small and medium enterprises are an important link in the chain. The SME Sustainability Contribution can help them on their way. Customers can get 12.5% of their costs (up to a maximum of € 10,000) back, for example, when they buy electrical/electronic company assets, solar panels, a heat pump, or insulation. SMEs can still register to avail of this offer.
Rabo Transition Fund
Ambitious sustainability plans are not always easy to finance. The Rabo Transition Fund, a fund of the Nationaal Groenfonds, helps make sustainability ambitions a reality. We support our customers with attractive additional funding for plans they have relating to sustainable energy, the food transition, and circularity. The first projects have already received funding. Interested customers can still apply.
Participate and win in the 'Rabo Sportactie'
Playing sports can win you friends. And maybe one day, gold! For that reason, ‘Rabo Sportactie’ is happy to contribute EUR 125 towards the sports club membership fees of youth aged 10 to 20. It’s a one-off payment for young people who play for teams or associations that are registered with NOC*NSF (the umbrella organization for sports in the Netherlands) through a sports federation. Find out if you’re eligible for the ‘Rabo SportAcie’ and start your registration in the Rabo App or with Rabo Online Banking under “Products” > Rabo Sportactie.
Rabo ClubSupport
Everybody deserves to be part of a club. It should be like a second home. A place to meet each other and make each other stronger, where you can truly be yourself. In a nutshell, we can’t do without clubs and associations. That’s why we are delighted to support them with knowledge, networks, and funding to achieve their ambitions.
