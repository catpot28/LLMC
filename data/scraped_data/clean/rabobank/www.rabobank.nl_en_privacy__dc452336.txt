Privacy and your personal data
You share all kinds of personal data with us. For example, when you visit our website or when you bank online. We process this data carefully and we want to inform you clearly about this.
Privacy and cookies
In our Privacy Statement we explain how we handle the processing of your personal data and what rights you have. Examples are given within the privacy statement. Furthermore, you will find more information about the use of cookies on our website and how you can exercise your rights.

Frequently asked questions about privacy

What can Rabobank use my data for?
We can process your personal data for various purposes. We process data because you want to become a customer of ours and if you want to purchase a product or service from us. We conduct customer research. Or if we are legally obliged to do so. For example, if we have to pass on your details to the tax authorities. We also process your data to prevent payment data fraud. Or if we want to approach you for marketing purposes. In chapter 6 of the Privacy Statement, you can read exactly for what purposes and on what basis we process your personal data.

Read the Privacy Statement

With what parties does Rabobank share my data?
We only share your data with others if there is a good reason or obligation to do so. For example, we can share your personal data with other divisions within the Rabobank Group. We also pass on your data to other parties outside Rabobank. Consider, for example, sharing data with the government, companies we work with or other financial service providers. In the Privacy Statement you can read when and under what conditions we share your data.

Read the Privacy Statement

Why does Rabobank use cookies?
Rabobank uses cookies and similar techniques to make the website work properly and securely. We also use cookies to be able to better inform you.

What if I have a question or complaint about the processing of my personal data?
Do you have a general question or complaint about the processing of personal data? Then contact your local bank. Are you dissatisfied with the way in which your question or complaint has been handled by us? Then you can contact our
Data Protection Officer
. Of course, you can also submit your question or complaint to the
Dutch Data Protection Authority
.
