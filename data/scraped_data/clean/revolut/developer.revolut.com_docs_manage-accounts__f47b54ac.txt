Introduction to the Business API
Introduction to the Business API
Save time and reduce costs and errors with our Business API.
Automate your business processes: view accounts, add counterparties, make payments, exchange currency, and more.
Sign up
Sign up for a Revolut Business account.
Get started
Get started with the Business API in a few steps.
API reference
Check out the technical details to our Business API.
Use cases
Check how our Business API can help you with everyday tasks.
Reconcile accounts and transactions
Automatically sync account balances and transaction details to simplify reconciliation and get real-time visibility into your finances in your own systems.
Set up smart, flexible payouts that run on autopilot
Automate your payout workflows and tailor them to your needs. Send money via link, make instant payments, or schedule and draft payments for approval — to Revolut accounts, external bank accounts, or credit and debit cards.
Sync expenses and receipts
Automate expense and receipt syncing to keep your systems up to date and reduce manual effort.
Control access to your business finances
Give your team the access they need – no more, no less. Use roles and permissions to stay in control of who can view, move, or manage money, helping you reduce risk and streamline financial operations as your business grows.
Validate account names
Validate a counterparty's account name before you add a new counterparty or make a payment. Available only in the UK.
Exchange money
Get currency exchange rates and exchange money using our API.
