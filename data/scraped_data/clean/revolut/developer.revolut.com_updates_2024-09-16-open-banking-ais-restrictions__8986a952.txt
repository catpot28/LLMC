Restrictions on AIS endpoints
September 16, 2024
·
One min read
Revolut
Revolut team
Breaking Change
Several endpoints will no longer be accessible after the first 5 minutes from PSU authorisation.
This will affect both existing and new consents.

As of
18 November 2024
, AIS endpoints will only be accessible 5 minutes after PSU has authorised the consent.
After these 5 minutes, an HTTP
403
error will be returned.

This restriction does not apply to the Balances and Transactions endpoints.

GET /account-access-consents/{ConsentId}
is not affected by this change.

Affected endpoints
​

GET /accounts

GET /accounts/{AccountId}

GET /accounts/{AccountId}/beneficiaries

GET /accounts/{AccountId}/direct-debits

GET /accounts/{AccountId}/standing-orders
