Open Banking API SCA restrictions update
February 3, 2025
·
One min read
Revolut
Revolut team
On 10 February 2025, we'll add the
/accounts
endpoint to the list of SCA-exempt endpoints.

As a reminder
On 18 November 2024,
we introduced restrictions
which limited access to several Account Information Service (AIS) endpoints to 5 minutes after obtaining the user’s Strong Customer Authentication (SCA), with the
/balances
and
/transactions
sub-endpoints exempt from those restrictions.

What's changing?
​

We have taken your feedback into account and reassessed these restrictions.
As a result, on 10 February 2025, we'll add the
/accounts
endpoint to the list of SCA-exempt endpoints.

This means that as of 10 February, the following endpoints will be accessible beyond the 5-minute window after SCA:

/accounts

/accounts/*/transactions

/accounts/*/balances

Note that this change will affect both new and existing consents.
