Royal Smilde Foods

Creating flavour in retail, foodservice and industry

Salads, sauces and spreads, cooking, baking and spreading, quiches and pies. In Heerenveen and Oosterwolde, we work every day on sustainable, innovative products for our customers, differentiating ourselves precisely by doing things differently.

Our extensive experience ensures we are a valued sparring partner for our customers. We are in tune with their thinking, and actively respond to new developments.

Brands and private label

You may not be aware of it, but there'll more than likely be a Royal Smilde Foods product in your shopping trolley every time you visit the supermarket. That's because we are specialists in supplying private label solutions to well-known national and international supermarket chains and restaurant formats. We also focus on an international market. From Belgium, Germany and France to England, Asia and West Africa. In a nutshell: you'll find Royal Smilde Foods everywhere.
