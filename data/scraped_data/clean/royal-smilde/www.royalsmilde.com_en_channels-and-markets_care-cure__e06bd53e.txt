Care & Cure

Health, physical and mental well-being and vitality are key aspects of our society. Royal Smilde responds to these health-related subjects with a broad product range. For example, we exploit the positive properties of vegetable oil, and functional ingredients such as added vitamin D and nutritional proteins. Many of our products are free from artificial additives and the most common allergens, including cows' milk protein and lactose.
