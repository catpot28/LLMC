Your own private label?

With the private label solutions of Smilde Homebaking, we help to develop customised baking mixes. We would like to work with you on reducing the content of sugar, fat and salt in the recipe and plastic reduction in cartons. Thus we arrive at a specially formulated baking mix for you that is ready for the future!

Featured

Our pancake mix in a gable-top carton is an innovative and sustainable alternative in baking mixes. For instance, we use 87% less plastic in the gable-top carton in comparison with a plastic shaker.

About Smilde Homebaking

Smilde Homebaking falls under Royal Smilde Bakery, established in 2004, and is a renowned producer of baking mixes. We produce baking mixes for the Dutch and international markets. Our production site is in Roden (Drenthe, the Netherlands). We have an extensive R&D team that is aware of the latest developments and trends in baking mixes. With this knowledge we can give you appropriate advice to provide for your wishes and needs.
