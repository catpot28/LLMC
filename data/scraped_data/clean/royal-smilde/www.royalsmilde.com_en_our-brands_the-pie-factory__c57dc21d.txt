The Pie Factory

Savoury tarts based on French quiche or English pie in contemporary flavours.

Our products

A complete, delicious and ready-made fresh meal size pie hot from the oven, or a tasty savoury snack. Our quiches and pies are available in a choice of formats and different flavours. We prepare our quiches and pies using fresh pastry and fresh ingredients. Our savoury tarts are entirely shaped, filled, baked, chilled or frozen, and packaged in line.
