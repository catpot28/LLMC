Quiche and Pie

Royal Smilde bakes quiches and savoury tarts, as well as traditional English pies in a wide variety of flavours, combinations and recipes. We produce our quiches and pies from start to finish starting with our own pastry through to packaging

as required, on one of the most advanced production lines in Europe.

Our quiches and pies can be supplied either chilled or frozen.
