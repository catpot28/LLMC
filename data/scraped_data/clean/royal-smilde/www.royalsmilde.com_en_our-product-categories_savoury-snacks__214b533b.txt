Savoury snacks

Royal Smilde supplies the most delicious savoury snacks in more than 60 countries. Our extensive range brings you a wide selection of dough types, fillings and shapes. In addition to our own brands, Royal Smilde also has a number of co-branding success stories in the savoury snacks segment. Examples include Beemster cheese rolls and the vegetarian range in collaboration with The Vegetarian Butcher.
