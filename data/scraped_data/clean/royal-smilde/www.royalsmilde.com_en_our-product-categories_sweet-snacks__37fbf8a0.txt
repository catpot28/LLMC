Sweet snacks

Royal Smilde supplies a broad range of sweet snacks under the Pruvé brand and with various private label products. These products enable you to offer a delicious sweet snack that's perfect in-between meals or with coffee. Examples include apple turnovers, apricot turnovers and filled cake.

By continually delivering quality and developing innovative flavours,

Royal Smilde is a major player in this segment in Europe and beyond.
