Quality assurance at the highest level

Royal Smilde has a clear vision of quality; quality means meeting the customer's expectations and wishes. The product of course also has to be visually appealing, taste good, be healthy where applicable and, most importantly, it has to comply with food safety requirements. Royal Smilde uses a digital quality and HACCP system that complies with the most stringent market requirements.
