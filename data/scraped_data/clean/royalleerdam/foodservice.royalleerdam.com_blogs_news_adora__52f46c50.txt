Adora

The name of this adorable series is well
chosen. ADORA, derived from the Latin word
‘adoro’ means ‘worship’, ‘adore’, and
‘honor’.
​

​
The ADORA range has a lovely,
classical
appeal
.
With the elegant shape of the bowl
in combination with an
eye-catching ribbed
effect
this will be a distinctive addition to
your stemware.
​

​
The elegant bowl shape was specifically
designed to allow the wine to breathe to
bring out its aroma and bouquet.

​

​
The range includes 2 wine glasses 50cl and
38cl, a champagne flute 21cl a coupe 30cl and a matching
tumbler 44cl.

shape.
