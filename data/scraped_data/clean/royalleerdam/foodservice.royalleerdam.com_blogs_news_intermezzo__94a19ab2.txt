Intermezzo

Royal Leerdam and INTERMEZZO are inseparable. Both are Dutch originals and they share a long and successful history. An excellent choice for serving any other liqueur or vodka besides jenever, Intermezzo is a speciality glass that every bartender should have in his arsenal. Also a perfect choice for serving sake and gin.
