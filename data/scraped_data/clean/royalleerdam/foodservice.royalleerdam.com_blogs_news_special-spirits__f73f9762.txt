Special Spirits

The SPECIALS SPIRITS collection is specially designed for professional use in any foodservice area that serves special drinks. The range complements any of our wine ranges. The design of these glasses is based on the ideal design for professional use. New in the range are the Brandy, Grappa and Whiskey/Rum. With their angled bowl these glasses are the perfect modern addition to your spirits collection. Furthermore, the Grappa and Whiskey/Rum glass feature an elegant 7mm tall stem which makes them very elegant.

Elegant shapes

Optimal enjoyment of Brandy, Grappa and more.
