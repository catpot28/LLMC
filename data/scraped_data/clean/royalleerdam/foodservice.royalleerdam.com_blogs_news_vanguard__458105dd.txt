Vanguard

The VANGUARD series offers a unique drinks presentation thanks to the tulip-shaped bowl and tall stem. It's 125mm tall stem gives the VANGUARD family a true presence while the graciously curved bowl ensures the glasses do not dominate the table setting. It’s therefore well suited for the fine dining segment.
