Glass Handling Guide

Glassware in
one
of the most important tools used in the hospitality industry. It is also one of the hardest materials around, yet under certain conditions it can also be fragile. The number one reason for glass breakage is improper handling. This guide presents you and your staff with insights into how to minimize breakage through proper handling of you glassware. By applying these tips you can save 20-30% on glassware costs.
