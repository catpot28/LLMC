What is SAP Customer Appreciation Network?
SAP Customer Appreciation Network is a program designed to connect SAP customers with one another and our organization while providing exclusive professional development opportunities, access to key members of the SAP team, and benefits along the way.

Expand your network
Meet industry peers, exchange ideas, and connect with other members of SAP Customer Appreciation Network.

Follow your passion
Chart your own path. Whether you have your sights set on the main stage or prefer to contribute behind the scenes, it’s easy to connect.

Unlock exclusive perks
We love rewarding our members! Join the network and gain access to special privileges.

Stay in the know
Track your progress with our new member portal. Log in anytime to see your activity and access your exclusive benefits—all in one place.

Your network will grow with you
With SAP Customer Appreciation Network, you’re stepping into a space where your interests, connections, and rewards grow with you.

You can choose how you engage with the program and your peers, earn points with activities, and redeem rewards for yourself and your team.

Earn your way from Innovator to Visionary, and finally to Icon—each level unlocking even more premium benefits.

Interested in joining SAP Customer Appreciation Network?
If you enjoy sharing your success with SAP solutions, there are a variety of ways to get involved.

There’s an activity for everyone: tell your story online, speak at events, or connect with prospective customers. Not a fan of public speaking? No problem— engage on social media, write software reviews, or provide feedback through surveys or customer sessions.

Join us and start making an impact today!

Frequently asked questions
Program overview
Why did SAP launch this program?
We created SAP Customer Appreciation Network to provide a modern, unified way for customers to engage with SAP, be recognized for their contributions, and access a wide range of advocacy opportunities—like speaking at events, sharing a success story, or providing feedback on SAP innovations.

How is this different from previous SAP reference programs?
SAP Customer Appreciation Network gives you more flexibility and transparency. You can choose how and when to participate, see your progress in a members-only portal, and earn rewards for a wider variety of activities—not just reference activities.

Membership and participation
Who can join SAP Customer Appreciation Network?
Any SAP customer is welcome to join. Simply register via our online portal by
clicking here
.

Can I invite my colleagues to join?
Yes. We encourage you to invite colleagues who also use SAP products to join. There's even a portal activity for doing just that—and it lets you earn points.

Can I stay in the program if I change companies?
Yes. If you move to a new company, you can update your profile to reflect your new role and continue participating.

Can I still participate if I work in a regulated industry and can’t accept gifts?
Yes. Even if your company policy restricts physical rewards, you can still take part in activities, earn recognition, and receive non-monetary benefits, such as digital badges.

Advocacy activities
What types of activities can I participate in?
Activities include speaking at events, providing peer references, sharing testimonials, participating in surveys, writing product reviews, and more. You’ll see personalized opportunities when you log into the portal.

Can I connect with other SAP customers through SAP Customer Appreciation Network?
Yes. As a member, you’ll have access to a private community of advocates and opportunities to join exclusive virtual events and innovation sessions.

Rewards and recognition
What kinds of rewards can I earn?
Rewards across three different membership tiers include discounts on SAP Learning Hub, passes to SAP events, charitable donation opportunities, exclusive experiences, SAP swag, and more.

How do the program tiers work?
As you earn points, your points help you climb through three membership tiers: Innovator, Visionary, and Icon.

Can I see how many points I’ve earned?
Yes. When logged into the portal, you’ll see both your current and lifetime points, as well as how many points are needed to reach the next tier.

What happens to my points if I don’t use them?
Points will remain active until January 1, 2027, allowing participants time to build progress and move up through the program tiers. Following the points reset on January 1, 2027, points will reset each calendar year, but your tier status will carry into the following year.

Where can I see the benefits for each tier?
Log into SAP Customer Appreciation Network and click on “My Rewards” to see the benefits available at your current tier, as well as how to unlock the next level.

Support and feedback
How can I give feedback about SAP Customer Appreciation Network?
You can provide feedback within the portal or reach out to us directly at sapcustomerappreciationnetwork@mail.sap.com.

I’m having issues logging into my SAP SSO account. How do I resolve them?
Please reach out to us directly at sapcustomerappreciationnetwork@mail.sap.com and we’d be happy to assist.
