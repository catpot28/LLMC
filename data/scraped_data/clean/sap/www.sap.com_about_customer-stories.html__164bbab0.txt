FAQs
What is SAP software used for in companies?

Companies that use SAP are often looking to streamline operations, improve decision-making, and drive business growth. SAP solutions integrate critical business functions—including finance, supply chain, manufacturing, and HR—into a single system for real-time access to data from across the enterprise.

What companies use SAP?
SAP serves organizations of all sizes and industries, from fast-growing midsize businesses to some of the world's largest enterprises. Companies in manufacturing, retail, healthcare, financial services, energy, and many other sectors rely on SAP to manage complex business processes, maintain regulatory compliance, and accelerate digital transformation. Whether optimizing supply chains or enhancing customer experiences, companies that use SAP appreciate its flexibility, scalability, and industry-specific solutions.

What is the total number of companies using SAP?

SAP is trusted by more than 320,000 businesses across 180+ countries.

How many companies using SAP are in the Fortune 500?

SAP is the backbone of many of the world's most successful companies. Over 90% of Fortune 500 companies use SAP solutions to manage key business processes, enhance operational efficiency, and gain a competitive edge through data-driven decision-making.
