SAP Americas Management Team

Regional Management
SAP Americas oversees the company’s business operations in North and South America.

Jan Gilg
Chief Revenue Officer, Americas & Global Business Suite
Jan Gilg is responsible for the strategy, day-to-day operations, and overall customer success in North and South America.

Learn more about Jan Gilg

John Gurski
SAP Business Suite Leader, SAP Americas
John Gurski heads SAP's Business Suite operations across the Americas, with a mission to strengthen customer relationships, expedite the adoption of SAP innovations, and accelerate regional growth.

Learn more about John Gurski

Juliet Randall
Chief Marketing Officer,
SAP Americas
Juliet Randall leads marketing for the SAP Americas region, leading strategic marketing teams across North America, Latin America, and the Caribbean and enabling seamless alignment from digital engines to field activation.

Learn more about Juliet Randall

Megan Smith
Head of Human Resources
Megan Smith provides HR leadership across the region, with a focus on the company’s overall people strategy.

Learn more about Megan Smith

Sonja Simon
Chief Financial Officer
Sonja Simon is responsible for driving SAP’s commercial support, operational excellence, and finance transformation in the region.

Learn more about Sonja Simon

Mary Beth Hanss
General Counsel
Mary Beth Hanss is general counsel for SAP in North America. In this role, she manages the legal and contracts department supporting all business in the U.S. and Canada.

Learn more about Mary Beth Hanss

Yaad Oren
Managing Director, SAP Labs US, and Global Head of SAP Research & Innovation
Yaad Oren is responsible for the overall cross-company collaboration of U.S.-based employees developing cloud solutions.

Learn more about Yaad Oren

Dennison John
Managing director of SAP Labs Latin America VP & Head of Support Center Network, SAP Americas
Dennison John ensures R&D success for the LAC region by integrating and collaborating with the different lines of business in SAP Latin America.

Learn more about Dennison John

Cindy Fagen
Managing Director SAP Labs Canada
Cindy Fagen is Managing Director of SAP Labs Canada where she leads the strategic direction of SAP’s product development and innovation hubs in Vancouver, Montreal, and Waterloo.

Learn more about Cindy Fagen
