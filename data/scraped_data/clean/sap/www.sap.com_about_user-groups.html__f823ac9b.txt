SAP User Groups

Exchange ideas, information, and experiences in SAP user groups

Connect with SAP customers and industry and technology experts from SAP and our partners through SAP user groups. Supported by the Global User Groups organization, these independent, not-for-profit groups educate members, facilitate customer involvement, and influence SAP strategy by:

Enabling SAP and SAP users to exchange information of mutual interest and value.
Giving SAP users an opportunity to share experiences, knowledge, and ideas.
Providing SAP with user feedback in all technical and functional areas of interest.

Find your local SAP User Group

Our Vision and Mission
Our Vision
To ensure all customers have the opportunity to participate in a network of vibrant, meaningful, and growing user group communities.

As a member of an SAP User Group, you can easily:

Share information and receive education about SAP products, solutions, technologies, and services.
Network with peers.
Learn how to optimize your SAP investment.
Help influence SAP’s future product direction.

Our Mission
To advocate on behalf of both SAP and SAP customers by establishing a strategic partnership with SAP user groups worldwide.

The Global User Groups organization helps SAP user groups:

Craft and execute short- and long-term action plans focused on future direction and growth.
Communicate with SAP stakeholders on matters related to SAP's future product direction.
Showcase the latest SAP innovations through events, forums, and direct customer contact.

Experience the benefits of membership in your local SAP User Group
Maximize the return on your investment in SAP software by connecting with local customers and industry and technology experts. Find local SAP User Groups by region to exchange ideas, learn about educational events, and keep up with the latest innovations for your specific business needs.

Get connected with other user groups to engage in an open dialogue between members and SAP. The SAP user group executive network (SUGEN) includes leaders from multiple user groups worldwide who are focused on driving the market toward excellence and innovation.

Knowledge transfer for SAP user groups
Set the cornerstone for your success by attending webinars dedicated to SAP user groups. SAP is renowned for its vast product, solution, and services portfolio across all industries and lines of businesses. These Knowledge Transfer for SAP User Groups offerings provide:

A big picture overview of SAP’s portfolio, diving into details as we go along.
The latest information on roadmaps, technical and business updates, etc.
A showcase of the latest product innovations.
Unique overviews on strategic topics, end-to-end, with eBooks.
Mark your calendar and attend a session live, or listen to a recording and view the presentations when it is more convenient for you.
