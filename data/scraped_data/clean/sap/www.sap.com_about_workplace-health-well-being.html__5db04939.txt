SAP Global Mindfulness Practice
Mindfulness is the self-regulation of attention with an attitude of curiosity, openness, and acceptance.

Inspire personal inner growth and enable people to thrive in the future of work
The SAP Global Mindfulness Practice shares SAP's own experience in establishing mindfulness-based emotional intelligence programs in global organizations.

This initiative models mindfulness best practices to inspire inner growth to create a thriving work environment with meaningful connections. SAP Global Mindfulness Practice can help:

Leaders solve complex problems.
Build resilient teams in times of change and transformation.
Support personal development in the workplace.
Enhance self-awareness and emotional intelligence.
Foster compassion and authenticity.

Unlock your employee’s potential

Help ensure your workforce is thriving and resilient in a rapidly-changing global economy with experts from the SAP Global Mindfulness Practice.

SAP Mindful Practice - 3 Breaths
Learn how to take three mindful breaths to refresh, to be more present, to get off autopilot and to make a choice about what to do next.

SAP Global Mindful Practice - Shift to Connection
Learn how to build a connection with a person for whom you feel little empathy and create within three breaths a positive domino effect that makes cooperation much easier.
