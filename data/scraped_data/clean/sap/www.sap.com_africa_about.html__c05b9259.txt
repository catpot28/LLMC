African SAP User Group
Embark on a transformative journey with the African SAP User Group (AFSUG), an independent, non-profit organisation dedicated to championing the interests of SAP customers and partners across the diverse landscapes of Africa.

AFSUG is more than a network; it is a dynamic community fostering collaboration, knowledge exchange, and shared experiences. As ONE VOICE of SAP stakeholders, AFSUG propels discussions on best practices, facilitating a collective influence that accelerates business implementations. Our Special Interest Groups (SIG’s) deliver expert content from fellow customers, SAP partners, and SAP itself, ensuring a rich tapestry of insights.

Join AFSUG to amplify your SAP knowledge through engaging community events, both in-person and online. Whether you prefer webinars, workshops, or face-to-face gatherings, AFSUG offers a platform for meaningful connections and invaluable learnings.
