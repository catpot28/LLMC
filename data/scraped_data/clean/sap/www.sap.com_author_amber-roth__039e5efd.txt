As Vice President of Global Presales & Strategy for SAP Fieldglass, Amber Roth leads a team of sales engineers dedicated to helping organizations optimize their external workforce programs. With over 14 years of experience in sales engineering, she brings deep expertise in aligning technology with customer needs and solving complex workforce challenges. Amber oversees global presales strategy and works closely with customers and prospects to showcase how SAP Fieldglass solutions, particularly in Contingent Labor and Services Procurement (SOW), drive measurable improvements in spend optimization, compliance, quality, and visibility.

Known for her collaborative leadership, customer-first mindset, and strategic vision, Amber serves as a bridge between product management, integration, and implementation teams to ensure customer feedback informs SAP’s innovation roadmap. Her mission is to elevate sales engineering as a growth driver and empower organizations to manage their external workforce with greater agility and intelligence.
