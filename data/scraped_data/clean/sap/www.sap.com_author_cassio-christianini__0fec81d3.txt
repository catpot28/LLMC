Cássio Christianini is the Head of AI for SAP Latin America & Caribbean, where he leads the go-to-market strategy for AI and incubation solutions, helping over 50,000 customers unlock value through innovation. With a strong background in business transformation, strategy, and enterprise cloud solutions, he has played a key role in positioning SAP as a leader in enterprise technology across the region. With a major in engineering major, he also holds an MBA from the University of São Paulo and a specialization in Corporate Finance from the University of Michigan. His career reflects a deep commitment to blending technology with business impact to drive sustainable growth.
