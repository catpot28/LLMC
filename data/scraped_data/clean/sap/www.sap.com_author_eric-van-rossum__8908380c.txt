Eric van Rossum has been with SAP for over 25 years and has been a key player in shaping SAP’s Cloud ERP strategy. He oversees development for industry-specific solutions and ensures that SAP’s product portfolio meets the local regulatory, legal, tax and language requirements of SAP’s globally diverse customer base. He is also responsible for the strategic messaging, commercial design, and positioning to drive the success and adoption of the SAP Business Suite.
