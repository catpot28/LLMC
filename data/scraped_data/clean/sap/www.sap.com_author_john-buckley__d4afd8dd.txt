Buckley is currently SAP’s Midwest Market Unit Consumer Products Industry Principal. He provides industry perspective, strategic solution advice and thought leadership to support customer co-innovation programs, pipeline growth and demand enabling strategies for SAP. John has over 30 years of experience within the Consumer Products industry working for Tyson Foods, Sara Lee / Hillshire Farms, KraftHeinz, Maytag Appliances and Jacuzzi Whirlpool Bath. He held vice president and senior director roles in supply chain planning, manufacturing, procurement, network distribution strategy, while leading multiple supply chain transformation initiatives. He also worked for EY as a consulting senior manager prior to rejoining SAP.
