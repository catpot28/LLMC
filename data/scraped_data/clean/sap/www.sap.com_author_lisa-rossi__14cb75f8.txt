Lisa Arianna Rossi
is an engineer and circular-systems specialist with experience delivering consulting and research projects for multinational enterprises across manufacturing, heavy-duty, automotive, electronics, and materials sectors. She works at the intersection of strategy and operations, helping organisations translate circular-economy goals into practical solutions, including circular supply-network design, resource-flow mapping, and ecosystem mapping tools.

She works closely with cross-functional teams to develop and test operational strategies, including applying a circular ecosystem mapping approach to identify data gaps and digital intervention points across supply chains. Her work combines analytical depth with real-world implementation considerations.

Her experience includes collaborations with the World Economic Forum, the British Standards Institution, and global industry partners. Lisa holds a PhD in Engineering from the University of Cambridge, focused on supply-chain digitalisation for circular economy applications.
