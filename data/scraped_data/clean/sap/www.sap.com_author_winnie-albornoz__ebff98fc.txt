Winnie Albornoz is the Director of Product Marketing for SAP Customer Experience. Her background encompasses marketing, finance, and strategy in retail and consumer goods. She is passionate about assisting organizations in these industries to realize their full potential through digital innovation, delivering best-in-class customer experiences. She holds a degree in Mathematical Sciences from Bentley College and an MBA from the University of North Carolina’s Kenan-Flagler Business School.
