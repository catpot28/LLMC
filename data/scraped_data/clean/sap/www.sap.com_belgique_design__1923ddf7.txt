Unveiling the Autonomous Enterprise

SAP Design is thrilled to introduce
Joule Work
–a dynamic, intent-driven workspace that surfaces the right insights, automates routine work and coordinates AI agents across systems. Part of the Autonomous Enterprise, Joule Work gives users a unified, adaptive workspace across SAP and non-SAP systems, so leveraging the most relevant insights, agents, and models feels effortless. The result?
A new human advantage
that frees users from disjointed systems, manual handoffs, and time-consuming reconciliation. This shift exemplifies SAP Design’s unwavering commitment to keeping human needs at the heart of our AI advancements.
