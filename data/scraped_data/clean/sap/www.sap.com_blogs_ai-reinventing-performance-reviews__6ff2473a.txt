How AI is redefining performance reviews for continuous development

AI is transforming performance reviews from a stressful annual task into a continuous, insightful conversation that empowers employees and managers alike

It’s that time of year again: annual performance reviews—a process historically known for being time-consuming and stressful for both managers and employees. For many employees, it means hours spent gathering notes from a year’s worth of work. For managers, it brings packed calendars, rushed write-ups, and administrative overload. And when it’s all said and done, the process often feels more like a marathon than a moment to reflect, discuss development, and plan for the future.

Organizations are steadily shifting away from that once-a-year performance model with support from modern HCM solutions like
SAP SuccessFactors
, which makes it possible to manage performance more continuously through goal updates, feedback exchanges, and year-round documentation. With AI, this shift becomes even more effective—streamlining processes, sharpening feedback, and giving both managers and employees clearer, actionable guidance.

Turning performance reviews into a continuous dialogue

Continuous performance management keeps goals, updates, and feedback visible throughout the year, giving managers and employees a clear view of progress as it happens.
SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals
supports this by capturing activity in real time, transforming performance from a backward-looking recap into an ongoing, dynamic conversation.

At the same time, annual reviews still remain an important milestone for many organizations. SAP SuccessFactors provides flexibility to support both approaches—continuous feedback throughout the year and structured year-end evaluations—so companies can choose the model that best fits their culture and needs.

Building on this flexibility, AI takes performance management a step further, surfacing relevant insights, improving tone and clarity in written feedback, and providing conversational guidance that supports more effective check-ins.

The
Performance and Goals Agent
acts as an intelligent companion throughout the year. It automates data collection, generates tailored talking points for managers, and recommends actionable next steps—like scheduling 1:1s, capturing notes, or requesting feedback from peers. It can even summarize peer input on a manager’s behalf, helping ensure conversations start from a place of clarity and preparation.

Connecting work to business impact

Continuous performance management doesn’t just make reviews easier—it helps employees see the bigger picture. They gain clarity on how their contributions support team and organizational goals, receive timely recognition, and feel empowered to drive their growth.

The broader SAP SuccessFactors portfolio—including
SAP SuccessFactors Career and Talent Development
, which leverages the talent intelligence hub—provides employees with insights into their strengths, identifies skill gaps, and recommends personalized learning and development paths. Together, these tools show employees not only what they’ve achieved, but what comes next and how their work fuels long-term organizational success.

Real-world results: Standard Chartered

These capabilities are already delivering measurable improvements for organizations.
Standard Chartered,
for example, rolled out AI-enabled features in SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals to 85,000 employees in just three months. Early employee survey results show that AI has already made a meaningful impact, with a 36% increase in perceived ease to write goals, a 30% increase in perceived ease to write feedback, and a nearly 50% increase in the number of employees receiving more than four pieces of continuous feedback in a quarter. These results show how AI can make performance practices more efficient and meaningful without replacing the human elements that matter most, while helping employees feel recognized, supported, and confident that their contributions are understood.

A better review experience for everyone

With an AI-enhanced HCM platform like SAP SuccessFactors, performance reviews evolve from a stressful annual event into an ongoing partnership. Managers gain clarity and confidence. Employees receive recognition, direction, and a clearer sense of growth. And organizations benefit from stronger alignment, better decision-making, and a culture of continuous development.

AI doesn’t replace the human side of performance; it strengthens it. By grounding conversations in year-round insight and context, every discussion becomes more informed, more equitable, and more impactful.
