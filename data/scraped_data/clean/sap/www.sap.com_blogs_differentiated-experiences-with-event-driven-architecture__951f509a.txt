Data readiness, analytical readiness, and infrastructure readiness lie at the heart of a data-driven organization, where data is embedded in every decision, interaction, and process.

Data readiness ensures good-quality data is available to the right person at the right time, supported by governance policies to streamline access. Analytical readiness not only ensures people are given the tools to use data effectively but also ensures they have the skills to interpret data and develop a deeper understanding to inform their decisions. Infrastructure readiness also reflects a responsive technology foundation that allows data to be harnessed across the business.

Event-driven architecture
provides a modern way for you to embed data in every decision, interaction, and process. It empowers your company to build highly scalable and responsive systems to harness the power of real-time data processing and asynchronous communication, leading to improved performance and customer experience. Because it allows a business to respond to events as they occur naturally and supports processes to happen simultaneously, event-driven architecture enables you to conduct related activities in parallel. This adds speed and flexibility to any process, improving agility and scalability to deliver differentiated customer experiences.

Building a data-driven culture on event-driven architecture

With APIs playing a foundational role in making it easier and faster to integrate cloud and on-premise applications, event-driven architecture has emerged as a crucial part of modern enterprise integration strategies. By decoupling components and enabling asynchronous communication through events, event-driven architecture offers significant advantages over traditional architectures. It transforms back-end systems into dynamic environments that can respond instantly to any changes or updates. Using events to trigger and communicate between decoupled services, it provides a powerful way to build real-time, responsive systems, enabling real-time data use across multiple the enterprise.

Helping organizations become more data-led,
event-driven architecture
offers scalability, resilience, and real-time processing. By working through event producers that create the signals, brokers that direct traffic, and consumers that take action, the decoupled, asynchronous communication model helps share critical business events instantaneously. Enhancing the speed and scalability of business interactions across diverse applications, these systems can also scale horizontally, handling massive amounts of concurrent users with ease.

Collaborate and exchange information instantly business-wide

Providing a comprehensive solution for running event-driven architecture for all industries, SAP Integration Suite allows you to smoothly manage and processes this influx of data at scale. For example, in terms of differentiated customer experiences for retailers, by capturing everything from sensor updates and Web site clicks to system changes, it enables instantaneous stock updates. This allows for automated replenishment, helping ensure product availability and boosting sales. Elsewhere, airlines can generate immediate notifications about gate changes or delays to enhance passenger experiences and operational efficiency. Meanwhile, utilities companies can run smart water grids that continuously monitor for leaks, water quality, waste, and customer satisfaction.

An event-driven architecture on SAP Integration Suite can help unlock the potential of your business transformation journey. The suite connects providers and consumers into enterprise-ready event meshes that can be easily monitored, managed, and scaled across distributed IT landscapes and enable real-time data use across multiple systems. By driving smooth communication between cloud and on-premise applications, you can raise the bar on event mediation, orchestration, catalog, and brokerage services.
