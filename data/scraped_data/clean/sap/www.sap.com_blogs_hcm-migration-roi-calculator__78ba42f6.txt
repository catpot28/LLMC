Putting numbers behind the move to cloud HR

HR leaders increasingly recognize that the future of human capital management lies in the cloud. Yet many struggle to quantify what migration is really worth. How long until costs are recovered? What kinds of efficiencies can be expected? And how do you make the case to skeptical stakeholders?

Our ROI calculator for HCM migration is designed to provide clear, data-based answers. Whether you enter a handful of simplified inputs or a more detailed dataset, the tool generates a tailored analysis that helps decision-makers evaluate the financial and operational impact of moving to SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central, Time Tracking, and Employee Central Payroll.

Why HR leaders are rethinking legacy systems

Large enterprises have long depended on on-premises HR systems. But with globalization, compliance demands, and hybrid work reshaping the workforce, many organizations are finding those systems too rigid to keep pace. Managing payroll, compliance, and employee experiences across multiple countries and shifting workplace models requires more agility than legacy systems can provide. This is why many enterprises are prioritizing HR digital transformation to modernize systems and experiences.

How the ROI calculator clarifies the case for change

One of the biggest barriers to HCM transformation is uncertainty about outcomes. Our ROI calculator addresses this directly by showing tailored scenarios that translate HR complexity into financial and operational terms executives can act on.

The calculator helps decision-makers move past guesswork. By entering a few data points about your HR landscape, you can see:

Estimated savings from reduced IT maintenance and infrastructure

Efficiency gains through automation and streamlined processes

Risk reduction via improved compliance, security, and continuity

Productivity improvements for both HR teams and employees

Benchmarks from similar organizations to strengthen your case

The result is a scenario-based, data-driven report that helps leaders evaluate options, build consensus, and set priorities for migration.

Seeing strategic value beyond cost savings

Cost reduction matters, but it’s not the whole story.
SAP SuccessFactors HCM
also supports strategic initiatives that help organizations adapt and grow.

Innovation and agility
With
SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central
as the core, HR gains access to continuous innovation—embedded AI, advanced analytics, and new capabilities delivered through regular updates. This helps future-proof HR operations by shifting the function from transactional processing to strategic workforce planning and employee experience initiatives.

Compliance and risk management
SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll
is localized for more than 50 countries, helping to simplify legal and regulatory compliance. A modern, integrated cloud solution reduces reliance on customizations, lowers operational costs, and ensures ongoing compliance with changing global regulations.

Workforce efficiency and experience
SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking
improves accuracy and flexibility in managing schedules, time capture, and absences. With intuitive, AI-enabled self-services and personalized experiences, employees and managers gain visibility and control, while HR teams focus on higher-value work that supports organizational agility.

Taken together, these benefits help HR teams focus on higher-value work while giving employees intuitive self-service tools and consistent experiences.

Benchmarks from organizations already in the cloud

The benefits of HCM migration are not theoretical. Cloud adoption accelerates HR transformation, enabling organizations to streamline processes and adopt new AI-driven capabilities. As I highlighted in another recent blog post about the Josh Bersin Company report,
Maximizing the Impact of AI in the Age of the Superworker
, enterprises that have adopted cloud HCM solutions report measurable improvements, such as:

Up to 95% less time spent on routine HR searches

90% faster completion of common HR transactions

Significant reductions in payroll errors and compliance issues

While results vary, these benchmarks illustrate what’s possible when organizations modernize HR systems and processes. By connecting core HR with adjacent systems—such as talent management and workforce management tools—SAP SuccessFactors solutions create a single source of truth and a cohesive HR ecosystem. And because they’re part of the broader
SAP Business Suite
