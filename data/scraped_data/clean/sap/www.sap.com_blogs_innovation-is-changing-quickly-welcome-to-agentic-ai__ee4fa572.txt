Priya starts her day the way many planning leaders do – by checking the KPIs her company cares most about. One alert immediately stands out: the aggregate corporate forecast isn’t tracking at acceptable levels. In the past, this would have kicked off a days-long scramble to pull data, align teams, run analyses, and prepare slides before anyone could decide what to do next.

Not anymore.

See how translating data analysis to action is made easy with AI agent-driven capabilities

With a single glance at her dashboard, Priya sees the severity of the issue. She selects a root cause analysis prompt, and Joule agents go to work. In moments, she has an AI-generated, multi-angle performance summary at her fingertips.

Performance patterns reveal the location of the problem and possible root causes. The Joule agent generates deeper dive prompts, or alternatively, Priya can just ask for specific deeper analysis. Priya chooses to move from insight to action. The agents pinpoint the likely impact factors and ask if she wants to initiate corrective measures.

From insight to action

Priya opts to initiate the corrective measures. The agents orchestrate the workflows across planning, data stewardship, and people enablement—no waiting, no handoffs, no endless email threads. What once took multiple people and days of effort is now managed by one leader in minutes.
