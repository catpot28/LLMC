Building resilient, intelligent supply chains for a disrupted world

Over 80% of global supply chains have faced major disruptions in the past three years—from port congestion to the Suez Canal blockage—revealing vulnerabilities that traditional systems simply can’t withstand. Disruption is no longer a question. Disruption is the new normal—how will your business proactively build resilience into its supply chain?

SAP Business Suite provides the answer. Built upon more than five decades of industry expertise, it delivers a modular, scalable architecture that unifies finance, procurement, supply chain, and HR on a single business technology platform. By integrating applications, AI engines, and data pipelines, SAP empowers organizations to stay agile, resilient, and intelligent—ready to adapt and thrive amid constant change.

Applications, data, and AI: end-to-end example

SAP Business Suite demonstrates how integrated applications, data, and AI streamline complex processes such as engineer-to-order projects. Key capabilities include:

AI-driven project summarization and resource selection

Seamless transition from design (with integrated CAD) to production workflows

Full transparency for controllers, from work breakdown structures to billing

Embedded analytics and automated margin analysis for real-time insights

These features ensure that every stage of the process is supported by intelligent assistants, dashboards, and workflow automation.

Tailored licensing: SCM base and premium

SAP recently introduced tailored solution packages to make adoption of SAP Business Suite more flexible and transparent for customers:

SCM Base
- Provides essential supply chain functionality, including integrated product development and production management tools such as material requirements planning and production scheduling.

SCM Premium
– Includes all capabilities in SCM Base and builds on them with sustainability features such as environmental management, workplace safety, product compliance, and carbon footprint tracking, natively embedded into the suite.

Customers can start with either package and scale up, add users, or extend functionality as business needs evolve.

Customer value and integration benefits

The modularity of these packages means that customers aren’t locked into an all-or-nothing approach. Instead, they gain access to advanced features as their needs mature.

The out-of-the-box integration benefits include:

Lower integration costs

Faster deployment

A harmonized data layer that enables smoother end-to-end workflows across the enterprise

Flexible, transparent journey ahead

With fully transparent pricing and support for a “start where you need it” journey, SAP’s latest offerings are designed to empower organizations of any size to lead with supply chain excellence. SAP’s cloud suite is not only addressing contemporary supply chain chaos but is also equipping customers with the agility and intelligence needed to thrive in a rapidly evolving landscape.
