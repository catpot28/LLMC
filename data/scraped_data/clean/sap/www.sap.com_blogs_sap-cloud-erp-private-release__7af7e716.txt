The need to evolve to a modern ERP

As innovation accelerates, organizations are under constant pressure to operate with more agility while modernizing the systems that support their business. This means streamlining complex landscapes, moving toward cloud-based architectures, and ensuring they can tap into new capabilities as soon as they’re available.

At the same time, the rise of AI is widening skill gaps, making it harder for enterprises to advance meaningful transformation on their own. SAP plays a critical role here, offering process- and context-aware AI built on unified, semantically rich data—creating a strong base for transformation that delivers real and lasting outcomes.

The 2025 release of SAP Cloud ERP Private brings this vision to life. It provides the innovations that modern customers need, including a robust data foundation and embedded AI with intelligent agents that elevate how everyday work gets done.

During our recent
Rise into the Future virtual event
—which announced the new release, Mickey North Rizza, Group Vice President Enterprise Software of IDC, captured the moment clearly:

“…ERP itself must evolve. That means embedding more intelligence directly into the processes, delivering industry-specific capabilities that address unique business needs, and ensuring stronger integration and extensibility so enterprises can connect data, applications, and workflows seamlessly.”

This evolution sets the stage for the next challenge many organizations face: the limitations of on‑premise systems.

The problem with on-premises

Many organizations assume they can keep their on-premises systems running indefinitely; however, the risks associated with this approach are growing. As maintenance timelines expire and systems age, companies miss out on cloud-driven advantages and accumulate technology debt that becomes increasingly costly to manage.

Migrating to SAP Cloud ERP Private addresses these challenges directly. Businesses gain the full spectrum of cloud benefits—from reduced systemic risk to continuous innovation—while also taking advantage of a simplified legal and contractual framework that removes significant complexity.

The transition from an on-premises system to a tailored-to-fit cloud ERP brings several key advantages:

Streamlined operations and support
, allowing SAP to take on the technical operations of your essential business systems.

Innovation on your terms
, supported by a minimum five-year upgrade cycle, annual releases, and regular support packages.

High availability of your mission-critical systems
, with a standard 99.7% service level agreement (SLA) for SAP Cloud ERP Private, helping ensure consistent uptime for essential processes.

Lower total cost of ownership (TCO)
through a subscription model that shifts upfront capital spending to more manageable operating expenses.

These benefits set the foundation for a more resilient and future-ready IT landscape—one designed to evolve as your enterprise does.

The solution that evolves with your business

With the latest release of SAP Cloud ERP Private, customers gain a platform designed to grow and adapt as their needs change. The offering brings together embedded AI, agent-driven automation, and data-powered capabilities to help transform insights into action and support more confident, timely decisions.

SAP is also broadening ERP functionality across finance, compliance, operations, and industry-specific areas—such as value chain analysis—so organizations can advance their transformation with tools built for their unique contexts.

At the Rise into the Future event, Aarti Singh, Senior Vice President and Enterprise CIO of Mahindra & Mahindra, described how SAP Cloud ERP Private enabled the company to push past basic modernization:

“For us, this journey has been about much more than technology modernization. It’s truly a business-led transformation designed to drive operational excellence, enhance agility, and deliver differentiated value to our customers…SAP has given us the foundation to innovate continuously while keeping our digital core clean and future-ready.”

For many organizations, migrating to SAP Cloud ERP Private is an opportunity to tailor their ERP environment to specific needs while keeping essential configurations and customizations intact. It also opens the door to capabilities that can strengthen competitive differentiation.

Several features set SAP Cloud ERP Private apart from SAP ERP, including:

Real-time insights
through the unification of transactional and analytical data in a single system

New embedded functionality
, such as central procurement, customer management, and production planning and scheduling

Continuous updates
that reflect the latest industry requirements

AI that streamlines and automates
critical business processes

Predefined integration and a unified experience
, supported by SAP best practices and extensions that connect processes across the business

Together, these advancements position SAP Cloud ERP Private as a solution built to adapt alongside your enterprise—supporting transformation not just today, but well into the future.

The power of clean core principles

A major advantage of this release of SAP Cloud ERP Private is the flexibility to modernize at your own pace. Clean core principles help maintain a reliable and scalable digital foundation, reducing the risk of disruption and allowing businesses to evolve without compromising stability.

A
clean core strategy
is built on guiding practices that support continuous transformation by helping organizations design ERP systems that are agile, innovative, and efficient. It reduces long‑standing complexity, boosts agility, and accelerates innovation by ensuring data remains trustworthy and ready for AI‑fueled insights.

These five guiding principles work to directly strengthen your cloud ERP landscape:

Processes:
Keeping business processes close to SAP standard helps reduce complexity and maintain competitiveness, creating a streamlined foundation for future enhancements.

Extensibility:
By decoupling extensions from the core and using released APIs, organizations can adopt the most suitable extensibility options—whether on‑stack or side‑by‑side with SAP BTP—while preserving system stability.

Data:
Clean data requires ongoing stewardship, compliance, and a disciplined governance model, ensuring the accuracy needed for innovation and confident decision‑making.

Integrations:
Standardized, secure, and scalable technologies support clean integrations, enabling a reliable, future‑ready approach to connecting systems across the business.

Operations:
Embedding best practices across governance, people, processes, and tools allows IT teams to operate more efficiently and support business transformation with greater agility.

As summarized at the Rise into the Future virtual event:

…with clean core principles as a guiding blueprint, organizations now have a sustainable path to value in their technology transformation.

Muhammad Alam, SAP SE Executive Board Member for SAP Product & Engineering

Shaping the future together

The 2025 release of SAP Cloud ERP Private builds on the principles of a clean, scalable core by making it easier than ever for customers to stay current. It’s fully compatible with previous scopes, removing the need for complex, multi‑step upgrades.

This means that businesses can upgrade to the latest version with minimal effort and immediately leverage advancements across applications, data, and AI. With near-zero downtime upgrades and automated updates, maintenance windows shrink, enabling faster adoption, reduced disruption, and quicker time to value.

These innovations open the door to new levels of efficiency and insight, allowing you to:

Automate routine tasks and enhance decision-making with Joule Agents

Anticipate warehouse staffing needs using predictive labor demand planning

Streamline communications through personalized, trackable, and scalable messaging

Each of these capabilities helps teams shift their focus to higher‑value work and stay ahead of what’s next.
