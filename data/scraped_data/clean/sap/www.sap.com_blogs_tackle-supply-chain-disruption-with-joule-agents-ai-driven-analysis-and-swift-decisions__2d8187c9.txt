From Firefighting to Foresight: See How SAP Joule Agents Reimagine Supply Chain Planning

In the unpredictable world of supply chain, even the best plans can be upended by events outside your control - port congestion, weather, regulatory changes and more. The typical response is a scramble: teams hunt for data across systems, extract and clean it, stitch it together, and only then start problem-solving. Hours tick by. Customers wait. Margins and trust erode.

Imagine that experience transformed

That’s the premise of our new Supply Chain Planning Reimagined
Vision Demo Vignette

Meet Liam, a supply chain planner of the future. His homepage highlights the KPIs he cares about most and prioritizes alerts by severity, so the urgent threats rise to the top. The moment a delivery risk appears, he’s alerted. One notification flags a material risk to revenue and key customers. Liam starts a dialogue with Joule - agentic AI that investigates root causes and proposes paths forward.

What happens next shows the power of decision-centric planning and agent-driven decision making.

Instead of chasing data, Liam asks questions about alternative options. The Joule agent surfaces three contract manufacturer options with different resource availability to offset a congested port. Liam requires a bit more information and requests a feasibility analysis across quantitative factors. Within minutes, the agent identifies the most viable alternative to waiting for the port to clear. He then asks for trade-offs across cost, service levels, and carbon impact. The results are clear:

Option one has the largest negative impact on margins and sustainability.

Option two has the largest negative impact on customer service.

Option three has the smallest negative impact across all three KPIs.

Confident in the recommendation, Liam selects option three. From there, agentic AI takes over: Joule’s agents map required tasks, initiate transactions, update commitments, notify stakeholders, and synchronize plans across systems. What once took hours or days and dozens of people now happened in minutes. Liam focuses his expertise on judgment calls and exceptions while agents handle data gathering, organization, and routine execution.

Accelerate results with autonomous planning and decisioning

This approach delivers instant risk sensing with prioritized alerts tied to the KPIs that matter, followed by a guided conversation with Joule that explains the issue and proposes alternatives It includes rapid feasibility analysis across quantitative factors to identify the best path forward and provides clear trade-off comparisons. Finally, one-click selection enables autonomous, end-to-end execution
.

Expedite sustainable time-to-value

Achieve time to value by compressing analysis and coordination from hours to minutes, and clarity with decision-grade insight without stitching data from multiple systems. Strike balance by protecting revenue while honoring service and sustainability goals and maintain focus by letting agents handle the busy work so humans can apply judgment where it counts. Build confidence through transparent reasoning and measurable outcomes that make decisions defensible and repeatable.

What you’ll take away from the demo

Supply chain volatility isn’t going away - but you can improve your response to it. Watch the
Vision Vignette
to see how Joule Agents helps teams move to orchestrated resilience.
