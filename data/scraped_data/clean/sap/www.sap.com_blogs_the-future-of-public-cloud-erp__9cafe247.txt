The future of public cloud ERP: Integrated, modular, intelligent, and ready to run

Service and product-centric industries need modular and composable public cloud ERP solutions. The powerful combo SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition + SAP BTP gets it done.

In the age of AI, public cloud ERP is seeing renewed importance and interest from enterprises of all sizes. Public cloud ERP is foundational for running the entire business; it supports automation and processes in finance, sales, procurement, services, manufacturing, supply chain, and more.

Companies today are under tremendous pressure and must have the business agility to adapt to fast-changing customer and market demands. They are looking for public cloud ERP solutions that are future-proof, easy to implement, modular, and composable so they can adapt, maximize their benefits, and thrive under all market conditions.

With its laser focus on innovation and integration and close working ties with its customer base, SAP offers the most modern SaaS public cloud ERP solution, SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition. It is modular, offering core finance, sales, and procurement ERP modules. It provides robust modular capabilities for the service-centric and product-centric industries. See Figure 1.

A powerful combo

SAP also delivers new, purpose-built, cloud-native apps on SAP BTP (see Figure 2). These modular and composable apps are fine-tuned to work closely with SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition to provide added agility to enterprises while extending end-to-end business capabilities and processes.

GROW with SAP

SAP offers GROW with SAP commercial packages to help customers adopt our cloud solutions quickly and predictably and achieve business outcomes. These packages bundle a complete offering of solutions, adoption acceleration services, community, and learning.

Take the case of Syntax, a leading global technology and solutions services provider headquartered in Montreal, Canada. Syntax had grown organically and through acquisitions and needed public cloud ERP capabilities to automate its business processes and help it manage its growth. Moreover, as a services company, it required HR and project and resource management capabilities to work hand in hand with the public cloud ERP. They evaluated the market and selected SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, SAP SuccessFactors, SAP BTP, and SAP Project and Resource Management, a BTP-based app.

We need to keep our consultants around the world busy and know what they’re working on and when they have availability to take on new customer projects. We had a whole bunch of different systems managing that. So, by implementing the SAP S/4HANA Public Cloud through
GROW with SAP
and one of its key resource-management modules, we could integrate data sets and get visibility into the availability of those consultants but also know in real time where we stand on billing, training and utilization.
