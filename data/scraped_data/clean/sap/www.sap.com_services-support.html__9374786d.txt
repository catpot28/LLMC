Frequently asked questions
What is SAP Services and Support?
SAP Services and Support offers customers the unified, enterprise-wide engagement needed to maximize the value of individual applications, accelerate complex transformations, and consistently adopt new innovations and AI, all while keeping business-critical systems running smoothly.

What is a success plan?
Success plans are tiered engagements that provide a rich set of features ranging from self-guided resources to a personalized partnership. Customers can choose the plan they need to ensure their continuous success.

Why has the SAP Services and Support portfolio evolved?
We’ve evolved the SAP Services and Support portfolio to deliver a simpler, more powerful experience that meets your individual needs while driving measurable results.

Whether you are a new or existing SAP customer, you expect choice, clarity, and simplicity, backed by a consistent experience across your SAP journey. And you can expect predictable costs and reliable ROI—not only at go-live, but throughout your entire innovation journey with SAP.

With our success plans, you can expect a stronger, tiered engagement model. Designed for transparency, speed, and flexibility, the plans make adopting SAP solutions—and continuously innovating with them—easier than ever.

Does a customer need to subscribe to the same success plan across SAP Business Suite solutions?
We offer customers the flexibility to choose the success plan they want to subscribe to for each cloud solution depending on the level of engagement they require. Customers are not limited to one success plan across all their solutions. Instead, they can pick the success plan that suits them for each solution area. For example, a customer can choose the Advanced Success Plan for SAP Customer Experience and the Max Success Plan for finance and spend management.
