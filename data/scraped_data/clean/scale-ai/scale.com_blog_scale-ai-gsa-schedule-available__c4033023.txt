U.S. Government Can Now Access Scale Capabilities Through GSA Schedule
·
May 20, 2026
·
2 min read
Copy Link
The pace of AI innovation has been blistering, but the paths for the U.S. Government to adopt those frontier technologies are slowed by both longstanding acquisition hurdles and the challenge of ensuring model reliability. Scale AI is proud to announce today that we have partnered with
Carahsoft Technology Corp.
to add Scale’s GenAI Platform, Test & Evaluation (T&E) capabilities, and Professional & Deployment Services to Carahsoft’s GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS), making them widely available to any U.S. Government customer through a streamlined, transparent acquisition process.
Trusted AI for the Toughest Missions
Today’s announcement has concrete implications for every corner of the U.S. Government. It provides rapid access to AI capabilities and T&E services already proven to meet standards for AI reliability and transparency on the most mission-critical defense and national security missions. It also removes acquisition barriers for any department or agency looking to move beyond small-scale pilots. With Scale’s Professional & Deployment services now on the schedule, agencies can access the expert support required to operationalize AI and strengthen decision-making in alignment with federal innovation priorities.
Meeting the Mandate
Addition to the GSA Schedule directly supports the Administration’s push for a more agile, accountable, and rigorous federal AI landscape. Specifically, our availability aligns with two critical directives:
OMB Memorandum M-25-21:
By making the Scale GenAI Platform and our specialized Professional & Deployment Services available via GSA, we are helping agencies fulfill the requirement to accelerate federal use of AI by providing the expert support needed to move from initial evaluation to full-scale production.
OMB Memorandum M-25-22:
Today’s announcement answers the call to drive efficient acquisition of AI. By providing pre-negotiated, discounted pricing, we allow contracting officers to bypass traditional bureaucratic hurdles and acquire industry-leading AI capabilities in a fraction of the time.
A Strategic Partnership with Carahsoft
Our integration into the GSA Schedule is bolstered by our partnership with Carahsoft. Scale’s capabilities are now accessible through Carahsoft’s extensive network of government-wide contract vehicles, specifically: Carahsoft’s GSA Schedule No. 47QSWA18D008F, SEWP V contracts NNG15SC03B and NNG15SC27B, ITES-SW2 Contract W52P1J-20-D-0042, NASPO ValuePoint Master Agreement #AR2472, and OMNIA Partners Contract #R240303.
With our addition to the GSA Schedule, Scale is reinforcing its commitment to being a long-term, trusted partner and helping the U.S. Government build, evaluate, and deploy AI solutions for the world’s most important decisions.
