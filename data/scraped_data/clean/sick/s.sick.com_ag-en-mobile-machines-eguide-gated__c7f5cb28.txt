Automation on the Move

Environments in which mobile machines frequently operate range from deep mud and hard gravel to bright sunlight, dense dust or fog. Traditional working methods in agriculture, construction or municipal vehicles are increasingly reaching their physical and economic limits. Manufacturers and operators are faced with the constant challenge of increasing productivity and reducing costs at the same time. This means that not only people, but also technology is required - and automation is crucial.

Who is the e-guide for?
The e-guide is aimed at project managers, innovation managers and integrators of mobile machines. Anyone looking to invest in robust, visionary AI-supported systems in order to increase efficiency and position themselves as a technology leader will find guidance and concrete solutions here.
