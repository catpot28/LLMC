interactive world of mobile machines

Experience the future of mobile machines: The panoramic view offers you the opportunity to experience sensor solutions in the world of construction, mining, agricultural or forestry machinery - and to find the right solution for you.
SICK offers a comprehensive portfolio of precise, robust and intelligent sensor technology - particularly developed for outdoor use in mobile machines.

Mining machines and mining vehicles
Solutions for object detection and navigation support for mining vehicles or rear area monitoring for front loaders

Construction machines
Solutions for levelling the excavator arm, collision avoidance for road rollers or environment detection for work processes on the excavator

Agricultural machines , special crops and forestry machines
Solutions for windrow guidance for baling automation, quality control in soil cultivation or collision avoidance and navigation support for agricultural robots
