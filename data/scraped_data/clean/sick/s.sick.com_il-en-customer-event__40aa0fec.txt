Industry 4.0
Industry 4.0 clearly relates to networking in the industrial sector. SICK plays a very important role in the value creation chain in this area. The prerequisite for communication is an abundance of information, which is what SICK sensors deliver.

Smart Sensors
Smart Sensors generate and receive data and information which goes beyond traditional switching signals or measured process parameters. They therefore enable substantial increases in efficiency, more flexibility, and better planning security for predictive plant maintenance.

Connectivity
Networked production and control processes in complex machine environments determine the industrial future. Sensor intelligence is required for the highly complex process of converting physical signals into sensor information. Intelligent sensor systems are already being used to support dynamic industry processes involving real-time optimization and self-organization.

IO-Link and Networks
IO-Link offers new options for communication between the system control and field level: Sensors and actuators become active process participants in an end-to-end automation network.

Digitization in Production
In transparent production processes, machines communicate both with one another as well as directly over the Ethernet or in the cloud via intelligent sensors. Let's raise the quality of processed information to a new level.

Smart Cameras
Machine vision solutions make your manufacturing processes more efficient and competitive. Now you can have customizable 2D and 3D vision sensors, tailored to your business.

Safe work
environment

SICK offers safety solutions that make your processes run smoothly, enable human and machine to work as a team, and allow us to jointly push the boundaries of what is achievable. From consultation and planning to execution and acceptance - increase your productivity with smart safety solutions for your individual needs!

Safe human-robot collaboration
SICK’s extensive portfolio of safety solutions enables unimpaired and safe human intervention into the robot system and reduces downtime in production. This can be achieved with an adaptive perception of the environment with the aid of intelligent, rugged, and reliable sensors and safe systems.

Artificial Intelligence
Artificial intelligence (AI) to us means valuable data is collected by our sensors, interpreted by our algorithms. So you can focus on the big picture, optimize your workflows, and make efficient use of your resources.
