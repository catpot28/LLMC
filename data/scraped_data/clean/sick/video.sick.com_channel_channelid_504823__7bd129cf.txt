Discover how the microScan3 Configuration Tool – ABB makes safeguarding your freely-accessible robot applications quick and easy. Compatible with SWIFTI™ and GoFa™ robots from ABB, this tool simplifies the configuration process and enhances safety in your robotic applications.
Our tutorial playlist guides you through the setup process, taking safety and efficiency to a new level.
