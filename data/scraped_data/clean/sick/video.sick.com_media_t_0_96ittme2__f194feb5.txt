In this tutorial, we demonstrate how to handle IO-Link service data (ISDUs) using EtherNet/IP explicit messaging.

Learn how to access service data via message instructions (MSGs) and
device-specific add-on instructions (AOIs), simplifying the process of reading
and writing ISDUs.

Perfect
for anyone looking to streamline their PLC integration process with the SIG300.
