In this tutorial, we demonstrate how to assign
a defined cut-off behavior to monitoring cases and understand the
implications of these states in various situations, such as when a vehicle is
in parking mode or when it is in battery charging and safe driving zones.

Our microScan3 supports up to 8
simultaneous cut-off paths, ensuring versatile and reliable safety solutions
for industrial applications.
