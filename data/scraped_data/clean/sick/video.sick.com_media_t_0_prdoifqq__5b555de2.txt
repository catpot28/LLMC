Do you want to optimize your machine processes and minimize downtimes?
Discover the ANS/ANM58 PROFINET – the powerful 58 mm absolute encoder from
SICK!

In this video you can see how the ANS/ANM58 offers an excellent solution
for various applications - from packaging machines and warehouse automation to
classic mechanical engineering - thanks to its mechanical variability, compact
design with an installation depth of just 39 mm (radial version) and the
PROFINET interface with encoder profile V4.2.

Thanks to isochronous real time (IRT) and comprehensive diagnostic
functions via SOPAS ET, the encoder enables precise motion monitoring,
condition-based maintenance and maximum productivity.
See how the ANS/ANM58 synchronizes your processes, reduces downtime, and
boosts productivity!
