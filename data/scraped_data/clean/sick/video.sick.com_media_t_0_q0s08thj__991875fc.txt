Tired of juggling different tools for every SICK device? Meet SICK Creator – SICK’s unified Engineering Platform for nearly all SICK
devices.

In this tutorial, we'll show you how to use the Device Manager Extension
to configure almost any SICK device quickly and intuitively. You'll learn how
to install the extension, add devices, navigate the Device UI, change IP
addresses, transfer SOPAS files, and more.
Watch now for a first look at the productive version and discover how
SICK Creator can streamline your device management process!
