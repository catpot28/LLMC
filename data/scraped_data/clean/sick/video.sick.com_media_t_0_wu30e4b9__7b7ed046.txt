Thinking about upgrading your SICK safety laser scanner from the S3000 to a
new safety laser scanner like the microScan3?

This step-by-step guide walks you through the entire replacement process—from
the selection of a suitable successor variant to commissioning—ensuring a
smooth and safe transition to the next generation of safety laser scanner.

🔧

What you'll learn in this video:

Safely preparing for the migration
process
Using our support
documents and website to select the right microScan3 variant
Using the conversion
software “CDS to SD Converter” to transfer the current configuration
Setting up a
configuration for your new microScan3
Removing the S3000 and
installing the new safety laser scanner
Verifying and commissioning
the setup

💡

Note:
The replacement of an S3000 with a microScan3, as
shown in this video, is just one possible upgrade path to a new-generation
safety laser scanner. Your specific options will depend on your application and
may include transitioning from an S3000, S300, or S300 Mini to either a
microScan3 or a nanoScan3.

📘
For further information, visit our official replacement guide
, which includes detailed documentation and configuration tools to support
your upgrade.
