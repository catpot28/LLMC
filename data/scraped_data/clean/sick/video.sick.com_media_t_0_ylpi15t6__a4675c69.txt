The AI Blob Finder (dStudio) uses instance segmentation technology to detect, count, and measure objects within a defined region using a trained neural network. It identifies and separates each object in an image, pixel by pixel—even when multiple objects of the same type overlap significantly—providing precise masks rather than bounding boxes.
This capability enables accurate measurement of length, width, and area, making it ideal for quality control tasks such as counting, sorting, positioning, and defect analysis. It can also deliver high-precision size data for cracks or surface flaws and determine material quantities with exceptional accuracy.
