Why Drivetrain Analyzer Onsite?
Drivetrain Analyzer Onsite is your on-premises software solution for monitoring the health of your machinery from a single, convenient location. Effortlessly process sensor and soon sensorless data to gain clear insights into the operational status of all your machines. Seamless integration possibilities allow you to extract raw data from your hardware and forward it directly to your own analytics software for further in-depth analysis.

Key benefits
Reduce Unplanned Downtime with AI Analytics
Detect anomalies and emerging issues weeks before failure using built-in AI algorithms and intuitive traffic light-style indicators. Empower your maintenance team to act proactively – no data science expertise required.
Enjoy Fast and Easy Deployment
Connect drivetrain equipment from any manufacturer using open, documented interfaces with zero vendor lock-in. Choose how your data is collected, processed, and used – entirely within your own infrastructure.
Monitor All Your Assets
Oversee individual drivetrain components or entire fleets across multiple plants from a single interface. Reduce unplanned downtime by up to 45% and extend asset life by 20–30% through targeted, data-driven maintenance.

Featured capabilities
Predictive Maintenance with Industrial AI
Built-in AI algorithms automatically detect anomalies and emerging issues before they escalate into costly failures. Complex machine data is translated into clear, traffic light-style indicators – giving operators and plant managers instant visibility into machine status and required actions.
Universal Compatibility and Open Architecture
Connect drivetrain equipment from virtually any manufacturer, regardless of age or type, using open, documented interfaces. Seamlessly integrate with your existing IT/OT systems via standard protocols such as MQTT, gRPC, and OPC UA – with zero vendor lock-in.
Scalable Asset Monitoring Across Your Fleet
Monitor individual drivetrain components in detail or oversee entire fleets across multiple lines and plants from a single unified interface. Continuous real-time data collection using sensor-based and sensorless technologies ensures full coverage of your installed base.
On-Premises Security and Data Sovereignty
Your data stays inside your own infrastructure at all times, protected by encrypted communication and a containerized, industrial-grade cybersecurity architecture. Maintain full control over access and usage while meeting demanding industry and regulatory standards.
Intuitive Interface for Every Team Member
A purpose-built user interface removes training barriers, enabling maintenance and operations teams to work independently from day one – no external experts required. Turn complex raw data into clear, actionable insights for maintenance planning and day-to-day operations.
