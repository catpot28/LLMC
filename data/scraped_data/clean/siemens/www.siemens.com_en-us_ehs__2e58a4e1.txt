Environmental protection
As a global player, we bear a special responsibility for worldwide challenges such as demographic change, climate change, and limited resources. Environmental protection has been a central focus for us since 1971.
We harmonize economic, ecological, and social requirements and meet our social responsibility head on. We use technological progress and innovative solutions to minimize the negative impact of our business activities on the environment. At the same time, we ensure our long-term competitiveness by making the most of valuable and often expensive raw materials.
At Siemens, we offer a comprehensive range of expertise, all in one place. We provide competitive, environmentally friendly product design, improved resource efficiency, assessment and advice on environmental risks, and support for legal certainty.

Conserving natural resources through a circular economy
The goal of a circular economy is to conserve natural resources by closing material and energy cycles as efficiently as possible. This process begins with product design. At every stage of processing, materials are used in a way that minimizes residual waste and maintains closed-loop systems. After a long lifespan, the end product can be almost entirely recycled, keeping a significant portion of raw materials within the production cycle. By supporting this approach, we contribute to protecting our environment.
Towards Zero Waste – Waste Separation at the Munich Perlach Site
We take waste and resource management very seriously. In a world with a rapidly growing population and increasingly scarce resources, this issue is becoming ever more important. That’s why we have made waste management a key component of the global EHS environmental program.
Our goal is to ensure that no waste ends up in landfills but remains within the economic cycle. With a recycling rate of 99 %, the Munich Perlach site is well on its way to achieving this objective.

Take-back offer for packaging, old electrical appliances and batteries
Some packaging, electrical appliances and batteries contain harmful substances that pollute the environment hen released. They also contain valuable resources that can be reused through recycling. Points of sale for electrical appliances are therefore obliged to take back old appliances, and our customers can also return packaging, electrical appliances, and batters to enable resource-conserving disposal.

Health management
It is a simple fact that a healthy working environment is a prerequisite for satisfied, motivated and creative employees.
We align health activities in the company with the requirements of today's working world and continuously improve occupational health management.
Our workplace health promotion strengthens the physical and mental health of people in the company, increasing motivation in the long term, reducing sickness costs, improving the employer image and increasing competitiveness. We rely on a planned approach and support the implementation of local requirements for the promotion of health issues with suitable instruments.

We are committed to integrating the promotion and empowerment of people as an indispensable part of our corporate strategy. This approach has also been recognised and appreciated externally.
We have been confirmed as leaders in occupational health management following a comprehensive and successful Corporate Health Audit, which led to the presentation of the Corporate Health Award 2022.
Our workplace health promotion is tailored to the needs of Siemens employees and offers a wide range of programs and preventive measures. We make a significant contribution in the area of resilience and mental health in particular. We know that investing in our colleagues is the most valuable investment we can make in our company.

EHS-FAQs
Here you will find answers to frequently asked questions about environmental protection, health management and safety (EHS).

Environmental protection
How can Siemens as a company protect the environment in the long term?
At Siemens, we are committed to social and environmental responsibility. As a participant in the UN Global Compact, we advocate for human rights, labor rights, environmental protection, and the fight against corruption. These principles apply to our entire company as well as our suppliers and business partners worldwide. Our policies comply with international standards and include, among others, the UN Conventions, OECD Guidelines, and Agenda 21 for sustainable development.
More information can be found in our Business Conduct Guidelines.
How can employees at Siemens participate in environmental protection?
People at Siemens can contribute to environmental protection by promoting resource efficiency, sharing ideas, participating in projects, and furthering their education. Everyone plays an important role in our goal of becoming climate-neutral by 2030. Our culture of personal responsibility encourages responsible action, and the emissions reduction we have already achieved confirms our successful path. Employees actively contribute to reaching our CO2 goals and are also incentivized with benefits such as climate-friendly company cars, discounted public transport annual tickets, or a bicycle program ("Jobrad").
What are the environmental protection training opportunities at Siemens?
At Siemens we offer various training opportunities on environmental protection. This includes access to a learning platform for self-directed learning, where our people can expand their knowledge flexibly, anytime, and anywhere. Additionally, expert-led lectures are regularly offered to convey current developments and best practices in environmental protection. Our EHS experts also ensure their knowledge is continuously expanded and stay up-to-date with legal and technical changes through both external and internal training and events.
As environmental protection is an integral part of our corporate strategy, this topic is also of high importance in corporate communication and is consistently considered in all business decisions. The development of our employees is a key priority, and we strongly encourage them to engage in continuous learning – both internally and externally – including training opportunities in the area of environmental protection.
Is there an environmental protection program at Siemens?
Yes, at Siemens, we have a comprehensive environmental protection program guided by our DEGREE framework. This includes measures for decarbonization, ethical business practices, effective corporate governance, resource efficiency, equity, and the promotion of employability for our people.
Our EHS program is an integral part of this and a significant step towards organizational resilience. It consists of two modules – Healthy & Safe @ Siemens and Eco Efficiency @ Siemens – running through 2030. Under the Eco Efficiency @ Siemens initiative, we focus on three key categories:
Responsible Product Development
: We evaluate our portfolio and apply an eco-design approach to relevant product families to support the sales of eco-efficient products and solutions.
Clean Supply Chain
: We procure more secondary materials and reduce the use of hazardous substances to promote the decoupling of natural resource consumption.
Efficient Operations
: We enhance waste disposal practices and use clean energy more effectively to minimize the environmental impact of our own production sites and offices.

Health management
What measures are being taken by Siemens directly at the workplace?
At Siemens, we have implemented various measures in the area of health management in the workplace. These include ergonomic workplace design, regular health checks and consultations, as well as programs for stress management and promoting a healthy lifestyle. Additionally, there are training sessions and workshops on topics such as ergonomics at the workplace and mental health. Our company doctors (Medical Services), Siemens Social Counseling, and health experts from Health Promotion are available to discuss individual concerns and offer supportive measures. Furthermore, we provide a comprehensive range of activities focused on physical activity, healthy work environments, nutrition, and mental health, such as active breaks, lectures, podcasts, and a wide e-learning offering for self-paced, location-independent learning.
Does Siemens offer online programs for employees to use at home?
Yes, Siemens offers a wide range of online health programs for our people to use at home. On My Learning World, our learning platform, they can access health-related educational offerings at any time. Additionally, we offer online fitness courses under the name "livesport@home." These courses are location-independent and provide a variety of training options, including Cardio Power Mix, Core Training, Zumba, Fascia Training, Fit into the Day, Back Express, Pilates, Evening Relaxation, and Yoga. Employees can conveniently enhance their health and fitness from home. Additionally, there is a selection of training videos available anytime on My Learning World.
How does Siemens support the mental health of its employees?
At Siemens, we support the mental health of our people through a variety of measures. Trainings and workshops raise awareness of mental health and provide them with information on stress management, resilience, and other relevant topics based on the latest scientific findings. In addition, they have access to professional counseling and support services, including medical and psychological advice, as well as coaching, to receive individual support in personal challenges or stressful situations.
We are committed to creating a positive and supportive work environment that fosters the mental health of our people. This includes measures to improve work-life balance, flexibility in working hours, and promoting an open and respectful work atmosphere. Programs and resources for stress management, such as relaxation techniques, yoga courses, or stress management workshops, also help them lead a balanced life.
Overall, we place great importance on maintaining and promoting the mental health of our people by implementing both preventive and supportive measures to create a healthy work environment. We are also proud to have received the
ISSA Vision Zero Award
for our commitment.

Safety
How is occupational health and safety organized at Siemens?
Our
DEGREE framework
sets clear priorities with regard to sustainability, including measures to enhance the employability of our people. Our EHS program is an integral part of this, aiming to /content/tenants/siemens-com/master/en/company/sustainability/social-commitments/health-safety/foster-employee-well-being.html improve health and safety culture and strengthen the resilience of both people and the organization. It consists of two modules: "Healthy and Safe @ Siemens" and "Eco-Efficiency @ Siemens". Through the "Healthy and Safe @ Siemens" module, we shape occupational health and safety at Siemens. It aims to ensure that ou people contribute effectively to health and safety in all business activities, offering space and opportunities for them to lead themselves and others better, learn from each other, and keep well-being in focus. Personal strengths, individuality, and expertise play a crucial role in this.
The program promotes a healthy, safe, and productive work environment and is based on five core principles that guide our approach and actions regarding health and safety in the workplace:
We care for our own well-being and that of others.
We speak up and actively contribute to creating a healthy and safe working environment.
We are inclusive and incorporate a wide range of perspectives on health and safety.
We learn from each other and exchange ideas on how to work better, safer, and healthier.
We prepare for changing circumstances and adapt effectively to them.

How can employees at Siemens contribute to occupational safety?
Our people at Siemens can contribute to occupational safety by taking responsibility and actively participating in safety measures. The health and safety of each individual is always a priority, and we encourage our employees to actively advocate for safe and healthy working conditions. This includes adhering to safety regulations, reporting safety hazards or accidents, regularly maintaining and inspecting work equipment, and participating in training, safety courses, and awareness events on occupational health and safety. Through their actions, they help ensure a safe working environment for themselves and their colleagues.
