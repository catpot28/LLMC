Sligro 90 years of entrepeneurship

On 17 April 2025, it was exactly 90 years since Abel Slippens, driven by strong entrepreneurial spirit, started his commission trade in margarine in Veghel. Discover how this simple and transparent business grew into the Sligro Food Group of today.

Koen Slippens CEO and Rob van der Sluijs CFO

Multi-year plan

2026 - 2030

We have built our multi-year plan around three concrete priorities: growth towards a healthier revenue mix in the Netherlands, revenue growth and, from that, improved profitability in Belgium, and fundamental cost reduction.
