Company profile

Sligro Food Group comprises food service companies in the Netherlands and Belgium, offering a comprehensive range of food and food-related non-food products and services in the wholesale market.

Executive Board

The Sligro Food Group Executive Board has two members, the CEO and the CFO

International Board

Sligro Food Group has an International Board that consists of the Executive Board and executives with responsibilities in the areas of Cash & Carry, Delivery, ICT, supply chain, HRM and procurement and assortment management.
