CSR vision: how we work

Sligro Food Group's added value is not only expressed in terms of our financial performance; environmental, societal and good governance issues are also key themes for us. Corporate social responsibility is part of our integral vision on entrepreneurship, in which we create value in the context of all ESG themes. From that perspective, we see the OECD guidelines for ICSR as a natural frame of reference for our policy on international corporate social responsibility.
