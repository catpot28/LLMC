JAVA Foodservice

The synergy between the locally rooted company JAVA Foodservice and the international strength of Sligro Food Group offers the most suitable assortment and a partner in growth and development for all our customers and stakeholders.

JAVA Foodservice is the leading Belgian foodservice company for food professionals in the healthcare sector, government, defense, and education.
