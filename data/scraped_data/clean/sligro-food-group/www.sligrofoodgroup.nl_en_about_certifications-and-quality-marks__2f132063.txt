Certifications and Quality Marks

Certifications and Quality Marks
Sligro Food Group considers it essential to provide customers with safe products. Therefore, together with our suppliers, we do everything possible to minimize risks. We require that all supplied goods fully comply with the applicable standards for product and/or food safety and sustainability in the broadest sense. To safeguard this within our own processes, we make use of widely recognized standards and certifications.

Certifications
