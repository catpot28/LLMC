Frequently asked questions

What sets Sligro Food Group apart as a company?

Sligro Food Group encompasses foodservice companies selling directly and indirectly to the entire food and beverage market, using a multichannel strategy involving different forms of marketing and distribution (including self-service and delivery).

In what countries is Sligro Food Group active?

The Netherlands and Belgium.

What is Sligro Food Group’s dividend policy?

The established pay-out ratio is around 50% and may be increased by a variable amount, depending on the financial position.

In what years did Sligro Food Group share splits occur?

In 1995, 1998, 2003 and 2007.
