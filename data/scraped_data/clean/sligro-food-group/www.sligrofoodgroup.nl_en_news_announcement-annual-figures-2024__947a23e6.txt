Presentation annual figures 2024
Sligro Food Group will publish its 2024 annual figures on Thursday 27 March 2025.
Press conference

Thursday, 27 March 2025, 11.00 AM
Location: Sligro Amsterdam, Van der Madeweg 39, Amsterdam-Duivendrecht

Analyst Meeting

Thursday, 27 March 2025, 01.30 PM
Location: Sligro Amsterdam, Van der Madeweg 39, Amsterdam-Duivendrecht
Conference call via Microsoft Teams
Rob van der Sluijs (CFO) will present the 2024 year figures of Sligro Food Group.
Thursday 27 March 2025
Time: 4:00 PM CET / 3:00 PM GMT / 10:00 AM EST
Duration: Approximately 60 minutes
Please confirm your attendance by Monday, 24 March by e-mail to Charissa Kleij:
ckleij@sligro.nl

Upon confirmation, you will receive the conference call details, including the Teams link, dial-in number, and access code.
