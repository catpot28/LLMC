Revenue for Sligro Food Group N.V. for 2025 came in at €2,668 million

Veghel, 8 January 2026
Revenue for Sligro Food Group N.V. for 2025 came in at €2,668 million, an increase of 1.7% compared to the 2024 revenue excluding tobacco of €2,623 million.
Total revenue (excluding tobacco) per quarter:
In the Netherlands, growth picked up in the fourth quarter compared to earlier quarters this year, supported by a strong December. The acquisition of GEPU contributed €6 million to revenue this year. In Belgium, the gradual recovery in revenue continued, with an encouraging increase in the fourth quarter.
