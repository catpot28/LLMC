Sales Sligro Food Group 2010; € 2,286 mln

Sligro Food Group sales in 2010 amounted to € 2,286 million, an increase of € 28 million or 1.2% (2009: € 2,258 million).

The 2009 financial year comprised 53 weeks (generating additional sales of € 40 million). Sanders Supermarkten was consolidated in Q4 2010 (accounting for sales of € 25 million). Read more in the press release.
