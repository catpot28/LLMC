Share buyback programme

Share buyback programme 2026
On 5 February 2026, we announced a share buyback programme of up to €26 million, to be executed between 6 February and 14 November 2026.
On 12 June, the 2026 share buyback programme was completed.
In the period 6 February 2026 up to and including 12 June 2026, a total of 1,946,758ordinary shares were repurchased for €25,999,987.32, representing 4.4% of the company’s issued share capital, at an average price of €13.36 per share.
After cancellation, the total number of issued shares will amount to 42,308,257. The capital reduction is expected to become effective in Q3 2026, subject to the completion of the required legal formalities.
The programme was carried out in accordance with the requirements of Article 5 of the Market Abuse Regulation (EU) No. 596/2014 and our Articles of Association.
Detailed information on the progress of the 2026 share buyback programme, as well as all individual transactions, is available via the link below and in the weekly press releases.
