Michael Miliopoulos, Antisel's Director of HR shared with us how he uses Synthesia to develop stellar onboarding experiences for remote employees.
Industry Context
Scientific services firms need to onboard remote teams quickly and consistently. Traditional onboarding like PDFs or Zoom calls are slow to scale, lack personalization, and struggle to engage a multilingual workforce.
The Challenge
With COVID raging in 2021, Michael needed to find a way to provide a good onboarding experience for new remote employees. His goal was to make sure everyone received clear and consistent guidance in the first few weeks at work.
The current onboarding process was mostly a mix of ad hoc zoom calls and emails consisting of PDFs and Powerpoints.
Michael was aware that it lacked a personal touch and was, frankly speaking, boring. He was convinced that personalised video would be the perfect format for welcoming new employees.
But traditional video production was not an option for Michael, it simply had too many limitations:
Speed
: It would take weeks to create a video, considering the amount of preparation needed to record the footage.
Flexibility:
It would be nearly impossible to update the videos once created, and it would take additional time and resources to recreate the videos.
Scaleability:
Without a big budget, it's almost impossible to scale video production.
Michael, however, didn't want to give up his idea. He set out to find a solution that would allow him to create video at scale while making the experience personal and engaging. With Synthesia, he found what he was looking for.
The Solution
Michael is now greeting remote new beginners with a series of bite-sized, personalised AI videos, all created with Synthesia.
This is how Michael does it:
Michael chose one of
Synthesia’s 50+ templates
and made it fit Antisel's brand guidelines.
He then creates a script for the particular topic he wants to present to the new employee.
He translates the script into the language spoken by the new colleague.
He inserts his copy into the Synthesia script box.
Picks an avatar, adds text, pictures or screen recordings.
and voilà, the video is ready to be sent.

The Results
In just a few months, Michael has transformed the entire onboarding process:
Great Employee Satisfaction:
The new process received a

from the 18 new hires who came on board with AI video.

Onboarding Automation:
Michael has created a series of short introductory videos about Antisel's mission, vision, and core values, as well as a series of videos that walk new employees through their first week.
Award winning:
