What to expect
Frequently Asked Questions
Can anyone join?
Anyone can join Synthesia's community—it's alright if you're still
Synthesia-curious!
If you care about making great AI videos and growing your skills, you belong here. Synthesia creators gain access to exclusive forums, challenges, events, and workshops, so
be sure to sign up with the same email address you use for Synthesia.
What can I do in the community?
You can make feature requests, join monthly video creation challenges, get feedback on your work, attend exclusive events, share tips and tricks, ask questions, access beta features, and connect with other creators who share the same passions (and the same challenges) that you do.
Do I need to be an expert to participate?
Not at all! Whether you’re just starting or you’ve created hundreds of Synthesia videos, the community is here to help you grow. You'll find peers at every experience level.
Can I invite my team to join?
A resounding yes—we’d
love
to have them here! The more voices, experiences, and ideas we have in the mix, the better it gets for everyone. We all benefit from shared knowledge, peer support, and collective creativity.
