Find out more about AI Actors
What is an AI actor?
An AI actor is a digital presenter that delivers your script on video. It looks and speaks like a real person, but you control it from text.
Teams use AI actors for training, marketing, and explainer videos to produce professional content without cameras, actors, or studios.
Can I create an AI actor that looks like me?
Yes. You can easily generate a custom AI actor that looks and sounds like you. Just record yourself using your webcam or phone, follow a few simple steps, and your AI actor will be generated.
What kind of videos can I make with AI actors?
AI actors support a wide range of video use cases. Teams use them to create training modules, onboarding programs, internal communications, product demos, customer education, and social media videos.
Is it free to try Synthesia’s AI actors?
Yes. You can create videos with AI actors for free in Synthesia. The free plan includes up to 10 minutes of video per month, so you can explore how AI actors fit into your workflow.
You can use customizable avatars at no cost. Prompt outfits, environments, and actions to match your message and audience. Write your script, choose an AI actor, and generate a professional video in minutes.
You can also try many core platform features, including AI Playground, video translation, AI Screen Recorder, video templates, and much more.
Do AI actors support multiple languages?
Yes. Synthesia AI actors support 160+ languages and 1,000+ natural voices.
Do I need video production experience to create videos?
No. Synthesia is built for non-technical teams.
Select an AI actor, paste your script, and produce a polished video without cameras, studios, or editing software.
How are Synthesia’s AI actors created?
All Synthesia AI actors are created using real actors who provide consent and are paid per video generated using their likeness. These performances are captured and transformed into AI models that can then speak any script in 160+ languages.
