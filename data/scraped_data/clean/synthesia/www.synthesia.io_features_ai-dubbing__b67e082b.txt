FAQs
AI video dubbing FAQs
Which is the best AI dubbing tool?
Synthesia stands out as the
best tool for AI dubbing
because it offers the complete package of voice preservation, lip synchronization, and secure editing capabilities in one platform. While many tools provide basic
video translation
or subtitle generation, Synthesia maintains the original speaker's voice across all languages and ensures perfect lip sync, creating a natural viewing experience that feels authentic rather than artificially generated.
Beyond technical capabilities, Synthesia prioritizes safety and ease of use with built-in content moderation, collaborative editing features, and a multilingual video player that automatically serves content in the viewer's preferred language. This combination of advanced dubbing technology with enterprise-grade security and user-friendly design makes it particularly valuable for businesses that need to create professional multilingual content at scale while maintaining quality and brand consistency.
What is AI dubbing and how does it work?
AI dubbing is a technology that automatically translates and replaces the original audio in your videos with a new language while preserving the speaker's unique voice characteristics and syncing with their lip movements. This process transforms your content into multiple languages in minutes rather than weeks, making your videos accessible to global audiences without the traditional costs and complexity of hiring voice actors and recording studios.
Unlike traditional dubbing methods that require extensive time and resources, Synthesia’s AI dubbing maintains the authenticity of your original content by keeping the same voice tone and emotional delivery across all languages. This means your training videos, marketing content, or company updates can reach international teams and customers with the same impact as your original version, helping you expand your reach and improve engagement across different markets.
Is AI dubbing safe?
Yes, AI dubbing with Synthesia is designed with robust safety measures to protect your content and ensure ethical use. The platform implements rigorous content moderation at the point of creation, using advanced technology filters and human oversight to prevent inappropriate or harmful content from being created or distributed.
Synthesia's secure editing features ensure that any changes to translated scripts are moderated to maintain the original message's meaning and prevent misuse of anyone's likeness. With ISO 42001 certification for AI safety and full GDPR compliance, your data and content remain protected throughout the dubbing process, giving you confidence that your videos are translated responsibly while maintaining your brand's integrity.
How do I dub a video with AI in Synthesia?
Dubbing a video with Synthesia is a straightforward three-step process that takes just minutes to complete. First, you upload your existing video to the dubbing tool, where it automatically detects and preserves each speaker's voice, even in videos with multiple speakers. Next, you select your target languages from over 130 available options, and the AI generates translated versions that maintain your original voice characteristics and lip sync.
Once the translation is complete, you can review and edit the transcript to ensure accuracy while the platform's content moderation tools protect the integrity of your message. The final dubbed video can be downloaded, shared via a single link, or embedded using Synthesia's multilingual player that automatically plays in your viewer's preferred language, making global distribution seamless and professional.
What languages are supported?
Synthesia supports 130+ languages and dialects for AI dubbing, including right‑to‑left scripts and regional variants. Popular options include: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Russian, Chinese (Mandarin), Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Bengali, Turkish, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Polish, and Swedish.
What rights and permissions do I need to dub a video with Synthesia?
To dub a video with Synthesia, you must have all necessary rights, licenses, and permissions for the content you're uploading. This means you need to own the video or have explicit permission from the copyright holder to create translated versions, including rights to any voices, images, and other content appearing in the video.
When using AI dubbing for videos featuring real people, you should have their consent to translate and distribute their likeness in other languages. Synthesia's platform includes safeguards through its content moderation system and adherence to ethical AI practices, but the responsibility for ensuring proper permissions lies with you as the content creator to maintain legal compliance and respect for all parties involved.
Is AI dubbing free?
Yes, you can try Synthesia’s AI dubbing for free. Upload a video, auto‑detect the source language, and generate translated, lip‑synced versions to experience the full workflow end to end. The free experience lets you quickly evaluate voice preservation, timing, and subtitles on your own content with no complex setup.
For higher usage and scale, paid plans unlock more languages and voices, advanced editing controls, collaboration, and publishing options like smart links. These plans give teams the capacity and enterprise features needed to roll out multilingual content across campaigns, training, and product docs with confidence.
Which file formats and platforms are supported?
Synthesia’s AI Dubbing works in your web browser, no software to install. You can upload videos in MP4, MOV, or WebM.
