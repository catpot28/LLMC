FAQS
Frequently asked questions about AI humans
What is the best AI human generator tool?
The best tool for creating AI humans lets you generate realistic, expressive video performances without filming. Synthesia combines customizable AI humans, ready-made avatars, and personal AI avatars in one platform, making it easy to create professional videos that scale across teams, languages, and use cases.
Can I generate an AI person for free?
Yes. You can get started for free and use prompts to customize certain AI people, adjust outfits and environments, and prompt AI humans to act. Paid plans unlock full video creation and advanced features.
Can I customize AI humans?
Yes. You can customize AI humans by changing their appearance, voice, language, accent, and visual context. Depending on how you create your AI human, you can also control outfits, backgrounds, scenes, and performance style to match your brand and message.
Can AI humans act or perform on camera?
Yes. In Synthesia, AI humans can be prompted to perform dynamically, enabling more engaging, scene-based videos instead of static talking heads.
Do AI humans support multiple languages?
Yes. AI humans in Synthesia can speak in 160+ languages, with 1,000+ natural-sounding voices, making it easy to localize videos for global audiences without re-filming or manual translation workflows.
Do I need video production experience to create AI humans?
No. Synthesia's AI human generator and video creation platform is designed for non-technical users. You can create professional videos by choosing an AI human, adding a script, and generating the video without filming or editing skills.
What is an AI human?
An AI human is a digital person generated by artificial intelligence that can appear in video, speak naturally, and display realistic facial expressions and lip sync. AI humans are commonly used for training videos, marketing, onboarding, and internal communications.
What can I use AI humans for?
AI humans can be used to create training videos, onboarding content, internal communications, product explainers, marketing videos, and more. They allow you to deliver consistent, human-led messages at scale without relying on cameras, studios, or live presenters.
