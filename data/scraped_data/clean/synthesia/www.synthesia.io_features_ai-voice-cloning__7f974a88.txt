Frequently asked questions
Can I download my custom AI voice from Synthesia?
Yes, you can. After creating videos in Synthesia using your cloned voice, you can download the audio file and use it in any other editing software or tool.
In which languages can my cloned voice speak?
Your cloned voice can speak in the following languages: English, Hindi, Portuguese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Arabic, Russian, Korean, Indonesian, Italian, Dutch, Turkish, Polish, Swedish, Filipino, Malay, Romanian, Ukrainian, Greek, Czech, Danish, Finnish, Bulgarian, Croatian, Slovak, and Tamil. We are continually working to expand this list with more languages in the near future.
What are the requirements for my custom AI voice recording to ensure a high-quality AI voice copy?
For optimal voice cloning, we recommend recording your voice in a quiet room using a good quality microphone. Better recorded audio will result in the highest possible end quality for your cloned voice.
How will my voice sound in other languages?
Your cloned voice will retain some of the unique characteristics of the original speaker, though it may not completely preserve your accent. For example, a German speaker's cloned English voice might have a slight German accent but could sound more American than the actual speaker.
How does AI voice cloning work?
AI voice cloning uses advanced artificial intelligence to create a synthetic copy of an individual’s voice. This process involves multiple steps, primarily utilizing machine learning algorithms to replicate the distinctive features of the original voice.
Can I clone any voice?
Synthesia strictly prohibits cloning voices without explicit consent. We only allow the cloning of your own voice and require users to provide verbal consent during the voice cloning process.
How can I clone my voice with Synthesia?
To clone your voice, you'll need a Synthesia Enterprise Plan account.
Within your account, navigate to the “Voices” tab and click “New Voice.” You'll then name your voice, select the language and gender, and read a few paragraphs of text followed by providing your voice cloning consent.
Once the recording is complete, we will generate your cloned voice within a few minutes and notify you via email when it’s ready for preview and use.
Can I re-record my voice if I don’t like the end result?
Yes, you can delete any previously cloned voices and create new ones. Additionally, you might want to have multiple voices to convey different emotions, such as cheerful, sad, or narration styles.
