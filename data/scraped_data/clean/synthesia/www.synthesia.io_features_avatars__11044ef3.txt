Frequently asked questions about AI avatars
What is an AI avatar and how does it work?
An AI avatar is a realistic digital human, generated by artificial intelligence, that can present any script on screen without a camera, studio, or filming session.
Synthesia's stock avatars are built from footage of real actors who give their explicit consent and are paid each time their avatar is used. That footage trains an AI model, which learns the person's appearance, movement, and facial structure in detail. When you create a video, the AI generates new lip-sync, expressions, and body language to match your script, powered by Synthesia's Express technology. A voice from the 1,000+ library is layered on top, in any of 160+ languages.
Personal avatars work the same way but start with you: upload a photo or a short video, and Synthesia builds a digital twin from your own likeness and cloned voice.
Can I create an AI avatar video for free?
Yes. Synthesia's free plan gives you access to stock and customizable avatars, the core editor, and video generation in 160+ languages, with no credit card required. With customizable avatars on the free plan, you can prompt outfits, environments, and on-screen actions and generate AI-powered scenes with Veo 3.

What type of avatar is right for me?
It depends on your use case and how much you want the avatar to represent you or your brand.
Stock avatars
are the easiest starting point. Choose from 240+ ready-made avatars and start creating immediately, with no setup required. Best for teams who want to produce video content quickly without any configuration.
Customizable avatars
are ideal if you need the avatar to reflect a specific role, industry, or brand. Describe the outfit, setting, and actions in natural language and Veo 3 generates the scene. Best for creative, scene-based, or heavily branded visual content.
Personal avatars
are the right choice if you want video content that looks and sounds like you, without being on camera. Create your avatar from a single photo and optionally clone your voice. Best for individuals who communicate regularly and want to scale their presence across training, comms, or marketing.
Studio avatars
are for organizations that need the highest level of realism. Work with our team in a professional studio session to capture your likeness in full detail. Best for executives, spokespeople, and enterprise teams where on-screen presence and credibility matter most.
Can I customize AI avatars with my own branding, outfits, and style?
Yes, extensively. With customizable avatars, you prompt the outfit, setting, and on-screen actions in natural language and Veo 3 generates the scene. That includes industry-specific clothing like uniforms, hard hats, safety glasses, or lanyards, as well as branded outfits featuring your company colors and logo.
Apply your Brand Kit to layer logos, colors, and other assets across every video automatically.
Are AI avatars safe to use?
Yes, with multiple layers of protection built in. Every video goes through content moderation before delivery. Personal avatars require a live on-camera consent verification that cannot be uploaded or bypassed: the person shown in the consent video must match the person in the avatar footage, and they must be at least 18 years of age.
Synthesia is SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, and GDPR compliant, with a dedicated Trust and Safety team. The platform is also a member of the Content Authenticity Initiative, and avatar owners can request deletion of their data at any time.

Can I create an AI avatar of a colleague or team member?
Yes, through Synthesia's Personal Avatar Requests feature, available on Enterprise plans. You send the person a unique link by email, they complete the recording and live consent verification themselves, and the avatar is created on your account. The person does not need their own Synthesia account to complete the process.
The consent video must always be recorded live. You cannot upload a pre-recorded consent clip on someone else's behalf, and the person shown in the consent video must be the same individual as in the avatar footage. Once created, avatars and voices can be shared across your workspace on Enterprise plans.
What voices do the AI avatars have?
Synthesia includes 1,000+ AI voices across 160+ languages and accents, available across our avatar library. Some voices include multiple emotional styles, so you can match tone to content, whether that is a calm instructional delivery or a more energetic announcement. Specific avatars including Ellie, Michael, and Zola offer multiple voice styles, paired with script-synced body language. If you create a Personal Avatar, you can also record a voice sample to
clone your own voice
.
Do I need video editing experience to create AI avatar videos?
No. Synthesia is designed for people who have never made an avatar video before. You can start from a document, a prompt, a PowerPoint, or a blank canvas, and the AI generates the script, picks the scenes, and produces a finished avatar video with lip-sync and voice included.
If you want to go further, the editor lets you add animations, images, music, captions, motion graphics, interactivity, and more, but none of that requires technical skills. Most users create their first AI avatar video within minutes of signing up.
