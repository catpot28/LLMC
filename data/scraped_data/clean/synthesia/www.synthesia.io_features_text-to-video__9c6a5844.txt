Frequently asked questions about AI text to video
What makes Synthesia the best AI tool for text to video?
Synthesia is the
top-rated AI video generator on G2
, with over 2,500 reviews. It goes beyond other text to video tools by combining AI avatars, AI-generated visuals from Sora 2 and Veo 3.1, motion graphics, and full brand controls in one platform, so you can build complete, professional business videos from a single workspace.
Is Synthesia free to use for text to video?
Yes. The free plan lets you generate up to 10 minutes of video per month with access to a range of AI avatars and voiceovers in 160+ languages. Free videos include the Synthesia logo. To remove it and unlock downloads, custom avatars, and Brand Kit, you'll need a paid plan.
How long does it take to generate a video from text?
Most videos are ready within 2 to 5 minutes of hitting generate. Length and complexity affect the time, but there is no lengthy rendering queue. You can edit and regenerate as many times as you need before exporting.
How is Synthesia different from Sora or Veo?
Synthesia includes both. Sora 2 and Veo 3 are built directly into the platform, so you can generate cinematic b-roll and visual assets from a text prompt without leaving the editor. Alongside that, Synthesia's
AI avatars
give you a consistent human presenter for training, explainers, and business comms. The result is a complete video, not just a generative clip, with everything produced in one place.
What languages does Synthesia support for text to video?
Synthesia supports 160+ languages with built-in
AI voices
, so you can create a video in one language and
translate
it into others without re-recording. The platform includes a multilingual player that lets viewers select their preferred language directly in the video.
Can I convert existing content like articles or PowerPoint slides into videos?
Yes, Synthesia supports multiple input formats. You can upload a document or paste a URL and it will transform that content into a narrated, structured video. Try our:
PPT to video converter
PDF to video converter
URL to video converter
Is text to video AI suitable for enterprise use?
Yes. Synthesia is used by 50,000+ businesses. For enterprise teams, the platform includes Brand Kit for consistent styling, SSO, live collaboration, version control, and compliance certifications including SOC 2 Type II, ISO 42001, and GDPR.
Can I use Synthesia for commercial text to video projects?
Yes. Videos you create in Synthesia are yours to use commercially, including for marketing, client-facing training, sales materials, and customer comms. Enterprise plans include additional legal and compliance controls for teams with stricter requirements.
Can ChatGPT convert text to video?
ChatGPT generates text, not video. It can help you write or refine a script, but you need a separate tool to turn that into a finished video. Synthesia connects with ChatGPT so you can generate a script with GPT and convert it into a video in the same workflow.
