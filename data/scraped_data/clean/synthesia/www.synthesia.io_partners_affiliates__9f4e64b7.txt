Can I refer myself?
The affiliate is not allowed to refer themselves, and the affiliate will not receive commission on purchases coming from their account.
How much commission can I earn?
Commission is agreed in the amount of 25% of the net amount of the payments made by a qualified customer. There’s no limit on how much you can earn as an affiliate 🚀
What terms apply to Synthesia's Affiliate program?
Full affiliate terms can be found
here
. If you're unsure about any aspect of the program or need clarification on our terms, contact our affiliate support team at

What’s the cookie lifetime for affiliate sales?
The duration of the cookie is set to 60 days. This means that if a user comes to Synthesia's website via your affiliate link and purchases a plan within 60 days from clicking the link, you will receive a commission for that sale.
Where can I find my affiliate link and see my referrals?
