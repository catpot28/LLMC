new and popular AI tools

In this post I’ll share specific ways I'm using AI in my marketing strategies, and show you the specific tools and use cases I recommend.

Ru‎nway
{lite-youtube videoid="LnCpGsZgTkI" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/LnCpGsZgTkI/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
Runway
is a useful tool for creating creative short, reusable video assets, but the key issue I have struggled with is the randomness of its outputs. This unpredictability can make it challenging—and costly—to create videos that stay on-brand.
After some trial and error, I developed a simple workflow that ensures I consistently produce high-quality, on-brand video assets every time.
My workflow:
Midjourney (text to image)
Runway (image to video)
Topaz Labs (upscaling)
The trick is to generate brand-aligned images in
MidJourney
and then animate them using Runway's image-to-video tool, where I typically add motion effects, zooms, and transitions. This process ensures consistency with my brand while creating short, reusable video assets adaptable for various marketing formats.

has been my tool of choice for straightforward video editing. I use it to enhance my marketing clips by adding transitions, effects, and motion tracking.
The interface is intuitive, even for beginners, and its pre-designed templates help me create polished videos without much effort. The AI tools, like scene detection and auto-cropping, saved me hours on repetitive tasks while cleaning up my videos.
Op‎us Clip

is great for repurposing long-form content into short, engaging videos. I often use it to turn webinar recordings into bite-sized clips for social media, and the AI does an impressive job of identifying key moments to highlight.
It automatically adds captions and text overlays, which makes the content more accessible and visually appealing. While it’s not ideal for highly customized edits, its ability to quickly generate shareable clips has made it invaluable for my marketing efforts.
Co‎ntent Writing
AI content writing tools help me brainstorm ideas, create outlines, and even draft full pieces. Whenever I hit a creative block or need to save time, it’s there to provide a strong starting point. They can also help ensure my writing stays consistent and tailored to specific audiences.
AI Content Writing Tools
Ch‎atGPT
‎I often see recommendations for dedicated AI writing tools (e.g.
Jasper
), but I think that with the right prompts the best AI tool for writing assistance is still either
ChatGPT (with Canvas)
or
Claude
.
I often hear people say that Claude is better than ChatGPT for writing. I’ve also tried Claude for writing tasks, and while it has some strengths, I find ChatGPT more versatile. Claude seems to excel in crafting long, coherent narratives, like novel drafts, and many users praise its ability to maintain context over extended writing sessions. However, in my experience, ChatGPT offers better responsiveness for short-form content and brainstorming.
Where Claude feels more rigid, ChatGPT provides a flexible approach, allowing for easy tweaks and experimentation. For general marketing and content creation, ChatGPT continues to be my go-to.
Ry‎tr
‎If you insist on going for a dedicated writing tool,
Rytr
has my vote. It's useful for short writing tasks like product descriptions and social media posts. I've also tested using it to draft 500-word posts, even with technical terms, in a fraction of the time—revising is so much faster than starting from scratch. Its templates and plagiarism checker save time and effort, making it a useful tool for creating polished, ready-to-go content.
While it’s probably not suitable for longer or more complex writing projects, at just $9/month, it’s super afforable and good value for money.

Social Media Management Tools
Vi‎sta Social
{lite-youtube videoid="_DQUOY61P3Y" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/_DQUOY61P3Y/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
Vista Social
became a core part of my workflow for managing social media. One feature I rely on is the visual scheduling and preview tool—it ensures every post looks polished and aligns with my brand before going live. The AI Assistant has saved me a lot of time by generating captions and responding to messages effortlessly. I also love how it consolidates all my comments and reviews into one hub, making audience engagement much easier to handle. Plus, the analytics help me see what’s working and adjust my strategy as needed.
Fe‎edHive
{lite-youtube videoid="YYSPMTWjWbw" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/YYSPMTWjWbw/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
‎
FeedHive
is another of my favorite tools for managing social media. The AI suggestions and hashtag recommendations are a lifesaver when I’m short on ideas, and scheduling content is super easy. I love how the previews show exactly how posts will look, so everything stays on-brand. The analytics help me see what’s working, and the collaboration tools make getting approvals a breeze. It’s not great for tracking brand mentions, but for $19/month, it’s perfect for small businesseses.
In‎fluencity
{lite-youtube videoid="phyO-I0oxL4" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/phyO-I0oxL4/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
Influencity
is an AI-powered influencer platform. When I started using it I was hoping to simplify my influencer marketing campaigns, and it made a huge difference. It helped me find influencers who really fit my brand by letting me filter through tons of profiles based on things like location, industry, and follower count.
What I loved most was how easy it was to track results. I could see engagement metrics and campaign performance in real-time, which meant I wasn’t left guessing if my strategy was working. It’s made collaborations so much smoother and saved me a ton of time.
SE‎O
These AI SEO tools help me identify the best keywords, analyze competitors, and suggest ways to optimize my content for search engines. Whether it’s refining meta descriptions or improving website performance, they takes the guesswork out of SEO.
AI SEO tools

Surfer

Surfer NLP-optimized

Ad‎ Campaign Management
Managing ad campaigns has become so much easier with AI. It helps me create compelling ad copy, craft the right landing page, and even optimize bids in real time.
AI Ad Campaign Tools
Ad‎Creative
{lite-youtube videoid="TIpVTuINFZQ" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/TIpVTuINFZQ/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
AdCreative
is a helpful tool for generating professional ad designs quickly. By entering basic campaign details, I can create polished, platform-specific ads ready for use with minimal tweaks. I like the ability to produce multiple variations for A/B testing, which saves a lot of time.
While the tool excels at clean, modern designs, it struggles with creativity for niche or highly unique campaigns. The copy suggestions also felt formulaic, so I often rewrote them to fit my brand. Despite its limits, AdCreative is great for efficient ad creation and campaign optimization.
Cr‎eatopy
{lite-youtube videoid="vtrtIUfewjU" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/vtrtIUfewjU/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
Creatopy
has been a solid tool for managing ad variations across multiple platforms. One feature I’ve come to rely on is the feed system—it lets me pull ad variations straight from spreadsheets, which has made it possible for me to handle very high-volume campaigns. The resizing tool is also handy for adapting designs to different formats, though I still have to tweak things manually sometimes to get them just right.
Creatopy isn’t without its quirks. There are occasional bugs, but the support team is great—they’re quick to jump in and help, which makes a big difference when you’re on a deadline. The interface is straightforward and easy to use, and while it’s not perfect for more creative or complex projects, it’s been a real time-saver for simple, professional-looking ad campaigns.
‎E‎m‎ail Marketing
These tools help with a range of email marketing tasks including drafting personalized email copy and scheduling campaigns. I can create tailored emails for different segments of my audience without spending hours on manual work. The result is better engagement and conversion rates, all while saving me a ton of time.
AI Email Marketing Tools
Hu‎bSpot AI Email Writer
HubSpot’s AI email writer
has been a great tool for writing personalized email campaigns quickly. The AI generates drafts for everything from sales outreach to newsletters, and I can easily tweak the tone, length, or content to suit my needs.
Naturally it's integrated with HubSpot’s CRM, so emails are automatically logged, making it easy to track progress and follow up at the right time. Being able to access the assistant directly from Gmail is convenient workflow amd saves me from jumping between tabs.
The tool is free, with more advanced email features available in HubSpot’s paid Marketing Hub plans starting at $15/month.
Sa‎lesHandy
{lite-youtube videoid="b2G1Fjx2pI8" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/b2G1Fjx2pI8/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
SalesHandy
has been a practical AI tool for managing cold email campaigns. Its helpful email tracking feature lets me know exactly when messages are opened or links clicked so that I can strategically time my follow-ups. The mail merge and automated follow-up features make personalizing bulk emails simple, and has saved me hours of repetitive work while keeping messages tailored.
It doesn’t have the depth of a full CRM, but for lead generation and outreach it’s a straightforward and budget-friendly option. You can get started with the tool for just $9/month and it’s made my cold outreach much more efficient, so I'd say it's worth it.

Whether it’s creating social media posts or banners for a campaign, I can produce professional visuals quickly and without needing advanced design skills.

Figma AI
can generate design elements based on simple prompts, making the initial stages of designing mockups and layouts much faster. I’ve used it to quickly create UI wireframes that would have taken much longer to draft manually. It can also easily handle tedious tasks like resizing components and aligning layouts.
The AI-generated designs can sometimes feel a bit generic, and I’ve found that they often require additional tweaks to meet specific brand guidelines or aesthetic preferences. Still, it’s a great way to get past creative blocks or speed up repetitive parts of the design process.
Cu‎stomer Support
It's not
technically
marketing, but customer support contributes to the overall customer experience and perception of a brand, so I'm going to include it here!
AI has improved my ability to offer timely and personalized customer support - principally via automated chatbots that can handle common inquiries, freeing up time for the team and I to address more complex issues.
AI Customer Support Tools
Ti‎dio
{lite-youtube videoid="1FYOU5vd8ZU" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/1FYOU5vd8ZU/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
Tidio
has been a great addition to our customer support workflow. The Lyro AI Chatbot efficiently handles around 70% of routine inquiries, allowing me to focus on more complex customer needs. I also like the reply assistant feature, which helps transform my initial drafts into polished, professional customer service responses.
I’ve also found the Smart Views feature incredibly useful—it gives real-time insights into customer behavior, like which pages they’re browsing, making it easier to provide proactive support. Tidio also offers detailed analytics to track response times and satisfaction ratings.
Hi‎ver
{lite-youtube videoid="zWAsNhrt_i4" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/zWAsNhrt_i4/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
Hiver
has been a fantastic tool for managing our shared email inboxes and improving customer support efficiency. Its seamless integration with Gmail meant our team could jump right in without any learning curve.
The AI Summarizer has been particularly helpful, condensing lengthy email threads into clear overviews, which saves me time and ensures I never miss key points or follow-ups.
Harvey, Hiver’s AI assistant, keeps our inbox organized by filtering out repetitive or low-priority messages, letting us focus on more complex customer queries. I wish there were options for better customization for filtering, but Hiver has still been invaluable for streamlining email management and boosting our customer support team's productivity.
Ma‎rketing Analytics
AI has transformed how I analyze my marketing efforts. It automatically pulls data from multiple sources and turns it into actionable insights. I can see which campaigns are really working, understand audience behavior, and spot social trends in real time. This has helped me make data-driven decisions that improve performance without spending hours crunching numbers.
AI Marketing Analytics Tools
Br‎and24
{lite-youtube videoid="jqR7Plvcu8w" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/jqR7Plvcu8w/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
Brand24
has been a huge help for staying on top of brand mentions and customer sentiment. The new AI Reports feature have been particularly useful, giving me weekly and monthly summaries that highlight trends, spikes in mentions, and what’s driving them. For example, when one of our campaigns saw a sudden surge in mentions, the report pinpointed the cause and suggested ways to capitalize on it, like sharing user-generated content or doubling down on similar strategies. It’s quick, clear, and has saved me a ton of time analyzing data manually.
Se‎gmentStream
{lite-youtube videoid="jjdbLfZfulk" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/jjdbLfZfulk/hqdefault.jpg');" }
SegmentStream
solves the challenge of accurately attributing credit to upper-funnel campaigns and traffic sources in complex customer journeys, where traditional analytics fail due to cookie restrictions and multi-device behavior.
SegmentStream uses AI to analyze visitor behavior in order to predict which users are likely to convert later, even if they do so through different channels or devices.
The insights from this platform has allowed me to allocate ad spend more effectively and confidently invest in top-of-funnel campaigns that might otherwise seem a waste of time and money. It’s made a noticeable positive difference in how I approach budget decisions and my overall digital marketing outcomes.

How is AI used for marketing?
AI can be used to improve marketing in the following ways:
Video Content Creation
: Automating the production of professional-quality videos for social media, product demos, explainer videos, and personalized video campaigns.
Content Writing
: Assisting with generating blog posts, social media captions, email drafts, and more by using AI to create, refine, and tailor text to specific audiences.
Social Media Management
: Streamlining content scheduling, generating engaging posts, analyzing audience interactions, and managing multi-platform campaigns with minimal effort.
SEO
: Identifying relevant keywords, optimizing content for search engines, analyzing competitor strategies, and improving website performance.
Ad Campaign Management
: Creating high-performing ad creatives, automating A/B testing, optimizing targeting, and analyzing campaign performance for better ROI.
Email Marketing
: Crafting personalized email campaigns at scale, automating follow-ups, and tracking performance metrics to maximize engagement.
Design
: Generating visuals, templates, and animations quickly, making professional designs accessible to marketers without extensive graphic design skills.
Customer Support
: Enhancing response times and customer satisfaction with AI chatbots, automated query handling, and data-driven insights for human agents.
Marketing Analytics
: Analyzing complex data sets to uncover actionable insights, predict trends, measure ROI, and optimize overall marketing strategies.
Can AI completely replace human marketers?
No, AI complements human marketers by automating repetitive tasks and providing insights, but creativity, strategy, and nuanced decision-making still require human input.
Are AI tools worth the investment for small businesses?
Yes, AI tools are often cost-effective and can save small businesses time and resources by automating tasks like content creation, email marketing, and social media management.
