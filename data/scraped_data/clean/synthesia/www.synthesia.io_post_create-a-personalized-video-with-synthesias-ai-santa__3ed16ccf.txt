This holiday season, Synthesia is bringing Santa 🎅 to life like never before! We’re thrilled to announce the launch of our brand-new landing page where you can create your very own
customized Santa video
in just minutes.
You type in a short script—whether it’s a heartfelt holiday message, a funny inside joke, or a quirky update for your team—and voilà!
An AI-powered Personal Avatar
of Santa will deliver it directly on video.
But wait, there’s more! 🎁 You can choose from multiple AI Santa avatars to match your vibe:
Classic Santa
delivering your message from the North Pole
Naughty-or-nice Santa
reading a book next to a Christmas tree
Gifting Santa
joyfully handing out a present.
‎This is the perfect way to replace traditional Christmas cards or emails with something way more memorable. Whether it’s to delight your co-workers, surprise friends, or make family members smile, our AI Santa has you covered.
✨
How it works:
Visit our Santa landing page
https://synthesia.io/santa

Type in your script (up to 250 characters).
Choose your favorite Santa AI avatar.
Download or share your video—completely free!
So why send a plain old email when you can send a
ho-ho-hilarious
AI Santa video instead?
Head over to our landing page today and bring some extra magic to your holiday greetings!
