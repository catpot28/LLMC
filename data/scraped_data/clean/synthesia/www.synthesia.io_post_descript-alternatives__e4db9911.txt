Overview of Descript
If you're a content creator or thinking about making a YouTube channel or starting your
podcast
, you may have heard of Descript. If not, it's all good, as we'll give you a good overview of this video editor to start with.
Descript is a popular
AI-based video editing
toolkit that makes writing, transcribing, editing, collaborating, and sharing videos or podcasts easy.
However, as cool as it is, this platform has a weakness: it works best using a single video file. And let's face it, how many creators work with a single video file while editing and making their video? We don't know many, do you?
{lite-youtube videoid="Bl9wqNe5J8U" style="background-image: url(' https://img.youtube.com/vi/Bl9wqNe5J8U/hqdefault.jpg '); " }
Pros of Descript
👍
Easy to edit.
Multitrack audio editing software.
On-screen recording.
Automated transcription.
Full-featured waveform editor.
Non-destructive export to other audio applications.
Text-to-speech using your voice or an AI voice.
Invite or add team members to collaborate.
Cons of Descript
Not designed to work with multiple video files.
Limited timeline function.
Users comment that the application crashes on small devices.
Sound and video speakers do not synchronize with the transcript all the time.

Descript has 4 plans.
➡️ Free:
You can create 1 hour of video per month for free.
➡️ Creator:
10 hours per month for $12.

30 hours per month for $24

Contact their support center to customize your plan.

Best for those in need of a free screen recorder

Next on this list is Screencast-O-Matic or ScreenPal.
Screencast-O-Matic is a two-in-one software; on the one hand, it is a
screen recorder
, and on the other hand, it is a video editing tool.
Wondering how this alternative to Descript works?
Easy-peasy ...
First, you can record your screen (for free) and create your
tutorial video
. Once you have recorded your how-to video, you can access the basic video editing features for free or its advanced options for a reasonable price. The last step is to download or export your video.
Pros of Screencast-O-Matic
👍
Screen capture and video recording.
Free version.
Edit video is easy.
Performance Metrics.
Video streaming.
Meeting management.
The app is available.
Online storage and publishing.
Cons of Screencast-O-Matic
The editing tools in the free version are limited.
Some users have questioned the audio quality.
The free version of Screen O-Matic does not support full-screen recording.
💸 Pricing
As you know, this video editing software is great if you need to record your screen and certain editing features for free, but in addition, Screencast-O-Matic offers 4 different subscriptions:
➡️ Solo Deluxe:
$3 monthly paid annually.
➡️ Solo Premier:
$6 monthly paid annually.
➡️ Solo Max:
$10 monthly, paid annually.
➡️Team Business:
$8 monthly per user.
How does it compare to Descript?
The first and biggest difference between these two video editors is that you can technically create a free video tutorial with Screen-O-Matic.
Also, while Descript is better for new content creators who need a video for social media or a Podcast, Screen-O-Matic is better for how-to videos.
Alternative 3: Adobe Premiere Pro
The best alternative for professionals
{lite-youtube videoid="imK3tZs6PX8" style="background-image: url(' https://img.youtube.com/vi/imK3tZs6PX8/hqdefault.jpg '); " }
This may be your first time editing a video; however, for some reason, the name Adobe Premiere Pro sounds quite familiar to you.
Adobe Premiere Pro is part of the Adobe family, which owns famous editing tools like Photoshop and Lightroom. Does it make sense now why it sounds so familiar?
Adobe Premiere Pro is video editing software that is up to the task of whatever you need to create an impressive video. However, like other Adobe products, it requires more knowledge of editing tools and concepts, which makes it a better choice for those creators with video editing experience.
Pros of Adobe Premiere Pro
👍
You can import sequences from any file format.
Blur and sharpening effects.
Motion graphics templates.
Supports multiple video files.
Delates background noise.
Text-based editing
Powerful editing tools.
Cool drag-and-drop video effects.
Compatible with virtual reality.
Cons of Adobe Premiere Pro
Not so easy to use for beginners.
High system requirements.
Limited compatibility with other programs outside the Adobe family.
Expensive, and there is no "basic alternative subscription."
💸 Pricing
Adobe Premiere Pro has a free trial version; after that, you can purchase the services in 3 different subscriptions.
➡️
Annual paid monthly for $20.99
➡️
Annual prepaid for $239.88
➡️
Monthly for $31.49
How does it compare to Descript?
Adobe Premiere and Descript are excellent choices for content creators who want to create innovative videos. Nevertheless, Adobe Premiere Pro is a better alternative for those with previous video editing experience or for professional video editors that master audio.

We both know that social media users hardly ever take more than 60 seconds to watch a video. So with that information, the best thing to do is to use Piktochart Video to cut, edit, and create a new and short video highlighting the best moments of your webinar.
Pros of Piktochart Video

Cons of Piktochart Video

Best for those who prefer to work from their phone

Let's face it, our phone has become an extension of our body, almost like a second hand, which explains why many prefer to work on their phones and not on a computer.
That's why we've decided to bring to the conversation CapCut, a great alternative that is ideal for those who edit and create videos with their phones.
CapCut is a very popular video editor among TikTokers, who use its templates and all the features to design and create trending videos.
As a bonus, CapCut also has an online browser version if you prefer to work using a computer.
Pros of CapCut
👍
You can edit your videos from virtually anywhere.
Suitable for iOS and Android.
Online editing version available.
Background removal
Various templates.
Cool effects, filters, and transitions.
Easy to export.
Extensive music library.
Voice-to-text and text-to-speech.
Cons of CapCut
Lack of advanced editing features
No support for landscape mode on smartphones and tablets
Cannot set frame rate higher than 60 FPS
Applications have bugs and glitches
💸 Pricing
CapCut, like some other alternatives, offers a free option; however, this alternative will limit your access to certain features or tools.
If what you want is not included in the free version, CapCut has different prices that will vary depending on your region.
How does it compare to Descript?
Characterized by multiple available templates and trendy visual effects, CapCut is a favorite tool among TikTokers.
Moreover, CapCut features an online editing version, apps, and a downloadable system, unlike Descript.
