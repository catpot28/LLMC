A compelling story has the power to transform the world—or at least your business metrics.
Take this for example:

While Dropbox’s success story remains legendary, the landscape of explainer videos has evolved significantly since then.

explainer video software

To help you navigate this dynamic market, we’ve rounded up the top 20 explainer video production companies of 2026. Let’s dive in!
How to choose an explainer video company
An explainer video is a short-form video made for marketing or sales purposes that highlights a company's product, service, or business idea compellingly and efficiently. So, plenty of video production companies can create such explainers, from AI-driven platforms to traditional animation studios.
When selecting an explainer video company, consider the following key factors to ensure your final video aligns with your vision and goals.
Portfolio & creativity
Look at the company's portfolio and see if you can picture your project there. Do their style and quality match what you're envisioning for your target audience?
Each brand and message is unique, so a one-size-fits-all creative approach rarely captures the audience's attention in explainer videos. Your chosen explainer video company should tailor its services to meet your needs and creatively convey your message.
Experience & expertise
An explainer video company that has worked on topics relevant to your industry or has a proven track record with similar clients can bring valuable insights and understanding to your project. These industry-specific insights will be useful, once again, for capturing the audience's attention with messages that resonate.
Look for a company with a well-structured
video production process
that aligns with your timeline, ensuring your project is completed efficiently and on schedule.
Cost and value
While
cost
is always a consideration, focus more on the value offered than just the price. For example, choosing an explainer video company that is responsive and open to collaboration will make the creative process smoother and more enjoyable for you. And that often means a better output.
It's also a good idea to find a company or solution that offers a streamlined process with additional services, such as marketing and distribution support, which can further fast-track your project.
Check if they're on trend with technology
Look on their blog and social media to see if the company values constantly learning and innovating in video production. The more innovative ideas an explainer video company embraces, the more it has an edge in creating engaging and relevant videos on a tighter budget.
Companies that utilize AI and productivity tools are adept at innovative ideas and can often offer you more bang for your buck. Most of their time is spent refining your video to suit your target audience and marketing funnel rather than doing the more mundane parts of
video creation
(such as resizing videos).

What is the purpose of explainer videos?
Explainer videos serve marketing or sales purposes and highlight a company's product, service, or business idea in a compelling and efficient way. They use short, engaging animations or live-action.
How much does a 30-second explainer video cost?
A 30-second explainer video typically costs
$250 to $1000
or more. The price can vary based on the video's style, complexity, and production quality. High-quality videos can cost even $20,000.
Which companies need explainer videos the most?
Companies that benefit most from explainer videos include tech startups, which often have innovative but complex products; healthcare and pharmaceutical companies, needing to simplify medical information for their target audience; educational technology firms, aiming to make learning engaging; and any business introducing a new concept or service that requires clear, concise explanation.
