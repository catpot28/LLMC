Be sure to check out our comprehensive guide on
how to create an explainer video
before you get started on your video.
What makes a good explainer video?
Be Unique—Stand Out from the Crowd
Your explainer video should be a reflection of your brand’s personality, not a carbon copy of what’s already out there. Find a fresh angle, use distinctive visuals, and make sure your message feels original.
Inject Humor to Boost Engagement
A little comedy can go a long way in making your video more shareable. A well-placed joke or lighthearted moment keeps viewers entertained while ensuring they remember your message.
Keep It Clear and Concise
The whole point of an explainer video is to simplify complex ideas, not overcomplicate them. Stick to the essentials and avoid unnecessary fluff to make your content easy to digest.
Know Your Audience
Think about who you're trying to reach and tailor your video style to their preferences. A playful, animated approach might work for a younger crowd, while a sleek, professional tone may be better suited for B2B audiences.
How to Make an Explainer Video
Before diving into video creation, take a moment to define your objectives. What do you want viewers to learn or do after watching? Once that’s clear, follow these key steps:
1) Craft a Strong Script
Every great explainer video starts with a solid script. A common framework includes:
Introducing a relatable problem
Envisioning a world where the problem is solved (“Imagine if…”)
Presenting your product or service as the perfect solution
2) Find the Right Voice Over Talent
A compelling voice over adds professionalism and keeps viewers engaged. While DIY recording is an option, hiring a professional often delivers better results. Platforms like
Fiverr
offer budget-friendly choices, while
VoiceBunny
provides higher-end options.
3) Select the Best Explainer Video Software
There are plenty of user-friendly tools designed for making explainer videos, many of which offer free trials. The right choice mostly depends on which type of explainer video you want to make...
What are the main types of explainer videos?
Live action
{lite-youtube videoid="wwm8TAKjN04" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/wwm8TAKjN04/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
A
live-action explainer video
uses real people, settings, and props to demonstrate a product, service, or concept. This style builds trust and creates a personal connection with the audience, making it ideal for showcasing tangible products, company culture, or human-centered stories. It often involves professional filming, editing, and scripting for a polished look.
AI Avatar
{lite-youtube videoid="JkENp3eiPXA" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/JkENp3eiPXA/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
An
AI avatar explainer video
features a
digital character
generated using
platforms powered by artificial intelligence
. These
AI avatars
deliver the message in a lifelike, customizable, and scalable way. It’s a cost-effective and efficient option for creating professional-looking videos that removes the need for live actors or extensive production setups.
2D animated
{lite-youtube videoid="MlLw7KdEyA8" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/MlLw7KdEyA8/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
A
2D animated explainer video
uses flat, two-dimensional graphics to convey a story or explain a concept. It’s characterized by its simplicity and versatility, making it ideal for engaging audiences and breaking down complex ideas in a visually appealing and easy-to-understand way.
3D animated
{lite-youtube videoid="U-Sv_pv6nkg" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/U-Sv_pv6nkg/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
A
3D animated explainer video
uses three-dimensional visuals to create realistic or stylized representations of objects, characters, or environments. This style adds depth and detail, making it ideal for showcasing products, demonstrating complex processes, or creating immersive storytelling experiences. It’s especially effective for industries like technology, engineering, and architecture.
Whiteboard explainer
{lite-youtube videoid="7X3EcggN-84" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/7X3EcggN-84/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
A
whiteboard explainer video
is a type of animation where illustrations and text are drawn on a whiteboard or white background, often accompanied by narration. It mimics the effect of someone teaching by drawing, making it highly engaging and effective for explaining complex concepts in a simple, visual way.
Stop-motion animated
{lite-youtube videoid="64hM5XuCVq4" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/64hM5XuCVq4/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
A
stop-motion animated explainer video
is created by capturing a series of individual photos of physical objects or scenes, moving them slightly between each shot. When played in sequence, these images create the illusion of motion. This technique gives the video a unique, handcrafted feel, often used to convey creativity, charm, or authenticity.
Kinetic typography
{lite-youtube videoid="ZfomZ6EeWGs" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/ZfomZ6EeWGs/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
A
kinetic typography explainer video
focuses on animated text to convey a message or tell a story. The text moves, transforms, and interacts dynamically with the screen, often synchronized with music or narration. This style is highly effective for emphasizing key points and creating an energetic, attention-grabbing presentation.
Screencast
{lite-youtube videoid="ASxoHquGoXc" style="background-image:url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/ASxoHquGoXc/maxresdefault.jpg');" }
A
screencast explainer video
is a recording of a computer or device screen, often paired with voiceover narration, to demonstrate how software, websites, or digital tools work. It’s an effective and straightforward way to guide viewers through processes or features step-by-step.
