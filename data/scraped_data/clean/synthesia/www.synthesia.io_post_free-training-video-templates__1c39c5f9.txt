16 Free Training Video Templates You Can Use Today
Written by
Kevin Alster
March 12, 2026
Create engaging training videos in 160+ languages.
Get started for FREE
In this article
Text Link
Synthesia offers training video templates built around common learning scenarios.
Each template is designed for short, focused videos that work seamlessly on screen, helping you
quickly create engaging training videos
without starting from scratch.
You can use each template on its own or combine several into a larger
training program
as needed.
These templates include:
A clear, ready-made video structure
Scene-by-scene visual layouts
Built-in guidance for narration that aligns with on-screen visuals
With the structure already in place, you can focus on your message and confidently deliver
video-based training
that is clear, effective, and easy to follow.
1. Standard training
This template is built for structured, instructor-led learning. It helps you present information in a clear, logical flow that is easy to follow.
Use it to quickly create engaging
employee training videos
for onboarding, compliance, or skills development where consistency and clarity matter most.
2. Interactive training
Create an interactive training video
with quizzes, branching paths, and clickable elements. This template encourages active participation instead of passive viewing.
It is ideal for teams that want to make engaging interactive training videos that improve retention and reinforce learning through feedback.
3. Onboarding
‍
This template helps you
create an onboarding video
that makes a strong first impression. Introduce your mission, tools, and workflows in a structured, welcoming format.
It is designed to set expectations clearly while helping new hires feel confident from day one.
4. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
Create a clear and reliable SOP video
that breaks processes into simple, visual steps. Teams can follow the same workflow every time.
A well-structured video SOP helps reduce errors, maintain consistency, and preserve operational knowledge across roles and locations.
5. Compliance training
‍
Use this template to
create a compliance training video
that explains policies and regulations in straightforward terms.
You can also add quizzes or checks to confirm understanding and support accountability.
6. Cybersecurity training
‍
Build a cybersecurity training video
that covers threats like phishing, password misuse, and safe data handling.
This template helps you turn complex guidance into practical lessons employees can remember and apply immediately.
7. Product demos
‍
‍
Create a product demo video
that clearly walks viewers through features and real-world use cases.
It is ideal for marketing, onboarding, or support content where clarity and speed are key.
8. Technical skills training
‍
This template helps you
create a software training video
that aligns narration directly with on-screen actions.
It makes complex tools and systems easier to understand with step-by-step visual guidance.
9. Customer service training
‍
Create a customer service training video
that strengthens communication and builds confidence.
It works well for role-play scenarios, objection handling, and example-driven learning.
10. Health and safety
‍
Use this template to
create a health and safety training video
that clearly explains procedures, hazards, and emergency responses.
It is designed for situations where accuracy, compliance, and risk reduction are critical.
11. Sales training
‍
This template is built for creating practical sales training videos that show reps exactly how to communicate value and move deals forward.
Use it to break down effective pitches, demonstrate strong discovery techniques, and reinforce what good looks like at each stage of the sales process.
12. Leadership development
‍
Create a leadership training video
focused on one core idea at a time, such as decision-making or communication.
This format supports reflection, reinforcement, and practical application in the workplace.
13. Scenario-based training
‍
This template helps you
create a scenario-based training video
built around realistic workplace situations.
Learners explore choices and consequences in a safe environment, making it ideal for complex or high-stakes topics.
14. Microlearning video
Create short, focused lessons that deliver one clear takeaway at a time. This template is designed to help you quickly
create engaging microlearning videos
that fit into busy schedules.
Use it for quick refreshers, skill reinforcement, or bite-sized updates that are easy to watch and easy to remember.
15. Interactive corporate quiz
A corporate quiz video that tests knowledge and reinforces key messages. This template makes it simple to build engaging, interactive moments into your training.
Use it as a standalone assessment or pair it with other formats to check understanding and improve retention.
16. Company culture video
Create a
company culture video
that brings your values, mission, and ways of working to life. This template helps you share what makes your organization unique in a clear and authentic way.
Use it to align teams, welcome new hires, or reinforce culture across locations with engaging, easy-to-follow storytelling.
