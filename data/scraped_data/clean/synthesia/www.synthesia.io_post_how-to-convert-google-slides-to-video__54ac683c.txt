How to Convert Google Slides to Video for Free (in 1 Minute)
Written by
Kyle Odefey
January 27, 2026
Convert your Google slides into engaging videos in 160+ languages.
Get started for FREE
In this article
Text Link
Google Slides are great for live presentations, but they are not ideal when you need to share information asynchronously, onboard new hires, or train teams across time zones.
A video version of your slides lets you add narration, visual continuity, and a human presence, so your audience can watch and learn on their own schedule.
While Google Slides does not offer a native way to turn a deck into a professional narrated video with presenters, you can easily convert your presentation into a video using Synthesia.
By exporting your Slides to PowerPoint and importing them into Synthesia, you can transform each slide into a scene with AI avatars, natural voiceovers, captions, and translations.
Here is how to
convert Google Slides to video
in just a few steps.
Step 1: Export your Google Slides as PowerPoint
Open your Google Slides presentation and go to
File → Download → Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx)
.
EXPORTING MY SLIDES AS A PPTX FILE
This will download your deck as a PowerPoint file, preserving slide layouts, images, and speaker notes, which can later be used as your
video script
.
Step 2: Create a new video in Synthesia
Click
here
to log in or to sign up for a free account.
LOG INTO SYNTHESIA
On the Synthesia homepage choose the option to
Import PowerPoint / PPTX
to create a video from your slides.
SELECT 'IMPORT POWERPOINT'
Step 3: Upload your PPTX file
Upload the PowerPoint file you just downloaded from Google Slides.
UPLOADING MY GOOGLE SLIDES AS A PPTX FILE
Synthesia will automatically detect each slide and prepare it as a video scene.
Step 4: Enable editable import
IMPORT OPTIONS
There are now 3 options. Here’s what each does:
Convert speaker notes into script
Takes the speaker notes from each slide and turns them into the
scene script
in Synthesia.
One slide’s notes → one scene’s script (which the avatar will read).
Convert slides to editable text, shapes, and media
Imports slide content as
editable elements
on the canvas instead of a flat image.
Text boxes become editable text.
Shapes, images, and embedded videos come in as separate objects you can move, resize, recolor (where supported), or replace.
Add avatar to each scene
Automatically places a
Synthesia AI avatar
on every imported slide/scene.
Saves you from manually adding an avatar to each scene after import.
Before clicking 'Import' make sure that at least the first two options are turned on (like you see in the image above)
Step 5: Let Synthesia process your presentation
Your deck will now be processed. This usually takes a few seconds, but larger presentations may take a bit longer.
Synthesia supports decks up to 100 slides or 1GB in size.
CONVERTING MY SLIDES TO VIDEO
Step 6: Review and polish in the editor
EDITING MY VIDEO
The editor is where you can turn your imported slides into complete, engaging videos using:
Realistic AI avatars and natural-sounding
AI voiceovers
to present each slide
Script-to-speech from your speaker notes with automatic timing across scenes
Built-in captions and subtitles with fully editable timings
AI translation
to localize voiceovers and captions at scale
Brand controls including colors, fonts, logos, lower thirds, and reusable styles
Templates
for common formats like training, onboarding, and product demos
Interactive video elements
such as quizzes, CTAs, and branching scenarios
Collaboration tools like shareable links, comments, and version history for reviews
You can also enhance your slides with additional generative visuals directly inside the editor, and combine them with AI-generated video clips, images, and other assets to create more dynamic, story-driven presentations.
Step 7: Generate and share your video
Preview your full video, then generate it.
You can:
Download the MP4
Share a link
Embed it in your LMS, website, or knowledge base
Translate it into 160+ languages with AI voiceovers and captions

Frequently asked questions
Is there a way to convert Google Slides to video directly?
Google Slides does not natively export to narrated video with avatars. The most reliable workflow today is exporting to PowerPoint and importing the file into Synthesia, where slides become fully editable video scenes.
Do speaker notes become the voiceover automatically?
Yes. When you enable editable import, each slide’s speaker notes are converted into the script for that scene, which is then read by an AI voice or avatar.
Can I use this for training and onboarding videos?
Absolutely. This is one of the most common use cases. You can turn slide decks into structured, narrated learning videos with chapters, quizzes, and multilingual versions.
Is this available on the free plan?
You can import PowerPoint and preview the video for free. Final export and advanced features depend on your plan, but you can test the full workflow without upfront payment.
Can I translate my Google Slides video into other languages?
Yes. Once your video is in Synthesia, you can translate the script and regenerate the video in 160+ languages with localized voiceovers and subtitles in just a few clicks.
