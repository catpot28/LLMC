Sora 2 is one of the most advanced AI video models available today, capable of generating realistic, cinematic video clips from simple text prompts.
While you typically need a subscription to use Sora 2, you can try it for free using Synthesia’s AI Playground. This lets you generate short video clips directly in your browser, with no downloads or technical setup required.
Here is how to use Sora 2 for free in just a few steps.
Step 1: Sign up for a free Synthesia account
Start by
creating a free Synthesia account
. You can sign up using your email address or a Google account.
Verify via the code sent to your inbox.
Alternatively, to create a video with Veo 3,
check out this quick guide on how to use Veo 3 for free.
Step 2: Navigate to the AI Playground
After logging in and going through the onboarding steps, navigate to the
AI Playground
which can be found in the left-hand sidebar from the main Synthesia dashboard.
The AI Playground is where you can generate video clips and images using text prompts. It allows you to test multiple generative AI models in one place and quickly compare results.

💡 Tips for Sora 2 prompts

Keep it short and precise:
3–6 sentences that lock in the essentials.

Subject and setting:
clearly state who or what is in the scene, and where it takes place.

Style and mood:
define the look and feel — cinematic, documentary, photorealistic; calm, energetic, moody.

Camera and motion:
specify movement and framing — locked tripod, slow dolly-in, gentle orbit.

Lighting:
guide the scene with cues like soft key, rim light, high-key, or low contrast.

Must-haves or exclusions:
call out constraints such as “minimal background,” “no handheld shake,” or brand colour cues.

‍
Step 3: Input your prompt
In the prompt field, describe the video you want to generate. The more specific your prompt is, the better the results will be.

💡 How to iterate on Sora 2 prompts

Change one variable at a time: motion, lighting, or composition

Use negative prompts to remove artefacts: “no flicker,” “no fast zooms”

If it matters, say it once, clearly. Trim vague adjectives.

Step 4: Generate video
After submitting your prompt, your video clip will begin generating.
It will take a few moments to generate, Whilst waiting, you can explore the pre-generated visuals in the AI Playground, or explore other areas of the Synthesia platform.
Step 6: Share, download, or start creating with your clip
Once your clip is ready, you can:
Preview the full video
Share it using a link or invite collaborators
Download the clip in your preferred format
Reuse the prompt and make adjustments to change the output
Step 7: Create a video from your clip in the Synthesia editor
To turn your Sora 2 clip into a complete video, open it in the Synthesia editor by clicking 'Continue in editor'.
‍

Built in captions and subtitles with fully editable timings
Brand controls including colours, fonts, logos, lower thirds, and reusable styles
Templates for common video formats, with duplication flows to help you move faster
Collaboration tools like shareable links, comments, and version history for reviews
You can also generate additional Sora 2clips directly inside the editor, as well as other generative AI assets with models like Veo 3, FLUX.2 and Nano Banana Pro.
