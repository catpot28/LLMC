Introducing AI Video Assistant, a new way to turn text into engaging videos
Written by
Sundar Solai
January 31, 2024
Create AI videos with 240+ avatars in 160+ languages.
Get started for FREE
In this article
Text Link
Convert your documents into videos with this easy-to-use tool from Synthesia
Synthesia, the world’s leading AI video creation platform for enterprises, is introducing a brand new feature called AI Video Assistant that helps make the video creation process easier and more accessible for all businesses and users.
AI Video Assistant helps users directly create a video based on existing public links, files, or other text-based sources. This process will help streamline the process of converting text into video, bypassing the need to write a script from scratch or design the layout of the video manually in Synthesia. AI Video Assistant has been in testing for a few weeks, and
is launching publicly for all Synthesia users on January 31
.
How to use the AI Video Assistant
Users can upload files in
.pdf,

.doc, .docx, or .txt formats
, as well as public links. For now, there’s a 4500-word limit on documents and websites that AI Video Assistant will transform into a video.
Uploading a file and generating the real-time script takes only a few minutes and users have the ability to view, stop, and regenerate the script as needed.
When creating the video, users have the option to choose from one of our templates and then refine the content to match a specific audience, objective, or tone. Users can also edit the video's language or how many scenes it has.
{lite-youtube videoid="7OzA72JVn58" style="background-image: url('https://img.youtube.com/vi/7OzA72JVn58/hqdefault.jpg');" }
AI Video Assistant can be used by anyone who wants to repurpose existing text-based content into videos; for example, content marketers at software companies can turn
existing developer-focused blog posts into videos
for social networks such as TikTok or YouTube; or owners of a pet store who have written nutrition tips for different dog breeds can create videos that help their customers better understand a dog’s specific nutrition needs; or an HR lead at a law firm who has lengthy onboarding and compliance documents can make them easier to access by transforming them into a series of bite-sized videos and instantly translate them for colleagues around the world.
Why is the AI Video Assistant important?
When companies hand out employee information, it usually comes in the form of a long, dense handbook, PDF, or deck. These physical, text-based documents often go unread, leaving employees without vital information. Furthermore, people remember
95% of what they watch in videos
, but only 10% of what they read in text. AI Video Assistant allows users to turn disorganized, text-based information into memorable and appealing videos that stick with audiences long after any presentation.
Synthesia is always looking for new ways to make video production simple and intuitive without the need for cameras or studios. AI Video Assistant is the latest breakthrough in bringing our users the ability to produce high-quality video content at a fraction of the time and cost of traditional video productions.

here
.
