No marketing strategy is complete without video. 📹
After all, when done correctly, video marketing can help you demonstrate an idea in creative ways and reach new audiences with your message.
We've unpacked eight fantastic examples of smart businesses taking their marketing videos. To make it easier to compare and understand each of the video marketing examples, they’re broken up into
five different categories
for your video marketing efforts.
Let’s get started with the most popular video category, explainer videos.
Three explainer video examples from competing brands
Ever heard of a “digital whiteboard”?
It’s a huge industry that’s drawing in millions of dollars every year in software subscription fees. There are thousands of companies that offer these services. We’re going to look at three video marketing examples from competing whiteboard brands: Miro, Whimsical, and FigJam.
1️⃣ Miro is the B2B solution. It’s popular with large enterprises and SMBs in the marketing space. In the video, you’ll see a huge emphasis on working with big teams on live video calls:

Example of an effective onboarding video
Onboarding videos walk new users through your software or services. They can introduce a new idea, or actually walk users through how to complete various tasks.
Onboarding videos tend to be
short, to the point, and informative rather than entertaining
. In the case of complicated software or systems, it’s best practice to create lots of small onboarding videos that each focus on a specific task.
In this video, we’re taught how to use Dixa’s agent dashboard. Onboarding videos like this help brand new users navigate your software without getting confused or overwhelmed.

Hubspot’s social media videos on Twitter
Unless you rescue marooned sailors on desert islands (in which case, please stop reading this and go searching for those sailors!), chances are your consumer base spends a significant time scrolling social media.
So should you.
Spend time scrolling to find out what content your target audience watches and which viral video trends could apply to your brand. And then go create similar content that can grab attention.
Videos for social media can take on different forms for different platforms. Your YouTube channel, Twitter, and even TikTok profile will all have different follower bases, and different influencers and cater to different styles of videos, from
reviews, walk-throughs, or a quick behind-the-scenes video
.

Two types of how-to video marketing strategies

Did you know?

In 2022, just under
50%
of internet users watched a how-to or tutorial video every week.

Whether you’ve got a flat tire or your boss just mentioned
another
marketing strategy you haven’t heard of… YouTube is your best friend.
via GIPHY
