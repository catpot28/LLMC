Beyond the Demo
How companies are overcoming the AI productivity paradox

Around 95% of enterprise GenAI pilots fail to achieve a measurable return on investment. Many projects remain at the experimental stage, proof-of-concepts never reach production, and even where AI is deployed, its economic impact is often limited.

The causes of this AI productivity paradox lie outside the technology itself. The real problems are organisational in nature. In Germany, 64 per cent of employees use AI tools at least occasionally. This is a high adoption rate by international standards. Individual productivity gains are therefore already taking place, but are not being implemented at company level.

Companies that focus on specific problems, systematically integrate informal use, redesign workflows and set realistic goals can turn the promise of AI into real value creation

We support companies in overcoming the AI productivity paradox. Together, we identify relevant use cases, prioritise them according to their value and feasibility, and develop scalable AI solutions. This enables AI to generate measurable value in everyday business operations.
