Climate and the environment

Responsible use of natural resources is an important goal for us. We are constantly striving to reduce our carbon footprint and promote environmentally friendly practices.

This is reflected in various initiatives, including measures to reduce CO
2
emissions and the use of green energy in our offices. We also pursue a sustainable strategy with regard to the use of electronic devices and work with Klim, a start-up that promotes regenerative agriculture.

Environment. Climate Action. Herzenswald.

In our digital industry, CO₂ emissions are unavoidable. That’s why we’ve looked for ways to support the environment—regionally and sustainably.

Climate action
, close to home.

We support climate action through regional projects—partnering with Klim. The agritech platform is a great fit for us: it’s digital and enables farmers to transition to regenerative agriculture. Through carbon farming, CO₂ is captured from the atmosphere and stored in the soil—improving soil health and biodiversity in the long term.

Klim

Herzenswald through
teamwork
.

Together with their families and friends, our employees planted 300 trees. With the Herzenswald project, we’re supporting reforestation in the Taunus region—meaningful, sustainable, and a great team effort that’s also fun.
