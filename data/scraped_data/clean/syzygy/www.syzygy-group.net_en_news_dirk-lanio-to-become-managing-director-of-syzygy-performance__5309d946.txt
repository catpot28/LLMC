Dirk Lanio to become managing director of SYZYGY Performance

Published

November 14, 2022

SYZYGY Performance, one of the leading digital media and performance marketing agencies, has appointed Dirk Lanio as a new managing director with effect from 1 April 2023. Lanio (53) will be responsible for developing new business areas and models, for the strategic direction of the agency and for new business. He will lead SYZYGY Performance together with existing managing director Dr André Vieregge.

Dirk Lanio is a recognised digital marketing expert with many years of experience across all areas of digital marketing and communications. He joins SYZYGY Performance from Hamburg-based advertising agency Grabarz & Partner, where he drove forward digital development and added new services to the firm’s portfolio as managing director. He played a key role in digital transformation and the development of digital campaigns for clients such as Volkswagen, Lufthansa Technik and, most recently, Deutsche Bahn’s HR marketing department. Lanio is an advocate of data-driven communication and campaigns, and intends to expand SYZYGY Performance further when he joins the agency in April 2023.

SYZYGY Performance develops end-to-end digital marketing solutions for clients including the German federal government, Miles & More, Commerzbank and Samsung. In addition to planning and managing all relevant channels, the agency focuses on coordinating key tasks through strategy development, consulting and project management. Around 120 people are employed at its offices in Hamburg and Munich.

Dirk Lanio is an excellent addition to our team. He has extensive knowledge of the synergies between content creation and campaign management. I’m looking forward to continuing to drive our expansion programme together with Dirk.

Dr André Vieregge

Managing Director SYZYGY Performance

SYZYGY Performance is an integral part of the SYZYGY GROUP and is at the forefront of media competence and media performance in this market. In Dirk Lanio, we have secured an experienced and successful managing director with an entrepreneurial mindset and a proven ability to successfully deliver digital strategies and campaigns. Dirk’s expertise and experience around digital transformation and communication is the perfect fit for us. He rounds out the SYZYGY Performance management team and will enable us to boost the integration of performance and digital media services within the Group.

Franziska von Lewinski

CEO SYZYGY GROUP

Specialist for Performance Marketing
