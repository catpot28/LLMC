Investor Day

Investor Day

INVESTOR DAY 2025

Tuesday, 11 November, 2025, Athens, Greece

Titan Cement International SA is hosting today an Investor Day in Athens, Greece. Members of the management team will present to institutional investors, analysts, banks, and stockbrokers Titan’s strategic directions, performance, priorities, and ambitious growth targets set for 2026, which aim to shape the future of the Group and drive long-term stakeholders’ value. A visit to the Group’s “Kamari” cement plant will follow the morning presentations, showcasing part of the Group’s modern asset base, product variety, decarbonization initiatives, and novel digital operating model.

On behalf of TITAN Group and its Executive Committee, Mr. Marcel Cobuz, Chair of the Group Executive Committee, and Mr. Michael Colakides, Managing Director of Titan SA and Group CFO, have invited institutional investors, analysts, banks, and stockbrokers to TITAN’s 2025 Investor Day, to take place on November 11, 2025, in Athens, Greece. The event will feature a comprehensive presentation on the Group’s updated strategy and long-term vision.
