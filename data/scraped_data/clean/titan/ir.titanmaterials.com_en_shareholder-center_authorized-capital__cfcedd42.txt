Share Capital

The share capital of the company amounts to EUR 959,347,807.86. It is represented by 78,325,475 shares, without nominal value, with voting rights, each representing an equal share of the capital.

The board of directors may increase the share capital of the company in one or several times by a (cumulated) amount of maximum EUR 959,347,807.86 pursuant to the authorisation granted by the extraordinary shareholders’ meeting on 9 May 2022.
