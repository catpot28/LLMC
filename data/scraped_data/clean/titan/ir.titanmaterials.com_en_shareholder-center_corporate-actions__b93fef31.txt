Corporate Actions

DATE

CORPORATE BODY

CONTENT

05/5/2025

EGM

Change of the Company’s name from “Titan Cement International” to “Titan”.

09/5/2022

EGM

As of 9.5.2022, the share capital of Titan Cement International (“TCI”) has been decreased by an amount of EUR 200,000,000 from €1,159,347,807.86 to €959,347,807.86 without cancellation of shares and by way of reimbursement in cash to the shareholders in accordance with the relevant decision of the Extraordinary Shareholders’ Meeting. As of the same above date, the share capital of TCI amounts to €959,347,807.86, divided into 78,325,475 shares.

22/3/2021

13/5/2019

BoD

EGM

As of 22.6.2021 the total number of voting rights of TCI has been decreased as a result of the cancellation of 4,122,393 own shares. As of the same above date, the share capital of TCI amounts to €1,159,347,807.86, divided into 78,325,475 shares (new denominator).

13/5/2019

AGM

Increase in share capital by 3,779,241 shares effective as of August 28, 2019. Thus the share capital amounts to €1,159,347,807.86, divided into 82,447,868 shares
