ANNOUNCEMENTS: GROUP CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER LEAVES TITAN

Group Chief Financial Officer, Howard Prince-Wright is leaving Titan in March 2008, after six fruitful years. During his tenure, Howard was instrumental in the Group’s internationalization and organizational evolution.

Resulting organizational changes will be announced in due course.
