TITAN Group is included in the FTSE4Good Emerging Index for its enduring commitment to sustainable development

Acknowledged for its enduring commitment to sustainable development, TITAN Group is confirmed as a constituent of the FTSE4Good Emerging Index. The FTSE4Good Index Series is designed to measure the performance of companies demonstrating strong environmental, social and governance (ESG) practices. Τhe FTSE4Good Emerging Index covers over 20 emerging countries, including Greece.

TITAN remains focused on achieving sustainable growth and multiplying the value it creates for and shares with all its stakeholders, at all levels. To this effect, it enhances collaborative action by actively participating as a leader, or even as a founder, in a number of national, European, international and sector initiatives, such as CSR Europe and national partner organizations, the Cement Sustainability Initiative (CSI) operating under the auspices of the World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD), the UN Global Compact and the European Pact for Youth.

About the FTSE4Good Index Series

The FTSE4Good Series is designed to help investors integrate environmental, social and governance (ESG) factors into their investment decisions. The indexes identify companies that better manage ESG risks.
