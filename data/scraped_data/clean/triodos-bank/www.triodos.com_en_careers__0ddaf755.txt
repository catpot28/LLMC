About working at Triodos Bank
Working at Triodos Bank
The most important thing about working with us is that you are part of a community that is changing the world for the better. Our mission is to create a society that protects and promotes the quality of life of all its members with human dignity at its core.
What we offer you
An engaging work culture
We are constantly developing our work culture to create conditions where every person can thrive, so you are able to:
find a sense of meaning in your work
create positive energy and impact with your co-workers
build mutual trust and respect in working relationships
enjoy fulfilment at a professional and personal level
Co-workers are involved in developing the organisation’s mission, and we encourage collaboration across departments. Everyone attends a regular team meetings and all new co-workers are given a comprehensive induction into Triodos Bank's way of working. We also organise meetings at lunchtime or after work that offer an opportunity to discuss various subjects or to listen to a presentation from an invited guest. Those sessions are open to all, and anyone is welcome to suggest a subject.
An inspiring working environment
Our award-winning sustainable head office is based in Driebergen-Rijsenburg, the Netherlands at De Reehorst Estate, just a 3 minutes’ walk from the train station. Whenever you are at the office, it is an inspiring place to work and connect with your colleagues. One of the interesting facts about it is that we can completely take the building apart and move it to a new location. Curious to see more of our office building, then please visit:
www.triodos.nl/reehorst

We work in a hybrid model, which means we also care about you having a safe, and healthy place to work at home. If you need support, we’ll consult with you on your working conditions, provide recommendations, and offer a budget for necessary equipment.
Fair remuneration and working conditions
At Triodos Bank, we care deeply about the wellbeing of our co-workers - because building a more sustainable and inclusive world starts from within. Our benefits reflect our values and help you bring your whole self to work.
Remuneration:
Salary appropriate to the position, our remuneration policy and your experience
Working from home allowance per home office day
Holiday allowance, to plan your vacation
Thirteenth salary, based on the CLA for banks
Making time for rest and relaxation:
30 days of annual leave for a full-time contract (40 hours), plus the option to buy 2 extra weeks of holiday
Extra leave for voluntary work – paid time off dedicated to community service
Option to work part-time, supporting flexibility and work–life balance
Vitality Leave – after 5 years of service, you will be eligible to take a part-paid sabbatical of 2 months. This benefit is then available every 7 years of service thereafter
Planning for the future:
Attractive pension policy with a significant contribution from Triodos Bank
80-80-100 scheme (80% work, 80% income and 100% accrual of pension rights), if you are within seven years of your state retirement age
Pension replacement scheme to build up a suitable pension for the part above the tax limit
Looking after your health:
Mental Health First Aid support, through a network of co-workers and access to One session therapy with an online specialist
Bicycle reimbursement, up to 1,500 EUR toward purchasing a company bike
Suitable home office equipment up to 750 EUR every 5 years
Access to Single Session Therapy anonymously and for free, with additional support from work-life coaches, confidential counsellors, mentors, and advisors
Computer glasses reimbursement
Supporting you and your family:
26 weeks of parental leave if you have a child younger than eight years old to care for, partially paid
6 weeks of (additional) birth leave for partners at 100% pay
6 weeks of paid adoption/foster care leave at 100% pay
10 days of short-term care leave per 12 months (based on 40h), partially paid
Possibility of a long-term care leave, based on your personal needs
And on top of all that:
Mobility card that covers commuting via public transport, with the possibility of free private transport, or a reimbursement for using your own vehicle.
Refer a Friend scheme
Triodos FIT activities
Restaurant with organic food
Learning and Development
At Triodos Bank, we encourage and support every co-worker to continuously learn and develop. Training and education are a very important part of our co-worker experience. Our training programmes combine e-learning, facilitated sessions (both online and in person) and social/peer learning. Not only do they focus on supporting the development of personal and professional skills, but also on the alignment to our mission, values and the impact we want to have in society. Regular personal development dialogues are part of our performance culture at Triodos Bank, giving co-workers the opportunity to discuss their ambitions and growth with their managers.
Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion
Triodos Bank wants to create a welcoming work environment for all. One of our core values is Equity, and a diverse co-worker community contributes positively to the organisation’s effectiveness, vitality and resilience . Triodos Bank aims to reduce barriers to enter and progress within the organisation. This includes:
Initiatives to improve access to the labour market (for example, with refugees in the Netherlands).
Awareness raising activities (such as role modelling of senior leaders)
Targeted co-worker engagement and learning opportunities
Considering diversity within our approach to talent management.
We foster an environment in which all co-workers and our broader community can feel safe and included. We have a zero tolerance approach to discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind. Examples of how we have recently done this include:
Increasing the availability of inclusive hiring training and support for inclusion forums and co-worker led networks
Mutual mentoring with senior leaders
Integrated questions on workplace inclusion as part of the co-worker survey
Channels such as ‘Speak Up’ through which to raise concerns

Frequently asked questions
How do I apply for a job?
Triodos Bank’s policy is to only accept applications for existing job opportunities. We post all our current job opportunities on this page. Applications are welcome for them and can be made via the website.
Does Triodos Bank pay bonuses?
No. At Triodos Bank, we believe that paying all our co-workers fairly and appropriately is very important. We have a remuneration policy designed to create both fair rewards and a relatively flat structure across the bank. And we closely monitor differences in remuneration between co-workers, locally and internationally. Bonuses have no part to play in sustainable banking.
Read more about our International Remuneration policy
, PDF
How are co-workers involved in the bank's mission?
All co-workers take part in a weekly meeting and all new co-workers enjoy a comprehensive introduction to the work of the organisation. There are meetings at lunchtime or after work, which provide all co-workers with an opportunity to discuss subjects of shared interest, or to listen to a presentation from an invited guest.
If I work for Triodos Bank, do I have to live sustainably?
Triodos Bank has a clear mission to work for positive change in society and to promote quality of life for all. Naturally, we think it's very important that all co-workers should have a personal commitment to these values, but that does not mean we expect everyone to live a carbon neutral life.
Does Triodos Bank have an equity, diversity, and inclusion policy?
Yes, you can read about Triodos Bank’s approach to equity, diversity, and inclusion (EDI) in its
Group EDI Policy
, PDF
Our mission is to create a society that protects and promotes the quality of life for all, with human dignity at its core. We are committed to role modeling the equitable society we want to be part of, to being a diverse organisation which reflects the communities and clients we serve and to being transparent about our journey. We seek to be fully inclusive for all current and future co-workers, clients and partners and we know that we must work hard to achieve this.
Triodos Bank also has a set of minimum standards for its investment and financing activities. This includes social standards such as labour rights.
Welcome to positive change
Our approach to banking is different. We work with organisations that contribute to positive social, environmental and cultural change.
