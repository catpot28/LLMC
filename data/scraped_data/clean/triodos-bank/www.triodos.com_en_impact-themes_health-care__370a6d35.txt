Health care
Social
Triodos Bank finances medical centres that offer complementary health services and care for the elderly and terminally ill people.
Our vision on health care
Triodos Bank supports providers of care services who demonstrate a human-centred approach at the heart of their organisation.
More about our vision
By financing values-based care providers, especially those focused on elderly and special-needs, we can help improve the quality of life for individuals in care and the overall well-being of a society in the midst of a transition in how we organise social care.
Quality care
We also see that by focusing on the quality of care, the organisations we finance are better equipped to attract and retain high quality people which has a positive impact for the people being cared for, and the business’ commercial viability.
The choices we make
We have set priorities about what to finance and how to extend our impact through our activities based on our experience and expertise in these sectors.
Our priorities
Given the need to increase the provision of high quality human-centred social care, we focus in particular on funding the creation or development of additional care facilities.
The increasing demand for the provision of elderly care for an ageing society, means we focus our finance on elderly-care facilities. We also concentrate on supporting care services for people with special needs which respond to the challenges and abilities of each individual.
Our activities
The demand for health and social care services in Europe is changing reflecting demographic trends and shifting governmental policy. Whereas every country has its own system for funding health and care services, there is a shared need to increase the availability of high quality care provision.
Managing and reporting impact
We aim to be a leader in impact-driven reporting in the financial industry. Our focus is on managing impact in ways that improve people’s quality of life and on communicating this impact to our stakeholders.

More about impact measures
Triodos Bank believes good physical and mental health and well-being are fundamental to a better quality of life. That’s why we finance medical centres that offer complementary health services and care for the elderly and terminally ill people.
In assessing the impact of health care, indicators of ‘quality’ are necessarily more qualitative. Whilst the organisations we finance ‘comply’ with best practice guidance, we especially look for those where the focus on quality of care is embedded into the organisational culture.

How we measure impact
We measure the number of elderly care homes financed and the number of individuals that live in those care homes, across Triodos Bank’s European network and Triodos Investment Management.
In our calculations we only measure the projects with a direct relationship to our finance or investment and we exclude care homes that are (fully) under construction or offer only day care facilities.
We include 100% of the impact when we co-finance a project unless this represents the results unfairly. If it is not possible to record 100% of the data required, we use conservative estimates.
The ‘Impact per customer’ calculations used throughout the annual report are based on a total of 744,000 customers at the end of 2022.

Impact in different sectors
We finance multiple sustainable sectors. Take a look around.
Community and housing
We finance the development of disadvantaged individuals through businesses that address specific social objectives, and organisations that provide affordable housing for the people who need it most.
Inclusive finance
Whoever you are, and wherever you live in the world, you're likely to want permanent access to financial services. Institutions working for an inclusive financial sector provide these services to underserved populations, including people living on low-incomes and small and medium-sized enterprises.
