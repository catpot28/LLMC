Depository Receipt Holders' Survey
Perspectives on Triodos Bank
We want to know what our stakeholders in general, and investors in the bank itself (or ‘Depository Receipt Holders’) in particular, think about Triodos Bank’s work and the wider world we share.
This helps us understand how well, or not, we’re doing and provides a broader perspective on our work. So every year we ask our depository receipt holders a number of questions. In 2018, Depository Receipt Holders again responded in overwhelming numbers: over 7,000 of more than 40,000 investors responded.
