Investing in sustainable banking
Triodos Bank is a leading expert and global pioneer in sustainable banking with more than 40 years in positive impact creation. Since its inception, Triodos Bank’s mission has been to make money work for positive social, environmental and cultural change. We want to help to create a society that protects and promotes the quality of life of all its members and that has human dignity at its core.
Investors directly contribute to achieving this mission, because the capital they invest in Triodos Bank enables it to finance more organisations working on positive impact. One of the ways investors can support Triodos Bank’s mission, is by investing in Triodos Bank Depository Receipts or Green Bonds.
