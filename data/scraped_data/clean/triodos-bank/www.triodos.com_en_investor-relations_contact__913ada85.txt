Frequently asked questions
ABN AMRO's Corporate Broking Portal
Why am I receiving an invitation from ABN AMRO for the SAAT Meeting of Depository Receipt Holders?
Under a cooperation agreement between Triodos Bank and SAAT, Triodos Bank is responsible for organising the Meeting of Depository Receipt Holders and registering votes at this meeting. Given the operational scale of this activity, Triodos Bank has opted to outsource these tasks by choosing ABN AMRO's specialist Corporate Broking Portal, also used by other listed companies for this purpose.
What data does Triodos Bank share with ABN AMRO for the SAAT Meeting of Depository Holders and what is the legal basis for this?
Triodos Bank and SAAT have entered into a cooperation agreement. Under this agreement, Triodos Bank is responsible for organising the Meeting of Depository Holders and registering votes at this meeting. Triodos has chosen to outsource these tasks by using ABN AMRO's Corporate Broking Portal.
The right to vote at the Meeting of Depository Receipt Holders is a right agreed contractually with the depository receipt holders. The legal basis for sharing the data is therefore 'performance of a contract' (AVG Art. 6-1-b).
To enable the organisation of the Meeting of Depository Receipt Holders, Triodos shares the following data with ABN AMRO:
name of the Depository Receipt Holder
e-mail address of the Depository Receipt Holder
physical address of the Depository Receipt Holder
number of certificates per Depository Receipt Holder
ABN AMRO only uses this data for the agreed purpose of organising the Meeting of Depository Receipt Holders and registering votes. The above data will be destroyed by ABN AMRO one month after the end of the meeting.
Fractions of Depository Receipts
What happens to fractions of Triodos Bank Depository Receipts?
Fractions are parts of a whole certificate, such as 0.25 or 0.5 of a certificate. You own part of a certificate, but not a whole one. Fractions cannot be traded on Euronext Amsterdam. If you transfer your Triodos Bank Depository Receipts to another broker or are a customer of Captin, Triodos Bank will round up your position to the nearest whole certificate, depending on your situation:
I have an account with Captin
If you already have an account with Captin, rounding will take place automatically before the listing.
I am transferring Triodos Bank Depository Receipts to a bank or broker
If you are transferring to a bank or broker, rounding will occur just before the transfer.
I do not yet have a securities account
If you do not yet have a securities account, the fraction will remain unchanged until you transfer your Triodos Bank Depository Receipts.

Trading Conditions
Can I trade Triodos Bank Depository Receipts as soon as I have a securities account?
No, this is only possible once the Triodos Bank Depository Receipts have been transferred to your securities account with a bank or broker that supports trading on Euronext, from that moment on you can participate in trading on Euronext Amsterdam.
Can I sell Triodos Bank Depository Receipts without a securities account?
No, you must transfer your Triodos Bank Depository Receipts to a securities account with a bank or broker that supports trading on Euronext, in order to trade. A list of banks and brokers that support trading is available
here
.
Can I purchase additional Triodos Bank Depository Receipts via Euronext?
Yes, you can purchase additional Triodos Bank Depository Receipts via your bank or broker, subject to supply and demand on the exchange.
Data processing and cooperation Triodos Bank
Who registers the Triodos Bank Depository Receipt (DR) Holders?
Based on a cooperation agreement with Stichting Administratiekantoor Aandelen Triodos Bank (SAAT), Triodos Bank is responsible for registering DR Holders. Triodos Bank has entered into an agreement with Captin BV (Captin) for the management of the register.
Have agreements been made with Captin regarding data processing?
Captin and Triodos have entered into a cooperation agreement. As an appendix to this agreement, Triodos and Captin have also entered into a data processing agreement in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. The data processing agreement governs the data processing carried out by Captin on behalf of Triodos for the purpose of managing the register of Depository Receipts.
What role does ABN AMRO play in providing services to DR Holders?
ABN AMRO has been engaged to provide various banking services in connection with the listing and trading on Euronext Amsterdam of the certificates issued by SAAT. Their tasks include:
Agent for the listing
Operational support
Organisation of shareholders meetings and sending invitations to these meetings
Paying agent
Representing SAAT vis-à-vis Euroclear Netherlands
Have SAAT and Triodos Bank made agreements with ABN AMRO regarding data processing?
Yes, SAAT and Triodos Bank have entered into a ‘service agreement’ with ABN AMRO, with a ‘data processing agreement’ attached as an appendix, in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.
