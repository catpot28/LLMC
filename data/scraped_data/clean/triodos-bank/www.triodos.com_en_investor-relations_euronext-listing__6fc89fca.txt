Transfer Forms
To participate in trading Triodos Bank Depository Receipts on Euronext, your next steps depend on your current situation:
If you
do not wish to trade
, no action is required.
If you
already hold a trading account with Captin
and intend to trade, please initiate the transfer directly via the Captin platform. You do not need to complete the transfer request form available on this website.
If you
do not have a Captin account
and wish to trade on Euronext, you will first need to open a securities account with a bank or broker of your choice. Once your securities account is open (or if you already have one), please complete the transfer request form available on this website to be able to participate in trading.
