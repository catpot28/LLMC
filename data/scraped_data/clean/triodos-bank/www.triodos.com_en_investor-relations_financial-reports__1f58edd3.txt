This download center provides access to a variety of reports and documents related to Triodos Bank’s financial performance and impact reporting.
​​​​​​​Triodos Bank creates positive change through lending, investing and giving. Triodos Bank aims to achieve its mission as a sustainable bank in three ways: as a values-driven service provider; as a relationship bank and as a frontrunner in responsible banking. Since 1980, we have enabled individuals, organisations and businesses to use their money in ways that benefit people and the environment. We report on our impact and financial performance in our integrated online report.
