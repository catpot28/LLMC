Financial and impact reporting
Triodos Bank finances entrepreneurs and initiatives through its banking activities in 5 European countries and its investment management in 81 countries worldwide.

Triodos Bank creates positive change through lending, investing and giving. Triodos Bank aims to achieve its mission as a sustainable bank in three ways: as a values-driven service provider; as a relationship bank and as a frontrunner in responsible banking. Since 1980, we have enabled individuals, organisations and businesses to use their money in ways that benefit people and the environment. We report on our impact and financial performance in our integrated online report.

Impact of Triodos Bank
Triodos Bank and its investment funds, offered via Triodos Investment Management, finance and co-finance enterprises in a range of sectors. We want to connect Depository Receipt Holders and investors with socially responsible businesses to build a movement for a sustainable, socially inclusive society, built on the conscious use of money.
