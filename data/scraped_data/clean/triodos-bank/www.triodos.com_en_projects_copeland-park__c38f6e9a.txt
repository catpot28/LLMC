Arts and culture
Copeland Park is a social, cultural and creative hub in Peckham, an area of London that is becoming increasingly known as a haven for artistic individuals and collectives. At Copeland Park, traditional warehouses and industrial buildings have been transformed into workspace for a number of creative businesses. The historic Bussey Building, for example, now provides a home for artists' studios, theatre groups, live music venues, fitness studios and faith groups - along with incomparable views of London.
