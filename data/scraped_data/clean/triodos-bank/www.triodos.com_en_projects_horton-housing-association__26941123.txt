Horton Housing Association is a charitable organisation that aims to deliver selfesteem, respect and independence to people who are disadvantaged by housing and social circumstances, discrimination, financial exclusion and other forms of social need. Horton Housing offers personalised housing-related support and training and develops housing schemes for those who struggle to access mainstream housing and statutory services.
