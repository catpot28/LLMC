Are you working on a project that restores nature, strengthens communities, or helps society move in a fairer direction? And do you need support where social and environmental value matters more than financial return? Then you’re in the right place.
​​​​​​​
Many transformative ideas don’t fit the traditional banking model. At Triodos Regenerative Money Centre, they do. Here, you can access funding created for pioneers like you. Whether you’re looking for investment to grow, or a donation to get started: your commitment to a greener, fairer future comes first.
How you benefit
Are you ready to create meaningful change for people, nature and future generations? At Triodos Regenerative Money Centre, you receive more than funding. You join a community that believes in your vision, amplifies your impact, and helps you realise solutions that restore and renew.
By supporting early-stage changemakers, we help you unlock stuck systems and drive change where it is needed most. Whether you focus on restoring natural ecosystems or building a fairer society, you’ll find the resources and partnership to help your idea thrive.
Investments
€ 0.5 to € 3 million
Do you need capital to drive large-scale change? Then you can apply for an investment. If your project in Western Europe has a proven track record, a forward-looking model and the power to inspire others, we’d like to explore options with you.
Financing is tailored to your needs. This can include equity, debt or more creative solutions that fit your situation.
Donations
€ 5,000 to € 20,000
Are you running a non-profit project in the Netherlands that you want to get off the ground or move to the next phase? Through Triodos Foundation, you can apply for a donation. Even if your impact cannot easily be measured in financial terms, your commitment to serving people and future generations could be supported.

Investments
For ambitious changemakers
Triodos Regenerative Money Centre offers investments of €0.5 to €3 million for pioneers who want to drive real change. If you are ready to challenge the status quo and show that another way is possible, we may be the right partner for you.
Request an investment of €0.5 to €3 million
Is our investment right for you?
You can apply if your initiative:
drives transformative change in sustainability or social impact
has at least two years of market traction and an experienced team
is scalable or replicable
values collaboration with other impact-driven funders
has not yet attracted commercial investment
delivers tangible benefits for people and planet (especially in agri-food or nature restoration)
operates in Western Europe
Why apply for an investment?
Secure substantial funding
Access €0.5–€3 million to help your initiative grow, be replicated and inspire others.
Flexible financial tools
Benefit from tailored finance. This may include equity, debt or innovative solutions that fit your project.
Impact-first partnership
Work with a partner that puts social and environmental impact first, not just financial returns.

For purpose-driven non-profits
Through Triodos Foundation, you can apply for a donation between €5,000 and €20,000 to support bold, innovative and non-profit projects. Whether your idea is large or small, what matters is your commitment to positive change.

Is our donation right for you?
Your project is eligible if it:
makes a real difference for people and planet
contributes to a better world for future generations
operates as a non-profit (with no commercial motives)
is based in the Netherlands, with potential for nationwide impact
brings fresh, constructive ideas
focuses on services, advocacy, empowerment/education or research

ANBI status: serving the public good
Triodos Sustainable Finance Foundation and Triodos Foundation are entities of Triodos Regenerative Money Centre. These entities hold ANBI status. Triodos Regenerative Money Centre adheres to ANBI regulations.
ANBI is a Dutch designation. It stands for “Public Benefit Organisation” and is specific to the Netherlands. An organisation with ANBI status is recognised by the Dutch tax authorities as serving the public good. This grants certain tax benefits to the organisation and its donors, such as tax-deductible donations.
