Research and Insights
Our research and insights focus on how to finance change and change finance. We study economic transformations, focusing on five essential transitions in food, energy, resources, society and wellbeing and the structural reforms needed in our economy and financial system. By combining rigorous analysis with a clear normative perspective, we aim to make visible both the problems our world is facing and propose how various actors can play their role addressing them.

Meet the team
The Impact & Economics team works to align finance with a fair and sustainable economy. We analyse how economic systems, financial markets and public policy shape social and environmental outcomes. Through publications, media contributions and advocacy, we seek to accelerate the transition to a prosperous life for people on a thriving planet. Interested in our perspectives? Get in touch!
Show information about Maritza Cabezas
Maritza Cabezas
Economist
As economic researcher, Maritza Cabezas helps shape the company’s economic and financial market views, with a strong emphasis on sustainability. Before joining Triodos, she worked at the Dutch Central Bank and ABN AMRO. She holds master’s degrees in development economics and public finance.
Maritza focuses on emerging markets and the role of finance in reducing inequalities. She developed a model for assessing country risk in emerging markets and provides internal ratings for these markets. Maritza also coordinates the integration of gender lens investing and shares her expertise in international working groups.

Researcher
As economic researcher, Ernst Hobma studies the transitions needed to bring the economy and financial system within planetary boundaries. Matija Kajić
Researcher
As sustainability researcher, Matija Kajić focuses on the resource and energy transitions. She is committed to advancing responsible resource use in ways that enable wellbeing while respecting planetary boundaries. Before joining Triodos, Matija obtained her PhD at Utrecht University.
Drawing on experience across non-profit, for-profit and academic settings, Matija emphasises the importance of cross-sector and multidisciplinary collaboration. She believes research should inform action and prioritises advocacy that helps create the conditions for systemic change.

Emma de Leeuw
Impact Manager
As impact manager, Emma de Leeuw works to advance finance for positive change. She strengthens Triodos’ impact strategy by translating sustainability ambitions into practical actions and decision-making frameworks. Show information about Sven Renon
Sven Renon
Impact manager
As impact manager, Sven Renon knows how to shape climate and nature policy that keeps us within the limits of our planet, as a financial sector and with national and European policy. He joined Triodos in 2023 after several years at Impact Institute. He also has extensive expertise in industry standards for impact measurement and reporting.
Sven advocates reducing absolute emissions and investing in real climate solutions in the short term, rather than relying primarily on net-zero targets, voluntary alliances or carbon credits.

Show information about Hans Stegeman
Hans Stegeman
Chief economist
As chief economist and group director Impact & Economics, Hans Stegeman works on transforming the economic system, with finance as a key lever for change. His focus is on post-growth economics, sustainability transitions and the institutional reforms needed to align the economy with planetary boundaries.
Before joining Triodos, he worked at Rabobank, the Netherlands Bureau for Economic Policy Analysis (CPB), and the labour union FNV. In his PhD dissertation
Transforming Economics for Sustainability
(2025), he argues that sustainability must be the starting point of economic thinking. Hans writes for
Vrij Nederland
, newspapers such as NRC and Het Financieele Dagblad, and the podcast
Money for Change
.

Pjotr Tjallema

Show information about Joeri de Wilde
Joeri de Wilde
Economist
As economic researcher, Joeri de Wilde writes columns, articles and research at the intersection of economics and sustainability.
