World Circular Economy Forum
Open invitation to join the Triodos Bank accelerator session on institutional set-up for a circular transition
At this year’s World Circular Economy Forum (WCEF), on the 16th of May Triodos Bank will host an accelerator session titled ‘Global Insights: Institutional Set-Up for a Circular Transition’. The growing demand for natural resources is depleting the planet’s reserves and degrading ecosystems. In response to this Triodos Bank has
continually called
for a shift from a wasteful, linear economy to a restorative, circular one.
This session examines how various institutional frameworks are set-up and identifies the optimal approach for advancing the circular economy. Participants will gain insights on what makes up the enabling environment for circular business and finance - what works and what doesn’t? And who is driving the transition towards circularity - governments or businesses?
Join us for this exciting session and contribute to a growing research initiative on institutional drivers of circularity by
registering here
!
What can you expect of this session?
Newest insights linking institutional quality to circular economy outcomes
Audience engagement to connect findings to real-world professional experiences
Relevant policy and company examples from each continent
A discussion of approaches in South America specifically, since this year’s WCEF is hosted in Brazil
