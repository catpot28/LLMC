Registration Form
Event
Europe is at the dawn of a new phase in digital policy. As a strong foundation has been laid by regulation Europe’s challenge remains at the table:

‘
How to strengthen Europe’s competitiveness in the Digital Domain
’

This will be the central theme of an event on
Wednesday March 26
in Amsterdam.

The event will take place in the context of the meeting of the digital advanced member states, the D9+.

Together with business federations in these member states, VNO-NCW is part of the B9+.

We expect an interesting program, with speakers of the EC, government, scientific community and last but not least business, great and small.

Details
Planning: Program from 13.30 until 17.00,

from 17.00 until 18.00 networking event/drinks

Location: Amsterdam
